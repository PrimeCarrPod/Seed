## The Planck Scale d = 16 — UV/IR Meeting Point

The self-dual gap $d=16$ (A1-36) is the Planck scale where UV and IR physics meet. It is the horizon of the emergent AdS$_2$ bulk.

**Energy at $d=16$.** The energy scale corresponding to gap $d$ is $E(d) = \hbar/(\kappa d)$ (A1-17). At $d=16$:
$$E_{16} = \frac{\hbar}{\kappa \cdot 16}$$
From A1-09, the Compton scale is reached after $N = 3.67 \times 10^9$ steps with total proper time $\tau_C = N \kappa \bar{d}$. The ratio gives:
$$\frac{E_{\text{Compton}}}{E_{16}} = \frac{\hbar/(\kappa \tau_C)}{\hbar/(\kappa \cdot 16)} = \frac{16}{\tau_C} \sim 10^{19} \text{ GeV}$$
This identifies $E_{16}$ with the Planck energy $E_{\text{Pl}} \sim 10^{19}$ GeV.

**Self-duality at Planck scale.** The IR/UV duality $d \leftrightarrow 256/d$ (A1-36, since $16^2 = 256$) exchanges UV ($d < 16$) and IR ($d > 16$). At $d=16$, the theory is self-dual. This is the hallmark of a Planck-scale fixed point.

**Horizon geometry.** The $d=16$ surface is a bifurcate Killing horizon in the AdS$_2$ bulk. The horizon area is:
$$\mathcal{A} = \int_{d=16} \sqrt{g} dx = 4G S_{\text{BH}}$$
The Bekenstein-Hawking entropy is $S_{\text{BH}} = \mathcal{A}/(4G) = \log 78$ (the BPS count, A1-28).

**Temperature and entropy.** The horizon temperature is $T_H = 1/(2\pi) \cdot (1/16) = 1/(32\pi)$ in gap units. The entropy is $S = 2\pi E_{16}/T_H = \log 78$.

**Light sector (exterior).** For $d < 16$ ($z < 1$), the geometry is the AdS$_2$ exterior. The metric is regular. Gap multiplicities are high (UV dominance).

**Heavy sector (interior).** For $d > 16$ ($z > 1$), the geometry is the AdS$_2$ interior. The BPS states live here. The metric is regular down to the Poincaré horizon at $z \to \infty$.

**RG flow as radial evolution.** The RG flow from A1-36 is exactly the radial evolution in the bulk. Integrating out UV modes ($d < 16$) moves the cutoff inward from $z=1$. Integrating out IR modes ($d > 16$) moves the cutoff outward.
