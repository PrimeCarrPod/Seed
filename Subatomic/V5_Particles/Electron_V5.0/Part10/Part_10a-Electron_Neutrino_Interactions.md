### 1. ν-e Scattering: Standard Model

**Charged current (νₑ only)**: νₑ + e⁻ → νₑ + e⁻ (W exchange)
**Neutral current (all flavors)**: ν + e⁻ → ν + e⁻ (Z exchange)

**Cross section** (low energy E_ν ≪ M_W):
$$\sigma(\nu_e e) = \frac{G_F^2 m_e E_\nu}{2\pi} [(g_L^e+1)^2 + (g_R^e)^2/3]$$
$$\sigma(\nu_\mu e) = \frac{G_F^2 m_e E_\nu}{2\pi} [(g_L^e)^2 + (g_R^e)^2/3]$$

**Couplings**: g_L^e = -1/2 + sin²θ_W, g_R^e = sin²θ_W

**pp chain**: pp, ⁷Be, ⁸B, hep neutrinos

### 3. Reactor & Atmospheric Neutrinos

**Reactors**: ν̄ₑ from fission → ν̄ₑ + p → e⁺ + n (inverse beta decay)

### 4. Weak Mixing Angle at Low Q²

**sin²θ_W(Q²)** running:
- Q² = 0: sin²θ_W = 0.2387(16) (from APV)
- Q² = M_Z²: sin²θ_W = 0.23122(4)
- **Low Q² sensitivity** to BSM (Z', dark photon, etc.)

**Electron experiments**:
- APV (Cs, Yb, Ra⁺) → sin²θ_W at Q² ≈ 0
- MOLLER, P2 (Part 7) → sin²θ_W at Q² ~ 0.005 GeV²
- ν-e scattering (CHARM, BNL E734) → sin²θ_W at Q² ~ 0.1 GeV²
