### Abstract

BSM contributions to d_e from SUSY, 2HDM, left-right symmetric models, extra dimensions. Global fits combining EDM, g-2, collider, and flavor constraints. Worldline kink phase for each BSM vertex.

### 1. SUSY (MSSM) — Phases in Soft Terms

**CP-violating phases**: φ_μ, φ_A, φ_1, φ_2, φ_3 (gaugino masses)

**Dominant diagrams**:
- Chargino-sneutrino loop: d_e ~ (α/4π) (m_e/m_SUSY²) tanβ sin(φ_μ + φ_A - φ_2)
- Neutralino-selectron loop: similar structure

**Constraints from ACME II**:
- |φ_μ|, |φ_A| < 0.01 for m_SUSY = 1 TeV, tanβ = 10
- "SUSY CP problem": phases naturally O(1), but EDM forces small values

**Worldline**: SUSY partners = worldline excursions into SUSY sector with CP-odd phases.

---

### 2. Two-Higgs-Doublet Model (2HDM)

**Type II** (MSSM-like): tanβ enhanced
**Type X** (lepton-specific): Enhanced e, μ, τ couplings

**d_e from Barr-Zee diagrams** (2-loop):
- Top/τ loop with h⁰, H⁰, A⁰ exchange
- d_e ~ (α/4π)² (m_e m_τ/m_A²) tan³β

**Constraints**: m_A > 500 GeV for tanβ = 10; m_A > 1 TeV for tanβ = 50.

---

### 3. Left-Right Symmetric Models

**Gauge group**: SU(3)_c × SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R × U(1)_B-L

**New CP phases**: In W_R mixing, Higgs sector

**d_e scaling**: d_e ~ (m_e/m_W_R²) × phase

**ACME constraint**: m_W_R > 10 TeV for O(1) phases.

---

### 4. Extra Dimensions

**ADD (flat)**: KK gravitons → d_e ~ m_e/M_Pl² × (TeV/M_Pl)^n
**Randall-Sundrum (warped)**: KK gluons/gravitons → d_e ~ m_e/m_KK²

**Constraints**: m_KK > 5 TeV typically.

---

### 5. Global Fits (2024): EDM + g-2 + Collider + Flavor

| Observable | Constraint | BSM Sensitivity |
|------------|------------|-----------------|
| **d_e** | < 1.1×10⁻²⁹ e·cm | Leptonic CPV, ~10 TeV |
| **d_μ** | < 1.8×10⁻¹⁹ e·cm | 2nd gen CPV, ~1 TeV |
| **d_n** | < 1.8×10⁻²⁶ e·cm | Hadronic CPV, ~1 TeV |
| **a_e** | 0.13 ppb | Universal BSM, ~10 TeV |
| **a_μ** | 4.2σ tension | Chiral BSM, ~1 TeV |
| **LHC** | Direct searches | Mass limits |
| **B-physics** | Flavor anomalies | Chiral BSM |

**Key result**: Electron EDM + g-2 **breaks degeneracies** in global SUSY/2HDM fits.

**Worldline view**: Each observable = different moment of worldline kink distribution:
- d_e = CP-odd 1st moment (phase)
- a_e = CP-even 2nd moment (variance)
- d_μ, a_μ = same for 1st excitation (gap=4)
