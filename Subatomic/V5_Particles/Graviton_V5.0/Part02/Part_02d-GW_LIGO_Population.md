### Abstract

BBH/BNS population properties from O4. Merger rates, mass functions, spin distributions, redshift evolution. Worldline ensemble statistics.

---

### 1. Merger Rate Densities

**BBH**: $R_{\text{BBH}} = 17-44$ Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹ (GWTC-3)
**BNS**: $R_{\text{BNS}} = 10-1700$ Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹
**NSBH**: $R_{\text{NSBH}} = 7.8-140$ Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹
**O4 projection**: Rates measured to ~20% precision

---

### 2. Mass Function Models

**Power-law + peak**: $p(m) \propto m^{-\alpha} + \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma)$
**Broken power-law**: Two regimes with break at $m_{\text{break}}$
**O4 preference**: Power-law + peak for BBH; BNS consistent with flat

**Worldline**: Mass = integrated prime gap density at dir 3.0.

---

### 3. Spin Distribution

**Magnitude**: $\chi \in [0, 0.9]$ — uniform or beta distribution
**Orientation**: Isotropic or aligned
**Effective spin**: $\chi_{\text{eff}}$ consistent with 0
**Precession**: $\chi_p$ measured for loud events

---

### 4. Redshift Evolution

**Rate evolution**: $R(z) = R_0 (1+z)^\kappa$
**O4**: $\kappa \sim 1-3$ (mild evolution)
**Future**: LISA, ET will measure to $z \sim 10$

---

### 5. Pair-Instability Mass Gap

**Theory**: 50-120 M⊙ gap from pulsational pair-instability SNe
**Observations**: GW190521 (85+66 M⊙) in upper gap
**Interpretation**: Hierarchical mergers, stellar evolution uncertainties
