### 2. FCC-hh: 100 TeV pp Collider

- $t\bar{t}h$: 1.5 pb
- $hh$: 1.4 pb → $\kappa_\lambda 5\%$

| $\kappa_\lambda$ | 5% | $hh$ |

### 3. Muon Collider: Clean Higgs Factory

**$\sqrt{s} = 10-30$ TeV**, $L = 10^{34-35}$ cm⁻²s⁻¹
**Advantages**:
- No PDF uncertainty (point-like $\mu$)
- $s$-channel $h$ production: $\mu^+\mu^- \to h$
- Polarized beams possible
- Clean $h \to \gamma\gamma, Z\gamma, \mu\mu$

**Key**:
- $\sigma(\mu\mu \to h) \propto \kappa_\mu^2$ → direct $\kappa_\mu$
- $hh$ via $\mu\mu \to hh$ → $\kappa_\lambda$
- $h$ width from $s$-channel scan
