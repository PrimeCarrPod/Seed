### 3. Muon Collider — Clean Gluon Factory

**√s = 10-30 TeV**, L = 10³⁴ cm⁻²s⁻¹
**Advantages**:
- No PDF uncertainty (point-like μ)
- Clean gg→H, gg→HH
- Polarized beams possible

**Gluon physics**:
- gg→H cross section to 1% → κ_g
- gg→HH → λ_HHH to 5%
- Gluon PDF from μ+μ- → jet+X (no PDF)
