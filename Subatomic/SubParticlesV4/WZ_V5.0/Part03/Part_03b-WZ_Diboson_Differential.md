### 1. Differential Diboson Distributions

**Key observables**:
- $p_T^V$: Vector boson $p_T$ — sensitive to aTGCs, EFT
- $m_{VV}$: Diboson invariant mass — resonant BSM
- $\Delta \phi_{VV}$: Azimuthal separation — aTGCs
- $y_V$: Rapidity — PDF, EW corrections

**NNLO+PS**: $WW, WZ, ZZ$ matched to parton shower
**Precision**: 3-5% per bin (stat + syst)

---

### 2. VBF Diboson Production

**Process**: $qq \to qq VV$ (forward jets)
**Advantage**: Pure EW, no QCD background
**Channels**:
- $qq \to qq WW$ (same-sign $WW$ — golden)
- $qq \to qq WZ$
- $qq \to qq ZZ$

**Same-sign $WW$**: 
- $\sigma \approx 3$ fb at 13 TeV
- No $t\bar{t}$ background
- **Unitarity test**: $W_L W_L \to W_L W_L$ via VBF

**Measurements**: ATLAS/CMS → $\kappa_V$ at high $p_T$

---

### 3. High-$p_T$ Tails & EFT

**High-$p_T$ sensitivity**: $p_T^V > 500$ GeV → BSM reach

**SMEFT effects**:
- $\mathcal{O}_{WWW}$: $\lambda$ → $p_T^V$ enhancement ($\sim p_T^4$)
- $\mathcal{O}_{W}$: $\Delta \kappa$ → $p_T^V$ shape
- $\mathcal{O}_{HWB}$: $\Delta g_1^Z$ → $p_T^V$ at high $m_{VV}$

**HL-LHC reach** (3 ab⁻¹):
- $\Delta \kappa_\gamma$: **0.5%**
- $\Delta g_1^Z$: **1%**
- $\lambda_\gamma$: **0.005**
