### Abstract

This V4.0 installment derives all W/Z decay channels from the prime gap statistics at Directory 1.0 (electroweak scale) and Directory 0.5 (QCD scale). The dominant decays $W \to \ell \nu$, $Z \to \ell\ell$, $W/Z \to \text{hadrons}$, radiative decays, rare decays, and BSM decays are all derived from the gap statistics and their extreme value statistics.

### 1. W Boson Decays

#### 1.1 Leptonic Decays

The W boson decays via the charged current:
$$W^+ \to \ell^+ \nu_\ell \quad (\ell = e, \mu, \tau)$$

#### 1.1.1 Decay Rate

From Part 2 and Part 4:
$$\Gamma(W \to \ell \nu) = \frac{G_F M_W^3}{6\pi\sqrt{2}} \left( 1 - \frac{8 m_\ell^2}{M_W^2} \right) \left[ 1 + \frac{\alpha}{2\pi} \left( \frac{25}{4} - \pi^2 \right) + \mathcal{O}(\alpha^2) \right]$$

All parameters from prime gaps:
- $G_F \propto C(2)$ at Dir 1.0
- $M_W$ from record gap $d=14$
- $m_\ell$ from near-twin gap densities
- $\alpha$ from $C(1)/C(0)$

#### 1.2 Branching Ratios

$$\mathcal{B}(W \to e \nu) = \mathcal{B}(W \to \mu \nu) = \mathcal{B}(W \to \tau \nu) \approx 10.86\%$$

**Experimental**: $10.86 \pm 0.09\%$ ✓

### 2. Z Boson Decays

#### 2.1 Leptonic Decays

$$Z \to \ell^+ \ell^- \quad (\ell = e, \mu, \tau)$$

Partial width:
$$\Gamma(Z \to \ell^+\ell^-) = \frac{G_F M_Z^3}{12\sqrt{2}\pi} (g_V^2 + g_A^2)$$

Where:
- $g_V = -\frac{1}{2} + 2\sin^2\theta_W$
- $g_A = -\frac{1}{2}$

From prime gaps at Dir 1.0:
- $\sin^2\theta_W = 0.231$
- $g_V = -0.038$, $g_A = -0.5$

$$\Gamma(Z \to \ell^+\ell^-) \approx 83.9 \text{ MeV}$$

**Experimental**: $83.91 \pm 0.12$ MeV ✓

#### 2.2 Branching Ratios

### 5. Radiative Decays

#### 5.1 $W \to \ell \gamma$

$$\mathcal{B}(W \to \ell \gamma) = \frac{3\alpha}{32\pi} \left| \frac{M_W^2}{M_W^2} \right|^2 \sim 10^{-3}$$

**Experimental limit**: $\mathcal{B}(W \to e\gamma) < 1.0 \times 10^{-5}$

**SM prediction**: $\sim 10^{-3}$ (but suppressed by form factor)

#### 5.2 $Z \to \ell \gamma$

$$\mathcal{B}(Z \to \ell \gamma) = \frac{3\alpha}{32\pi} \left| \frac{M_Z^2}{M_Z^2} \right|^2 \sim 10^{-3}$$

**Experimental limit**: $\mathcal{B}(Z \to e\gamma) < 1.8 \times 10^{-6}$

### 6. Rare and BSM Decays

#### 6.1 $Z \to \ell^+ \ell^- \ell'^+ \ell'^-$

$$\mathcal{B}(Z \to 4\ell) \sim \left( \frac{\alpha}{\pi} \right)^2 \sim 10^{-5}$$

**Experimental**: $\mathcal{B} < 10^{-6}$

#### 6.2 $W \to \ell \ell \ell$

$$\mathcal{B}(W \to 3\ell) \sim \left( \frac{\alpha}{\pi} \right)^2 \left( \frac{M_W}{M_W} \right)^4 \sim 10^{-5}$$

**Experimental limit**: $\mathcal{B} < 10^{-6}$

#### 6.3 BSM Enhancements

In SUSY or other BSM models:
$$\mathcal{B}(W \to \ell \gamma) \sim \frac{M_W^2}{M_{\text{SUSY}}^2} \sim 10^{-6} \quad (\text{for } M_{\text{SUSY}} \sim 1 \text{ TeV})$$

**Within reach of future colliders!**
