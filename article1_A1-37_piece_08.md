## Modular Flow as Bulk Time Evolution

The modular Hamiltonian (A1-33) generates the modular flow, which is the bulk time evolution in the entanglement wedge.

**Modular Hamiltonian from gaps.** For a subregion $A$, the modular Hamiltonian is $K_A = -\log \rho_A$. For the thermal state $\rho_A = e^{-\beta H_A}/Z_A$, $K_A = \beta H_A$. In general, for Gaussian states (A1-34):
$$K_A = \frac{1}{2} x^T \Sigma_A^{-1} x$$
where $x$ are the worldline position/momentum operators restricted to $A$.

**Modular flow.** The modular flow is the one-parameter automorphism group:
$$\alpha_t(A) = \rho_A^{it} A \rho_A^{-it} = e^{i K_A t} A e^{-i K_A t}$$
For the bulk field $\phi(z,t)$, this acts as:
$$\alpha_t(\phi(z,0)) = \phi(z, t_{\text{bulk}}(z))$$
where the bulk time $t_{\text{bulk}}(z) = \beta(z) t$ with $\beta(z) = 2\pi z$ (local inverse temperature).

**Tomita-Takesaki theory.** The modular flow implements the bulk diffeomorphism generated by the Killing vector $\xi = \partial_t + 2\pi z \partial_z$. This is the bulk time translation in the Rindler wedge of AdS$_2$.

**Modular flow = bulk time.** For the vacuum state in AdS$_2$, the modular flow of a boundary interval is the bulk time evolution in the entanglement wedge. The modular Hamiltonian is the bulk Hamiltonian restricted to the wedge.

**Gap-dependent flow.** For the prime electron worldline, the modular flow frequency depends on the gap:
$$\omega_d = \frac{\beta \hbar}{\kappa d} = \frac{2\pi z}{d_0} = \frac{2\pi d}{16 \cdot 16} = \frac{\pi d}{128}$$
where $z = d/16$. Each gap sector evolves with its own frequency.

**BPS sector: trivial flow.** For BPS states ($d > 16$), $\rho_{\text{BPS}} = \frac{1}{78}\mathbb{I}$, so $K_{\text{BPS}} = 0$ and $\alpha_t = \text{id}$. The BPS bulk operators are time-independent — they are topological.

**Flow and relative entropy.** The relative entropy is the "distance" along the modular flow:
$$S(\rho||\sigma) = \int_0^\infty \frac{dt}{t} \langle (\alpha_t^\rho - \alpha_t^\sigma)^\dagger (\alpha_t^\rho - \alpha_t^\sigma) \rangle$$
This is the integral of the squared difference of modular flows.

**PrimeBookOne modular flow.** The 3500 books give the modular flow at each scale. The flow trajectory from book $b$ to $b'$ is the RG flow from scale $\mu_b$ to $\mu_{b'}$.
