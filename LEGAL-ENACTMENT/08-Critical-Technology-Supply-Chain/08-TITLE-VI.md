# TITLE VI — FUNDING MECHANISMS

## SEC. 601. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 601.** This title may be cited as the "Critical Technology Supply Chain Funding Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 602. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO FUNDING

**SEC. 602.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Funding Gap**
Current federal funding for critical technology supply chains is insufficient to address the scale of the challenge. The annual funding gap is estimated at $20 billion.

**(2) Dedicated Funding Needed**
A dedicated, sustainable funding mechanism is needed to provide reliable, long-term funding for critical technology supply chain programs.

**(3) Multiple Funding Sources**
A combination of funding sources is needed, including appropriations, tariffs, and fees.

**(4) Economic Return**
Every dollar invested in critical technology supply chains returns $2.50 in economic benefits.

---

## SEC. 603. PURPOSES OF TITLE VI

**SEC. 603.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a Critical Technology Supply Chain Fund.

**(2)** Provide tax incentives for domestic production.

**(3)** Ensure sustainable, long-term funding.

**(4)** Promote equitable distribution of funds.

---

## SEC. 604. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VI

**SEC. 604.** In this title:

**(1) "Critical Technology Supply Chain Fund"**
The fund established under Sec. 605.

**(2) "Tax Incentive"**
A tax credit, deduction, or exclusion provided under this title.

## SEC. 605. CRITICAL TECHNOLOGY SUPPLY CHAIN FUND

**SEC. 605.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Critical Technology Supply Chain Fund.

**(b) PURPOSES.—**The Fund shall provide financial assistance for:
(1) Semiconductor supply chain programs
(2) Rare earth minerals programs
(3) Pharmaceutical supply chain programs
(4) Strategic reserve
(5) International partnerships

**(c) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $200,000,000,000 over 10 years.

---

## SEC. 606. TAX INCENTIVES

**SEC. 606.** (a) DOMESTIC PRODUCTION TAX CREDIT.—**A 25% tax credit for domestic semiconductor manufacturing.

**(b) RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT TAX CREDIT.—**A 20% tax credit for critical technology research and development.

**(c) WORKFORCE TRAINING TAX CREDIT.—**A 15% tax credit for critical technology workforce training.

---

## SEC. 607. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE VI

**SEC. 607.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $200,000,000,000 over 10 years.
