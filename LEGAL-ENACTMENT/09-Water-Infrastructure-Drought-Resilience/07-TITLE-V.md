# TITLE V — CLIMATE ADAPTATION

## SEC. 501. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 501.** This title may be cited as the "Water Climate Adaptation Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 502. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO CLIMATE ADAPTATION

**SEC. 502.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Climate Change Impacts**
Climate change is intensifying droughts, floods, and water scarcity. Average temperatures have increased 1.8°F since 1900.

**(2) Sea Level Rise**
Sea level rise threatens coastal water infrastructure with saltwater intrusion, flooding, and storm damage.

**(3) Snowpack Decline**
Western snowpack has declined 20-30% since 1950, reducing natural water storage.

**(4) Extreme Events**
Both extreme drought and extreme flooding events are increasing in frequency and intensity.

**(5) Infrastructure Vulnerability**
Most water infrastructure was designed for historical climate conditions, not future climate.

---

## SEC. 503. PURPOSES OF TITLE V

**SEC. 503.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Adapt water systems to climate change through resilient infrastructure and integrated planning.

**(2)** Incorporate climate projections into water infrastructure design.

**(3)** Protect coastal water infrastructure from sea level rise.

**(4)** Develop climate-resilient water management strategies.

## SEC. 504. CLIMATE-RESILIENT INFRASTRUCTURE

**SEC. 504.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Climate-Resilient Infrastructure Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $15 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Incorporate climate projections into infrastructure design
(2) Upgrade existing infrastructure for climate resilience
(3) Develop climate-resilient construction standards
(4) Support nature-based climate adaptation

---

## SEC. 505. SEA LEVEL RISE ADAPTATION

**SEC. 505.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Sea Level Rise Adaptation Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Protect coastal water infrastructure
(2) Prevent saltwater intrusion
(3) Support managed retreat from high-risk areas
(4) Restore coastal wetlands

## SEC. 506. INTEGRATED WATER RESOURCES PLANNING

**SEC. 506.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established an Integrated Water Resources Planning Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Develop watershed-scale water management plans
(2) Integrate water supply, water quality, and flood management
(3) Incorporate climate change into water planning
(4) Support collaborative water governance

---

## SEC. 507. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE V

**SEC. 507.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $30,000,000,000 over 10 years.
