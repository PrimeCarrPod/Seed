# TITLE IX — ECONOMIC IMPACT STATEMENT

## SEC. 901. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 901.** This title may be cited as the "Water Infrastructure Economic Impact Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 902. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO ECONOMIC IMPACT

**SEC. 902.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Infrastructure Investment Gap**
The cost of inaction on water infrastructure is estimated at $14 billion annually.

**(2) Return on Investment**
Every dollar invested in water infrastructure returns $4.30 in economic benefits.

**(3) Job Creation**
Water infrastructure investment creates 15 jobs per $1 million invested.

---

## SEC. 903. PURPOSES OF TITLE IX

**SEC. 903.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Assess the economic impact of this Act.

**(2)** Identify the costs and benefits.

**(3)** Ensure equitable distribution of economic benefits.

---

## SEC. 904. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE IX

**SEC. 904.** In this title:

**(1) "Economic Impact"**
The effect of this Act on the economy.

**(2) "Cost-Benefit Analysis"**
A systematic process for calculating and comparing benefits and costs.

## SEC. 905. COST-BENEFIT ANALYSIS

**SEC. 905.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary shall conduct a comprehensive cost-benefit analysis of this Act.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**The analysis shall include:
(1) Costs (federal, state, private sector)
(2) Benefits (avoided losses, job creation, economic activity)
(3) Return on investment
(4) Distributional analysis

---

## SEC. 906. ECONOMIC IMPACT ON INDIVIDUALS

**SEC. 906.** (a) WORKERS.—**This Act creates 500,000 direct and indirect jobs.

**(b) CONSUMERS.—**This Act reduces water costs through efficiency.

**(c) LOW-INCOME HOUSEHOLDS.—**Low-income households receive priority for assistance.

---

## SEC. 907. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE IX

**SEC. 907.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.
