# TITLE III — MARITIME TRANSPORTATION
## Act 10: Coastal Port Infrastructure Act of 2026

### Short Title

**SEC. 501.** This title may be cited as the "Maritime Transportation Act of 2026."

### Definitions

**SEC. 502.** For purposes of this title:

**(A) "U.S. Flagged Vessel"** means a vessel documented under the laws of the United States and subject to the jurisdiction of the United States.

**(B) "Marine Highway Route"** means a designated route for domestic waterborne freight transportation under the Marine Highway Program.

**(C) "Vessel Modernization"** means the construction, conversion, or modernization of U.S. flagged vessels to improve efficiency, reduce emissions, and enhance safety.

**(D) "Navigation Safety"** means the safety of vessel movements in coastal waters, including the prevention of collisions, groundings, and other maritime accidents.

**(E) "Autonomous Vessel"** means a vessel that can operate with reduced crew or without crew, using sensors, artificial intelligence, and communication systems.

### Authorization of Appropriations

**SEC. 503.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $25,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2037, to be allocated as follows:

**(A) Vessel Modernization Program** — $10,000,000,000

**(B) Marine Highway Expansion Program** — $8,000,000,000

**(C) Navigation Safety and Technology Program** — $7,000,000,000

### Subtitle A — Vessel Modernization Program

**SEC. 510.** Establishment of Program

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — The Secretary of Transportation, acting through the Administrator of the Maritime Administration, shall establish and carry out a Vessel Modernization Program to provide loan guarantees and direct loans for the construction, conversion, and modernization of U.S. flagged vessels.

**(b) PROGRAM GOALS.** — The goals of the Vessel Modernization Program are:

> (1) Reverse the decline of the U.S. flagged merchant fleet;

> (2) Increase the U.S. flagged fleet from fewer than 175 vessels to 250 vessels by 2037;

> (3) Support the domestic shipbuilding industrial base;

> (4) Reduce the environmental impact of maritime transportation;

> (5) Enhance national security sealift capacity.

**SEC. 511.** Eligible Vessels

**(a) CONTAINER VESSELS.** — The program provides loan guarantees for the construction of new U.S. flagged container vessels ranging from 2,500 to 8,000 TEU capacity.

**(b) TANKER AND BULK CARRIERS.** — The program provides loan guarantees for the modernization of U.S. flagged tanker and bulk carrier vessels.

**(c) MILITARY SEALIFT VESSELS.** — The program provides loan guarantees for the construction of dual-use vessels that serve commercial markets during peacetime and are available for military sealift during contingencies.

**(d) VESSEL RECYCLING.** — The program provides funding for the environmentally sound recycling of obsolete U.S. flagged vessels and their replacement with modern vessels.

**SEC. 512.** Loan Terms

**(a) LOAN GUARANTEES.** — The program provides federal loan guarantees covering up to 80 percent of vessel construction or modernization costs.

**(b) DIRECT LOANS.** — The program provides direct loans at interest rates at or below the applicable federal rate.

**(c) REPAYMENT.** — Loan repayment terms of up to 25 years, with deferment of principal payments for up to 5 years.

**SEC. 513.** Domestic Shipbuilding Requirement

**(a) CONSTRUCTION IN U.S. SHIPYARDS.** — All vessels constructed with federal loan guarantees shall be constructed in U.S. shipyards, supporting the domestic shipbuilding industrial base.

**(b) BUY AMERICA.** — All materials and equipment used in vessel construction shall be produced in the United States, consistent with the Build America, Buy America Act of 2021.

**SEC. 514.** Environmental Standards

**(a) EMISSION REDUCTION.** — All vessels constructed or modernized with federal loan guarantees shall meet the most stringent applicable emission standards, including:

> (1) Tier 4 emission standards for diesel engines;

> (2) Zero-emission capability for vessels operating in port waters;

> (3) Ballast water treatment systems that comply with International Maritime Organization standards.

**(b) ALTERNATIVE FUELS.** — The program encourages the use of alternative fuels, including methanol, ammonia, and hydrogen, to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

**SEC. 515.** Coordination with Department of Defense

**(a) SEALIFT READINESS.** — The Secretary of Transportation shall coordinate with the Department of Defense to ensure that vessels constructed with federal loan guarantees include military utility features, including:

> (1) Roll-on/roll-off capability for military vehicles;

> (2) Container handling equipment for military containerized cargo;

> (3) Communications systems compatible with military networks.

**(b) READY RESERVE FORCE.** — The program supports the maintenance of the Maritime Administration's Ready Reserve Force, which provides sealift capacity during military contingencies.

### Subtitle B — Marine Highway Expansion Program

**SEC. 520.** Establishment of Program

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — The Secretary of Transportation, acting through the Administrator of the Maritime Administration, shall establish and carry out a Marine Highway Expansion Program to expand the nation's network of designated Marine Highway Routes.

**(b) PROGRAM GOALS.** — The goals of the Marine Highway Expansion Program are:

> (1) Increase domestic waterborne freight tonnage by 50 percent by 2037;

> (2) Reduce highway congestion by shifting freight from truck to water;

> (3) Reduce transportation costs for businesses and consumers;

> (4) Decrease greenhouse gas emissions and criteria pollutant emissions from freight transportation.

**SEC. 521.** Route Development

**(a) DESIGNATED ROUTES.** — The program supports the establishment of new Marine Highway services on designated routes, including:

> (1) Atlantic Coast Route (M-95);

> (2) Gulf Coast Route (M-10);

> (3) Pacific Coast Route (M-5);

> (4) Mississippi River Route (M-55);

> (5) Great Lakes Route (M-90).

**(b) OPERATIONAL SUBSIDIES.** — The program provides operational subsidies during the initial 3–5 years of service to enable new routes to achieve financial sustainability.

**SEC. 522.** Vessel Construction for Marine Highways

**(a) PURPOSE-BUILT VESSELS.** — The program provides funding for the construction of purpose-built vessels for Marine Highway services, including:

> (1) Articulated tug-barge combinations;

> (2) Roll-on/roll-off vessels;

> (3) Container-on-barge systems.

**(b) ENVIRONMENTAL STANDARDS.** — Vessels constructed for Marine Highway services shall meet the most stringent applicable emission standards.

**SEC. 523.** Terminal Infrastructure

**(a) LOADING AND UNLOADING EQUIPMENT.** — The program provides funding for the development of terminal facilities for Marine Highway services, including loading and unloading equipment.

**(b) INTERMODAL CONNECTIONS.** — The program provides funding for intermodal connections that enable the transfer of cargo between waterborne and land-based transportation.

### Subtitle C — Navigation Safety and Technology Program

**SEC. 530.** Establishment of Program

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — The Secretary of Transportation, acting through the Commandant of the Coast Guard, shall establish and carry out a Navigation Safety and Technology Program to modernize the nation's marine navigation safety infrastructure.

**(b) PROGRAM GOALS.** — The goals of the Navigation Safety and Technology Program are:

> (1) Modernize the Coast Guard's Vessel Traffic Service system;

> (2) Accelerate the production of electronic navigation charts;

> (3) Develop and deploy autonomous vessel navigation technology;

> (4) Expand the network of weather and oceanographic monitoring stations;

> (5) Improve the safety and efficiency of maritime transportation.

**SEC. 531.** Vessel Traffic Service Modernization

**(a) UPGRADE.** — The program provides $2,500,000,000 to upgrade the Coast Guard's Vessel Traffic Service (VTS) system at the nation's busiest ports, incorporating:

> (1) Advanced radar systems;

> (2) Automatic identification system (AIS) tracking;

> (3) Decision support tools;

> (4) Artificial intelligence systems for collision prediction.

**(b) COVERAGE.** — The upgraded VTS system shall provide coverage of all major port approaches and harbor areas.

**SEC. 532.** Electronic Navigation Chart Production

**(a) ACCELERATION.** — The program provides $1,500,000,000 to accelerate the production and maintenance of electronic navigation charts (ENCs) for all U.S. coastal waters.

**(b) REAL-TIME UPDATES.** — The program develops real-time chart update systems that incorporate hydrographic survey data and channel condition information as it becomes available.

**SEC. 533.** Autonomous Vessel Navigation Technology

**(a) RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT.** — The program provides $2,000,000,000 for the research and development of autonomous and semi-autonomous vessel navigation technology, including:

> (1) Sensor systems;

> (2) Artificial intelligence algorithms;

> (3) Communication systems;

> (4) Regulatory frameworks.

**(b) TEST BEDS.** — The program establishes test beds for autonomous vessel operations in designated areas.

**SEC. 534.** Weather and Oceanographic Monitoring

**(a) EXPANSION.** — The program provides $1,000,000,000 to expand the network of weather and oceanographic monitoring stations in coastal waters.

**(b) DATA INTEGRATION.** — Monitoring data shall be integrated into the national port data integration platform and made available to all maritime stakeholders.

### Subtitle D — General Provisions

**SEC. 540.** Buy America Requirements

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — All iron, steel, manufactured products, and construction materials used in projects funded under this title shall be produced in the United States.

**(b) WAIVERS.** — The Secretary of Transportation may grant waivers only when domestic products are unavailable, cost more than 25 percent above foreign alternatives, or application of the requirements would be inconsistent with the public interest.

**SEC. 541.** Labor Standards

**(a) PREVAILING WAGE.** — All projects funded under this title shall comply with the prevailing wage requirements of the Davis-Bacon Act.

**(b) PROJECT LABOR AGREEMENTS.** — All projects exceeding $25,000,000 shall be performed under project labor agreements.

**(c) APPRENTICESHIP.** — All projects exceeding $25,000,000 shall include apprenticeship provisions requiring a minimum of 15 percent of workforce hours to be performed by registered apprentices.

**SEC. 542.** Environmental Justice

**(a) MINIMUM FUNDING.** — A minimum of 40 percent of all funding under this title shall benefit environmental justice communities.

**(b) COMMUNITY BENEFIT AGREEMENTS.** — Projects receiving more than $10,000,000 shall negotiate and execute Community Benefit Agreements.

**SEC. 543.** Climate Resilience

**(a) DESIGN STANDARDS.** — All infrastructure funded under this title shall be designed to withstand projected climate conditions through 2100.

**(b) NATURE-BASED SOLUTIONS.** — Projects shall incorporate nature-based solutions where feasible.

**SEC. 544.** Cybersecurity

**(a) MINIMUM STANDARDS.** — All digital systems funded under this title shall comply with NIST cybersecurity standards.

**(b) ASSessments.** — Each project that includes digital systems shall include a cybersecurity assessment.

**SEC. 545.** Reporting and Accountability
