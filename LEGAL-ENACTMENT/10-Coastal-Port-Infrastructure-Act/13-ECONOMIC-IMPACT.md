### Short Title

**SEC. 1301.** This section may be cited as the "Economic Impact Assessment of the Coastal Port Infrastructure Act of 2026."

### Purpose

**SEC. 1302.** The purposes of this section are:

> (1) To provide a comprehensive assessment of the economic impacts of the Coastal Port Infrastructure Act of 2026;

> (2) To establish benchmarks for measuring the economic performance of programs authorized under this Act;

> (3) To identify the direct, indirect, and induced economic effects of investments authorized under this Act;

> (4) To assess the distribution of economic benefits across regions, industries, and demographic groups;

> (5) To provide a basis for ongoing program evaluation and adjustment.

### Definitions

**SEC. 1303.** For purposes of this section:

**(A) "Direct Economic Impact"** means the immediate economic effects of Federal spending under this Act, including construction expenditures, materials procurement, and labor income.

**(B) "Indirect Economic Impact"** means the secondary economic effects of spending under this Act, including supply chain multiplier effects.

**(C) "Induced Economic Impact"** means the tertiary economic effects of spending under this Act, including consumer spending multiplier effects.

**(D) "Fiscal Multiplier"** means the ratio of total economic output generated to the initial Federal investment.

**(E) "Benefit-Cost Ratio"** means the ratio of total quantified benefits to total quantified costs, expressed in present value terms.

**(F) "Return on Investment"** means the net economic benefit generated per dollar of Federal investment.

**(G) "Job-Years"** means one year of employment for one worker, used to measure employment impacts.

**(H) "GDP Contribution"** means the incremental contribution to gross domestic product attributable to spending under this Act.

**(I) "Counterfactual Baseline"** means the economic conditions that would exist in the absence of this Act, used as the basis for comparison.
