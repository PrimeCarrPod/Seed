# TITLE IV — COASTAL RESILIENCE
## Act 10: Coastal Port Infrastructure Act of 2026

### Short Title

**SEC. 601.** This title may be cited as the "Coastal Resilience Act of 2026."

### Definitions

**SEC. 602.** For purposes of this title:

**(A) "Coastal Resilience"** means the ability of port infrastructure to anticipate, absorb, accommodate, and recover from the effects of climate change and other hazards.

**(B) "Natural Infrastructure"** means naturally occurring features, including wetlands, dunes, oyster reefs, mangroves, and seagrass beds, that provide storm surge attenuation, erosion control, and habitat benefits.

**(C) "Structural Protection"** means hardened structures, including seawalls, flood barriers, and levees, that protect port facilities from storm surge and wave action.

**(D) "Facility Elevation"** means the raising of critical port infrastructure above projected flood levels.

**(E) "Managed Retreat"** means the orderly relocation of port facilities from high-risk areas to more resilient locations.

### Authorization of Appropriations

**SEC. 603.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $32,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2037, to be allocated as follows:

**(A) Port Resilience and Adaptation Program** — $18,000,000,000

**(B) Coastal Restoration and Natural Infrastructure Program** — $8,000,000,000

**(C) Emergency Preparedness and Response Program** — $6,000,000,000

### Subtitle A — Port Resilience and Adaptation Program

**SEC. 610.** Establishment of Program

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — The Secretary of Transportation, acting through the Administrator of the Maritime Administration, shall establish and carry out a Port Resilience and Adaptation Program to fund structural and non-structural measures to protect port infrastructure from climate threats.

**(b) PROGRAM GOALS.** — The goals of the Port Resilience and Adaptation Program are:

> (1) Protect port infrastructure from sea-level rise, storm surge, flooding, and coastal erosion;

> (2) Ensure that critical port facilities remain operational during and after a 500-year storm event;

> (3) Incorporate climate projections through 2100 into all port infrastructure design;

> (4) Develop and implement managed retreat strategies for facilities in high-risk areas.

**SEC. 611.** Structural Protection Measures

**(a) SEAWALLS AND FLOOD BARRIERS.** — The program provides $10,000,000,000 for the construction of seawalls, flood barriers, levees, and other hardened structures that protect port facilities from storm surge and wave action.

**(b) SPECIFIC PROJECTS.** — Specific projects include:

> (1) Construction of storm surge barriers at the Port of Houston, the Port of New Orleans, and the Port of New York and New Jersey;

> (2) Seawall construction at the Port of Miami, the Port of Charleston, and the Port of Savannah;

> (3) Levee improvements at the Port of New Orleans and the Port of Houston.

**SEC. 612.** Facility Elevation and Hardening

**(a) ELEVATION.** — The program provides $5,000,000,000 for the elevation of critical port infrastructure above projected flood levels, including:

> (1) Electrical substations;

> (2) Control centers;

> (3) Cargo handling equipment;

> (4) Administrative facilities.

**(b) HARDENING.** — The program provides funding for the hardening of buildings and equipment to withstand extreme weather.

**SEC. 613.** Natural Infrastructure Integration

**(a) INTEGRATION.** — The program provides $3,000,000,000 for the integration of natural features into port resilience strategies, including:

> (1) Wetland restoration that provides storm surge attenuation;

> (2) Living shorelines that provide erosion control and storm protection;

> (3) Dune restoration that provides natural storm surge protection.

**(b) REQUIREMENT.** — A minimum of 20 percent of resilience funding shall be directed to natural infrastructure solutions.

### Subtitle B — Coastal Restoration and Natural Infrastructure Program

**SEC. 620.** Establishment of Program

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — The Secretary of Transportation, in coordination with the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, shall establish and carry out a Coastal Restoration and Natural Infrastructure Program to invest in the restoration and creation of natural coastal features.

**(b) PROGRAM GOALS.** — The goals of the Coastal Restoration and Natural Infrastructure Program are:

> (1) Restore 50,000 acres of coastal wetlands by 2037;

> (2) Construct 100 miles of living shorelines by 2037;

> (3) Restore 10,000 acres of coastal dunes by 2037;

> (4) Provide storm surge attenuation, erosion control, and habitat benefits.

**SEC. 621.** Wetland Restoration

**(a) RESTORATION.** — The program provides $3,000,000,000 for the restoration of coastal wetlands that provide natural storm surge protection for port facilities.

**(b) DESIGN STANDARDS.** — Wetland restoration projects shall be designed to:

> (1) Maximize storm surge attenuation;

> (2) Provide habitat for fish and wildlife;

> (3) Improve water quality;

> (4) Sequester carbon dioxide.

**SEC. 622.** Living Shorelines

**(a) CONSTRUCTION.** — The program provides $2,500,000,000 for the construction of living shorelines that combine natural features with limited hardened structures.

**(b) DESIGN STANDARDS.** — Living shoreline projects shall be designed to:

> (1) Provide erosion control and storm protection;

> (2) Create habitat for marine species;

> (3) Improve water quality;

> (4) Adapt naturally to changing conditions.

**SEC. 623.** Dune Restoration

**(a) RESTORATION.** — The program provides $2,500,000,000 for the restoration of coastal dunes that provide natural storm surge protection.

**(b) DESIGN STANDARDS.** — Dune restoration projects shall be designed to:

> (1) Provide storm surge protection;

> (2) Stabilize coastal sediments;

> (3) Provide habitat for coastal species;

> (4) Provide recreational opportunities.

### Subtitle C — Emergency Preparedness and Response Program

**SEC. 630.** Establishment of Program

**(a) IN GENERAL.** — The Secretary of Transportation, in coordination with the Federal Emergency Management Agency, shall establish and carry out an Emergency Preparedness and Response Program to strengthen the nation's capacity to prepare for, respond to, and recover from port-related disasters.

**(b) PROGRAM GOALS.** — The goals of the Emergency Preparedness and Response Program are:

> (1) Establish emergency operations centers at major port facilities;

> (2) Pre-position emergency equipment and materials at strategic locations;

> (3) Conduct annual emergency preparedness exercises;

> (4) Develop comprehensive port recovery plans.

**SEC. 631.** Emergency Operations Centers

**(a) ESTABLISHMENT.** — The program provides $2,000,000,000 for the construction and equipping of emergency operations centers at major port facilities.

**(b) REQUIREMENTS.** — Emergency operations centers shall include:

> (1) Redundant communications systems;

> (2) Backup power systems;

> (3) Real-time monitoring capabilities;

> (4) Coordination facilities for emergency response.

**SEC. 632.** Pre-Positioned Equipment and Materials

**(a) PRE-POSITIONING.** — The program provides $2,000,000,000 for the pre-positioning of emergency equipment and materials, including:

> (1) Portable pumps;

> (2) Generators;

> (3) Flood barriers;

> (4) Communications equipment.

**SEC. 633.** Workforce Training and Exercises

**(a) TRAINING.** — The program provides $1,000,000,000 for training programs that prepare port workers, emergency responders, and government officials for disaster response.

**(b) EXERCISES.** — The program requires annual exercises at all major ports and coordinates with the Federal Emergency Management Agency's national exercise program.

**SEC. 634.** Post-Disaster Recovery Planning

**(a) PLANNING.** — The program provides $1,000,000,000 for the development and maintenance of comprehensive port recovery plans.

**(b) REQUIREMENTS.** — Port recovery plans shall include:

> (1) Priorities for post-disaster reconstruction;

> (2) Procedures for rapid mobilization of reconstruction resources;

> (3) Pre-negotiated contracts for emergency construction services.

**(a) MINIMUM FUNDING.** — A minimum of 40 percent of all funding under this title shall benefit environmental justice communities.

**(a) DESIGN STANDARDS.** — All infrastructure funded under this title shall be designed to withstand projected climate conditions through 2100.

**(b) NATURE-BASED SOLUTIONS.** — Projects shall incorporate nature-based solutions where feasible.

**SEC. 644.** Cybersecurity

**(a) MINIMUM STANDARDS.** — All digital systems funded under this title shall comply with NIST cybersecurity standards.

**(b) ASSESSMENTS.** — Each project that includes digital systems shall include a cybersecurity assessment.

**(d) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.** — The Secretary shall establish a public-facing dashboard.

### Legislative Findings and Purpose

**SEC. 646.** The Congress finds that:

**(a)** Over 60 percent of U.S. port infrastructure lies within areas subject to recurrent flooding, storm surge, and sea-level rise.

**(b)** The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration projects that sea levels along U.S. coastlines will rise by 10–12 inches by 2050.

**(c)** At current projections, 23 major U.S. ports will experience recurrent tidal flooding by 2050.

**(d)** Hurricane Katrina (2005) caused $2.5 billion in port infrastructure damage; Hurricane Sandy (2012) caused $1.8 billion; Hurricane Harvey (2017) caused an estimated $3.2 billion in economic losses.

**(e)** Approximately 30 percent of U.S. port facilities are located in areas experiencing significant coastal erosion.

**(f)** The interaction of multiple climate hazards creates compound risks that exceed the sum of individual hazards.

**SEC. 647.** The purpose of this subtitle is to:

**(a)** Protect port infrastructure from the impacts of climate change;

**(b)** Restore and create natural coastal features that provide storm surge attenuation and erosion control;

**(c)** Strengthen the nation's capacity to prepare for, respond to, and recover from port-related disasters;

**(d)** Ensure that the benefits of coastal resilience are shared equitably.
