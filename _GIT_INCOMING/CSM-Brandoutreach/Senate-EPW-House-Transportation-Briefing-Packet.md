## Executive Summary

Carrington Storm Motors respectfully submits this briefing regarding two critical pieces of legislation that directly impact the nation's civil infrastructure resilience and public works capacity. Our AEGIS-C Composite Shielding System and Dielectric Citadel Protocol provide the physical mitigation framework that these legislative initiatives were designed to support.

Together, Acts 01 and 05 authorize over $110 billion in federal spending over ten years and establish the regulatory framework within which our technology operates. Our research demonstrates that Aegis-C shielding delivers 72% modeled PML reduction and enables the structural longevity standards these acts mandate — outcomes that are technically complementary but fiscally uncoordinated without explicit integration.
