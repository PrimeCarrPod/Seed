## Act 04: Clean Industrial Manufacturing Tax Credit Act (CIMTCA) of 2026

**Authorization:** Tax credit authorizations across all titles (Green Steel Credit, Low-Carbon Cement Credit, Clean Hydrogen Credit, Industrial Heat Credit, Carbon Capture Credit, Advanced Manufacturing Credit, Transition Assistance)  
**Primary Sponsor:** Ways and Means Committee / Finance Committee jurisdiction

### Key Provisions Relevant to Committee Oversight

| Section | Provision | AEGIS-C Connection |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Title I — Green Steel Credit** | Tax credit for green steel production using electric arc furnaces with renewable electricity | Aegis-C composite basalt-fiber reinforcement (1,200 MPa tensile) for EAF vessel linings and exhaust systems; EMI shielding for control systems |
| **Title II — Low-Carbon Cement Credit** | Tax credit for cement manufacturing with carbon capture and renewable energy | Aegis-C basalt-fiber systems (20+ kV/mm dielectric) for kiln enclosures and emission control systems |
| **Title III — Clean Hydrogen Credit** | Tax credit for clean hydrogen production via electrolysis or steam methane reforming with carbon capture | Aegis-C EMI shielding for hydrogen production and compression equipment control systems |
| **Title IV — Industrial Heat Credit** | Tax credit for industrial heat decarbonization via electrification, renewable energy, or carbon capture | Aegis-C protects high-temperature process control systems and power electronics from EMI degradation |
| **Title V — Carbon Capture Credit** | Tax credit for carbon capture, utilization, and storage (CCS) deployment | Aegis-C enclosure systems for CO2 compression, transport, and storage facility electronics |
| **Title VI — Advanced Manufacturing Credit** | Tax credit for advanced manufacturing equipment, robotics, and automation | Aegis-C protects robotics, automation controllers, and high-precision equipment from EMI |
| **Title VII — Transition Assistance** | Workforce transition assistance for communities impacted by industrial transition | Aegis-C composite installation as new workforce specialization in clean industrial transition |
| **Title VIII — Buy America Provisions** | Domestic sourcing requirements for materials and components procured under the Act | Aegis-C: basalt-fiber is domestic (volcanic rock, abundant U.S. feedstock); MXene Ti3C2Tx development under DCMIA |
