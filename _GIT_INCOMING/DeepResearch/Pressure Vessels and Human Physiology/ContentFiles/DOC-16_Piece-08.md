#### 16.8 Clinical Translation Pathway

**16.8.1 Regulatory Pathway (FDA/EMA)**

**Device Classification:**
- **TLV Ventilator:** Class III (life-supporting, implant-adjacent)
- **PFC Fluid:** Combination product (device + drug)

**FDA Pathway (US):**
1. **Pre-IND Meeting** (Year 0) — FDA feedback on preclinical plan
2. **GLP Preclinical Studies** (Years 1–2):
   - Biocompatibility (ISO 10993)
   - Chronic toxicity (6-month primate TLV)
   - Ventilator reliability (10,000 hr MTBF)
   - MR safety (¹⁹F MRI compatibility)
3. **IND Submission** (Year 2) — FDA review (30 days)
4. **Phase I (Safety, n=10–20)** (Years 3–4):
   - Healthy volunteers (short TLV < 1 hr)
   - ARDS patients (compassionate use)
5. **Phase II (Efficacy, n=50–100)** (Years 4–5):
   - ARDS vs. conventional ventilation
   - Primary: Ventilator-free days at 28 days
6. **Phase III (Pivotal, n=300–500)** (Years 6–8):
   - Multi-center RCT vs. standard care
   - Primary: 28-day mortality
7. **BLA/PMA Submission** (Year 8) — FDA review (180 days)
8. **Approval** (Year 9)

**EMA Pathway (EU):** Similar, with **CE Mark** for ventilator (MDR 2017/745) + **CTA** for PFC.

**16.8.3 First-in-Human (FIH) Protocol Design**

**Inclusion Criteria (Compassionate Use ARDS):**
- **Berlin Definition:** Severe ARDS (P/F < 100)
- **Failed Conventional:** 24 hr on optimal conventional vent
- **Age:** 18–65
- **Contraindications:** None (active bleed, severe TBI, etc.)

**Protocol:**
1. **Pre-TLV:** Sedation, paralysis, arterial/central lines
2. **Induction:** Fill lungs with warm PFC (37°C) via bronchoscope
3. **Transition:** Switch from gas to liquid ventilator (5 min)
4. **TLV Period:** 4–24 hr (per protocol)
5. **Weaning:** Gradual PFC removal → gas ventilation
6. **Follow-up:** 28-day mortality, lung function, neurocognitive

**Safety Stopping Rules:**
- P_aCO₂ > 60 mmHg for > 30 min despite max RR
- P_aO₂ < 60 mmHg on 100% O₂
- Hemodynamic instability (MAP < 60 on pressors)
- New intracranial hemorrhage (CT)

**16.8.4 Manufacturing & Supply Chain**

**Ventilator (Class III Medical Device):**
- **Assembly:** ISO 13485 facility, Class 10,000 cleanroom
- **Components:** Single-source critical (motor, sensors, membranes)
- **Sterilization:** Gamma irradiation (25 kGy) for disposables
- **QC:** 100% functional test (24-hr burn-in)

**PFC Fluid (Combination Product):**
- **Synthesis:** GMP CoF₃ fluorination (validated process)
- **Purification:** Distillation + activated carbon + 0.1 µm filtration
- **QC:** GC-MS (purity > 99.99%), Karl Fischer (H₂O < 10 ppm), GC-FID (residuals)
- **Filling:** ISO 5 isolator, N₂ headspace, gamma sterilization
- **Stability:** 24 months at 2–8°C

**Supply Chain Risk Mitigation:**
- **Dual-source** critical components (pumps, sensors, membranes)
- **Strategic stockpile:** 6 months PFC, 12 months disposables
- **Cold chain:** 2–8°C monitored (GDP compliant)
