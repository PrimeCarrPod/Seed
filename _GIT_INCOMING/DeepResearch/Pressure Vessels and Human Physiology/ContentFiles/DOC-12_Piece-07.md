#### 12.7 Narcosis in Hyperbaric Chambers & Aerospace

**12.7.1 Hyperbaric Medical Treatment (HBOT) — Narcosis at Pressure**

**Typical HBOT Protocol:**
- **Pressure:** 2.0–2.8 ATA (10–18 msw equivalent)
- **Gas:** 100% O₂ (via mask/hood)
- **Chamber Air:** 21% O₂ / 79% N₂ at treatment pressure

**Nitrogen Partial Pressure in Chamber Air:**
| Treatment Pressure | $P_{N_2}$ [bar] | END [msw] | Narcosis Risk |
|--------------------|-----------------|-----------|---------------|
| **2.0 ATA** (10 msw) | 1.58 | 20 | **None** (Grade 0) |
| **2.4 ATA** (14 msw) | 1.90 | 24 | **None** |
| **2.8 ATA** (18 msw) | 2.21 | 28 | **Minimal** (Grade I threshold) |
| **3.0 ATA** (20 msw) | 2.37 | 30 | **Mild** (Grade I possible) |

**Clinical Reality:**
- **Most HBOT at 2.0–2.4 ATA:** No narcosis observed
- **At 2.8–3.0 ATA:** Rare mild euphoria reported
- **Inside O₂ Hood/Mask:** $P_{N_2} \approx 0$ → **Zero narcosis**
- **Attendants (breathing chamber air):** Monitor for Grade I symptoms

**12.7.2 Chamber Attendant Protocols**

**US Navy / UHMS Guidelines:**
- **Attendant END Limit:** 30 msw (max)
- **Rotation:** 30 min on / 30 min off (at > 2.8 ATA)
- **Monitoring:** Cognitive tests every 30 min
- **Emergency:** Rapid decompression to 1 ATA if Grade II+

**12.7.3 Aerospace — Narcosis in Hypobaric Environments?**

**Aircraft Cabin (Pressurized to 2,400 m / 8,000 ft):**
- $P_{amb} = 0.75$ bar
- $P_{N_2} = 0.78 \times 0.75 = 0.59$ bar
- **END = 7.4 msw** → **Zero narcosis risk**

**Unpressurized / Decompression Scenarios:**
- **Rapid Decompression to 10,000 m:** $P_{amb} = 0.26$ bar
- $P_{N_2} = 0.20$ bar → END = 2.5 msw
- **Zero narcosis** (hypoxia dominates)

**Space Suit (EMU, 4.3 psi = 0.3 bar, 100% O₂):**
- $P_{N_2} = 0$ → **Zero narcosis**

**High-Altitude Chamber Training (Military):**
- **Decompression to 25,000 ft (7,600 m):** Hypoxia training
- **No narcosis at any altitude** — only hypoxia/DCS

**12.7.4 Space — Narcosis in Planetary Environments?**

**Mars Surface (0.6 kPa, 95% CO₂):**
- **Habitat:** 101 kPa, Earth-like air
- **EVA Suit:** 30 kPa, 100% O₂ (EMU) or 55 kPa, 30% O₂ (Gateway)
- **No inert gas narcosis** in any current concept

**Venus Surface (92 bar, 96% CO₂):**
- **Hypothetical Habitat:** 92 bar, breathable mix
- **N₂ Narcosis:** Severe if N₂ used as diluent
- **Solution:** Helium/Neon diluent only

**Titan Surface (1.5 bar, 95% N₂ / 5% CH₄):**
- **Habitat:** 1.5 bar, O₂/He
- **EVA Suit:** 1.5 bar, O₂/He (no pressure differential!)
- **No narcosis** if He diluent
