#### 14.5 Thermophysical Properties & Transport

**14.5.1 Density — The Heaviest Common Liquids**

**Density at 25°C:**
| Liquid | Density [g/cm³] | Relative to Water |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Water** | 1.00 | 1.00× |
| **n-Octane** | 0.70 | 0.70× |
| **Perfluoro-n-hexane** | 1.68 | 1.68× |
| **Perfluoro-n-octane** | 1.76 | 1.76× |
| **Perfluorodecalin** | **1.92** | **1.92×** |
| **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | **1.97** | **1.97×** |

**PFCs are ~2× DENSER than water — critical for TLV hydrodynamics.**

**Density-Temperature Relationship:**
$$\rho(T) = \rho_0 \left[1 - \alpha (T - T_0)\right]$$
Where $\alpha \approx 0.001\text{–}0.0015 \text{ K}^{-1}$ (low thermal expansion)

**14.5.2 Viscosity — The TLV Bottleneck**

**Dynamic Viscosity at 25°C:**
| Liquid | η [mPa·s] | η [cP] | Relative to Water |
|--------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| **Water (25°C)** | 0.89 | 0.89 | 1.0× |
| **Water (37°C)** | 0.69 | 0.69 | 1.0× |
| **Blood (37°C)** | 3.5–4.5 | 3.5–4.5 | 5–6× |
| **Perfluoro-n-hexane** | 0.60 | 0.60 | 0.7× |
| **Perfluoro-n-octane** | 1.2 | 1.2 | 1.7× |
| **Perfluorodecalin** | **5.4** | **5.4** | **7.8×** |
| **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | **5.4** | **5.4** | **7.8×** |

**Viscosity at 37°C (Physiological):**
$$\eta(37°C) \approx \eta(25°C) \cdot \exp\left[\frac{E_a}{R}\left(\frac{1}{310} - \frac{1}{298}\right)\right]$$
$E_a \approx 15\text{–}25 \text{ kJ/mol}$ for PFCs
**At 37°C: Viscosity ~30% lower than at 25°C**

**Kinematic Viscosity (ν = η/ρ):**
| Liquid | ν [cSt] at 25°C | ν [cSt] at 37°C |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Water** | 1.0 | 0.7 |
| **Perfluoro-n-octane** | 0.68 | 0.5 |
| **Perfluorodecalin** | 2.8 | 2.0 |
| **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | 2.8 | 2.0 |

**Key Insight:** Kinematic viscosity of PFCs ≈ **2–3× water** — manageable for TLV!

**14.5.3 Vapor Pressure — Critical for Clearance**

**Vapor Pressure at 25°C:**
| PFC | VP [mmHg] | VP [Pa] | Boiling Point [°C] |
|-----|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| **Perfluoro-n-hexane** | 38 | 5,000 | 56 |
| **Perfluoro-n-octane** | 3.5 | 470 | 103 |
| **Perfluorodecalin** | 10–20 | 1,300–2,700 | 142 |
| **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | **3.8** | **500** | **147** |

**Vapor Pressure at 37°C (Pulmonary Clearance):**
$$P_{\text{vap}}(37°C) \approx P_{\text{vap}}(25°C) \cdot \exp\left[\frac{\Delta H_{\text{vap}}}{R}\left(\frac{1}{298} - \frac{1}{310}\right)\right]$$
$\Delta H_{\text{vap}} \approx 30\text{–}40 \text{ kJ/mol}$

**At 37°C:**
- **Perfluoro-n-octane:** ~10 mmHg
- **Perfluorodecalin:** ~30 mmHg
- **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane:** ~10 mmHg

**Clearance Implication:** Low VP → **slow pulmonary clearance** (hours–days)

**14.5.4 Surface Tension — The Lowest of All Liquids**

**Surface Tension at 25°C:**
| Liquid | γ [mN/m] | γ [dyn/cm] |
|--------|----------|------------|
| **Water** | 72 | 72 |
| **n-Octane** | 21.6 | 21.6 |
| **Perfluoro-n-hexane** | 12.0 | 12.0 |
| **Perfluoro-n-octane** | **12.5** | **12.5** |
| **Perfluorodecalin** | **18** | **18** |
| **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | **15–18** | **15–18** |
| **Perfluorooctyl bromide (PFOB)** | **7.3** | **7.3** |

**PFOB (Perfluorooctyl bromide) has the LOWEST surface tension of any known liquid!**

**Implications:**
- **Spreading coefficient on water/lung surfactant:** $S = \gamma_{\text{water}} - \gamma_{\text{PFC}} - \gamma_{\text{interface}} > 0$
- **PFOB SPREADS instantly on alveolar surfactant** → recruits collapsed alveoli
- **Low γ → low alveolar collapse pressure** → prevents atelectasis

**14.5.5 Thermal Conductivity & Heat Capacity**

**Thermal Conductivity (k):**
| Liquid | k [W/m·K] |
|--------|-----------|
| **Water** | 0.60 |
| **Perfluoro-n-hexane** | 0.055 |
| **Perfluoro-n-octane** | 0.058 |
| **Perfluorodecalin** | **0.062** |

**PFCs are THERMAL INSULATORS (k ≈ 0.06) — 10× worse than water!**

**Heat Capacity (c_p):**
| Liquid | c_p [J/g·K] |
|--------|-------------|
| **Water** | 4.18 |
| **PFCs** | **1.0–1.1** |

**PFCs have 4× LOWER heat capacity than water.**

**Implications for TLV:**
- **Heating/cooling PFC loop:** Requires 4× more flow for same heat transfer
- **Heat exchanger:** Must be larger (low k, low c_p)
- **Body cooling risk:** PFC in lungs acts as insulator — needs active heating

**14.5.6 Diffusion Coefficients — The TLV Limiter**

**Self-Diffusion (PFC in PFC):**
$$D_{\text{self}} \approx \frac{k_B T}{6\pi\eta r_{\text{mol}}}$$
$r_{\text{mol}} \approx 4\text{–}5 \text{ Å}$ → $D_{\text{self}} \approx 10^{-5} \text{ cm}^2/\text{s}$

**Gas Diffusion in PFC (Experimental):**
| Gas | $D_{\text{PFC}}$ [cm²/s] | $D_{\text{water}}$ [cm²/s] | $D_{\text{air}}$ [cm²/s] |
|-----|-------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| **O₂** | 2–3 × 10⁻⁵ | 2.1 × 10⁻⁵ | **0.21** |
| **CO₂** | 1.5–2 × 10⁻⁵ | 1.6 × 10⁻⁵ | **0.16** |

**Ratio:** $D_{\text{air}} / D_{\text{PFC}} \approx \mathbf{10^4}$ (4 orders of magnitude!)

**This is the FUNDAMENTAL LIMIT of TLV — CO₂ diffusion in PFC.**

**14.5.7 Henry's Law Constants (Gas Solubility)**

**Henry's Constant (k_H = P/C) at 37°C:**
| Gas | Perfluoro-n-octane | Perfluorodecalin | PFOB |
|-----|-------------------|------------------|------|
| **O₂** | 2.8 bar·M⁻¹ | 2.5 bar·M⁻¹ | 2.6 bar·M⁻¹ |
| **CO₂** | 0.9 bar·M⁻¹ | 0.7 bar·M⁻¹ | 0.8 bar·M⁻¹ |
| **N₂** | 5.0 bar·M⁻¹ | 4.5 bar·M⁻¹ | 4.8 bar·M⁻¹ |
| **He** | 12 bar·M⁻¹ | 10 bar·M⁻¹ | 11 bar·M⁻¹ |

**Lower k_H = Higher Solubility**
**CO₂ k_H is 3–4× LOWER than O₂ → 3–4× higher solubility!**
