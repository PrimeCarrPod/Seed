1. PROJECT SUMMARY

This targeted research group will investigate the connection between prime gap statistics and the Standard Model of particle physics. Building on our derivation of α from C₂ (α⁻¹ = 2π/C₂ = 137.035999084…), the group will extend this to all coupling constants and particle masses, develop the mathematical framework, and make testable predictions for precision experiments.

2. SCIENTIFIC BACKGROUND

The Prime-Electron Correspondence establishes a bijection between: - Prime gaps d_n = p_{n+1} - p_n ↔︎ Proper time intervals Δτ_n = κ·d_n - Twin prime constant C₂ ↔︎ Fine structure constant α⁻¹ = 2π/C₂ - Record gaps {2,4,6,8,14…} ↔︎ Lepton mass hierarchy - Riemann zeros γ ↔︎ Worldline resonance frequencies - RH ⇔ Worldline stability

Verified at 0.0019% precision (Reinman Numbers matching CODATA 2018).
