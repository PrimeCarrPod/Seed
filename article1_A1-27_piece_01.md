# Piece 01: Introduction — The N=1 Superalgebra from Prime Gaps

The N=1 superalgebra for the prime electron worldline extends the SUSY algebra of A1-25 and the supercharge construction of A1-26. The full algebra includes the supercharges $Q_\alpha$, their adjoints $\bar{Q}_{\dot{\alpha}}$, the Hamiltonian $H$, the central charge $Z=78$, and the R-symmetry generator $R$. In our 1D worldline context, the 4D super-Poincaré algebra reduces to a 1D N=1 superalgebra with central charge.

The superalgebra relations are:

$$
\{Q_\alpha, \bar{Q}_{\dot{\beta}}\} = 2 \sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot{\beta}} P_\mu + Z \delta_{\alpha\dot{\beta}}, \quad \{Q_\alpha, Q_\beta\} = \{\bar{Q}_{\dot{\alpha}}, \bar{Q}_{\dot{\beta}}\} = 0
$$

In 1D with $\tau$ as the only coordinate, $P_\mu \to H$, $\sigma^\mu \to 1$, and the spinor indices reduce to a single component. The algebra becomes:

$$
\{Q, Q^\dagger\} = 2H + Z, \quad \{Q, Q\} = \{Q^\dagger, Q^\dagger\} = 0
$$

with $Z = 78$ from the index theorem (A1-24). This matches the A1-25 algebra, but now we embed it in the full superconformal structure with generators $S, \bar{S}, K, D, R$.

The supercharges from A1-26 are $Q = \sum_n \psi_n \sqrt{d_n d_{n+1}}$, $Q^\dagger = \sum_n \psi_n^\dagger \sqrt{d_n d_{n+1}}$. These satisfy the algebra exactly on the 256-dim Hilbert space. The central charge $Z=78$ is the topological invariant from the index theorem, protected by the 78 record gaps of PrimeBookOne.

The R-symmetry $U(1)_R$ is generated by $R = F - 128$ (shifted fermion number from A1-26 Piece 03). It acts on the supercharges as $[R, Q] = Q$, $[R, Q^\dagger] = -Q^\dagger$, and commutes with $H$ and $Z$. The R-charge of the BPS states is zero, consistent with their annihilation by $Q$.

This piece sets up the full superconformal algebra that will be developed in subsequent pieces, connecting to the wall crossing formula (A1-29), BPS spectrum (A1-28), and holographic dual (A1-38).
