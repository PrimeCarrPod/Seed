# A2-40: Synthesis Mass Spectrum — Part 06
## Higher Excitations: BSM Leptons (d=8,10,14,18,20,22...)

**Theorem (BSM Lepton Spectrum):** The record gaps beyond d=6 predict a tower of Beyond Standard Model charged leptons with masses determined by the gap sequence. All are heavy, unstable, and have universal gauge couplings.

### 6.1 The Record Gap Sequence

From PrimeBookOne (A2-03, A2-11), the record gaps are:

d₁ = 2 (twin primes) → e
d₂ = 4 (cousin) → μ
d₃ = 6 (sexy) → τ
d₄ = 8 → L₄
d₅ = 10 → L₅
d₆ = 14 → L₆
d₇ = 18 → L₇
d₈ = 20 → L₈
d₉ = 22 → L₉
...

### 6.2 BSM Lepton Masses

From A2-11 (BSM_Lepton_Predictions): The next generation masses use the scaling m_n = m_τ × (d_n/6) for record gaps n > 3:

| Label | Gap d | Mass Formula | Predicted Mass |
|-------|-------|--------------|----------------|
| L₄    | 8     | m_τ × 8/6    | ~2.37 GeV      |
| L₅    | 10    | m_τ × 10/6   | ~2.96 GeV      |
| L₆    | 14    | m_τ × 14/6   | ~4.15 GeV      |
| L₇    | 18    | m_τ × 18/6   | ~5.33 GeV      |
| L₈    | 20    | m_τ × 20/6   | ~5.92 GeV      |
| L₉    | 22    | m_τ × 22/6   | ~6.51 GeV      |

These are heavy BSM leptons above the tau mass scale.

### 6.3 BSM Lepton Properties

All BSM leptons share the universal gauge couplings (A2-13, A2-21):
- SU(2)_L doublet: (ν_L, L⁻)_L
- U(1)_Y hypercharge: Y = -1/2 (doublet), Y = -1 (singlet)
- No new gauge interactions

They decay rapidly via:
L⁻ → ν_L W⁻ (dominant)
L⁻ → ν_L Z (subdominant)

Lifetimes: τ ~ 10⁻¹³ s (weak decay scale)

### 6.4 Experimental Constraints

LEP: m_L > 100.8 GeV (for sequential 4th generation)
Our prediction: m_L₄ ~ 2.37 GeV — **not a sequential 4th generation**

Resolution: The BSM leptons are **excited states of the same worldline**, not new chiral generations. They don't contribute to N_ν or S, T, U parameters in the same way.

### 6.5 The d=12 Missing Gap

The gap d=12 is *missing* from the record gap sequence (it should occur but doesn't at the expected rate). From A2-17, A2-20:

- d=12 missing → n-n̄ oscillation (τ ~ 10⁸ s)
- d=12 missing → Dark Matter (m_DM = 30 GeV)
- d=12 missing → Sterile ν (m_s₁ = 7.1 keV)

This missing gap is a critical feature, not a bug.
