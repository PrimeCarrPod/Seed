## Muon Mass from First Record Gap Excitation (d=4, 6, 8)

The muon mass m_μ = 105.66 MeV emerges from the first record gap excitation above the twin prime scale.

**Excitation spectrum.** The gap energy spectrum E(d) = 0.511 MeV / d has discrete levels at each gap value. The "particle" masses correspond to resonant excitations where the gap structure enhances the density of states.

**Muon as first excitation.** The twin prime gap d=2 gives the UV boundary. The first non-twin record gaps are d=4, 6, 8, 10, 14... These correspond to excitations. The muon mass ratio m_μ/mₑ = 206.77 suggests an effective gap:
d_μ ≈ 0.511/105.66 = 1/206.77 ≈ 0.0048

This is not a single gap. The muon mass is a collective effect of the gap distribution near the first record gaps.

**Record gap statistics.** The record gaps grow roughly as d_i^record ~ log² p_i (Cramér). The first record gaps:
d=2 (twin), 4, 6, 8, 10, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 40...

**Muon from d=4,6,8 cluster.** The gaps 4, 6, 8 appear early. Their combined effect:
1/4 + 1/6 + 1/8 = 0.25 + 0.167 + 0.125 = 0.542
