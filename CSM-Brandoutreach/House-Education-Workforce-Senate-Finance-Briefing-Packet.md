## Executive Summary

Carrington Storm Motors respectfully submits this briefing regarding legislation that directly impacts the nation's workforce development infrastructure, clean energy career pathways, and the skilled labor requirements for infrastructure hardening investments. Our AEGIS-C Composite Shielding System and Dielectric Citadel Protocol provide the physical mitigation framework that workforce development and industrial strategy initiatives were designed to support.

Act 11 (WDGJA) establishes the workforce development framework that enables the skilled labor force required for infrastructure hardening. Our research demonstrates that Aegis-C deployment creates new skilled-lane career pathways and that the workforce development these acts authorize are technically complementary but programmatically uncoordinated without explicit integration of mitigation career pathways.
