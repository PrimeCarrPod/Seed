## Act 06: Wildfire Prevention Grid Resilience Act (WPGRA) of 2026

**Authorization:** $150 billion over ten fiscal years (FY2027–FY2036)  
**Primary Sponsor:** Energy Committee jurisdiction

### Key Provisions Relevant to Committee Oversight

| Section | Provision | AEGIS-C Connection |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Title I — Undergrounding** ($60B) | Federal cost-share for 10,000 miles of high-fire-threat line undergrounding | Aegis-C provides dielectric isolation that complements undergrounding by protecting aboveground infrastructure components (transformers, switches, connectors) from GIC exposure |
| **Title II — DCD** ($10B) | Mandatory deployment of Downed Conductor Detection on high-fire-threat circuits | Aegis-C shielding reduces GIC risk at the enclosure level, working in parallel with DCD for comprehensive grid protection |
| **Title III — Weather/Sensor Network** ($10B) | Grants for 10,000 weather stations and HD camera networks | Aegis-C provides EMI shielding for sensor enclosures, ensuring measurement integrity in high-EMF environments |
| **Title IV — PSPS Reform** ($5B) | National standards for Public Safety Power Shutoff, medically vulnerable customer protections, backup power grants | Aegis-C enables continuous operation, reducing reliance on PSPS events for critical infrastructure |
| **Title V — Microgrid/Distributed Energy** ($40B) | Community microgrid development, battery storage, solar-plus-storage for critical facilities | Aegis-C enclosures protect microgrid power electronics and battery storage from GIC and EMI degradation |
| **Title VI — Vegetation Management** ($20B) | Enhanced clearance standards, risk mapping, workforce training, prescribed fire | Aegis-C composite basalt-fiber materials (1,200 MPa tensile) can supplement vegetation management buffer zones |
| **Title VII — Transmission Hardening** ($10B) | Structural upgrades, composite poles, covered conductor, fire-resistant materials | Aegis-C ZrB2-SiC ceramic matrix with MXene EMI layer provides the electromagnetic hardening component within a comprehensive grid resilience strategy |
| **Title VIII — Data Center Protection** ($5B) | Grid integration standards, behind-meter storage, demand response | Aegis-C provides 148–165 dB shielding effectiveness for data center enclosures, complementing PUE standards |
