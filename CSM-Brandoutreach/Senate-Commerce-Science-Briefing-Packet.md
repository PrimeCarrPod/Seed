## Executive Summary

Carrington Storm Motors respectfully submits this briefing regarding two critical pieces of legislation that directly impact the nation's advanced technology supply chain, semiconductor onshoring, and electromagnetic infrastructure protection capabilities. Our AEGIS-C Composite Shielding System and Dielectric Citadel Protocol provide the physical mitigation framework that these legislative initiatives were designed to support.

Our research demonstrates that Aegis-C shielding enables the electromagnetic reliability required for advanced semiconductor manufacturing, advanced nuclear facility operation, and the critical infrastructure these acts authorize.

## Act 03: Advanced Nuclear Energy Deployment Act (ANEDA) of 2026

**Authorization:** Varies by title (comprehensive authorizations across all 10 titles, including SMR deployment, advanced reactors, fusion energy)  
**Primary Sponsor:** Science & Technology Committee jurisdiction; Energy & Natural Resources Committee oversight

### Key Provisions Relevant to Committee Oversight

| Section | Provision | AEGIS-C Connection |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Title I — SMR Deployment** | Small modular reactor licensing and deployment framework | Aegis-C provides electromagnetic isolation for SMR control rooms, instrumentation, and power electronics from ambient EMI and GIC exposure |
| **Title II — Advanced Reactors** | Advanced reactor licensing, construction, and operation standards | Aegis-C ZrB2-SiC ceramic matrix withstands >2,000°C while maintaining dielectric integrity for advanced reactor enclosures |
| **Title III — Fusion Energy** | Fusion research funding, ITER partnership, DEMO reactor planning | Aegis-C shielding for fusion experiment electromagnetic environments and diagnostic equipment enclosures |
| **Title IV — HALEU Fuel Supply** | High-Assured Low-Enriched Uranium fuel supply chain security | Aegis-C EMI shielding for HALEU fuel handling, processing, and storage facility electronics |
| **Title V — NRC Reform** | NRC licensing process modernization, regulatory timelines, staffing | Aegis-C provides the measurable EMI data that updated NRC regulatory frameworks can reference |
| **Title VI — Deployment Incentives** | Financial incentives for advanced reactor and SMR deployment | Aegis-C enables qualified advanced nuclear property by protecting against GIC/EMI degradation (supports tax credit eligibility) |
| **Title VII — Workforce Development** | Nuclear workforce pipeline, training programs, university partnerships | Aegis-C composite installation as new nuclear specialization track within existing apprenticeship frameworks |
| **Title VIII — Environmental Justice** | Nuclear facility siting, community engagement, radiation monitoring | Aegis-Mesh IoT (LoRa 915 MHz, 256-node self-healing) for radiation and environmental monitoring networks |
| **Title IX — Fusion Materials** | Advanced materials research for fusion reactors, tritium breeding materials | Aegis-C basalt-fiber reinforcement (1,200 MPa tensile) for fusion vessel structural components |
| **Title X — Fusion Power Plant** | Fusion pilot plant licensing framework, grid integration standards | Aegis-C provides electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) for fusion plant grid connection and control systems |

### Committee-Oversight-Relevant Metrics

- **SMR pipeline:** Multiple units projected online by early 2030s (Act 03, Title I)
- **Fusion timeline:** DEMO reactor pathway dependent on materials and magnet development timeline
- **HALEU supply:** Secure fuel cycle critical for both civilian and defense nuclear applications
- **NRC reform:** Modernization aimed at reducing licensing timelines from ~6 years to ~3 years
- **Workforce:** Nuclear workforce pipeline projections under current and projected retirement rates
- **Emissions:** Advanced nuclear as zero-carbon generation source supporting national climate targets
- **GIC protection:** Critical for reactor fleet resilience in Carrington-event scenarios
