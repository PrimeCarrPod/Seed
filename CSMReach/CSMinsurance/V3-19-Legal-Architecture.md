## Legal Architecture Summary

### Key Alignment Principles

1. **WDGJA (Act 11) is the workforce baseline** — All other acts' workforce provisions are aligned to the framework established in the Workforce Green Jobs Act. Act 11 provides the comprehensive $50B/10yr structure that all other acts reference for workforce development components.

2. **CIMTCA (Act 04) is the tax credit baseline** — All other acts' tax credit provisions are aligned to the framework established in the Clean Industrial Manufacturing Tax Credit Act. Act 04 provides the comprehensive tax credit structure that other acts reference or align with.

3. **DCMIA (Act 02) is the critical minerals baseline** — All other acts' critical minerals provisions are aligned to the framework established in the Domestic Critical Minerals Independence Act. Act 02 provides the comprehensive critical minerals structure that other acts reference or align with.

4. **Insurance product tiers map to authorization levels:**
   - Tier 1 ($50K–$250K research): Acts with authorizations under $50B or foundational framework acts (01, 02, 04, 08, 09)
   - Tier 2 ($250K–$1M development): Acts with moderate authorizations ($50B–$80B) or development-focused acts (03, 05, 06, 07, 10, 12)
   - Tier 3 ($1M+ strategic): Acts with the largest authorizations or strategic impact (07 with $80B, CRIDA)

5. **Cross-act statutory consistency:** All 12 acts reference consistent definitions, timelines, and dollar amounts where applicable. The legal architecture ensures that:
   - Definitions of key terms (e.g., "critical infrastructure," "GIC exposure," "carbon removal") are consistent across acts
   - Authorization timelines are aligned (all are 10-year authorization periods where specified)
   - Phase consistency is maintained across implementation timelines
   - Cross-references to other acts where appropriate provide a comprehensive regulatory framework
