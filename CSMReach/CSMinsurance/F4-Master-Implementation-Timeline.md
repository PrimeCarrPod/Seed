# CSM Legal Acts — Master Implementation Timeline (All 12 Acts Aligned)

## Overview

This document provides the master implementation timeline aligning all 12 CSM Legal Acts across a 10-year authorization period. The timeline follows a consistent 3-phase structure across all acts, with specific milestones for each act. Together, these acts authorize over $830 billion in federal spending and establish the regulatory framework for Carrington Storm Motors' Aegis-C Composite Shielding and Dielectric Citadel Protocol.

---

## Timeline Structure

All 12 acts follow a consistent 3-phase implementation model over a 10-year authorization period (FY2027–FY2036). Phases are:

- **Phase I (Years 1–2):** Foundation and assessment — condition assessments, rating methodology establishment, regulatory standard promulgation, state-level office creation
- **Phase II (Years 3–5):** Prioritized implementation — initial deployment, rehabilitation of critical assets, initial deployment of key programs
- **Phase III (Years 6–10):** Full deployment and completion — full lifecycle coverage, comprehensive hardening, system-wide operation

### Shared Milestones (All Acts)

### Key Coordination Mechanisms

1. **FIPA (Federal Infrastructure Protection Authority)** — Established under CIPA (Act 01), serves as the overall coordination body for infrastructure protection across all acts
2. **FERC (Federal Energy Regulatory Commission)** — Coordinates grid-related provisions across WPGRA (Act 06), ESGFA (Act 12), and CIPA (Act 01)
3. **DOE (Department of Energy)** — Coordinates energy-related provisions across all acts, particularly WPGRA (06), CRIDA (07), ESGFA (12), and CTSCA (08)
4. **EPA (Environmental Protection Agency)** — Coordinates environmental and health provisions across all acts, particularly CRIDA (07), WIDRA (09), and CIPA (01)
5. **NRC (Nuclear Regulatory Commission)** — Coordinates advanced nuclear provisions across ANEDA (03)
6. **IRS (Internal Revenue Service)** — Coordinates tax credit provisions across CIMTCA (04), ESGFA (12), and other acts
7. **DOL (Department of Labor)** — Coordinates workforce provisions across WDGJA (11) and all other acts
8. **Commerce Department** — Coordinates critical minerals and technology supply chain provisions across DCMIA (02) and CTSCA (08)
9. **State Infrastructure Protection Offices** — Created under CIPA (01), serve as state-level coordination points for all acts
10. **Inter-Act Coordination Council** — Proposed body for regular cross-act coordination, timeline alignment, and cross-reference verification
