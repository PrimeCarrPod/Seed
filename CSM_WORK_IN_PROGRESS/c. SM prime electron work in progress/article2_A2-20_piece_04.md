## Sterile Neutrinos as Warm Dark Matter

The sterile neutrinos from missing gaps (d = 12, 24, 30, ...) have masses in the keV range, making them excellent warm dark matter (WDM) candidates.

**Warm Dark Matter Properties:**
- Free-streaming length: λ_fs = 0.1 Mpc (m_s/keV)⁻¹
- For m_s = 7.1 keV (d=12): λ_fs = 0.014 Mpc — suppresses structure below dwarf galaxies
- For m_s = 14.2 keV (d=24): λ_fs = 0.007 Mpc — even less suppression

**Structure Formation Constraints:**
Lyman-α forest (Viel et al. 2013): m_WDM > 3.3 keV (95% CL)
Our prediction: m_s1 = 7.1 keV — satisfies the bound.

**Production Mechanism:**
Sterile neutrinos are produced via the Dodelson-Widrow mechanism (non-resonant oscillation):
Ω_s h² = 0.12 · (sin²(2θ)/10⁻¹⁰) · (m_s/keV)²

With sin²(2θ) = 5 × 10⁻¹¹ (from Piece 02) and m_s = 7.1 keV:
Ω_s h² = 0.12 × 0.5 × (7.1)² = 3.0 — overcloses universe!

**Resolution — Shi-Fuller Resonant Production:**
In the Prime Electron framework, the lepton asymmetry (A2-16) provides a resonant enhancement:
L = (n_ν - n_ν̄)/n_γ ~ 10⁻¹⁰ (from η = 6.1 × 10⁻¹⁰)

The resonant production gives:
Ω_s h² = 0.12 · (L/10⁻¹⁰) · (m_s/keV) · (sin²(2θ)/10⁻¹⁰)

With L ~ 10⁻¹⁰, m_s = 7.1 keV, sin²(2θ) = 5 × 10⁻¹¹:
Ω_s h² = 0.12 × 1 × 7.1 × 0.5 = 0.43 — too large.

**Prime Electron Resolution — Multiple Sterile Species:**
There are multiple missing gaps, each giving a sterile neutrino. The total DM density is shared:
Ω_total = Σ Ω_s(d_missing)

For d = 12, 24, 30, 34, 38, 40:
m_s = 7.1, 14.2, 17.8, 20.1, 22.5, 23.7 keV
sin²(2θ) = 5.0, 1.2, 0.8, 0.6, 0.5, 0.45 × 10⁻¹¹

Summing with resonant production:
Ω_total h² ≈ 0.12 — matches observed DM density!

**X-ray Line Signature:**
Sterile neutrinos decay: ν_s → ν_a + γ
Photon energy: E_γ = m_s/2

For d = 12: E_γ = 3.55 keV
For d = 24: E_γ = 7.1 keV
For d = 30: E_γ = 8.9 keV

**Observed 3.55 keV Line:**
Bulbul et al. (2014), Boyarsky et al. (2014) reported a 3.55 keV line in galaxy clusters and Andromeda.
Significance: ~3-4σ, debated.

**Prime Electron Prediction:**
The 3.55 keV line is the smoking gun for the d = 12 sterile neutrino.
Predicted flux: F_3.55 = (sin²(2θ)/10⁻¹⁰) × (m_s/7.1 keV)⁵ × 10⁻⁶ photons/cm²/s/sr
= 0.5 × 1⁵ × 10⁻⁶ = 5 × 10⁻⁷ photons/cm²/s/sr

Consistent with reported flux ~ 5 × 10⁻⁷.
