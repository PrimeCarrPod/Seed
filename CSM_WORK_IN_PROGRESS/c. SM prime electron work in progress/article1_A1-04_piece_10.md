## 21.1 Partition Function

The zeta function is the worldline partition function at imaginary proper time:
ζ(β) = Σ_n n^{-β} = Tr(exp(-β H))

where H is the worldline Hamiltonian with eigenvalues ln p_n.

## 21.2 Analytic Continuation

Analytic continuation to β = 1/2 + iT corresponds to real proper time:
ζ(1/2 + iT) = Tr(exp(-(1/2+iT)H))

The zeros are the poles of the analytically continued partition function.

## 21.3 Thermodynamic Limit

As x → ∞ (UV limit), the zero density increases.
The worldline approaches a critical point at the UV fixed point (directory 3.0).

### 22. FUNCTIONAL EQUATION AS WORLDLINE DUALITY

## 22.1 Functional Equation

ζ(s) = 2^s π^{s-1} sin(πs/2) Γ(1-s) ζ(1-s)

In worldline terms: this is a duality between IR (s → 0) and UV (s → 1).
The sine factor gives the zero spectrum symmetry γ ↔ -γ.

## 22.2 Prime Gap Duality

The gap sequence at scale x is dual to the gap sequence at scale 1/x.
This reflects the worldline's self-similarity under RG flow.

### 23. RIEMANN-SIEGEL FORMULA FOR GAPS

## 23.1 Riemann-Siegel Formula

The zeta function on the critical line:
Z(t) = e^{iθ(t)} ζ(1/2+it) = 2 Σ_{n≤√(t/2π)} n^{-1/2} cos(θ(t) - t ln n) + R(t)

## 23.2 Gap Sequence from Riemann-Siegel

The prime gaps are the coefficients in the Riemann-Siegel expansion:
d_n ~ Re[ n^{-1/2} e^{-iγ ln n} ] summed over zeros.
