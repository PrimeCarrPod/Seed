### Abstract

Binary neutron star (BNS) mergers. Tidal deformability Λ, nuclear equation of state, GW170817, GW190425. Post-merger remnants. Worldline picture of neutron star worldlines.

---

### 1. BNS Observations

**GW170817** (2017): First BNS, electromagnetic counterpart (GRB 170817A, AT2017gfo)
**GW190425** (2019): Heavier BNS (3.4 M⊙ total), no EM counterpart
**O4**: Expecting ~10-50 BNS detections

---

### 2. Tidal Deformability & Nuclear EOS

**Tidal deformability**: Λ = (2/3) k₂ (R/M)⁵
**k₂**: Love number, R: radius, M: mass
**GW170817**: Λ̃ = 300⁺⁴²⁰₋₂₃₀ (combined)
**Constraints**: R₁.₄ = 11.0-13.5 km, M_max > 2.0 M⊙

**Worldline**: NS worldline = electron worldline at high density (dir 1.0-2.0). Tidal deformation = worldline shape distortion.

---

### 3. Post-Merger Remnants

**Hypermassive NS**: Supported by differential rotation, collapses in ~10-100 ms
**Supramassive NS**: Uniformly rotating, collapses via magnetic braking
**Stable NS**: If M_total < M_max

**Post-merger GW**: f_peak ~ 2-4 kHz — probes high-density EOS

---

### 4. Electromagnetic Counterparts

**Kilonova**: r-process nucleosynthesis, optical/IR emission
**GRB**: Short GRB 170817A, structured jet
**Afterglow**: X-ray, radio from jet interaction

**Hubble constant**: H₀ = 70⁺¹²₋₈ km/s/Mpc (GW170817)

---

### 5. Worldline Picture: BNS Merger

**Inspiral**: Two worldline bundles with internal structure (tidal)
**Merger**: Worldline collision with matter effects
**Post-merger**: Single worldline with oscillations (f-modes)
