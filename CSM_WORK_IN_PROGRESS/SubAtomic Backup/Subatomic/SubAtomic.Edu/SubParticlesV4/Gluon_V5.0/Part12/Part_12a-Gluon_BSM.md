### Abstract

BSM physics with gluon couplings. Axigluons, colorons, massive color-octet vectors. Flavor anomalies. Gluon compositeness. Dark sector via gluon portal. LHC constraints.

---

### 1. Axigluon (Axial Color-Octet)

**Motivation**: Peccei-Quinn + color, flavor anomalies
**Lagrangian**: 
$$\mathcal{L} = \frac{∂_μ a}{f_a} g_s \bar{q} γ^μ γ^5 T^a q + \frac{α_s}{8π} \frac{a}{f_a} G^a_{μν} \tilde{G}^{aμν}$$

**Signatures**:
- t t̄ forward-backward asymmetry
- Dijet resonance at M_axigluon
- gg → axigluon → t t̄, b b̄, jj

**Constraints**: M > 2-3 TeV (LHC dijet, t t̄)

---

### 2. Coloron (Massive Color-Octet Vector)

**Topcolor/Topcolor-assisted technicolor**:
- Color SU(3)_1 × SU(3)_2 → SU(3)_c
- Coloron = heavy gluon from broken symmetry

**Couplings**: Chiral, flavor-dependent
**Signatures**: 
- t t̄ resonance
- b b̄ resonance
- Dijet angular distribution

**Constraints**: M > 4-5 TeV (LHC)

---

### 3. Z' with Gluon Couplings

**L_μ - L_τ with color**: Anomaly-free with colored fermions
**B-L gauge boson**: Couples to gluons via quark loops

**Signatures**: 
- Dijet resonance
- t t̄ + MET
- Mono-jet + MET

---

### 4. Gluon Compositeness

**Contact interactions**:
$$\mathcal{L}_{comp} = \frac{4π}{Λ^2} (g_s^2) (f^{abc} G^b_{μν} G^{cμν})^2$$

**Effective gluon self-coupling modification**:
- g_s → g_s (1 + c_g Q²/Λ²)
- 4-gluon vertex modified

**Constraints**: Λ > 10-20 TeV (dijet angular)

---

### 5. Dark Sector via Gluon Portal

**Dark gluon**: SU(3)_dark × SU(3)_QCD mixing
**Kinetic mixing**: ε G^a_{μν} G'^{aμν}
**Dark matter**: Dark glueballs, dark quarks

**Signatures**:
- Emerging jets (dark shower)
- Displaced vertices
- Missing E_T + jets
