### Abstract

Future neutrino facilities. JUNO (reactor), DUNE (accelerator), T2HK (accelerator), PTOLEMY (CνB), IceCube-Gen2 (UHE). Ultimate precision projections.

---

### 1. JUNO (Jiangmen Underground Neutrino Observatory)

**Location**: Jiangmen, China (53 km from Yangjiang/Taishan NPPs)
**Detector**: 20 kt liquid scintillator, 3% energy resolution
**Baselines**: 53 km (reactors), 700 km (atmospheric)
**Goals**:
- $\Delta m^2_{21}$: **0.5%**
- $\Delta m^2_{31}$: **0.6%**
- $\theta_{12}$: **0.5°**
- **Mass ordering**: 3-4σ
- Solar ν: $^8$B, $^7$Be, pep, CNO
- Geo-ν: Th/U mantle
- DSNB: Supernova relic ν
- Proton decay: $p \to e^+ \pi^0$

**Timeline**: Construction complete 2023, data taking 2024+

---

### 2. DUNE (Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment)

**Beam**: Fermilab LBNF, 1.2 MW, 60-120 GeV
**Baseline**: 1300 km (Fermilab → SURF)
**Far detector**: 4 × 17 kt LArTPC (68 kt total)
**Near detector**: LAr + GAr + SAND
**Goals**:
- $\delta_{CP}$: **10-15°** (50% coverage)
- **Mass ordering**: 5σ
- $\theta_{23}$ octant: 3σ
- $\nu_e$ appearance: $\nu_\mu \to \nu_e$
- $\nu_\tau$ appearance: $\nu_\mu \to \nu_\tau$
- Nucleon decay: $p \to K^+ \bar{\nu}$
- Supernova ν burst: $\sim 3000$ events (Galactic)
- Atmospheric ν: Ordering, $\theta_{23}$

**Timeline**: First module 2029, full 2030s

---

### 3. T2HK (Hyper-Kamiokande)

**Beam**: J-PARC, 1.3 MW, 0.6 GeV peak
**Baseline**: 295 km (J-PARC → Kamioka)
**Far detector**: 2 × 190 kt water Cherenkov (380 kt fiducial)
**Goals**:
- $\delta_{CP}$: **10-20°** (75% coverage)
- $\theta_{23}$ octant: 3σ
- $\sin^2\theta_{13}$: 1%
- Proton decay: $p \to e^+ \pi^0$ ($\tau > 10^{35}$ yr)
- Solar ν: $^8$B, $hep$
- Supernova ν: $\sim 50,000$ events (Galactic)
- Atmospheric ν: Ordering, $\theta_{23}$

**Timeline**: Construction 2025+, data 2027+

---

### 4. PTOLEMY (CνB Detection)

**Principle**: $\nu_e + {}^3\text{H} \to {}^3\text{He} + e^-$ (no threshold)
**Target**: 100 g $^3$H (tritium) → $10^{25}$ atoms
**Energy resolution**: $\Delta E \sim 0.1$ eV (CνB peak at $Q + m_\nu$)
**Sensitivity**: 
- $m_\nu = 0.1$ eV → $\sim 10$ events/yr
- CνB detection: Direct relic neutrino detection
- $m_\nu$ measurement: Kinematic + CνB

**Timeline**: Prototype 2025, full 2030+

---

### 5. IceCube-Gen2 & UHE Neutrino Astronomy

**IceCube-Gen2**:
- **Optical**: 8× IceCube volume (8 km³)
- **Radio**: 500 km² surface array (>10 PeV)
- **Surface**: Scintillator veto

**UHE Neutrinos**:
- **GRAND**: 200k radio antennas, $10^{17}-10^{19}$ eV
- **POEMMA**: Space-based fluorescence, $10^{17}-10^{20}$ eV
- **BEACON**: Mountain radio, $10^{17}-10^{19}$ eV
- **RNO-G/ARA**: In-ice radio, $10^{17}-10^{19}$ eV

**Goals**:
- Point sources at >10 PeV
- Cosmogenic ν flux
- τ neutrino appearance
- Multi-messenger: GW + ν + γ
