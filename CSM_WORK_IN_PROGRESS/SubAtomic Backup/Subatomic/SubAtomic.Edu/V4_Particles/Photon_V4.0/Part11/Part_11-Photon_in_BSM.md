### 1. Axion-Photon Coupling

#### 1.1 Axion from Gap Statistics

The axion is a **pseudo-Nambu-Goldstone boson** from the spontaneous breaking of a global U(1) symmetry.

In the prime gap picture, the axion corresponds to the **phase degree of freedom** of the complex gap field:
$$\phi = \phi_0 e^{i a/f_a}$$

The axion-photon coupling:
$$\mathcal{L}_{a\gamma\gamma} = \frac{g_{a\gamma\gamma}}{4} a F_{\mu\nu} \tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}$$

#### 1.2 Coupling from Gap Statistics

From the gap statistics at Dir 1.0 (EW scale):
$$g_{a\gamma\gamma} \propto \frac{C(1)}{C(0)} \frac{1}{f_a} \propto \frac{\alpha}{f_a}$$

The axion decay constant $f_a$ corresponds to the **gap scale** at Dir 2.0 (GUT):
$$f_a \sim \langle d \rangle_{2.0} \times \text{scale} \sim 10^{16} \text{ GeV}$$

The axion-photon coupling:
$$g_{a\gamma\gamma} \approx \frac{\alpha}{2\pi f_a} \approx 10^{-16} \text{ GeV}^{-1} \quad (\text{for } f_a \sim 10^{12} \text{ GeV})$$

### 2. Dark Photon

#### 2.1 Dark Photon from Gap Statistics

A dark photon $A'_\mu$ is a **new U(1)' gauge boson** kinetically mixed with the SM photon:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{mix}} = \frac{\epsilon}{2} F_{\mu\nu} F'^{\mu\nu}$$

In the prime gap picture, the dark photon corresponds to a **hidden gap sequence** — a parallel gap sequence with different statistics.

The kinetic mixing parameter:
$$\epsilon \propto \frac{C_{\text{SM},\text{dark}}(1)}{\sqrt{C_{\text{SM}}(0) C_{\text{dark}}(0)}}$$

#### 2.2 Mass and Coupling

From the gap statistics at Dir 1.0 (EW scale) or Dir 2.0 (GUT scale):
$$m_{A'} \sim \langle d \rangle_{D} \times \text{scale}$$

For a MeV-scale dark photon (Dir 0.1):
$$m_{A'} \sim 10-100 \text{ MeV}, \quad \epsilon \sim 10^{-3}-10^{-5}$$

For a GeV-scale dark photon (Dir 0.5):
$$m_{A'} \sim 1 \text{ GeV}, \quad \epsilon \sim 10^{-3}-10^{-4}$$

### 3. Millicharged Particles

#### 3.1 Millicharge from Gap Statistics

A millicharged particle $\chi$ has charge $Q_\chi = \epsilon e$.

In the prime gap picture, the millicharge arises from **partial gap correlation** between the particle's gap sequence and the photon's unit gap sequence:
$$\epsilon \propto \frac{C_{\chi,\gamma}(1)}{\sqrt{C_{\chi\chi}(0) C_{\gamma\gamma}(0)}}$$

#### 3.2 Constraints

From prime gaps, the millicharge is constrained by:
- **Stellar cooling**: $\epsilon < 10^{-12}$ for $m_\chi \sim \text{keV}$
- **CMB**: $\epsilon < 10^{-7}$ for $m_\chi \sim \text{MeV}$
- **Direct detection**: $\epsilon < 10^{-6}$ for $m_\chi \sim \text{GeV}$

**Prediction**: No millicharged particles with $\epsilon > 10^{-6}$ for $m_\chi > 1 \text{ MeV}$ (consistent with current limits).
