## 1. SBIR PHASE I PROPOSAL

**Solicitation:** NASA SBIR/STTR 2025 Phase I — Topic X1.01 Lightweight Structures for Lunar Landers  
**Duration:** 6 months  
**Requested Funding:** $150,000  
**Status:** Submitted

### Phase I Objectives
1. Fabricate a one-meter lander leg segment representative of CLPS lander scale using AFP with in-situ digital twin documentation
2. Conduct static load testing to 200% DLL, thermal cycling from -170°C to +120°C, and radiation exposure to 50 krad(Si)
3. Correlate digital twin predictions with as-tested mechanical and thermal behavior
4. Develop bottom-up cost model for CLPS lander structures demonstrating >50% recurring cost reduction

### Phase I Budget Breakdown

| Category | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| PI Salary (50% FTE, 6 mo) | $30,000 |
| AI Compute (Nematron) | $15,000 |

| Test Lab Fees | $25,000 |

| **Total** | **$150,000** |

## 8. ROI ANALYSIS

### Cost per Lander Structure Set (Recurring, at 10 Units/Year)

| Cost Element | Al/Ti/CFRP Baseline | LBFRP-001 AFP | Savings |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------|---------|
| **Raw Materials** | $1.2M | $0.4M | 67% |
| **Fabrication** | $3.5M | $1.2M | 66% |
| **Assembly/Integration** | $2.8M | $0.3M | 89% |
| **Inspection/NDE** | $0.8M | $0.2M | 75% |
| **Tooling (Amortized)** | $1.5M | $0.3M | 80% |
| **Total per Lander Structure Set** | **$9.8M** | **$2.4M** | **75%** |

### ROI for CLPS Contractors
- **Cost savings per lander:** $7.4M
- **Mass savings:** 60% structural mass → 2.5× payload margin
- **Schedule savings:** 70% integration labor reduction → years to months
- **Risk reduction:** Zero bolted joints in primary load path
