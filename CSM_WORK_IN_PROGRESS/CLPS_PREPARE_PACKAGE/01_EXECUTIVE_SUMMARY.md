## 3. TECHNOLOGY: LBFRP-001

**LBFRP-001** — Lonsdaleite-Reinforced Basalt Fiber Thermoplastic Composite.

One material. Four critical functions:

| Function | Mechanism | Performance |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Primary Structure** | Continuous basalt fiber (12K tow) in Elium thermoplastic matrix | 1.8 GPa tensile, 1.2 GPa compressive, ρ = 1.6 g/cm³ |
| **Radiation Shielding** | Lonsdaleite nanodiamonds (3 wt%, 58% harder than cubic diamond) + H-rich Elium | 3× polyethylene at 1/3 mass |
| **Thermal Management** | Integrated pyrolytic graphite substrates (1,500 W/m·K in-plane) | Spreads 120°C/-170°C lunar day/night loads |
| **Micrometeoroid/Regolith Protection** | Self-healing Elium thermoplastic (reflow at 200°C) + nanodiamond crack blunting | 5+ heal cycles at 90% strength recovery |

**Fabrication:** Automated Fiber Placement (AFP) — 6-axis robotic arm, 16 tows, in-situ consolidation, full digital twin traceability of every tow placement.
