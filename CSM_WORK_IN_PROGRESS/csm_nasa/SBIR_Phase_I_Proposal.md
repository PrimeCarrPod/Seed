### ABSTRACT
Carrington Storm Motors proposes to demonstrate automated fiber placement (AFP) fabrication of **LBFRP-001**, a monolithic lonsdaleite-reinforced basalt fiber thermoplastic composite, for primary structural components of NASA CLPS-class lunar landers. LBFRP-001 integrates four critical functions — primary structure, radiation shielding, thermal management, and micrometeoroid protection — into a single laminate, enabling **60% structural mass reduction** and **70% integration labor reduction** versus traditional aluminum/titanium/carbon-fiber approaches.

**Phase I Objectives:**
1. Fabricate a one-meter lander leg segment representative of CLPS lander scale using AFP with in-situ digital twin documentation of every tow placement
2. Conduct static load testing to 200% design limit load, thermal cycling from -170°C to +120°C, and radiation exposure to 50 krad(Si) to validate multi-function performance
3. Correlate digital twin predictions with as-tested mechanical and thermal behavior to establish design allowables
4. Develop a bottom-up cost model for CLPS lander structures (Griffin, Nova-C, Blue Ghost, SERIES-2 classes) demonstrating >50% recurring cost reduction

**Technical Innovation:** The combination of lonsdaleite nanodiamond reinforcement (58% harder than cubic diamond) in a self-healing Elium thermoplastic matrix with basalt fiber — a lunar ISRU-compatible feedstock — enabling in-situ manufacturing on the Moon.

**Commercial Application:** Direct infusion into the NASA CLPS program, where fixed-price contracts create urgent demand for mass-efficient, integration-eliminating, risk-reducing structural materials.

### 2. WORK PLAN

#### Month 1-2: AFP Process Development & Digital Twin Calibration
- **Week 1-2:** AFP cell setup at partner facility (qualified AFP robot, Elium resin system, basalt fiber supply, lonsdaleite dispersion protocol)
- **Week 3-4:** Process parameter optimization — tow tension, laydown speed, compaction force, temperature profile, lonsdaleite dispersion uniformity
- **Week 5-6:** Digital twin calibration — correlate in-situ sensor data (tow position, tension, temperature) with cured laminate properties (coupon testing)
- **Week 7-8:** Baseline design allowables generation (B-basis) from coupon database (n≥30 per property)

#### Month 3-4: Demonstration Article Fabrication
- **Article 1:** Lander leg segment — 1m length, tapered cross-section, instrumented (strain gauges, thermocouples, fiber Bragg gratings)
- **Article 2:** Pressure vessel quarter-section — representative diameter, integrated pyrolytic graphite thermal substrates
- **Article 3:** Payload adapter ring — integrated bolt patterns, harness channels, thermal paths
- **All Articles:** Full digital twin capture (STEP, NASTRAN, tow log, cure record)

#### Month 5: Test Campaign
| Test | Facility | Acceptance Criteria |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| Static Load (200% DLL) | Partner load frame | No structural failure; strain < allowable |
| Thermal Cycle (-170°C to +120°C) | Thermal vacuum chamber | 100 cycles, zero delamination, thermal gradient <10°C across wall |
| Radiation (50 krad Si) | Proton/electron beam | Property retention >95% |
| Vibration (GEVS Protoflight) | Shaker table | No damage; frequency response unchanged |

#### Month 6: Data Analysis & Phase II Preparation
- Digital twin correlation final report
- Design allowables update with article-level data
- Cost model finalization (material, machine time, labor, tooling, overhead)
- Phase II proposal: Full-scale articles, FAA AST pre-application, CLPS contractor deliveries
- Commercialization plan update
