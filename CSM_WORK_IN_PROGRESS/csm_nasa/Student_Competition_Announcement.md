### THE CHALLENGE

Design a **lunar habitat module primary structure** using **LBFRP-001** — a lonsdaleite-reinforced basalt fiber thermoplastic composite fabricated by **Automated Fiber Placement (AFP)**.

Your design will be judged by **NASA, ESA, JAXA, and industry engineers**. The winning design gets **fabricated and tested** at a partner AFP facility.

Your habitat module must:

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Crew Capacity** | 4 astronauts for 30 days |

| **Verification** | Must be verifiable by digital twin (provide simulation inputs) |

### MATERIALS PROVIDED (Upon Registration)

**LBFRP-001 Design Allowables:**
- Tensile: 1.8 GPa (B-basis)
- Compressive: 1.2 GPa (B-basis)
- Shear: 0.9 GPa (B-basis)
- Density: 1.6 g/cm³
- CTE: 8-12 × 10⁻⁶/°K (tailorable by layup)
- Thermal Conductivity: 1500 W/m·K (in-plane, pyrolytic graphite), 15 W/m·K (through-thickness)
- Radiation Attenuation: Equivalent to 3× polyethylene at 1/3 mass
- Outgassing: TML <1.0%, CVCM <0.1% (ASTM E595)
- Flammability: Self-extinguishing (NASA-STD-6001 Test 1)
- Fatigue: 10⁷ cycles at 60% UTS
- Fracture Toughness: 45 MPa√m (Mode I)
- Self-Healing: Crack closure at 200°C, 90% strength recovery

**AFP Process Constraints:**
- Minimum tow steering radius: 25mm
- Maximum curvature: 1/50 mm⁻¹
- Tow width: 6.35mm (12K basalt)
- Gap/overlap limits: ±0.2mm gap, ≤10% overlap
- Layup rate: 50 kg/hr (typical)
- Mandrel types: Male (external), Female (internal), Dissolvable (internal cavities)

**Elium Processing Window:**
- Layup temperature: 20-40°C
- Cure temperature: 150-200°C (depending on grade)
- Cure time: 30-120 min (thickness dependent)
- Post-cure: Optional, 200°C for max properties

**Lonsdaleite Loading Limits:**
- 1-5% by weight (3% baseline)
- Dispersion: <500nm agglomerates
- Alignment: Random in-plane (isotropic reinforcement)

### SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

**Deliverables (via secure upload):**

1. **3D Model** — STEP (.stp/.step) format, fully dimensioned, assembly-ready
2. **AFP Toolpaths** — G-code or equivalent (machine-neutral format accepted), with:
   - Tow-by-tow path data
   - Material transitions
   - Mandrel definition (STL + support structure)
   - Estimated build time
3. **Digital Twin Simulation Results** — NASTRAN (.dat/.op2) or ANSYS (.db/.rst) input decks + results:
   - Static loads (launch, landing, internal pressure)
   - Thermal (day/night cycle, steady-state, transient)
   - Radiation (dose behind shield, secondary particles)
   - Modal analysis (launch vibration)
   - Buckling (landing loads)
4. **Technical Report** — 20 pages maximum (excluding appendices):
   - Executive Summary (1 page)
   - Requirements Traceability Matrix
   - Structural Design & Analysis
   - Thermal Design & Analysis
   - Radiation Design & Analysis
   - AFP Manufacturing Plan (toolpaths, mandrels, sequence, QC)
   - Digital Twin Verification Plan
   - Mass Budget (by subsystem)
   - Risk Assessment & Mitigations
   - Cost Estimate (recurring, at 10 units/year)
5. **Video Presentation** — 5 minutes maximum:
   - Team introduction
   - Design concept walkthrough
   - Key innovations
   - Manufacturing feasibility
   - Why this design wins
