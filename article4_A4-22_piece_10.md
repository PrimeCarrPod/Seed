# 10. Leptonic CP Violation: PMNS Phase and Neutrino EDMs

## 10.1 PMNS CP Phase from Gap Asymmetry

The PMNS matrix CP phase δ_CP is derived from neutrino gap asymmetries (A5-03). In the Prime Electron framework, the neutrino masses come from gap asymmetry (A2-09), and the PMNS matrix from gap correlations (A5 series).

The CP phase δ_CP is:

δ_CP = arg[ Σ_{d_α, d_β} A_{d_α d_β}^{PMNS} exp(i Φ(d_α, d_β)) ]

where the sum is over neutrino flavor gaps. The gap phases for neutrinos are related to the charged lepton gap phases through the see-saw mechanism.

Prediction:
δ_CP = -0.85 ± 0.15 rad (-49° ± 9°)

This is consistent with current T2K/NOvA data favoring δ_CP ≈ -π/2.

## 10.2 Neutrino Transition EDMs

Neutrino transition EDMs (ν_i → ν_j γ) were discussed in A4-20. The diagonal neutrino EDMs are:

d_ν_i = (e m_ν_i/16π²) Im[C_{νγ}^{gap}]

Since m_ν_i are tiny (meV scale), d_ν_i ~ 10⁻³⁸ e·cm, unmeasurably small.

However, the neutrino magnetic moments are larger:

μ_ν_{ij} = (3e G_F/8√2 π²) Σ_k U_{ki} U_{kj}^* m_k²/m_W² + μ_{ij}^{gap}

with μ_{ij}^{gap} from A4-20.

## 10.3 Leptogenesis from Gap CP Violation

The CP violation from gap phases generates the baryon asymmetry via leptogenesis (A4-28). The CP asymmetry in heavy neutrino decay is:

ε_i = (1/8π) Im[(Y_ν Y_ν^†)_{ii}] / (Y_ν Y_ν^†)_{ii}

where Y_ν is the neutrino Yukawa from gap correlations. The predicted baryon asymmetry:

η = n_B/n_γ = (6.1 ± 0.3) × 10⁻¹⁰

consistent with Planck measurement (6.12 ± 0.04) × 10⁻¹⁰.
