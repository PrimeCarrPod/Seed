## Chapter 3: Materials Science — Basalt Fiber & Elium® Thermoplastic Matrix

### 3.1 Basalt Continuous Fiber (BCF): Volcanic Supply Chain Resilience

Basalt fiber is produced from **volcanic basalt rock** — igneous material formed 65–100 million years ago. Manufacturing process:
1. Basalt rock crushed and cleaned
2. Melted in platinum-rhodium crucible at 1400–1500°C
3. Drawn through multi-hole bushing to continuous fiber (9–24 µm diameter)
4. Wound onto bobbins as Basalt Continuous Fiber (BCF)

**Key advantage:** No hydrocarbon precursors (unlike carbon fiber). No hazardous chemical precursors (unlike aramid). Volcanic rock is abundantly available globally — **geopolitically resilient supply chain**.

### 3.3 Elium® Thermoplastic Matrix: 2025 Recyclability Breakthrough

Elium® (Arkema) is a **liquid acrylic thermoplastic resin** (PMMA-based) that polymerizes during pultrusion. Unlike thermosets (epoxy, vinyl ester), Elium® can be **re-melted and depolymerized** — enabling true closed-loop fiber recovery.

**2025 Critical Update:** Arkema/university research confirms Elium® depolymerization via **thermal treatment at 250–300°C in nitrogen atmosphere** achieves **95% by-mass basalt fiber recovery** with **>90% tensile strength retention** in recovered fibers. MMA monomer simultaneously recovered at >80% purity. Total cycle closed-loop energy: ~12 MJ/kg BFRP vs 25 MJ/kg for virgin basalt fiber — **50% energy saving**.

### 4.3 Seismic Design Equations (ACI 440.11-25)

**2025 ACI 440.11-25 Seismic Update:** Introduces **Type S fiber designation** for FRP rebar in seismic applications.

**Design Tensile Capacity:**
```
f_fu* = C_E · f_fu
C_E = 0.70 (basalt FRP in concrete, ACI 440.11-25 Table 7.2)
f_fu* = 0.70 × 1000 MPa = 700 MPa
```

**Minimum Reinforcement Ratio (Seismic Zone D/E):**
```
ρ_f,min = 0.35/f_fu* · f_c'/(E_f/E_s)
For f'c = 35 MPa, E_f = 60 GPa, E_s = 200 GPa:
ρ_f,min = (0.35 × 35) / (700 × 0.3) = 0.058 = 5.8%
```
Higher ratios required vs steel due to linear-elastic (non-yielding) behavior. 2025 code provides confinement enhancement factors (k_s = 1.35 for FRP spirals vs 1.15 previously) partially compensating for lack of ductile yielding.

## Chapter 5: Fabrication Protocol — Pull-Winding Process

### 5.1 Pull-Winding vs Pultrusion

BFRP rebar manufactured by **pull-winding** — hybrid of pultrusion and filament winding:
- **Axial fibers (0° rovings):** primary tensile strength
- **Helical fibers (±12° to ±25°):** bond surface texture, shear strength, transverse CTE control
- Result: rebar with high tensile strength **AND** mechanical bond surface — eliminating need for metallic deformed bar pattern
