## Chapter 2: Theoretical Foundation — GIC Kill-Chain Physics

### 2.1 Inductive Coupling of Standard Appliances

Standard ovens are constructed from ferromagnetic steel and utilize **Nichrome (Nickel-Chromium) resistance wire** for heating. Nichrome (σ ≈ 10⁶ S/m) is wound into coils to increase resistance. In the context of a Geomagnetic Disturbance (GMD) or directed energy flux, these coils act as inductors.

**Eddy Current Power Dissipation:**
```
P ∝ (B_max² · f² · d²) / ρ
```
Where B_max = magnetic flux density, f = frequency, d = component thickness/loop diameter, ρ = material resistivity.

In a standard oven, the steel chassis provides massive surface area (d²) for Eddy current circulation. During high-frequency electromagnetic events, the skin effect confines these currents to the surface, causing rapid localized superheating capable of warping steel and igniting polymer insulation.

By utilizing Steatite — a metamorphic rock with zero free electrons and effectively infinite electrical resistivity — we eliminate the capacity for Eddy current formation entirely. The oven is **transparent to magnetic flux**. It cannot be heated by induction. It is a dielectric void in the electromagnetic threat landscape.

### 4.1 Why Standard Convection Fans Fail

- **GIC victims:** Copper windings act as antennas; induced EMF destroys motor insulation and driver electronics in milliseconds
- **Fire hazard:** Motor failure under GIC drives resistive overheating of windings
- **Single-point failure:** One failed motor eliminates entire convection function

### 4.2 Pneumatic Convection System

The CSM pneumatic system replaces the induction motor with a **Venturi-tube vortex generator** driven by compressed air from a non-metallic pressure vessel:

```
V̇_airflow = C_d · A_throat · √(2 ΔP / ρ_air)
```

Where C_d ≈ 0.62 (smooth Venturi), A_throat = throat area, ΔP = driving pressure differential, ρ_air = air density.

4. **Thermochromic tile indicator** (Section 8) — visual temperature feedback without electronics

### 5.2 Thermal Stability Analysis

**Thermal time constant of steatite mass:**
```
τ = (m · c_p) / (h · A_surface)
```
For 12 kg soapstone, h = 8 W/m²·K (natural convection), A = 0.4 m²:
```
τ = (12 × 980) / (8 × 0.4) = 11,760 / 3.2 ≈ 3,675 seconds ≈ 61 minutes
```

This **61-minute thermal time constant** means the oven maintains temperature within **±5°C during a complete 10-minute power outage** — a performance specification **no metallic oven can match**.
