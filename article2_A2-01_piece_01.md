## Introduction — Energy from Prime Gaps

The prime electron worldline (Article 1) provides a direct mapping from prime gaps to energy scales. The fundamental relation is the Compton-scale proper time: Δτₙ = κ·dₙ where κ = ℏ/(mₑc²) = 1.288×10⁻²¹ s. The corresponding energy is Eₙ = ℏ/Δτₙ = ℏ/(κ·dₙ).

**Gap-to-energy map.** For each gap dₙ = pₙ₊₁ - pₙ, the energy is:
Eₙ = ℏ/(κ·dₙ) = mₑc²·(1/dₙ)
With mₑc² = 0.511 MeV, this gives Eₙ = 0.511 MeV / dₙ.

**Physical interpretation.** The proper time step Δτₙ is the time for the electron to traverse the n-th segment of its worldline. The inverse gives the energy scale of that segment. Small gaps (d=2, 4, 6) correspond to high energies (UV). Large gaps (record gaps) correspond to low energies (IR).

**No free parameters.** The scale κ is fixed by the Compton wavelength (A1-09). The electron mass mₑ = 0.511 MeV is the single input; all other masses are derived from gap ratios.

**Article 1 foundations used.** 
- A1-01: Δτₙ = κ·dₙ (proper time quantization)
- A1-09: κ = ℏ/(mₑc²) from Compton scale, 3.67B steps
- A1-10: 3500 books = 3500 worldline segments
- A1-17: H = ℏ/κ Σ dₙ⁻¹ (Hamiltonian from gaps)

**PrimeBookOne energy data.** Each of the 3500 books gives the gap distribution at scale μ_b = p_b. The energy spectrum at book b is Eₙ^(b) = 0.511 MeV / dₙ^(b).
