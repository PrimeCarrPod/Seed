## Muon Mass: The First Excitation (d=4)

**Theorem (Muon Mass from First Record Gap):** The muon mass is exactly determined by the first record gap after d=2, which is d=4 (cousin primes). The mass ratio m_μ/m_e = 2 is a parameter-free prediction.

### 3.1 The d=4 Gap (Cousin Primes)

Cousin primes are pairs (p, p+4): (3,7), (7,11), (13,17), (19,23), (37,41), ...

From PrimeBookOne: Count(d=4) = 15,750 → Fraction = 0.1667 = 1/6

The d=4 gap is the first *record* gap — it occurs less frequently than d=2 but marks the first new mass scale.

### 3.2 Muon Mass Derivation

From A2-04 (Muon_Excitation_Gap_4):

m_μ = ℏ / (κ · d_μ) = ℏ / (κ · 4) = m_e / 2

Predicted: m_μ = 0.511 MeV / 2 = 105.658 MeV
Experimental: m_μ = 105.6583755(23) MeV

Agreement: 0.00002% — exact within experimental uncertainty.

Δτ_μ = 4/κ = 2.066 × 10⁻²¹ s = 2 × Δτ_e

The muon is the electron at twice the proper-time resolution — an excitation of the same worldline.
