## CONCEPT OVERVIEW

**Premise:** A practice drill for "Wall of Water Day" — the annual civil defense exercise simulating a crustal displacement mega-tsunami event (3,200m wave height per DeepResearch CrustalDisplacementAnalysis). This broadcast IS the drill. The agents are deployed to 15 Lonsdaleite Outskirt Community structures (CSMFAB068) and 2 Atlantis-class underwater pods (CSMFAB077), reporting LIVE as the simulated event unfolds.

**War of the Worlds Realism:** The broadcast presents as if the event is ACTUALLY HAPPENING. Listeners should believe the wall of water is coming. The heartbeat of the episode is the S-Bus beacon network transmitting from each structure — a rhythmic pulse that accelerates as the wave approaches.

**Duration:** 7x standard episode (~900,000 words). Created in 17 parallel pieces, then glued.
