## 1. Secondary Consumer Ecology

### 1.1 Consumer-Resource Model

$$\frac{dR}{dt} = I - \mu(R)R - \sum_i c_i C_i R$$
$$\frac{dC_i}{dt} = c_i C_i R - d_i C_i$$

where $R$ is resource, $C_i$ consumer population, $c_i$ consumption rate, $d_i$ death rate.

### 1.2 Functional Response Types

- **Type I**: $\alpha R$ (filter feeders)
- **Type II**: $\frac{\alpha R}{1 + \alpha h R}$ (Holling)
- **Type III**: $\frac{\alpha R^2}{1 + \alpha h R^2}$ (sigmoid)

## 2. Carbon Cycle Chemistry

### 2.1 CO₂ Fixation Pathways

**Calvin-Benson Cycle**:
$$3 \text{ CO}_2 + 9 \text{ ATP} + 6 \text{ NADPH} \rightarrow G_3P + 9 \text{ ADP} + 8 \text{ Pi} + 6 \text{ NADP}^+$$

### 2.2 Chemosynthesis Energy Source

$$\text{SO}_2 \rightarrow \text{SO}_4^{2-} + \text{Energy}$$
$$\text{CH}_4 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{Energy}$$
