## 1. Respiratory Chain Complexes

### 1.1 Electron Transport Chain

Complexes I-VI:

- Complex I: NADH $\rightarrow$ ubiquinone
- Complex II: Succinate $\rightarrow$ ubiquinone
- Complex III: Ubiquinol $\rightarrow$ cytochrome c
- Complex IV: Cytochrome c $\rightarrow$ O₂

### 1.2 Proton Motive Force

$$\Delta p = \Delta \psi - \frac{RT}{F} \Delta \text{pH}$$

The chemiosmotic hypothesis: $\Delta p$ drives ATP synthesis.

## 2. Fermentation Pathways

### 2.1 Glycolysis Endpoint

Pyruvate fate depends on environment:

- Aerobic: $\rightarrow$ acetyl-CoA $\rightarrow$ TCA cycle
- Anaerobic: $\rightarrow$ lactate, ethanol, or other products
