## 1. Detection Strategy

### 1.1 Mass Reconstruction

The invariant mass distribution for $G \rightarrow gg \rightarrow 4\text{-had}$:

$$\frac{dN}{dm} \propto \mathcal{B}(G \rightarrow gg) \times \mathcal{B}(gg \rightarrow X)$$

The Breit-Wigner shape:

$$f(m) = \frac{1}{m} \frac{\Gamma_{\text{total}}/2}{(m-m_0)^2 + (\Gamma_{\text{total}}/2)^2}$$

### 1.2 Angular Correlations

The double-tag analysis using two photons:

$$\frac{d^2N}{d\cos\theta^* d\phi^*} \propto 1 + \cos^2\theta^* + A_{\text{LT}} \cos\phi^* \sin^2\theta^*$$
