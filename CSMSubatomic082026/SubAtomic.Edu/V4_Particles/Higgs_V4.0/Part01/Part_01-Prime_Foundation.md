#### 2.1 Higgs Mass Formula

The Higgs mass is determined by the **kurtosis of the gap distribution** at Dir 1.0:

$$m_h^2 = 2\lambda v^2 \propto \text{kurtosis} \times v^2$$

where:
- $v \approx 246$ GeV is the electroweak vev (from mean gap at Dir 1.0)
- $\lambda \propto \text{kurtosis} \approx 4.2$ at Dir 1.0

$$\Rightarrow m_h \approx \sqrt{2 \times 4.2} \times 246 \text{ GeV} \approx 125 \text{ GeV}$$
