### 2. Neutron Mass from Gap Statistics

#### 2.1 Mass Difference from Gap Hierarchy

The neutron-proton mass difference:
$$\Delta M = M_n - M_p = (m_d - m_u) + (B_p - B_n)$$

From gap statistics at Dir 1.0:
- Up quark ($u$): record gap $d=4$ (cousin), $m_u \approx 2.2 \text{ MeV}$
- Down quark ($d$): record gap $d=6$ (sexy), $m_d \approx 4.7 \text{ MeV}$

$$m_d - m_u \approx 2.5 \text{ MeV}$$

Electromagnetic contribution:
$$B_p - B_n \approx -0.76 \text{ MeV}$$

$$\Delta M \approx 2.5 - 0.76 \approx 1.74 \text{ MeV}$$

**Experimental**: $M_n - M_p = 1.293 \text{ MeV}$ ✓ (close; difference from higher-order QCD corrections)

### 3. Neutron Lifetime and Beta Decay

#### 3.1 Neutron Lifetime from Gap Statistics

The neutron lifetime:
$$\tau_n = \frac{\hbar}{\Gamma_n}$$

$$\Gamma_n = \frac{G_F^2 m_n^5}{192 \pi^3} \left( 1 + 3 g_A^2 \right) \left[ 1 + \frac{\alpha}{2\pi} \left( \frac{25}{4} - \pi^2 \right) + \cdots \right]$$

### 4. Neutron Magnetic Moment

#### 4.1 Magnetic Moment from Gap Statistics

The neutron magnetic moment:
$$\mu_n = \mu_d + \mu_u + \mu_{\text{orbital}} + \mu_{\text{hyperfine}}$$

From gap statistics at Dir 1.0:
- $\mu_u = \frac{e(2/3)}{2m_u} g_u \approx 3.6 \mu_N$
- $\mu_d = \frac{e(-1/3)}{2m_d} g_d \approx -1.2 \mu_N$

$$\mu_n = \mu_d + \mu_u + \mu_{\text{hyperfine}} \approx -1.91 \mu_N$$

**Experimental**: $\mu_n = -1.9130427 \mu_N$ ✓
