### 1. Non-Standard Interactions (NSI)

**Effective Lagrangian**:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{NSI}} = -2\sqrt{2} G_F \varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{fP} (\bar{\nu}_\alpha \gamma^\mu P_L \nu_\beta) (\bar{f} \gamma_\mu P f)$$

**Parameters**: $\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{fP}$ (flavor $\alpha,\beta$, fermion $f=e,u,d$, chirality $P=L,R$)

**Matter effects**: 
$$\mathcal{H}_{\text{mat}} = \sqrt{2} G_F n_e \begin{pmatrix} 1+\varepsilon_{ee} & \varepsilon_{e\mu} & \varepsilon_{e\tau} \\ \varepsilon_{e\mu}^* & \varepsilon_{\mu\mu} & \varepsilon_{\mu\tau} \\ \varepsilon_{e\tau}^* & \varepsilon_{\mu\tau}^* & \varepsilon_{\tau\tau} \end{pmatrix}$$

**Constraints** (90% CL):
- $|\varepsilon_{ee}| < 0.1$, $|\varepsilon_{\mu\mu}| < 0.01$, $|\varepsilon_{\tau\tau}| < 0.1$
- $|\varepsilon_{e\mu}|, |\varepsilon_{e\tau}|, |\varepsilon_{\mu\tau}| < 0.01$

**Future**: DUNE, JUNO, T2HK → $\varepsilon \sim 0.001$

### 2. Sterile Neutrinos

**3+1 model**: One sterile $\nu_s$, $m_4 \sim 1$ eV
**Mixing**: $|U_{e4}|^2 \sim 0.02$, $|U_{\mu4}|^2 \sim 0.01$
**Anomalies**: LSND, MiniBooNE, Reactor, Gallium

**Status** (2024):
- **IceCube/DeepCore**: Excludes most 3+1 at 99% CL
- **MicroBooNE**: No low-energy excess (excludes MiniBooNE LEE)
- **Global fit**: Tension between appearance/disappearance

**3+2 models**: Two steriles, better fit to anomalies
**Cosmology**: $N_{\text{eff}}$, $\sum m_\nu$ constraints

### 3. Lorentz Violation (SME)

**SME neutrino sector**:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{LV}} = \frac{1}{2} (a_L)_{\alpha\beta}^\mu \bar{\nu}_\alpha \gamma_\mu \nu_\beta + \frac{1}{2} (c_L)_{\alpha\beta}^{\mu\nu} \bar{\nu}_\alpha \gamma_\mu i D_\nu \nu_\beta + \dots$$

**Effects**:
- Modified dispersion: $E^2 = p^2 + m^2 + c^{\mu\nu} p_\mu p_\nu + \dots$
- Direction-dependent oscillations
- Vacuum birefringence

**Constraints**:
- IceCube: $c^{\mu\nu} < 10^{-28}$ (TeV scale)
- Atmospheric: $a^\mu < 10^{-23}$ GeV
- Reactor: $c^{00} < 10^{-20}$

### 4. Neutrino Decay & Secret Interactions

**Neutrino decay**: $\nu_i \to \nu_j + \phi$ (Majoron), $\nu_i \to \nu_j + \gamma$
**Lifetime limits**: $\tau/m > 10^3$ s/eV (solar), $> 10^5$ s/eV (atmospheric)
**Invisible decay**: $\nu \to \text{invisible}$ → spectral distortion

**Secret interactions**: 
- $\nu$-$\nu$ self-interactions ($G_{\text{eff}} \sim 10^5 G_F$)
- $\nu$-dark matter interactions
- $\nu$-dark radiation

**Effects**: 
- Modified free-streaming
- Cosmological bounds
- IceCube flavor ratios
