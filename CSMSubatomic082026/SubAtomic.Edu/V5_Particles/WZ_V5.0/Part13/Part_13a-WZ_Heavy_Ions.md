### Abstract

W/Z in heavy-ion collisions. Ultra-peripheral collisions (UPCs), QGP probes, nuclear PDFs, W/Z asymmetry. LHC Run 3, Run 4.

---

### 1. Ultra-Peripheral Collisions (UPCs)

**Process**: $\gamma + A \to W/Z + A$ (photon-induced)
**Photon flux**: $n(\omega) \sim Z^2 \alpha / \omega$
**Cross section**: $\sigma_{\gamma A \to W/Z} \sim 1-10$ pb
**LHC**: $W/Z$ in Pb+Pb UPCs measured by ATLAS/CMS

**Advantages**: 
- Clean $W/Z$ production (no pileup)
- Probe nuclear PDFs at small $x$
- $\gamma$-induced $W/Z$ → $d$ quark PDF in nucleus

---

### 2. W/Z as QGP Probes

**$W/Z$ in QGP**: 
- No strong interaction → penetrate QGP unchanged
- Produced early ($t \sim 0.1$ fm/c)
- **Tomography**: $W/Z$ yield vs centrality → initial state

**Measurements**:
- $W/Z$ yield vs $N_{\text{part}}$ → binary scaling
- $W^+/W^-$ ratio → $u/d$ PDF in nucleus
- $Z$ $p_T$ spectrum → energy loss (negligible)

**Results**: Binary scaling holds, no QGP modification of $W/Z$

---

### 3. W/Z Asymmetry & Nuclear PDFs

**$W^+/W^-$ asymmetry**: 
$$A_W = \frac{N_{W^+} - N_{W^-}}{N_{W^+} + N_{W^-}}$$
**Sensitive to**: $u/d$ ratio in nucleus
**Nuclear modification**: $R_i^A(x,Q^2) = f_i^A(x,Q^2) / (A f_i^p(x,Q^2))$

**LHC Run 2**: $W$ asymmetry in p+Pb, Pb+Pb
**EIC**: $W$ production in $e+A$ → direct $d$ quark PDF

---

### 4. Z in Heavy Ions

**$Z$ production**: $q\bar{q} \to Z$ (NC), $q\bar{q}' \to W$ (CC)
**Nuclear modification**: $R_{AA}^Z = 1$ (binary scaling)
**$Z$ $p_T$ spectrum**: No energy loss (colorless)
**$Z$ rapidity**: Probes $x \sim 10^{-3}-10^{-1}$

**Quarkonia vs Z**: 
- $J/\psi$ suppressed (QGP)
- $Z$ not suppressed (no strong interaction)
- **Control**: $Z$ as reference for $J/\psi$ suppression
