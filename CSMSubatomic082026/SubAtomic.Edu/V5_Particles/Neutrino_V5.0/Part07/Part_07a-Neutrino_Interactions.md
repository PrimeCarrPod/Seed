### 1. Neutrino-Nucleus Cross Sections

**Processes**:
- **CCQE**: $\nu_\mu + n \to \mu^- + p$ (quasi-elastic)
- **2p2h**: $\nu + NN \to \mu + N + N$ (meson exchange current)
- **RES**: $\nu + N \to \mu + \Delta \to \mu + N + \pi$
- **DIS**: $\nu + N \to \mu + X$ (deep inelastic)
- **Coherent**: $\nu + A \to \nu + A$ (elastic)

**Energy regimes**:
- $E_\nu < 1$ GeV: CCQE, 2p2h dominate
- $1 < E_\nu < 10$ GeV: RES important
- $E_\nu > 10$ GeV: DIS dominates

---

### 2. Nuclear Effects & Models

**Fermi gas**: Smith-Moniz, relativistic
**Spectral function**: $S(p,E)$ — hole + correlation
**Ab initio**: GFMC, SCGF, CC for light nuclei ($^{12}$C, $^{16}$O)
**Lattice QCD**: Axial form factors, $g_A$, $g_P$

**Key uncertainties**:
- Axial form factor $F_A(Q^2)$ (dipole vs $z$-expansion)
- 2p2h normalization ($\sim 30\%$)
- $Q^2$ shape, flux

---

### 3. DUNE Near Detector & Cross Section Program

**ND complex**: 
- **ND-LAr**: 67 t LArTPC (high-res)
- **ND-GAr**: 10 t gaseous Ar (magnetized)
- **SAND**: On-axis tracker + calorimeter

**Goals**:
- Measure $\nu_\mu, \nu_e$ cross sections on Ar
- Constrain flux $\times$ cross section
- Reduce systematics for oscillation analysis

**Channels**: $\nu_\mu$ CC, $\nu_e$ CC, NC, $\nu_\tau$ (via $\tau$ appearance)

---

### 4. ν-e Scattering: Precision Probe

**Process**: $\nu + e^- \to \nu + e^-$
**SM**: $g_L^2 + g_R^2/3$ (NC), +1 for $\nu_e$ (CC)
**Cross section**: $\sigma \approx 9.2 \times 10^{-45} (E_\nu/\text{MeV})$ cm²

**Precision measurements**:
- **CHARM**: $\sin^2\theta_W$ at $Q^2 \sim 0.1$ GeV²
- **BNL E734**: $\nu_e e, \bar{\nu}_e e$
- **Future**: DUNE $\nu_e e$, JUNO reactor $\nu_e e$

**BSM sensitivity**: $\nu_e e$ → NSI, $Z'$, $m_{\nu_e}$

---

### 5. Flux Uncertainties & Constraints

**Flux sources**: 
- Hadron production (NA61/SHINE)
- Beamline simulation
- In-situ constraints (ND)

**Techniques**:
- $\nu_\mu$ CC on C/H (CH target) → flux shape
- $\nu_\mu$ CC on Ar → Ar cross section
- $\nu_e$ CC → $\nu_e$ flux
