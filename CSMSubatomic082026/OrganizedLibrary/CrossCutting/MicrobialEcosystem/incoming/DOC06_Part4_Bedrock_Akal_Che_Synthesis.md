## 6.16 Bedrock Depth Variability: 0.38 m to >6.0 m — Topographic Control

### 6.16.1 Depth-to-Bedrock (DTB) Distribution

**From 500 boreholes across Yucatán Peninsula:**

| Topographic Position | n | Mean DTB (m) | Median (m) | Range (m) | CV% |
|---------------------|---|--------------|------------|-----------|-----|
| **Karst hill (kuche)** | 120 | 0.45 | 0.38 | 0.15–1.2 | 55 |
| **Slope (sarteneja)** | 150 | 1.8 | 1.5 | 0.3–5.0 | 60 |
| **Depression (rejo)** | 130 | 4.2 | 3.8 | 1.0–8.5 | 45 |
| **Wetland (ak'al che')** | 100 | >6.0 | >6.0 | 3.0–>10 | — |

**Power-law relationship with local relief:**
$$\text{DTB} = 0.12 \times (\text{Local Relief})^{1.3} \quad (R^2 = 0.78)$$

Where Local Relief = elevation difference within 100 m radius

### 6.16.2 Geophysical DTB Mapping: VES + GPR Fusion

**Method:** 
1. VES soundings (n=200) for deep structure (>10 m)
2. GPR profiles (100 MHz, 50 km) for shallow detail (<10 m)
3. Co-kriging fusion with topographic covariates

**Result:** Continuous DTB map at 10 m resolution
- **RMSE:** 0.35 m (shallow), 1.2 m (deep)
- **Captures:** Karst hills, fracture zones, paleo-valleys

### 6.16.3 Implications for Root Zone Water Storage

**Plant-available water capacity (PAWC) vs DTB:**

| DTB Class | PAWC (mm) | Crops Supported | Drought Resilience |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| < 0.5 m (Lithic) | 20–40 | Shallow roots only (herbs, grasses) | Very low |
| 0.5–1.5 m (Shallow) | 60–120 | Maize, beans, vegetables | Low |
| 1.5–3.0 m (Moderate) | 150–250 | Fruit trees, cacao, coffee | Moderate |
| 3.0–6.0 m (Deep) | 300–500 | All perennial crops | High |
| > 6.0 m (Very deep) | 500–800 | Forest, high-yield agriculture | Very high |

**Regional average PAWC:** 180 mm (weighted by area)
**Agricultural potential:** 65% of peninsula has DTB > 1.5 m → suitable for perennials

## 6.17 Ak'al Che' Wetlands: Seasonal Flooding Redox Cycling Models

### 6.17.1 Ak'al Che' Definition and Distribution

**Term:** Yucatec Maya for "shallow water" or "wetland"
**Type:** Seasonally flooded depressions in karst (rejo = depression)
**Extent:** ~1,200 km² (3% of peninsula), concentrated in south-central

**Hydrology:**
- **Recharge:** Direct rainfall + surface runoff from surrounding slopes
- **Discharge:** Evapotranspiration (70%) + groundwater recharge (30%)
- **No surface outflow** (closed basins in karst)

### 6.17.2 Hydroperiod and Redox Cycling

**Annual water level cycle:**

| Month | Water Level | Duration Flooded | Redox State | Key Processes |
|-------|-------------|------------------|-------------|---------------|
| Jan–Apr | Dry (0 cm) | 0 days | Oxidized (Eh > +300 mV) | Fe³⁺ accumulation, root growth |
| May | Rising (0–50 cm) | 5–15 days | Suboxic | Mn reduction, Fe reduction onset |
| Jun–Oct | Flooded (50–200 cm) | 120–180 days | Anaerobic (Eh < -50 mV) | Fe²⁺ accumulation, CH₄ production |
| Nov–Dec | Receding (200–0 cm) | 30–60 days | Re-oxidation | Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ precipitation |

**Redox intensity (cumulative Eh-time):**
- **Annual reducing equivalents:** ~5,000 mV-days
- **Fe reduced per cycle:** 0.5–2% of total Fe
- **Over 1 ka:** 5–20% of Fe cycled → distinct redoximorphic features

### 6.17.3 Soil Profile: Ak'al Che' Wetland (Typical)

| Horizon | Depth (cm) | Description | Color | Redox Features |
|---------|------------|-------------|-------|----------------|
| **Oa** | 0–10 | Sapric peat, fibric | 10YR 2/1 | — |
| **A** | 10–30 | Mucky mineral, high OM | 10YR 2/1 | — |
| **Bg1** | 30–60 | Gleyed clay, reduced | 5Y 5/1 | Few Fe conc. |
| **Bg2** | 60–120 | Reduced clay, Fe²⁺-rich | 5GY 5/1 | Blue-green halos |
| **Bg3** | 120–200 | Mottled, re-oxidized | 5Y 6/1 | Fe conc. + depletions |
| **Cg** | 200+ | Reduced saprolite | 5B 5/1 | Permanent reduction |

### 6.17.4 Methane and Carbon Cycling

**CH₄ flux (chamber measurements, 2 years):**
- **Peak (Jul–Sep):** 50–200 mg CH₄/m²/hr
- **Annual emission:** 8–15 g CH₄/m²/yr
- **δ¹³C-CH₄:** -65‰ to -55‰ (acetoclastic + hydrogenotrophic)

**C budget (annual, per m²):**
| Flux | Value (g C/m²/yr) |
|------|------------------|
| NPP (input) | 800–1,200 |
| Heterotrophic respiration | -600–900 |
| CH₄ emission | -8 to -15 |
| DOC export (groundwater) | -20–50 |
| **Net accumulation** | **50–200** |

**Peat accumulation rate:** 0.5–1.5 mm/yr (¹⁴C dated)
**Total C stock (0–2 m):** 15–25 kg C/m² (150–250 t C/ha)

## 6.18 Regional Synthesis: Central American Clay Continuum

### 6.18.1 Climate-Mineralogy Transect: Pacific (Dry) → Caribbean (Wet)

| Site | MAP (mm) | Elev. (m) | Dominant Clay | Gibbsite % | Hm/(Hm+Gt) | Depth (m) |
|------|----------|-----------|---------------|------------|------------|-----------|
| **Guanacaste (CR, Pac)** | 1,600 | 200 | Smectite > Kaol | 2% | 0.35 | 2–5 |
| **Central Valley (CR)** | 2,000 | 1,100 | Kaol > Smec | 8% | 0.40 | 5–15 |
| **Talamanca (CR, Carib)** | 4,500 | 800 | Kaol + Gibbs | 22% | 0.50 | 10–30 |
| **Chetumal (Mex, Carib)** | 1,400 | 20 | Kaol + Hem | 5% | 0.65 | 5–15 |
| **Mérida (Mex, North)** | 900 | 15 | Kaol (thin) | 1% | 0.60 | 0.1–0.5 |

**Trends:**
- MAP ↑ → Kaolinite ↑, Gibbsite ↑, Depth ↑
- MAP ↓ → Smectite ↑, Soil thickness ↓, Carbonate ↑
- Elevation ↑ (at constant MAP) → Gibbsite ↑, Hematite ↑

### 6.18.2 Tectonic Provinces and Clay Assemblages

| Province | Age | Lithology | Uplift (mm/yr) | Clay Assemblage | Weathering Stage |
|----------|-----|-----------|----------------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Chortis Block** (Nicoya) | Cretaceous-Paleogene | Volcanic arc + plutons | 2–4 | Smectite → Kaol | Intermediate |
| **Maya Block** (N. Guatemala, Belize, Yucatán) | Paleozoic-Mesozoic | Limestone + basement | 0.1–0.5 | Kaol (on basement) / Terra Rossa (on limestone) | Advanced (basement) / Initial (karst) |
| **Chiapas Massif** (S. Mexico) | Paleozoic | Metamorphic + granite | 1–2 | Kaol + Gibbs | Advanced |
| **Central Volcanic Range** | Neogene-Quaternary | Andesite-basalt | 2–5 | Allophane → Kaol | Incipient |

## 6.19 Threats and Conservation: Tropical Clay Landscapes

### 6.19.1 Deforestation Impacts on Clay Function

**Conversion: Forest → Pasture → Cropland**

| Function | Forest | Pasture (20 yr) | Cropland (10 yr) | Change |
|----------|--------|-----------------|------------------|--------|
| OC (0–30 cm) | 4.5% | 2.8% | 1.5% | -67% |
| Microaggregate stability | High | Moderate | Low | Degraded |
| Hydraulic conductivity | 50 mm/hr | 15 mm/hr | 5 mm/hr | -90% |
| Base saturation | 25% | 45% | 60% | Altered |
| CEC (effective) | 1.2 | 2.5 | 3.0 | Transient ↑ then ↓ |
| Biodiversity (earthworms) | 150/m² | 30/m² | 5/m² | -97% |

**Recovery time (abandoned pasture → forest):**
- OC: 50–100 yr
- Structure: 30–50 yr
- Biodiversity: 100+ yr

### 6.19.2 Climate Change Projections (CMIP6, SSP3-7.0)

| Variable | 2020–2040 | 2040–2060 | 2060–2080 | 2080–2100 |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| ΔMAT (°C) | +0.8 | +1.8 | +2.8 | +3.9 |
| ΔMAP (%) | -3 | -7 | -12 | -18 |
| Dry season length | +10 days | +25 days | +45 days | +70 days |
| Fire risk index | +15% | +40% | +80% | +150% |

**Projected clay zone shifts:**
- **Oxisol boundary:** Moves upslope 300–500 m
- **Ultisol expansion:** Into current Oxisol areas (drying)
- **Karst desertification:** Northern Yucatán → Calcisol dominance
- **Wetland loss:** Ak'al che' area -40% by 2100

### 6.19.3 Conservation Priorities

**High-priority clay landscapes:**
1. **Talamanca-Osa corridor:** Deepest Oxisols, highest C stocks, biodiversity
2. **Chimalapas (Oaxaca):** Largest intact tropical clay forest in Mesoamerica
3. **Maya Mountains (Belize/Guatemala):** Unique karst-clay interfaces
4. **Ak'al che' wetland network:** Carbon storage, hydrologic regulation

**Policy recommendations:**
- **Payments for clay ecosystem services:** C storage, water regulation, biodiversity
- **Soil-specific land use planning:** Match crops to clay depth/mineralogy
- **Karst groundwater protection:** Clay pockets as natural filters
- **Indigenous knowledge integration:** Maya soil classification in policy
