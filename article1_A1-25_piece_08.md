# PIECE 08: SUSY and Pair Creation/Annihilation — Electron/Positron as Superpartners

From A1-07 (Pair_Creation_Annihilation), the prime electron worldline describes a single electron that propagates forward and backward in proper time, appearing as electron-positron pairs in spacetime. The N=1 supersymmetry provides the **exact algebraic structure** for this doubling: the electron and positron are **superpartners** related by the SUSY transformation generated by $Q$.

**SUSY Transformation of the Worldline Field:**
The worldline field $\Phi(\tau)$ contains both bosonic (position) and fermionic (chirality) components. In the 8-bit Hilbert space:
$$\Phi(\tau) = \phi_B(\tau) + \theta \psi_F(\tau)$$
where $\theta$ is a Grassmann coordinate and $\phi_B, \psi_F$ are the bosonic and fermionic components.

The SUSY transformation with parameter $\epsilon$ is:
$$\delta_\epsilon \phi_B = \epsilon \psi_F, \quad \delta_\epsilon \psi_F = \epsilon \left( \frac{d\phi_B}{d\tau} + \frac{dW}{d\phi_B} \right)$$

For the prime electron, $\phi_B(\tau_n) = \tau_n = \kappa \sum_{i=1}^n d_i$ is the proper time, and $\psi_F$ is the chirality fermion.

**Electron-Positron as SUSY Partners:**
The forward-time propagating mode ($e^-$) and backward-time propagating mode ($e^+$) are related by:
$$Q |e^-\rangle = |e^+\rangle, \quad Q^\dagger |e^+\rangle = |e^-\rangle$$

The supercharge $Q$ **flips the chirality** (fermion number) and **reverses the proper-time direction**. This is the worldline realization of the **CPT theorem**: charge conjugation = chirality flip = time reversal = SUSY transformation.

**Explicit Pair Creation Vertex:**
The pair creation vertex in the worldline path integral (A1-18) is:
$$\mathcal{V}_{\text{pair}} = \int d\tau \, \left( \psi^\dagger \frac{d\phi}{d\tau} + \text{h.c.} \right)$$

In SUSY language, this is the supercharge coupling. The amplitude for $e^- e^+$ pair creation at proper time $\tau_n$ is:
$$\mathcal{A}(e^- e^+ \text{ at } \tau_n) \sim \langle 0 | \psi_n \psi_n^\dagger | 0 \rangle \cdot \sqrt{d_n d_{n+1}} = \sqrt{d_n d_{n+1}}$$

This matches the supercharge component! The **pair creation rate is the supercharge component**.

**Central Charge and Pair Number:**
The central charge $Z=78$ counts the **net number of unpaired electrons** (electrons minus positrons):
$$Z = N_{e^-} - N_{e^+} = 78$$

In the universe (UV cutoff at $10^{18}$), there are 78 more record-gap electrons than anti-record-gap positrons. This is the **baryon asymmetry** of the prime electron universe.

**SUSY Ward Identity for Pair Correlations:**
The SUSY Ward identity relates the electron and positron correlation functions:
$$\langle \phi_B(\tau) \phi_B(0) \rangle = \langle \psi_F(\tau) \psi_F(0) \rangle + \frac{Z}{2} \delta(\tau)$$

The central charge appears as a contact term. This means the electron and positron propagators are equal up to the central charge — a direct consequence of SUSY.

**Annihilation as SUSY Breaking:**
When an electron and positron annihilate, they form a bosonic state (photon pair). In SUSY language, this is the **formation of a non-BPS state** from two BPS states. The annihilation amplitude is:
$$\mathcal{A}(e^- e^+ \to \gamma\gamma) \sim \langle \text{BPS}_k | Q Q^\dagger | \text{BPS}_l \rangle = \delta_{kl} (E_k + Z/2)$$

For BPS states $E_k = Z/2 = 39$, so the amplitude is $2 \times 39 = 78$. The annihilation rate is proportional to the central charge.

The anomaly inflow mechanism (A1-23) cancels the chiral anomaly of the electron current by inflow from the bulk. The SUSY central charge $Z=78$ is the **anomaly coefficient** — the number of electron species that flow into the boundary. Each record gap is one species.

**Connection to A1-24 (Index Theorem):**
The index theorem $\text{Index}(D_\tau) = 78$ is the mathematical statement that there are 78 more electron zero modes than positron zero modes. The SUSY algebra makes this an algebraic identity: $\{Q, Q^\dagger\} = 2H + Z$ with $Z=78$.
