PART 1: ORGANIZATIONS TO SIGN UP FOR
================================================================================

1. AMERICAN PHYSICAL SOCIETY (APS)
   Website: https://www.aps.org
   Action: Create an account, join the Division of Particles and Fields (DPF)
   Cost: ~$150/year (professional), ~$75 (early career)
   Why: APS publishes Physical Review Letters (PRL) — the top journal for this work

2. AMERICAN MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY (AMS)
   Website: https://www.ams.org
   Action: Create an account, join the Number Theory section
   Cost: ~$150/year
   Why: AMS publishes the top number theory journals

3. EUROPEAN PHYSICAL SOCIETY (EPS)
   Website: https://www.eps.org
   Action: Create an account
   Cost: Varies by country
   Why: European reach for physics community

4. INTERNATIONAL UNION OF PURE AND APPLIED PHYSICS (IUPAP)
   Website: https://iupap.org
   Action: Check for relevant commissions (C11 for Particles, C18 for Math Physics)
   Why: International recognition

5. MATHEMATICAL ASSOCIATION OF AMERICA (MAA)
   Website: https://www.maa.org
   Action: Create an account
   Cost: ~$100/year
   Why: For the mathematical aspects of the work

6. FOUNDATIONAL QUESTIONS INSTITUTE (FQXi)
   Website: https://fqxi.org
   Action: Create an account, apply for grants
   Cost: Free to join
   Why: Funds foundational physics research, very interested in physics-math connections

7. PERIMETER INSTITUTE FOR THEORETICAL PHYSICS
   Website: https://perimeterinstitute.ca
   Action: Create an account, check for visitor programs
   Cost: Free to join
   Why: Top theoretical physics institute, interested in new frameworks

8. INSTITUTE FOR ADVANCED STUDY (IAS)
   Website: https://www.ias.edu
   Action: Check for visitor programs, public lectures
   Cost: Free to join mailing list
   Why: Where Witten, Arkani-Hamed work

9. KAVLI INSTITUTE FOR THEORETICAL PHYSICS (KITP)
   Website: https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu
   Action: Create an account, check for programs
   Cost: Free to join
   Why: Top physics research institute

10. WOLFRAM PHYSICS PROJECT
    Website: https://www.wolframphysics.org
    Action: Create an account, join the community
    Cost: Free
    Why: Computational approach to physics, aligned with your work

================================================================================

1. arXiv.org
   Website: https://arxiv.org/submit
   Action: Submit to math.NT (Number Theory) or hep-th (High Energy Physics)
   Cost: Free
   Why: This is where ALL physicists and mathematicians check for new results
   IMPORTANT: You need an endorser. Get one by contacting a professor at a university

2. viXra.org
   Website: https://vixra.org/submit
   Action: Submit as a backup (no endorsement needed)
   Cost: Free
   Why: Open alternative to arXiv

   Cost: Free
   Why: Academic social network, good for visibility

   Why: Makes the work searchable

   Why: Professional network, good for media coverage

7. Twitter/X
