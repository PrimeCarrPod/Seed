## SEC. 108. WASTEWATER TREATMENT MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 108.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Wastewater Treatment Modernization Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Expand treatment capacity to eliminate CSOs
(2) Upgrade treatment processes to meet nutrient limits
(3) Install energy recovery systems
(4) Produce reclaimed water for beneficial reuse

---

## SEC. 109. STORMWATER MANAGEMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 109.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Stormwater Management Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Reduce urban flooding through green infrastructure
(2) Improve stormwater quality
(3) Recharge groundwater aquifers
(4) Reduce combined sewer overflows

---

## SEC. 110. SMART WATER TECHNOLOGY PROGRAM

**SEC. 110.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Smart Water Technology Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall deploy:
(1) Advanced leak detection sensors
(2) Real-time water quality monitoring
(3) Automated meter reading systems
(4) Predictive analytics platforms
