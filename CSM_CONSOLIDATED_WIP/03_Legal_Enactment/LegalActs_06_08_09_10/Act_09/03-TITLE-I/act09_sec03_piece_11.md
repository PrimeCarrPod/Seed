## SEC. 122. INFRASTRUCTURE FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 122.** The Secretary shall prioritize water infrastructure for hospitals, emergency response facilities, and other critical infrastructure.

## SEC. 123. INFRASTRUCTURE PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 123.** The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for water infrastructure programs, including miles of pipe replaced and water loss reduction.
