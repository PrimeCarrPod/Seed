## SEC. 106. LEAD SERVICE LINE REPLACEMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 106.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Lead Service Line Replacement Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $15 billion over 10 years.

**(c) GOAL.—**The program shall eliminate all lead service lines within 10 years.

**(d) REQUIREMENTS.—**
(1) Full service line replacement (public and private portions)
(2) Provision of filters during replacement process
(3) Post-replacement water quality testing
(4) Priority for communities with children and pregnant women

## SEC. 107. WATER TREATMENT PLANT MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 107.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Water Treatment Plant Modernization Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) ELIGIBILITY.—**Grants shall be available for:
(1) Upgrading treatment processes
(2) Installing advanced treatment technologies
(3) Improving energy efficiency
(4) Enhancing resilience to climate change
