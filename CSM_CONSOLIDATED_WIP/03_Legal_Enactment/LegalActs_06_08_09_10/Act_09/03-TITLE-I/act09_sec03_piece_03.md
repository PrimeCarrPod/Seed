## SEC. 105. WATER MAIN REPLACEMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 105.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Water Main Replacement Program to provide grants for the replacement of aging water mains.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $20 billion over 10 years.

**(c) ELIGIBILITY.—**Grants shall be available to:
(1) Public water systems
(2) Tribal water systems
(3) Small and disadvantaged communities (priority)

**(d) PRIORITY.—**Grants shall be prioritized based on:
(1) Age and condition of existing mains
(2) Frequency of main breaks
(3) Water loss rates
(4) Environmental justice considerations

**(e) REQUIREMENTS.—**Grant recipients shall:
(1) Replace mains with ductile iron, PVC, or other approved materials
(2) Install smart leak detection sensors
(3) Develop asset management plans
(4) Provide workforce training
