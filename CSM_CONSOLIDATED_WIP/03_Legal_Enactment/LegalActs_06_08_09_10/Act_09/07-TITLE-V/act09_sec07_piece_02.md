## SEC. 504. CLIMATE-RESILIENT INFRASTRUCTURE

**SEC. 504.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Climate-Resilient Infrastructure Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $15 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Incorporate climate projections into infrastructure design
(2) Upgrade existing infrastructure for climate resilience
(3) Develop climate-resilient construction standards
(4) Support nature-based climate adaptation

## SEC. 505. SEA LEVEL RISE ADAPTATION

**SEC. 505.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Sea Level Rise Adaptation Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Protect coastal water infrastructure
(2) Prevent saltwater intrusion
(3) Support managed retreat from high-risk areas
(4) Restore coastal wetlands
