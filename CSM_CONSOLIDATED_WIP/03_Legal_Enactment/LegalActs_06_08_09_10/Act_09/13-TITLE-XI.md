## SEC. 1111. APPENDIX F — GLOSSARY OF TERMS

**SEC. 1111.** The following terms are defined for purposes of this Act:

**(1) "Aquifer"**
An underground geological formation that stores and transmits water.

**(2) "Watershed"**
The area of land that drains to a particular water body.

**(3) "Potable Water"**
Water that is safe for human consumption.

**(4) "Reclaimed Water"**
Wastewater that has been treated for beneficial reuse.

**(5) "Environmental Flows"**
The quantity, timing, and quality of water flows required to sustain aquatic ecosystems.

## SEC. 1115. APPENDIX J — ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE ANALYSIS
