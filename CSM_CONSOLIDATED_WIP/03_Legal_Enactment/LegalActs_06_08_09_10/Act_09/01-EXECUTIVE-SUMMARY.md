# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — Act 09: Water Infrastructure & Drought Resilience Act

## OVERVIEW

The Water Infrastructure and Drought Resilience Act of 2026 establishes a comprehensive framework for modernizing the United States' water infrastructure and building drought resilience.

---

## THE PROBLEM

**(1) Aging Infrastructure**
The United States' water infrastructure is aging and deteriorating. Many water systems are over 100 years old.

**(2) Water Scarcity**
Climate change is increasing water scarcity, particularly in the western United States.

**(3) Flood Risk**
Flooding causes $17 billion in damages annually.

**(4) Climate Change**
Climate change is intensifying droughts and floods.

---

## THE SOLUTION

**(1) Infrastructure Modernization**
$50 billion for water infrastructure modernization.

**(2) Drought Resilience**
$30 billion for drought resilience programs.

**(3) Flood Management**
$25 billion for flood management.

**(4) Water Conservation**
$20 billion for water conservation.

**(5) Climate Adaptation**
$25 billion for climate adaptation.

## KEY DEFINITIONS

**Water Infrastructure**
Systems for the collection, treatment, storage, and distribution of water.

**Drought Resilience**
The ability of water systems to maintain adequate supply during periods of water scarcity.

**Flood Management**
Systems and practices for reducing flood risk.

**Water Conservation**
Practices and technologies that reduce water consumption.

**Climate Adaptation**
Adjustments in ecological, social, or economic systems in response to actual or expected climate change.
