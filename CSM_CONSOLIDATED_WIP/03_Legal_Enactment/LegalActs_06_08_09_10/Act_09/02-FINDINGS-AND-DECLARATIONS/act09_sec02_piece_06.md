## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — WATER QUALITY

**SEC. 211.** (a) LEAD CONTAMINATION.—**An estimated 6-10 million homes still receive water through lead service lines. Lead exposure causes irreversible neurological damage in children.

**(b) PFAS CONTAMINATION.—**Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) contaminate drinking water for an estimated 200 million Americans. Current treatment technologies are expensive and not widely deployed.

**(c) AESTHETIC AND HEALTH CONCERNS.—**Taste, odor, and color issues affect consumer confidence in tap water. Disinfection byproducts pose long-term health risks.

---

## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — AGRICULTURAL WATER USE

**SEC. 212.** (a) IRRIGATION EFFICIENCY.—**Flood irrigation, the least efficient method, is still used on 40% of irrigated acres. Drip and sprinkler irrigation can reduce water use by 30-50%.

**(b) CROP SELECTION.—**Water-intensive crops are grown in arid regions due to historical water rights. Incentives for crop switching could reduce agricultural water use significantly.
