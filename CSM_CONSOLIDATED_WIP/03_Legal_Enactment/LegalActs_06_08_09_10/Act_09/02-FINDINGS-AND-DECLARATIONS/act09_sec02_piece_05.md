## SECTION 209. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 209.** This Act amends the Safe Drinking Water Act, Clean Water Act, and other federal statutes as provided in Title VIII.

**SEC. 210.** Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of any federal agency under existing law.
