## SECTION 205. GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE

**SEC. 205.** (a) IN GENERAL.—This Act applies to all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and all U.S. Territories.

**(b) WATERSHED APPROACH.—**Implementation shall follow watershed boundaries rather than political boundaries to ensure integrated water management.

**(c) TRIBAL LANDS.—**This Act applies to Tribal lands subject to federal trust responsibilities, with implementation through government-to-government consultation.

---

## SECTION 206. TEMPORAL SCOPE

**SEC. 206.** (a) EFFECTIVE DATE.—This Act takes effect on the date of enactment.

**(b) SUNSET.—**This Act shall remain in effect for 10 years from the date of enactment, unless reauthorized by Congress.

---

## SECTION 207. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 207.** If any provision of this Act is held invalid, the remainder shall not be affected.

---

## SECTION 208. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS

**SEC. 208.** There is authorized to be appropriated $150,000,000,000 over 10 years.
