## SEC. 307. STORMWATER MANAGEMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 307.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Stormwater Management Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Reduce urban flooding through green infrastructure
(2) Improve stormwater quality
(3) Recharge groundwater aquifers
(4) Reduce combined sewer overflows

## SEC. 308. FLOOD FORECASTING IMPROVEMENT

**SEC. 308.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Flood Forecasting Improvement Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Upgrade flood forecasting models
(2) Install additional stream gauges
(3) Improve flash flood warning systems
(4) Develop inundation mapping
