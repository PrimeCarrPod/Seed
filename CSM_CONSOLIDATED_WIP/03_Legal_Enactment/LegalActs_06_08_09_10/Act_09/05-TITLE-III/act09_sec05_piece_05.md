## SEC. 309. COASTAL FLOOD PROTECTION

**SEC. 309.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Coastal Flood Protection Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Protect coastal communities from sea level rise
(2) Restore coastal wetlands and barrier islands
(3) Improve coastal storm surge modeling
(4) Support managed retreat from high-risk areas

## SEC. 310. NATIONAL FLOOD INSURANCE REFORM

**SEC. 310.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a National Flood Insurance Reform Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Update FEMA flood maps
(2) Incorporate climate change into flood risk assessments
(3) Increase flood insurance coverage
(4) Provide affordability assistance
