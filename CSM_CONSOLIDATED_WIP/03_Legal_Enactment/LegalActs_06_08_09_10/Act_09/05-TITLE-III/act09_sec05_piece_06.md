## SEC. 311. DAM SAFETY PROGRAM

**SEC. 311.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Dam Safety Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Inspect and assess all high-hazard dams
(2) Repair deficient dams
(3) Remove obsolete dams
(4) Develop dam emergency action plans
