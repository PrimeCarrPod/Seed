## SEC. 605. WATER INFRASTRUCTURE FUND

**SEC. 605.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Water Infrastructure Fund.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $150,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(c) ALLOCATION.—**Funds shall be allocated as follows:
(1) Title I (Water Infrastructure): $80,000,000,000
(2) Title II (Drought Resilience): $35,000,000,000
(3) Title III (Flood Management): $45,000,000,000
(4) Title IV (Water Conservation): $20,000,000,000
(5) Title V (Climate Adaptation): $30,000,000,000

---

## SEC. 606. TAX INCENTIVES

**SEC. 606.** (a) WATER INFRASTRUCTURE TAX CREDIT.—**A 25% tax credit for water infrastructure investment.

**(b) WATER CONSERVATION TAX CREDIT.—**A 20% tax credit for water conservation investment.

## SEC. 607. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE VI

**SEC. 607.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $150,000,000,000 over 10 years.
