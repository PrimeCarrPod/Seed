## SEC. 220. DROUGHT RESILIENCE FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 220.** The Secretary shall prioritize drought resilience for hospitals, emergency response facilities, and other critical infrastructure.

---

## SEC. 221. DROUGHT RESILIENCE PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 221.** The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for drought resilience programs, including water recycling capacity and groundwater recharge volume.
