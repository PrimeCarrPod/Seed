## SEC. 203. PURPOSES OF TITLE II

**SEC. 203.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Build drought resilience through water recycling, desalination, conservation, and watershed management.

**(2)** Restore and maintain reservoir levels in major western reservoirs.

**(3)** Protect agricultural communities from drought impacts.

**(4)** Restore aquatic ecosystems damaged by drought.

**(5)** Recharge depleted groundwater aquifers.

**(6)** Develop alternative water sources for drought-prone communities.

---

## SEC. 204. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE II

**SEC. 204.** In this title:

**(1) "Drought Resilience"**
The ability of water systems to maintain adequate supply during periods of water scarcity.

**(2) "Water Recycling"**
The treatment and reuse of wastewater for beneficial purposes, including potable reuse.

**(3) "Desalination"**
The removal of salt from seawater or brackish water to produce potable water.

**(4) "Aquifer Storage and Recovery (ASR)"**
The injection of treated water into underground aquifers for later recovery.

**(5) "Environmental Flows"**
The quantity, timing, and quality of water flows required to sustain aquatic ecosystems.
