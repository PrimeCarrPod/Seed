## SEC. 209. WATERSHED RESTORATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 209.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Watershed Restoration Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Restore headwater forests
(2) Reconnect floodplains
(3) Restore riparian habitats
(4) Reduce erosion and sedimentation

---

## SEC. 210. AGRICULTURAL DROUGHT RESILIENCE

**SEC. 210.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established an Agricultural Drought Resilience Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Install efficient irrigation systems
(2) Develop drought-resistant crops
(3) Provide drought insurance
(4) Support water banking
