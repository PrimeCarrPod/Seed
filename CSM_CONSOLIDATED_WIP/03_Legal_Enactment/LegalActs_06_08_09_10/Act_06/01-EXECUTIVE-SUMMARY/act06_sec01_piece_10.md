## TRIBAL PROVISIONS

### Tribal Sovereignty Recognition
- Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish Tribal sovereignty
- Federal agencies must engage in government-to-government consultation
- Tribal nations may develop alternative standards that meet or exceed federal requirements
- Traditional ecological knowledge shall be incorporated into planning

### Tribal Set-Aside
- 10% of all grant funds reserved for Tribal nations
- Direct funding to Tribal governments (no state pass-through)
- Technical assistance for VMP development on Tribal lands
- Cultural burning practices recognized as valid prevention strategy

### Tribal Employment Preference
- Indian preference in hiring for all federally funded projects on Tribal lands
- Tribal business preference for contracting opportunities
- Workforce training programs for Tribal members
- Internship programs for Tribal college students
