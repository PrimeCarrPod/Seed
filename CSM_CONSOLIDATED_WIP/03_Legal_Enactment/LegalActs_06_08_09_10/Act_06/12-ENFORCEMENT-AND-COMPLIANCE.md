# TITLE X — ENFORCEMENT AND COMPLIANCE

## SEC. 1201. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 1201.** This title may be cited as the "Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Enforcement Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 1202. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1202.** The Congress finds the following with respect to enforcement:

**(1) Compliance Necessity**
Effective enforcement is essential to ensure compliance with the provisions of this Act. Without enforcement, the objectives of this Act will not be achieved.

**(2) Clear Standards**
Clear, measurable standards are necessary for effective enforcement. Standards must be specific enough to determine compliance.

**(3) Adequate Penalties**
Penalties must be sufficient to deter non-compliance. Penalties that are too low will not change behavior.

**(4) Due Process**
Enforcement must include due process protections, including notice, opportunity to be heard, and judicial review.

**(5) Coordination**
Enforcement must be coordinated across federal, state, and local agencies to avoid duplication and ensure consistency.

## SEC. 1203. PURPOSES OF TITLE X

**SEC. 1203.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish enforcement mechanisms to ensure compliance with this Act.

**(2)** Define penalties for non-compliance.

**(3)** Provide for due process protections.

**(4)** Coordinate enforcement across federal, state, and local agencies.

**(5)** Establish reporting requirements to track enforcement activities.

---

## SEC. 1204. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE X

**SEC. 1204.** In this title:

**(1) "Enforcement"**
The process of ensuring compliance with the provisions of this Act.

**(2) "Compliance"**
Conformity with the requirements of this Act.

**(3) "Penalty"**
A punishment imposed for non-compliance with this Act.

**(4) "Due Process"**
The legal requirements that the state must respect when enforcing this Act.

## SEC. 1205. CIVIL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1205.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person who fails to comply with the provisions of this Act shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation per day.

**(b) FACTORS.—**In determining the amount of the penalty, the Secretary shall consider:

**(1)** The nature, circumstances, and extent of the violation
**(2)** The violator's ability to pay
**(3)** The violator's history of previous violations
**(4)** The degree of culpability
**(5)** The economic benefit gained through the violation

**(c) ASSESSMENT.—**The Secretary shall assess civil penalties by written notice to the violator.

**(d) COLLECTION.—**Civil penalties shall be collected by the Secretary of the Treasury.

---

## SEC. 1206. CRIMINAL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1206.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person who willfully violates the provisions of this Act shall be subject to criminal penalties, including:

**(1)** A fine of up to $500,000 per violation
**(2)** Imprisonment for up to 5 years
**(3)** Both fine and imprisonment

**(b) AGGRAVATING FACTORS.—**Criminal penalties shall be enhanced if the violation:

**(1)** Results in injury or death
**(2)** Causes significant environmental damage
**(3)** Involves fraud or deception
**(4)** Is part of a pattern of violations

**(c) PROSECUTION.—**Criminal penalties shall be prosecuted by the Department of Justice.

---

## SEC. 1207. INJUNCTIVE RELIEF

**SEC. 1207.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary may seek injunctive relief in federal district court to compel compliance with this Act.

**(b) TEMPORARY RESTRAINING ORDER.—**The Secretary may seek a temporary restraining order to prevent imminent harm.

**(c) PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION.—**The Secretary may seek a preliminary injunction to maintain the status quo pending a final decision.

**(d) PERMANENT INJUNCTION.—**The Secretary may seek a permanent injunction to permanently compel compliance.

## SEC. 1208. ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES

**SEC. 1208.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary may impose administrative penalties for violations of this Act.

**(b) TYPES.—**Administrative penalties may include:

**(1)** Monetary penalties
**(2)** Compliance orders
**(3)** Permit revocation or suspension
**(4)** Debarment from federal programs
**(5)** Corrective action orders

**(c) PROCEDURE.—**Administrative penalties shall be imposed through a formal administrative process, including:

**(1)** Notice of violation
**(2)** Opportunity to respond
**(3)** Administrative hearing
**(4)** Final decision
**(5)** Judicial review

---

## SEC. 1209. CITIZEN SUITS

**SEC. 1209.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person may commence a civil action against any person alleged to be in violation of this Act.

**(b) NOTICE.—**The plaintiff shall provide 60 days notice to the Secretary and the alleged violator before filing a citizen suit.

**(c) STANDING.—**Any person with standing may file a citizen suit.

**(d) RELIEF.—**The court may grant:

**(1)** Injunctive relief
**(2)** Civil penalties
**(3)** Attorney's fees and costs

---

## SEC. 1210. JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 1210.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this Act may seek judicial review in the United States Court of Appeals for the circuit in which the person resides.

**(b) STANDARD OF REVIEW.—**The court shall set aside any action found to be arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.

**(c) EXHAUSTION.—**A person must exhaust administrative remedies before seeking judicial review.

**(d) VENUE.—**Judicial review shall be in the United States Court of Appeals for the circuit in which the person resides.

**(e) TIMING.—**A petition for judicial review must be filed within 60 days of the final action.

## SEC. 1211. FEDERAL ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1211.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy and the Attorney General, shall enforce the provisions of this Act.

**(b) INVESTIGATIONS.—**The Secretary may conduct investigations of alleged violations of this Act.

**(C) SUBPOENAS.—**The Secretary may issue subpoenas to compel the production of documents and testimony.

**(d) INSPECTIONS.—**The Secretary may conduct inspections to verify compliance with this Act.

---

## SEC. 1212. STATE ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1212.** (a) IN GENERAL.—States shall enforce the provisions of this Act within their jurisdictions.

**(b) STATE IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**States shall develop state implementation plans that include enforcement procedures.

**(c) FEDERAL OVERSIGHT.—**The Secretary shall oversee state enforcement and may take over enforcement if a state fails to adequately enforce this Act.

---

## SEC. 1213. LOCAL ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1213.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Local governments shall enforce the provisions of this Act within their jurisdictions.

**(b) LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Local governments shall develop local implementation plans that include enforcement procedures.

**(c) STATE OVERSIGHT.—**States shall oversee local enforcement.

---

## SEC. 1214. TRIBAL ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1214.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Tribal nations shall enforce the provisions of this Act on Tribal lands.

**(b) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans that include enforcement procedures.

**(c) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate enforcement with Tribal nations through government-to-government consultation.

---

## SEC. 1215. ENFORCEMENT COORDINATION

**SEC. 1215.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate enforcement with:
(A) Department of Energy
(B) Department of Interior
(C) Department of Justice
(D) Environmental Protection Agency

**(b) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state agencies on enforcement.

**(c) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with Tribal nations on enforcement.

---

## SEC. 1216. ENFORCEMENT REPORTING

**SEC. 1216.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on enforcement activities, including:
(A) Number of investigations
(B) Number of violations
(C) Penalties assessed
(D) Compliance rates
(E) Expenditures

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of enforcement activities.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying enforcement data.

---

## SEC. 1217. ENFORCEMENT PREEMPTION

**SEC. 1217.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national enforcement standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt enforcement standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 1218. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1218.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 1219. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 1219.** (a) ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT.—**This title does not amend the Administrative Procedure Act.

**(b) STATE ENFORCEMENT AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state enforcement agencies.

**(c) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.

---
