# TITLE IV — BUILDING STANDARDS

## SEC. 601. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 601.** This title may be cited as the "Fire-Resistant Building Standards Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 602. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO BUILDING STANDARDS

**SEC. 602.** The Congress finds the following with respect to building standards:

**(1) Structure Ignitability**
Structure ignitability is the primary determinant of structure loss during wildfires. Over 90% of structures destroyed in wildfires are ignited by embers, not direct flame contact. Fire-resistant building materials and designs can reduce structure ignition probability by 80%.

**(2) Building Code Deficiency**
Current building codes in most Wildland-Urban Interface jurisdictions do not require fire-resistant construction materials or designs. Only 5 states have adopted comprehensive WUI building codes. The majority of structures in the WUI are built to standards that do not address wildfire risk.

**(3) Retrofit Cost-Effectiveness**
Retrofitting existing structures with fire-resistant materials is cost-effective. The average retrofit cost of $15,000-25,000 per structure is far less than the average wildfire loss of $250,000 per structure. Every dollar invested in fire-resistant construction returns $10 in avoided losses.

**(4) Insurance Incentives**
Insurance companies are increasingly refusing to insure structures in the WUI that do not meet fire-resistant construction standards. Fire-resistant construction can reduce insurance premiums by 30-50%.

**(5) Life Safety**
Fire-resistant construction provides additional time for evacuation, reducing the risk of fatalities. Structures built to fire-resistant standards maintain structural integrity 3x longer during fire exposure.

**(6) Community Resilience**
Fire-resistant construction at the community level reduces the cascading fire spread that destroys entire neighborhoods. Communities with fire-resistant construction standards experience 70% lower structure loss rates.

## SEC. 603. PURPOSES OF TITLE IV

**SEC. 603.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish national fire-resistant building standards for the Wildland-Urban Interface.

**(2)** Require all new construction in the WUI to meet fire-resistant building standards.

**(3)** Provide incentives for retrofitting existing structures in the WUI to meet fire-resistant building standards.

**(4)** Create a national certification program for fire-resistant building materials.

**(5)** Support research and development of new fire-resistant building materials and designs.

**(6)** Establish accountability metrics and reporting requirements to ensure building code compliance.

**(7)** Protect lives and property through fire-resistant construction.

**(8)** Reduce the economic burden of wildfire on individuals, businesses, and governments.

---

## SEC. 604. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE IV

**SEC. 604.** In this title:

**(1) "Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)"**
The zone where structures and other human development meet or intermingle with undeveloped wildland or vegetative fuels, as mapped by the USDA Forest Service SILVIS laboratory.

**(2) "Fire-Resistant Building Material"**
Any construction material that meets or exceeds the Class A fire resistance rating under ASTM E84 and has been certified under the Fire-Resistant Material Certification Program.

**(3) "Ignition-Resistant Construction"**
Building methods and materials designed to resist ignition from ember exposure, radiant heat, and direct flame contact.

**(4) "Ember-Resistant Vent"**
A vent designed to prevent the entry of embers into a structure, with screening of 1/8-inch mesh or smaller.

**(5) "Class A Roof Assembly"**
A roof assembly that meets the highest fire resistance rating under ASTM E108, including non-combustible covering and underlayment.

**(6) "Defensible Space"**
The area within 100 feet of a structure where vegetation and combustible materials are managed to reduce fire spread potential.

## SEC. 605. NATIONAL WUI BUILDING CODE

**SEC. 605.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, the Secretary of Homeland Security, in coordination with the Secretary of Agriculture, shall establish a national building code for the Wildland-Urban Interface.

**(b) REQUIREMENTS.—**The code shall require:

**(1) Roofing**
Class A roof assembly with non-combustible covering and ember-resistant gutter systems.

**(2) Exterior Walls**
Non-combustible siding materials or ignition-resistant treatments, with sealed gaps and joints.

**(3) Windows**
Tempered or multi-pane windows with metal screens to resist ember entry and radiant heat.

**(4) Vents**
Ember-resistant vents with 1/8-inch mesh screening on all attic, foundation, and soffit vents.

**(5) Decks and Fencing**
Non-combustible decking and fencing within 5 feet of structure, or ignition-resistant treatments.

**(6) Eaves and Soffits**
Enclosed eaves and soffits to prevent ember entry.

**(7) Chimneys**
Spark arrestors on all chimneys.

**(8) Garage**
Fire-resistant garage doors with sealed gaps, and fire-resistant walls separating garage from living space.

**(c) APPLICABILITY.—**The code shall apply to:

**(1)** All new construction in the WUI
**(2)** All substantial renovations (exceeding 50% of structure value) in the WUI
**(3)** All new construction within 1 mile of federal lands in high fire risk areas

**(d) COMPLIANCE TIMELINE.—**

**(1)** New construction shall comply with the code upon adoption.
**(2)** Substantial renovations shall comply with the code within 2 years of adoption.
**(3)** Existing structures shall be encouraged to retrofit through incentive programs.

(D) Vent systems

(B) Factory inspection
(C) Certification issuance
(D) Labeling requirements
(E) Periodic re-certification

**(1) PUBLICATION.—**The Secretary shall publish and maintain a list of approved fire-resistant building materials.

**(2) UPDATES.—**The list shall be updated quarterly to include newly certified materials.

## SEC. 607. RETROFIT INCENTIVE PROGRAM

**SEC. 607.** (a) GRANTS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Homeland Security shall provide grants of up to $25,000 per structure for retrofitting existing structures in the WUI to meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(2) LOW-INCOME ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide 100% cost coverage for retrofitting of structures owned by low-income households.

**(b) INSURANCE INCENTIVES.—**

**(1) PREMIUM REDUCTIONS.—**Structures retrofitted to meet fire-resistant construction standards shall qualify for reduced insurance premiums.

**(2) COVERAGE AVAILABILITY.—**Insurers shall make coverage available to structures that meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(c) PROPERTY TAX INCENTIVES.—**

**(1) ASSESSMENT REDUCTION.—**Local governments shall provide property tax assessment reductions for structures retrofitted to meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(2) FEE WAIVERS.—**Local governments shall waive permit fees for fire-resistant retrofits.

## SEC. 608. BUILDING CODE ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 608.** (a) LOCAL ENFORCEMENT.—

**(1) RESPONSIBILITY.—**Local governments shall be responsible for enforcement of the WUI building code.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide training for local building officials on WUI building code enforcement.

**(3) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to local governments for WUI building code implementation.

**(b) STATE OVERSIGHT.—**

**(1) OVERSIGHT.—**States shall oversee local enforcement and report compliance rates to the Secretary annually.

**(2) MODEL ORDINANCES.—**The Secretary shall develop model ordinances for local governments to adopt the WUI building code.

**(c) FUNDING CONDITION.—**

**(1) COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT.—**Federal wildfire prevention funding to states shall be conditioned on adoption and enforcement of the WUI building code.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**States that fail to adopt and enforce the WUI building code shall be subject to reduced federal wildfire prevention funding.

**(d) INSPECTION PROGRAM.—**

**(1) MANDATORY INSPECTIONS.—**Local governments shall conduct mandatory inspections of new construction and substantial renovations in the WUI for compliance with fire-resistant building standards.

**(2) CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY.—**No certificate of occupancy shall be issued for new construction in the WUI without certification of compliance with fire-resistant building standards.

**(3) EXISTING STRUCTURE INSPECTIONS.—**Local governments shall conduct voluntary inspections of existing structures in the WUI to identify retrofit opportunities.
