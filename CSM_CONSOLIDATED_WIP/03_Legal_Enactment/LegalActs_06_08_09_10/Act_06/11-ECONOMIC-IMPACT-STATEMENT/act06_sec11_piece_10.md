## SEC. 1123. ECONOMIC IMPACT PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 1123.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for economic impacts, including:

**(1)** Return on investment by program
**(2)** Job creation by program
**(3)** Economic activity generated
**(4)** Distributional equity

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on economic performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

---

## SEC. 1124. ECONOMIC IMPACT EVALUATION

**SEC. 1124.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of economic impacts every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of economic strategies
(B) Efficiency of economic operations
(C) Equity of economic distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
