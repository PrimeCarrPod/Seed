## SEC. 1125. ECONOMIC IMPACT SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 1125.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of economic impacts, including:

**(1)** Assessment of economic effectiveness
**(2)** Recommendations for reauthorization
**(3)** Proposed modifications to economic structure
**(4)** Assessment of remaining wildfire risk
**(5)** Recommendations for future economic activities

---

## SEC. 1126. ECONOMIC IMPACT TRANSITION

**SEC. 1126.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of economic activities if the authorities are not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF ACTIVITIES.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing economic activities and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.
