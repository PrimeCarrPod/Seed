## SECTION 221. COORDINATION WITH OTHER FEDERAL PROGRAMS

**SEC. 221.** (a) DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE.—**The Secretary of Defense shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on wildfire response activities, including use of military assets for fire suppression.

**(b) DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY.—**The Administrator of FEMA shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on wildfire preparedness, response, and recovery activities.

**(c) DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on forest management, vegetation management, and rural fire prevention activities.

**(d) DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE.—**The Administrator of NOAA shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on weather forecasting, early warning, and climate monitoring activities.

**(e) ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY.—**The Administrator of EPA shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on air quality monitoring, environmental compliance, and health impact assessment.

**(f) NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION.—**The Administrator of NASA shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on satellite-based fire detection and monitoring.

**(g) NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION.—**The Director of NSF shall coordinate with the Secretary of Interior on wildfire research and development activities.

**(h) FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY.—**The Administrator of FEMA shall integrate wildfire preparedness into all-hazards planning and response frameworks.

## SECTION 222. SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 222.** (a) SUNSET.—**Except as provided in subsection (c), this Act shall cease to be effective 10 years after the date of enactment.

**(b) REVIEW FOR REAUTHORIZATION.—**Not later than 8 years after enactment, the President shall submit to Congress a comprehensive evaluation of this Act and recommendations for reauthorization.

**(c) CONTINUING OBLIGATIONS.—**The following provisions shall continue in effect after the sunset date:
(1) Funding commitments made prior to sunset
(2) Reporting requirements for 5 years after sunset
(3) Enforcement actions initiated prior to sunset
(4) Research and development programs in progress

**(d) TRANSFER OF FUNCTIONS.—**Upon sunset, remaining functions shall be transferred to existing federal agencies as determined by the President.
