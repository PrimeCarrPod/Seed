## SECTION 215. RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 215.** (a) FIRE BEHAVIOR RESEARCH.—**There is authorized $500,000,000 for research into fire behavior, climate change impacts, fire spread modeling, and smoke transport.

**(b) SUPPRESSION TECHNOLOGY.—**There is authorized $300,000,000 for development of next-generation fire retardants, autonomous suppression systems, and night operations technology.

**(c) GRID TECHNOLOGY.—**There is authorized $200,000,000 for development of fire-resistant conductor materials, advanced fault detection systems, and microgrid control systems.

**(d) BUILDING TECHNOLOGY.—**There is authorized $200,000,000 for development of fire-resistant materials, structure hardening techniques, and smart home fire detection.

**(e) ECOLOGICAL RESEARCH.—**There is authorized $100,000,000 for research into post-fire ecosystem recovery, prescribed burn effectiveness, and carbon sequestration impacts.

**(f) RESEARCH PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary of Interior, in cooperation with the National Science Foundation, shall establish research partnerships with universities, national laboratories, and private sector entities.

**(g) TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER.—**All technologies developed with funding under this Act shall be made available for commercialization and deployment.

## SECTION 216. INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION

**SEC. 216.** (a) MUTUAL AID AGREEMENTS.—**The Secretary of State shall negotiate and maintain mutual aid agreements with Canada, Australia, the European Union, and other nations for wildfire response.

**(b) TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER.—**The United States shall share wildfire prevention technologies with other nations through existing international cooperation frameworks.

**(c) JOINT RESEARCH.—**The National Science Foundation shall establish joint research programs with international partners on wildfire science and management.

**(d) CAPACITY BUILDING.—**The U.S. Agency for International Development shall provide technical assistance to developing nations for wildfire prevention and response.

**(e) EMISSIONS MONITORING.—**The Environmental Protection Agency shall coordinate with international partners on wildfire emissions monitoring and reporting.

## SECTION 217. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

**SEC. 217.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**Not later than 1 year after enactment, and annually thereafter, the President shall submit to Congress a comprehensive report on the implementation of this Act.

**(b) GAO REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a comprehensive review of this Act's implementation 3 years after enactment and every 5 years thereafter.

**(c) INSPECTOR GENERAL AUDITS.—**The Inspector General of each implementing agency shall conduct annual audits of programs under this Act.

**(d) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary of Interior shall maintain a publicly accessible dashboard displaying program metrics, expenditures, and outcomes in real time.

**(e) STATE REPORTS.—**Each state receiving funds under this Act shall submit annual reports on implementation progress and outcomes.
