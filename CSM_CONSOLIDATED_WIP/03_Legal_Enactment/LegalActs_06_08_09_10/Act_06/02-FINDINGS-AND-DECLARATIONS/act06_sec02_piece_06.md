## SECTION 209. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS

**SEC. 209.** (a) WILDFIRE PREVENTION FUND.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the National Wildfire Prevention Fund established under Sec. 602, $15,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) GRID RESILIENCE PROGRAM.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the Grid Resilience Program established under Sec. 201, $12,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(c) BUILDING STANDARDS AND RETROFIT.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fire-Resistant Building Standards Program established under Sec. 301, $8,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(d) EARLY WARNING SYSTEMS.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the National Early Warning System established under Sec. 401, $3,500,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(e) RESPONSE MODERNIZATION.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the Federal Wildfire Response Modernization Program established under Sec. 501, $6,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(f) ADMINISTRATIVE COSTS.—There is authorized to be appropriated for administrative costs under this Act, $1,500,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(g) TOTAL AUTHORIZATION.—**The total amount authorized to be appropriated under this Act is $46,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

## SECTION 210. OFFSET PROVISIONS

**SEC. 210.** (a) CARBON PRICING.—There is hereby established a carbon pricing mechanism that imposes a fee of $25 per metric ton of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) emissions from fossil fuel combustion, increasing by $5 per year annually.

**(b) FOSSIL FUEL SUBSIDIREDIRECTION.—**The following tax preferences for fossil fuel production and consumption are hereby repealed:
(1) Intangible drilling costs deduction (26 U.S.C. §263(c))
(2) Percentage depletion for oil and gas wells (26 U.S.C. §613A)
(3) Domestic manufacturing deduction for fossil fuels (26 U.S.C. §199)
(4) Enhanced oil recovery credit (26 U.S.C. §43)
(5) Geological and geophysical amortization (26 U.S.C. §167(h))

**(c) GREEN BONDS.—**The Secretary of Treasury is authorized to issue up to $12,000,000,000 in Climate Resilience Green Bonds, with proceeds deposited in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund.

**(d) GENERAL FUND.—**There is authorized to be appropriated from the General Fund of the Treasury $8,000,000,000 for the purposes of this Act.

**(e) NET COST.—**The net cost to the federal government of this Act is $0, as the $46,000,000,000 in authorizations are fully offset by $46,000,000,000 in revenues and savings.

## SECTION 211. TRIBAL CONSULTATION

**SEC. 211.** (a) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—The Secretary of Interior shall initiate government-to-government consultation with Tribal nations within 90 days of enactment to develop Tribal implementation plans.

**(b) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish Tribal sovereignty or federal trust responsibilities.

**(c) TRIBAL SET-ASIDE.—**Ten percent of all grant funds under this Act shall be reserved for Tribal nations.

**(d) TRADITIONAL ECOLOGICAL KNOWLEDGE.—**Traditional ecological knowledge shall be incorporated into all planning and implementation activities under this Act.
