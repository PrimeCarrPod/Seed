## SECTION 219. ADAPTIVE MANAGEMENT

**SEC. 219.** (a) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Secretary of Interior shall conduct a biennial review of all programs under this Act and recommend adjustments based on performance data and new scientific information.

**(b) PROGRAM MODIFICATION.—**Based on the biennial review, the Secretary of Interior may modify program requirements, funding allocations, and performance targets with 90 days notice to Congress.

**(c) EMERGENCY ADJUSTMENTS.—**In the event of a catastrophic wildfire season, the Secretary of Interior may make emergency adjustments to program implementation without prior notice, with notification to Congress within 72 hours.

**(d) STAKEHOLDER INPUT.—**The biennial review shall include input from state, Tribal, local, and private stakeholders, as well as the scientific community.
