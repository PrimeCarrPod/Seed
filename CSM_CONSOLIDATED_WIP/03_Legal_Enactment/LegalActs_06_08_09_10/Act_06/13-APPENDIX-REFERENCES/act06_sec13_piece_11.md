## SEC. 1314. APPENDIX J — ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE ANALYSIS

**SEC. 1314.** (a) DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 40% of Fund expenditures shall benefit disadvantaged communities.

**(2) PRIORITY.—**Disadvantaged communities shall receive priority for:
(A) Defensible space assistance
(B) Fire-resistant retrofit grants
(C) Job training and employment
(D) Community Wildfire Protection Plan development
(E) Fire-Adapted Communities certification

**(b) TRIBAL COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures shall benefit Tribal communities.

**(2) PRIORITY.—**Tribal communities shall receive priority for:
(A) Cultural burning programs
(B) Tribal wildfire prevention funding
(C) Technical assistance and training
(D) Co-management agreements

**(c) RURAL COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 20% of Fund expenditures shall benefit rural communities.

**(2) PRIORITY.—**Rural communities shall receive priority for:
(A) Vegetation management assistance
(B) Grid hardening assistance
(C) Volunteer fire department support
(D) Economic development programs

## SEC. 1315. APPENDIX K — TRIBAL CONSULTATION REQUIREMENTS

**SEC. 1315.** (a) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on all activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) TIMING.—**Consultation shall occur at the earliest possible stage of planning.

**(b) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—

**(1) DEVELOPMENT.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(2) APPROVAL.—**Tribal implementation plans shall be approved by the Secretary.

**(c) CULTURAL BURNING PROTECTION.—

**(1) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to limit the right of Tribal nations to conduct cultural burns on their lands.

**(2) CO-MANAGEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for cultural burning on federal lands within their ancestral territories.
