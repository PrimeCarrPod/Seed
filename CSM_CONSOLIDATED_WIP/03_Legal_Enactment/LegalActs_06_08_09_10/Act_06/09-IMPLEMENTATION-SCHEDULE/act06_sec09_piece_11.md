## SEC. 924. IMPLEMENTATION PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 924.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for implementation, including:

**(1)** Milestone completion rate
**(2)** Expenditure efficiency
**(3)** Program effectiveness
**(4)** Stakeholder satisfaction
**(5)** Risk reduction achieved

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on implementation performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

## SEC. 925. IMPLEMENTATION EVALUATION

**SEC. 925.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of implementation every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of implementation strategies
(B) Efficiency of implementation operations
(C) Equity of implementation distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
