## SEC. 926. IMPLEMENTATION SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 926.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of implementation, including:

**(1)** Assessment of implementation effectiveness
**(2)** Recommendations for reauthorization
**(3)** Proposed modifications to implementation structure
**(4)** Assessment of remaining wildfire risk
**(5)** Recommendations for future implementation

---

## SEC. 927. IMPLEMENTATION TRANSITION

**SEC. 927.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of implementation activities if the authorities are not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF PROJECTS.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing projects and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.

---

## SEC. 928. IMPLEMENTATION SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 928.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
