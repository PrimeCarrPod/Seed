## SEC. 1227. ENFORCEMENT SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 1227.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of enforcement activities, including:

**(1)** Assessment of enforcement effectiveness
**(2)** Recommendations for reauthorization
**(3)** Proposed modifications to enforcement structure
**(4)** Assessment of remaining wildfire risk
**(5)** Recommendations for future enforcement activities

---

## SEC. 1228. ENFORCEMENT TRANSITION

**SEC. 1228.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of enforcement activities if the authorities are not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF ACTIVITIES.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing enforcement activities and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.

---

## SEC. 1229. ENFORCEMENT SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1229.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
