## SEC. 1223. ENFORCEMENT FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 1223.** (a) HOSPITALS AND MEDICAL FACILITIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce fire-resistant construction standards for hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Hospitals and medical facilities that fail to comply with fire-resistant construction standards shall be subject to civil penalties.

**(b) EMERGENCY RESPONSE FACILITIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce fire-resistant construction standards for emergency response facilities in the WUI.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Emergency response facilities that fail to comply with fire-resistant construction standards shall be subject to civil penalties.

**(c) WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce fire-resistant construction standards for water and wastewater systems in the WUI.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Water and wastewater systems that fail to comply with fire-resistant construction standards shall be subject to civil penalties.
