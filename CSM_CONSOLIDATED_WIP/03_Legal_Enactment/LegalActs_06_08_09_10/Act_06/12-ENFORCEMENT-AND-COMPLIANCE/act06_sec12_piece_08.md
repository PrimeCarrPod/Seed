## SEC. 1225. ENFORCEMENT PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 1225.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for enforcement, including:

**(1)** Investigation completion rate
**(2)** Violation resolution rate
**(3)** Penalty collection rate
**(4)** Compliance rate
**(5)** Stakeholder satisfaction

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on enforcement performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

---

## SEC. 1226. ENFORCEMENT EVALUATION

**SEC. 1226.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of enforcement every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of enforcement strategies
(B) Efficiency of enforcement operations
(C) Equity of enforcement distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
