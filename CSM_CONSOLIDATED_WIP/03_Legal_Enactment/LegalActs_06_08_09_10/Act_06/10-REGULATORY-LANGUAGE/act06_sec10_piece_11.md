## SEC. 1031. AMENDMENTS TO OSHA ACT

**SEC. 1031.** The Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 (29 U.S.C. 651 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 31. WILDFIRE PREVENTION WORKER SAFETY.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall establish occupational safety standards for wildfire prevention workers, including:
(A) Heat illness prevention
(B) Smoke exposure limits
(C) Fatigue management
(D) Personal protective equipment standards
(E) Emergency response procedures

**(b) TRAINING.—**All wildfire prevention workers shall complete mandatory safety training before field assignments."

## SEC. 1032. AMENDMENTS TO FAIR LABOR STANDARDS ACT

**SEC. 1032.** The Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 201 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 21. WILDFIRE PREVENTION WORKER COMPENSATION.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**Wildfire prevention workers shall receive:
(A) Overtime compensation for hours worked in excess of 40 hours per week
(B) Hazard pay for work during extreme fire weather conditions
(C) Premium pay for night work

**(b) BENEFITS.—**Wildfire prevention workers shall receive:
(A) Comprehensive health insurance
(B) Mental health coverage
(C) Workers' compensation coverage"
