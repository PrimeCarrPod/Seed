## SEC. 1029. REGULATORY PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 1029.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for regulatory activities, including:

**(1)** Rulemaking completion rate
**(2)** Compliance rate
**(3)** Enforcement action rate
**(4)** Stakeholder satisfaction
**(5)** Risk reduction achieved

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on regulatory performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

---

## SEC. 1030. REGULATORY EVALUATION

**SEC. 1030.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of regulatory activities every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of regulatory strategies
(B) Efficiency of regulatory operations
(C) Equity of regulatory distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
