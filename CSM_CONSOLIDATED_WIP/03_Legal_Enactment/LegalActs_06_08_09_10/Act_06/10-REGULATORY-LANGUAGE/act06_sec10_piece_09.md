## SEC. 1025. AMENDMENTS TO CONGRESSIONAL BUDGET ACT

**SEC. 1025.** The Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 U.S.C. 601 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 107. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND GRID RESILIENCE FUNDING.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the National Wildfire Prevention Fund $10,000,000,000 annually for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) EMERGENCY APPROPRIATIONS.—**There is authorized to be appropriated an additional $5,000,000,000 annually for emergency wildfire prevention and response activities.

**(c) AVAILABILITY.—**Funds appropriated under this section shall remain available until expended."

## SEC. 1026. AMENDMENTS TO ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT

**SEC. 1026.** The Administrative Procedure Act (5 U.S.C. 500 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 91. WILDFIRE PREVENTION RULEMAKING.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy and the Secretary of Interior, shall promulgate rules to implement the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) EXPEDITED RULEMAKING.—**The Secretary may use expedited rulemaking procedures for rules necessary to implement this Act.

**(c) PUBLIC COMMENT.—**The Secretary shall provide for public comment on all rules promulgated under this Act."

---

## SEC. 1027. REGULATORY SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 1027.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of regulatory activities, including:

**(1)** Assessment of regulatory effectiveness
**(2)** Recommendations for reauthorization
**(3)** Proposed modifications to regulatory structure
**(4)** Assessment of remaining wildfire risk
**(5)** Recommendations for future regulatory activities

---

## SEC. 1028. REGULATORY TRANSITION

**SEC. 1028.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of regulatory activities if the authorities are not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF ACTIVITIES.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing regulatory activities and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.
