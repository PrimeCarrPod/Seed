## SEC. 312. WILDFIRE PREVENTION TECHNOLOGY PROGRAM

**SEC. 312.** (a) TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT.—

**(1) RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT.—**The Secretary shall conduct a comprehensive research and development program for wildfire prevention technologies, including:
(A) AI-powered ignition detection systems
(B) Satellite-based fuel moisture monitoring
(C) Drone-based prescribed fire ignition
(D) Automated defensible space assessment
(E) Predictive fire behavior modeling
(F) Remote sensing for vegetation management planning
(G) Advanced fire-resistant materials
(H) Smart grid technologies for ignition prevention

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500 million annually for wildfire prevention technology research and development.

**(b) TECHNOLOGY DEPLOYMENT.—**

**(1) FIRE DETECTION CAMERA NETWORK.—**The Secretary shall deploy a network of 10,000 AI-powered fire detection cameras in high fire risk areas within 3 years.

**(2) SATELLITE MONITORING SYSTEM.—**The Secretary shall deploy a satellite-based monitoring system capable of detecting fire starts within 2 minutes and monitoring fuel moisture levels daily.

**(3) DRONE PRESCRIBED FIRE PROGRAM.—**The Secretary shall establish a drone-based prescribed fire ignition program capable of igniting 1 million acres annually.

**(4) AUTOMATED DEFENSIBLE SPACE ASSESSMENT.—**The Secretary shall deploy automated defensible space assessment technology using satellite imagery and drone surveys.

**(c) DATA AND ANALYTICS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREVENTION DATABASE.—**The Secretary shall establish a national database for wildfire prevention data, including:
(A) Vegetation treatment records
(B) Defensible space compliance data
(C) Ignition detection records
(D) Community preparedness assessments
(E) Technology performance metrics

**(2) PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS.—**The Secretary shall develop predictive analytics tools for wildfire prevention, including:
(A) Fire risk forecasting
(B) Treatment prioritization algorithms
(C) Resource allocation optimization
(D) Performance measurement dashboards

**(d) TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER.—**

**(1) PRIVATE SECTOR PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with private sector technology companies to accelerate the development and deployment of wildfire prevention technologies.

**(2) INNOVATION CHALLENGES.—**The Secretary shall conduct innovation challenges with prize awards for breakthrough wildfire prevention technologies.

**(3) DEPLOYMENT GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to state and local governments for the deployment of proven wildfire prevention technologies.
