## SEC. 313. ACCOUNTABILITY AND REPORTING

**SEC. 313.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress an annual performance report on the wildfire prevention programs established under this title.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Acres treated by treatment type and location
(B) Defensible space compliance rates by jurisdiction
(C) Prescribed fire acres completed vs. targets
(D) Cultural burning acres completed
(E) Ignition detection system performance
(F) Community Wildfire Protection Plan status
(G) Fire-Adapted Communities certifications
(H) Workforce hiring and retention data
(I) Technology deployment progress
(J) Expenditures by program and activity
(K) Performance metrics and trend analysis
(L) Recommendations for program improvements

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General of the United States shall conduct a biennial review of the wildfire prevention programs established under this title.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Program effectiveness in reducing wildfire risk
(B) Efficiency of program operations
(C) Adequacy of funding levels
(D) Compliance with statutory requirements
(E) Achievement of performance targets
(F) Recommendations for legislative changes

**(c) INSPECTOR GENERAL OVERSIGHT.—**

**(1) AUDITS.—**The Inspector General of the Department of Agriculture shall conduct annual audits of wildfire prevention program expenditures.

**(2) INVESTIGATIONS.—**The Inspector General shall investigate any allegations of waste, fraud, or abuse in wildfire prevention programs.

**(d) PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying real-time wildfire prevention program data.

**(2) PUBLIC COMMENT.—**The Secretary shall provide for public comment on the annual performance report and biennial GAO review.

## SEC. 314. ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

**SEC. 314.** (a) NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ACT.—

**(1) PROGRAMMATIC ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STATEMENT.—**The Secretary shall prepare a programmatic environmental impact statement for the wildfire prevention programs established under this title.

**(2) CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.—**The Secretary shall develop categorical exclusions for routine wildfire prevention activities, including:
(A) Defensible space creation within 100 feet of existing structures
(B) Prescribed fire in accordance with approved prescription plans
(C) Mechanical thinning of trees less than 12 inches diameter
(D) Fuel break construction in previously treated areas

**(b) ENDANGERED SPECIES ACT.—**

**(1) PROGRAMMATIC BIOLOGICAL OPINION.—**The Secretary shall obtain a programmatic biological opinion for wildfire prevention activities.

**(2) CONSERVATION MEASURES.—**Wildfire prevention activities shall incorporate conservation measures for threatened and endangered species.

**(c) CLEAN WATER ACT.—**

**(1) STORMWATER MANAGEMENT.—**Wildfire prevention activities shall incorporate stormwater management measures to prevent erosion and water quality impacts.

**(2) BUFFER ZONES.—**Vegetation management activities shall maintain buffer zones around waterways and wetlands.
