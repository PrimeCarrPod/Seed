## SEC. 307. PRESCRIBED FIRE EXPANSION PROGRAM

**SEC. 307.** (a) FINDINGS.—The Congress finds that:

**(1)** Prescribed fire is the most cost-effective and ecologically appropriate vegetation management tool available, costing $50-500 per acre compared to $500-5,000 per acre for mechanical treatment.

**(2)** Indigenous peoples have used fire as a land management tool for over 10,000 years, maintaining healthy fire-adapted ecosystems.

**(3)** The exclusion of fire from fire-adapted ecosystems over the past century has created the current wildfire crisis.

**(4)** Prescribed fire application must increase by at least 300% to address the current fuel backlog.

**(b) PRESCRIBED FIRE TARGETS.—**

**(1) FEDERAL LANDS.—**The Secretary of Agriculture and Secretary of Interior shall collectively apply prescribed fire to a minimum of 5 million acres annually by year 3 and 10 million acres annually by year 7.

**(2) STATE AND PRIVATE LANDS.—**The Secretary shall provide technical and financial assistance to achieve prescribed fire application on 3 million acres of state and private lands annually by year 5.

**(c) PRESCRIBED FIRE LIABILITY REFORM.—**

**(1) FEDERAL LIABILITY.—**The United States shall assume liability for prescribed fire conducted in accordance with an approved prescription plan by federal agencies or their contractors.

**(2) STATE LIABILITY PROTECTION.—**The Secretary shall provide model legislation for states to limit liability for prescribed fire conducted in accordance with certified prescription plans.

**(3) CERTIFICATION PROGRAM.—**The Secretary shall establish a national prescribed fire certification program for prescribed fire practitioners, including training requirements, experience standards, and continuing education.

**(d) AIR QUALITY COORDINATION.—**

**(1) INTERAGREEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enter into agreements with the Environmental Protection Agency and state air quality agencies to coordinate prescribed fire smoke management.

**(2) SMOKE EXEMPTION.—**Prescribed fire conducted in accordance with an approved prescription plan shall be exempt from state air quality standards during the approved burn window, provided that best smoke management practices are employed.

**(3) MONITORING.—**The Secretary shall deploy smoke monitoring equipment in prescribed fire areas to ensure smoke impacts are minimized.

**(e) CULTURAL BURNING PROGRAM.—**

**(1) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit the right of Tribal nations to conduct cultural burns on their lands.

**(2) CO-MANAGEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for cultural burning on federal lands within their ancestral territories.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500 million annually for the cultural burning program, including funding for Tribal burn practitioners, equipment, and training.
