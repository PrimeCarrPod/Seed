## SEC. 615. BUILDING STANDARDS ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 615.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress an annual performance report on WUI building code implementation.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Building code adoption rates by state and locality
(B) Compliance rates for new construction
(C) Retrofit activity data
(D) Structure loss data
(E) Workforce data
(F) Expenditures by program
(G) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of WUI building code implementation.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Program effectiveness in reducing structure loss
(B) Efficiency of program operations
(C) Adequacy of funding levels
(D) Compliance with standards
(E) Achievement of targets

## SEC. 616. BUILDING STANDARDS PREEMPTION

**SEC. 616.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national WUI building code established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local building codes, except where state or local codes are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States and local governments may adopt building codes that are more protective than the national WUI building code.

---

## SEC. 617. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 617.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 618. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 618.** (a) NATIONAL HOUSING ACT.—**This title amends the National Housing Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) STATE BUILDING CODE AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state and local governments to adopt more protective building codes.

**(c) INSURANCE REGULATION.—**This title does not change the regulation of insurance by state insurance commissioners.
