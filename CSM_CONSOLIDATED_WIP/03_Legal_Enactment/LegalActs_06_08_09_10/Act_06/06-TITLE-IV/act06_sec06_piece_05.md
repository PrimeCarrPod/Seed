## SEC. 608. BUILDING CODE ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 608.** (a) LOCAL ENFORCEMENT.—

**(1) RESPONSIBILITY.—**Local governments shall be responsible for enforcement of the WUI building code.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide training for local building officials on WUI building code enforcement.

**(3) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to local governments for WUI building code implementation.

**(b) STATE OVERSIGHT.—**

**(1) OVERSIGHT.—**States shall oversee local enforcement and report compliance rates to the Secretary annually.

**(2) MODEL ORDINANCES.—**The Secretary shall develop model ordinances for local governments to adopt the WUI building code.

**(c) FUNDING CONDITION.—**

**(1) COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT.—**Federal wildfire prevention funding to states shall be conditioned on adoption and enforcement of the WUI building code.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**States that fail to adopt and enforce the WUI building code shall be subject to reduced federal wildfire prevention funding.

**(d) INSPECTION PROGRAM.—**

**(1) MANDATORY INSPECTIONS.—**Local governments shall conduct mandatory inspections of new construction and substantial renovations in the WUI for compliance with fire-resistant building standards.

**(2) CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY.—**No certificate of occupancy shall be issued for new construction in the WUI without certification of compliance with fire-resistant building standards.

**(3) EXISTING STRUCTURE INSPECTIONS.—**Local governments shall conduct voluntary inspections of existing structures in the WUI to identify retrofit opportunities.

## SEC. 609. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION WORKFORCE

**SEC. 609.** (a) WORKFORCE EXPANSION.—

**(1) HIRING TARGET.—**The Secretary shall establish a target of 50,000 new fire-resistant construction workers within 5 years.

**(2) POSITIONS.—**Fire-resistant construction workers shall include:
(A) Carpenters
(B) Roofers
(C) Glaziers
(D) Insulation installers
(E) Building inspectors
(F) Architects and designers

**(b) TRAINING PROGRAMS.—**

**(1) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAMS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for fire-resistant construction careers.

**(2) COMMUNITY COLLEGE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with community colleges to develop fire-resistant construction certificate and degree programs.

**(3) CONTINUING EDUCATION.—**The Secretary shall provide continuing education for building professionals on fire-resistant construction techniques.
