## SEC. 620. BUILDING STANDARDS SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

**SEC. 620.** (a) CONSTRUCTION SAFETY.—

**(1) OSHA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary of Labor shall ensure that fire-resistant construction activities comply with OSHA safety standards.

**(2) TRAINING.—**All fire-resistant construction workers shall complete mandatory safety training.

**(b) BUILDING SAFETY.—

**(1) STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY.—**Fire-resistant building standards shall not compromise structural integrity or seismic safety.

**(2) FIRE DEPARTMENT ACCESS.—**Fire-resistant building designs shall maintain fire department access and ventilation capabilities.

**(3) EGRESS.—**Fire-resistant building designs shall maintain adequate egress for occupants during fire events.

## SEC. 621. BUILDING STANDARDS ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW

**SEC. 621.** (a) STREAMLINED REVIEW.—

**(1) CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish categorical exclusions for routine fire-resistant construction activities.

**(2) PROGRAMMATIC REVIEWS.—**The Secretary shall conduct programmatic environmental reviews for fire-resistant construction programs.

**(b) MITIGATION.—

**(1) HABITAT MITIGATION.—**Fire-resistant construction activities that impact wildlife habitat shall include habitat mitigation measures.

**(2) CULTURAL RESOURCE PROTECTION.—**Fire-resistant construction activities shall include measures to protect cultural resources.

## SEC. 622. BUILDING STANDARDS COORDINATION

**SEC. 622.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) FEDERAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Homeland Security shall coordinate fire-resistant building standards with:
(A) Department of Agriculture
(B) Department of Housing and Urban Development
(C) Department of Commerce
(D) Environmental Protection Agency

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state building code agencies on fire-resistant building standards implementation.

**(b) INDUSTRY COORDINATION.—**

**(1) BUILDING INDUSTRY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with the building industry on fire-resistant construction standards and techniques.

**(2) INSURANCE INDUSTRY COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with the insurance industry on fire-resistant construction incentives.
