## SEC. 626. BUILDING STANDARDS FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 626.** (a) HOSPITALS AND MEDICAL FACILITIES.—

**(1) FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION.—**All new hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI shall be constructed to fire-resistant standards.

**(2) RETROFIT REQUIREMENT.—**Existing hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI shall be retrofitted to fire-resistant standards within 5 years.

**(b) EMERGENCY RESPONSE FACILITIES.—

**(1) FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION.—**All new emergency response facilities in the WUI shall be constructed to fire-resistant standards.

**(2) RETROFIT REQUIREMENT.—**Existing emergency response facilities in the WUI shall be retrofitted to fire-resistant standards within 5 years.

**(c) SCHOOLS.—

**(1) FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION.—**All new schools in the WUI shall be constructed to fire-resistant standards.

**(2) RETROFIT REQUIREMENT.—**Existing schools in the WUI shall be retrofitted to fire-resistant standards within 10 years.

## SEC. 627. BUILDING STANDARDS FOR AFFORDABLE HOUSING

**SEC. 627.** (a) AFFORDABLE HOUSING REQUIREMENTS.—

**(1) FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION.—**All new affordable housing in the WUI shall be constructed to fire-resistant standards.

**(2) FUNDING ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide funding assistance for fire-resistant construction of affordable housing.

**(b) LOW-INCOME RETROFIT PROGRAM.—

**(1) 100% COST COVERAGE.—**The Secretary shall provide 100% cost coverage for fire-resistant retrofits of low-income housing.

**(2) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance for fire-resistant retrofits of low-income housing.
