## SEC. 612. TRIBAL BUILDING STANDARDS PROGRAM

**SEC. 612.** (a) TRIBAL BUILDING STANDARDS FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Tribal Building Standards Fund" to provide financial assistance for fire-resistant construction on Tribal lands.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $1,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**

**(1) TRIBAL HOUSING OFFICES.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to Tribal nations for fire-resistant construction planning and implementation.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide training for Tribal members on fire-resistant construction techniques.

**(c) PRIORITY.—**Tribal fire-resistant construction projects shall receive priority for federal funding, with the federal share not exceeding 95%.

## SEC. 613. RURAL BUILDING STANDARDS PROGRAM

**SEC. 613.** (a) RURAL BUILDING STANDARDS FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Rural Building Standards Fund" to provide financial assistance for fire-resistant construction in rural areas.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $2,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) PRIVATE HOMEOWNER ASSISTANCE.—**

**(1) GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to private homeowners for fire-resistant retrofits, with priority for low-income households.

**(2) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to private homeowners for fire-resistant construction planning.

## SEC. 614. BUILDING STANDARDS INNOVATION

**SEC. 614.** (a) INNOVATION CHALLENGES.—

**(1) FIRE-RESISTANT MATERIAL CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a fire-resistant building material that costs no more than conventional materials.

**(2) RETROFIT COST REDUCTION CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a retrofit technique that reduces retrofit costs by 50%.

**(b) RESEARCH PARTNERSHIPS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL LABORATORY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with national laboratories on fire-resistant construction research.

**(2) UNIVERSITY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with universities on fire-resistant construction research.
