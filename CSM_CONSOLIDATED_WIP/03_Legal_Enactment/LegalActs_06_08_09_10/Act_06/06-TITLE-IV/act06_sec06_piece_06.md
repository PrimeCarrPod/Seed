## SEC. 610. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION RESEARCH

**SEC. 610.** (a) RESEARCH PROGRAM.—

**(1) MATERIALS RESEARCH.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall conduct research on fire-resistant building materials, including:
(A) Advanced roofing materials
(B) Fire-resistant siding systems
(C) Impact-resistant window systems
(D) Ember-resistant vent systems
(F) Fire-resistant insulation
(G) Fire-resistant sealants

**(2) DESIGN RESEARCH.—**The Secretary shall conduct research on fire-resistant building design, including:
(A) Structure spacing and layout
(B) Landscaping for fire resistance
(C) Community-scale fire resistance
(D) Cost-effective retrofit techniques

**(b) RESEARCH FACILITIES.—**

**(1) FIRE RESEARCH LABORATORY.—**The Secretary shall maintain and expand fire research laboratories.

**(2) FIELD TESTING.—**The Secretary shall conduct field testing of fire-resistant building materials and designs.

**(c) TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER.—**

**(1) INDUSTRY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with private sector companies to commercialize fire-resistant building technologies.

**(2) BUILDING PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION.—**The Secretary shall provide education to building professionals on fire-resistant construction techniques.

## SEC. 611. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION DATA

**SEC. 611.** (a) NATIONAL WUI BUILDING DATABASE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish a national database for WUI building data, including:
(A) Building code compliance data
(B) Retrofit activity data
(C) Structure loss data
(D) Insurance claims data
(E) Material certification data

**(2) DATA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary shall establish data standards for WUI building data collection and reporting.

**(b) PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS.—**

**(1) STRUCTURE IGNITION MODELING.—**The Secretary shall develop predictive models for structure ignition risk, incorporating:
(A) Building materials and design
(B) Surrounding vegetation
(C) Weather conditions
(D) Topography

**(2) RETROFIT PRIORITIZATION.—**The Secretary shall develop tools for prioritizing retrofit investments based on ignition risk.

**(c) PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying WUI building code compliance and retrofit data.

**(2) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on WUI building code implementation.
