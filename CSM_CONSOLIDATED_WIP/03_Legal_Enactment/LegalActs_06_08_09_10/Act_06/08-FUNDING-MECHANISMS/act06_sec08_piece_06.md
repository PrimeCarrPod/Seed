## SEC. 811. EQUITABLE DISTRIBUTION

**SEC. 811.** (a) DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITY PRIORITY.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 40% of Fund expenditures shall benefit disadvantaged communities.

**(2) DEFINITION.—**For purposes of this section, "disadvantaged community" means any community identified as disadvantaged under the Justice40 Initiative, CalEnviroScreen, or equivalent state screening tool.

**(b) RURAL COMMUNITY PRIORITY.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 20% of Fund expenditures shall benefit rural communities.

**(2) DEFINITION.—**For purposes of this section, "rural community" means any community with a population of less than 50,000 that is not part of a metropolitan statistical area.

**(c) TRIBAL COMMUNITY PRIORITY.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures shall benefit Tribal communities.

**(2) CONSULTATION.—**Fund expenditures benefiting Tribal communities shall be developed in consultation with the affected Tribal nations.

## SEC. 812. FUND ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 812.** (a) PERFORMANCE METRICS.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for Fund expenditures, including:
(A) Acres treated per dollar spent
(B) Structures protected per dollar spent
(C) Ignition risk reduction per dollar spent
(D) Community preparedness improvement per dollar spent

**(2) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on Fund performance against established metrics.

**(b) INSPECTOR GENERAL OVERSIGHT.—

**(1) AUDITS.—**The Inspector General of the Department of Agriculture shall conduct annual audits of Fund expenditures.

**(2) INVESTIGATIONS.—**The Inspector General shall investigate any allegations of waste, fraud, or abuse in Fund expenditures.

**(c) WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTIONS.—

**(1) PROTECTIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish whistleblower protections for persons who report waste, fraud, or abuse in Fund expenditures.

**(2) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall establish a hotline for reporting waste, fraud, or abuse.

## SEC. 813. FUND TRANSPARENCY

**SEC. 813.** (a) PUBLIC DATABASE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish a public database of all Fund expenditures, including:
(A) Grant recipients
(B) Loan recipients
(C) Project descriptions
(D) Expenditure amounts
(E) Performance outcomes

**(2) ACCESSIBILITY.—**The database shall be accessible to the public through a user-friendly online interface.

**(b) ANNUAL REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on Fund activities.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Fund receipts and expenditures
(B) Grant and loan activity
(C) Performance outcomes
(D) Equity analysis
(E) Recommendations for improvement
