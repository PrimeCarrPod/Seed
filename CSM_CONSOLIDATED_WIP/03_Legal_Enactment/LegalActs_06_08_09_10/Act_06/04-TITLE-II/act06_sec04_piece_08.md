## SEC. 410. GRID HARDENING WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 410.** (a) WORKFORCE EXPANSION.—

**(1) HIRING TARGET.—**The Secretary of Energy shall establish a target of 100,000 new grid hardening workers within 5 years.

**(2) POSITIONS.—**Grid hardening workers shall include:
(A) Lineworkers
(B) Electrical engineers
(C) Vegetation management specialists
(D) Equipment technicians
(E) Project managers
(F) Safety inspectors
(G) GIS technicians

**(b) TRAINING PROGRAMS.—**

**(1) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAMS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for grid hardening careers, combining classroom instruction with field experience.

**(2) COMMUNITY COLLEGE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with community colleges to develop grid hardening certificate and degree programs.

**(3) VETERAN HIRING.—**The Secretary shall give hiring preference to veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces for grid hardening positions.

**(c) SAFETY TRAINING.—**

**(1) MANDATORY CERTIFICATION.—**All grid hardening workers shall complete mandatory safety certification before working on electrical infrastructure.

**(2) CONTINUING EDUCATION.—**Grid hardening workers shall complete annual continuing education on safety practices and new technologies.

## SEC. 411. GRID HARDENING TECHNOLOGY PROGRAM

**SEC. 411.** (a) RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT.—

**(1) ADVANCED CONDUCTORS.—**The Secretary shall fund research and development of advanced conductor technologies, including:
(A) High-temperature low-sag conductors
(B) Composite core conductors
(C) Superconducting cables
(D) Self-monitoring conductors

**(2) IGNITION DETECTION.—**The Secretary shall fund research and development of advanced ignition detection technologies, including:
(A) Arc detection sensors
(B) Thermal imaging cameras
(C) AI-powered fault prediction
(D) Drone-based line inspection

**(b) TECHNOLOGY DEPLOYMENT.—**

**(1) DEMONSTRATION PROJECTS.—**The Secretary shall fund demonstration projects for advanced grid hardening technologies.

**(2) DEPLOYMENT GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to utilities for the deployment of proven grid hardening technologies.
