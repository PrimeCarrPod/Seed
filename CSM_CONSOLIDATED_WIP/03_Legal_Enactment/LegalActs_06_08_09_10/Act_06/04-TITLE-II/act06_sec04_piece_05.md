## SEC. 407. GRID HARDENING FUNDING PROGRAM

**SEC. 407.** (a) NATIONAL GRID HARDENING FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "National Grid Hardening Fund" to provide financial assistance for grid hardening activities.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $30,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) COST-SHARE PROGRAM.—**

**(1) FEDERAL COST-SHARE.—**The Secretary of Energy shall provide federal cost-share assistance for grid hardening projects, with the federal share not exceeding:
(A) 75% for investor-owned utilities
(B) 85% for publicly owned utilities
(C) 90% for rural electric cooperatives
(D) 95% for Tribal utilities

**(2) PRIORITY.—**Cost-share assistance shall be prioritized for:
(A) Highest fire risk areas
(B) Disadvantaged communities
(C) Aging infrastructure
(D) High-ignition-risk equipment

**(c) GRANT PROGRAM.—**

**(1) COMPETITIVE GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide competitive grants for grid hardening projects, with priority for projects that:
(A) Achieve the greatest ignition risk reduction per dollar invested
(B) Serve disadvantaged communities
(C) Deploy innovative technologies
(D) Create the greatest number of jobs

**(2) FORMULA GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide formula grants to states for grid hardening, allocated based on:
(A) Fire risk acreage
(B) Miles of overhead line in high fire risk areas
(C) Population in high fire risk areas
(D) Historical ignition data

**(d) LOAN PROGRAM.—**

**(1) LOW-INTEREST LOANS.—**The Secretary shall provide low-interest loans for grid hardening projects, with interest rates not exceeding 2% and terms up to 30 years.

**(2) LOAN FORGIVENESS.—**The Secretary may forgive up to 50% of loan principal for projects that achieve exceptional risk reduction or serve disadvantaged communities.

**(e) RATE RECOVERY.—**

**(1) FEDERAL GUARANTEE.—**The Secretary shall provide federal guarantees for utility rate recovery of grid hardening costs, ensuring that utilities can recover prudent hardening investments through rates.

**(2) RATEPAYER PROTECTION.—**Grid hardening costs recovered through rates shall be subject to reasonableness review by state public utilities commissions.
