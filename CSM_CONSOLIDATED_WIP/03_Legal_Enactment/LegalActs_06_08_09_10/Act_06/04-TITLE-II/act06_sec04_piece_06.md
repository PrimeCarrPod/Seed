## SEC. 408. GRID MODERNIZATION AND RESILIENCE

**SEC. 408.** (a) GRID MODERNIZATION PROGRAM.—

**(1) ADVANCED GRID TECHNOLOGIES.—**The Secretary of Energy shall establish a program to deploy advanced grid technologies in high fire risk areas, including:
(A) Advanced distribution management systems
(B) Fault location, isolation, and service restoration (FLISR)
(C) Volt/VAR optimization
(D) Advanced metering infrastructure
(E) Distribution automation
(F) Energy storage systems

**(2) SMART GRID INVESTMENT.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000,000 over 10 years for smart grid investments in high fire risk areas.

**(b) MICROGRID PROGRAM.—**

**(1) COMMUNITY MICROGRIDS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for community microgrids in high fire risk areas, with priority for:
(A) Critical facilities (hospitals, emergency services, water systems)
(B) Disadvantaged communities
(C) Remote communities with limited grid access
(D) Communities with frequent PSPS events

**(2) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance for microgrid planning, design, and deployment.

**(3) REGULATORY REFORM.—**The Secretary shall develop model regulations for microgrid interconnection and operation.

**(c) ENERGY STORAGE PROGRAM.—**

**(1) BATTERY STORAGE DEPLOYMENT.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for battery storage deployment in high fire risk areas, with the goal of deploying 10 GW of storage within 5 years.

**(2) LONG-DURATION STORAGE.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for long-duration energy storage (8+ hours) to support grid resilience during extended PSPS events.

**(3) RESILIENCE HUBS.—**The Secretary shall establish resilience hubs — community facilities with solar + storage that can provide power during grid outages.

**(d) RENEWABLE ENERGY INTEGRATION.—**

**(1) DISTRIBUTED GENERATION.—**The Secretary shall provide incentives for distributed renewable energy generation in high fire risk areas, including rooftop solar and community solar.

**(2) GRID INTERCONNECTION.—**The Secretary shall streamline grid interconnection procedures for distributed generation in high fire risk areas.

**(3) NET METERING.—**The Secretary shall develop model net metering regulations that compensate distributed generation for its resilience value.
