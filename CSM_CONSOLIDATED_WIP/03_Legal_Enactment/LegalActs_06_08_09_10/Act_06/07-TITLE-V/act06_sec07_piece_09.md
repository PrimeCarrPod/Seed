## SEC. 720. TRIBAL RESPONSE MODERNIZATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 720.** (a) TRIBAL RESPONSE FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Tribal Wildfire Response Fund" to provide financial assistance for wildfire response on Tribal lands.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $1,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**

**(1) TRIBAL FIRE OFFICES.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to Tribal nations for wildfire response planning and implementation.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide training for Tribal members on wildfire response techniques.

**(c) PRIORITY.—**Tribal wildfire response projects shall receive priority for federal funding, with the federal share not exceeding 95%.

## SEC. 721. RURAL RESPONSE MODERNIZATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 721.** (a) RURAL RESPONSE FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Rural Wildfire Response Fund" to provide financial assistance for wildfire response in rural areas.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $2,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) VOLUNTEER FIRE DEPARTMENT ASSISTANCE.—

**(1) GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to volunteer fire departments in rural areas for equipment, training, and operations.

**(2) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to volunteer fire departments.

---

## SEC. 722. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION INNOVATION

**SEC. 722.** (a) INNOVATION CHALLENGES.—

**(1) FIREFIGHTING TECHNOLOGY CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $50,000,000 for the development of a breakthrough wildfire firefighting technology.

**(2) EVACUATION TECHNOLOGY CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a technology that improves community evacuation during wildfire events.

**(b) RESEARCH PARTNERSHIPS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL LABORATORY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with national laboratories on wildfire response research.

**(2) UNIVERSITY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with universities on wildfire response research.
