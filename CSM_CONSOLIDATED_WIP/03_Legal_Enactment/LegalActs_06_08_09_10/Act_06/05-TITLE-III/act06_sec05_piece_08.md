## SEC. 511. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT COORDINATION

**SEC. 511.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) FEDERAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate vegetation management activities with:
(A) Department of Interior
(B) Department of Energy
(C) Environmental Protection Agency
(D) Department of Defense
(E) Department of Homeland Security

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state foresters and other state agencies on vegetation management planning and implementation.

**(b) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—**

**(1) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on vegetation management activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) CO-MANAGEMENT AGREEMENTS.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for vegetation management on federal lands within their ancestral territories.

**(c) PRIVATE LANDOWNER COORDINATION.—**

**(1) LANDOWNER INCENTIVES.—**The Secretary shall provide incentives for private landowners to participate in vegetation management programs.

**(2) NEIGHBORHOOD COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall encourage neighborhood-scale vegetation management to achieve landscape-level risk reduction.

## SEC. 512. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT RESEARCH

**SEC. 512.** (a) RESEARCH PROGRAM.—

**(1) FOREST HEALTH RESEARCH.—**The Secretary shall conduct research on forest health, including:
(A) Fire-adapted forest conditions
(B) Climate change impacts on forest health
(C) Invasive species management
(D) Disease and insect outbreak prevention

**(2) FIRE BEHAVIOR RESEARCH.—**The Secretary shall conduct research on fire behavior, including:
(A) Fuel treatment effectiveness
(B) Fire spread modeling
(C) Ember transport
(D) Structure ignition mechanisms

**(b) RESEARCH FACILITIES.—

**(1) FIRE SCIENCE LABORATORIES.—**The Secretary shall maintain and expand fire science laboratories.

**(2) EXPERIMENTAL FORESTS.—**The Secretary shall utilize experimental forests for vegetation management research.

**(c) TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT.—**

**(1) REMOTE SENSING.—**The Secretary shall develop remote sensing technologies for vegetation management planning and monitoring.

**(2) AUTOMATED ASSESSMENT.—**The Secretary shall develop automated vegetation assessment technologies.
