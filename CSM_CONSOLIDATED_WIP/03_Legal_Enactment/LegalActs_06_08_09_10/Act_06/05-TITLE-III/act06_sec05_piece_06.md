## SEC. 508. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT WORKFORCE

**SEC. 508.** (a) WORKFORCE EXPANSION.—

**(1) HIRING TARGET.—**The Secretary of Agriculture and Secretary of Interior shall collectively hire an additional 30,000 vegetation management professionals within 5 years.

**(2) POSITIONS.—**Vegetation management professionals shall include:
(A) Foresters and silviculturists
(B) Prescribed fire practitioners
(C) Fuels management specialists
(D) Heavy equipment operators
(E) Biomass processing technicians
(F) Environmental compliance specialists
(G) GIS and remote sensing analysts

**(b) TRAINING PROGRAMS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL VEGETATION MANAGEMENT ACADEMY.—**The Secretary shall establish a National Vegetation Management Academy to provide training for vegetation management professionals.

**(2) COMMUNITY COLLEGE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with community colleges in fire-prone regions to develop vegetation management certificate and degree programs.

**(3) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAMS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for vegetation management careers.

**(c) TRIBAL WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT.—**

**(1) TRIBAL TRAINING CENTERS.—**The Secretary shall fund the establishment of Tribal vegetation management training centers.

**(2) CULTURAL BURN PRACTITIONER CERTIFICATION.—**The Secretary shall establish a certification program for cultural burn practitioners.

**(d) VETERAN HIRING.—**

**(1) VETERAN PREFERENCE.—**The Secretary shall give hiring preference to veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces for vegetation management positions.

**(2) SKILLS TRANSLATION.—**The Secretary shall develop programs to translate military skills to vegetation management careers.

**(e) YOUTH CORPS.—**

**(1) VEGETATION MANAGEMENT CORPS.—**The Secretary shall establish a Vegetation Management Corps for young adults aged 18-25.

**(2) SERVICE YEAR.—**Corps members shall serve a minimum of 1 year, receiving a living allowance, health benefits, and education award upon completion.
