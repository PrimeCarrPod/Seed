## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS

**SEC. 225.** (a) DOMESTIC ENVIRONMENTAL STANDARDS.—**This Act ensures that critical technology production meets U.S. environmental standards, which are among the world's most stringent.

**(b) RECYCLING AND SUSTAINABILITY.—**This Act promotes rare earth recycling and sustainable mining practices, reducing environmental impact.

**(c) CLEAN ENERGY TRANSITION.—**This Act supports the clean energy transition by securing supply chains for electric vehicles, wind turbines, and solar panels.

---

## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — WORKFORCE ANALYSIS

**SEC. 226.** (a) WORKFORCE SHORTAGE.—**The critical technology industries face severe workforce shortages. Over 1 million skilled workers are needed in semiconductor manufacturing, rare earth processing, and pharmaceutical production.

**(b) TRAINING PROGRAMS.—**Training programs must be expanded to meet the workforce needs of critical technology industries.

**(c) REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT.—**Investments in critical technology supply chains will promote regional development by locating manufacturing facilities in economically distressed communities.
