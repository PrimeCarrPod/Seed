## SECTION 217. PRIVACY AND CIVIL LIBERTIES

**SEC. 217.** (a) PRIVACY PROTECTIONS.—**Implementation of this Act shall include appropriate privacy protections.

**(b) CIVIL LIBERTIES PROTECTIONS.—**Implementation of this Act shall include appropriate civil liberties protections.

**(c) OVERSIGHT.—**The Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board shall oversee implementation of privacy and civil liberties protections.

---

## SECTION 218. ANTITRUST CONSIDERATIONS

**SEC. 218.** (a) ANTITRUST EXEMPTION.—**Activities under this Act shall be exempt from antitrust laws to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of this Act.

**(b) OVERSIGHT.—**The Department of Justice shall oversee activities under this exemption.

---

## SECTION 219. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PROTECTIONS

**SEC. 219.** (a) PATENT PROTECTIONS.—**Inventions developed with federal funding under this Act shall be subject to applicable patent protections.

**(b) TRADE SECRET PROTECTIONS.—**Trade secrets developed under this Act shall be protected.

**(c) BAYH-DOLE ACT COMPLIANCE.—**Inventions developed with federal funding shall comply with the Bayh-Dole Act.
