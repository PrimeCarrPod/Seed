## SEC. 107. WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 107.** (a) SEMICONDUCTOR WORKFORCE PROGRAM.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Labor shall establish a semiconductor workforce development program.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(3) PROGRAMS.—**The program shall include:
(A) Community college training programs
(B) University degree programs
(C) Apprenticeship programs
(D) Incumbent worker training
(E) Career pathway programs

**(b) PARTNERSHIPS.—

**(1) INDUSTRY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with semiconductor companies on workforce development.

**(2) EDUCATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with educational institutions on curriculum development.

**(c) SCHOLARSHIPS AND FELLOWSHIPS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall provide scholarships and fellowships for students pursuing semiconductor-related degrees.

**(2) AMOUNT.—**Scholarships shall be up to $50,000 per year for up to 4 years.

## SEC. 108. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE I

**SEC. 108.** (a) IN GENERAL.—There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $60,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) ALLOCATION.—**Funds shall be allocated as follows:
(1) Fabrication facility grants: $40,000,000,000
(2) Equipment and materials grants: $15,000,000,000
(3) Advanced packaging grants: $5,000,000,000
(4) Research and development: $10,000,000,000
(5) Workforce development: $5,000,000,000
