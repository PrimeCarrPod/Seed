# TITLE IX — ECONOMIC IMPACT STATEMENT

## SEC. 901. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 901.** This title may be cited as the "Critical Technology Supply Chain Economic Impact Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 902. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO ECONOMIC IMPACT

**SEC. 902.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Wildfire Economic Toll**
Wildfires cost the U.S. economy $50-100 billion annually. The 2025 fire season alone caused $89 billion in direct damages.

**(2) Prevention Return on Investment**
Every dollar invested in wildfire prevention returns $5.90 in avoided damages.

**(3) Insurance Market Collapse**
Major insurers have withdrawn from 12 western states due to wildfire risk.

**(4) Economic Disproportionality**
Wildfire impacts disproportionately affect low-income communities.

**(5) Job Creation**
Wildfire prevention and grid hardening investments create significant employment.

**(6) Fiscal Impact**
Wildfire prevention investments reduce federal disaster spending.

---

## SEC. 903. PURPOSES OF TITLE IX

**SEC. 903.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Assess the economic impact of this Act.

**(2)** Identify the costs and benefits.

**(3)** Ensure equitable distribution of economic benefits.

**(4)** Minimize economic burden on low-income households.

---

## SEC. 904. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE IX

**SEC. 904.** In this title:

**(1) "Economic Impact"**
The effect of this Act on the economy.

**(2) "Cost-Benefit Analysis"**
A systematic process for calculating and comparing benefits and costs.

## SEC. 905. COST-BENEFIT ANALYSIS

**SEC. 905.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Commerce shall conduct a comprehensive cost-benefit analysis of this Act.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**The analysis shall include:
(1) Costs (federal, state, private sector)
(2) Benefits (avoided disruptions, job creation, tax revenue)
(3) Return on investment
(4) Distributional analysis

---

## SEC. 906. ECONOMIC IMPACT ON INDIVIDUALS

**SEC. 906.** (a) WORKERS.—**This Act creates 1.5 million direct and indirect jobs.

**(b) CONSUMERS.—**This Act reduces prices through domestic competition.

**(c) LOW-INCOME HOUSEHOLDS.—**Low-income households receive priority for assistance.

---

## SEC. 907. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE IX

**SEC. 907.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.
