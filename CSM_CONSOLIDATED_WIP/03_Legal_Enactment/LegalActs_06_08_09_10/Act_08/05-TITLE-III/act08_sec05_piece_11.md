## SEC. 322. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN FOR VETERANS

**SEC. 322.** (a) VETERANS AFFAIRS PHARMACEUTICAL REQUIREMENTS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall identify pharmaceutical requirements for veterans' healthcare.

**(2) DOMESTIC SOURCING.—**Veterans' pharmaceutical requirements shall be sourced from domestic suppliers to the maximum extent practicable.

**(b) VETERANS AFFAIRS STRATEGIC STOCKPILE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall establish a strategic stockpile of pharmaceuticals for veterans.

**(2) STOCKPILE CONTENTS.—**The stockpile shall include essential medicines for veterans' healthcare.
