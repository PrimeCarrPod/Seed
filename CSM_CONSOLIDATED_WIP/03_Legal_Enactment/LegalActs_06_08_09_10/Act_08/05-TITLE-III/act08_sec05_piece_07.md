## SEC. 315. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 315.** (a) HOSPITALS AND MEDICAL FACILITIES.—

**(1) PHARMACEUTICAL REQUIREMENTS.—**The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall identify pharmaceutical requirements for hospitals and medical facilities.

**(2) DOMESTIC SOURCING.—**Hospital pharmaceutical requirements shall be sourced from domestic suppliers to the maximum extent practicable.

**(b) EMERGENCY RESPONSE FACILITIES.—

**(1) PHARMACEUTICAL REQUIREMENTS.—**The Secretary of Homeland Security shall identify pharmaceutical requirements for emergency response facilities.

**(2) DOMESTIC SOURCING.—**Emergency response pharmaceutical requirements shall be sourced from domestic suppliers to the maximum extent practicable.
