## SEC. 313. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 313.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with pharmaceutical supply chain requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with pharmaceutical supply chain activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 314. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 314.** (a) REVIEW.—**Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.

**(b) STANDARD OF REVIEW.—**The court shall set aside any action found to be arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.
