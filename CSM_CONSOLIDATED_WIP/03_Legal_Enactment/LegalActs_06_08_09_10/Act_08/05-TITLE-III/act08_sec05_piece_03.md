## SEC. 306. DRUG SHORTAGE PREVENTION

**SEC. 306.** (a) EARLY WARNING SYSTEM.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall establish an early warning system for drug shortages.

**(2) FUNCTIONS.—**The early warning system shall:
(A) Monitor pharmaceutical supply chains
(B) Identify potential shortages
(C) Alert manufacturers and distributors
(D) Coordinate response to shortages

**(b) STRATEGIC STOCKPILE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish a strategic stockpile of essential medicines.

**(2) STOCKPILE CONTENTS.—**The stockpile shall include:
(A) Antibiotics
(B) Anesthetics
(C) Cancer treatments
(D) Cardiovascular medications
(E) Insulin
(F) Other essential medicines

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

## SEC. 307. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE III

**SEC. 307.** (a) IN GENERAL.—There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $25,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) ALLOCATION.—**Funds shall be allocated as follows:
(1) Manufacturing grants: $15,000,000,000
(2) API production grants: $5,000,000,000
(3) Drug shortage prevention: $5,000,000,000
