## SEC. 304. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE III

**SEC. 304.** In this title:

**(1) "Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API)"**
Any substance or mixture of substances intended to be used in the manufacture of a drug product and that, when used in the production of a drug, becomes an active ingredient of the drug product.

**(2) "Finished Dosage Form"**
A pharmaceutical product in the form in which it is marketed for use, including tablets, capsules, injectables, and topicals.

**(3) "Generic Drug"**
A pharmaceutical product that is equivalent to a brand-name drug in dosage, strength, route of administration, quality, performance, and intended use.

**(4) "Essential Medicine"**
A medicine included on the World Health Organization Model List of Essential Medicines or the FDA list of essential medicines.

## SEC. 305. DOMESTIC PHARMACEUTICAL MANUFACTURING

**SEC. 305.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established in the Department of Health and Human Services a Domestic Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Program.

**(b) MANUFACTURING GRANTS.—**

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall provide grants for the construction or expansion of domestic pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities.

**(2) GRANT AMOUNT.—**Grants shall be up to $2 billion per facility.

**(3) PRIORITY.—**Grants shall be prioritized for:
(A) Facilities producing essential medicines
(B) Facilities in economically distressed communities
(C) Facilities with workforce development programs
(D) Facilities with environmental sustainability plans

**(c) API PRODUCTION GRANTS.—**

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for domestic API production facilities.

**(2) GRANT AMOUNT.—**Grants shall be up to $1 billion per facility.
