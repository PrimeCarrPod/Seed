## SEC. 1115. APPENDIX J — ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE ANALYSIS

**SEC. 1115.** (a) DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—**Not less than 40% of Fund expenditures shall benefit disadvantaged communities.

**(b) TRIBAL COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—**Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures shall benefit Tribal communities.

**(c) RURAL COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—**Not less than 20% of Fund expenditures shall benefit rural communities.

---

## SEC. 1116. APPENDIX K — TRIBAL CONSULTATION REQUIREMENTS

**SEC. 1116.** (a) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on all activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(b) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans.

**(c) CULTURAL BURNING PROTECTION.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to limit the right of Tribal nations to conduct cultural burns.
