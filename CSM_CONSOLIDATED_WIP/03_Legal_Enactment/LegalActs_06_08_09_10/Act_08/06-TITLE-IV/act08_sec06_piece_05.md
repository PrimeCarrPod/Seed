## SEC. 413. STRATEGIC RESERVE ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 413.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with strategic reserve requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with strategic reserve activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 414. STRATEGIC RESERVE JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 414.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
