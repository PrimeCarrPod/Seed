## SEC. 515. INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 515.** The Secretary of State shall prioritize international partnerships for defense and critical infrastructure facilities.

## SEC. 516. INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 516.** The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for international partnerships, including number of agreements, technology sharing, and joint research projects.
