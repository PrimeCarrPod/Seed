## SEC. 220. RARE EARTH MINERALS ENVIRONMENTAL STANDARDS

**SEC. 220.** (a) MINING STANDARDS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Rare earth mining under this Act shall comply with all applicable environmental standards.

**(2) RECLAMATION.—**Mining sites shall be reclaimed in accordance with applicable reclamation standards.

**(3) WATER QUALITY.—**Mining activities shall comply with applicable water quality standards.

**(b) PROCESSING STANDARDS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Rare earth processing under this Act shall comply with all applicable environmental standards.

**(2) WASTE MANAGEMENT.—**Processing facilities shall manage waste in accordance with applicable waste management standards.

**(3) AIR QUALITY.—**Processing activities shall comply with applicable air quality standards.
