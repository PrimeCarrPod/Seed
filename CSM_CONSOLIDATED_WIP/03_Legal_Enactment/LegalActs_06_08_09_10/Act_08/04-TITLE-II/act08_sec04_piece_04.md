## SEC. 208. RARE EARTH MINERALS ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 208.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Energy shall submit an annual performance report on rare earth minerals programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Domestic rare earth production volume
(B) Domestic rare earth processing capacity
(C) Rare earth recycling rate
(D) Dependence on foreign rare earth imports
(E) Expenditures by program
(F) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of rare earth minerals programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Program effectiveness
(B) Efficiency of operations
(C) Adequacy of funding
(D) Compliance with standards
(E) Achievement of targets

## SEC. 209. RARE EARTH MINERALS PREEMPTION

**SEC. 209.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national rare earth minerals standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt rare earth minerals standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 210. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 210.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
