## 5. SOPP ECOSYSTEM

**SOPP (Standard Operating Personality Procedures)** is the agent framework that defines each AI persona's:

- **Traits** — 35+ trait deep personas (e.g., KEYMAKER: Matrix, Beatrix Kiddo, Hunter S. Thompson, Linus Torvalds)
- **Heuristics** — Williams Paradise Man V3 EXTREME or El Segundo EXTREME
- **Communication style** — Distinct voice, pacing, and register
- **Domain expertise** — Materials, fabrication, integration, regulatory, business

The SOPP ecosystem enables a two-person team to operate with the breadth and depth of a much larger organization.

## 7. OPERATING MODEL

Carrington Storm Motors operates as a **distributed R&D lab**:

- **No dedicated factory** — Manufacturing via partner AFP facilities
- **No large staff** — AI agents handle analysis, documentation, simulation, and communication
- **No traditional hierarchy** — Human judgment + AI execution at software speed
- **IP-centric** — Patent portfolio on composition, process, and digital twin methodology
- **Open source (selective)** — AFP G-code generator, digital twin schema, test data format (Apache 2.0)
