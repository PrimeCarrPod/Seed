# Piece 11: Goldstino Supermultiplet and Soft Breaking

From A1-25 Piece 11 and A1-26 Piece 11, SUSY is spontaneously broken by the chirality fermion (bit 7). The Goldstino is the fermionic mode associated with the broken superconformal generator. In the superconformal algebra, the Goldstino corresponds to the broken $Q$ and $S$ charges in the bit-7 sector.

**Goldstino supermultiplet:**

The Goldstino lives in a supermultiplet of the unbroken superalgebra. The unbroken algebra is generated by $H, Z, Q_{\text{bulk}}, Q_{\text{bulk}}^\dagger$ (the bulk sector, bits 0-6). The broken generators are $Q_{\text{Goldstino}}, S_{\text{Goldstino}}$ and their adjoints, plus the R-symmetry $R$ (which is broken because the Goldstino has R-charge ±1).

The Goldstino supermultiplet contains:
- Goldstino $\eta$ (fermion, R-charge +1)
- Goldstino scalar $\phi_7$ (boson, R-charge 0)
- Auxiliary field $F_7$ (boson, R-charge 0)

The superconformal transformations act as:
$$
Q_{\text{Goldstino}} \phi_7 = \eta, \quad Q_{\text{Goldstino}} \eta = F_7, \quad S_{\text{Goldstino}} \phi_7 = \eta, \quad S_{\text{Goldstino}} \eta = F_7
$$

The Goldstino is massless in the exact SUSY limit, but gets a mass $m_{3/2} \sim 10^{-8}$ from soft breaking (A1-26 Piece 11).

**Soft SUSY breaking terms:**

The soft breaking Lagrangian in the superconformal basis is:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{soft}} = m_{3/2}^2 |\phi_7|^2 + m_{3/2} \bar{\eta} \eta + B \phi_7^2 + \text{h.c.}
$$

where $B \sim m_{3/2}^2$. The Goldstino mass $m_{3/2}$ is the order parameter for SUSY breaking. From A1-26 Piece 11, $m_{3/2} \sim 10^{-8}$ in Planck units.

**Super-Higgs mechanism:**

The Goldstino is eaten by the gravitino (if gravity is included) or by the R-symmetry gauge field. In our 1D worldline, the R-symmetry is a global $U(1)_R$, and the Goldstino remains as a physical massless mode in the exact SUSY limit. With soft breaking, it gets mass $m_{3/2}$.

**Protection of BPS sector:**

The 78 BPS states are in the bulk sector (bits 0-6) and are completely unaffected by the Goldstino. The superalgebra of the bulk sector has exact SUSY with central charge $Z=78$. The Goldstino sector (bit 7) decouples from the bulk in the IR limit.

**Mass hierarchy and Goldstino:**

The Goldstino mass $m_{3/2} \sim 10^{-8} M_{\text{Pl}} \sim 10^{11}$ GeV is the scale of SUSY breaking. This is much larger than the electroweak scale ($\sim 10^2$ GeV) but much smaller than the Planck scale ($\sim 10^{19}$ GeV). The hierarchy $m_{3/2} / M_{\text{Pl}} \sim 10^{-8}$ matches the gap ratio $2/86 \sim 10^{-2}$ scaled by the Compton factor from A1-09.

**Connection to A1-35 Quantum Error Correction:**

The Goldstino is the syndrome bit in the $[[256,1,3]]$ code. The logical qubit is encoded in the bulk 7 bits (bits 0-6). The Goldstino (bit 7) measures the error syndrome. The soft breaking terms are the error correction operations.

**Connection to Article 2 (Mass Spectrum):**

The Goldstino mass sets the scale for the neutrino masses (Article 2, A2-09). The neutrino mass from gap asymmetry is $m_\nu \sim m_{3/2} \times (\text{gap asymmetry}) \sim 10^{-8} \times 10^{-2} \sim 10^{-10}$ eV, which is in the right ballpark for neutrino masses.
