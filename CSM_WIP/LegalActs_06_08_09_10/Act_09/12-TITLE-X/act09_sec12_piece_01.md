# TITLE X — ENFORCEMENT AND COMPLIANCE

## SEC. 1001. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 1001.** This title may be cited as the "Water Infrastructure Enforcement Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 1002. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1002.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Compliance Necessity**
Effective enforcement is essential to ensure compliance with this Act.

**(2) Clear Standards**
Clear, measurable standards are necessary for effective enforcement.

---

## SEC. 1003. PURPOSES OF TITLE X

**SEC. 1003.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish enforcement mechanisms.

**(2)** Define penalties for non-compliance.

---

## SEC. 1004. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE X

**SEC. 1004.** In this title:

**(1) "Enforcement"**
The process of ensuring compliance with this Act.

**(2) "Compliance"**
Conformity with the requirements of this Act.
