# TITLE III — FLOOD MANAGEMENT

## SEC. 301. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 301.** This title may be cited as the "Flood Management Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 302. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO FLOOD MANAGEMENT

**SEC. 302.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Flood Damages**
Flooding causes $17 billion in damages annually in the United States. An estimated 13 million Americans live in flood-prone areas.

**(2) Climate Change Impact**
Climate change is intensifying extreme precipitation events. Sea level rise threatens coastal communities. Storm surge is increasing in frequency and severity.

**(3) Aging Infrastructure**
Many levees, dams, and flood control structures are aging and deteriorating. The average age of the nation's 91,000 dams is 57 years.

**(4) Development in Floodplains**
Development in floodplains has increased flood damages. An estimated 25% of floodplain development occurred after FEMA flood maps were published.

**(5) Environmental Impact**
Traditional flood control approaches have degraded ecosystems. Levees disconnect rivers from floodplains. Channelization increases downstream flooding.

**(6) Insurance Gap**
The National Insurance Program faces a $20 billion debt. Only 30% of flood-prone properties carry flood insurance.
