## SEC. 305. LEVEE SAFETY PROGRAM

**SEC. 305.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Levee Safety Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Inspect and assess all federal and non-federal levees
(2) Repair deficient levees
(3) Upgrade levees to withstand climate change impacts
(4) Develop levee emergency action plans

## SEC. 306. FLOODPLAIN RESTORATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 306.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Floodplain Restoration Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Reconnect rivers to floodplains
(2) Restore wetlands and riparian habitats
(3) Remove obsolete flood control structures
(4) Acquire flood-prone properties
