## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — INFRASTRUCTURE CONDITION

**SEC. 213.** (a) WATER MAIN BREAKS.—**An estimated 240,000 water main breaks occur annually in the United States, wasting 2 trillion gallons of treated water.

**(b) SEWER OVERFLOWS.—**Combined sewer overflows discharge 850 billion gallons of untreated wastewater annually, contaminating waterways and causing public health risks.

**(c) TREATMENT PLANT AGE.—**Many water treatment plants are operating well beyond their design life, with some facilities dating to the early 20th century.

## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — CLIMATE IMPACTS

**SEC. 214.** (a) SNOWPACK DECLINE.—**Western snowpack has declined 20-30% since 1950, reducing natural water storage and altering seasonal runoff patterns.

**(b) SEA LEVEL RISE.—**Sea level rise threatens coastal water infrastructure with saltwater intrusion, flooding, and storm damage.

**(c) EXTREME EVENTS.—**Both extreme drought and extreme flooding events are increasing in frequency and intensity due to climate change.
