## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — TRIBAL WATER RIGHTS

**SEC. 221.** (a) WINTERS DOCTRINE.—**The Winters Doctrine establishes that the creation of a federal Indian reservation implies a reservation of water rights sufficient to accomplish the reservation's purpose.

**(b) UNSETTLED CLAIMS.—**Many Tribal water rights claims remain unsettled, creating uncertainty for water management.

**(c) INFRASSION INVESTMENTS.—**Federal investment in Tribal water infrastructure is a trust responsibility that remains unfulfilled.
