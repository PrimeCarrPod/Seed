# TITLE VII — IMPLEMENTATION SCHEDULE

## SEC. 701. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 701.** This title may be cited as the "Water Infrastructure Implementation Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 702. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO IMPLEMENTATION

**SEC. 702.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Urgency**
The water infrastructure crisis demands immediate action.

**(2) Phased Approach**
A phased implementation approach is necessary.

**(3) Coordination Complexity**
Implementation requires coordination across multiple federal agencies.

---

## SEC. 703. PURPOSES OF TITLE VII

**SEC. 703.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a phased implementation schedule.

**(2)** Define clear roles and responsibilities.

**(3)** Establish milestones and deadlines.

---

## SEC. 704. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VII

**SEC. 704.** In this title:

**(1) "Implementation Plan"**
The comprehensive plan developed under Sec. 705.

**(2) "Milestone"**
A specific, measurable achievement.

## SEC. 705. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

**SEC. 705.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary shall develop a comprehensive implementation plan.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**The plan shall include:
(1) Roles and responsibilities
(2) Phased schedule
(3) Resource requirements
(4) Performance metrics
(5) Risk assessment

**(c) UPDATES.—**The plan shall be updated annually.

---

## SEC. 706. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE VII

**SEC. 706.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.
