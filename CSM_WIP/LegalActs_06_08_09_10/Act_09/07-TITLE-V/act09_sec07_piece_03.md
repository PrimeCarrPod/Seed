## SEC. 506. INTEGRATED WATER RESOURCES PLANNING

**SEC. 506.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established an Integrated Water Resources Planning Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Develop watershed-scale water management plans
(2) Integrate water supply, water quality, and flood management
(3) Incorporate climate change into water planning
(4) Support collaborative water governance
