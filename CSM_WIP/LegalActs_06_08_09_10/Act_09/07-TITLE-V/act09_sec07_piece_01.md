# TITLE V — CLIMATE ADAPTATION

## SEC. 501. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 501.** This title may be cited as the "Water Climate Adaptation Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 502. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO CLIMATE ADAPTATION

**SEC. 502.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Climate Change Impacts**
Climate change is intensifying droughts, floods, and water scarcity. Average temperatures have increased 1.8°F since 1900.

**(2) Sea Level Rise**
Sea level rise threatens coastal water infrastructure with saltwater intrusion, flooding, and storm damage.

**(3) Snowpack Decline**
Western snowpack has declined 20-30% since 1950, reducing natural water storage.

**(4) Extreme Events**
Both extreme drought and extreme flooding events are increasing in frequency and intensity.

**(5) Infrastructure Vulnerability**
Most water infrastructure was designed for historical climate conditions, not future climate.

---

## SEC. 503. PURPOSES OF TITLE V

**SEC. 503.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Adapt water systems to climate change through resilient infrastructure and integrated planning.

**(2)** Incorporate climate projections into water infrastructure design.

**(3)** Protect coastal water infrastructure from sea level rise.

**(4)** Develop climate-resilient water management strategies.
