# TITLE III — FLOOD MANAGEMENT

## SEC. 301. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 301.** This title may be cited as the "Flood Management Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 302. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO FLOOD MANAGEMENT

**SEC. 302.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Flood Damages**
Flooding causes $17 billion in damages annually in the United States. An estimated 13 million Americans live in flood-prone areas.

**(2) Climate Change Impact**
Climate change is intensifying extreme precipitation events. Sea level rise threatens coastal communities. Storm surge is increasing in frequency and severity.

**(3) Aging Infrastructure**
Many levees, dams, and flood control structures are aging and deteriorating. The average age of the nation's 91,000 dams is 57 years.

**(4) Development in Floodplains**
Development in floodplains has increased flood damages. An estimated 25% of floodplain development occurred after FEMA flood maps were published.

**(5) Environmental Impact**
Traditional flood control approaches have degraded ecosystems. Levees disconnect rivers from floodplains. Channelization increases downstream flooding.

**(6) Insurance Gap**
The National Insurance Program faces a $20 billion debt. Only 30% of flood-prone properties carry flood insurance.

## SEC. 303. PURPOSES OF TITLE III

**SEC. 303.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Reduce flood risk through levee improvements, floodplain restoration, and stormwater management.

**(2)** Modernize the National Flood Insurance Program.

**(3)** Promote nature-based flood management solutions.

**(4)** Improve flood forecasting and warning systems.

**(5)** Reduce development in flood-prone areas.

**(6)** Protect communities from coastal flooding and sea level rise.

---

## SEC. 304. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE III

**SEC. 304.** In this title:

**(1) "Floodplain"**
The area adjacent to a river or stream that is subject to flooding during high water events.

**(2) "Levee"**
An embankment constructed to prevent flooding from a river or other water body.

**(3) "Floodway"**
The channel of a river or stream and the adjacent land areas that must be reserved to discharge the base flood.

**(4) "Nature-Based Solutions"**
Approaches that use natural processes to reduce flood risk, including wetland restoration and floodplain reconnection.

## SEC. 305. LEVEE SAFETY PROGRAM

**SEC. 305.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Levee Safety Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Inspect and assess all federal and non-federal levees
(2) Repair deficient levees
(3) Upgrade levees to withstand climate change impacts
(4) Develop levee emergency action plans

---

## SEC. 306. FLOODPLAIN RESTORATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 306.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Floodplain Restoration Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Reconnect rivers to floodplains
(2) Restore wetlands and riparian habitats
(3) Remove obsolete flood control structures
(4) Acquire flood-prone properties
