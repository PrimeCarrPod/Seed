## SEC. 113. WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 113.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Water Workforce Development Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Establish water operator training programs
(2) Create apprenticeship programs
(3) Support community college water technology programs
(4) Provide continuing education for water professionals
