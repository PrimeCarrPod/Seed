## SEC. 120. INFRASTRUCTURE ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 120.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with water infrastructure requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with water infrastructure activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 121. INFRASTRUCTURE JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 121.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
