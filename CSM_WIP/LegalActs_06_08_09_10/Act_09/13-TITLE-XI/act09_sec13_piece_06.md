## SEC. 1114. APPENDIX I — PERFORMANCE METRICS SUMMARY

**SEC. 1114.** The following performance metrics are established:

**(1) Water Infrastructure Metrics**
(A) Miles of water mains replaced
(B) Number of water treatment plants upgraded
(C) Water loss reduction

**(2) Drought Resilience Metrics**
(A) Water recycling capacity
(B) Desalination capacity
(C) Drought preparedness levels

**(3) Flood Management Metrics**
(A) Miles of levees improved
(B) Flood risk reduction
(C) Stormwater management capacity

**(4) Water Conservation Metrics**
(A) Water savings achieved
(B) Agricultural water efficiency
(C) Industrial water efficiency
