# TITLE VIII — REGULATORY LANGUAGE

## SEC. 801. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 801.** This title may be cited as the "Water Infrastructure Regulatory Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 802. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO REGULATORY LANGUAGE

**SEC. 802.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Statutory Clarity**
Clear, precise regulatory language is essential for effective implementation.

**(2) Regulatory Coordination**
Regulatory language must coordinate with existing federal, state, and local regulations.

---

## SEC. 803. PURPOSES OF TITLE VIII

**SEC. 803.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Amend existing federal statutes to implement this Act.

**(2)** Establish clear regulatory language.

---

## SEC. 804. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VIII

**SEC. 804.** In this title:

**(1) "Regulatory Language"**
The specific statutory language used to implement this Act.

**(2) "Amendment"**
A change to an existing federal statute.

## SEC. 805. AMENDMENTS TO SAFE DRINKING WATER ACT

**SEC. 805.** The Safe Drinking Water Act is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 1459. WATER INFRASTRUCTURE MODERNIZATION.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Administrator shall implement water infrastructure modernization programs consistent with the Water Infrastructure and Drought Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) AUTHORIZATION.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $80,000,000,000 over 10 years."

---

## SEC. 806. AMENDMENTS TO CLEAN WATER ACT

**SEC. 806.** The Clean Water Act is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 521. WATER INFRASTRUCTURE MODERNIZATION.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Administrator shall implement water infrastructure modernization programs consistent with the Water Infrastructure and Drought Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) AUTHORIZATION.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $70,000,000,000 over 10 years."

---

## SEC. 807. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE VIII

**SEC. 807.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.
