## SEC. 414. WATER CONSERVATION ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 414.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with water conservation requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with water conservation activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 415. WATER CONSERVATION JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 415.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
