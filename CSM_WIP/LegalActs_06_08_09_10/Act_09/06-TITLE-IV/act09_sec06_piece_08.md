## SEC. 416. WATER CONSERVATION FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 416.** The Secretary shall prioritize water conservation for hospitals, emergency response facilities, and other critical infrastructure.

**SEC. 417.** The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for water conservation programs, including water savings achieved and efficiency improvements.
