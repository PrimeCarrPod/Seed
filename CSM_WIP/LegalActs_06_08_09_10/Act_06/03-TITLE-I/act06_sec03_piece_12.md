## SEC. 315. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE I

**SEC. 315.** (a) IN GENERAL.—There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $45,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036, to be allocated as follows:

**(1) Vegetation Management Program (Sec. 306)**
$15,000,000,000 over 10 years ($1.5 billion annually)
- Federal land treatment: $10,000,000,000
- State and private cost-share: $3,000,000,000
- Biomass utilization: $2,000,000,000

**(2) Prescribed Fire Expansion Program (Sec. 307)**
$8,000,000,000 over 10 years ($800 million annually)
- Federal prescribed fire: $4,000,000,000
- State and private assistance: $2,000,000,000
- Cultural burning program: $1,000,000,000
- Training and certification: $1,000,000,000

**(3) Defensible Space Program (Sec. 308)**
$6,000,000,000 over 10 years ($600 million annually)
- Rebates and incentives: $3,000,000,000
- Low-income assistance: $2,000,000,000
- Community Wildfire Protection Plans: $1,000,000,000

**(4) Ignition Prevention Program (Sec. 309)**
$3,000,000,000 over 10 years ($300 million annually)
- Detection technology: $1,500,000,000
- Public education: $1,000,000,000
- Enforcement: $500,000,000

**(5) Ignition-Resistant Construction (Sec. 310)**
$5,000,000,000 over 10 years ($500 million annually)
- Retrofit grants: $3,000,000,000
- Low-income assistance: $1,500,000,000
- Materials certification: $500,000,000

**(6) Workforce Development (Sec. 311)**
$4,000,000,000 over 10 years ($400 million annually)
- Hiring and salaries: $2,500,000,000
- Training programs: $1,000,000,000
- Youth Corps: $500,000,000

**(7) Technology Program (Sec. 312)**
$4,000,000,000 over 10 years ($400 million annually)
- Research and development: $2,000,000,000
- Technology deployment: $1,500,000,000
- Data and analytics: $500,000,000

**(b) EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL.—**There is authorized to be appropriated an additional $10,000,000,000 for emergency vegetation management in areas affected by wildfire, insect infestation, or disease, to be available upon declaration by the Secretary.

**(c) COST-SHARE.—**Except as otherwise provided, the federal share of costs under this title shall not exceed 75%.

**(d) MAINTENANCE OF EFFORT.—**As a condition of receiving funds under this title, states shall maintain their existing level of wildfire prevention spending, adjusted for inflation.

**(e) AVAILABILITY.—**Funds appropriated under this title shall remain available until expended.

---

## SEC. 316. EFFECTIVE DATE

**SEC. 316.** This title shall take effect 180 days after the date of enactment, except that the Secretary may take such actions as are necessary to prepare for implementation prior to the effective date.
