## SEC. 308. DEFENSIBLE SPACE PROGRAM

**SEC. 308.** (a) NATIONAL DEFENSIBLE SPACE STANDARD.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, the Secretary shall establish a national defensible space standard for all structures in the Wildland-Urban Interface.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**The standard shall require:
(A) Zone 1: 0-5 feet from structure — Non-combustible landscaping, no combustible mulch, no vegetation within 5 feet of structure
(B) Zone 2: 5-30 feet from structure — Low-fuel landscaping, spaced vegetation, no ladder fuels, pruned tree limbs to 6 feet minimum
(C) Zone 3: 30-100 feet from structure — Reduced fuel density, spaced tree crowns, removed dead vegetation, managed understory
(D) Zone 4: 100-200 feet from structure (where applicable) — Strategic fuel breaks, reduced canopy cover, managed vegetation

**(3) COMPLIANCE TIMELINE.—**All structures in the WUI shall achieve compliance with the defensible space standard within 3 years of the standard's establishment.

**(b) DEFENSIBLE SPACE INCENTIVE PROGRAM.—**

**(1) REBATES.—**The Secretary shall provide rebates of up to $5,000 per property for defensible space creation and maintenance.

**(2) LOW-INCOME ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide 100% cost coverage for defensible space creation on properties owned by low-income households.

**(3) CONTRACTOR CERTIFICATION.—**The Secretary shall establish a certification program for defensible space contractors, including training on fire-resistant landscaping, vegetation management, and structural vulnerability reduction.

**(c) COMMUNITY WILDFIRE PROTECTION PLANS.—**

**(1) REQUIREMENT.—**All communities in the WUI shall develop and maintain a Community Wildfire Protection Plan (CWPP) as a condition of receiving federal wildfire prevention funding.

**(2) PLAN CONTENTS.—**Each CWPP shall include:
(A) Community risk assessment
(B) Prioritized treatment areas
(C) Structural ignitability assessment
(D) Evacuation plan
(E) Public education strategy
(F) Funding strategy

**(3) APPROVAL.—**CWPPs shall be approved by the applicable state forester in consultation with the Secretary.

**(d) FIRE-ADAPTED COMMUNITIES CERTIFICATION.—

**(1) PROGRAM.—**The Secretary shall establish a Fire-Adapted Communities Certification Program that recognizes communities that have achieved comprehensive wildfire risk reduction.

**(2) CERTIFICATION CRITERIA.—**Certification shall require:
(A) Adopted and enforced defensible space ordinance
(B) Approved CWPP
(C) Building code compliance
(D) Evacuation plan tested within past 2 years
(E) Public education program
(F) Dedicated funding for maintenance

**(3) INCENTIVES.—**Certified Fire-Adapted Communities shall receive priority for federal wildfire prevention funding and reduced insurance premiums under the National Wildfire Insurance Program established under Sec. 605.
