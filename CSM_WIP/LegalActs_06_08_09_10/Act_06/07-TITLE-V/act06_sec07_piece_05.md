## SEC. 708. WILDFIRE RESPONSE WORKFORCE EXPANSION

**SEC. 708.** (a) HIRING TARGETS.—

**(1) FEDERAL FIREFIGHTERS.—**The Secretary of Agriculture and Secretary of Interior shall collectively hire an additional 20,000 federal wildland firefighters within 5 years.

**(2) POSITIONS.—**Wildfire response positions shall include:
(A) Hotshot crew members
(B) Smokejumpers
(C) Helitack crew members
(D) Engine crew members
(E) Fire behavior analysts
(F) Incident commanders
(G) Dispatch specialists
(H) Aviation managers

**(b) COMPENSATION REFORM.—

**(1) PAY INCREASE.—**Federal wildland firefighters shall receive a minimum pay increase of 50% above current levels.

**(2) BENEFITS.—**Federal wildland firefighters shall receive:
(A) Comprehensive health insurance
(B) Mental health coverage
(C) Hazard pay for fire assignments
(D) Overtime compensation
(E) Retirement benefits

**(c) MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT.—

**(1) COUNSELING SERVICES.—**The Secretary shall provide confidential counseling services to all wildland firefighters.

**(2) PEER SUPPORT.—**The Secretary shall establish peer support programs for wildland firefighters.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500,000,000 over 10 years for mental health support.

**(d) SAFETY EQUIPMENT.—

**(1) PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT.—**The Secretary shall provide state-of-the-art personal protective equipment to all wildland firefighters.

**(2) EMERGENCY SHELTERS.—**The Secretary shall provide next-generation emergency shelters to all wildland firefighters.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500,000,000 over 10 years for safety equipment.

## SEC. 709. EARLY WARNING AND EVACUATION SYSTEMS

**SEC. 709.** (a) EARLY WARNING SYSTEM DEPLOYMENT.—

**(1) CAMERA NETWORKS.—**The Secretary shall deploy a network of 10,000 AI-powered fire detection cameras in high fire risk areas within 3 years.

**(2) SATELLITE MONITORING.—**The Secretary shall deploy satellite-based fire detection systems for real-time monitoring.

**(3) ALERT SYSTEMS.—**The Secretary shall deploy integrated alert systems that provide:
(A) Wireless Emergency Alerts to all mobile phones in affected areas
(B) Sirens and public address systems
(C) Social media alerts
(D) Television and radio alerts

**(b) EVACUATION PLANNING.—

**(1) COMMUNITY EVACUATION PLANS.—**All communities in the WUI shall develop and maintain evacuation plans.

**(2) EVACUATION ROUTE IMPROVEMENTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for evacuation route improvements, including:
(A) Road widening
(B) Additional egress routes
(C) Signage and lighting
(D) Traffic management systems

**(3) VULNERABLE POPULATION ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide assistance for evacuation of vulnerable populations, including:
(A) Elderly residents
(B) Disabled residents
(C) Medical facilities
(D) Schools and daycare centers

**(c) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000,000 over 10 years for early warning and evacuation systems.
