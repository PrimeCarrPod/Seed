## SEC. 723. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION REPORTING

**SEC. 723.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on wildfire response modernization activities, including:
(A) Fleet modernization progress
(B) Interagency coordination improvements
(C) Technology deployment progress
(D) Workforce expansion data
(E) Early warning system performance
(F) Post-fire recovery progress
(G) Expenditures by program
(H) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of wildfire response modernization programs.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying wildfire response data.

## SEC. 724. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 724.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
