## SEC. 710. POST-FIRE RECOVERY PROGRAM

**SEC. 710.** (a) WATERSHED RESTORATION.—

**(1) EMERGENCY STABILIZATION.—**The Secretary shall conduct emergency stabilization of burned watersheds within 30 days of fire containment.

**(2) LONG-TERM RESTORATION.—**The Secretary shall conduct long-term watershed restoration, including:
(A) Erosion control
(B) Revegetation
(C) Debris removal
(D) Infrastructure repair

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $3,000,000,000 over 10 years for watershed restoration.

**(b) DEBRIS REMOVAL.—

**(1) HAZARDOUS MATERIALS.—**The Secretary shall conduct hazardous materials removal from burned structures.

**(2) STRUCTURAL DEBRIS.—**The Secretary shall conduct structural debris removal from burned properties.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $1,000,000,000 over 10 years for debris removal.

**(c) COMMUNITY REBUILDING.—

**(1) REBUILDING ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to communities for rebuilding after wildfire.

**(2) FIRE-RESISTANT REBUILDING.—**Rebuilt structures shall comply with fire-resistant building standards.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000,000 over 10 years for community rebuilding.

**(d) MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT.—

**(1) COMMUNITY COUNSELING.—**The Secretary shall provide mental health counseling to communities affected by wildfire.

**(2) LONG-TERM SUPPORT.—**Mental health support shall be available for 5 years following a wildfire event.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500,000,000 over 10 years for community mental health support.

## SEC. 711. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 711.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress an annual performance report on wildfire response modernization.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Fleet modernization progress
(B) Interagency coordination improvements
(C) Technology deployment progress
(D) Workforce expansion data
(E) Early warning system performance
(F) Post-fire recovery progress
(G) Expenditures by program
(H) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of wildfire response modernization programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Program effectiveness in improving response
(B) Efficiency of program operations
(C) Adequacy of funding levels
(D) Compliance with statutory requirements
(E) Achievement of performance targets
