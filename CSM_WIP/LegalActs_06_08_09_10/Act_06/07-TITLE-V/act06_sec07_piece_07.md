## SEC. 712. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION COORDINATION

**SEC. 712.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) FEDERAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate wildfire response modernization with:
(A) Department of Interior
(B) Department of Defense
(C) Federal Emergency Management Agency
(D) Department of Homeland Security
(E) National Weather Service

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state fire agencies on response modernization.

**(b) INTERNATIONAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with international organizations on wildfire response modernization.

**(2) RESOURCE SHARING.—**The Secretary shall maintain international mutual aid agreements for wildfire response resource sharing.

---

## SEC. 713. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION PREEMPTION

**SEC. 713.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national wildfire response standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt wildfire response standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 714. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 714.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 715. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 715.** (a) STAFFORD ACT.—**This title amends the Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) STATE EMERGENCY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state emergency management agencies.

**(c) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.

$3,000,000,000 over 10 years ($300 million annually)

**(5) Early Warning and Evacuation (Sec. 709)**
$2,000,000,000 over 10 years ($200 million annually)

$1,000,000,000 over 10 years ($100 million annually)
