# TITLE V — RESPONSE MODERNIZATION

## SEC. 701. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 701.** This title may be cited as the "Wildfire Response Modernization Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 702. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO RESPONSE MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 702.** The Congress finds the following with respect to wildfire response:

**(1) Aging Fleet**
The federal wildfire response fleet is dangerously obsolete. The average age of the federal air tanker fleet is 52 years. Many aircraft are no longer supported by manufacturers. Maintenance costs have increased 300% in the past decade.

**(2) Inadequate Coordination**
Interagency coordination protocols have not been updated since 2003. Communication systems are incompatible across agencies. Joint training exercises are infrequent and underfunded.

**(3) Technology Gap**
Advanced firefighting technologies are not widely deployed. Night vision capabilities are limited. Unmanned aerial systems are underutilized. Real-time fire mapping is not available to all incident commanders.

**(4) Workforce Shortage**
Federal agencies face 25% vacancy rates in firefighter positions. The wildfire workforce has not kept pace with increasing fire activity. Mental health support for firefighters is inadequate.

**(5) Community Notification**
Early warning and evacuation systems are inadequate. Alert times in covered areas average 8 minutes — insufficient for vulnerable populations. False alarm rates of 35% reduce public trust.

**(6) Post-Fire Recovery**
Post-fire recovery programs are underfunded and slow. Watershed restoration, debris removal, and community rebuilding take years. Mental health support for affected communities is inadequate.
