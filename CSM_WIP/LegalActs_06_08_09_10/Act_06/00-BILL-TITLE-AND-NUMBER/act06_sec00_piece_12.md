## MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

### Technical and Clerical Amendments

The Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized to make technical and clerical corrections to this Act as necessary to reflect the intent of Congress.

### Sense of Congress

It is the sense of Congress that:
(1) Wildfire prevention and grid resilience are national priorities requiring sustained investment
(2) Climate change adaptation must be integrated into all infrastructure planning
(3) Federal agencies should prioritize nature-based solutions where feasible
(4) Private sector innovation should be incentivized through public-private partnerships
(5) Environmental justice communities should receive prioritized access to grant programs
(6) International cooperation on wildfire management should be strengthened

### Communication with Congress

Each federal agency responsible for implementing this Act shall submit an annual report to the appropriate congressional committees detailing:
(1) Progress toward meeting the goals of this Act
(2) Expenditures and obligations by program
(3) Accomplishments and outcomes achieved
(4) Challenges and barriers encountered
(5) Recommendations for legislative action

### GAO Comptroller General Review

The Comptroller General of the United States shall conduct a comprehensive review of this Act's implementation 3 years after enactment and every 5 years thereafter, and submit findings to Congress.

### Public Availability

All reports, plans, and data generated under this Act shall be made publicly available on the appropriate federal agency websites, subject to applicable privacy and security restrictions.
