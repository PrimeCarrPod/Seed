### Transition Provisions

(a) EXISTING PROGRAMS.—All programs authorized under existing law that are reauthorized or amended by this Act shall continue to operate under the terms of existing law until the effective date of the applicable title.

(b) PENDING APPLICATIONS.—Applications for grants or assistance under existing law that are pending on the date of enactment shall be processed under the terms of existing law unless the applicant elects to apply under this Act.

(c) REGULATIONS.—Federal agencies shall promulgate interim final regulations within 180 days of enactment to implement this Act, with final regulations to be issued within 18 months.

(d) TRIBAL CONSULTATION.—The Secretary of Interior shall initiate government-to-government consultation with Tribal nations within 90 days of enactment to develop Tribal implementation plans.

### Grandfathering

Structures built prior to the effective date of Title III shall be exempt from the building code requirements for a period of 5 years, provided the owner implements defensible space requirements within 2 years.
