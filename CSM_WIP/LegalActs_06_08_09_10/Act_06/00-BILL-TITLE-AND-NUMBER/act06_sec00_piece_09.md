## DEFINITIONS AND SCOPE

### Key Definitions

**"Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)"** — The zone where structures and other human development meet or intermingle with undeveloped wildland or vegetative fuels, as mapped by the USDA Forest Service SILVIS laboratory.

**"Vegetation Management Plan (VMP)"** — A documented plan for the systematic management of vegetation within utility rights-of-way and on federal lands to reduce wildfire ignition risk, consistent with the standards established under Title I of this Act.

**"Fire-Resistant Building Material"** — Any construction material that meets or exceeds the Class A fire resistance rating under ASTM E84 and has been certified under the Fire-Resistant Material Certification Program established under Sec. 302.

**"Grid Hardening"** — The physical modification of electrical infrastructure to reduce ignition risk during high-wind, low-humidity events, including but not limited to covered conductor installation, undergrounding, rapid fault isolation, and fast curve switching.

**"Public Safety Power Shutoff (PSPS)"** — The preemptive de-energization of electrical lines during extreme fire weather conditions, subject to the standards and protocols established under Sec. 204.

**"Defensible Space"** — The area within 100 feet of a structure where vegetation and combustible materials are managed to reduce fire spread potential, as defined by the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE) or equivalent state authority.

**"Microgrid"** — A group of interconnected loads and distributed energy resources within clearly defined electrical boundaries that acts as a single controllable entity with respect to the grid.

**"Covered Conductor"** — An electrical conductor encased in a thick layer of insulating material rated to withstand momentary contact with vegetation without arcing or ignition.

### Geographic Scope

This Act applies to:
- All 50 states and the District of Columbia
- All U.S. Territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, USVI, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands)
- Tribal lands subject to federal trust responsibilities
- Federal lands managed by the Department of Interior and USDA Forest Service

### Temporal Scope

This Act takes effect upon enactment, with implementation phases as specified in Title VII.
