## SEC. 1234. ENFORCEMENT FOR GOVERNMENT AGENCIES

**SEC. 1234.** (a) FEDERAL AGENCIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce the provisions of this Act on federal agencies.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Federal agencies that fail to comply with this Act shall be subject to corrective action.

**(b) STATE AGENCIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce the provisions of this Act on state agencies.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**State agencies that fail to comply with this Act shall be subject to reduced federal funding.

**(c) LOCAL AGENCIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce the provisions of this Act on local agencies.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Local agencies that fail to comply with this Act shall be subject to reduced federal funding.

## SEC. 1235. FINAL ENFORCEMENT PROVISIONS

**SEC. 1235.** (a) CUMULATIVE REMEDIES.—**The remedies provided under this title are cumulative and do not exclude any other remedy available under law.

**(b) NO LIMITATION.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to limit the authority of any federal, state, or local agency to enforce any other law.

**(c) SEVERABILITY.—**If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
