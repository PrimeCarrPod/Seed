## SEC. 1230. ENFORCEMENT FOR UTILITIES

**SEC. 1230.** (a) ELECTRICAL UTILITIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary of Energy shall enforce grid hardening standards for electrical utilities.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Electrical utilities that fail to comply with grid hardening standards shall be subject to civil penalties of up to $1,000,000 per day.

**(b) NATURAL GAS UTILITIES.—

**(1) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary of Energy shall enforce fire safety standards for natural gas utilities in high fire risk areas.

**(2) PENALTIES.—**Natural gas utilities that fail to comply with fire safety standards shall be subject to civil penalties.

---

## SEC. 1231. ENFORCEMENT FOR BUILDERS AND DEVELOPERS

**SEC. 1231.** (a) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce fire-resistant construction standards for builders and developers in the WUI.

**(b) PENALTIES.—**Builders and developers that fail to comply with fire-resistant construction standards shall be subject to civil penalties.
