## SEC. 516. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT DATA AND ANALYTICS

**SEC. 516.** (a) NATIONAL VEGETATION MANAGEMENT DATABASE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish a national database for vegetation management data, including:
(A) Treatment records by location, type, and date
(B) Forest health assessments
(C) Fire hazard severity ratings
(D) Treatment effectiveness data
(E) Biomass utilization data
(F) Workforce data

**(2) DATA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary shall establish data standards for vegetation management data collection and reporting.

**(b) PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS.—**

**(1) FIRE RISK MODELING.—**The Secretary shall develop predictive models for fire risk, incorporating:
(A) Vegetation data
(B) Weather data
(C) Topography
(D) Historical fire data

**(2) TREATMENT OPTIMIZATION.—**The Secretary shall develop tools for optimizing vegetation management treatment locations and methods.

**(c) PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying vegetation management progress and performance data.

**(2) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on vegetation management progress.

## SEC. 517. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT PREEMPTION

**SEC. 517.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national vegetation management standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt vegetation management standards that are more protective than the national standards.

## SEC. 518. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 518.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

## SEC. 519. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 519.** (a) FEDERAL LAND POLICY AND MANAGEMENT ACT.—**This title amends the Federal Land Policy and Management Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) NATIONAL FOREST MANAGEMENT ACT.—**This title amends the National Forest Management Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(c) STATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state agencies.
