## SEC. 507. BIOMASS UTILIZATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 507.** (a) MARKET DEVELOPMENT.—

**(1) BIOMASS MARKET ANALYSIS.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall conduct a comprehensive analysis of biomass market opportunities, including:
(A) Bioenergy production
(B) Biochar production
(C) Engineered wood products
(D) Soil amendments
(E) Animal bedding
(F) Industrial feedstocks

**(2) MARKET DEVELOPMENT GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for biomass market development, including:
(A) Feasibility studies
(B) Business planning
(C) Market analysis
(D) Technology development

**(b) PROCESSING INFRASTRUCTURE.—**

**(1) GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for biomass processing infrastructure in fire-prone regions, including:
(A) Mobile chipping equipment
(B) Biochar kilns
(C) Small-diameter sawmills
(D) Pellet mills
(E) Composting facilities
(F) Anaerobic digesters

**(2) LOAN GUARANTEES.—**The Secretary shall provide loan guarantees for biomass processing facilities.

**(c) ECONOMIC VIABILITY.—**

**(1) COST OFFSET.—**Biomass utilization programs shall be designed to offset treatment costs and create economic opportunities in rural communities.

**(2) REVENUE SHARING.—**Revenues from biomass sales shall be shared between the federal government and the treatment contractor, with the federal share not exceeding 25%.

**(d) RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT.—**

**(1) BIOMASS INNOVATION LABORATORY.—**The Secretary shall establish a Biomass Innovation Laboratory to develop new uses for biomass removed during vegetation management.

**(2) PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT.—**The Secretary shall fund research and development of new products from small-diameter trees and other biomass materials.
