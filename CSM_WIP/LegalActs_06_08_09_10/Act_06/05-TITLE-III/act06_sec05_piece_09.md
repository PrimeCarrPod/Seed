## SEC. 513. TRIBAL VEGETATION MANAGEMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 513.** (a) TRIBAL VEGETATION MANAGEMENT FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Tribal Vegetation Management Fund" to provide financial assistance for vegetation management on Tribal lands.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $3,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**

**(1) TRIBAL FORESTRY OFFICES.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to Tribal nations for vegetation management planning and implementation.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide training for Tribal members on vegetation management techniques.

**(c) CULTURAL BURNING.—**

**(1) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit the right of Tribal nations to conduct cultural burns on their lands.

**(2) CO-MANAGEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for cultural burning on federal lands within their ancestral territories.

**(d) PRIORITY.—**Tribal vegetation management projects shall receive priority for federal funding, with the federal share not exceeding 95%.

## SEC. 514. RURAL VEGETATION MANAGEMENT PROGRAM

**SEC. 514.** (a) RURAL VEGETATION MANAGEMENT FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Rural Vegetation Management Fund" to provide financial assistance for vegetation management in rural areas.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) PRIVATE LANDOWNER ASSISTANCE.—**

**(1) COST-SHARE.—**The Secretary shall provide cost-share assistance to private landowners for vegetation management, with the federal share not exceeding 75%.

**(2) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to private landowners for vegetation management planning and implementation.

**(c) COMMUNITY PROGRAMS.—**

**(1) FIRE-ADAPTED COMMUNITIES.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for community-scale vegetation management programs.

**(2) LANDOWNER COOPERATIVES.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for landowner cooperatives that coordinate vegetation management across multiple ownerships.

## SEC. 515. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT INNOVATION

**SEC. 515.** (a) INNOVATION CHALLENGES.—

**(1) TREATMENT COST REDUCTION CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a technology that reduces vegetation management costs by 50%.

**(2) BIOMASS UTILIZATION CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a new use for biomass that creates economic value.

**(b) RESEARCH PARTNERSHIPS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL LABORATORY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with national laboratories on vegetation management research and development.

**(2) UNIVERSITY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with universities on vegetation management research and development.
