## SECTION 227. SENSE OF CONGRESS

**SEC. 227.** It is the sense of Congress that:

**(1)** Wildfire prevention and grid resilience are national priorities requiring sustained investment over multiple decades.

**(2)** Climate change adaptation must be integrated into all infrastructure planning, land management, and disaster risk reduction programs.

**(3)** Federal agencies should prioritize nature-based solutions where feasible, including prescribed burning, forest thinning, and ecosystem restoration.

**(4)** Private sector innovation should be incentivized through public-private partnerships, tax credits, and regulatory certainty.

**(5)** Environmental justice communities should receive prioritized access to grant programs and technical assistance.

**(6)** International cooperation on wildfire management should be strengthened through mutual aid agreements, technology transfer, and joint research.

**(7)** The United States should be a global leader in wildfire prevention and management, sharing knowledge and resources with other nations.

**(8)** Traditional ecological knowledge held by Tribal nations should be respected and incorporated into wildfire management planning.

**(9)** The workforce of the future must be trained in wildfire prevention, grid resilience, and climate adaptation.

**(10)** Every dollar invested in prevention returns nearly six dollars in avoided damages, making this Act one of the most cost-effective investments Congress can make.
