## SECTION 224. REGULATORY FLEXIBILITY

**SEC. 224.** (a) SMALL BUSINESS EXEMPTION.—**Businesses with fewer than 50 employees shall be exempt from the building code requirements of Title III, provided they implement defensible space requirements.

**(b) HARDSHIP WAIVER.—**The Secretary of Interior may grant hardship waivers for any requirement of this Act upon a showing of undue financial burden or physical impossibility.

**(c) ALTERNATIVE COMPLIANCE.—**States and Tribal nations may propose alternative compliance methods that achieve equivalent or superior protection.

**(d) PHASED IMPLEMENTATION.—**The Secretary of Interior may approve phased implementation schedules for states and utilities demonstrating good faith effort but facing capacity constraints.

**(e) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**Federal agencies shall provide technical assistance to states, Tribal nations, and local governments to support compliance.

## SECTION 225. JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 225.** (a) STANDING.—**Any person who suffers legal wrong because of agency action under this Act, or who is adversely affected or aggrieved by agency action, is entitled to judicial review.

**(b) VENUE.—**Actions under this Act may be brought in the United States District Court for the district in which the plaintiff resides or in the United States District Court for the District of Columbia.

**(c) STANDARD OF REVIEW.—**The reviewing court shall set aside agency action found to be arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.

**(d) EXHAUSTION.—**Exhaustion of administrative remedies is required before seeking judicial review, except in cases of emergency.

**(e) ATTORNEY FEES.—**The court may award reasonable attorney fees and costs to any substantially prevailing party in any action under this Act.
