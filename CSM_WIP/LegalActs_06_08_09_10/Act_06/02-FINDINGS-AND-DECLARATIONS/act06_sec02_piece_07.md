## SECTION 212. ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE

**SEC. 212.** (a) PRIORITIZED FUNDING.—Forty percent of all grant funds under this Act shall be allocated to disadvantaged communities as identified by the Justice40 Initiative or equivalent state screening tools.

**(b) LANGUAGE ACCESS.—**All materials, notices, and public engagement activities under this Act shall be provided in English, Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, and other languages as appropriate for the affected community.

**(c) COMMUNITY BENEFIT AGREEMENTS.—**All projects funded under this Act with a total cost exceeding $10,000,000 shall include a Community Benefit Agreement developed in consultation with affected community members.

**(d) HEALTH IMPACT ASSESSMENT.—**All projects funded under this Act shall include a health impact assessment evaluating effects on air quality, water quality, mental health, and community well-being.

**(e) WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT.—**Thirty percent of jobs created by this Act shall be targeted to residents of disadvantaged communities. Training programs shall be provided at no cost to participants.

**(f) TRIBAL CONSULTATION.—**Government-to-government consultation with Tribal nations shall be conducted for all projects affecting Tribal lands, resources, or cultural sites.

## SECTION 213. LABOR STANDARDS

**SEC. 213.** (a) PREVAILING WAGE.—All laborers and mechanics employed on projects funded under this Act shall be paid prevailing wages as determined by the Secretary of Labor under the Davis-Bacon Act (40 U.S.C. §3141 et seq.).

**(b) APPRENTICESHIP.—**All projects funded under this Act with a total cost exceeding $2,000,000 shall include an apprenticeship utilization plan providing for not less than 10% of total labor hours to be performed by registered apprentices.

**(c) LOCAL HIRE.—**To the maximum extent practicable, projects funded under this Act shall hire workers from the local community.

**(d) WORKER SAFETY.—**All projects funded under this Act shall comply with OSHA safety standards and provide additional wildfire-specific safety training.

**(e) LABOR AGREEMENTS.—**Nothing in this Act shall preclude the use of project labor agreements or collective bargaining agreements on funded projects.

## SECTION 214. ANTI-CORRUPTION

**SEC. 214.** (a) PROHIBITED USES.—**No funds under this Act shall be used for:
(1) Lobbying or political campaign activities
(2) Personal benefit of any government official
(3) Payment of any penalty or fine imposed by a court or regulatory body
(4) Entertainment or recreational activities
(5) Any purpose unrelated to the purposes of this Act

**(b) WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTIONS.—**Any employee of a federal agency, contractor, or grantee who reports waste, fraud, or abuse under this Act shall be protected from retaliation under the Whistleblower Protection Act (5 U.S.C. §2302(b)(8)).

**(c) INSPECTOR GENERAL ACCESS.—**The Inspector General of each implementing agency shall have unrestricted access to all records, personnel, and activities related to this Act.

**(d) AUDIT REQUIREMENTS.—**All grantees and contractors receiving more than $1,000,000 under this Act shall submit to an annual independent audit.

**(e) PENALTIES FOR MISCONDUCT.—**Any person who knowingly misuses funds under this Act shall be subject to civil penalties of up to $250,000 and criminal penalties of up to 5 years imprisonment.
