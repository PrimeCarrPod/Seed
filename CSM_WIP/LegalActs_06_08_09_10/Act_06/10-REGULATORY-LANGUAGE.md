# TITLE VIII — REGULATORY LANGUAGE

## SEC. 1001. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 1001.** This title may be cited as the "Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Regulatory Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 1002. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO REGULATORY LANGUAGE

**SEC. 1002.** The Congress finds the following with respect to regulatory language:

**(1) Statutory Clarity**
Clear, precise regulatory language is essential for effective implementation. Ambiguity in statutory language leads to inconsistent implementation, legal challenges, and delayed action.

**(2) Regulatory Coordination**
Regulatory language must coordinate with existing federal, state, and local regulations to avoid conflicts and duplication.

**(3) Enforcement**
Regulatory language must include clear enforcement provisions to ensure compliance.

**(4) Adaptability**
Regulatory language must be adaptable to changing conditions and new information.

**(5) Stakeholder Input**
Regulatory language must be developed with input from stakeholders to ensure practicality and effectiveness.

## SEC. 1003. PURPOSES OF TITLE VIII

**SEC. 1003.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Amend existing federal statutes to implement the provisions of this Act.

**(2)** Establish clear regulatory language for implementation.

**(3)** Coordinate regulatory language with existing federal, state, and local regulations.

**(4)** Provide for enforcement of regulatory requirements.

**(5)** Ensure adaptability of regulatory language to changing conditions.

---

## SEC. 1004. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VIII

**SEC. 1004.** In this title:

**(1) "Regulatory Language"**
The specific statutory language used to implement the provisions of this Act.

**(2) "Amendment"**
A change to an existing federal statute to implement the provisions of this Act.

**(3) "Enforcement"**
The process of ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

## SEC. 1005. AMENDMENTS TO FEDERAL LAND POLICY AND MANAGEMENT ACT

**SEC. 1005.** The Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (43 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 117. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND VEGETATION MANAGEMENT.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall conduct vegetation management activities on public lands to reduce wildfire risk, consistent with the National Wildfire Prevention Strategy established under the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) ANNUAL TARGETS.—**The Secretary shall treat a minimum of 5 million acres of public lands annually for hazardous fuel reduction.

**(c) PRESCRIBED FIRE.—**The Secretary shall apply prescribed fire to a minimum of 3 million acres of public lands annually.

**(d) CULTURAL BURNING.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for cultural burning on public lands within their ancestral territories."

## SEC. 1008. AMENDMENTS TO STAFFORD DISASTER RELIEF ACT

## SEC. 1009. AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ACT

**SEC. 1009.** The National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 52. WILDFIRE PREVENTION CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The following wildfire prevention activities are categorically excluded from the requirement to prepare an environmental assessment or environmental impact statement:

**(1)** Defensible space creation within 100 feet of existing structures
**(2)** Prescribed fire in accordance with approved prescription plans
**(3)** Mechanical thinning of trees less than 12 inches diameter
**(4)** Fuel break construction in previously treated areas
**(5)** Covered conductor installation on existing rights-of-way
**(6)** Equipment replacement on existing structures
**(7)** Undergrounding in existing road corridors
**(8)** Vegetation management within existing rights-of-way

**(b) CONDITIONS.—**Categorical exclusions under this section are available only if the activity incorporates applicable conservation measures for threatened and endangered species."

## SEC. 1015. REGULATORY COORDINATION

(A) Department of Energy
(B) Department of Interior
(C) Department of Homeland Security
(D) Environmental Protection Agency
(E) Department of Commerce

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state agencies on regulatory implementation.

**(b) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on regulatory activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) TRIBAL REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the regulatory authority of Tribal nations.

---

## SEC. 1016. REGULATORY PREEMPTION

**SEC. 1016.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national regulatory standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt regulatory standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

**SEC. 1017.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

**SEC. 1018.** (a) ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT.—**This title does not amend the Administrative Procedure Act.

**(b) STATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state regulatory agencies.

## SEC. 1022. REGULATORY ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1022.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—

**(1) NON-COMPLIANCE.—**Any person who fails to comply with regulatory requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(2) FALSE REPORTING.—**Any person who falsely reports regulatory data shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $100,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—

**(1) FRAUD.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with regulatory activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

**(2) EMBEZZLEMENT.—**Any person who embezzles regulatory funds shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 1023. REGULATORY JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 1023.** (a) REVIEW.—**Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review in the United States Court of Appeals for the circuit in which the person resides.

**(b) STANDARD OF REVIEW.—**The court shall set aside any action found to be arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.

---

## SEC. 1024. REGULATORY SAVINGS CLAUSE

**SEC. 1024.** Nothing in this title shall be construed to:

**(1)** Diminish the authority of state or local governments.

**(2)** Affect the regulation of insurance by state insurance commissioners.

**(3)** Affect the authority of the Occupational Safety and Health Administration over workplace safety.

**(4)** Affect the authority of the Environmental Protection Agency over environmental protection.

**(5)** Diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.
