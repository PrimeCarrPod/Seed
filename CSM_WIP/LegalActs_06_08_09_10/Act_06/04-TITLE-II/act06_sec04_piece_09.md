## SEC. 412. ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE FOR GRID HARDENING

**SEC. 412.** (a) NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ACT.—

**(1) PROGRAMMATIC ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STATEMENT.—**The Secretary of Energy shall prepare a programmatic environmental impact statement for grid hardening activities.

**(2) CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.—**The Secretary shall develop categorical exclusions for routine grid hardening activities, including:
(A) Covered conductor installation on existing rights-of-way
(B) Equipment replacement on existing structures
(C) Undergrounding in existing road corridors
(D) Vegetation management within existing rights-of-way

**(b) ENDANGERED SPECIES ACT.—**

**(1) PROGRAMMATIC BIOLOGICAL OPINION.—**The Secretary shall obtain a programmatic biological opinion for grid hardening activities.

**(2) CONSERVATION MEASURES.—**Grid hardening activities shall incorporate conservation measures for threatened and endangered species.

**(c) CLEAN WATER ACT.—**

**(1) STORMWATER MANAGEMENT.—**Grid hardening activities shall incorporate stormwater management measures to prevent erosion and water quality impacts.

**(2) BUFFER ZONES.—**Undergrounding activities shall maintain buffer zones around waterways and wetlands.

## SEC. 413. TRIBAL GRID HARDENING PROGRAM

**SEC. 413.** (a) TRIBAL GRID HARDENING FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Tribal Grid Hardening Fund" to provide financial assistance for grid hardening on Tribal lands.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $2,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**

**(1) TRIBAL ENERGY OFFICES.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to Tribal nations for grid hardening planning and implementation.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide training for Tribal members on grid hardening construction and maintenance.

**(c) PRIORITY.—**Tribal grid hardening projects shall receive priority for federal funding, with the federal share not exceeding 95%.

## SEC. 414. RURAL GRID HARDENING PROGRAM

**SEC. 414.** (a) RURAL GRID HARDENING FUND.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "Rural Grid Hardening Fund" to provide financial assistance for grid hardening in rural areas.

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to the Fund $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(b) COOPERATIVE UTILITIES.—**

**(1) PRIORITY.—**Rural electric cooperative utilities shall receive priority for federal funding, with the federal share not exceeding 90%.

**(2) TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide technical assistance to rural electric cooperatives for grid hardening planning and implementation.
