## SEC. 415. GRID HARDENING INNOVATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 415.** (a) INNOVATION CHALLENGES.—

**(1) IGNITION PREVENTION CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary of Energy shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $50,000,000 for the development of a technology that reduces electrical infrastructure ignition risk by 99%.

**(2) UNDERGROUNDING COST REDUCTION CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a technology that reduces undergrounding costs by 50%.

**(3) MICROGRID RESILIENCE CHALLENGE.—**The Secretary shall conduct an innovation challenge with a prize of $25,000,000 for the development of a microgrid system that can sustain critical facilities for 72+ hours during PSPS events.

**(b) RESEARCH PARTNERSHIPS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL LABORATORY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with national laboratories on grid hardening research and development.

**(2) UNIVERSITY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with universities on grid hardening research and development.

**(3) INDUSTRY PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with private sector companies on grid hardening research and development.

**(c) TESTING FACILITIES.—**

**(1) GRID HARDENING TEST CENTER.—**The Secretary shall establish a national grid hardening test center for testing new technologies and materials.

**(2) FIELD TESTING.—**The Secretary shall fund field testing of advanced grid hardening technologies in high fire risk areas.

## SEC. 416. GRID HARDENING DATA AND ANALYTICS

**SEC. 416.** (a) NATIONAL GRID HARDENING DATABASE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish a national database for grid hardening data, including:
(A) Utility system hardening plans
(B) Grid hardening project data
(C) Ignition incident data
(D) Equipment condition data
(E) Vegetation management data
(F) PSPS event data

**(2) DATA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary shall establish data standards for grid hardening data collection and reporting.

**(b) PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS.—**

**(1) IGNITION RISK MODELING.—**The Secretary shall develop predictive models for ignition risk, incorporating:
(A) Weather data
(B) Vegetation data
(C) Equipment condition data
(D) Historical ignition data

**(2) RESOURCE ALLOCATION.—**The Secretary shall develop tools for optimizing grid hardening resource allocation based on ignition risk.

**(c) PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying grid hardening progress and performance data.

**(2) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on grid hardening progress.
