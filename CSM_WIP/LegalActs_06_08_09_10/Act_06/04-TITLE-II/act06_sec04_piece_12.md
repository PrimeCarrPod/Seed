## SEC. 420. GRID HARDENING SAFETY STANDARDS

**SEC. 420.** (a) OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY.—

**(1) OSHA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary of Labor, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy, shall establish occupational safety standards for grid hardening workers, including:
(A) Fall protection requirements
(B) Electrical safety requirements
(C) Heat illness prevention
(D) Personal protective equipment standards
(E) Emergency response procedures

**(2) TRAINING REQUIREMENTS.—**All grid hardening workers shall complete mandatory safety training before working on electrical infrastructure.

**(b) PUBLIC SAFETY.—

**(1) ELECTROMAGNETIC FIELD STANDARDS.—**The Secretary shall establish standards for electromagnetic field exposure from undergrounded lines.

**(2) FIRE SAFETY.—**The Secretary shall establish fire safety standards for battery storage systems and microgrids.

**(3) ACCESSIBILITY.—**The Secretary shall ensure that grid hardening activities maintain accessibility for emergency vehicles and personnel.

---

## SEC. 421. GRID HARDENING ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW

**SEC. 421.** (a) STREAMLINED REVIEW.—

**(1) CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish categorical exclusions for routine grid hardening activities to streamline environmental review.

**(2) PROGRAMMATIC REVIEWS.—**The Secretary shall conduct programmatic environmental reviews for grid hardening programs to avoid project-by-project review.

**(b) MITIGATION.—

**(1) HABITAT MITIGATION.—**Grid hardening activities that impact wildlife habitat shall include habitat mitigation measures.

**(2) CULTURAL RESOURCE PROTECTION.—**Grid hardening activities shall include measures to protect cultural resources.

---

## SEC. 422. GRID HARDENING PREEMPTION

**SEC. 422.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national grid hardening standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt grid hardening standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 423. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 423.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 424. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 424.** (a) FEDERAL POWER ACT.—**This title amends the Federal Power Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) STATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state public utilities commissions.

**(c) UTILITY REGULATION.—**This title does not change the regulatory status of electrical utilities.
