## SEC. 405. NATIONAL GRID HARDENING STANDARDS

**SEC. 405.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, the Secretary of Energy, in coordination with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC), shall establish national grid hardening standards for electrical infrastructure in high fire risk areas.

**(b) STANDARDS.—**The standards shall include:

**(1) Covered Conductor Requirements**
All new overhead distribution lines in high fire risk areas shall use covered conductor. Existing overhead distribution lines in high fire risk areas shall be retrofitted with covered conductor within 10 years.

**(2) Undergrounding Requirements**
All new distribution lines in the highest fire risk areas (Fire Hazard Severity Zone "Very High") shall be undergrounded. Existing distribution lines in these areas shall be undergrounded within 15 years where technically feasible.

**(3) Rapid Fault Isolation**
All distribution lines in high fire risk areas shall be equipped with rapid fault isolation technology within 5 years.

**(4) Fast Curve Switching**
All protective relays on distribution lines in high fire risk areas shall be configured with fast curve switching settings within 2 years.

**(5) Equipment Replacement**
All aging equipment (conductors, insulators, transformers, switches) on distribution lines in high fire risk areas shall be replaced on an accelerated schedule, with priority for equipment over 40 years old.

**(6) Vegetation Management**
All vegetation within the utility right-of-way in high fire risk areas shall be managed consistent with the standards established under Sec. 309(b)(1).

**(7) Weather Monitoring**
All utilities in high fire risk areas shall deploy weather monitoring stations at intervals of no more than 10 miles along distribution lines.

**(8) System Hardening Plans**
All utilities shall develop and submit to the Secretary a system hardening plan that identifies the schedule, cost, and prioritization of hardening activities.

**(c) COMPLIANCE TIMELINE.—**

**(1) SHORT-TERM.—**Utilities shall achieve compliance with fast curve switching and weather monitoring requirements within 2 years.

**(2) MEDIUM-TERM.—**Utilities shall achieve compliance with rapid fault isolation and equipment replacement requirements within 5 years.

**(3) LONG-TERM.—**Utilities shall achieve compliance with covered conductor and undergrounding requirements within 10-15 years.
