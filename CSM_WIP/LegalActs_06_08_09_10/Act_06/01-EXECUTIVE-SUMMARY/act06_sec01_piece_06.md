**Department of Interior**
- Lead agency for federal lands vegetation management
- Develop and implement Vegetation Management Plans (VMPs)

**USDA Forest Service**
- National forest fire prevention and suppression
- Community firebreak grant administration
- Research and development programs
- International cooperation

**Department of Energy**
- Grid hardening technical assistance
- Research and development for fire-resistant infrastructure
- Coordination with FERC and NERC
- Energy storage deployment

**FEMA**
- Emergency response coordination
- Community grant administration
- Post-fire recovery programs
- National Incident Management System integration

**NIST**
- Building material standards development

- WUI mapping and risk assessment

### State Implementation Plans
- States required to submit implementation plans within 18 months
- Plans must demonstrate compliance with federal minimum standards
- Federal approval required within 12 months of submission
- Annual progress reporting required

### Compliance and Enforcement
- Federal agencies authorized to inspect and audit
- Civil penalties for non-compliance (up to $50,000/day)
- Citizen suit provisions for enforcement
- Administrative appeals process
