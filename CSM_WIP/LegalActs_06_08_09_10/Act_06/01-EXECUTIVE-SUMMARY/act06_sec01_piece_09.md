## ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE PRIORITIES

1. **Grant Funding Priority:** 40% of funds allocated to disadvantaged communities
2. **Language Access:** All materials in English, Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese
3. **Tribal Consultation:** Government-to-government consultation required
4. **Health Impact Assessment:** Required for all funded projects
5. **Community Benefit Agreements:** Required for projects over $10M
6. **Workforce Development:** Targeted training for EJ community members
