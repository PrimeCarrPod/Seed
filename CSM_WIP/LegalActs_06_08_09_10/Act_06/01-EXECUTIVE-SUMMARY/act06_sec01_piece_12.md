## CONCLUSION

The Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026 represents a comprehensive, fully-funded national response to the wildfire crisis. With $46 billion in authorized spending over 10 years, the Act will:

1. **Reduce wildfire ignitions** through mandatory vegetation management and grid hardening
2. **Protect communities** through updated building codes and defensible space requirements
3. **Save lives** through advanced early warning and detection systems
4. **Modernize response** through fleet expansion and interagency coordination
5. **Create jobs** through infrastructure investment and workforce development
6. **Protect property values** through risk reduction and insurance market stabilization
7. **Advance environmental justice** through prioritized funding for disadvantaged communities

The Act is fully offset, with no net cost to the federal government. The benefit-cost ratio of 5.9:1 demonstrates strong return on investment. Implementation begins immediately upon enactment, with full deployment within 10 years.
