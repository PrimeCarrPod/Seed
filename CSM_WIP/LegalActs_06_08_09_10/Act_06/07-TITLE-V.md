# TITLE V — RESPONSE MODERNIZATION

## SEC. 701. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 701.** This title may be cited as the "Wildfire Response Modernization Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 702. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO RESPONSE MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 702.** The Congress finds the following with respect to wildfire response:

**(1) Aging Fleet**
The federal wildfire response fleet is dangerously obsolete. The average age of the federal air tanker fleet is 52 years. Many aircraft are no longer supported by manufacturers. Maintenance costs have increased 300% in the past decade.

**(2) Inadequate Coordination**
Interagency coordination protocols have not been updated since 2003. Communication systems are incompatible across agencies. Joint training exercises are infrequent and underfunded.

**(3) Technology Gap**
Advanced firefighting technologies are not widely deployed. Night vision capabilities are limited. Unmanned aerial systems are underutilized. Real-time fire mapping is not available to all incident commanders.

**(4) Workforce Shortage**
Federal agencies face 25% vacancy rates in firefighter positions. The wildfire workforce has not kept pace with increasing fire activity. Mental health support for firefighters is inadequate.

**(5) Community Notification**
Early warning and evacuation systems are inadequate. Alert times in covered areas average 8 minutes — insufficient for vulnerable populations. False alarm rates of 35% reduce public trust.

**(6) Post-Fire Recovery**
Post-fire recovery programs are underfunded and slow. Watershed restoration, debris removal, and community rebuilding take years. Mental health support for affected communities is inadequate.

## SEC. 703. PURPOSES OF TITLE V

**SEC. 703.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Modernize the federal wildfire response fleet with next-generation aircraft and equipment.

**(2)** Improve interagency coordination through updated protocols, compatible communication systems, and joint training.

**(3)** Deploy advanced firefighting technologies, including night vision, unmanned aerial systems, and real-time fire mapping.

**(4)** Expand and support the wildfire response workforce through recruitment, training, and mental health programs.

**(5)** Improve early warning and evacuation systems to provide adequate time for community evacuation.

**(6)** Strengthen post-fire recovery programs for watershed restoration, debris removal, and community rebuilding.

**(7)** Establish accountability metrics and reporting requirements to ensure response modernization achieves measurable improvements.

**(8)** Protect the lives of firefighters and the public through improved safety equipment and protocols.

---

## SEC. 704. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE V

**SEC. 704.** In this title:

**(1) "Incident Command System (ICS)"**
A standardized on-scene management system designed to enable effective and efficient incident management by integrating facilities, equipment, personnel, procedures, and communications.

**(2) "Unmanned Aerial System (UAS)"**
An aircraft operated without the possibility of direct human intervention from within or on the aircraft, including drones and other autonomous systems.

**(3) "Night Vision Capability"**
The ability to conduct aerial firefighting operations during nighttime hours using night vision technology.

**(4) "Real-Time Fire Mapping"**
The use of satellite, aerial, and ground-based sensors to produce current maps of fire perimeter, intensity, and spread.

**(5) "Post-Fire Recovery"**
The process of restoring areas affected by wildfire, including watershed restoration, debris removal, erosion control, and community rebuilding.

## SEC. 705. FEDERAL WILDFIRE RESPONSE FLEET MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 705.** (a) AIR TANKER MODERNIZATION.—

**(1) REPLACEMENT.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall develop and implement a plan to replace the federal air tanker fleet with next-generation aircraft within 10 years.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**Next-generation air tankers shall:
(A) Have a minimum capacity of 3,000 gallons
(B) Be capable of night operations with night vision technology
(C) Have a maximum response time of 30 minutes from dispatch
(D) Be equipped with real-time fire mapping sensors
(E) Have a service life of at least 30 years

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000,000 over 10 years for air tanker modernization.

**(b) HELICOPTER MODERNIZATION.—

**(1) REPLACEMENT.—**The Secretary shall develop and implement a plan to replace the federal helicopter fleet with next-generation helicopters within 10 years.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**Next-generation helicopters shall:
(A) Have a minimum capacity of 1,000 gallons
(B) Be capable of night operations with night vision technology
(C) Have a maximum response time of 20 minutes from dispatch
(D) Be equipped with real-time fire mapping sensors

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $3,000,000,000 over 10 years for helicopter modernization.

**(c) GROUND EQUIPMENT MODERNIZATION.—

**(1) FIRE ENGINES.—**The Secretary shall replace aging fire engines with next-generation engines equipped with:
(A) GPS tracking
(B) Real-time communication
(C) Night vision capabilities
(D) Remote-controlled nozzles

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000,000 over 10 years for ground equipment modernization.

## SEC. 706. INTERAGENCY COORDINATION MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 706.** (a) UPDATED PROTOCOLS.—

**(1) REVISION.—**The Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Interior and the Federal Emergency Management Agency, shall revise interagency wildfire coordination protocols within 1 year.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**Updated protocols shall include:
(A) Unified command structure
(B) Standardized communication procedures
(C) Joint resource allocation
(D) Shared situational awareness
(E) Integrated evacuation planning

**(b) COMMUNICATION SYSTEMS.—

**(1) INTEROPERABLE SYSTEMS.—**The Secretary shall deploy interoperable communication systems across all federal, state, and local wildfire agencies.

**(2) SATELLITE COMMUNICATION.—**The Secretary shall deploy satellite communication systems for use in areas without cellular coverage.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $1,000,000,000 over 10 years for communication system modernization.

**(c) JOINT TRAINING.—

**(1) ANNUAL EXERCISES.—**The Secretary shall conduct annual interagency wildfire training exercises.

**(2) TRAINING CENTER.—**The Secretary shall establish a National Wildfire Training Center for interagency training.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500,000,000 over 10 years for joint training programs.

## SEC. 707. ADVANCED FIREFIGHTING TECHNOLOGIES

**SEC. 707.** (a) NIGHT VISION PROGRAM.—

**(1) DEPLOYMENT.—**The Secretary shall deploy night vision capabilities to all federal aerial firefighting assets within 5 years.

**(2) TRAINING.—**The Secretary shall provide night vision training for all aerial firefighting crews.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500,000,000 over 10 years for night vision deployment.

**(b) UNMANNED AERIAL SYSTEMS.—

**(1) DEPLOYMENT.—**The Secretary shall deploy UAS for fire mapping, ignition detection, and aerial ignition operations.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**UAS shall be capable of:
(A) Real-time fire perimeter mapping
(B) Thermal imaging for hotspot detection
(C) Aerial ignition for prescribed fire
(D) Communication relay in remote areas

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $1,000,000,000 over 10 years for UAS deployment.

**(c) REAL-TIME FIRE MAPPING.—

**(1) DEPLOYMENT.—**The Secretary shall deploy real-time fire mapping capabilities to all incident command posts.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**Real-time fire mapping shall provide:
(A) Fire perimeter updates every 15 minutes
(B) Fire intensity mapping
(C) Fire spread prediction
(D) Resource tracking

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $500,000,000 over 10 years for real-time fire mapping.

## SEC. 710. POST-FIRE RECOVERY PROGRAM

**SEC. 710.** (a) WATERSHED RESTORATION.—

**(1) EMERGENCY STABILIZATION.—**The Secretary shall conduct emergency stabilization of burned watersheds within 30 days of fire containment.

**(2) LONG-TERM RESTORATION.—**The Secretary shall conduct long-term watershed restoration, including:
(A) Erosion control
(B) Revegetation
(C) Debris removal
(D) Infrastructure repair

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $3,000,000,000 over 10 years for watershed restoration.

**(b) DEBRIS REMOVAL.—

**(1) HAZARDOUS MATERIALS.—**The Secretary shall conduct hazardous materials removal from burned structures.

**(2) STRUCTURAL DEBRIS.—**The Secretary shall conduct structural debris removal from burned properties.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $1,000,000,000 over 10 years for debris removal.

**(c) COMMUNITY REBUILDING.—

**(1) REBUILDING ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to communities for rebuilding after wildfire.

**(2) FIRE-RESISTANT REBUILDING.—**Rebuilt structures shall comply with fire-resistant building standards.

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000,000 over 10 years for community rebuilding.

**(d) MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT.—

**(1) COMMUNITY COUNSELING.—**The Secretary shall provide mental health counseling to communities affected by wildfire.

**(2) LONG-TERM SUPPORT.—**Mental health support shall be available for 5 years following a wildfire event.

## SEC. 727. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 727.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—

**(1) NON-COMPLIANCE.—**Any person who fails to comply with wildfire response standards shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(2) FALSE REPORTING.—**Any person who falsely reports wildfire response data shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $100,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—

**(1) WILLFUL NON-COMPLIANCE.—**Any person who willfully fails to comply with wildfire response standards, resulting in injury or death, shall be subject to criminal penalties.

**(2) FRAUD.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with wildfire response funding shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 728. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 728.** (a) REVIEW.—**Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review in the United States Court of Appeals for the circuit in which the person resides.
