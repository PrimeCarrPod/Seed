## SEC. 805. NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREVENTION FUND

**SEC. 805.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "National Wildfire Prevention Fund."

**(b) PURPOSES.—**The Fund shall provide financial assistance for:

**(1)** Wildfire prevention activities under Title I
**(2)** Grid hardening activities under Title II
**(3)** Vegetation management activities under Title III
**(4)** Fire-resistant construction activities under Title IV
**(5)** Wildfire response modernization under Title V
**(6)** Implementation activities under Title VII
**(7)** Economic impact mitigation under Title IX
**(8)** Enforcement activities under Title X

**(c) FUNDING SOURCES.—**The Fund shall receive revenues from:

**(1)** Carbon pricing receipts under Sec. 806
**(2)** Green bond proceeds under Sec. 807
**(3)** Utility assessments under Sec. 808
**(4)** Federal appropriations under Sec. 809
**(5)** Interest earnings on Fund balances
**(6)** Repayments of loans made from the Fund

**(d) ALLOCATION.—**Funds in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund shall be allocated as follows:

**(1)** Title I (Wildfire Prevention): 25%
**(2)** Title II (Grid Hardening): 25%
**(3)** Title III (Vegetation Management): 15%
**(4)** Title IV (Building Standards): 10%
**(5)** Title V (Response Modernization): 15%
**(6)** Title VII (Implementation): 5%
**(7)** Title IX (Economic Impact): 3%
**(8)** Title X (Enforcement): 2%

**(e) MANAGEMENT.—**The Fund shall be managed by the Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy and the Secretary of Interior.

**(f) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on Fund receipts, expenditures, and balances.
