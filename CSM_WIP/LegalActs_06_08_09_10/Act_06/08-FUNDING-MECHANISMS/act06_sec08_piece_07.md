## SEC. 814. FUND COORDINATION

**SEC. 814.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) FEDERAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate Fund activities with:
(A) Department of Energy
(B) Department of Interior
(C) Department of the Treasury
(D) Department of Homeland Security
(E) Environmental Protection Agency

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state agencies on Fund distribution and oversight.

**(b) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on Fund activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) CO-MANAGEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for Fund activities on federal lands within their ancestral territories.

---

## SEC. 815. FUND PREEMPTION

**SEC. 815.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national funding standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt funding standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 816. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 816.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 817. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 817.** (a) BUDGET ACT.—**This title amends the Congressional Budget Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) STATE TAXING AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state and local governments to impose taxes or fees.

**(c) UTILITY REGULATION.—**This title does not change the regulatory status of electrical utilities.
