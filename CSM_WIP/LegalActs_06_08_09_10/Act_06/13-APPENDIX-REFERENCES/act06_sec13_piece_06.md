## SEC. 1308. APPENDIX D — DATA SOURCES

**SEC. 1308.** The following data sources are incorporated by reference:

**(1) Fire Data**
(A) National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC) — Wildfire statistics and incident reports
(B) Monitoring Trends in Burn Severity (MTBS) — Burn severity mapping
(C) Fire INventory from NCAR (FINN) — Fire emissions estimates
(D) Global Fire Emissions Database (GFED) — Global fire emissions

**(2) Climate Data**
(A) National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) — Climate and weather data
(B) PRISM Climate Group — High-resolution climate mapping
(C) U.S. Drought Monitor — Drought conditions and forecasts

**(3) Vegetation and Fuel Data**
(A) LANDFIRE — Vegetation, fuel, and fire regime data
(B) USDA Forest Service Forest Inventory and Analysis (FIA) — Forest inventory data
(C) National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS) — Agricultural and land use data

**(4) Socioeconomic Data**
(A) U.S. Census Bureau — Demographic and economic data
(B) Bureau of Labor Statistics — Employment and wage data
(C) Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) — Health data

**(5) Infrastructure Data**
(A) Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) — Electrical grid data
(B) U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) — Water and watershed data
(C) Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) — Flood and disaster data
