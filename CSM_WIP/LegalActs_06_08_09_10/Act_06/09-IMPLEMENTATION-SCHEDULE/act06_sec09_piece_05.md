## SEC. 909. PHASE 4 — MATURATION (MONTHS 61-120)

**SEC. 909.** (a) PHASE 4 ACTIVITIES.—During months 61-120 following enactment, the following activities shall be completed:

**(1)** Scaling of vegetation management to 25 million acres annually
**(2)** Scaling of prescribed fire to 10 million acres annually
**(3)** Full deployment of detection camera network
**(4)** Hiring of 50,000 additional prevention workers
**(5)** Retrofitting of 2,000,000 structures to fire-resistant standards
**(6)** Hardening of 50,000 miles of power lines
**(7)** Certification of 5,000 Fire-Adapted Communities
**(8)** Completion of 5,000 Community Wildfire Protection Plans

**(b) PHASE 4 MILESTONES.—**The following milestones shall be achieved by the end of Phase 4:

**(1)** 25 million acres treated annually
**(2)** 10 million acres of prescribed fire annually
**(3)** 10,000 detection cameras deployed
**(4)** 95,000 total prevention workers
**(5)** 2,000,000 structures retrofitted
**(6)** 50,000 miles of power lines hardened
**(7)** 5,000 Fire-Adapted Communities certified
**(8)** 5,000 Community Wildfire Protection Plans completed

## SEC. 910. IMPLEMENTATION ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 910.** (a) ANNUAL IMPLEMENTATION REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress an annual implementation report.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Progress against implementation milestones
(B) Expenditures by program and activity
(C) Performance metrics and trend analysis
(D) Lessons learned and best practices
(E) Recommendations for course correction
(F) Updated implementation plan

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of implementation progress.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Progress toward milestones
(B) Effectiveness of implementation strategies
(C) Adequacy of funding
(D) Coordination across stakeholders
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying implementation progress.

**(2) DATA.—**The dashboard shall display:
(A) Milestone completion status
(B) Expenditure data
(C) Performance metrics
(D) Geographic progress maps
