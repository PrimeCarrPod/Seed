## SEC. 905. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

**SEC. 905.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment, the Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy, the Secretary of Interior, and other relevant federal agencies, shall develop a comprehensive implementation plan for this Act.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**The implementation plan shall include:

**(1) Roles and Responsibilities**
Clear definition of roles and responsibilities for each federal agency, state government, Tribal nation, local government, and utility involved in implementation.

**(2) Phased Schedule**
A phased implementation schedule with specific milestones and deadlines for each title of this Act.

**(3) Resource Requirements**
An assessment of the resources (funding, personnel, equipment, technology) required for implementation.

**(4) Coordination Mechanisms**
Mechanisms for coordinating implementation across federal, state, Tribal, local, and private stakeholders.

**(5) Performance Metrics**
Quantifiable performance metrics for measuring implementation progress.

**(6) Risk Assessment**
An assessment of implementation risks and mitigation strategies.

**(7) Adaptive Management Framework**
A framework for adaptive management, including procedures for course correction based on experience.

**(c) UPDATES.—**The implementation plan shall be updated annually to reflect progress, lessons learned, and changing conditions.

**(d) PUBLIC COMMENT.—**The Secretary shall provide for public comment on the implementation plan and annual updates.

## SEC. 906. PHASE 1 — IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (MONTHS 1-12)

**SEC. 906.** (a) PHASE 1 ACTIVITIES.—During the first 12 months following enactment, the following activities shall be completed:

**(1)** Establishment of the National Wildfire Prevention Fund
**(2)** Development of the National Wildfire Prevention Strategy
**(3)** Development of grid hardening standards
**(4)** Development of the WUI building code
**(5)** Hiring of implementation staff
**(6)** Development of grant and loan application procedures
**(7)** Initiation of workforce recruitment
**(8)** Deployment of emergency detection cameras
**(9)** Development of prescribed fire certification program
**(10)** Initiation of public education campaigns

**(b) PHASE 1 MILESTONES.—**The following milestones shall be achieved by the end of Phase 1:

**(1)** Implementation plan published
**(2)** National Wildfire Prevention Fund established
**(3)** Grid hardening standards published
**(4)** WUI building code published
**(5)** Initial grant and loan applications accepted
**(6)** 5,000 new prevention workers hired
**(7)** 1,000 detection cameras deployed
**(8)** Prescribed fire certification program operational
