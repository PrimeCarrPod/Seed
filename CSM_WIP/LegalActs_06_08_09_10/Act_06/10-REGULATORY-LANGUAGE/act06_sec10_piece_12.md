## SEC. 1033. AMENDMENTS TO TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY PROVISIONS

**SEC. 1033.** (a) TRIBAL CONSULTATION.—**All regulatory activities under this Act shall include government-to-government consultation with affected Tribal nations.

**(b) TRIBAL REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the regulatory authority of Tribal nations.

**(c) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

---

## SEC. 1034. REGULATORY COORDINATION WITH STATES

**SEC. 1034.** (a) STATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt regulatory standards that are more protective than the national standards.

**(b) STATE IMPLEMENTATION.—**States shall develop state implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(c) STATE ENFORCEMENT.—**States shall enforce regulatory standards within their jurisdictions.

---

## SEC. 1035. REGULATORY SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1035.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
