## SEC. 1005. AMENDMENTS TO FEDERAL LAND POLICY AND MANAGEMENT ACT

**SEC. 1005.** The Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (43 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 117. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND VEGETATION MANAGEMENT.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall conduct vegetation management activities on public lands to reduce wildfire risk, consistent with the National Wildfire Prevention Strategy established under the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) ANNUAL TARGETS.—**The Secretary shall treat a minimum of 5 million acres of public lands annually for hazardous fuel reduction.

**(c) PRESCRIBED FIRE.—**The Secretary shall apply prescribed fire to a minimum of 3 million acres of public lands annually.

**(d) CULTURAL BURNING.—**The Secretary shall enter into co-management agreements with Tribal nations for cultural burning on public lands within their ancestral territories."

## SEC. 1006. AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL FOREST MANAGEMENT ACT

**SEC. 1006.** The National Forest Management Act of 1976 (16 U.S.C. 1600 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 23. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND VEGETATION MANAGEMENT.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall conduct vegetation management activities on National Forest System lands to reduce wildfire risk, consistent with the National Wildfire Prevention Strategy established under the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) ANNUAL TARGETS.—**The Secretary shall treat a minimum of 5 million acres of National Forest System lands annually for hazardous fuel reduction.

**(c) PRESCRIBED FIRE.—**The Secretary shall apply prescribed fire to a minimum of 3 million acres of National Forest System lands annually.

**(d) BIOMASS UTILIZATION.—**The Secretary shall develop markets for biomass removed during vegetation management activities."

## SEC. 1007. AMENDMENTS TO FEDERAL POWER ACT

**SEC. 1007.** The Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 26. GRID HARDENING STANDARDS.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Commission shall establish grid hardening standards for electrical transmission and distribution facilities in high fire risk areas, consistent with the standards established under the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) COMPLIANCE.—**All electrical utilities shall comply with the grid hardening standards established under this section.

**(c) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Commission may impose penalties for non-compliance with grid hardening standards."
