## SEC. 1008. AMENDMENTS TO STAFFORD DISASTER RELIEF ACT

**SEC. 1008.** The Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5121 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 69. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND RESILIENCE.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Administrator shall conduct wildfire prevention and resilience activities, consistent with the National Wildfire Prevention Strategy established under the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) PRE-DISASTER MITIGATION.—**The Administrator shall provide pre-disaster mitigation grants for wildfire prevention activities.

**(c) POST-DISASTER RECOVERY.—**The Administrator shall provide post-disaster recovery assistance for communities affected by wildfire, including watershed restoration, debris removal, and community rebuilding."

## SEC. 1009. AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ACT

**SEC. 1009.** The National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 52. WILDFIRE PREVENTION CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The following wildfire prevention activities are categorically excluded from the requirement to prepare an environmental assessment or environmental impact statement:

**(1)** Defensible space creation within 100 feet of existing structures
**(2)** Prescribed fire in accordance with approved prescription plans
**(3)** Mechanical thinning of trees less than 12 inches diameter
**(4)** Fuel break construction in previously treated areas
**(5)** Covered conductor installation on existing rights-of-way
**(6)** Equipment replacement on existing structures
**(7)** Undergrounding in existing road corridors
**(8)** Vegetation management within existing rights-of-way

**(b) CONDITIONS.—**Categorical exclusions under this section are available only if the activity incorporates applicable conservation measures for threatened and endangered species."

## SEC. 1010. AMENDMENTS TO CLEAN AIR ACT

**SEC. 1010.** The Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 709. PRESCRIBED FIRE SMOKE EXEMPTION.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**Prescribed fire conducted in accordance with an approved prescription plan shall be exempt from state air quality standards during the approved burn window, provided that best smoke management practices are employed.

**(b) BEST SMOKE MANAGEMENT PRACTICES.—**The Administrator shall establish best smoke management practices for prescribed fire operations."
