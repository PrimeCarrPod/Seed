## SEC. 1011. AMENDMENTS TO ENDANGERED SPECIES ACT

**SEC. 1011.** The Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 20. WILDFIRE PREVENTION CONSERVATION MEASURES.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**Wildfire prevention activities shall incorporate conservation measures for threatened and endangered species.

**(b) PROGRAMMATIC BIOLOGICAL OPINION.—**The Secretary shall obtain a programmatic biological opinion for wildfire prevention activities.

**(c) CONSERVATION MEASURES.—**The Secretary shall develop conservation measures for threatened and endangered species affected by wildfire prevention activities."

## SEC. 1012. AMENDMENTS TO CLEAN WATER ACT

**SEC. 1012.** The Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1251 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 902. WILDFIRE PREVENTION STORMWATER MANAGEMENT.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**Wildfire prevention activities shall incorporate stormwater management measures to prevent erosion and water quality impacts.

**(b) BUFFER ZONES.—**Vegetation management activities shall maintain buffer zones around waterways and wetlands.

**(c) STORMWATER PERMITS.—**Wildfire prevention activities shall obtain applicable stormwater permits."

## SEC. 1013. AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL HOUSING ACT

**SEC. 1013.** The National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 61. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall establish fire-resistant construction standards for housing in the Wildland-Urban Interface, consistent with the standards established under the Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026.

**(b) FEDERAL HOUSING.—**All new federal housing in the WUI shall comply with fire-resistant construction standards.

**(c) FEDERAL LOAN GUARANTEES.—**Federal loan guarantees for housing in the WUI shall require compliance with fire-resistant construction standards."

## SEC. 1014. AMENDMENTS TO INTERNAL REVENUE CODE

**SEC. 1014.** The Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 45BB. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION TAX CREDIT.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**There is allowed a tax credit of up to $10,000 for taxpayers who retrofit their primary residence to meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(b) COMMERCIAL BUILDINGS.—**Commercial buildings retrofitted to meet fire-resistant construction standards shall qualify for accelerated depreciation."
