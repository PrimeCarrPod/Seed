## SEC. 1015. REGULATORY COORDINATION

**SEC. 1015.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) FEDERAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate regulatory activities with:
(A) Department of Energy
(B) Department of Interior
(C) Department of Homeland Security
(D) Environmental Protection Agency
(E) Department of Commerce

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state agencies on regulatory implementation.

**(b) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on regulatory activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) TRIBAL REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the regulatory authority of Tribal nations.

---

## SEC. 1016. REGULATORY PREEMPTION

**SEC. 1016.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national regulatory standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt regulatory standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 1017. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1017.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 1018. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 1018.** (a) ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT.—**This title does not amend the Administrative Procedure Act.

**(b) STATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state regulatory agencies.

**(c) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.
