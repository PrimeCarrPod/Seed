## SEC. 1111. ECONOMIC MONITORING AND REPORTING

**SEC. 1111.** (a) ANNUAL ECONOMIC REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress an annual economic report.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Program expenditures by activity
(B) Benefits realized by activity
(C) Return on investment by program
(D) Economic impact by region
(E) Distributional analysis
(F) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of economic impacts.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Accuracy of cost-benefit analysis
(B) Distributional effects
(C) Return on investment
(D) Recommendations for improvement

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying economic data.

**(2) DATA.—**The dashboard shall display:
(A) Expenditure data
(B) Benefit data
(C) ROI data
(D) Geographic distribution

## SEC. 1112. ECONOMIC IMPACT PREEMPTION

**SEC. 1112.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national economic standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt economic standards that are more protective than the national standards.

## SEC. 1113. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1113.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 1114. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 1114.** (a) BUDGET ACT.—**This title amends the Congressional Budget Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) STATE TAXING AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state and local governments to impose taxes or fees.

**(c) UTILITY REGULATION.—**This title does not change the regulatory status of electrical utilities.
