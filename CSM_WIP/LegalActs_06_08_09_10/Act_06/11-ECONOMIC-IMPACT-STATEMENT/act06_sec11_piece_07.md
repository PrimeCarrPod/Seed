## SEC. 1116. ECONOMIC IMPACT REPORTING

**SEC. 1116.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on economic impacts, including:
(A) Program expenditures
(B) Benefits realized
(C) Return on investment
(D) Distributional analysis
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of economic impacts.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying economic data.

## SEC. 1117. ECONOMIC IMPACT SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1117.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
