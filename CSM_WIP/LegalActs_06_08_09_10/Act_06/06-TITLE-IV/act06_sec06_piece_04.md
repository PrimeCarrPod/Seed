## SEC. 606. FIRE-RESISTANT MATERIALS CERTIFICATION

**SEC. 606.** (a) CERTIFICATION PROGRAM.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall establish a Fire-Resistant Material Certification Program.

**(2) STANDARDS.—**The program shall establish testing standards for:
(A) Roof assemblies
(B) Exterior wall materials
(C) Window systems
(D) Vent systems
(E) Decking materials
(F) Fencing materials
(G) Insulation materials
(H) Sealants and caulks

**(3) CERTIFICATION PROCEDURES.—**The program shall establish procedures for:
(A) Material testing
(B) Factory inspection
(C) Certification issuance
(D) Labeling requirements
(E) Periodic re-certification

**(b) APPROVED MATERIALS LIST.—**

**(1) PUBLICATION.—**The Secretary shall publish and maintain a list of approved fire-resistant building materials.

**(2) UPDATES.—**The list shall be updated quarterly to include newly certified materials.

**(c) INNOVATION INCENTIVES.—**

**(1) RESEARCH GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants for research and development of new fire-resistant building materials.

**(2) TESTING ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide testing assistance to manufacturers developing new fire-resistant materials.

## SEC. 607. RETROFIT INCENTIVE PROGRAM

**SEC. 607.** (a) GRANTS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Homeland Security shall provide grants of up to $25,000 per structure for retrofitting existing structures in the WUI to meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(2) LOW-INCOME ASSISTANCE.—**The Secretary shall provide 100% cost coverage for retrofitting of structures owned by low-income households.

**(b) INSURANCE INCENTIVES.—**

**(1) PREMIUM REDUCTIONS.—**Structures retrofitted to meet fire-resistant construction standards shall qualify for reduced insurance premiums.

**(2) COVERAGE AVAILABILITY.—**Insurers shall make coverage available to structures that meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(c) PROPERTY TAX INCENTIVES.—**

**(1) ASSESSMENT REDUCTION.—**Local governments shall provide property tax assessment reductions for structures retrofitted to meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(2) FEE WAIVERS.—**Local governments shall waive permit fees for fire-resistant retrofits.
