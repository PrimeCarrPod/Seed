## SEC. 603. PURPOSES OF TITLE IV

**SEC. 603.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish national fire-resistant building standards for the Wildland-Urban Interface.

**(2)** Require all new construction in the WUI to meet fire-resistant building standards.

**(3)** Provide incentives for retrofitting existing structures in the WUI to meet fire-resistant building standards.

**(4)** Create a national certification program for fire-resistant building materials.

**(5)** Support research and development of new fire-resistant building materials and designs.

**(6)** Establish accountability metrics and reporting requirements to ensure building code compliance.

**(7)** Protect lives and property through fire-resistant construction.

**(8)** Reduce the economic burden of wildfire on individuals, businesses, and governments.

## SEC. 604. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE IV

**SEC. 604.** In this title:

**(1) "Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)"**
The zone where structures and other human development meet or intermingle with undeveloped wildland or vegetative fuels, as mapped by the USDA Forest Service SILVIS laboratory.

**(2) "Fire-Resistant Building Material"**
Any construction material that meets or exceeds the Class A fire resistance rating under ASTM E84 and has been certified under the Fire-Resistant Material Certification Program.

**(3) "Ignition-Resistant Construction"**
Building methods and materials designed to resist ignition from ember exposure, radiant heat, and direct flame contact.

**(4) "Ember-Resistant Vent"**
A vent designed to prevent the entry of embers into a structure, with screening of 1/8-inch mesh or smaller.

**(5) "Class A Roof Assembly"**
A roof assembly that meets the highest fire resistance rating under ASTM E108, including non-combustible covering and underlayment.

**(6) "Defensible Space"**
The area within 100 feet of a structure where vegetation and combustible materials are managed to reduce fire spread potential.
