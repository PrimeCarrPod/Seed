## SEC. 805. AMENDMENTS TO CHIPS AND SCIENCE ACT

**SEC. 805.** The CHIPS and Science Act of 2022 is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 107. CRITICAL TECHNOLOGY SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall implement critical technology supply chain security programs consistent with the Critical Technology Supply Chain Act of 2026.

**(b) AUTHORIZATION.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $60,000,000,000 over 10 years."

---

## SEC. 806. AMENDMENTS TO DEFENSE PRODUCTION ACT

**SEC. 806.** The Defense Production Act of 1950 is amended by adding at the end the following:

**"SEC. 723. CRITICAL TECHNOLOGY SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY.**

**(a) IN GENERAL.—**The President shall prioritize critical technology supply chain security under this Act.

**(b) AUTHORIZATION.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $50,000,000,000 over 10 years."
