## EXECUTIVE BRANCH COORDINATION

**SEC. 10.** (a) WHITE HOUSE COORDINATION.—The President shall designate a senior official in the White House to coordinate implementation of this Act across federal agencies.

**(b) AGENCY ROLES.—**The following agencies shall have primary responsibility for implementation:
(1) Department of Commerce — Overall coordination and semiconductor programs
(2) Department of Defense — National security aspects and strategic reserve
(3) Department of Energy — Rare earth minerals and energy technologies
(4) Department of Health and Human Services — Pharmaceutical supply chain
(5) Department of State — International partnerships
(6) Department of the Treasury — Funding mechanisms and tax incentives
(7) Office of the United States Trade Representative — Trade policy coordination

**(c) INTERAGENCY COUNCIL.—**There is established a Critical Technology Supply Chain Council to coordinate implementation across federal agencies.

## TRIBAL CONSULTATION

**SEC. 11.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Commerce shall consult with Tribal nations on implementation of this Act affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(b) TRIBAL PARTICIPATION.—**Tribal nations shall be eligible for grants and other assistance under this Act.

---

## STATE AND LOCAL COORDINATION

**SEC. 12.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Commerce shall coordinate with state and local governments on implementation of this Act.

**(b) STATE IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**States may develop state implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.
