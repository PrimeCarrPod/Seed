## NATIONAL SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

**SEC. 16.** (a) NATIONAL SECURITY REVIEW.—**The Secretary of Defense shall conduct a national security review of critical technology supply chains.

**(b) FOREIGN DEPENDENCY ASSESSMENT.—**The national security review shall include an assessment of foreign dependencies in critical technology supply chains.

**(c) MITIGATION STRATEGIES.—**The national security review shall include recommendations for mitigating foreign dependencies.

---

## ENVIRONMENTAL CONSIDERATIONS

**SEC. 17.** (a) ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall conduct an environmental review of activities under this Act.

**(b) SUSTAINABILITY.—**Activities under this Act shall be conducted in a manner that promotes environmental sustainability.

**(c) MINING STANDARDS.—**Rare earth mining and processing under this Act shall comply with applicable environmental standards.

---

## WORKFORCE CONSIDERATIONS

**SEC. 18.** (a) WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT.—**The Secretary of Labor shall develop workforce development programs for critical technology industries.

**(b) LABOR STANDARDS.—**Activities under this Act shall comply with applicable labor standards.

**(c) PREVAILING WAGE.—**Construction and manufacturing activities under this Act shall pay prevailing wages.
