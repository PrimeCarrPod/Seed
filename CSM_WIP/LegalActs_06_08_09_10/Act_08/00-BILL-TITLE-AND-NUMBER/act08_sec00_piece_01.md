## BILL SUMMARY

The Critical Technology Supply Chain Act of 2026 establishes a comprehensive framework for securing and strengthening the United States' critical technology supply chains. The Act addresses vulnerabilities in semiconductor manufacturing, rare earth minerals processing, pharmaceutical production, and other critical technology sectors. It creates incentives for domestic production, establishes strategic reserves, and coordinates international partnerships to reduce dependence on foreign suppliers.
