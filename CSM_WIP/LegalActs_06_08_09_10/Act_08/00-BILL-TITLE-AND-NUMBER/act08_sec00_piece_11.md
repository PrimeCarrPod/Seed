## APPROPRIATIONS

**SEC. 26.** (a) IN GENERAL.—**There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this Act $200,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) EMERGENCY APPROPRIATIONS.—**There is authorized to be appropriated an additional $50,000,000,000 for emergency critical technology supply chain activities.

**(c) AVAILABILITY.—**Funds appropriated under this Act shall remain available until expended.
