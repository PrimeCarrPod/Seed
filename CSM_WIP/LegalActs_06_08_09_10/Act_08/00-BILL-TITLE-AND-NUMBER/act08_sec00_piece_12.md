## SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 28.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this Act shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary of Commerce shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of this Act.

---

## TRANSITION

**SEC. 29.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of activities under this Act if the Act is not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF PROJECTS.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing projects and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.

---

## FINAL PROVISIONS

**SEC. 30.** (a) SEVERABILITY.—**If any provision of this Act or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the Act and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

**(b) SAVINGS CLAUSE.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of any federal agency under existing law.
