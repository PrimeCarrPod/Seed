## REGULATORY IMPACT ANALYSIS

**SEC. 13.** (a) REGULATORY IMPACT.—The Secretary of Commerce shall conduct a regulatory impact analysis of this Act.

**(b) SMALL BUSINESS IMPACT.—**The regulatory impact analysis shall include an assessment of the impact on small businesses.

**(c) UNFUNDED MANDATES.—**The regulatory impact analysis shall include an assessment of any unfunded mandates on state and local governments.
