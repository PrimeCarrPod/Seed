## ACCOUNTABILITY AND TRANSPARENCY

**SEC. 22.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall submit an annual report to Congress on implementation of this Act.

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of this Act.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying implementation data.

---

## WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTIONS

**SEC. 23.** (a) PROTECTIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish whistleblower protections for persons who report waste, fraud, or abuse under this Act.

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall establish a hotline for reporting waste, fraud, or abuse.

---

## CONSTRUCTION AND INTERPRETATION

**SEC. 24.** (a) RULES OF CONSTRUCTION.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to:
(1) Authorize the taking of private property without just compensation
(2) Supersede any existing federal law
(3) Create a private right of action

**(b) SEVERABILITY.—**If any provision of this Act is held invalid, the remainder of the Act shall not be affected.
