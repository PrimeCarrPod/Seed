## SEC. 323. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 323.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
