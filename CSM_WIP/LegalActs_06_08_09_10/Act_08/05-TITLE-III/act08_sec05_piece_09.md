## SEC. 318. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 318.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of pharmaceutical supply chain activities.

---

## SEC. 319. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN TRANSITION

**SEC. 319.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of pharmaceutical supply chain activities if the authorities are not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF ACTIVITIES.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing pharmaceutical supply chain activities and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.
