## SEC. 311. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN REPORTING

**SEC. 311.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall submit an annual report to Congress on pharmaceutical supply chain activities.

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of pharmaceutical supply chain programs.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying pharmaceutical supply chain data.

---

## SEC. 312. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 312.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
