**Research and Development**

- Public-private research partnerships

**Workforce Development**
- $5 billion for semiconductor workforce training
- Partnerships with community colleges and universities
- Apprenticeship programs for semiconductor manufacturing

### TITLE II — RARE EARTH MINERALS

**Domestic Mining and Processing**

**Environmental Standards**

- Reclamation requirements for mining sites
- Water and air quality protections

### TITLE III — PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN

**Domestic Manufacturing**

- $5 billion for active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) production

**Drug Shortage Prevention**
- Early warning system for drug shortages
- Strategic stockpile of critical medications
- Incentives for domestic production of essential medicines
