### TITLE IV — STRATEGIC RESERVE

**National Critical Technology Reserve**
- $15 billion to establish a strategic reserve of critical materials
- Stockpile of semiconductors, rare earth minerals, and pharmaceuticals
- Rotation and maintenance of reserve stocks
- Emergency release protocols

**Reserve Management**
- Department of Defense manages the reserve
- Regular audits and inventory management
- Coordination with industry partners

### TITLE V — INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

**Allied Supply Chain Agreements**
- $5 billion for supply chain agreements with allied nations
- Technology sharing with trusted partners
- Joint research and development programs
- Coordinated export controls

**Technology Transfer Protections**
- Safeguards against unauthorized technology transfer
- Intellectual property protections
- Research security requirements

### TITLE VI — FUNDING MECHANISMS

**Critical Technology Supply Chain Fund**
- $200 billion over 10 years
- Funded through appropriations, tariffs, and fees
- Equitable distribution across programs

**Tax Incentives**
- 25% tax credit for domestic semiconductor manufacturing
- 20% tax credit for rare earth processing
- 15% tax credit for pharmaceutical manufacturing
- Accelerated depreciation for critical technology investments
