## NATIONAL SECURITY BENEFITS

**(1) Reduced Foreign Dependence**
This Act reduces U.S. dependence on foreign suppliers for critical technologies, reducing vulnerability to supply chain disruptions and geopolitical leverage.

**(2) Defense Industrial Base**
This Act strengthens the defense industrial base by ensuring domestic production of critical components for weapons systems and military equipment.

**(3) Technology Leadership**
This Act maintains U.S. technology leadership by investing in next-generation semiconductor, rare earth, and pharmaceutical technologies.

**(4) Allied Cooperation**
This Act strengthens alliances through technology sharing and supply chain cooperation with trusted partners.

**(1) Domestic Environmental Standards**
This Act ensures that critical technology production meets U.S. environmental standards, which are among the world's most stringent.

This Act promotes rare earth recycling and sustainable mining practices, reducing environmental impact.

**(3) Clean Energy Transition**
This Act supports the clean energy transition by securing supply chains for electric vehicles, wind turbines, and solar panels.
