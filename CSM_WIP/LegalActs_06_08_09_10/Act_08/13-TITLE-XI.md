## SEC. 1111. APPENDIX F — GLOSSARY OF TERMS

**SEC. 1111.** The following terms are defined for purposes of this Act:

**(1) "Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API)"**
Any substance or mixture of substances intended to be used in the manufacture of a drug product.

**(2) "Critical Technology"**
Any technology designated as critical by the Secretary of Commerce.

**(3) "Domestic"**
Produced or manufactured within the United States or its territories.

**(4) "Foreign Entity of Concern"**
Any entity owned by, controlled by, or subject to the jurisdiction of a foreign country of concern.

**(5) "Rare Earth Minerals"**
The 17 chemical elements in the periodic table that are essential for modern technology.

**(6) "Semiconductor"**
A material that has electrical conductivity between that of a conductor and an insulator.

**(7) "Strategic Reserve"**
A stockpile of critical materials or products maintained by the federal government.

**(8) "Supply Chain"**
The network of organizations, people, activities, information, and resources involved in producing and delivering a product or service.

## SEC. 1115. APPENDIX J — ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE ANALYSIS

**SEC. 1115.** (a) DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—**Not less than 40% of Fund expenditures shall benefit disadvantaged communities.

**(b) TRIBAL COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—**Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures shall benefit Tribal communities.

**(c) RURAL COMMUNITY BENEFITS.—**Not less than 20% of Fund expenditures shall benefit rural communities.
