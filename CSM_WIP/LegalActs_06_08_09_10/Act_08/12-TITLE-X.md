# TITLE X — ENFORCEMENT AND COMPLIANCE

## SEC. 1001. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 1001.** This title may be cited as the "Critical Technology Supply Chain Enforcement Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 1002. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1002.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Compliance Necessity**
Effective enforcement is essential to ensure compliance with this Act.

**(2) Clear Standards**
Clear, measurable standards are necessary for effective enforcement.

**(3) Adequate Penalties**
Penalties must be sufficient to deter non-compliance.

---

## SEC. 1003. PURPOSES OF TITLE X

**SEC. 1003.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish enforcement mechanisms.

**(2)** Define penalties for non-compliance.

**(3)** Provide for due process protections.

---

## SEC. 1004. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE X

**SEC. 1004.** In this title:

**(1) "Enforcement"**
The process of ensuring compliance with this Act.

**(2) "Compliance"**
Conformity with the requirements of this Act.

## SEC. 1005. CIVIL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1005.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person who fails to comply with this Act shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation per day.

**(b) FACTORS.—**In determining the penalty amount, the Secretary shall consider:
(1) The nature and extent of the violation
(2) The violator's ability to pay
(3) The degree of culpability
(4) The economic benefit gained

---

## SEC. 1006. CRIMINAL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1006.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person who willfully violates this Act shall be subject to criminal penalties, including:
(1) A fine of up to $500,000 per violation
(2) Imprisonment for up to 5 years
(3) Both fine and imprisonment

---

## SEC. 1007. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE X

**SEC. 1007.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $5,000,000,000 over 10 years.
