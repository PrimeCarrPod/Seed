## SECTION 211. TRIBAL CONSULTATION

**SEC. 211.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Commerce shall consult with Tribal nations on implementation of this Act affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(b) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(c) TRIBAL PRIORITY.—**Tribal communities shall receive priority for critical technology projects under this Act.

---

## SECTION 212. STATE AND LOCAL COORDINATION

**SEC. 212.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Commerce shall coordinate with state and local governments on implementation of this Act.

**(b) STATE IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**States may develop state implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(c) LOCAL GOVERNMENT COORDINATION.—**Local governments shall be consulted on implementation activities affecting their jurisdictions.

---

## SECTION 213. ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

**SEC. 213.** (a) NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ACT.—**Activities under this Act shall comply with the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969.

**(b) ENDANGERED SPECIES ACT.—**Activities under this Act shall comply with the Endangered Species Act of 1973.

**(c) CLEAN AIR ACT.—**Activities under this Act shall comply with the Clean Air Act.

**(d) CLEAN WATER ACT.—**Activities under this Act shall comply with the Clean Water Act.
