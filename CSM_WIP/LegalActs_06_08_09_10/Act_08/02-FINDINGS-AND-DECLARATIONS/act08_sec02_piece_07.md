## SECTION 214. WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 214.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Labor shall develop workforce development programs for critical technology industries.

**(b) PARTNERSHIPS.—**Workforce development programs shall include partnerships with:
(1) Community colleges
(2) Universities
(3) Industry associations
(4) Labor organizations

**(c) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAMS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for critical technology careers.

---

## SECTION 215. RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 215.** (a) IN GENERAL.—The Secretary of Commerce shall coordinate research and development activities under this Act.

**(b) NATIONAL LABORATORIES.—**The Secretary shall utilize national laboratories for critical technology research and development.

**(c) PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall establish public-private research partnerships for critical technology development.

## SECTION 216. INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMPLIANCE

**SEC. 216.** (a) WTO COMPLIANCE.—**This Act shall be implemented in a manner consistent with United States obligations under the World Trade Organization.

**(b) TRADE AGREEMENT COMPLIANCE.—**This Act shall be implemented in a manner consistent with United States trade agreements.

**(c) DISPUTE RESOLUTION.—**Any disputes arising under this Act shall be resolved through applicable trade dispute resolution mechanisms.
