## SECTION 205. GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE

**SEC. 205.** (a) IN GENERAL.—This Act applies to all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and all U.S. Territories.

**(b) TRIBAL LANDS.—**This Act applies to Tribal lands subject to federal trust responsibilities, with implementation through government-to-government consultation.

**(c) STATE AND PRIVATE LANDS.—**This Act applies to state and private lands through grant programs, tax incentives, and regulatory requirements.

**(d) INTERNATIONAL APPLICATION.—**The provisions of Title V (International Partnerships) apply to international activities undertaken pursuant to this Act.

## SECTION 207. SEVERABILITY
