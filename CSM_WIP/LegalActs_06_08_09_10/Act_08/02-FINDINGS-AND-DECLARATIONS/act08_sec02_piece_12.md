## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — INTERNATIONAL ANALYSIS

**SEC. 227.** (a) ALLIED COOPERATION OPPORTUNITY.—**The United States has an opportunity to strengthen alliances through critical technology supply chain cooperation.

**(b) COMPETITION WITH CHINA.—**China invests $500 billion annually in critical technology industries. The United States must respond to maintain its technology leadership.

**(c) SUPPLY CHAIN RESILIENCE.—**Diversifying supply chains across allied nations reduces dependence on any single country.

## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — TECHNOLOGY ANALYSIS

**SEC. 228.** (a) SEMICONDUCTOR TECHNOLOGY.—**Semiconductors are the foundation of modern technology. Securing semiconductor supply chains is essential for economic and national security.

**(b) RARE EARTH TECHNOLOGY.—**Rare earth minerals are essential for defense systems, electric vehicles, wind turbines, and consumer electronics.

**(c) PHARMACEUTICAL TECHNOLOGY.—**Pharmaceutical supply chains are essential for public health and national security.
