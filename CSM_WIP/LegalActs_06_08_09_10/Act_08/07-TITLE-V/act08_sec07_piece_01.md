# TITLE V — INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

## SEC. 501. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 501.** This title may be cited as the "Allied Critical Technology Partnership Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 502. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

**SEC. 502.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Allied Cooperation**
The United States has an opportunity to strengthen alliances through critical technology supply chain cooperation. Allied nations share concerns about supply chain vulnerabilities.

**(2) Diversification**
Diversifying supply chains across allied nations reduces dependence on any single country. Allied nations have complementary capabilities in critical technologies.

**(3) Technology Sharing**
Technology sharing with trusted partners strengthens the entire allied technology base. Joint research and development accelerates innovation.

**(4) Export Controls**
Coordinated export controls prevent technology transfer to adversaries. Allied nations should coordinate export control policies.

---

## SEC. 503. PURPOSES OF TITLE V

**SEC. 503.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish allied supply chain agreements with trusted partners.

**(2)** Coordinate technology sharing with allied nations.

**(3)** Strengthen export control coordination.

**(4)** Promote joint research and development.

**(5)** Secure alternative supply sources in allied nations.

---

## SEC. 504. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE V

**SEC. 504.** In this title:

**(1) "Allied Nation"**
A nation with which the United States has a mutual defense treaty or close security relationship.

**(2) "Technology Transfer"**
The transfer of technology, technical data, or technical assistance to a foreign person or entity.

**(3) "Export Controls"**
Restrictions on the export of goods, technology, or technical data for national security or foreign policy reasons.
