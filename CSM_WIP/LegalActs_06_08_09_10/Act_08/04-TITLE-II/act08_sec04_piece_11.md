## SEC. 221. RARE EARTH MINERALS WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 221.** (a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Labor shall develop workforce development programs for rare earth minerals industries.

**(b) PARTNERSHIPS.—**Workforce development programs shall include partnerships with:
(1) Community colleges
(2) Universities
(3) Industry associations
(4) Labor organizations

**(c) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAMS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for rare earth minerals careers.

---

## SEC. 222. RARE EARTH MINERALS RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 222.** (a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Energy shall coordinate research and development activities for rare earth minerals.

**(b) NATIONAL LABORATORIES.—**The Secretary shall utilize national laboratories for rare earth minerals research and development.

**(c) PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall establish public-private research partnerships for rare earth minerals development.
