## SEC. 216. RARE EARTH MINERALS PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 216.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for rare earth minerals programs, including:

**(1)** Domestic rare earth production volume
**(2)** Domestic rare earth processing capacity
**(3)** Rare earth recycling rate
**(4)** Dependence on foreign rare earth imports
**(5)** Environmental compliance

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on rare earth minerals performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

## SEC. 217. RARE EARTH MINERALS EVALUATION

**SEC. 217.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of rare earth minerals programs every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of rare earth minerals strategies
(B) Efficiency of rare earth minerals operations
(C) Equity of rare earth minerals distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
