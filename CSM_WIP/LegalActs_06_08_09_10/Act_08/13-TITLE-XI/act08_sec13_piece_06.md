## SEC. 1114. APPENDIX I — PERFORMANCE METRICS SUMMARY

**SEC. 1114.** The following performance metrics are established:

**(1) Semiconductor Metrics**
(A) U.S. share of global semiconductor manufacturing
(B) Number of domestic fabrication facilities
(C) Domestic semiconductor production capacity
(D) Semiconductor workforce size

**(2) Rare Earth Metrics**
(A) Domestic rare earth production volume
(B) Domestic rare earth processing capacity
(C) Rare earth recycling rate
(D) Dependence on foreign rare earth imports

**(3) Pharmaceutical Metrics**
(A) Domestic pharmaceutical manufacturing capacity
(B) Domestic API production capacity
(C) Drug shortage frequency and duration
(D) Dependence on foreign pharmaceutical imports

**(4) Strategic Reserve Metrics**
(A) Reserve stock levels
(B) Reserve rotation frequency
(C) Emergency release readiness

**(5) International Partnership Metrics**
(A) Number of allied supply chain agreements
(B) Technology sharing agreements
(C) Joint research projects
