## SEC. 109. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 109.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary of Commerce shall submit to Congress an annual performance report on semiconductor supply chain programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) U.S. share of global semiconductor manufacturing
(B) Number of domestic fabrication facilities
(C) Domestic semiconductor production capacity
(D) Semiconductor workforce size
(E) Expenditures by program
(F) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of semiconductor supply chain programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Program effectiveness
(B) Efficiency of operations
(C) Adequacy of funding
(D) Compliance with standards
(E) Achievement of targets

## SEC. 110. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN PREEMPTION

**SEC. 110.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national semiconductor standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt semiconductor standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 111. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 111.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
