## SEC. 117. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 117.** (a) DEFENSE FACILITIES.—

**(1) SEMICONDUCTOR REQUIREMENTS.—**The Secretary of Defense shall identify semiconductor requirements for defense systems.

**(2) DOMESTIC SOURCING.—**Defense semiconductor requirements shall be sourced from domestic suppliers to the maximum extent practicable.

**(b) INTELLIGENCE FACILITIES.—

**(1) SEMICONDUCTOR REQUIREMENTS.—**The Director of National Intelligence shall identify semiconductor requirements for intelligence systems.

**(2) DOMESTIC SOURCING.—**Intelligence semiconductor requirements shall be sourced from domestic suppliers to the maximum extent practicable.

**(c) CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE.—

**(1) SEMICONDUCTOR REQUIREMENTS.—**The Secretary of Homeland Security shall identify semiconductor requirements for critical infrastructure.

**(2) DOMESTIC SOURCING.—**Critical infrastructure semiconductor requirements shall be sourced from domestic suppliers to the maximum extent practicable.

## SEC. 118. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN FOR SCHOOLS

**SEC. 118.** (a) SEMICONDUCTOR EDUCATION.—

**(1) K-12 EDUCATION.—**The Secretary of Education shall develop semiconductor education programs for K-12 schools.

**(2) HIGHER EDUCATION.—**The Secretary of Education shall support semiconductor programs at colleges and universities.

**(b) SEMICONDUCTOR RESEARCH.—

**(1) UNIVERSITY RESEARCH.—**The Secretary shall fund semiconductor research at universities.

**(2) GRADUATE FELLOWSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall provide graduate fellowships for semiconductor research.
