## SEC. 121. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 121.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of semiconductor supply chain activities, including:

**(1)** Assessment of semiconductor supply chain effectiveness
**(2)** Recommendations for reauthorization
**(3)** Proposed modifications to semiconductor supply chain structure
**(4)** Assessment of remaining semiconductor supply chain vulnerabilities
**(5)** Recommendations for future semiconductor supply chain activities

---

## SEC. 122. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN TRANSITION

**SEC. 122.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of semiconductor supply chain activities if the authorities are not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF ACTIVITIES.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing semiconductor supply chain activities and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.
