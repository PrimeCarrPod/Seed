## SEC. 106. SEMICONDUCTOR RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 106.** (a) RESEARCH PROGRAM.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall establish a semiconductor research and development program.

**(2) FOCUS AREAS.—**The program shall focus on:
(A) Next-generation chip technologies (2nm and below)
(B) Advanced packaging technologies
(C) Compound semiconductors
(D) Photonics and quantum computing
(E) Artificial intelligence hardware

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(b) PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIPS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall establish public-private research partnerships for semiconductor development.

**(2) INDUSTRY PARTICIPATION.—**Partnerships shall include participation from:
(A) Semiconductor companies
(B) Equipment manufacturers
(C) Materials suppliers
(D) Universities
(E) National laboratories

**(c) NATIONAL SEMICONDUCTOR RESEARCH CENTER.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**There is established a National Semiconductor Research Center.

**(2) FUNCTIONS.—**The Center shall:
(A) Conduct basic and applied research
(B) Develop new manufacturing processes
(C) Train semiconductor researchers
(D) Coordinate research across institutions
