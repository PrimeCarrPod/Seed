## SECTION 3. DEFINITIONS

**SEC. 3.** In this Act:

**(1) "Port Infrastructure"**
Facilities and systems for the loading, unloading, and storage of cargo at coastal ports, including docks, wharves, cranes, and container terminals.

**(2) "Supply Chain Resilience"**
The ability of supply chains to withstand and recover from disruptions.

**(3) "Maritime Transportation"**
The movement of goods and passengers by water, including shipping, ferries, and barges.

**(4) "Coastal Resilience"**
The ability of coastal infrastructure to withstand and recover from storms, sea level rise, and other climate impacts.
