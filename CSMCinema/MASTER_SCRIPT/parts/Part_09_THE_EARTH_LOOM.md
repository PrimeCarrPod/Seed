# SCENE 1: GAIA'S CAVERN

EXT. MOUNT OLYMPUS - BASE - DAY

The crew travels on foot toward Olympus.

ATALANTA
We're going to Olympus? Home of the gods?

MEDEA
(writing)
The earth loom is beneath it. Where Gaia wove the world into being.

ZETES
Gaia. The first goddess.

CALAUS
Older than the Titans. Older than everything.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The earth remembers. Every step. Every life. Every death.

---

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - NIGHT

The crew descends. Into the mountain. Into the earth.

The walls pulse. Not stone. Something alive. Breathing.

The cavern opens. Vast. Impossible. Roots the size of ships. And in the center — a loom. Not made of anything. Just — there. Always there. The EARTH LOOM.

And beside it — GAIA (ageless, neither young nor old, mother of everything, eyes that have seen it all).

GAIA
(without turning)
I wondered when you'd come.

JASON
You knew we were coming?

GAIA
(smiling)
I know everything that walks on me. (turning) You carry five threads. You want the sixth.

JASON
We want to understand.

GAIA
(sadly)
Understanding is overrated. (beat) But you'll learn that. In time.

# SCENE 2: THE STORY OF MAKING

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

GAIA
Sit. All of you. I'll tell you a story.

The crew sits.

GAIA (CONT'D)
Before me — there was chaos. And I chose. The first choice. To weave order from chaos. And that choice created everything. The gods. The world. You.

JASON
Why?

GAIA
Because I was lonely. (beat) Chaos is free — but it's alone. I wanted connection.

MEDEA
(writing)
And the suffering?

GAIA
(sadly)
The cost. Every thread I wove — excluded every other thread.

# SCENE 3: THE GIFT

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

GAIA
(to Jason)
Give me your hand.

Jason extends his hand. Gaia touches it.

The cavern disappears. Jason sees:

The world as Gaia sees it. Every root. Every river. Every mountain. Every life. All connected. All woven. All — one pattern.

And in the pattern — a gap. A hole. Something missing.

GAIA (V.O.)
The pattern is incomplete. It needs — a center. A consciousness. Someone who holds it all together.

JASON
(in the vision)
And if I refuse?

GAIA (V.O.)
Then the pattern continues without a center. The gods continue. The world continues. But something beautiful — will be lost.

The vision ends. Jason is back in the cavern.

GAIA
(placing a thread in his hand)
Green. Memory of the earth. Every root. Every seed. Every return. (beat) When you're lost — hold the thread. The earth will hold you.

JASON
Six threads.

GAIA
Six looms. One to go.
