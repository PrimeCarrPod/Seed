# SCENE 2: THE JUDGMENT

INT. HADES' PALACE - NIGHT

A vast hall. Shadows. Silence. And on a throne of bone — HADES (50s, dark, patient, remembers everything).

PERSEPHONE (40s, his queen, spring in winter, kind eyes) sits beside him.

HADES
Jason of Iolcus. You carry four threads. Sea. Sky. World. You want the fifth.

JASON
We want to understand.

HADES
(quietly)
Everyone says that. (beat) I don't test. I don't tempt. I judge. And my judgment is this: You've taken much. What have you given?

JASON
We gave ourselves. Our safety. Our—

HADES
(interrupting)
Wrong. You've taken. But you haven't given. Not really. Not yet.

PERSEPHONE
(softly)
What will you give?

JASON
(quietly)
My certainty.

HADES
(nodding)
You passed. Not because you were strong. Because you were willing to be weak.

# SCENE 3: THE THREAD

INT. HADES' PALACE - CONTINUOUS

Hades gestures. A thread detaches from his throne. Black. Dark. But not empty — full. Full of everything that ever was.

HADES
Memory of the underworld. Every ending. Every loss. Every goodbye. (beat) When you fear death — hold the thread. Remember: endings are part of the pattern.

JASON
(taking it)
Five threads.

HADES
Five looms. Two to go.
