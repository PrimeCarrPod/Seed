EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

A fisherman's Hut. Small. Stone. Thatched roof. Moonlight through the cracks.

JASON sleeps. Not peaceful. The sleep of the called. The sleep of— (searching) —of someone who said yes before knowing what yes meant.

PRESENCE.

Not sound. Not movement. Just— (searching) —weight. The air thickens. Pressure builds.

HECATE (30s — maiden face; 50s — mother face; 70s — crone face; all three on one head, all three aware, all three watching; three torches in one hand: green flame, blue flame, red flame) stands at the foot of his bed.

Not physical. Not dream. Between.

Jason opens his eyes. Doesn't flinch. Twenty years with Chiron. He knows the gods when they come.

JASON
(quietly)
You're the old woman from Anauron.

HECATE
(voice triple — maiden high, mother middle, crone low, all simultaneous)
I am Hecate. Maiden, Mother, Crone. And I have come to collect.

JASON
(sitting)
Collect what? I carried you across. The debt is paid.

HECATE
(smiling — three smiles)
That was the down payment.

She raises her torches. The hut fills with triple light.

HECATE (CONT'D)
The fleece you seek — it's mine. The grove — mine. The priestess — mine. When you reach Colchis, you'll need me. Remember the river. Remember the debt. Remember that the goddess does not forget.

JASON
What priestess?

HECATE
Her name is Medea. She will love you. She will destroy you. She will save you. She will betray her father for you. And you— (pausing) —you will break her heart.

JASON
Why are you telling me this?

HECATE
Because knowing doesn't change anything. The pattern is the pattern.

She raises her torches higher. The hut fills with triple light. Jason sees:

A SHIP — fifty oars, timber from Olympus, waiting to be born.

A CREW — heroes gathering from across Greece. Heracles. Orpheus. Castor and Pollux. Atalanta.

A DRAGON — sleepless, vast, coiled around gold.

And a WOMAN — black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes, holding torches identical to Hecate's.

MEDEA. Always Medea.

JASON
(awed)
She's—

HECATE
Yours. And you are hers. And the pattern holds you both.

The vision fades. Hecate fades. The hut is dark. The moon is full. Jason lies still.
