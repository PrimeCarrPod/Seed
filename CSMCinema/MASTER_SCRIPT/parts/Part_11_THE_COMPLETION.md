# SCENE 1: THE EIGHTH THREAD CALLS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

KORE at the center. The eighth thread around her wrist. It pulses. Not calling. Not pulling. Just — waiting.

MEDEA
(writing)

JASON

MEDEA
(writing)

JASON
(smiling)
Now I'm — here. With you. Home.

CHILD
(awed)

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
Welcome to the center.

KORE
(breathing)
I'm free.

JASON
(hugging her)
You're free.

KORE
(crying)
What do I do now?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever you want.

KORE
(thinking)

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then do that.
