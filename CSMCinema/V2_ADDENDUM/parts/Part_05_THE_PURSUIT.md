## MUSICAL KEY

**Bb minor** — The key of pursuit. The key of flight. The key of the chase that never ends. Bb minor is the sound of running. The sound of fleeing. The sound of the hunter and the hunted. It's the key of Chopin's Funeral March. The key of the pursuit that is also a transformation. The key that says: you cannot stop. You can only run. And in running, become.

## TEMPO

**108 BPM** — The heartbeat of flight. Faster than trial (96 BPM). Faster than creation (88 BPM). The heartbeat of someone who is fleeing. The heartbeat of someone who is chasing. The heartbeat of the pursuit.

## LEITMOTIF: THE PURSUIT THEME

**Description:** The Fire Theme transformed. Same four notes. F-Ab-C-F. But now they're played legato. Flowing. Running. The sound of flight. The sound of the chase. The sound of the pursuit that is also a transformation.

**Instrumentation:** Bodhran (footsteps) + strings (running) + French horn (the gold being stolen) + oboe (the hunter's call).

**Recurrence:** The Pursuit Theme will return in Part 08 (The Underworld) as the Debt Theme — same notes, slower, the debt being paid. It will return in Part 10 (The Convergence) as the Release Theme — same notes, resolved, the pursuit ending. It will return in Part 11 (The Completion) as the Home Theme — same notes, peaceful, the flight home.
