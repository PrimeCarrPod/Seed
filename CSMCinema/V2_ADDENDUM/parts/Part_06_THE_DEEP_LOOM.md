## MUSICAL KEY

**Eb minor** — The key of the deep. The key of the sea. The key of the underwater world where light doesn't reach and sound travels forever. Eb minor is the sound of pressure. The sound of depth. The sound of the ancient. It's the key of the abyss. The key of the deep. The key that says: you are entering a world that was here before you and will be here after you. Be humble. Be awed. Be transformed.

**54 BPM** — The heartbeat of the deep. Slower than fear (66 BPM). Slower than the ordinary world (72 BPM). The heartbeat of the ocean. The heartbeat of the ancient. The heartbeat of the deep loom.

**Instrumentation:** Didgeridoo (the deep) + whale song (the ancient) + submarine bell (the divine) + glass harmonica (the loom).

It will return in Part 11 (The Completion) as the Memory Theme — same notes, remembered, the deep remembered.

## PRE-SCENE ABSURD DETAIL

### Geography

The deep. The place where light doesn't reach. The place where pressure is everything. The place where the ancient lives.

The deep is not a place. The deep is a state. The deep is the place where the world was woven. The deep is the place where the gods were born. The deep is the place where you go to become.

The water is clear. The water is cold. The water is the deep. The water is the transformation.

### Architecture

The loom of the sea. Coral. Bone. Pearl. Woven by Poseidon himself.

The loom is ancient. The loom is alive. The loom is the deep. The loom is the transformation. The loom is the place where the sea was woven.

The loom is not a thing. The loom is a process. The loom is the sea weaving itself. The loom is the deep weaving itself. The loom is the transformation weaving itself.

### Weather

Underwater. Temperature: 35 Fahrenheit. Pressure: 100 atmospheres. Visibility: unlimited.

The water is clear. The water is cold. The water is the deep. The water is the transformation.

### Biology

**The Fish:** The fish are ancient. The fish are bioluminescent. The fish are the deep. The fish are the loom. The fish are the transformation.

**The Coral:** The coral is alive. The coral is ancient. The coral is the loom. The coral is the deep. The coral is the transformation.

**The Whales:** The whales sing. The whales are the deep. The whales are the loom. The whales are the transformation.

### History

The deep has been here since before the gods. The deep will be here after the gods. The deep is patient. The deep is ancient. The deep is the loom.

### Sensory

**Sound:** The deep (sub-bass drone). The whales (song). The loom (11.11 Hz). Jason's breath (slow, 8 BPM). The deep (silence).

**Smell:** Salt. Pressure. The deep. The smell of the loom. The smell of the ancient. The smell of transformation.

**Touch:** The water (cold). The pressure (heavy). The loom (alive). The deep (electric).

**Taste:** Salt. Pressure. The taste of the deep. The taste of the loom. The taste of transformation.

### Metaphysical

The pattern is here. The pattern is always here. The pattern is in the deep. The pattern is in the loom. The pattern is in the sea.

Clotho spins slower. Lachesis measures the pressure. Atropos waits. Hecate watches: the deep is here. The deep is now. The deep is the transformation.

### Musical

**Opening Theme:** The Sea Theme. Low drones. Whale song. The sound of the deep.

**Instrumentation:** Didgeridoo (deep) + whale song (ancient) + submarine bell (divine) + glass harmonica (loom).

**Rhythm:** 54 BPM. The heartbeat of the deep. Slower than fear. Slower than the ordinary world.

**Harmony:** Eb minor. The key of the deep. The key of the sea. The key of the ancient.

**Melody:** Low drones. Whale song. The sound of the world before air.

**Dynamics:** pppp. So quiet you can hear the whales singing. So quiet you can hear the loom weaving. So quiet you can hear the deep breathing.

**Cross-Reference:** The Sea Theme will return in Part 08 as the River Theme. In Part 09 as the Root Theme. In Part 11 as the Memory Theme.
