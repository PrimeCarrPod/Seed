# TITLE SEQUENCE — PART 02: THE ASSEMBLY

---

## MUSICAL KEY

**E minor** — The key of creation. The key of timber and sweat and the sound of axes on heartwood. E minor is the sound of something being born. Not the cry of the newborn. The groan of the mother. The creak of the ship. The sound of the world being shaped by hands that know what they're doing.

---

## TEMPO

**88 BPM** — The heartbeat of creation. Faster than the ordinary world (72 BPM). Faster than waiting. The heartbeat of someone building. Someone shaping. Someone bringing something into existence that wasn't there before.

---

## LEITMOTIF: THE SHIP THEME

**Description:** A rising figure. Five notes. E-F#-G-A-B. Ascending. The sound of timber rising. The sound of a keel being laid. The sound of something that was a tree becoming something that will sail.

**Instrumentation:** Bodhran (footsteps) + hammered dulcimer (the sound of axes on wood) + uilleann pipes (the sound of Athena whispering through the grain).

**Recurrence:** The Ship Theme will return in Part 03 (The Threshold) as the Storm Theme — same five notes, chaotic, sea-tossed. It will return in Part 05 (The Pursuit) as the Flight Theme — same five notes, desperate, fleeing. It will return in Part 11 (The Completion) as the Home Theme — same five notes, resolved, sailing home.

---

## VISUAL PALLET

**Colors:** Green and gold. Pine needles. Oak grain. The silver of axe blades. The bronze of sweat on skin. The white of sawdust. The gold of Athena's geometry. The world is being cut. The world is being shaped. The world is becoming.

**Lighting:** Mid-morning. The sun is above Pelion now. The light comes through the pines in shafts. Each shaft is a column. Each column is a ship mast that hasn't been raised yet. The forest is a dry dock. The forest is a temple. The forest is where ships are born.

**Fog:** Gone. Burned off. The air is clear. The air smells of resin and possibility.

**Camera:** Slow pan. The Bunraku puppeteer's eye moving across the forest. Revealing. Discovering. The camera doesn't move quickly. The camera has time. The ship has time. Everything has time when you're building something that will last.

---

## NARRATOR ONE PRELUDE

Before the first axe. Before the first timber. Before the first hero. There was the mountain. And the mountain was covered in trees. And the trees had been growing for three hundred years. Waiting. Not knowing what they were waiting for. Just growing. Just being. Just reaching for the sun.

And then a shipwright came. And the shipwright listened. And the shipwright heard. The trees weren't just trees. The trees were a ship. The trees had always been a ship. The trees were just waiting for someone who could hear it.

The assembly begins.

### ARGUS — The Shipwright

**Williams Register:** FISHER KING — The wounded healer who builds because he can't heal. The shipwright who listens because he can't speak.

**Age:** 40s

**Height:** 5'9"

**Build:** Strong in the way of someone who has worked with his hands for twenty years. Not bulky. Dense. The kind of strength that comes from lifting timber and swinging axes and shaping wood with patience and time.

**Hair:** Brown. Short. Sawdust in it. Always sawdust. The sawdust is permanent. The sawdust is part of him.

**Eyes:** Grey-blue. The color of the sea on an overcast day. The color of someone who looks at wood and sees ships. The color of someone who listens more than he speaks.

**Outfit:**
- **Work robes:** Brown. Leather apron. The apron has pockets — each pocket holds a tool. Marking gauge. Chisel. Adze. The tools are old. The tools were his father's. The tools will be someone else's.
- **Bare feet:** Argus doesn't wear sandals. Argus needs to feel the ground. Needs to feel the mountain. Needs to feel the timber. The bare feet are calloused. The bare feet know Pelion.
- **Hands:** The hands are scarred. The hands are shaped. The hands have swung an axe ten thousand times. The hands know wood.

**Mannerisms:**
- **The Listening:** Argus puts his hands on bark and closes his eyes. This is not performance. This is communion. Argus listens to the tree. The tree tells him where to cut. The tree tells him what it wants to become.
- **The Axe:** Argus swings the axe like it's part of his arm. No wasted motion. No hesitation. Each stroke is exactly where it needs to be. Each stroke is the tree saying "yes."
- **The Silence:** Argus doesn't speak unless speaking is necessary. Argus has been listening for so long that speaking feels like interrupting.

**Personality:**
- **Patience:** 100%. Argus can wait. Argus can listen. Argus can let the tree tell him what it needs.
- **Humility:** 100%. Argus doesn't build. Argus reveals. The ship was always there. Argus just freed it.
- **Purpose:** 100%. Argus was born for this. Argus was taught for this. Argus is building the ship that will carry the heroes.
- **Fun Factor:** Maximum. Argus loves this. Loves the wood. Loves the listening. Loves the moment when the tree becomes the ship.

**Stage Position:** Center stage. Always center. Argus is the still point. The crew gathers around him. The forest gathers around him. The ship is born around him.

# SCENE 1: THE SHIPWRIGHT — ADDENDUM DETAIL

## PRE-SCENE ABSURD DETAIL

### Geography

Mount Pelion. The mountain rises 1,610 meters above the Pagasetic Gulf. The summit is above the tree line. The clearing where Argus kneels is at 1,200 meters — in the pine zone. The pines are Pinus nigra. Black pines. Ancient pines. Three hundred years of growth. Three hundred years of waiting.

The clearing is circular. Fifty meters in diameter. The circle is natural. The trees grew this way. The trees made room. The trees knew.

The north road leads down to Iolcus. The south road leads up to the summit. The east road leads to Thessaly. The west road leads to the sea. The clearing is the crossroads. The clearing is where paths meet. The clearing is where the ship is born.

### Architecture

There is no architecture. There is only forest. But the forest IS architecture. The pines are columns. The needle bed is the floor. The canopy is the roof. The forest is a cathedral. The forest is a dry dock. The forest is a temple.

The largest pine — the one Argus kneels before — is three meters in diameter. Three hundred years of growth. The bark is thick. The bark is scarred. The bark tells the story of lightning strikes and droughts and winters and the slow patient work of becoming.

### Weather

Mid-morning. Temperature: 62 Fahrenheit. Humidity: 65 percent. Fog: gone. Visibility: unlimited. The air is clear. The air is sharp. The air smells of resin and pine needles and the kind of clarity that comes after fog.

The wind is from the west. The wind carries the sea. The wind carries salt. The wind carries the smell of the quest. The wind says: build. The wind says: sail. The wind says: go.

### Biology

**The Pines:** Black pines. Three hundred years old. The pines have seen empires rise and fall. The pines have seen heroes come and go. The pines are patient. The pines are waiting. The pines know what they're becoming.

**The Centaurs:** In the valley below. Chiron watches. Chiron nods. Chiron approves. Chiron taught Argus. Chiron taught Jason. Chiron is the thread that connects. Chiron is the pattern made flesh.

**The Birds:** Hawks. Eagles. The birds circle above the clearing. The birds watch. The birds know. The birds are Athena's eyes.

### History

This mountain has been here for ten million years. These pines have been here for three hundred. This clearing has been here for ten thousand. This clearing has seen rituals. This clearing has seen sacrifices. This clearing has seen the birth of heroes.

And now the clearing sees the birth of a ship.

### Sensory

**Sound:** The axe on heartwood (deep bell, C3). The wind through pines (shaped white noise). The birds (high, distant). Argus's breathing (slow, 12 BPM). The grain resonating (110 Hz fundamental).

**Smell:** Resin. Pine needles. Sweat. The mountain. The kind of smell that makes you believe in gods.

**Touch:** The bark (rough, warm). The needle bed (soft, yielding). The axe handle (smooth, worn). The ground (solid, patient).

**Taste:** Pine. Resin. The taste of creation. The taste of something being born.

### Metaphysical

Athena is here. Athena is in the grain. Athena is in the geometry. Athena is whispering. Athena is teaching. Athena is the blueprint.

The pattern is weaving. The ship is not just a ship. The ship is a promise. The ship is a purpose. The ship is the thing that will carry the heroes to the fleece and back.

Clotho spins faster. Lachesis measures the timber. Atropos waits. Hecate watches.

### Musical

**Opening Theme:** The Ship Theme. Five notes ascending. E-F#-G-A-B.

**Instrumentation:** Bodhran (footsteps) + hammered dulcimer (axes on wood) + uilleann pipes (Athena whispering).

**Rhythm:** 88 BPM. The heartbeat of creation. Faster than the ordinary world. Faster than waiting.

**Harmony:** E minor. The key of creation. The key of timber and sweat.

**Melody:** Rising. Five notes. The sound of something being born.

**Dynamics:** mp. Not loud. Not quiet. The volume of creation. The volume of work.

**Cross-Reference:** The Ship Theme will return in Part 03 as the Storm Theme. In Part 05 as the Flight Theme. In Part 11 as the Home Theme.
