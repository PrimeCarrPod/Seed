# TITLE SEQUENCE — PART 03: THE THRESHOLD

---

## MUSICAL KEY

**F minor** — The key of the threshold. The key of the space between. F minor is the sound of standing at the edge. The edge of the known. The edge of the world. The edge of the self. It's the key of Liszt's Totentanz. The key of Beethoven's Appassionata. The key that says: you cannot go back. You can only go through.

---

## TEMPO

**66 BPM** — The heartbeat of fear. Slower than the ordinary world (72 BPM). Slower than creation (88 BPM). The heartbeat of someone who knows they're about to die. Or be transformed. Or both.

---

## LEITMOTIF: THE FLEECE THEME

**Description:** The Sandal Theme transformed. Same uneven rhythm. Same prophecy in meter. But now the notes are different. F-Ab-C-F. The sound of gold. The sound of something precious. The sound of something guarded. The sound of the thing you came for.

**Instrumentation:** Bodhran (footsteps) + French horn (the sound of gold) + glass harmonica (the sound of the divine).

**Recurrence:** The Fleece Theme will return in Part 04 (The Trials) as the Fire Theme — same notes, transformed, burning. It will return in Part 05 (The Pursuit) as the Theft Theme — same notes, desperate, stolen. It will return in Part 11 (The Completion) as the Gift Theme — same notes, resolved, given.

## VISUAL PALETTE

**Colors:** Black and gold. The black of the whale's belly. The black of the space between. The gold of the fleece. The gold of the thing you came for. The gold that is the only color that matters.

**Lighting:** Subaqueous. The light comes from below. Bioluminescent. The light is alive. The light is the sea breathing. The light is the pattern made visible.

**Fog:** Gone. Replaced by water. The water is clear. The water is deep. The water is the threshold between worlds.

**Camera:** Slow push. The Bunraku puppeteer's eye moving forward. Into the whale. Into the threshold. Into the space between.

## NARRATOR ONE PRELUDE

Before the passage. Before the whale. Before the threshold. There was the open sea. And the open sea was free. And the open sea was safe. And the open sea was the ordinary world.

And then the rocks. And the rocks clashed. And the rocks opened. And the rocks said: through. Not back. Not around. Through.

The threshold is not a place. The threshold is a moment. The moment when you choose. The moment when you commit. The moment when you say: I will go through. I will not go back. I will not go around. I will go through.

The threshold begins.

# SCENE 1: THE ROCKS — ADDENDUM DETAIL

---

## PRE-SCENE ABSURD DETAIL

### Geography

The Symplegades. The Clashing Rocks. Two massive cliffs face each other across a channel two hundred meters wide. The cliffs are limestone. White. Ancient. The cliffs have been clashing since before the gods. The cliffs will clash after the gods are gone.

The channel is the only passage. The channel is the threshold. The channel is the space between the known and the unknown. The channel is the space between the ordinary and the extraordinary.

The water between the rocks is white. Not blue. Not green. White. The white of churning. The white of violence. The white of the space between.

### Architecture

There is no architecture. There is only stone. But the stone IS architecture. The cliffs are walls. The channel is the gate. The passage is the door. The rocks are the threshold made solid.

The rocks are carved. Not by hands. By water. By time. By the slow patient work of the sea. The carvings are waves. The carvings are fish. The carvings are the symbols of the threshold. The carvings are the symbols of the passage. The carvings are the symbols of the space between.

### Weather

Midday. Temperature: 68 Fahrenheit. Humidity: 70 percent. Fog: gone. Visibility: unlimited. The air is clear. The air is sharp. The air smells of salt and stone and the kind of clarity that comes before the storm.

The wind is from the west. The wind carries the sea. The wind carries salt. The wind carries the smell of the threshold. The wind says: through. The wind says: now. The wind says: choose.

### Biology

**The Dove:** White. Domestic. The dove is the test. The dove is the threshold. The dove is the question: will you pass? The dove is the answer: yes. The dove is the price: a feather. The dove is the promise: you will pass but you will pay.

**The Fish:** Below. In the white water. The fish are silver. The fish are fast. The fish know the rocks. The fish know the passage. The fish have been passing through since before the gods.

**The Birds:** Above. Hawks. Eagles. The birds circle. The birds watch. The birds know. The birds are Athena's eyes. The birds are the pattern's eyes. The birds are the threshold's eyes.

### History

The rocks have been clashing for ten thousand years. The rocks have crushed a thousand ships. The rocks have killed a thousand heroes. The rocks are patient. The rocks are waiting. The rocks are the threshold.

And now the rocks wait for the Argo. And now the rocks wait for Jason. And now the rocks wait for the moment when the threshold is crossed.

### Sensory

**Sound:** The rocks clash (18 Hz infrasound). The spray (80 Hz). The wind (white noise). The dove wings (12 Hz). Jason's breath (steady, 48 BPM). The crew's breath (elevated, various). The threshold (silence).

**Smell:** Salt. Stone. Fear. The smell of the threshold. The smell of the space between. The smell of the moment before.

**Touch:** The rail (cold, wet). The dove (warm, alive). The air (sharp, salt). The threshold (electric).

**Taste:** Salt. Fear. The taste of the threshold. The taste of the moment before. The taste of the choice.

### Metaphysical

The pattern is here. The pattern is always here. The pattern is in the rocks. The pattern is in the water. The pattern is in the dove. The pattern is in the choice.

Clotho spins faster. Lachesis measures the opening: three seconds. Atropos positions her shears: not yet. Hecate watches: the threshold is here. The threshold is now. The threshold is the moment.
