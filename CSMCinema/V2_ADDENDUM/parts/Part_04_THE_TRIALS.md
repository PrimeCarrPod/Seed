## PRE-SCENE ABSURD DETAIL

### Geography

The Plains of Ares. The field stretches four hundred meters square. Volcanic soil depth two meters. Plow path required: sixteen hundred meters.

The field is black. Not brown. Not grey. Black. The black of volcanic soil. The black of the earth that has been burned. The black of the earth that has been forged.

The field is cracked. The cracks are deep. The cracks are the earth breathing. The cracks are the earth saying: I am alive. I am hot. I am waiting.

The subterranean pens are below. The pens are stone. The pens are hot. The pens are where the bulls wait. The pens are where the fire waits. The pens are where the trial waits.

### Architecture

There is no architecture. There is only field. But the field IS architecture. The field is the forge. The field is the trial. The field is the place where heroes are tested.

The throne of knives is at the edge of the field. The throne is elevated. The throne is gold. The throne is the price of the fleece. The throne is the price of power. The throne is the price of everything.

### Weather

Midday. Temperature: 95 Fahrenheit. The air is hot. The air is sharp. The air smells of sulfur and smoke and the kind of heat that comes before the storm.

The wind is from the east. The wind carries the fire. The wind carries the smoke. The wind carries the smell of the trial. The wind says: burn. The wind says: now. The wind says: choose.

### Biology

**The Bulls:** Two bulls. Hephaestus-forged. Bronze hooves. Fire nostrils. Breath that melts stone. Breath temperature: 1,200 degrees Celsius. Hoovfall impact force: 4,000 PSI. Bronze mass per bull: 800 kilograms. The bulls are not alive. The bulls are not dead. The bulls are something else. The bulls are the trial made flesh. The bulls are the fire made visible.

**The Earth:** The earth is hot. The earth is alive. The earth is breathing. The earth is the forge. The earth is the trial. The earth is the transformation.

**The Smoke:** The smoke from the bulls is black. The smoke is thick. The smoke is the breath of the trial. The smoke is the breath of the fire. The smoke is the breath of transformation.

### History

This field has been here for ten thousand years. This field has seen a thousand trials. This field has seen a thousand heroes. This field has seen a thousand transformations.

And now the field waits for Jason. And now the field waits for the moment when the trial begins. And now the field waits for the moment when the hero is tested.

### Sensory

**Sound:** The bulls roar (80 Hz). The fire breath (sub-bass rumble). The hoovfall (seismic thud). Jason's breath (steady, 48 BPM). The crew's breath (elevated, various). The trial (silence).

**Smell:** Sulfur. Smoke. Burning. The smell of the trial. The smell of the fire. The smell of transformation.

**Touch:** The earth (hot). The air (sharp). The fire (dangerous). The trial (electric).

**Taste:** Sulfur. Fear. The taste of the trial. The taste of the fire. The taste of transformation.

### Metaphysical

The pattern is here. The pattern is always here. The pattern is in the fire. The pattern is in the bulls. The pattern is in the trial. The pattern is in the transformation.

Clotho spins faster. Lachesis measures the fire. Atropos positions her shears: not yet. Hecate watches: the trial is here. The trial is now. The trial is the moment.

**Instrumentation:** Bodhran (footsteps) + anvil strikes (forge) + distorted electric guitar (fire) + French horn (gold beneath fire).
