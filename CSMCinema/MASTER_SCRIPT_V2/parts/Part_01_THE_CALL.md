# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 01 OF 11: THE CALL

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Iolcus. Dawn. The Olympus mount reflects first light on the coast like a gold coin flipped by gods who don't care about the outcome because they already know it.

The market square is empty. Fish scales still wet. Smoke from brick ovens curving like thread, like fate, like the spine of a story that hasn't decided whether it's a comedy or a tragedy yet.

A dog crosses the square. Purposeful. Knowing. The dog knows what's coming. The dog always knows.

A cart rattles over cobblestones. Irregular rhythm. Two beats per second. The heartbeat of the ordinary world.

And on the balcony — twelve feet above the square — a king who hasn't slept in twelve years watches the north road. His heart rate: eighty-eight beats per minute. His hands grip the railing. His breath is shallow.

This is Pelias. This is the usurper. This is the man who stole a throne and has been paying for it every night since in sleeplessness and paranoia and the slow digestion of guilt.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

HEY THERE, LISTENER. YOU EVER SEE A GUY WITH ONE SANDAL WALK INTO TOWN LIKE HE OWNS THE PLACE? THAT'S JASON. THAT'S THE WHOLE GAME. HE AIN'T GOT THE FLEECE. HE AIN'T GOT THE SHIP. ## SCENE 2: THE KING WATCHES

**PELIAS** stands on his balcony. Elevation: twelve feet. Sightline: north road clear. Heart rate: elevated, eighty-eight beats per minute. Psychological state: anxiety high, paranoia elevated, recall oracle twelve years present, vigilance continuous.

**ADVISOR** stands beside him. Loyal. Afraid.

**PELIAS**
He comes.

**ADVISOR**
My lord—

**PELIAS**
One sandal. Just as the oracle said.

**ADVISOR**
We could—

**PELIAS**
We could what? Kill him? The gods sent him. You don't kill what the gods send. You send it somewhere else.

## SCENE 3: THE HEIR WALKS

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — JASON APPROACH VECTOR: NORTH ROAD SPEED 3 MILES PER HOUR. DISTANCE TO MARKET SQUARE: 0.4 MILES. ESTIMATED ARRIVAL: 8 MINUTES. JASON STATE: CONFIDENCE HIGH. IGNORANCE OF THRONE CLAIM COMPLETE. RIVER CROSSING AT ANAUROS: RECENT. ONE SANDAL LOST TO OLD WOMAN — HECATE DISGUISED.]**

JASON enters the square. Twenty years old. Golden hair like the fleece before the fleece. River mud on his left ankle. One sandal on his right foot. Traveler's robes that have seen Chiron's cave, the river at Anauron, and the long road home.

His footsteps: left bare — higher pitch. Right sandal — lower. Uneven rhythm. Prophecy in meter.

He walks like he owns it. Not arrogant. Present. Exactly where he's supposed to be.

He looks up at the balcony. Sees Pelias. Their eyes meet across forty yards of morning air.

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

ONE SANDAL AIN'T MISSING, MAN. IT'S FOUND. THE RIVER TOOK ONE. THE RIVER GAVE PROPHECY. SAME WAVE THAT WASHES AWAY LEAVES THE PEARL. JASON DON'T LOSE A SANDAL. HE GAINS A SIGN. IOLCUS AIN'T TOWN. IT'S THE WAX. THE SEAL AIN'T BROKEN. NOT YET. THE RIVER AT ANAUROS TOOK THE SANDAL. THE RIVER AT ANAUROS GAVE HECATE. OLD WOMAN DISGUISED. JASON CARRIED HER ACROSS. FIRST CONTACT: UNWITTING. DEBT INCURRED. THE GODDESS DOESN'T FORGET. THE PATTERN DOESN'T FORGET. NOTHING FORGETS, MAN. NOTHING.

## SCENE 4: THE CONFRONTATION

**JASON**
(calling, clear, voice carrying across forty yards of morning)
Uncle. I've come for my father's throne.

Silence. The dog stops. The cart stops. Even the smoke seems to hold.

**PELIAS**
(long pause, heart rate now ninety-two beats per minute)
Your father died in exile. Tragic.

**JASON**
(shaking his head)
My father was murdered. By you.

The court murmurs. Hands on weapons. Fifty warriors present. Jason empty-handed.

---

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the first thread — Iolcus dawn gold. The spindle turns. Beginning.

**LACHESIS** measures — forty yards between king and heir. Not far. Not near. Exact.

**ATROPOS** holds her shears — not yet. Not here. The thread continues.

**HECATE** sleeps — torches unlit. But the debt at Anauron remains. She will collect.

## SCENE 5: THE COUNTER-CALL

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — ARISTAEUS FATE: JASON FATHER DEPOSED BY PELIAS. THRONE STOLEN. HEIR SENT TO CHIRON. RETURN NOW. TIMING: PROPHECY-FULFILLMENT WINDOW. CALL INITIATION PROBABILITY: 99.8 PERCENT. ALL CONDITIONS MET. PROPHECY ALIGNED. HEIR ARRIVES. THRONE VACANT IN ALL BUT NAME.]**

---

**PELIAS**
(heart rate steadying, mind working, the usurper's calculus)
Prove it.

**JASON**
Prove what?

**PELIAS**
The throne. Prove you're worthy. The Golden Fleece. Retrieve it from Colchis. Bring it here. Then we'll discuss your birthright.

The court murmurs. Everyone knows this is a death sentence.

**JASON**
(without hesitation, confidence absolute)
I accept.

**PELIAS**
(startled)
Just like that?

**JASON**
(smiling)
Just like that.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The market exhales. Pelias breathes. Jason breathes steady at forty-eight beats per minute — Chiron trained. The fog thins. Visibility now four hundred yards. Dawn light floods the square.

The call is accepted. The counter-call is initiated. The fleece is named. The quest begins.

And somewhere — in the space between gods and mortals — Hecate's torches flicker. Green. Blue. Red. The goddess knows. The goddess always knows.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

NIGHT. IOLCUS HARBOR. JASON SLEEPS IN A FISHERMAN'S HUT. THE MOON IS FULL. THE SEA IS STILL. NOT WIND. NOT WAVE. NOT BIRD. SILENCE THAT IS NOT SILENCE. PRESENCE. SHE COMES. NOT AS OLD WOMAN. NOT AS CRONE. AS HERSELF. HECATE. TRIPLE GODDESS. MAIDEN MOTHER CRONE. THREE FACES ON ONE HEAD. THREE TORCHES IN ONE HAND. GREEN BLUE RED. SHE STANDS AT THE FOOT OF HIS BED. NOT PHYSICAL. NOT DREAM. BETWEEN. THE SPACE WHERE GODS SPEAK.

---

A fisherman's hut. Small. Stone. Thatched roof. Moonlight through cracks.

JASON sleeps. Not peaceful. The sleep of the called. The sleep of someone who said yes before knowing what yes meant.

PRESENCE. Not sound. Not movement. Weight. The air thickens.

HECATE stands at the foot of his bed. Three faces. Three torches. Triple flame.

Jason opens his eyes. Doesn't flinch. Twenty years with Chiron taught him: you know the gods when they come.

---

## SCENE 7: THE DEBT

**JASON**
(quietly)
You're the old woman from Anauron.

**HECATE**
(voice triple — maiden high, mother middle, crone low, all simultaneous)
I am Hecate. Maiden, Mother, Crone. And I have come to collect.

**JASON**
Collect what? I carried you across. The debt is paid.

**HECATE**
(smiling — three smiles)
That was the down payment.

She raises her torches. The hut fills with triple light.

**HECATE** (CONT'D)
The fleece you seek — it's mine. The grove — mine. The priestess — mine. When you reach Colchis, you'll need me. Remember the river. Remember the debt. Remember that the goddess does not forget.

**JASON**
What priestess?

**HECATE**
Her name is Medea. She will love you. She will destroy you. She will save you. She will betray her father for you. And you — you will break her heart.

**JASON**
Why tell me this?

**HECATE**
Because knowing doesn't change anything. The pattern is the pattern.

THE FLEECE IS GUARDED. AND THE ONLY CURE IS THE QUEST.

## SCENE 8: THE VISION

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — VISION CONTENT: SHIP ARGO. CREW HERACLES ORPHEUS CASTOR POLLUX ATALANTA. DRAGON SLEEPLESS. FLEECE GOLDEN. MEDEA PRIESTESS TORCHES. VISION AUTHENTICITY: SOURCE HECATE DIVINE. CONFIDENCE 99.9 PERCENT. PURPOSE PREPARATION. EFFECT JASON NOW KNOWS THE PATH.]**

---

HECATE raises her torches higher. The hut fills with triple light. Jason sees:

A SHIP — fifty oars, timber from Olympus, waiting to be born.

A CREW — heroes gathering from across Greece.

A DRAGON — sleepless, vast, coiled around gold.

A WOMAN — black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes, torches identical to Hecate's.

MEDEA. Always Medea.

The vision fades. Hecate fades. The hut is dark. The moon is full.

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the divine — golden thread for the fleece, blue thread for Hecate, red thread for Medea. Three threads. One loom.

**LACHESIS** measures the vision — future to Colchis. 1,200 miles. But the vision is now. The future is present.

**ATROPOS** shears mortal limitation — cut. Jason is no longer mortal-only. He is divine-touched.

**HECATE** reveals herself — full form. Triple face. Triple torch. The goddess is invested. The debt is transformed. The alliance is sealed.

## NARRATOR ONE — CLOSING

The ordinary world is over. The call has been made. The goddess has spoken. The debt is set. The quest begins.

Jason lies in the dark. The moon is full. The sea is still. And somewhere — in the space between what is and what will be — the pattern weaves.

The fleece waits. The ship waits. The crew waits. Medea waits.

And the story — the story is just beginning.
