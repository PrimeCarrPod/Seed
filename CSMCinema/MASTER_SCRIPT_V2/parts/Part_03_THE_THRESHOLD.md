# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 03 OF 11: THE THRESHOLD

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The Symplegades. The Clashing Rocks. Two massive cliffs face each other across a channel no ship has ever survived.

The rocks clash. BOOM. Open. Close. BOOM. Each crash sends spray fifty meters high. The sound is 18 Hz infrasound — felt in bones, not heard in ears. Gravitational pulse. The rocks are not stone. They are — judgment.

The Argo approaches. Fifty oars. One hull. Fifty heroes who don't yet know if they're about to be heroes or corpses.

Jason stands at the prow. In his hand: a white dove. The test. The threshold. The moment before.

**ATALANTA**
(from the masthead)
No ship survives this.

**ORPHEUS**
(playing softly)
No ship has survived this.

**ZETES**
We could go around.

**CALAUS**
There is no around.

**JASON**
(at the prow, dove in hand)
Release the dove.

Zetes releases a white dove. It flies toward the rocks.

The crew watches. The rocks clash. The dove flies through — barely — losing only a tail feather.

**JASON** (CONT'D)
Row. Now.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

THE ROCKS CLASH. OPEN. CLOSE. BOOM. BOOM. BOOM. EACH CRASH A BODY. EACH BODY A BROTHER. VOICE IS 18 HZ. RANGE IS BONE. THE ACTOR DON'T ACT. THE ACTOR REMEMBERS. THE ROCKS AIN'T ROCKS. THEY'RE THE THRESHOLD. THE PLACE WHERE HEROES DIE. OR DON'T. THE DOVE FLIES. THE FEATHER FALLS. THE PRICE IS PAID. EVEN FROM DOVES.

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

THE THRESHOLD AIN'T A WALL, MAN. IT'S A DOOR. THE ROCKS CLASH BUT THEY ALSO OPEN. THE DOVE KNOWS. THE DOVE DON'T THINK. THE DOVE JUST FLIES. YOU DON'T FIGHT A WAVE. YOU RIDE IT. THE DUDE ABIDES. THE DUDE DON'T FIGHT THE ROCKS. THE DUDE FLIES THROUGH. SWEET.

## SCENE 2: THE PASSAGE

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — SYMPLADES ACOUSTICS: ROCK CLASH 18 HZ. SPRAY 80 HZ. OAR STROKE 2 HZ. DOVE WINGS 12 HZ. JASON BREATH 48 BPM. PASSAGE WINDOW: 3 SECONDS.]**

---

The crew rows. Fifty oars. The Argo shoots forward.

The rocks begin to close.

**ORPHEUS**
(playing, loud)
Faster!

The oars dig deeper. The ship accelerates. The rocks close—

The Argo passes through. The rocks clash behind them. The stern post is clipped — splinters — but the ship is through.

The crew cheers.

**ATALANTA**
(from above)
The feather. The dove lost a feather.

**JASON**
(quietly)
Everything has a price.

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the threshold — the last ocean mile woven in gold.

**LACHESIS** measures distance from Iolcus — she finds it infinite.

**ATROPOS** shears retreat option — snip. The way back is dust.

**HECATE** torches mark the harbor entrance — green, blue, red triple light welcoming and warning.

The Argo glides through mist. Gold-laced. Volatile. Poisonous.

The cliffs rise like teeth. The harbor opens like a mouth.

**JASON**
(quietly)
We've entered the whale.

**ORPHEUS**
(playing softly)
Then let's not be digested.

The ship drifts forward. Mist swallows the stern.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

They have crossed the threshold. The ordinary world is gone. They are inside the whale now — inside the place where heroes are digested or transformed.

Colchis. The kingdom of the Sun King. The grove of the sleepless dragon. The temple of Hecate where Medea prays.

The harbor is a throat. The cliffs are teeth. The mist is breath.

And somewhere — in the temple — a young priestess with black hair and gold eyes feels them arrive. She doesn't know how. She just knows.

The pattern tightens.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

THE WHALE SWALLOWED US. WE IN THE GUT NOW, MAN. NO MORE OCEAN. NO MORE SKY. JUST WALLS OF ROCK AND AIR THAT TASTES LIKE BURNED HAIR. VOICE IS CAVERN. RANGE IS SUBTERRANEAN. THE ACTOR ENTERS THE STOMACH. THE ROLE DIGESTS THE ACTOR. HE STANDS WHERE NO GREEK STOOD. HE STANDS WHERE ONLY FIRE KNOWS THE WAY. HE DON'T KNOW THE WAY. HE ENTERS ANYWAY.

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - DAY

MEDEA kneels before the altar. Nineteen years old. Black hair. Gold eyes. White priestess robes. Triple torches burn.

She prays in a language Jason wouldn't understand.

**HECATE**
(O.S., triple voice)
He comes. The one you will love. The one you will destroy. The one who will destroy you. The fleece is yours to give. The choice is yours to make.

**MEDEA**
(whispering)
I am a servant of the goddess. I have no choice.

**HECATE**
(O.S.)
You have every choice. That is the curse.
