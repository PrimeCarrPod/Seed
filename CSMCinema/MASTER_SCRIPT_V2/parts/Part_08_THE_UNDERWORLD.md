# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 08 OF 11: THE UNDERWORLD

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Cap Sounion. Night. The Argo anchors at a cliff. A cave below. Dark. Breathing.

The entrance to the underworld. The crew descends. Torchlight. Damp. The walls breathe.

CERBERUS blocks the path. Three-headed dog. Guardian. Growling.

Jason steps forward. Holds out his hand. The four threads glow. Cerberus sniffs. Recognizes something. Steps aside.

The River Styx. Black. CHARON poles a boat. Hollow eyes.

**CHARON**
Fare.

Each crew member gives a memory. A true memory. Not a lie. Not a wish. Truth.

The river receives each. Poles them across.

## SCENE 2: THE JUDGMENT

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — HADES PALACE: VAST. SHADOWS. SILENCE. THRONE OF BONE. HADES: 50s. DARK. PATIENT. REMEMBERS EVERYTHING. PERSEPHONE: 40s. HIS QUEEN. SPRING IN WINTER. KIND EYES. ---

INT. HADES' PALACE - NIGHT

A vast hall. Shadows. Silence. And on a throne of bone — HADES.

PERSEPHONE sits beside him.

**HADES**
Jason of Iolcus. You carry four threads. Sea. Sky. World. You want the fifth.

**JASON**
We want to understand.

**HADES**
(quietly)
Everyone says that. (beat) I don't test. I don't tempt. I judge. And my judgment is this: You've taken much. What have you given?

**JASON**
We gave ourselves. Our safety. Our—

**HADES**
(interrupting)
Wrong. You've taken. But you haven't given. Not really. Not yet.

**PERSEPHONE**
(softly)
What will you give?

**JASON**
(quietly)
My certainty.

**HADES**
(nodding)
You passed. Not because you were strong. Because you were willing to be weak.

## SCENE 3: THE BLACK THREAD

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — UNDERWORLD GIFT: BLACK THREAD. MEMORY OF THE UNDERWORLD. EVERY ENDING. EVERY LOSS. EVERY GOODBYE. WHEN YOU FEAR DEATH — HOLD THE THREAD. REMEMBER: ENDINGS ARE PART OF THE PATTERN.]**

---

**HADES**
(placing a thread in his hand)
Black. Memory of the underworld. Every ending. Every loss. Every goodbye. When you fear death — hold the thread. Remember: endings are part of the pattern.

**JASON**
(taking it)
Five threads.

**HADES**
Five looms. Two to go.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The underworld gives the black thread. The earth loom awaits. Below and within. Earth and the first loom. The pattern weaves. The hero becomes.

Jason now carries five threads: gold from the fleece, silver from the world, blue from the sea, white from the sky, black from the underworld, green from the earth (not yet).

The pattern is building. The center is forming. The hero is becoming.
