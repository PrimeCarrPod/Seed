# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 07 OF 11: THE SKY LOOM

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The sky. Night. The Argo floats. Not on water. On sky. Solid sky. Translucent. Stars visible through it.

And above — the constellations. Close. Immediate. Real.

The crew disembarks. Walks on sky. Each step ripples. Like water. Like light.

In the distance — a structure. Vast. Impossible. A loom made of light and darkness. Star and void. The SKY LOOM.

And before it — ZEUS (60s, beard of storm clouds, eyes like lightning, carries no weapon — he is the weapon).

---

## SCENE 1: THE SKY

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — SKY LOOM APPROACH: ARGO FLOATS ON SOLID SKY. STARS VISIBLE THROUGH IT. CREW WALKS ON SKY. EACH STEP RIPPLES. LIKE WATER. LIKE LIGHT. ZEUS PRESENCE: THUNDER IN HUMAN FORM. BEARD OF STORM CLOUDS. EYES LIKE LIGHTNING. CARRIES NO WEAPON — HE IS THE WEAPON.]**

---

**ZEUS**
Jason of Iolcus. You've refused Athena. Walked through Poseidon's wave. Touched the sea loom. And now — you come to mine.

**JASON**
We come to understand.

**ZEUS**
You want to understand the pattern so you can become it.

**JASON**
(quietly)
Yes.

**ZEUS**
(smiling)
At least you're honest. (beat) Face what you fear most. Accept it. And you pass.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

THEY RISE. NOT BECAUSE THEY'RE LIFTED. BECAUSE THEY'RE READY. THE FISHER KING KNOWS: YOU DON'T HEAL BY STAYING DOWN. YOU HEAL BY ASCENDING. BY REACHING. BY TOUCHING THE STARS. VOICE IS SKY. RANGE IS INFINITE. THE ACTOR ASCENDS. THE ROLE ASCENDS. THE STORY ASCENDS.

---

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

THE SKY AIN'T ABOVE, MAN. IT'S WITHIN. THE STARS AIN'T OUT THERE. THEY'RE IN HERE. IN THE PATTERN. IN THE LOOM. IN THE HEART. THE ARGO RISES BECAUSE THE HEART RISES. THE SKY IS JUST THE HEART SEEN FROM OUTSIDE.

---

## SCENE 2: THE TEST

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — SEPARATION PROTOCOL: CREW SEPARATED. EACH ALONE. EACH FACING FEAR. ATALANTA FEAR: OLDER. SLOWER. UNABLE TO HUNT. ORPHEUS FEAR: SILENCE. ABSOLUTE. PERMANENT. ZETES FEAR: STILLNESS. UNABLE TO FLY. CALAUS FEAR: SOLITUDE. NO BROTHER. MEDEA FEAR: JASON LEAVING. JASON FEAR: SUCCESS. POWER. KING OF EVERYTHING. ALONE. KORE FEAR: FORGOTTEN AGAIN.]**

---

The crew separated. Each alone. Each facing something different.

ATALANTA faces herself — older, slower, unable to hunt.

ORPHEUS faces silence — absolute, permanent. No music. No sound. Nothing.

ZETES faces stillness — the inability to fly. Wings clipped.

CALAUS faces solitude — no brother. No partner. Alone.

MEDEA faces Jason — leaving. Walking away. Choosing something else.

JASON faces himself — successful, powerful, king of everything. And completely alone.

KORE faces forgetting — the ice. The cold. The silence.

---

## SCENE 3: JASON'S FEAR

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — JASON FEAR: SUCCESS. POWER. KING OF EVERYTHING. ALONE. THE FEAR OF ISOLATION. THE FEAR THAT POWER CORRUPTS. THE FEAR THAT SUCCESS MEANS LOSING EVERYTHING THAT MATTERS.]**

---

JASON faces himself. The successful version. The king. The loneliest man in the world.

**KING JASON**
You see? I won. Everything I wanted.

**JASON**
At what cost?

**KING JASON**
Does it matter? I won.

**JASON**
Look at you. You're alone. No crew. No Medea. No friends. Just subjects. That's not winning. That's losing everything that matters.

The king fades.

**ZEUS**
(appearing)
You passed. You chose love over power. That's the hardest choice. Most fail.

---

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the sky — the stars woven in white.

**LACHESIS** measures the height — infinite.

**ATROPOS** shears positioned for falling — but waiting.

**HECATE** torches mark the ascent path — green, blue, red triple light guiding.

---

## SCENE 4: THE WHITE THREAD

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — SKY LOOM GIFT: WHITE THREAD. MEMORY OF THE SKY. EVERY STAR. EVERY CONSTELLATION. EVERY CHOICE MADE IN LIGHT. WHEN YOU'RE LOST IN DARKNESS — HOLD THE THREAD. THE STARS WILL GUIDE YOU.]**

---

**ZEUS**
(placing a thread in his hand)
White. Memory of the sky. Every star. Every constellation. Every choice made in light. When you're lost in darkness — hold the thread. The stars will guide you.

**JASON**
(taking it)
Six threads.

**ZEUS**
Six looms. One to go.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The sky loom gives the white thread. The underworld awaits. Above and below. Light and dark. The pattern weaves. The hero descends.

Jason now carries six threads: gold from the fleece, silver from the world, blue from the sea, white from the sky, black from the underworld (not yet), green from the earth (not yet).

The pattern is building. The center is forming. The hero is becoming.
