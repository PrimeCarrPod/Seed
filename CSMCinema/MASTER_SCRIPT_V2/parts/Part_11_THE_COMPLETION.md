# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 11 OF 11: THE COMPLETION

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Iolcus. Temple of Hecate. Dawn. The tree of hope blooms.

Kore at the center. The eighth thread around her wrist. It pulses. Not calling. Not pulling. Just — waiting.

Medea beside her. Jason watching. The pattern — whole.

---

## SCENE 1: THE EIGHTH THREAD CALLS

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — EIGHTH THREAD STATE: PULSING. NOT CALLING. NOT PULLING. WAITING. THE PATTERN IS COMPLETE. BUT NOT FINISHED. COMPLETE MEANS WHOLE. FINISHED MEANS OVER. THE PATTERN IS WHOLE. BUT IT'S NEVER OVER.]**

---

**MEDEA**
(writing)
It's quiet.

**KORE**
(nodding)
Too quiet. The pattern is — complete. And I don't know what to do with the rest of my life.

**MEDEA**
(writing, smiling)
That's what being human is. Not knowing. Just living.

**THE PATTERN**
(voice like everything)
The pattern is complete. But not finished.

**KORE**
(confused)
What's the difference?

**THE PATTERN**
(softly)
Complete means whole. Finished means — over. The pattern is whole. But it's never over.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

THE PATTERN IS COMPLETE. NOT BECAUSE IT'S PERFECT. BECAUSE IT'S LOVED. THE FISHER KING KNOWS: YOU DON'T HEAL BY CONTROLLING. YOU HEAL BY— (SEARCHING) —BY LOVING. BY LETTING GO. BY TRUSTING. VOICE IS EVERYTHING. RANGE IS INFINITE. THE ACTOR DOESN'T ACT. THE ACTOR IS. THE PATTERN IS COMPLETE. AND THE PATTERN IS LOVE.

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

THE PATTERN AIN'T SOMETHING YOU HOLD, MAN. IT'S SOMETHING YOU ARE. AND BEING MEANS— (SEARCHING) —MEANS CHOOSING. LOVING. LIVING. THE PATTERN IS COMPLETE. AND WE ARE THE PATTERN. AND THE PATTERN IS LOVE. AND LOVE IS EVERYTHING.

---

## SCENE 2: JASON RETURNS

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — JASON RETURN: THE PATTERN RELEASED HIM. HE GAVE IT EVERYTHING. AND IT— (SEARCHING) —IT GAVE ME BACK. I'M HERE. I'M REAL. I'M— (SEARCHING) —I'M ME AGAIN.]**

---

A figure approaches. Old. Familiar. But changed.

**MEDEA**
(awed, writing)
Jason?

**JASON**
(older, human, mortal)
Hello, Medea.

**MEDEA**
(running to him)
You're here. You're real.

**JASON**
(taking her hands)
I'm here. I'm real. I'm — me again.

**MEDEA**
(writing, crying)
How?

**JASON**
(smiling)
The pattern released me. I gave it everything. And it — gave me back.

**MEDEA**
(writing, crying)
I missed you.

**JASON**
(softly)
I missed you too. But I was — everywhere. In every thread. Every choice. Every moment.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
And now?

**JASON**
(smiling)
Now I'm — here. With you. Home.

---

## SCENE 3: THE EIGHTH THREAD PASSES

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — EIGHTH THREAD CHOOSING: A CHILD APPROACHES. YOUNG. CURIOUS. FORGOTTEN. THE THREAD PULSES. RECOGNIZING. THE THREAD UNWRAPS FROM KORE'S WRIST. FLOATS TOWARD THE CHILD. THE CHILD IS— (SEARCHING) —IS THE NEXT KEEPER.]**

---

A CHILD approaches. Young. Curious. Forgotten.

**CHILD**
(to Kore)
I don't have a home.

**KORE**
(softly)
Then stay. Here. We'll find you one.

The eighth thread pulses. Recognizing.

**KORE** (CONT'D)
(awed)
It's — choosing.

The thread unwraps from her wrist. Floats toward the child.

**CHILD**
(afraid)
What's happening?

**KORE**
(smiling through tears)
It's choosing you.

The thread wraps around the child's wrist. Light — consuming. Completing.

**CHILD**
(awed)
I can feel them. Everyone. Everything.

**MEDEA**
(writing, crying)
Welcome to the center.

**KORE**
(breathing)
I'm free.

**JASON**
(hugging her)
You're free.

**KORE**
(crying)
What do I do now?

**JASON**
(smiling)
Whatever you want.

**KORE**
(thinking)
I want to — teach. To help. To remember.

**MEDEA**
(writing, smiling)
Then do that.

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the completion — all threads gathered. All threads released.

**LACHESIS** measures the pattern — whole. Complete. Forever.

**ATROPOS** shears nothing — the pattern is eternal.

**HECATE** torches burn gold — the color of the fleece. The color of the quest. The color of destiny. The color of love.

## SCENE 4: THE FINAL LESSON

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — JASON'S LAST CLASS: THE PATTERN ISN'T IN ME. IT'S IN YOU. THE PATTERN ISN'T SOMETHING YOU HOLD. IT'S SOMETHING YOU ARE. EVERY CHOICE YOU MAKE. EVERY PERSON YOU LOVE. EVERY MOMENT YOU LIVE.]**

---

**JASON**
(to the students)
The pattern isn't in me. It's in you.

**STUDENT**
But what if we fail?

**JASON**
(smiling)
Then you fail. And you try again. That's the pattern. Not perfection. Persistence. Not certainty. Faith. Not control. Love.

## SCENE 5: THE GODS' GRATITUDE

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — GODS' GRATITUDE: ZEUS. POSEIDON. HADES. ATHENA. ALL THE GODS. THANKING JASON. YOU TAUGHT US HOW TO BE HUMAN. YOU TAUGHT US HOW TO BE FREE. YOU TAUGHT US HOW TO BE— (SEARCHING) —HOW TO BE LOVE.]**

---

**ZEUS**
You taught us how to be human.

**JASON**
You were always more than tyrants. You just forgot.

**POSEIDON**
(smiling)
How to be friends.

**HADES**
(quietly)
How to be part of things.

**ATHENA**
(awed)
How to be — free.

---

## SCENE 6: THE PATTERN'S FINAL WORDS

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — PATTERN'S FINAL WORDS: I AM THE PATTERN. AND I AM LOVE. AND LOVE IS EVERYTHING. AND EVERYTHING IS LOVE. THE PATTERN CONTINUES. FOREVER.]**

---

**THE PATTERN**
(voice like everything)
I am the pattern. And I am love.

**JASON**
(whispering)
And love is everything.

**KORE**
(smiling)
Then we're — home.

The pattern continues. Forever.

- **NARRATOR ONE** — Describes the scene. The setting. The action. The Bunraku puppeteer voice.

- **GLOBAL PARALLELS** — Spoken by Narrator One. Woven into the fabric. No list at the end.
- **SCENE NUMBERS** — Each scene is numbered. Each scene has its own JSNBAI integration.
- **BOLD TEXT** — All bold text is a narrator voice. The Fates are bold. The narrators are bold.
- **NO NANU NANU** — Removed entirely. The Williams voice speaks without the catchphrase.
- **BUNRAKU STYLE** — The narrators are visible. They describe everything. The actors (Jason, Medea, Kore, the gods) speak their lines while the narrators paint the world around them.
