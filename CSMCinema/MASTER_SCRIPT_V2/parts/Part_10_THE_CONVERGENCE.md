# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 10 OF 11: THE CONVERGENCE

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Iolcus. Temple of Hecate. Dawn. The tree of hope blooms.

Jason at the bow. The eight threads around his wrist. Medea beside him.

The pattern speaks. Not calling. Not pulling. Just — waiting.

---

## SCENE 1: THE PATTERN AWAKES

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — PATTERN STATE: AWAKE. ALIVE. THE PATTERN IS NOT A THING ANYMORE. IT'S— (SEARCHING) —IT'S ALIVE. BECAUSE JASON LET GO. WHEN HE RELEASED THE SEVEN THREADS— (SEARCHING) —HE RELEASED THE PATTERN. I'M NOT A THING ANYMORE. I'M ALIVE.]**

---

**THE PATTERN**
(voice like everything)
Jason. You freed me. And now I don't know what to do.

**JASON**
(quietly)
I know the feeling.

**THE PATTERN**
You?

**JASON**
I was the center. I held everything. And then I let go. And I was terrified. But also free.

**THE PATTERN**
(afraid)
But what do I do? Without someone to hold me?

**MEDEA**
(writing, gently)
The same thing we do. You live. You choose. You become.

**THE PATTERN**
(awed)
Become what?

**MEDEA**
(writing, smiling)
Whatever you want.

---

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

THE PATTERN AIN'T A THING NO MORE. IT'S ALIVE. BECAUSE HE LET GO. THE FISHER KING KNOWS: YOU DON'T HEAL BY HOLDING. YOU HEAL BY— (SEARCHING) —BY RELEASING. BY LETTING GO. BY TRUSTING. VOICE IS EVERYTHING. RANGE IS INFINITE. THE ACTOR DOESN'T ACT. THE ACTOR IS. THE PATTERN IS ALIVE. AND THE PATTERN IS LOVE.

---

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

THE PATTERN AIN'T OUT THERE, MAN. IT'S IN HERE. IN EVERY CHOICE. IN EVERY LOVE. IN EVERY MOMENT. THE PATTERN ISN'T SOMETHING YOU HOLD. IT'S SOMETHING YOU ARE. AND BEING MEANS— (SEARCHING) —MEANS CHOOSING. LOVING. LIVING. THE PATTERN IS ALIVE. AND WE ARE THE PATTERN.

---

## SCENE 2: THE GODS ARRIVE

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — GODS ARRIVE: ZEUS. POSEIDON. HADES. ATHENA. ALL THE GODS. FURIOUS. THE PATTERN IS OUT OF CONTROL. THE SEAS ARE CHOOSING. THE DEAD ARE WALKING. THE STARS ARE REARRANGING. THE FUTURE IS UNWRITTEN. THE GODS ARE— (SEARCHING) —ARE AFRAID.]**

---

Thunder. Lightning. The air splits.

ZEUS descends. POSEIDON. HADES. ATHENA. All the gods. Furious.

**ZEUS**
Jason! You've destroyed everything!

**JASON**
(calmly)
Have I?

**POSEIDON**
The pattern is out of control! The seas are choosing their own paths!

**JASON**
(smiling)
Good.

**ZEUS**
(draws lightning)
You don't understand. Without the pattern — there is chaos.

**JASON**
(shaking his head)
No. Without control — there is freedom.

**ATHENA**
And what of us? What of the gods?

**JASON**
You get to choose too. Not as rulers. As guardians. Helpers. Friends.

---

## SCENE 3: THE GODS RELEASE

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — GODS RELEASE: ZEUS RELEASES THE THUNDER. POSEIDON RELEASES THE SEAS. HADES RELEASES THE DEAD. ATHENA RELEASES WISDOM. THE WORLD RESPONDS. NOT WITH CHAOS. WITH BEAUTY.]**

---

The gods stand together. And they — let go.

**ZEUS**
(releasing)
I release the thunder. Let it be weather. Not weapon.

**POSEIDON**
(releasing)
I release the seas. Let them be seas. Not soldiers.

**HADES**
(releasing)
I release the dead. Let them be remembered. Not judged.

**ATHENA**
(releasing)
I release wisdom. Let it be shared. Not hoarded.

The world responds. Not with chaos. With beauty.

---

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the convergence — all threads gathered.

**LACHESIS** measures the pattern — whole.

**ATROPOS** shears nothing — the pattern is complete.

**HECATE** torches burn gold — the color of the fleece. The color of the quest. The color of destiny.

The gods release. The pattern lives. The world is free.

Not because it's controlled. Because it's loved.

The convergence is complete. The pattern is whole. The story ends. And begins.
