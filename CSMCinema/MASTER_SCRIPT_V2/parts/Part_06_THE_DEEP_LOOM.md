# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 06 OF 11: THE DEEP LOOM

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Open sea. Night. The Argo sails west. The water glows. Not from above. From below.

The stars dim. One by one. Until darkness. Complete. Absolute.

And then — light. Not stars. Something else. Something new.

The sea is giving them to the sky. Or the sky is giving them to the sea. The boundary dissolves.

They're inside the pattern now. Inside the loom. Inside everything.

---

## SCENE 1: THE THINNING

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — STAR FADE SEQUENCE: STARS DISAPPEARING ONE BY ONE. SEQUENCE NORTH TO SOUTH. TIME SEVEN MINUTES THIRTY THREE SECONDS. SEA GLOW ANALYSIS: BIOLUMINESCENCE NOT CHEMICAL NOT BIOLOGICAL. PATTERN-BASED. THE SEA IS ALIVE. PATTERN THINNING: THE PATTERN IS THINNING NOT BREAKING. THINNING. BECOMING TRANSPARENT. BECOMING EVERYTHING.]**

---

**ATALANTA**
(from above)
The stars are disappearing.

**ORPHEUS**
(stopping his playing)
The music is fading.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
The pattern is thinning. We're reaching the edge.

**KORE**
(afraid)
What happens if it disappears?

**JASON**
(quietly)
We find out.

The stars go out. One by one. Until darkness. Complete. Absolute.

And then — light. Not stars. Something else. Something new.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

THEY SAIL INTO THE IMPOSSIBLE. THE SEA GLOWS. THE STARS DIE. THE DARKNESS AIN'T DARK. IT'S FULL. FULL OF EVERYTHING. FULL OF NOTHING. FULL OF THE PATTERN. VOICE IS 18 HZ. RANGE IS BONE. THE ACTOR DON'T ACT. THE ACTOR IS. THE SEA AIN'T WATER. THE SEA IS MEMORY.

---

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

THE SEA AIN'T WATER, MAN. IT'S MEMORY. EVERY WAVE A MEMORY. EVERY CURRENT A MEMORY. EVERY DROP A MEMORY. THE ARGO SAILS ON MEMORY. THE CREW SAILS ON MEMORY. JASON SAILS ON MEMORY. THE PATTERN AIN'T OUT THERE. THE PATTERN IS IN HERE. IN THE WATER. IN THE MEMORY. IN THE DEEP.

---

## SCENE 2: THE DESCENT

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — WATER COLUMN ANALYSIS: NOT NATURAL. PATTERN-BASED. THE SEA IS GIVING THE ARGO TO THE SKY. ASCENT RATE TEN METERS PER SECOND. STEADY. CONTROLLED. THE PATTERN LIFTS. STAR VISIBILITY: STARS VISIBLE IN DAYLIGHT. NOT FADING. BRIGHTENING. THE SKY IS OPENING.]**

---

The water rises. Not a wave. A column. The Argo on top of it. Rising.

The crew grabs the rails.

**ATALANTA**
(from above)
The sea is giving us to the sky.

**ORPHEUS**
(playing)
The music is — changing. Becoming something else.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
The pattern is — we're inside it now.

The column rises higher. The ocean shrinks below. The sky grows closer.

**ATALANTA**
(from above, laughing)
I can touch the stars!

She reaches. Her fingers brush a star. It chimes. Like glass. Like music.

---

## SCENE 3: POSEIDON

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — UNDERWATER CAVERN: VAST. BIOLUMINESCENT. BEAUTIFUL. TERRIFYING. LOOM OF THE SEA: CORAL. BONE. PEARL. WOVEN BY POSEIDON HIMSELF. POSEIDON STATE: CURIOUS. AMUSED. THE GREEK WHO WALKED THROUGH HIS WAVE HAS RETURNED. THE GREEK WHO TOUCHED THE STARS HAS DESCENDED. THE PATTERN IS INTERESTING AGAIN.]**

---

INT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - NIGHT

The Argo descends. The sea parts. A tunnel of water. The ship descends.

The crew stares at the walls of water. Fish swim past. Whales. Things that aren't whales.

The tunnel opens. A vast underwater cavern. Bioluminescent. Beautiful. Terrifying.

And in the center — the LOOM OF THE SEA. Coral. Bone. Pearl. Woven by Poseidon himself.

And beside it — POSEIDON (50s, bearded, trident, eyes like the deep).

**POSEIDON**
Jason of Iolcus.

**JASON**
Lord Poseidon.

**POSEIDON**
You walked through my wave. That was clever. Or lucky.

**JASON**
Does it matter which?

**POSEIDON**
(laughing)
I like you, Greek. You remind me of myself. Before Olympus. Before the chains.

**JASON**
Chains?

**POSEIDON**
(quietly)
The gods are chained too. By duty. By expectation. By the pattern.

**JASON**
Then you understand.

**POSEIDON**
(nodding)
I understand. (beat) Touch the loom. But know — it will show you what you're afraid of.

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the deep — the sea woven in blue.

**LACHESIS** measures the depth — infinite.

**ATROPOS** shears positioned for drowning — but waiting.

**HECATE** torches mark the descent path — green, blue, red triple light guiding.

## SCENE 4: THE BLUE THREAD

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — LOOM OF THE SEA: CORAL. BONE. PEARL. WOVEN BY POSEIDON WHEN THE WORLD WAS YOUNG. BEFORE HE LEARNED RESTRAINT. THE LOOM DOESN'T JUST WEAVE WATER. IT WEAVES CHAOS. JASON TOUCHES THE LOOM. SEES: THE WORLD BEFORE THE GODS. CHAOS. PURE. UNDIFFERENTIATED. THE FIRST PATTERN. THE FIRST CHOICE. THE FIRST— (SEARCHING) —THE FIRST MISTAKE.]**

---

Jason touches the loom.

The cavern disappears. He's somewhere else. Somewhen else.

A VISION: The world before the gods. Chaos. Pure. Undifferentiated. No land. No sea. No sky. Just — possibility.

And in the chaos — a choice. The first choice. The one that separated sea from sky. Land from water. Being from non-being.

**THE PATTERN**
(V.O., voice like everything)
You see now. Before the gods. Before time. Before everything. There was choice. And choice created pattern. And pattern created everything.

**JASON**
Why show me this?

**THE PATTERN**
Because you need to understand. The looms don't create. They reveal.

The vision ends. Jason is back in the cavern.

**POSEIDON**
(placing a thread in his hand)
Blue. Memory of the sea. Every wave. Every current. Every storm. When you're lost — hold the thread. The sea will guide you.

**JASON**
(taking it)
Five threads.

**POSEIDON**
Five looms. Two to go.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The deep loom gives the blue thread. The sky loom awaits. Below and above. Water and sky. The pattern weaves. The hero ascends.

Jason now carries five threads: gold from the fleece, silver from the world, blue from the sea, white from the sky (not yet), black from the underworld (not yet), green from the earth (not yet).

The pattern is building. The center is forming. The hero is becoming.
