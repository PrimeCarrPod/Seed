# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 09 OF 11: THE EARTH LOOM

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Mount Olympus. Base. Day. The crew travels on foot toward Olympus.

The earth loom is beneath it. Where Gaia wove the world into being.

The crew descends. Into the mountain. Into the earth.

The walls pulse. Not stone. Something alive. Breathing.

The cavern opens. Vast. Impossible. Roots the size of ships. And in the center — a loom. Not made of anything. Just — there. Always there. The EARTH LOOM.

And beside it — GAIA (ageless, neither young nor old, mother of everything, eyes that have seen it all).

---

## SCENE 1: GAIA

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — GAIA CAVERN: VAST. IMPOSSIBLE. ROOTS THE SIZE OF SHIPS. LOOM: NOT MADE OF ANYTHING. JUST THERE. ALWAYS THERE. GAIA: AGELESS. NEITHER YOUNG NOR OLD. MOTHER OF EVERYTHING. EYES THAT HAVE SEEN IT ALL. GAIA STATE: CURIOUS. THE GREEK WHO WALKED THROUGH FIRE HAS ENTERED THE DEEP. THE GREEK WHO TOUCHED THE STARS HAS ENTERED THE EARTH. THE PATTERN IS INTERESTING AGAIN.]**

---

**GAIA**
(without turning)
I wondered when you'd come.

**JASON**
You knew we were coming?

**GAIA**
(smiling)
I know everything that walks on me. (turning) You carry five threads. You want the sixth.

**JASON**
We want to understand.

**GAIA**
(sadly)
Understanding is overrated. (beat) But you'll learn that. In time.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

SHE STANDS WHERE NO GOD STANDS. NOT AS RULER. NOT AS QUEEN. AS— (SEARCHING) —AS MOTHER. THE FISHER KING KNOWS: YOU DON'T HEAL BY CONTROLLING. YOU HEAL BY— (SEARCHING) —BY MOTHERING. BY NURTURING. BY LETTING GROW. VOICE IS EARTH. RANGE IS INFINITE. THE ACTOR DOESN'T ACT. THE ACTOR IS. THE EARTH AIN'T GROUND. THE EARTH IS MOTHER.

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

THE EARTH AIN'T GROUND, MAN. IT'S MOTHER. EVERY ROOT A MEMORY. EVERY RIVER A MEMORY. EVERY MOUNTAIN A MEMORY. THE CREW WALKS ON MEMORY. JASON WALKS ON MEMORY. THE PATTERN AIN'T OUT THERE. THE PATTERN IS IN HERE. IN THE EARTH. IN THE MEMORY. IN THE DEEP.

---

## SCENE 2: THE STORY OF MAKING

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — GAIA STORY: BEFORE ME THERE WAS CHAOS. AND I CHOSE. THE FIRST CHOICE. TO WEAVE ORDER FROM CHAOS. AND THAT CHOICE CREATED EVERYTHING. THE GODS. THE WORLD. YOU. WHY? BECAUSE I WAS LONELY. CHAOS IS FREE BUT IT'S ALONE. I WANTED CONNECTION. AND THE SUFFERING? THE COST. EVERY THREAD I WOVE EXCLUDED EVERY OTHER THREAD.]**

---

**GAIA**
Sit. All of you. I'll tell you a story.

The crew sits.

**GAIA** (CONT'D)
Before me — there was chaos. And I chose. The first choice. To weave order from chaos. And that choice created everything. The gods. The world. You.

**JASON**
Why?

**GAIA**
Because I was lonely. (beat) Chaos is free — but it's alone. I wanted connection.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
And the suffering?

**GAIA**
(sadly)
The cost. Every thread I wove — excluded every other thread.

---

## SCENE 3: THE GREEN THREAD

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — EARTH LOOM GIFT: GREEN THREAD. MEMORY OF THE EARTH. EVERY ROOT. EVERY SEED. EVERY RETURN. WHEN YOU'RE LOST — HOLD THE THREAD. THE EARTH WILL HOLD YOU.]**

---

**GAIA**
(to Jason)
Give me your hand.

Jason extends his hand. Gaia touches it.

The cavern disappears. Jason sees:

The world as Gaia sees it. Every root. Every river. Every mountain. Every life. All connected. All woven. All — one pattern.

And in the pattern — a gap. A hole. Something missing.

**GAIA** (V.O.)
The pattern is incomplete. It needs — a center. A consciousness. Someone who holds it all together.

**JASON**
(in the vision)
And if I refuse?

**GAIA** (V.O.)
Then the pattern continues without a center. The gods continue. The world continues. But something beautiful — will be lost.

The vision ends. Jason is back in the cavern.

**GAIA**
(placing a thread in his hand)
Green. Memory of the earth. Every root. Every seed. Every return. When you're lost — hold the thread. The earth will hold you.

**JASON**
(taking it)
Six threads.

**GAIA**
Six looms. One to go.

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the earth — the world woven in green.

**LACHESIS** measures the depth — infinite.

**ATROPOS** shears positioned for burial — but waiting.

**HECATE** torches mark the descent path — green, blue, red triple light guiding.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The earth loom gives the green thread. The first loom awaits. The convergence. The completion. The pattern weaves. The hero becomes the center.

Jason now carries six threads: gold from the fleece, silver from the world, blue from the sea, white from the sky, black from the underworld, green from the earth.

The pattern is building. The center is forming. The hero is becoming.
