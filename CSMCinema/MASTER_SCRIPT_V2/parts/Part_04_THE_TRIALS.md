## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The Plains of Ares. Dawn breaks over volcanic soil — black earth cracked like dragon hide.

The field stretches four hundred meters square. Volcanic soil depth two meters. Plow path required: sixteen hundred meters.

Two bulls emerge from subterranean pens. Not cattle. Forged. Bronze hooves. Fire nostrils. Breath that melts stone.

HEPHAESTUS-FORGED. Created for Aetes by Hephaestus himself. Bronze alloy includes orichalcum. Fire source: stolen from Olympus. Breath temperature: 1,200 degrees Celsius. Hoovfall impact force: 4,000 PSI. Bronze mass per bull: 800 kilograms.

Jason stands at the field edge. Alone. Empty-handed. No weapon. No shield.

Only the invisible loom thread around his wrist — Medea's gift. Protection radius: thirty centimeters. Frequency: 11.11 Hz.

**AETES**
(from his throne of knives)
Yoke them. Plow the field. Sow the dragon's teeth. Bring me the fleece. Or burn.

Jason walks forward. Empty-handed.

The bulls charge. Fire erupts.

**JASON**
(to himself, steady)
Fire is not the enemy. Fire is the wave. You don't fight a wave. You ride it.

He reaches the first bull. Puts his hand on its muzzle.

The bull snarls. Breathes fire.

Jason doesn't burn. The thread holds.

**JASON** (CONT'D)
(quietly)
I'm not your enemy. I'm your new master. Yoke yourselves.

The bull kneels.

The second bull watches. Approaches. Jason touches its muzzle.

The second bull kneels.

## SCENE 2: THE TEETH

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — DRAGON'S TEETH SOWING: EACH TOOTH A BLACK DIAMOND. EACH ONE PULSING WITH SOMETHING ANCIENT. WHEN THEY SPRING FROM EARTH THEY'LL BE CONFUSED. DISORIENTED. TEN SECONDS BEFORE THEY ORGANIZE. THROW A STONE INTO THEIR MIDST. THEY'LL FIGHT EACH OTHER. THE SPARTOI — DRAGON'S TEETH MEN — ATTACK WHATEVER MOVES NEAREST.]**

---

Jason yokes the bulls. Plows the field. Sows the teeth.

The earth trembles.

Warriors spring from the soil. Armored. Armed. Confused. Angry. They number — ten — twenty — thirty — more.

Jason picks up a stone. Throws it into their midst.

The nearest warrior strikes at the movement. Hits another warrior. That warrior strikes back. Within seconds — they're fighting each other.

Jason watches. Waits. The warriors destroy each other.

One remains. It turns to Jason. Raises its sword.

Jason picks up a broken sword from a fallen warrior. They fight.

The warrior is strong but clumsy. Born minutes ago. No training. No instinct beyond violence.

Jason is trained. Chiron taught him. Twenty years of training.

He wins. The warrior falls. Dissolves into earth.

Silence.

## SCENE 3: MEDEA'S CHOICE

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — MEDECISION STATE: HEART RATE 72 BPM. RESPIRATION SHALLOW. CHOOSING JASON OVER FAMILY. OVER KINGDOM. OVER EVERYTHING. THE PATTERN DEMANDS IT. THE GODDESS COMMANDS IT. BUT THE CHOICE IS HERS. THAT IS THE CURSE.]**

---

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

Jason enters. Medea kneels before the altar.

**MEDEA**
(startled)
You shouldn't be here.

**JASON**
You gave me the vial.

**MEDEA**
I gave you medicine. For the bulls tomorrow.

**JASON**
You gave me more than that.

(long pause)

What's your name?

**MEDEA**
Medea.

(longer pause)

If I help you — I betray my father. My kingdom. Everything I've ever known.

**JASON**
Then don't help me.

**MEDEA**
(startled)
You'd fail. You'd die.

**JASON**
Probably. Likely.

(beat)

But it's your choice. Not the goddess's. Not the pattern's. Yours.

**MEDEA**
(quietly, after a long moment)
I choose to help you. I choose to betray my father. I choose... you.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The bulls are yoked. The field is plowed. The teeth are sown. The warriors are slain.

But the real trial — the real fire — is not in the field. It's in the temple. It's in the choice. It's in the moment when a priestess decides to betray everything she's ever known for a stranger with one sandal and a golden thread.

Medea chooses. Not because the goddess commands. Not because the pattern demands. Because she chooses.

That is the curse. That is the gift. That is the pattern.
