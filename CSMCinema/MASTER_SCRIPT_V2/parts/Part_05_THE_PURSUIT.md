## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 05 OF 11: THE PURSUIT

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Open sea. Day. The Argo sails. Behind: Colchis burns.

Three ships. Colchian colors. ABSYRTUS commands the lead ship. Twenty years old. Medea's brother. Golden armor. Thinks three moves ahead.

The crew stabilizes. The fleet closes.

Fifty warriors per ship. One hundred fifty combatants total. The Argo has fifty heroes. The math is simple. The math is death.

## SCENE 1: THE PURSUIT

**ATALANTA**
(from above)
Ships. Three of them. Colchian colors.

**JASON**
Absyrtus?

**ATALANTA**
No. Aetes himself. His entire fleet.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
He came.

**JASON**
How many ships?

**ATALANTA**
Three. Maybe more following.

**JASON**
We can't fight three ships.

**ORPHEUS**
We can't outrun them either.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
Then we don't go through.

**JASON**
There is no around. They're blocking the only entrance.

**MEDEA**
(writing, firmly)
Then we make a new entrance.

## SCENE 2: THE TRICK

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — JASON STRATEGY: CANNOT FIGHT. CANNOT OUTRUN. MUST TRICK. MEDEA KNOWS COLCHIAN TACTICS. HER BROTHER ABSYRTUS COMMANDS. HE'S SMARTER THAN AETES. BUT JASON HAS SOMETHING ABSYRTUS DOESN'T — THE PATTERN. THE THREAD. THE CHOICE MEDEA MADE.]**

---

**JASON**
(to the crew)
Lower the sail. Raise a white flag.

**ZETES**
We're surrendering?

**JASON**
We're pretending to surrender.

The Argo approaches the center of the Colchian line. Aetes' flagship pulls alongside.

**AETES**
(calling)
Greek! You stole my daughter! You stole the fleece! There's nowhere to run!

**JASON**
(calling back)
I know! I surrender! Let me aboard! Let's talk!

**AETES**
(calling)
Talk? You want to talk?

**JASON**
(calling)
What I took — I took for love! Not for war! Let me explain!

A long pause. Then:

**AETES**
(calling)
Come aboard! Alone! And bring my daughter!

**JASON**
(to Medea, quietly)
You don't have to—

**MEDEA**
(writing)
I'm coming.

## SCENE 3: THE FATHER

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — AETES STATE: ANGRY. AFRAID. HIS DAUGHTER BETRAYED HIM. HIS KINGDOM IS SHAMED. HIS POWER IS QUESTIONED. HE WANTS REVENGE. BUT HE ALSO WANTS— (SEARCHING) —WANTS HIS DAUGHTER BACK. THE CONFLICT IS TOTAL.]**

---

EXT. AETES' FLAGSHIP - DAY

Jason and Medea board. Aetes stands before them. Guards surround them.

**AETES**
(to Medea)
Daughter. Come home.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
No.

**AETES**
You'd choose this Greek over your father? Over your kingdom? Over your blood?

**MEDEA**
(writing)
I choose myself.

**AETES**
(angry)
Yourself? You are nothing without me! Without Colchis! Without—

**MEDEA**
(writing, firmly)
I am everything. I am the loom. I am the pattern. I am the priestess. I was never yours. I was always my own.

**AETES**
(draws his sword)
Then truth will kill you.

Medea steps between them. Writes:

**MEDEA** (CONT'D)
(writing)
You have to go through me.

A long moment. Aetes stares at his daughter. She stares back. Unflinching.

**AETES**
(lowering the sword)
Go. Both of you. Before I change my mind.

**MEDEA**
(writing)
Father—

**AETES**
(turning away)
You're not my daughter. Not anymore.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The pursuit ends. The escape succeeds. But the journey continues.

Medea has chosen. She has betrayed her father. She has chosen Jason. She has chosen herself.

And Aetes — the Sun King, the father, the king who lost his daughter — stands on his flagship and watches them go.

The pattern tightens. The debt is paid. The escape is complete.

But the journey is not over. The deep loom awaits.
