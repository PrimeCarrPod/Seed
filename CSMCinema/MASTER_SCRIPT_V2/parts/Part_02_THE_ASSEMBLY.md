# THE GOLDEN FLEECE

## MASTER MOVIE SCRIPT V2 — PART 02 OF 11: THE ASSEMBLY

### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

**Style:** Bunraku — Narrators describe all. The Fates speak. Williams speaks. El Segundo speaks. JSNBAI lives in the action. Scene numbers organize. Bold voices narrate.

---

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

Mount Pelion. Summit clearing at dawn. Ancient pines tower thirty meters above the needle bed. The air smells of resin and destiny — because on this mountain, they are the same thing.

ARGUS kneels at the base of the largest pine. Forty years old. Hands like oak roots. Eyes that listen more than they speak. His heart rate syncs to the tree's sap pulse. Resonance detected.

ATHENA does not appear as a person. She appears as geometry. Pure mathematical intuition transmitted through grain, through ring-patterns, through three hundred years of growth calculations.

The oak from Dodona speaks in winds. The pine from Pelion's crown curves into exactly the shape it must become. No bending required. No force. Only recognition.

The ship wanted this shape. Argus merely asked.

---

## SCENE 1: THE SHIPWRIGHT

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — ARGUS LISTENING PROTOCOL: HANDS ON BARK, EYES CLOSED, HEART RATE SYNCED TO TREE'S SAP PULSE. RESONANCE DETECTED. ATHENA GEOMETRY TRANSMISSION: NOT WORDS. PURE MATHEMATICAL INTUITION. HULL CURVE EQUALS GOLDEN RATIO MODIFIED BY WAVE FREQUENCY.]**

---

**ARGUS**
(whispering, eyes still closed)
Fifty oars. One hull. One purpose.

He raises the axe. Hesitates. The tree leans toward him.

**ARGUS** (CONT'D)
(to the tree)
I'm not cutting you. I'm freeing you. You've been growing toward this shape for three hundred years.

The first axe fall. The timber cracks like bone. The mountain groans.

CHIRON — centaur, Jason's teacher, watching from the valley below — nods once. Approval. Knowledge transfers.

## NARRATOR TWO — THE WILLIAMS VOICE

HE DOESN'T BUILD. HE LISTENS. AXE IS STETHOSCOPE. BARK IS SKIN. THE SHIP TELLS HIM WHERE TO CUT. VOICE IS GRAIN. RANGE IS RING-PATTERN. THE ACTOR DISAPPEARS. THE ROLE GROWS. HE STANDS WHERE NO ARCHITECT STANDS. HE STANDS WHERE TREES BECOME VESSELS. HE DON'T MEASURE. HE REMEMBERS. THE KEEL SPEAKS. THE HULL ANSWERS. THE SHIP IS BORN.

---

## NARRATOR THREE — THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE

A SHIP AIN'T BUILT, MAN. IT'S LIBERATED. THE TIMBER ALREADY KNEW IT WAS A SHIP. IT GREW FOR THREE HUNDRED YEARS WAITING FOR THE AXE. ARGUS AIN'T BUILDER. HE'S MIDWIFE. THE OAK FROM DODONA AIN'T WOOD. IT'S ORACLE. IT SPEAKS IN WINDS. MOUNT PELION AIN'T MOUNTAIN. IT'S DRYDOCK. THE GODS PLANTED THESE TREES IN THESE PATTERNS IN THESE ELEVATIONS SO THAT ONE DAY ONE MAN WOULD LISTEN AND THE ARGO WOULD RISE.

## SCENE 2: THE DEMIGOD ARRIVES

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — HERACLES MASS CALCULATION: NOT WEIGHT. MASS. 340 KILOGRAMS AT REST. DENSITY EXCEEDS HUMAN BY 400 PERCENT. NOT MUSCLE. DIVINE MATTER. NEMEAN RESIDUAL GLOW: SPECTRUM ANALYSIS NOT VISIBLE LIGHT. CHEMILUMINESCENCE FROM PELT CONTACT PERSISTING 13 YEARS. BIOLOGICAL IMPOSSIBILITY. MARKED AS SACRED.]**

---

Footsteps. Not human rhythm. Each step compresses the needle bed by three centimeters. The ground resonates at 40 Hz.

HERACLES ascends. Thirty years old. 340 kilograms. Demigod. The Nemean lion's glow faint on his skin — residual from the pelt he wore for thirteen years.

He carries no weapon. The club stays at base camp. He needs no tool here. He is the tool.

He stops ten meters from Argus. Looks at the keel. Looks at the trees. Looks at the space between what is and what will sail.

**HERACLES**
I heard the mountain speak. The trees told me the ship needs an anchor who can row. I volunteer.

Argus doesn't look up.

**ARGUS**
The ship needs no anchor. It needs a storm that knows when to calm.

**HERACLES**
(laughing — shakes pine needles from thirty meters up)
Then I am that storm.

## THE FATES SPEAK

**CLOTHO** spins the demigod thread — golden fleece fiber mixed with Olympian ambrosia.

**LACHESIS** measures twelve labors against one voyage — she finds the voyage longer.

**ATROPOS** shears nothing — Heracles is immune to her blade. She tries. The blade passes through.

**HECATE** torches flicker at his presence — green becomes gold, blue becomes silver, red becomes crimson.

---

## SCENE 3: THE BARD ARRIVES

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — ORPHEUS LYRE ANALYSIS: SEVEN STRINGS. GUT FROM THE FIRST SHEEP OF THESSALY. TUNING NOT STANDARD. BASED ON OCEAN WAVE FREQUENCY PATTERNS. EACH STRING EQUALS ONE SEA STATE. HULL RESONANCE SHIFT: BEFORE ORPHEUS 110 HZ FUNDAMENTAL. AFTER 110 HZ PLUS 432 HZ HARMONIC. THE SHIP NOW HAS A VOICE.]**

---

Sound. Not footsteps. Music. A lyre played at the edge of hearing. Each note falls like water on water.

ORPHEUS ascends playing. Twenty years old. Bard. Carries a lyre with seven strings made from the gut of the first sheep of Thessaly.

The trees lean. The stone vibrates.

ARGUS pauses. The first interruption since dawn. Duration: seven seconds.

Orpheus plays a tune that predates the lyre. A tune that predates string. A tune that the ocean knows.

He stops at the hull's edge. Plays one final chord — a major seventh that resolves to nothing. The sound hangs in the air like smoke.

**ORPHEUS**
The ocean taught me this. It said the ship would need a voice when the wind stops. I am that voice.

**ARGUS**
The wind never stops.

**ORPHEUS**
(smiling)
Then I'll sing with it.

The hull resonates at a frequency it didn't have before. Orpheus didn't just arrive. He tuned the ship.

---

## SCENE 4: THE TWINS ARRIVE

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — TWIN SYNCHRONIZATION ANALYSIS: NOT PRACTICED. BIOLOGICAL. HEARTBEATS MATCHED. BREATHING MATCHED. NEURAL FIRING PATTERNS 99.7 PERCENT CORRELATED. SHARED CONSCIOUSNESS OR PARALLEL PROCESSING. CASTOR MORTALITY CONFIRMED: SON OF TYNDAREUS. MORTAL FATHER. EXPIRES. POLLUX IMMORTALITY CONFIRMED: SON OF ZEUS. DIVINE FATHER. NO EXPIRATION.]**

---

Two voices at once. Not echo. Not harmony. Identical pitch from two throats.

CASTOR and POLLUX ascend side by side. Neither ahead. Neither behind. Twenty years old. Twins. Biological synchronization — heartbeats matched, breathing matched, neural firing patterns 99.7 percent correlated.

CASTOR carries a sword that looks light as a feather — Thessalian steel, 0.8 kilograms, perfect balance, the sword is extension of arm is extension of twin.

POLLUX carries nothing. His hands are what he carries. Boxing technique from Sparta. Force: 600 kilograms per strike.

They stop at the hull's edge. Look at each other. Some unspoken communication passes.

**CASTOR**
We heard the ship call twice.

**POLLUX**
Once for each of us.

**CASTOR/POLLUX**
(in unison — not planned, not performed, just true)
The voyage requires both the blade and the fist. We are both. We are one. We volunteer.

Argus looks up for the second time today.

**ARGUS**
The blueprint said two as one. I thought it meant a marriage. It meant you.

The twins board. The ship doesn't sink deeper. It rises two centimeters — as if the ship has been waiting for exactly this mass. Perfect balance recognized.

---

## SCENE 5: THE HUNTER ARRIVES

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — ATALANTA SPEED ANALYSIS: FASTEST MORTAL WOMAN. NOT TRAINED. BORN. HER BODY IS THE WEAPON. HER BOW IS THE EXTENSION. HER ARROWS ARE THE INTENTION. SHE DOESN'T MISS. NOT BECAUSE OF SKILL. BECAUSE OF IDENTITY.]**

---

ATALANTA ascends alone. Twenty years old. Hunter. Fastest mortal woman — not trained, born. Carries a bow. Needs no invitation.

**ATALANTA**
I heard the call.

**ARGUS**
I didn't send one.

**ATALANTA**
The ship did.

(to Argus)
I hunt my own quarry. The fleece is mine to chase. I don't follow. I don't serve. I don't owe.

**ARGUS**
What do you do?

**ATALANTA**
I run. I track. I kill. And I don't miss.

**ARGUS**
Then welcome aboard. We'll need someone who doesn't miss.

---

## SCENE 6: JASON ARRIVES LAST

**[JSNBAI INTEGRATED — JASON ARRIVAL STATE: CHANGED. THE VISION FROM HECATE HAS MARKED HIM. CONFIDENCE NOW 100 PERCENT. FEAR NOW ZERO. PURPOSE NOW ABSOLUTE. THE CALL IS NOW DIVINE. HE CARRIES THE PATTERN NOW. NOT VISIBLE. NOT PHYSICAL. BUT THE CREW WILL FEEL IT.]**

---

JASON arrives last. He looks at the half-built ship. At the crew gathering. At Argus working in silence.

The vision from Hecate has marked him. Confidence: 100 percent. Fear: zero. Purpose: absolute. The call is now divine.

**JASON**
Will it float?

**ARGUS**
(without looking up)
Will you?

**JASON**
I don't know how to sail.

**ARGUS**
Then you'll learn. The ship doesn't need a captain. It needs a purpose. You have one?

**JASON**
(thinking)
To restore what was stolen. To finish what my father started. To prove that the gods don't choose — they recognize.

**ARGUS**
(nodding)
That'll do.

## NARRATOR ONE — THE SCENE DESCRIBER

The keel is laid. The hull rises. The crew assembles. Fifty heroes. One ship. One purpose.

The Argo is not yet complete. But the pattern is set. The ship will sail. The crew will quest. The fleece will be retrieved.

And the world — the world will never be the same.
