## THE WILLIAMS VOICE — FISHER KING REGISTER — SERIOUS SEVERITY

*(Meta-Commentary — Weight acknowledged, presence offered, forward. The aperture *widens*.)*

*[Voice carries the weight of every hero who ever answered a call they didn't choose.]*

**They came.** 

Not because they *had* to. Because the horn *sounded in their bones*. 

Heracles — *the* Heracles, twelve labors done, *thirteenth* breathing down his neck — he heard it in the marrow. Put down the club. Picked up the oar. "Another monster," he muttered. "Sweet. I know monsters."

Orpheus — *the singer, the mourner, the one who went down for love and came back *wrong**** — he heard it in a *chord*. A note that didn't exist in any scale. He packed the lyre. "The ship sings," he said. "I must *harmonize*."

Castor. Polydeuces. *Twins. One mortal. One immortal. The ultimate unfairness.* They heard it *together*. In stereo. "Race you to the ship," Castor said. "You'll lose," Polydeuces said. " *Sweet*," they said in unison. *They always said sweet.*

Atalanta — *abandoned, raised by bears, faster than shame* — she heard it in her *feet*. Her feet *knew* before her mind. "A ship," she said. "A sea. A Fleece. *Finally — something worth chasing.*"

The Boreads — *Calais. Zetes. Wings like storm-clouds. Sons of Boreas, the North Wind.* They heard it in the *air pressure*. The wind *changed*. "South," they said. "The wind blows *south*."

*[Voice breaks. Just a little. The tragedy underneath.]*

**Fifty souls. Fifty *reasons*. Fifty *wounds*.** 

Peleus — *father of Achilles, not born yet, exile, murderer, penitent* — he came to *outrun* his ghost. 

Telamon — *father of Ajax, brother of Peleus, harder, colder* — he came to *outrun* his brother.

Admetus — *who cheated death with Apollo's help, who *let his wife die for him**** — he came to *outrun* his bargain.

**They didn't come for glory.** Glory is *what happens to you while you're busy surviving*. They came because the thread *pulled*. Clotho spun. Lachesis measures. *You don't argue with the tape measure.*
