## THE WILLIAMS VOICE — AWAKENINGS REGISTER — GRIEF SEVERITY

*(Meta-Commentary — Still, present, seeing, silent. The cup of water.)*

*[Voice barely above a whisper. Each word placed like a stone on a cairn.]*

**I only knew that you were thirsty.**

That's the line. From *Awakenings*. The doctor. The patient. The cup of water. *That's all it ever is.*

Look at this feast. *Really look.*

Aeson — *father, king, exile, survivor* — he holds Jason's hand. *Both hands.* His thumbs trace the lines on Jason's palm. *Reading the map.* "The Anaurus runs cold this year," he says. *Code.* "The olive harvest was good." *Code.* "Chiron sends his regards." *Code for: I love you. I'm terrified. Go. Come back.*

Alcimede — *mother, priestess, the one who smuggled you, the one who *waited**** — she doesn't speak. She *pours*. Water. Wine. Honey. Milk. Oil. Each libation: a *layer of armor*. Invisible. *Real.* "Athena walks with you," she whispers. *Not a prayer. A *fact*.*

Chiron — *teacher, wound, wisdom, the centaur who cannot heal himself* — he stands *apart*. *Always apart.* His eyes — *ancient, kind, *knowing**** — they don't watch Jason. They watch *the spaces between* Jason and the crew. *The bonds.* "The ship remembers," he says to no one. "The prow *listens*. The oars *remember* the hands that hold them."

*[A long pause. The sound of a single tear hitting a wine cup. Amplified. Sacred.]*

**And the crew.** 

Heracles — *loud, boisterous, *terrified of losing another one**** — he laughs too loud. Drinks too fast. Claps shoulders *too hard*. "We'll have that Fleece before the solstice!" *Lie. He knows it's a lie. He tells it anyway. For them.*

Orpheus — *quiet, string-silent, singing *inside**** — he sits at the edge. Fingers moving on invisible strings. *Composing the voyage before it happens.* The song *is* the map.

Atalanta — *watching, assessing, *not impressed, *impressed despite herself**** — she eats with her fingers. Meat. Bread. Olives. *Fuel.* She catches Jason's eye. *Nods once.* *Respect. The only currency.*

**Fifty cups raised. Fifty throats swallowing. Fifty hearts *knowing*: some of us *won't come back*.**

*[Voice rises. Not in volume. In *truth*.]*

**This isn't a feast. This is a *funeral* for the people they *were* tonight. The people who *haven't left yet*. Tomorrow — *tomorrow they become the story*. Tonight — *tonight they're just... people. Eating. Drinking. Loved. Terrified. *Alive*.***

## THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE — THE BURRITO IS AVAILABLE — PHYSICAL GROUNDING

*(Meta-Commentary — Have you eaten? The wave comes when it comes. Everything follows something.)*

So. Feast at Pagasae. *Classic.* 

**Food first. Always food.** You don't sail the edge of the world on an empty stomach. *Dude.* 

The spread: Barley cakes. Olives — *black, green, brine-cured, oil-cured*. Cheese — *sheep, goat, aged in caves*. Figs — *fresh, dried, honey-drizzled*. Goat stew. *Slow-cooked since dawn*. Wine — *watered, *civilized*, three parts water to one part wine, *Homeric ratio*. Honey cakes for dessert. *Sweet.*

Jason sits between his parents. *Aeson. Alcimede.* Chiron's there — *centaur at a human table, but he *fits*, he *always fits****. 

**The vibe:** Not "last supper." *Not* dramatic. *Real.* 

Heracles tells a labor story — *the stables, the dung, the rivers, "you should've seen the look on Eurystheus's face"* — and everyone *laughs*. *Real laughter.* The kind that *loosens* something in your chest.

Orpheus plays a tune. *Quiet. Just for the table near him.* Aeson *closes his eyes*. Alcimede *smiles*. Chiron *nods* — *hooves tapping the rhythm.*

Atalanta challenges Polydeuces to an arm-wrestle. *On a barrel.* She *wins*. Polydeuces *laughs*. "Rematch. Tomorrow. On the ship." "You're on."

*[Long pause. Smell of stew. Smoke. Salt air.]*

**The omens.** 

Idmon — *seer, knows he dies, *tells them anyway**** — he reads the entrails. *Liver. Heart. Lungs.* "The path is *perilous*," he says. *Flat. Calm.* "The return is *certain* for some. *Uncertain* for others. *The Fleece *will* be taken." 

Mopsus — *bird seer, watches the sky* — "Eagles. Two. Flying east. *Good.* But... *a third shadow. No bird. Just... shadow.*"

Jason *listens*. Doesn't flinch. *Nods.* "Sweet. Thanks for the intel."

**Aeson stands.** *Frail. Tall in spirit.* Raises his cup. *Water. Not wine.* "To the ship. To the crew. *To the return*."

**Fifty cups. Fifty voices.** "To the return."

*And then?* 

**They eat the honey cakes. They sleep. They dream. The ship *waits*.**
