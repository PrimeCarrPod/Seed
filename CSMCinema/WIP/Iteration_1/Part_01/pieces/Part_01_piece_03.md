## THE EL SEGUNDO VOICE — SURFER'S PATIENCE — DUDE/SWEET ECONOMY

*(Meta-Commentary — The wave comes when it comes. Recognition and appreciation.)*

So Pelias pours the wine. *Sweet gesture, right?* "Nephew! Welcome! Let's talk legacy over a nice vintage." 

And Jason — *Jason* — he just... *sits there*. Doesn't reach for the cup immediately. Watches the pour. Watches Pelias. *Listens to the pour*. Wine has a sound. Viscosity. Age. This wine? *Old*. *Expensive*. *Patient*. Like Pelias. 

"Here's the deal, nephew." Pelias leans back. *Casual*. "Iolcus is yours. Always was. Your father's. Your grandfather's. *But* — and it's a sweet little *but* — a king needs *authority*. The people need a *sign*. The Golden Fleece. Phrixus's ram. Skin of gold. Hangs in Ares's grove in Colchis. Guarded by a dragon. *Never sleeps*. Bring it home? The crown is yours. *No questions asked*."

*[Long pause. The sound of ocean, implied. The rhythm of breath.]*

Jason doesn't flinch. Doesn't argue. Doesn't *negotiate*. He *nods*. Once. 

"Sweet," he says. "I'll need a ship. And a crew."

Pelias *beams*. "Consider it done. Argus — finest shipwright — builds you a vessel. Athena herself advises. You just... *go get it*."

*And then?* 

Jason stands. Still hasn't touched the wine. "Cool. When do we leave?"

"Spring. The winds favor."

"Sweet. I'll pack."

**And then he walks out.** 

*[A beat. The respect, palpable.]*

*That's* the move. Not the acceptance — the *refusal to be trapped by the acceptance*. He took the quest. He *didn't take the wine*. He didn't take the *bait*. He took the *wave*. And now? Now he paddles.
