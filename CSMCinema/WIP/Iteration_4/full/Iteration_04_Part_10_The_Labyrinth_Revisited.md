# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 10: THE LABYRINTH REVISITED
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 10 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE RETURN TO CRETE

EXT. CRETE - HARBOR - DAY

The Argo docks. Minos waits. Older. Sadder. But — hopeful.

MINOS
Jason. Medea. You've returned.

JASON
We have something for the labyrinth.

MINOS
(looking at the seed)
What is that?

MEDEA
(writing)
Memory. (betrayal) For the labyrinth. For Asterion. For— (searching) —for everyone who was forgotten there.

MINOS
(quietly)
Asterion asks about you. (betrayal) He's learning to weave. Did you know?

JASON
(surprised)
Weave?

MINOS
(smiling)
He says — if he'd had a loom instead of a cage— (stopping) —he wouldn't have become the Minotaur.

---

# PIECE 2: THE LABYRINTH

INT. LABYRINTH - DAY

The crew enters. The walls no longer shift. No longer confuse. They're — calm. Waiting.

ASTERION (30s, still bull-headed but — different. Calm. Peaceful. Wearing weaver's robes) sits at a small loom. Weaving.

He sees them. Smiles.

ASTERION
Jason. Medea. (betrayal) Friends.

JASON
Asterion. You're—

ASTERION
Weaving. (betrayal) Yes. (betrayal) It helps. The weaving. It— (searching) —it gives the pain somewhere to go.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's what weaving is. (betrayal) Pain transformed into pattern.

ASTERION
(nodding)
I understand now. (betrayal) Why you do it. Why you— (searching) —why you became the loom.

---

# PIECE 3: THE PLANTING

INT. LABYRINTH - CENTER - CONTINUOUS

Jason holds the seed. The labyrinth responds. The walls pulse. Welcoming.

JASON
This is for you. (betrayal) For the labyrinth. For— (searching) —for everyone who was forgotten here.

He plants the seed in the center of the labyrinth. Where Asterion was once caged.

The seed grows. Instantly. Roots spreading. Branches reaching. A tree. Small but — alive. Growing.

And on its bark — names. The names of everyone who was ever sacrificed to the Minotaur. Everyone who was forgotten in the dark.

ASTERION
(touching the tree)
I know these names. (betrayal) They were— (stopping) —they were with me. In the dark. I heard them. Screaming.

JASON
(gently)
They're not screaming anymore.

ASTERION
(crying)
No. (betrayal) They're— (searching) —they're remembered.

---

# PIECE 4: THE TREE OF REMEMBRANCE

INT. LABYRINTH - CENTER - CONTINUOUS

The tree grows taller. Its branches reach the ceiling. Its roots reach the foundations.

And the labyrinth — changes. The walls soften. The stone becomes — not stone. Something alive. Something growing.

MEDEA
(awed)
The labyrinth is becoming a garden.

MINOS
(entering, staring)
My gods. (betrayal) It's— (searching) —it's beautiful.

JASON
The labyrinth was always a loom. (betrayal) Now it's becoming what it was meant to be. (betrayal) A place of growth. Not a place of— (searching) —of death.

ATALANTA
(touching the walls)
The walls are warm.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
They're singing. (betrayal) A different song. Not confusion.— (searching) —welcome.

---

# PIECE 5: ASTERION'S CHOICE

INT. LABYRINTH - CENTER - CONTINUOUS

ASTERION
(to Jason)
I want to stay here. (betrayal) Tend the tree. Remember the names. (betrayal) Make sure— (searching) —make sure no one else is forgotten.

JASON
Are you sure?

ASTERION
(smiling)
Yes. (betrayal) This is my purpose. (betrayal) Not the Minotaur. Not the monster. (betrayal) This. (betrayal) Remembering. Tending.— (searching) —healing.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then we'll visit. (betrayal) Often.

ASTERION
(smiling)
I'd like that.

---

# PIECE 6: THE GIFT OF CRETE

EXT. KNOSSOS - HARBOR - DAY

The crew prepares to sail. Minos approaches with a package.

MINOS
Take this. (betrayal) For your journey.

He unwraps it. A tapestry. Woven. Beautiful. Showing the Argo. The crew. The quests.

MINOS (CONT'D)
Asterion made it. (betrayal) His first— (searching) —his first real weaving.

MEDEA
(awed)
It's beautiful.

MINOS
(smiling)
He says — every thread is a choice. (betrayal) Every pattern is a story. (betrayal) And every— (searching) —every story deserves to be remembered.

JASON
(taking it)
Thank you. (betrayal) For everything.

MINOS
(shaking his head)
No. Thank you. (betrayal) You gave me back my son.

---

# PIECE 7: THE SAILING

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. Crete behind them. The tapestry displayed on the mast.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Where to now?

JASON
(looking at the seven threads)
The pattern is— (searching) —quiet. (betrayal) For now.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then we rest. (betrayal) We've earned it.

KORE
(cheering)
Rest!

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Rest. (betrayal) And then— (searching) —and then we see.

ZETES
See what?

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
What the pattern shows us next.

---

# PIECE 8: THE QUIET

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The crew sleeps. Jason and Medea at the bow. The stars above.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's been a long journey.

JASON
(smiling)
It has.

MEDEA
(writing)
Do you regret it? (betrayal) Becoming the center?

JASON
(long pause)
Sometimes. (betrayal) When the weight is heavy. When the forgotten cry out. When— (searching) —when I can't help everyone.

MEDEA
(writing)
But?

JASON
(smiling)
But then I look at you. At the crew. At Kore. At— (searching) —at everything we've built. (betrayal) And I know. (betrayal) It's worth it.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Good answer.

---

# PIECE 9: THE DREAM

EXT. ARGO - BOW - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. Dreams.

The First Weaver appears. Smiling.

THE FIRST WEAVER
You've done well. (betrayal) Better than I ever did.

JASON
(in the dream)
I had help.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(smiling)
That's the secret I never learned. (betrayal) You can't hold the pattern alone. (betrayal) You need— (searching) —you need people. Love. Connection.

JASON
Is that why you disappeared?

THE FIRST WEAVER
(sadly)
Yes. (betrayal) I tried to hold it all myself. (betrayal) And it— (searching) —it broke me. (betrayal) But you— (smiling) —you're doing it differently. (betrayal) You're holding it together.

JASON
(smiling)
That's the idea.

---

# PIECE 10: THE MORNING

EXT. ARGO - DAWN

Jason wakes. Medea beside him. The crew stirring.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Smoke on the horizon. (betrayal) Land.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The pattern calls.

JASON
(standing)
Then we answer.

The crew gathers. The Argo turns toward the smoke. Toward the next quest. Toward—

MEDEA
(writing)
Where are we going?

JASON
(smiling)
I don't know. (betrayal) But we'll find out together.

---

# PIECE 11: THE PATTERN CONTINUES

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. The seven threads gleaming. The tapestry flying. The crew at peace.

KORE
Jason? (betrayal) Will there always be quests?

JASON
(smiling)
Yes.

KORE
(sighing)
Forever?

JASON
(smiling)
Maybe. (betrayal) But that's not a bad thing. (betrayal) Quests mean— (searching) —quests mean we're needed. (betrayal) Quests mean we matter.

MEDEA
(writing)
And quests mean— (searching) —quests mean we're alive.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Land ahead!

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The pattern continues!

JASON
(to the crew)
Together!

CREW
(together)
Always!

The Argo sails toward land. Toward the next quest. Toward the next choice. Toward—

The pattern continues.

END OF PART 10
