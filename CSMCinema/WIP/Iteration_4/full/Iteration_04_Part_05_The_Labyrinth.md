# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 05: THE LABYRINTH
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 05 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE ISLAND OF CRETE

EXT. CRETE - HARBOR - DAY

The Argo approaches. A great palace on the hill. Knossos.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The labyrinth. I can see it from here. The walls. The pattern.

MEDEA
(writing)
The labyrinth is a loom. Woven from stone instead of thread. (beat) And the Minotaur — is what happens when the pattern goes wrong.

JASON
What do you mean?

MEDEA
(writing)
The Minotaur was a boy. Born of a curse. Locked away. Forgotten. (beat) When the pattern forgets someone — they become a monster.

---

# PIECE 2: THE KING

INT. KNOSSOS - THRONE ROOM - DAY

MINOS (60s, king of Crete, burdened by his secret) receives them.

MINOS
Jason of Iolcus. You carry six threads. I can see them glowing.

JASON
We seek the labyrinth.

MINOS
(sharply)
No one seeks the labyrinth. People flee it.

MEDEA
(writing)
We seek to heal it.

MINOS
(laughing bitterly)
Heal it? The labyrinth can't be healed. It can only be endured.

JASON
Everything can be healed. If you understand the pattern.

MINOS
(looking at him)
You're either very wise or very foolish. (beat) My son is in there. The Minotaur. Half-man. Half-bull. All suffering. (beat) If you can heal him — I'll give you anything.

MEDEA
(writing)
We don't want anything. We want to understand.

---

# PIECE 3: THE ENTRANCE

EXT. LABYRINTH ENTRANCE - DAY

A great stone mouth. Carved. Ancient. Breathing.

The crew stands before it.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
The walls are singing. (beat) They're singing a song of confusion. Of being lost.

ATALANTA
I don't like this.

ZETES
None of us do.

CALAUS
But we're going anyway.

JASON
(to Medea)
Will you wait here?

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
You already know the answer.

JASON
Together.

MEDEA
(writing)
Always.

---

# PIECE 4: THE LABYRINTH

INT. LABYRINTH - CONTINUOUS

They enter. The walls shift. Not moving — but the pattern changes. Corridors that were straight become curved. Paths that were open close.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's alive. The labyrinth is alive.

JASON
How do we navigate it?

MEDEA
(writing)
The same way we navigate everything. We follow the pattern.

She traces the wall. The stone responds. Glowing faintly. A path appears.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(writing)
The labyrinth wants to be understood. Not conquered.

They follow the glowing path. Deeper. Deeper.

---

# PIECE 5: THE MINOTAUR

INT. LABYRINTH - CENTER - CONTINUOUS

A vast chamber. And in the center — the MINOTAUR (30s, bull-headed, man-bodied, eyes full of pain).

He sees them. Roars. Charges.

ATALANTA
(draws bow)

JASON
No! Wait!

The Minotaur stops. Confused. No one has ever waited before.

JASON (CONT'D)
(quietly)
You're not a monster. You're a boy. Locked away. Forgotten.

The Minotaur stares. Tears form in his bull-eyes.

JASON (CONT'D)
What's your name?

The Minotaur tries to speak. Can't. He's been silent too long.

MEDEA
(writing, showing him)
Your name. What is it?

The Minotaur takes the chalk. Writes in the dust:

ASTERION

JASON
Asterion. Star. (beat) You were named for the stars.

ASTERION
(nodding, tears falling)

JASON (CONT'D)
You don't have to be the Minotaur anymore. You can be Asterion. You can be free.

---

# PIECE 6: THE HEALING

INT. LABYRINTH - CENTER - CONTINUOUS

MEDEA
(writing)
The labyrinth was built to contain him. But it also — preserved him. (beat) The pattern kept him alive. Even when everyone forgot.

JASON
Can we free him?

MEDEA
(writing)
Not by breaking the walls. By changing the pattern. (beat) The labyrinth is a loom. And Asterion — is the thread that got tangled.

She approaches Asterion. Places her hand on his head.

The six threads glow. The labyrinth responds. The walls shift. The pattern changes.

ASTERION
(speaking for the first time, voice rough)
Thank you.

The walls open. A path. To the surface. To light.

---

# PIECE 7: THE SURFACE

EXT. LABYRINTH ENTRANCE - DAY

The crew emerges. Asterion behind them. Blinking in sunlight.

Minos waits. Sees his son. Falls to his knees.

MINOS
Asterion. My son. (beat) I'm sorry. I'm so sorry.

ASTERION
(helping him up)
Father. It's all right. (beat) I'm free now.

MINOS
(to Jason)
How can I repay you?

JASON
You can't. (beat) But you can remember. The pattern forgets no one. If we remember.

MINOS
(nodding)
I'll remember. (beat) All of you.

---

# PIECE 8: THE GIFT OF CRETE

EXT. KNOSSOS - HARBOR - DAY

The crew prepares to sail. Minos approaches with a small box.

MINOS
This was my wife's. She wove. Before the curse. Before the Minotaur. (beat) She would want you to have it.

He opens the box. A spindle. Old. Beautiful. Worn smooth by hands that loved.

MEDEA
(taking it, writing)
Thank you.

MINOS
What will you do now?

JASON
The seventh loom. The first loom. The one that wove the weavers.

MINOS
(afraid)
Be careful. (beat) The first loom doesn't give. It takes.

JASON
I know.

MINOS
And what it takes — you don't get back.

---

# PIECE 9: THE SEVENTH THREAD

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The Argo sails. Medea at the bow. The spindle in her hands.

MEDEA
(writing)
The seventh thread isn't given. It's earned. (beat) When we're ready. When we understand. When we're willing to become.

JASON
Become what?

MEDEA
(writing)
The center. The consciousness. The one who holds the pattern together.

JASON
And if I refuse?

MEDEA
(writing)
Then the pattern continues without a center. Beautiful. But unfinished.

JASON
(long pause)
I don't know if I'm ready.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
No one ever is.

---

# PIECE 10: THE DREAM

EXT. ARGO - BOW - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. Dreams.

A VOICE (not Hecate, not Gaia, not any god — the pattern itself):

THE PATTERN
You carry six threads. You seek the seventh.

JASON
(in the dream)
I'm afraid.

THE PATTERN
Good. Fear means you understand. (beat) The seventh thread will make you the center. The consciousness. The weaver of weavers.

JASON
And if I refuse?

THE PATTERN
Then someone else will be chosen. (beat) But it will be harder for them. Because they didn't walk your path. Didn't carry your threads. Didn't learn what you learned.

JASON
What did I learn?

THE PATTERN
That the pattern isn't fate. It's choice. (beat) And choice — is love.

---

# PIECE 11: THE MORNING

EXT. ARGO - DAWN

Jason wakes. Medea beside him.

MEDEA
(writing)
You were talking in your sleep.

JASON
What did I say?

MEDEA
(writing)
You said yes.

JASON
(smiling)
I did?

MEDEA
(writing)
To the pattern. To the seventh thread. To becoming.

JASON
(quietly)
I don't remember.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Your heart remembered. Even if your mind didn't.

The sun rises. The Argo sails. Six threads gleaming.

One more to go.

The pattern continues.

END OF PART 05
