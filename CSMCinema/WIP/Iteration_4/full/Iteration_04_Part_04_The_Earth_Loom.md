# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 04: THE EARTH LOOM
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 04 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE JOURNEY TO OLYMPUS

EXT. GREESE - MOUNT OLYMPUS - DAY

The Argo is beached at Thessaly. The crew travels on foot toward Olympus.

ATALANTA
We're going to Olympus? Home of the gods?

MEDEA
(writing)
The earth loom is beneath it. Where Gaia wove the world into being.

ZETES
Gaia. The first goddess.

CALAUS
Older than the Titans. Older than everything.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The earth remembers. Every step. Every life. Every death.

The mountain looms. Clouds crown it. Lightning flickers.

---

# PIECE 2: THE GUARDIAN

EXT. MOUNT OLYMPUS - BASE - DAY

A figure blocks the path. ARGUS (hundred eyes, giant, guardian of Gaia).

ARGUS
No one passes.

JASON
We seek the earth loom.

ARGUS
(laughing, all hundred eyes blinking)
Everyone seeks. None find.

MEDEA
(writing)
Argus. You served Hera. You were killed by Hermes. Yet here you are.

ARGUS
(sharply)
I serve the earth now. Not the gods. (beat) The earth remembers what the gods forget.

JASON
What do they forget?

ARGUS
That they were made. Not born. (beat) Gaia wove them. Like she wove everything. (beat) And what is woven — can be unwoven.

---

# PIECE 3: THE TEST OF ARGUS

EXT. MOUNT OLYMPUS - BASE - CONTINUOUS

ARGUS
I'll let you pass. If you answer truly. (beat) What is the earth?

ATALANTA
Dirt. Stone. Ground.

ORPHEUS
Home. Life. Growth.

MEDEA
(long pause, then writing)
Memory. (beat) The earth remembers everything. Every root. Every bone. Every—

ARGUS
(interrupting)
Wrong. All wrong. And all right. (beat) The earth is patience. (beat) The earth waits. For seeds. For rain. For us to return. (beat) The earth is patient. Are you?

JASON
(quietly)
We can learn.

ARGUS
(smiling with all hundred eyes)
Then pass. (beat) But be warned. Gaia doesn't speak. She shows. And what she shows — you can't unsee.

---

# PIECE 4: THE CAVE BENEATH OLYMPUS

INT. OLYMPUS - CAVERN - NIGHT

The crew descends. Into the mountain. Into the earth.

The walls pulse. Not stone. Something alive. Breathing.

MEDEA
(awed)
We're inside Gaia. Inside the living earth.

The cavern opens. Vast. Impossible. Roots the size of ships. And in the center — a loom. Not made of anything. Just — there. Always there. The EARTH LOOM.

And beside it — GAIA (ageless, neither young nor old, mother of everything, eyes that have seen it all).

GAIA
(without turning)
I wondered when you'd come.

JASON
You knew we were coming?

GAIA
(smiling)
I know everything that walks on me. (turning) You carry five threads. You want the sixth.

JASON
We want to understand.

GAIA
(sadly)
Understanding is overrated. (beat) But you'll learn that. In time.

---

# PIECE 5: THE STORY OF MAKING

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

GAIA
Sit. All of you. (beat) I'll tell you a story.

The crew sits.

GAIA (CONT'D)
Before the gods. Before time. Before anything — there was chaos. And in chaos — I woke. (beat) Not born. Not made. Just — awake. (beat) And I chose. The first choice. To weave order from chaos. (beat) I wove the earth. The sky. The sea. (beat) And then — I wove the gods. My children. My— (stopping) —my mistake.

JASON
Mistake?

GAIA
The gods forgot they were woven. They thought they were eternal. (beat) They fought. They conquered. They chained their own father. (beat) And they forgot — they can be unwoven.

MEDEA
(writing)
Can they?

GAIA
(looking at her)
You know the answer. You're a weaver.

MEDEA
(writing)
Yes. (beat) But I wanted to hear you say it.

GAIA
(smiling)
The gods can be unwoven. (beat) But it would take someone who understands. Someone who carries all the threads. Someone who— (looking at Jason) —is willing to become the loom.

---

# PIECE 6: THE TERRIBLE CHOICE

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

JASON
Become the loom?

GAIA
The seventh loom isn't a place. It's a person. (beat) Someone who carries all the threads. Who understands all the patterns. Who becomes — the weaver of weavers.

MEDEA
(afraid)
Jason. No.

GAIA
(softly)
Not yet. He's not ready. (beat) But he will be. After the seventh thread. After the final choice.

JASON
What choice?

GAIA
Whether to weave or unweave. (beat) Whether to remake the world. Or let it end. (beat) Whether to become the pattern. Or remain — human.

Silence.

ATALANTA
(standing)
This is too much. We came for understanding. Not— (gesturing) —apotheosis.

GAIA
(smiling)
The two are the same. (beat) Understanding the pattern means becoming it. (beat) Becoming it means losing yourself. (beat) The question is — what are you willing to lose?

---

# PIECE 7: THE GIFT

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

GAIA
(to Jason)
Give me your hand.

Jason extends his hand. Gaia touches it.

The cavern disappears. Jason sees:

The world as Gaia sees it. Every root. Every river. Every mountain. Every life. All connected. All woven. All — one pattern.

And in the pattern — a gap. A hole. Something missing.

GAIA (V.O.)
The pattern is incomplete. It needs — a center. A consciousness. Someone who holds it all together. (beat) That's what the seventh thread does. It makes you the center.

JASON
(in the vision)
And if I refuse?

GAIA (V.O.)
The pattern continues. Without a center. Incomplete. Beautiful. But — unfinished.

The vision ends. Jason is back in the cavern.

GAIA
(placing a thread in his hand)
Green. Memory of the earth. Every root. Every seed. Every return. (beat) When you're lost — hold the thread. The earth will hold you.

JASON
Six threads.

GAIA
Six looms. One to go.

---

# PIECE 8: THE WARNING

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

MEDEA
(writing)
The seventh loom. The first loom. The one that wove the weavers.

GAIA
(serious)
It's not a place. Not a thing. (beat) It's a moment. (beat) The moment when the first choice was made. When chaos became order. When nothing became something.

JASON
How do we reach it?

GAIA
You don't reach it. It reaches you. (beat) When you carry all seven threads. When you understand all the patterns. When you're ready to become — or refuse. (beat) That's when the seventh loom appears.

MEDEA
(writing)
And if we refuse?

GAIA
(sadly)
Then the pattern continues without a center. The gods continue. The world continues. (beat) But something beautiful — will be lost.

---

# PIECE 9: THE ASCENT

EXT. MOUNT OLYMPUS - NIGHT

The crew ascends. Silent. Changed.

Six threads around Jason's wrist. Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black. Green.

ATALANTA
(quietly, to Orpheus)
Are you afraid?

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Terrified. (beat) But the music continues. So I continue.

ZETES
(to Calais)
Whatever happens—

CALAUS
(finishing)
We face it together.

MEDEA
(writing to Jason)
Are you okay?

JASON
(lying)
Yes.

MEDEA
(writing)
Liar.

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. I'm terrified. (beat) But I'm still here.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's all the gods ever were. (beat) Terrified — but still here.

---

# PIECE 10: THE RETURN

EXT. THESSALY - BEACH - DAWN

The Argo awaits. The crew boards.

Jason stands at the bow. Six threads gleaming.

MEDEA
(writing)
The seventh loom will come when we're ready.

JASON
How will we know?

MEDEA
(writing)
We won't. (beat) That's what makes it faith.

JASON
I gave up certainty. Remember?

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Then you're already ready.

---

# PIECE 11: THE PATTERN COMPLETES

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. The crew at peace. Changed. Ready.

ORPHEUS
(playing a new melody)
The Music of the Looms. (beat) Sea. Sky. Underworld. Earth. World. (beat) And soon — the Seventh.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Land ahead. Where?

MEDEA
(writing)
Crete. (beat) Where the Minotaur waits. Where the labyrinth begins.

JASON
The Minotaur?

MEDEA
(writing)
Another pattern. Another thread. (beat) The labyrinth is a loom. And the Minotaur — is what happens when the pattern goes wrong.

JASON
Then we fix it.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Yes. We do.

The Argo sails toward Crete. Toward the labyrinth. Toward the seventh loom.

The pattern continues.

END OF PART 04
