# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 08: THE FAR NORTH
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 08 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE JOURNEY NORTH

EXT. NORTHERN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails north. Ice floats in the water. Snow falls.

The crew bundled. Cold. But determined.

ATALANTA
(from above)
I can't feel my fingers.

ORPHEUS
(playing, teeth chattering)
I can't feel my strings.

MEDEA
(writing, breath visible)
Hyperborea ahead. The land where the sun never fully sets. Where the Hyperboreans live.

JASON
The child. The forgotten thread. (beat) How do we find them?

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern will guide. (beat) It always does.

---

# PIECE 2: THE HYPERBOREANS RETURN

EXT. HYPERBOREA - COAST - DAY

The same ice. The same snow. But this time — they're expected.

The three HYPERBOREANS wait at the shore.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Jason. Medea. You've returned.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
And you carry seven threads now.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
You're the center.

JASON
We seek a child. Forgotten by the pattern. Becoming something cold.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(sadly)
Yes. (beat) The child is in the deep ice. Where we wove the first winter. (beat) She's been forgotten for so long — she's becoming winter itself.

---

# PIECE 3: THE DEEP ICE

EXT. HYPERBOREA - ICE CAVERN - DAY

The crew descends. Deeper than before. Colder than before.

The ice glows. Blue. Ancient. Alive.

And in the center — a GIRL (10, frozen, ice forming on her skin, eyes open but unseeing).

MEDEA
(awed)
She's beautiful.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
She was. Before she was forgotten.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
The pattern forgot her. And when the pattern forgets — you become what surrounds you. (beat) She's becoming ice.

JASON
Can we reach her?

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Only the center can. (beat) Only you.

---

# PIECE 4: THE FORGOTTEN CHILD

INT. ICE CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

Jason approaches. The seven threads glow. Warm. Alive.

The ice resists. Cracking. Groaning. But — slowly — it lets him through.

He reaches the girl. Touches her hand.

She's cold. So cold. But — there's something. A heartbeat. Faint. Struggling.

JASON
(quietly)
I see you. (beat) The pattern sees you. (betrayal) I see you.

The girl's eyes focus. Just slightly.

GIRL
(voice like cracking ice)
Who...?

JASON
Jason. (beat) I'm here to bring you back.

GIRL
Back... where?

JASON
To the pattern. To the world. To— (smiling) —to people who will remember you.

---

# PIECE 5: THE MEMORY

INT. ICE CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

MEDEA
(writing)
Jason. The ice is closing. You have to hurry.

JASON
(to the girl)
What's your name?

GIRL
(long pause, remembering)
Kore. (beat) My name is Kore.

JASON
Kore. Beautiful name. (beat) Do you remember your mother?

KORE
(crying, ice melting)
Yes. She... she wove. She wove blankets. For me. To keep me warm. (beat) But then— (stopping) —then she forgot. Everyone forgot.

JASON
I won't forget. (beat) The pattern won't forget. (beat) You're remembered now.

He wraps his cloak around her. The seven threads glow brighter. The ice melts.

Kore breathes. Really breathes. Warm breath. Human breath.

KORE
(whispering)
Thank you.

---

# PIECE 6: THE HEALING

INT. ICE CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

Medea approaches. Places her hand on Kore's head.

The threads weave. Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black. Green. And the seventh — all colors.

The pattern recognizes Kore. Reclaims her. Remembers her.

The ice melts faster. The cavern warms. Kore's skin — no longer frozen — becomes warm. Alive.

KORE
(looking at her hands)
I'm... me again.

JASON
You are.

KORE
(looking at him)
You're the center. (beat) I can feel it. You hold everything.

JASON
(smiling)
Not alone. (beat) I have help.

He looks at Medea. At the crew. At the Hyperboreans.

JASON (CONT'D)
We all hold it. Together.

---

# PIECE 7: THE HYPERBOREANS' GIFT

EXT. HYPERBOREA - SHORE - DAY

Kore stands on the shore. Warm. Alive. The crew around her.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
The pattern is stronger now. (beat) Because she's remembered.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
Every thread matters. (beat) Every person. Every— (searching) —every child.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
(to Jason)
You've done well, center. (betrayal) But this is just one thread. There are more. Many more. Forgotten. Becoming. (beat) The pattern needs you.

JASON
I know. (beat) Where next?

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(smiling)
You'll know. (beat) When the time comes. (beat) The pattern will tell you.

---

# PIECE 8: KORE'S CHOICE

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAY

Kore stands on the ship. Looking back at Hyperborea. At the ice.

KORE
I don't want to go back there.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then don't. (beat) Come with us.

KORE
(looking at Medea)
With you?

MEDEA
(writing)
We'll find your mother. (beat) We'll help you remember. (beat) And you'll help us. (beat) Every thread matters. Even small ones.

KORE
(smiling through tears)
Especially small ones.

JASON
(to the crew)
One more? (beat) Can we fit one more?

ATALANTA
(from above)
We'll make room.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The music's better with more voices anyway.

---

# PIECE 9: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. HYPERBOREA - SHORE - DAY

The Hyperboreans watch the Argo sail.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(to the others)
He's different than before.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
More confident. (beat) More... center-like.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
(smiling)
He's learning. (beat) The center isn't about power. It's about — connection.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
The pattern chose well.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
The pattern always does. (beat) Eventually.

---

# PIECE 10: THE SAILING

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails south. The ice behind them. The warmth ahead.

Kore stands at the bow. Medea beside her. Jason behind.

KORE
(quietly)
What's it like? Being the center?

JASON
(thinking)
Lonely. Beautiful. (betrayal) Overwhelming. (beat) But worth it.

KORE
Does it hurt?

JASON
Sometimes. (betrayal) But the hurt means — you care. (beat) And caring is the point.

MEDEA
(writing)
He's right. (betrayal) The pattern isn't about power. It's about — love.

KORE
(smiling)
I can feel it. (beat) The pattern. It's... warm.

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. (beat) It is.

---

# PIECE 11: THE NEXT THREAD

EXT. ARGO - NIGHT

Jason stands at the bow. The seven threads gleaming. Kore asleep below. Medea beside him.

MEDEA
(writing)
How many more?

JASON
I don't know. (betrayal) The pattern doesn't tell you everything. Just — enough. (betrayal) Just what you need to know. When you need to know it.

MEDEA
(writing)
That must be frustrating.

JASON
(laughing softly)
Sometimes. (betrayal) But it's also — freeing. (betrayal) I don't have to know everything. Just — the next step.

MEDEA
(writing)
And the next step is?

JASON
(looking at the stars)
Rest. (betrayal) We just got back. We just healed Kore. We just— (searching) —we just did something good. (betrayal) Let's enjoy it.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
For how long?

JASON
(smiling back)
Until the pattern says otherwise.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then let's make it count.

They hold each other. The stars above. The pattern holding. The world continuing.

The Argo sails on.

The pattern continues.

END OF PART 08
