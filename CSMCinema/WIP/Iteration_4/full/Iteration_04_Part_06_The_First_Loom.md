# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 06: THE FIRST LOOM
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 06 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE EDGE OF EVERYTHING

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. But the sea is changing. Becoming transparent. Below — nothing. Not water. Not depth. Just — void.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The sea is disappearing.

ORPHEUS
(stopping his playing)
We're reaching the edge. Where the pattern ends.

MEDEA
(writing)
Where it began. (beat) The first loom is at the edge of everything. Where chaos became order. Where nothing became something.

JASON
How do we reach it?

MEDEA
(writing)
We don't. It reaches us.

The six threads begin to glow. Brighter. Brighter. Blinding.

The world WHITES OUT.

---

# PIECE 2: THE VOID

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

White. Infinite. Empty. Not dark — empty. The absence of everything.

The crew stands on nothing. Floats in nothing. Is nothing.

And then — a sound. Not sound. The memory of sound. The possibility of sound.

A figure forms. Not a person. Not a god. Just — presence.

THE FIRST WEAVER (ageless, neither male nor female, the one who made the first choice):

THE FIRST WEAVER
You came.

JASON
Who are you?

THE FIRST WEAVER
I am the one who chose. Before the gods. Before time. Before everything. (beat) I am the first pattern. The first choice. The first— (smiling) —mistake.

MEDEA
(writing)
Mistake?

THE FIRST WEAVER
I chose to weave order from chaos. And in doing so — I created suffering. Because order requires boundaries. And boundaries require exclusion. And exclusion — is pain.

---

# PIECE 3: THE FIRST CHOICE

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

THE FIRST WEAVER
Before me — there was only chaos. Beautiful. Terrible. Free. (beat) And I chose to change it. To weave it. To make it — something. (beat) And that choice — created everything. The gods. The world. You.

JASON
Why?

THE FIRST WEAVER
Because I was lonely. (beat) Chaos is free — but it's alone. I wanted — connection. Relationship. Pattern. (beat) So I wove. And the weaving created — everything.

MEDEA
(writing)
And the suffering?

THE FIRST WEAVER
(sadly)
The cost. (beat) Every thread I wove — excluded every other thread. Every choice I made — eliminated every other choice. (beat) Order is beautiful. But it's built on the graves of all the possibilities I didn't choose.

---

# PIECE 4: THE QUESTION

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

THE FIRST WEAVER
(to Jason)
You carry six threads. You seek the seventh. (beat) But the seventh thread isn't like the others. It doesn't give you power. It gives you — responsibility.

JASON
Responsibility for what?

THE FIRST WEAVER
For the pattern itself. (beat) The seventh thread makes you the center. The consciousness. The one who holds it all together. (beat) You become — me.

MEDEA
(afraid, writing)
Jason. No.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(looking at Medea)
You understand. You've carried a loom. You know what it costs. (beat) To hold the pattern — you must give up yourself. Your wants. Your fears. Your— (looking at Medea) —your love.

JASON
(long pause)
If I become the center — do I lose her?

THE FIRST WEAVER
(honestly)
Yes. (beat) The center can't love. The center can only — hold.

---

# PIECE 5: THE CHOICE

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

Silence. The crew watches. Medea watches. Jason thinks.

JASON
(quietly)
If I refuse?

THE FIRST WEAVER
The pattern continues. Without a center. Beautiful. Unfinished. (beat) The gods continue. The world continues. But — something will be lost. Something only a center can provide.

JASON
What?

THE FIRST WEAVER
Coherence. (beat) Without a center — the pattern frays. Slowly. Over eons. But inevitably. (beat) The gods will war. The world will fracture. Chaos will return.

MEDEA
(writing)
And if he accepts?

THE FIRST WEAVER
The pattern holds. The world continues. The gods are bound. (beat) But Jason — the man — dies. And the center — the consciousness — remains.

---

# PIECE 6: THE CREW SPEAKS

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

ATALANTA
(standing)
Then we refuse. (beat) We didn't come here to lose you.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The music doesn't need a center. It needs — players.

ZETES
(standing)
We choose Jason. Not the center.

CALAUS
(standing)
The man. Not the pattern.

MEDEA
(writing, tears falling)
I can't ask you to do this. (beat) I can't ask you to give up everything — for the world.

JASON
(quietly)
You're not asking. (beat) I'm choosing.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then choose yourself. Choose us. Choose—

JASON
(gently)
I choose both. (beat) I choose to become the center. But I choose to remember. (beat) I choose to hold the pattern — but I choose to hold you too.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(sharply)
That's not possible. The center can't—

JASON
(interrupting)
Watch me.

---

# PIECE 7: THE NEW PATTERN

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

JASON
You said the first choice was loneliness. That you wove because you were alone. (beat) But you were wrong. (beat) The first choice wasn't loneliness. It was love. (beat) You wove because you wanted to share. To connect. To create something beautiful that others could see.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(quietly)
Yes.

JASON
Then the center doesn't have to be alone. (beat) The center can love. The center can hold — and still feel. (beat) The center can be — human.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(awed)
No one ever— (stopping) —no one ever tried.

JASON
(smiling)
Then it's time someone did.

He reaches for the seventh thread. Not given. Taken.

The thread is — everything. All colors. No colors. The thread of threads.

He wraps it around his wrist. With the others.

Seven threads.

---

# PIECE 8: THE TRANSFORMATION

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

Light. Blinding. The threads weave together. Into Jason. Through Jason. Around Jason.

He screams. Not in pain. In — becoming.

He sees everything. Every thread. Every pattern. Every choice. Every life. All at once. All simultaneous. All—

And then — he chooses. To hold it. To be it. To become — the center.

But he also chooses — to remember. To feel. To love.

The light fades.

Jason stands. Changed. The same. Both.

JASON
(quietly)
I am the pattern. (beat) And I am Jason. (beat) Both. Always both.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(smiling)
You did it. (beat) You became the center — and remained yourself. (beat) In all the eons — no one has done that.

MEDEA
(running to him)
Jason?

JASON
(holding her)
I'm here. (beat) I'm still here. (beat) I'll always be here.

---

# PIECE 9: THE GIFT

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

THE FIRST WEAVER
The seventh thread is yours. (beat) Not as a gift. As a choice. (beat) You chose to hold the pattern. You chose to remain yourself. (beat) That's the secret I never learned.

JASON
What secret?

THE FIRST WEAVER
That holding and feeling aren't opposites. (beat) That the center can love. (beat) That the pattern — is love.

The First Weaver begins to fade.

JASON
Where are you going?

THE FIRST WEAVER
(smiling)
I'm not going. I'm — returning. (beat) I was the first choice. And now — you're the latest. (beat) The pattern continues. But differently now. (beat) Better.

THE FIRST WEAVER (CONT'D)
Goodbye, Jason. (beat) Goodbye, Medea. (beat) Goodbye, all of you. (beat) The pattern is in good hands.

The First Weaver fades. The void fades. The world returns.

---

# PIECE 10: THE RETURN

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo floats on blue water. Real water. The sky above. Real sky.

The crew gasps. Breathes. Lives.

Jason stands at the bow. Seven threads around his wrist. Gleaming. Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black. Green. And the seventh — all colors. No colors.

MEDEA
(writing)
You did it.

JASON
(smiling)
We did it.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Land. Greece. Home.

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)
Home.

The Argo sails. Toward Greece. Toward Iolcus. Toward home.

But Jason is different now. He carries the pattern. He is the pattern. And he is — still himself.

---

# PIECE 11: THE PATTERN HOLDS

EXT. OPEN SEA - SUNSET

Jason and Medea at the bow. The seven threads gleaming.

MEDEA
(writing)
What happens now?

JASON
Now we go home. (beat) We face Pelias. We face the future. We face — everything.

MEDEA
(writing)
Are you afraid?

JASON
(quietly)
Yes. (beat) But I'm also — ready. (beat) The pattern isn't fate. It's choice. (beat) And I choose — this. Us. Everything.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Then let's choose together.

JASON
Together.

The sun sets. The stars appear. Each one a thread. Each one a choice. Each one — held by the center.

The Argo sails home.

The pattern holds.

END OF PART 06
