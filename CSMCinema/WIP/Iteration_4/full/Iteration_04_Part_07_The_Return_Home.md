# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 07: THE RETURN HOME
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 07 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE APPROACH

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo approaches. The harbor is full. The entire city gathered.

ATALANTA
(from above)
They're waiting for us.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
They know. Somehow — they know.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern told them. (beat) When you became the center — everyone felt it.

JASON
(quietly)
What did they feel?

MEDEA
(writing)
That something changed. That the world is different now. That — you're different.

The Argo docks. The crowd is silent. Afraid. Awed.

Jason steps onto the dock. The seven threads visible. Glowing.

The crowd kneels.

JASON
(sharply)
Don't kneel. (beat) I'm not a god. I'm not a king. I'm— (stopping) —I'm just Jason.

PELIAS
(from the crowd, standing)
No. You're not.

---

# PIECE 2: THE KING'S SURRENDER

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - CONTINUOUS

Pelias pushes through the crowd. Older. Grayer. Broken.

PELIAS
You carry the pattern. I can see it. Feel it. (beat) You're the center now.

JASON
I am.

PELIAS
(kneeling)
Then I surrender. (beat) The throne is yours. Freely. (beat) I don't want to fight the pattern.

JASON
(helping him up)
I don't want the throne.

PELIAS
(confused)
You don't?

JASON
I have something bigger. (beat) But I need your help.

PELIAS
My help? (laughing bitterly) What could I possibly—

JASON
Wisdom. (beat) You ruled for twenty years. You know the people. The land. The— (searching) —the practical things. I know patterns. You know people. (beat) Together—

PELIAS
(together)
We could rule well.

JASON
(smiling)
Not rule. Serve.

---

# PIECE 3: THE PEOPLE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

Jason stands before the people. No throne. No crown. Just — himself.

JASON
I'm not your king. (beat) I'm your neighbor. Your friend. Your— (searching) —your fellow citizen. (beat) I carry the pattern now. But that doesn't make me better than you. It makes me — responsible. (beat) Responsible for holding things together. For making sure the pattern doesn't fray. For making sure — no one gets forgotten.

The crowd murmurs.

JASON (CONT'D)
Pelias will advise. The crew will protect. And I— (beat) I'll listen. To all of you. To your needs. Your fears. Your hopes. (beat) Because the pattern isn't just threads. It's people. (beat) And people matter more than patterns.

The crowd cheers. Not for a king. For — one of them.

---

# PIECE 4: THE TEMPLE

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. The seven threads gleaming.

MEDEA
(writing)
You did well today.

JASON
I was terrified.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
That's how you know it was right.

JASON
(quietly)
I can feel them. All of them. Every person in Iolcus. Their hopes. Their fears. Their— (stopping) —their love. It's overwhelming.

MEDEA
(writing)
You don't have to carry it alone.

JASON
I'm the center. I have to—

MEDEA
(writing, firmly)
No. (beat) The center holds. But the center isn't alone. (beat) You have me. You have the crew. You have— (smiling) —you have people who love you.

JASON
(taking her hand)
I love you.

MEDEA
(writing)
I know. (beat) I've known since the beginning. Since you told me not to help you. Since you chose mercy over revenge. Since— (stopping) —since you became the center and still chose to feel.

---

# PIECE 5: THE WEDDING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

A simple wedding. No gold. No grandeur. Just — love.

The crew gathered. The people of Iolcus. Pelias, smiling. Orpheus playing.

Jason and Medea before the altar. Hecate's priestess officiating.

PRIESTESS
Jason of Iolcus. Do you take this woman? Not as property. Not as prize. But as partner. As equal. As— (smiling) —as the other half of your pattern?

JASON
I do.

PRIESTESS
Medea of Colchis. Do you take this man? Not as hero. Not as center. But as partner. As equal. As— (smiling) —as the other half of your pattern?

MEDEA
(nods, then speaks — her voice returned)
I do.

Gasps. Medea speaks.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(smiling)
The pattern gave it back. (beat) When I chose love over power. When I chose connection over isolation. When I chose— (looking at Jason) —him.

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)
The pattern provides.

They kiss. The crowd cheers.

---

# PIECE 6: THE CELEBRATION

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - NIGHT

A feast. Simple. Joyful. The crew dancing. The people laughing.

ATALANTA
(dancing with Orpheus)
I never thought I'd see the day.

ORPHEUS
(playing and dancing)
What day?

ATALANTA
You. Dancing. (beat) You always said music was your dance.

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
I learned from the best.

ZETES and CALAUS arm-wrestling. The crew betting.

PELIAS
(to Jason)
You've built something here.

JASON
We've built something.

PELIAS
(shaking his head)
No. You. (beat) I ruled through fear. You rule through— (searching) —love. (beat) It's better.

JASON
I'm not ruling.

PELIAS
(smiling)
Exactly.

---

# PIECE 7: THE WARNING

EXT. IOLCUS - ROOFTOP - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. The stars above. The seven threads gleaming.

MEDEA
(quietly)
It's not over.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
The pattern is stable. But— (searching) —there are still threads that need mending. Still patterns that need healing. Still—

JASON
Still quests.

MEDEA
(smiling)
Always quests.

JASON
Where to next?

MEDEA
(looking at the stars)
The pattern will tell us. (beat) But not tonight.

JASON
What happens tonight?

MEDEA
(smiling)
Tonight — we rest. We love. We— (searching) —we live.

JASON
And tomorrow?

MEDEA
Tomorrow — the pattern continues.

---

# PIECE 8: THE PATTERN SPEAKS

EXT. IOLCUS - ROOFTOP - LATER

Jason sleeps. Dreams. The pattern speaks:

THE PATTERN
You did well, Jason. (beat) You became the center — and remained yourself. (beat) You held the pattern — and still loved. (beat) You proved — the center can feel.

JASON
(in the dream)
What now?

THE PATTERN
Now — you live. (beat) You love. You choose. (beat) And when the pattern needs you — you answer.

JASON
Will it always need me?

THE PATTERN
(smiling)
Always. (beat) But not always urgently. (beat) Rest now. You've earned it.

JASON
And the quests?

THE PATTERN
Will wait. (beat) They always do.

---

# PIECE 9: THE MORNING

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAWN

Jason wakes. Medea beside him. Sunlight through the window.

MEDEA
(whispering)
Good morning, center of the world.

JASON
(whispering back)
Good morning, my love.

MEDEA
(smiling)
How do you feel?

JASON
(thinking)
Different. The same. (beat) I carry the pattern. But I also carry— (taking her hand) —this. Us. Everything.

MEDEA
(smiling)
Both. Always both.

They rise. The city wakes. The pattern holds.

---

# PIECE 10: THE VISITOR

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

A stranger arrives. Old. Weathered. Carrying a staff.

STRANGER
Jason of Iolcus. I bring a message.

JASON
From whom?

STRANGER
From the pattern. (beat) There is a thread that needs mending. In the far north. Beyond the world you know.

JASON
(quietly)
Already?

STRANGER
(smiling)
The pattern is patient. But not infinitely. (beat) You have time. A month. Maybe two. (beat) But then — you must go.

JASON
Where?

STRANGER
Hyperborea. (beat) The land you visited before. But deeper. Older. (beat) The first winter. Where cold was born.

JASON
And the thread?

STRANGER
(sadly)
A child. Forgotten by the pattern. Becoming— (searching) —something else. Something cold. Something— (stopping) —something that needs to be remembered.

---

# PIECE 11: THE CHOICE

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - NIGHT

Jason tells Medea.

MEDEA
(quietly)
A month.

JASON
A month. Maybe two.

MEDEA
Then we use it well. (beat) We live. We love. We— (smiling) —we prepare.

JASON
You're coming?

MEDEA
(smiling)
Always. (beat) Where you go — I go. (beat) That's what love means.

JASON
The pattern—

MEDEA
(firmly)
The pattern can wait. (beat) This— (gesturing between them) —can't.

JASON
(smiling)
Then let's not waste it.

They hold each other. The seven threads gleaming. The pattern holding. The love — stronger.

Outside — the city sleeps. The stars turn. The world continues.

The pattern holds.

END OF PART 07
