# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 02: THE SKY LOOM
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 02 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE PILLARS OF HERACLES

EXT. PILLARS OF HERACLES - DAY

Two massive pillars. Stone. Ancient. Marking the edge of the known world. Beyond them — the Atlantic. The unknown.

The Argo approaches.

ATALANTA
(from above)
No one goes beyond the pillars.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Everyone says that. Then someone does.

MEDEA
(writing in the salt on deck)
The sky loom is beyond the pillars. Above the ocean. Where the stars are closest.

JASON
How do we reach a loom in the sky?

MEDEA
The same way we reached the loom beneath the sea. We ask.

---

# PIECE 2: THE SKEPTIC

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAY

The crew gathers. Jason stands before them.

JASON
The sky loom is beyond the pillars. In the realm of Zeus. (beat) Poseidon warned us. Zeus doesn't share power.

ZETES
Then why go?

JASON
Because the pattern demands it.

CALAIS
The pattern. Always the pattern.

JASON
(hearing the doubt)
You want to say something?

CALAIS
(standing)
Yes. We've followed you across the sea. Through the Symplegades. To Colchis. Under the ocean. (beat) And every time — it's "the pattern demands it." When do we get to choose?

Silence.

JASON
(quietly)
You're right.

CALAIS
(surprised)
I am?

JASON
The pattern doesn't demand. It suggests. And I — I've been using it as an excuse. (beat) So here's the truth. We don't have to go. We can turn back. Go home. Live.

ATALANTA
(quietly)
Do you want to turn back?

JASON
(long pause)
No. (beat) But I want you to choose. Not follow. Choose.

---

# PIECE 3: THE CHOICE

EXT. ARGO - DECK - CONTINUOUS

Silence. The crew looks at each other.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
I choose to go. (beat) The music led me here. I want to see where it leads.

ZETES
(standing)
We choose to go. (gesturing to Calais) Both of us.

CALAIS
(sighing, then smiling)
Both of us.

ATALANTA
(from above)
I choose to go. (beat) I haven't finished hunting.

PERICLYMENUS
I choose to go. The story isn't over.

MEDEAELA
I choose to go. (beat) Someone has to keep you alive.

One by one — the crew stands. Each choosing. Not following. Choosing.

JASON
(quietly)
Thank you.

ATALANTA
Don't thank us. Just — lead. Really lead. Not hide behind the pattern.

JASON
(nodding)
I will.

---

# PIECE 4: BEYOND THE PILLARS

EXT. ATLANTIC OCEAN - DAY

The Argo passes between the pillars. The water changes. Darker. Deeper. More alive.

The sky changes too. Closer. The stars visible even in daylight. Faint. Waiting.

MEDEA
(writing)
We're in the space between. Between the known and the unknown. Between earth and sky.

JASON
Is it dangerous?

MEDEA
(smiling)
Everything worth doing is.

The water begins to glow. Not from below. From above. The stars reflecting. Or — the stars reaching down.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The stars are moving.

ORPHEUS
(stopping his playing)
No. We're moving. Up.

---

# PIECE 5: THE ASCENT

EXT. ATLANTIC OCEAN - CONTINUOUS

The water rises. Not a wave. A column. The Argo on top of it. Rising. Slowly. Steadily.

The crew grabs the rails.

ZETES
What's happening?

MEDEA
(awed)
The sea is giving us to the sky.

CALAIS
That's not how water works.

MEDEA
(smiling)
It is here.

The column rises higher. The ocean shrinks below. The sky grows closer. The stars brighten.

ATALANTA
(from above, laughing)
I can touch them!

She reaches. Her fingers brush a star. It chimes. Like glass. Like music.

ORPHEUS
(whispering)
The stars are threads.

---

# PIECE 6: THE SKY

EXT. THE SKY - NIGHT

The Argo floats. Not on water. On sky. Solid sky. Translucent. Stars visible through it.

And above — the constellations. Close. Immediate. Real.

The crew disembarks. Walks on sky. Each step ripples. Like water. Like light.

JASON
(quietly)
This is where the gods were made.

MEDEA
(writing)
Not made. Patterned. (beat) The gods chose to become. Here. At this loom.

In the distance — a structure. Vast. Impossible. A loom made of light and darkness. Star and void. The SKY LOOM.

And before it — a figure. Thunder in human form.

ZEUS.

---

# PIECE 7: THE KING OF GODS

EXT. SKY LOOM - CONTINUOUS

ZEUS (60s, beard of storm clouds, eyes like lightning, carries no weapon — he is the weapon) turns.

ZEUS
Jason of Iolcus. (beat) You've refused Athena. Walked through Poseidon's wave. Touched the sea loom. And now — you come to mine.

JASON
We come in peace.

ZEUS
(laughing, thunder rolling)
Peace? You come to my loom — the place where I wove the stars — and speak of peace? (beat) Bold. Stupid. But bold.

MEDEA
(stepping forward)
We come to understand. Not to take.

ZEUS
(looking at her)
The priestess who became the loom. (beat) I know what you are. What you carry. (beat) You carry my brother's gift. And my brother's gift. And now you want mine.

JASON
We want to see. To understand. To—

ZEUS
(interrupting)
To become. (beat) That's what you really want. You want to understand the pattern so you can become it.

JASON
(quietly)
Yes.

ZEUS
(smiling)
At least you're honest. (beat) Very well. But my test is harder than Poseidon's. Harder than the sea.

---

# PIECE 8: THE TEST OF ZEUS

EXT. SKY LOOM - CONTINUOUS

Zeus gestures. The sky shifts. The crew separated. Each alone. Each facing something different.

ATALANTA faces herself. Older. Slower. Unable to hunt.

ORPHEUS faces silence. Absolute. Permanent. No music. No sound. Nothing.

ZETES faces stillness. The inability to fly. Wings clipped.

CALAIS faces solitude. No brother. No partner. Alone.

MEDEA faces Jason. Leaving. Walking away. Choosing something else.

JASON faces himself. Successful. Powerful. King of everything. And completely alone.

ZEUS
(voice from everywhere)
This is the test. Not strength. Not courage. Truth. (beat) Face what you fear most. Accept it. And you pass.

---

# PIECE 9: THE FEAR

EXT. SKY LOOM - TIMELESS

Jason faces himself. The successful version. The king. The loneliest man in the world.

KING JASON
You see? I won. Everything I wanted. The throne. The power. The glory.

JASON
At what cost?

KING JASON
Does it matter? I won.

JASON
Look at you. You're alone. No crew. No Medea. No friends. Just — subjects. (beat) That's not winning. That's losing everything that matters.

KING JASON
(angry)
You don't understand. You're still young. Still hopeful. Still—

JASON
Still choosing. Every day. To be with people I love. Not above them. With them.

The king fades.

ZEUS
(appearing)
You passed.

JASON
That's it?

ZEUS
(sharply)
You faced your deepest fear. That power corrupts. That success isolates. And you chose love over power. (beat) That's the hardest choice. Most fail.

---

# PIECE 10: THE GIFTS

EXT. SKY LOOM - NIGHT

The crew reunites. Each changed. Each whole.

ZEUS
(to the crew)
You all passed. (beat) Each of you faced your fear. Each of you chose love over power. Connection over isolation. (beat) You're worthy.

He gestures to the loom. A single thread detaches. White. Glowing. Warm.

ZEUS (CONT'D)
(to Jason)
Memory of the sky. Every star. Every constellation. Every choice made in light. (beat) When you're lost in darkness — hold the thread. The stars will guide you.

JASON
(taking it)
Four threads.

ZEUS
Four looms. Three to go. (beat) The underworld. The earth. And the last.

JASON
The one that wove the weavers.

ZEUS
(smiling)
You're learning. (beat) But beware. The underworld is not like the sea or sky. Hades doesn't test. He judges. And his judgment — is final.

---

# PIECE 11: THE DESCENT

EXT. THE SKY - DAWN

The Argo descends. The sky opens. The water receives them.

Four threads around Jason's wrist. Gold. Silver. Blue. White.

The crew is quiet. Changed. They faced their fears. They chose.

MEDEA
(writing)
Three looms left.

JASON
The underworld. The earth. And the last.

MEDEA
Hades will be different.

JASON
How?

MEDEA
(quietly)
Poseidon tests. Zeus tempts. But Hades — remembers. (beat) He remembers everything. Every soul. Every choice. Every regret.

JASON
Then we'll have to be honest.

MEDEA
(smiling)
You're getting good at that.

The Argo sails east. Toward the underworld. Toward Hades. Toward judgment.

The pattern continues.

END OF PART 02
