# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 09: THE FORGOTTEN ISLAND
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 09 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE SIGNAL

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The Argo sails. Jason at the bow. The seven threads pulse. Not glowing — pulsing. Like a heartbeat.

MEDEA
(writing)
The threads. They're—

JASON
Calling. (beat) Something's calling.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Island. Dead ahead. Wasn't there a moment ago.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
It appeared. Like it was waiting.

The island is small. Rocky. And on it — a single tree. Ancient. Dying.

MEDEA
(writing)
The tree of memory. (beat) I've heard of this place. (beat) It's where forgotten things go.

---

# PIECE 2: THE LANDING

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - SHORE - NIGHT

The crew lands. The island is silent. Not peaceful — empty. The silence of things that have been forgotten.

KORE
(afraid)
I don't like this place.

MEDEA
(writing)
No one does. (betrayal) But we need to understand.

The tree stands at the center. Its bark is covered in names. Thousands. Millions. All the forgotten.

JASON
(reading)
Names. People. Places. (betrayal) All forgotten.

ATALANTA
(reading)
Cities that fell. Heroes who died unnamed. Children who— (stopping) —who were never remembered.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
The saddest thing. Not dying. Being forgotten.

---

# PIECE 3: THE KEEPER

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - TREE - NIGHT

A figure emerges from behind the tree. OLD. Neither man nor woman. Wearing robes made of names.

THE KEEPER
Jason. Center of the world. (betrayal) You've come.

JASON
Who are you?

THE KEEPER
I keep the forgotten. (betrayal) Every name. Every story. Every— (searching) —every thread the pattern let go.

MEDEA
(writing)
Why?

THE KEEPER
(sadly)
Because someone should. (betrayal) The pattern forgets. But I remember. (betrayal) I remember everything.

JASON
Everything?

THE KEEPER
Every forgotten thing. Every lost soul. Every— (gesturing to the tree) —every name.

---

# PIECE 4: THE BURDEN

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - TREE - CONTINUOUS

THE KEEPER
It's heavy. Remembering. (betrayal) Every name is a weight. Every story is a— (searching) —a stone around my neck.

JASON
How long have you done this?

THE KEEPER
Since the beginning. Since the first thing was forgotten. (betrayal) I was the first to be forgotten. So I became the keeper. (betrayal) If I don't remember — who will?

MEDEA
(writing)
You were forgotten?

THE KEEPER
(sadly)
Yes. (betrayal) I was the first weaver's first friend. Before the pattern. Before the gods. (betrayal) And when the First Weaver chose to weave — they forgot me. (betrayal) So I came here. To remember. For everyone.

---

# PIECE 5: THE REQUEST

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - TREE - CONTINUOUS

THE KEEPER
(to Jason)
You're the center now. You can help me.

JASON
How?

THE KEEPER
Remember them. (betrayal) All of them. The forgotten. The lost. The— (searching) —the ones the pattern let go. (betrayal) You're the center. If you remember — they're not forgotten anymore.

JASON
(afraid)
There are millions. Billions. I can't—

THE KEEPER
(firmly)
You can. (betrayal) You're the center. You hold the pattern. (betrayal) Hold them too.

MEDEA
(writing)
Jason. If you take this on—

JASON
(finishing)
I know. (betrayal) It will change me. (betrayal) But— (looking at the names) —they deserve to be remembered.

---

# PIECE 6: THE REMEMBERING

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - TREE - NIGHT

Jason places his hands on the tree. The seven threads blaze.

He sees them. All of them. Every forgotten name. Every forgotten story. Every forgotten life.

Billions. Trillions. More than stars. More than grains of sand.

And he remembers. All of them. Every name. Every story. Every—

He screams. Not in pain. In — grief. The weight of all that forgetting. All that loss. All that—

Medea grabs his hand. Then Orpheus. Then Atalanta. Then the crew. Then Kore.

Together — they hold it. Together — they remember.

The tree blooms. Green. Alive. The names glow — not carved but growing. Living.

THE KEEPER
(crying)
Thank you. (betrayal) Thank you.

---

# PIECE 7: THE TRANSFORMATION

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - TREE - DAWN

Jason collapses. The crew catches him. He's breathing. Alive. But — different.

His eyes. They see more. Hold more. Remember more.

JASON
(quietly)
I know them all now. (betrayal) Every forgotten name. Every forgotten— (stopping) —everyone.

MEDEA
(writing)
How do you feel?

JASON
(thinking)
Heavier. Lighter. (betrayal) More. (betrayal) I'm still me. But I'm also— (searching) —I'm also them. All of them. The forgotten.

THE KEEPER
(smiling)
You're not just the center anymore. (betrayal) You're the memory. (betrayal) The pattern's memory.

---

# PIECE 8: THE GIFT

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - TREE - DAWN

THE KEEPER
I have something for you.

The Keeper reaches into the tree. Pulls out a seed. Glowing. Warm.

THE KEEPER (CONT'D)
Plant this wherever you go. (betrayal) It will grow. And its roots will remember. (betrayal) Every place you plant it — the forgotten will be remembered.

JASON
(taking it)
Thank you.

THE KEEPER
No. Thank you. (betrayal) For the first time since the beginning — I'm not alone. (betrayal) Someone else remembers. (betrayal) Someone else— (searching) —cares.

MEDEA
(writing)
You're not alone. (betrayal) You never were. (betrayal) The pattern remembers. Even when it forgets.

---

# PIECE 9: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. FORGOTTEN ISLAND - SHORE - DAY

The crew boards. The island behind them. The tree blooming.

KORE
(looking back)
Will we come back?

JASON
(smiling)
If we need to. (betrayal) But I don't think we will. (betrayal) The Keeper isn't alone anymore.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Where to now?

JASON
(looking at the seed)
Wherever this needs to be planted. (betrayal) Wherever the forgotten need remembering.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
That's everywhere.

JASON
(smiling)
Then everywhere it is.

---

# PIECE 10: THE PLANTING

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

Jason holds the seed. The crew around him.

MEDEA
(writing)
Where do we plant it first?

JASON
(thinking)
Where it's needed most. (betrayal) Where the forgetting is deepest.

He closes his eyes. The seven threads pulse. The pattern speaks:

JASON (CONT'D)
(quietly)
Crete. (betrayal) The labyrinth. Where Asterion was forgotten. (betrayal) Where the pattern went wrong.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then Crete it is.

---

# PIECE 11: THE SAILING

EXT. OPEN SEA - SUNSET

The Argo sails. The seed safe. The crew at peace.

Jason at the bow. Medea beside him. Kore on his other side.

KORE
Jason? (betrayal) What's it like? Remembering everyone?

JASON
(quietly)
Beautiful. Terrible. (betrayal) Necessary.

KORE
Does it hurt?

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. (betrayal) But the hurt means — they matter. (betrayal) And they do. (betrayal) Every single one.

MEDEA
(writing)
Even the forgotten?

JASON
(smiling)
Especially the forgotten.

The sun sets. The stars appear. Each one a name. Each one a story. Each one — remembered.

The Argo sails on.

The pattern remembers.

END OF PART 09
