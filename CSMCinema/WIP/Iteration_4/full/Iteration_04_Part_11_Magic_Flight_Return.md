# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 11: MAGIC FLIGHT RETURN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 11 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay

# PIECE 1: THE UNKNOWN LAND

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - HARBOR - DAY

The Argo docks. A land none of them recognize. The people speak a language none of them know.

But — the people smile. Welcome. Offer food. Offer shelter.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Who are they?

MEDEA
(writing)
I don't know. (betrayal) But they know us.

An ELDER (80s, kind eyes, speaks broken Greek) approaches.

ELDER
You are— (searching for words) —the thread-bearer. The center.

JASON
You know me?

ELDER
(smiling)
The pattern told us. (betrayal) You would come. When the seven threads were gathered. When the center was— (searching) —ready.

JASON
Ready for what?

ELDER
(seriously)
The last lesson.

---

# PIECE 2: THE LAST LESSON

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - VILLAGE - DAY

The crew is welcomed. Fed. Housed. Honored.

But Jason is uneasy.

JASON
(to Medea)
Something's wrong.

MEDEA
(writing)
What do you mean?

JASON
This is too easy. Too— (searching) —too welcoming. Every other quest had a test. A challenge. A—

MEDEA
(writing)
Maybe that's the test. (betrayal) Accepting kindness. Without suspicion.

JASON
(considering)
Maybe. (betrayal) Or maybe— (searching) —maybe the test is something else.

---

# PIECE 3: THE ELDER'S STORY

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - FIRE - NIGHT

The crew gathered around a fire. The Elder speaks.

ELDER
Long ago— (searching for words) —this land was— (gesturing) —broken. The pattern— (searching) —frayed. People forgot each other. Hated each other. Killed each other.

JASON
What changed?

ELDER
(smiling)
A weaver came. Long ago. She taught us— (searching) —taught us to remember. To weave. To— (gesturing) —to see the pattern.

MEDEA
(writing)
Who was she?

ELDER
(looking at Medea)
You know her. (betrayal) She had your eyes. Your— (searching) —your hands.

MEDEA
(awed, writing)
My mother.

---

# PIECE 4: THE MOTHER

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - FIRE - CONTINUOUS

MEDEA
(writing, tears falling)
My mother was here?

ELDER
(nodding)
She passed through. Many years ago. She was— (searching) —running. Hiding. From your father.

MEDEA
(writing)
Aetes.

ELDER
(shaking his head)
No. (betrayal) From the one before. The one who— (searching) —who hurt her. Who made her— (stopping) —who made her weave things she didn't want to weave.

JASON
(gently)
Medea. You never told me—

MEDEA
(writing)
I didn't know. (betrayal) My mother left when I was five. I thought— (searching) —I thought she abandoned me. (betrayal) But she was running. She was— (stopping) —she was protecting me.

---

# PIECE 5: THE MOTHER'S LOOM

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - HUT - DAY

The Elder leads them to a hut. Inside — a loom. Old. Worn. But — familiar.

MEDEA
(touching it, writing)
This is hers. I remember. (betrayal) The sound it made. The— (searching) —the rhythm.

On the loom — a half-finished tapestry. Showing a mother and child. Weaving together.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(writing, crying)
She was making this. When she— (stopping) —when she had to run.

JASON
(looking at the tapestry)
It's beautiful.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's unfinished. (betrayal) Like— (searching) —like everything she left behind.

---

# PIECE 6: THE CHOICE

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - HUT - CONTINUOUS

ELDER
She left something. For you. (betrayal) For when you came.

He hands Medea a letter. Old. Folded. Sealed.

MEDEA
(afraid, writing)
I can't.

JASON
(gently)
You can. (betrayal) I'm here.

She opens it. Reads silently. Tears falling.

MEDEA
(writing, showing Jason)
She says— (stopping) —she says she's sorry. She says she loved me. She says— (searching) —she says she wove me into the pattern. So I'd find my way here. So I'd— (stopping) —so I'd finish what she started.

JASON
What did she start?

MEDEA
(writing)
The mending. (betrayal) The pattern is fraying. It's been fraying since— (searching) —since the beginning. And she tried to mend it. But she couldn't. She was— (stopping) —she was too alone.

---

# PIECE 7: THE MENDING

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - VILLAGE - DAY

MEDEA
(writing)
I understand now. (betrayal) Why I became the loom. Why I wove myself into the pattern. Why— (searching) —why I found you.

JASON
Because of your mother?

MEDEA
(writing)
Because of her mending. (betrayal) She started it. I continued it. And now— (searching) —now we finish it.

JASON
How?

MEDEA
(writing)
Together. (betrayal) The way she couldn't. The way— (searching) —the way no one should have to.

She takes his hand. The seven threads glow.

The pattern responds. The fraying — visible now, like torn fabric — begins to mend.

---

# PIECE 8: THE WEAVING

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - VILLAGE - SUNSET

Medea weaves. Not on a loom. In the air. The threads visible. Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black. Green. And the seventh — all colors.

Jason holds the pattern. Steady. Strong. The center.

The crew watches. The villagers watch. The world watches.

And the mending continues. Thread by thread. Choice by choice. Love by love.

MEDEA
(weaving, speaking)
The pattern isn't fate. (betrayal) It's choice. (betrayal) And choice— (searching) —and choice is love.

The last thread weaves into place. The pattern — whole. Complete. Mended.

The world BREATHES. A sigh of relief. Of completion. Of—

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(collapsing)
It's done.

JASON
(catching her)
Medea!

MEDEA
(smiling, weak)
I'm okay. (betrayal) Just— (searching) —just tired.

---

# PIECE 9: THE CELEBRATION

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - VILLAGE - NIGHT

A feast. The villagers celebrating. The crew celebrating. The pattern — whole.

ELDER
(to Jason)
You did it. (betrayal) The pattern is mended. The fraying— (searching) —the fraying is healed.

JASON
We did it. (betrayal) All of us. Together.

ELDER
(smiling)
That's the secret. (betrayal) The pattern isn't held by one. It's held by— (searching) —by everyone. Together.

ATALANTA
(dancing with Orpheus)
We did it!

ORPHEUS
(playing and dancing)
We did!

ZETES and CALAUS
(dancing together)
We did!

KORE
(dancing with the village children)
We did!

---

# PIECE 10: THE MORNING AFTER

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - SHORE - DAWN

Jason and Medea at the water's edge. The Argo waiting.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern is whole. (betrayal) For now.

JASON
For now?

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern will fray again. (betrayal) It always does. (betrayal) But now— (searching) —now we know how to mend it.

JASON
Together.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Together.

JASON
What now?

MEDEA
(writing)
Now— (searching) —now we go home. (betrayal) We rest. We live. We— (stopping) —we love.

JASON
And the quests?

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
They'll wait. (betrayal) They always do.

---

# PIECE 11: THE SAILING HOME

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. The seven threads gleaming. The tapestry flying. The crew at peace.

Jason at the bow. Medea beside him. Kore on his other side. The crew behind.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Home. Iolcus. Two weeks.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
What will we do? When we get there?

JASON
(smiling)
Live. (betrayal) Love. (betrayal) Be.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's enough?

JASON
(smiling)
That's everything.

The Argo sails. The sun shines. The pattern holds.

Iteration 4 is complete.

The pattern continues.

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END OF ITERATION 4
