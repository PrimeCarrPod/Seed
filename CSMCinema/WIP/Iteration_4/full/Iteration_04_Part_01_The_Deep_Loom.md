# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 01: THE DEEP LOOM
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 01 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE WESTERN SEA

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails west. The crew at the oars. JASON at the bow, the golden and silver threads wrapped around his wrist. The fleece secured below deck.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
No land. No birds. Nothing but water for three days.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The map shows the loom beneath the sea. West of the Pillars of Heracles.

ZETES
The Pillars of Heracles? That's the edge of the world.

CALAIS
Beyond the edge of the world?

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
That's where the best things are.

MEDEA
(from below deck, calling up)
Jason!

Jason descends.

---

# PIECE 2: THE VISION

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - CONTINUOUS

Medea stands over the fleece. It glows — but differently now. Pulsing. Rhythmic.

MEDEA
The fleece is responding.

JASON
To what?

MEDEA
The loom. The one beneath the sea. It's close.

The glow intensifies. Images form in the light:

A vast underwater cavern. A loom made of coral and bone. And around it — sea creatures. Not fish. Something older.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
Poseidon's loom. Where he wove the seas into existence.

JASON
Can we reach it?

MEDEA
We'll need help.

JASON
From who?

MEDEA
(smiling)
The last person you'd expect.

---

# PIECE 3: THE TRITON

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The water glows. A figure rises. TRITON (40s, Poseidon's son, conch shell trumpet, part man, part sea) stands on the waves.

TRITON
Jason of Iolcus. You sail toward my father's domain.

JASON
We seek the loom beneath the sea.

TRITON
(laughing)
Everyone seeks something. Few find it. Most drown.

MEDEA
(stepping forward)
Triton. Son of Poseidon. We come in peace.

TRITON
(recognizing her)
The priestess. The one who became the loom. (beat) My father spoke of you. He's not pleased.

MEDEA
We don't need his pleasure. Just his permission.

TRITON
(sharply)
You need more than permission. You need his blessing. And my father doesn't bless. He tests.

JASON
Then test us.

TRITON
(considering)
Very well. But the test isn't for you. It's for them.

He gestures to the crew.

---

# PIECE 4: THE TEST

EXT. OPEN SEA - CONTINUOUS

Triton raises his conch. Blows. The sea responds.

Waves rise. Not attacking — testing. Each wave targets a different crew member.

A wave crashes toward ATALANTA. She leaps — lands on the mast — keeps her feet.

A wave crashes toward ZETES and CALAIS. They brace together — the wave splits around them.

A wave crashes toward ORPHEUS. He plays — the wave calms — passes gently.

TRITON
(to Jason)
They're worthy. But are you?

The largest wave yet rises. Towering. Impossible.

TRITON (CONT'D)
This one's for you.

JASON
(quietly)
I can't stop a wave.

MEDEA
(whispering)
You don't stop it. You become it.

Jason closes his eyes. The golden thread glows. The silver thread hums.

He opens his eyes. Steps off the bow. Onto the water.

He doesn't sink.

The wave passes beneath him. Over him. Through him.

He stands. Dry. Unmoved.

TRITON
(awed)
You walked through Poseidon's wave.

JASON
I didn't walk through it. I walked with it.

TRITON
(smiling)
My father will want to meet you.

---

# PIECE 5: THE DESCENT

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

Triton leads the Argo down. The sea parts. A tunnel of water. The ship descends.

The crew stares at the walls of water. Fish swim past. Whales. Things that aren't whales.

ATALANTA
(from above)
This is impossible.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
Impossible is just another word for "not yet understood."

The tunnel opens. A vast underwater cavern. Bioluminescent. Beautiful. Terrifying.

And in the center — the LOOM OF THE SEA. Coral. Bone. Pearl. Woven by Poseidon himself.

TRITON
The loom that wove the oceans. Where every wave was patterned. Where every current was chosen.

MEDEA
(awed)
It's beautiful.

TRITON
It's dangerous. (beat) My father wove this when the world was young. Before he learned restraint.

JASON
What does that mean?

TRITON
It means — this loom doesn't just weave water. It weaves chaos.

---

# PIECE 6: POSEIDON

INT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

The water shifts. POSEIDON (50s, bearded, trident, eyes like the deep) emerges from the darkness.

POSEIDON
Triton. You brought them.

TRITON
They passed the test.

POSEIDON
(testing)
Did they? (to Jason) You walked through my wave. That was clever. Or lucky.

JASON
Does it matter which?

POSEIDON
(laughing)
I like you, Greek. You remind me of myself. Before Olympus. Before the chains.

JASON
Chains?

POSEIDON
(quietly)
The gods are chained too. By duty. By expectation. By the pattern. (beat) You understand that, don't you?

JASON
I'm learning.

POSEIDON
Good. Because the loom you seek — it's not a gift. It's a burden.

MEDEA
We'll carry it.

POSEIDON
(looking at her)
You already carry one loom. You want another?

MEDEA
I want to understand.

POSEIDON
(nodding)
That's the right answer. (beat) Touch the loom. But know — it will show you what you're afraid of.

---

# PIECE 7: THE LOOM OF THE SEA

INT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

Jason approaches the loom. The threads are water. Liquid. Flowing. Constantly moving.

He touches them.

The cavern disappears. He's somewhere else. Somewhen else.

A VISION: The world before the gods. Chaos. Pure. Undifferentiated. No land. No sea. No sky. Just — possibility.

And in the chaos — a choice. The first choice. The one that separated sea from sky. Land from water. Being from non-being.

JASON
(in the vision)
The first pattern.

A VOICE (not Poseidon, not any god — the pattern itself):

THE PATTERN
You see now. Before the gods. Before time. Before everything. There was choice. And choice created pattern. And pattern created everything.

JASON
Why show me this?

THE PATTERN
Because you need to understand. The looms don't create. They reveal. They make visible what was always there.

JASON
And what was always there?

THE PATTERN
You.

---

# PIECE 8: THE RETURN

INT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

Jason opens his eyes. The crew around him. Medea holding his hand.

MEDEA
You were gone. For hours.

JASON
It was minutes.

MEDEA
Hours. (beat) What did you see?

JASON
The beginning. Before the gods. Before everything. (beat) And I saw — us. The pattern. It's not something we're part of. It's something we are.

POSEIDON
(nodding)
Now you understand. (beat) The looms aren't tools. They're mirrors. You see yourself. Your choices. Your possibilities.

JASON
And my fears?

POSEIDON
(smiling)
Those too.

---

# PIECE 9: THE GIFT

INT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

Poseidon gestures to the loom. A single thread detaches. Blue. Flowing. Liquid.

POSEIDON
Take this.

JASON
What is it?

POSEIDON
Memory of the sea. Every wave. Every current. Every storm. (beat) When you're lost — hold the thread. The sea will guide you.

JASON
Why give me this?

POSEIDON
Because you'll need it. (beat) The next loom is in the sky. And the sky — is less forgiving than the sea.

MEDEA
The sky loom?

POSEIDON
Where Zeus wove the stars. Where the constellations were patterned. (beat) But Zeus doesn't share power. And he doesn't forgive thieves.

JASON
We're not thieves.

POSEIDON
(smiling)
You stole from Colchis. You refused Athena. You walked through my wave. (beat) To Zeus — that's thievery.

---

# PIECE 10: THE ASCENT

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo rises. The sea closes above them. They surface.

The crew gasps. Air. Light. Sky.

ATALANTA
(from above)
I never thought I'd be happy to see the sun.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The sea gives gifts. The sky gives tests.

ZETES
Then let's get tested.

CALAIS
(smiling)
Optimist.

Jason stands at the bow. Three threads now around his wrist. Gold. Silver. Blue.

MEDEA
Three looms. Four to go.

JASON
Four?

MEDEA
The sky. The underworld. The earth. And the last one.

JASON
Which is?

MEDEA
(quietly)
The one that wove the wove the weavers.

JASON
That doesn't make sense.

MEDEA
(smiling)
It will.

---

# PIECE 11: THE HORIZON

EXT. OPEN SEA - SUNSET

The Argo sails. The crew rests. The threads gleam.

Jason and Medea at the bow.

JASON
Poseidon said the sky loom is where Zeus wove the stars.

MEDEA
Zeus doesn't share power.

JASON
Then we'll ask nicely.

MEDEA
(laughing)
You refused Athena. You refused the Persian king. You refused Pelias. (beat) You're not good at asking nicely.

JASON
I'm learning.

MEDEA
(smiling)
You already have.

The sun sets. The stars appear. Each one a thread in the sky loom. Each one a choice. Each one a pattern.

JASON
(looking at the stars)
They're beautiful.

MEDEA
They're waiting.

JASON
For what?

MEDEA
For us.

The Argo sails toward the stars. The pattern continues.

END OF PART 01
