# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 4 — PART 03: THE UNDERWORLD LOOM
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 4 of 7  
**Part:** 03 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE ENTRANCE

EXT. CAP SOUNION - NIGHT

The Argo anchors at a cliff. A cave below. Dark. Breathing.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The entrance to the underworld. Near Athens.

MEDEA
(writing)
Hades doesn't test like Poseidon. Doesn't tempt like Zeus. He judges.

JASON
Then we'll have to be honest.

MEDEA
(smiling)
You're getting good at that.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
The dead don't like music. It reminds them of life.

ZETES
Then why play?

ORPHEUS
To remind us we're still alive.

---

# PIECE 2: THE DESCENT

INT. UNDERWORLD ENTRANCE - NIGHT

The crew descends. Torchlight. Damp. The walls breathe.

CERBERUS (three-headed dog, guardian) blocks the path. Growling.

ATALANTA
(draws bow)
I can—

JASON
No. (stepping forward) We come to see Hades. Not to fight.

Cerberus growls louder. Jason holds out his hand. The four threads glow.

Cerberus sniffs. Recognizes something. Steps aside.

CALAUS
He let us pass.

MEDEA
(writing)
The threads. He recognizes the looms.

---

# PIECE 3: THE RIVER

INT. UNDERWORLD - RIVER STYX - NIGHT

A black river. CHARON (ancient, ferryman, hollow eyes) poles a boat.

CHARON
Fare.

JASON
We're not dead.

CHARON
(sharply)
Everyone here says that. (beat) The fare is a memory. A true memory. Not a lie. Not a wish. Truth.

Each crew member gives a memory:

ATALANTA gives the memory of her first kill. The deer. The eyes. The regret.

ORPHEUS gives the memory of his mother's voice. Teaching him the first note.

ZETES and CALAUS give the memory of their first flight. Together. Terrifying. Free.

MEDEA gives the memory of her first thread. The first pattern she ever wove.

JASON gives the memory of his father's face. The last time he saw him. Being taken away.

Charon accepts each. Poles them across.

---

# PIECE 4: THE JUDGMENT HALL

INT. HADES' PALACE - NIGHT

A vast hall. Shadows. Silence. And on a throne of bone — HADES (50s, dark, patient, remembers everything).

PERSEPHONE (40s, his queen, spring in winter, kind eyes) sits beside him.

HADES
Jason of Iolcus. (beat) You carry four threads. Sea. Sky. World. (beat) You want the fifth.

JASON
We want to understand.

HADES
(quietly)
Everyone says that. (beat) But understanding has a cost. (beat) Persephone understands. Don't you, my queen?

PERSEPHONE
(softly)
I understand that choice and consequence are the same thing.

HADES
(to Jason)
She chose to eat the pomegranate seeds. Chose to return here. Chose me. (beat) And every choice has a consequence.

---

# PIECE 5: THE JUDGMENT

INT. HADES' PALACE - CONTINUOUS

HADES
I don't test. I don't tempt. I judge. (beat) And my judgment is this: You've taken much. Fleece. Threads. Knowledge. Power. (beat) What have you given?

JASON
(giving it thought)
We gave... ourselves. Our safety. Our certainty. Our—

HADES
(interrupting)
Wrong. (beat) You've taken. But you haven't given. Not really. Not yet. (beat) The pattern demands balance. Taking requires giving. (beat) What will you give?

Long silence.

MEDEA
(writing)
Myself. My power. My connection to the loom. If that's what's required.

HADES
(softly)
Is it?

MEDEA
(long pause, then writing)
No. (beat) What you really want is something I can't give back.

HADES
(nodding)
The truth. (beat) You don't know what to give. Because you've never had to. (beat) The pattern has always provided. The looms have always welcomed. (beat) But here — in my realm — you must give before you receive.

---

# PIECE 6: THE GIFT

INT. HADES' PALACE - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(quietly)
What do you want?

HADES
(smiling)
Finally — the right question. (beat) I want your certainty.

JASON
My certainty?

HADES
You're certain the pattern is good. Certain the looms are right. Certain you're doing what you should. (beat) Give me that certainty. And I'll give you the fifth thread.

JASON
(afraid)
If I give up certainty — what do I have?

PERSEPHONE
(softly)
Faith. (beat) Faith is harder than certainty. But it's stronger.

JASON
(to Medea)
What do you think?

MEDEA
(writing)
I think — certainty is comfortable. But faith is alive. (beat) And we didn't come here to be comfortable.

JASON
(to Hades)
Done.

He feels it leave. The certainty. The knowing. The comfort.

He's afraid. But alive.

HADES
(rising)
You passed. (beat) Not because you were strong. Because you were willing to be weak.

---

# PIECE 7: THE THREAD

INT. HADES' PALACE - CONTINUOUS

Hades gestures. A thread detaches from his throne. Black. Dark. But not empty — full. Full of everything that ever was.

HADES
Memory of the underworld. Every ending. Every loss. Every goodbye. (beat) When you fear death — hold the thread. Remember: endings are part of the pattern.

JASON
(taking it)
Five threads.

HADES
Five looms. Two to go. (beat) The earth. And the last.

JASON
The one that wove the weavers.

HADES
(serious)
Be careful with that one, Jason. (beat) The first loom doesn't give. It takes. And what it takes — you don't get back.

PERSEPHONE
(gently)
But what you become — is worth it.

---

# PIECE 8: THE RETURN

INT. UNDERWORLD - RIVER STYX - DAWN

Charon poles them back. Each crew member quieter. Changed.

ATALANTA
I gave the memory of my first kill.

ORPHEUS
I gave my mother's voice.

MEDEA
I gave my first thread.

JASON
I gave my father's face.

Silence.

ZETES
We're lighter.

CALAIS
But heavier.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's what giving does. It empties you. So you can be filled again.

---

# PIECE 9: THE SURFACE

EXT. CAP SOUNION - DAWN

The Argo sails. The sun rises. Five threads around Jason's wrist.

Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black.

MEDEA
(writing)
Two looms left.

JASON
The earth. And the first.

MEDEA
The earth loom is beneath Olympus. Where Gaia wove the world.

JASON
And the first loom?

MEDEA
(afraid)
Where it all began. Before time. Before gods. Before everything.

JASON
Are you scared?

MEDEA
(writing)
Terrified. (beat) But I'm going anyway.
