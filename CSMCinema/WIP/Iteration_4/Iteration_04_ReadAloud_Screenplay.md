# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## An Iteration 4 Read-Aloud Screenplay
### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE — THE DEEP

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails west. JASON at the bow, seven threads around his wrist. MEDEA beside him.

ATALANTA
(from above)
No land. No birds. Nothing but water for three days.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The map shows the loom beneath the sea. West of the Pillars of Heracles.

ZETES
The Pillars of Heracles? That's the edge of the world.

MEDEA
(writing)
The edge is where the best things are.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

TRITON (Poseidon's son) rises from the waves.

TRITON
Jason of Iolcus. You sail toward my father's domain.

JASON
We seek the loom beneath the sea.

TRITON
My father doesn't bless. He tests.

JASON
Then test us.

TRITON
(considering)
Very well. But the test isn't for you. It's for them.

He blows his conch. Waves rise. Each crew member faces a wave. Each survives.

TRITON (CONT'D)
(to Jason)
This one's for you.

A massive wave rises. Jason steps onto the water. Walks through it. Dry.

TRITON (CONT'D)
(awed)
You walked through Poseidon's wave.

JASON
I didn't walk through it. I walked with it.

---

INT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - NIGHT

POSEIDON (bearded, trident) emerges.

POSEIDON
You walked through my wave. That was clever. Or lucky.

JASON
Does it matter which?

POSEIDON
(laughing)
I like you, Greek. (beat) Touch the loom. But know — it will show you what you're afraid of.

Jason touches the loom. Sees: the world before the gods. Chaos. Pure. Undifferentiated.

THE PATTERN (V.O.)
Before the gods. Before time. Before everything. There was choice. And choice created pattern. And pattern created everything.

JASON
Why show me this?

THE PATTERN
Because you need to understand. The looms don't create. They reveal.

---

## ACT TWO — THE SKY

EXT. ATLANTIC OCEAN - DAY

The Argo passes between the Pillars of Heracles. The water rises. The ship ascends.

ATALANTA
(from above, laughing)
I can touch the stars!

ORPHEUS
(whispering)
The stars are threads.

---

EXT. THE SKY - NIGHT

ZEUS (thunder in human form) stands before the sky loom.

ZEUS
You've refused Athena. Walked through Poseidon's wave. And now — you come to mine.

JASON
We come to understand.

ZEUS
You want to understand the pattern so you can become it.

JASON
(quietly)
Yes.

ZEUS
(smiling)
At least you're honest. (beat) Face what you fear most. Accept it. And you pass.

The crew separated. Each faces their fear.

ATALANTA faces herself — older, slower, unable to hunt.

ORPHEUS faces silence — absolute, permanent.

JASON faces himself — successful, powerful, king of everything, completely alone.

KING JASON
You see? I won. Everything I wanted.

JASON
At what cost?

KING JASON
Does it matter? I won.

JASON
Look at you. You're alone. No crew. No Medea. No friends. Just subjects. That's not winning. That's losing everything that matters.

The king fades.

ZEUS
(appearing)
You passed. You chose love over power. That's the hardest choice. Most fail.

---

## ACT THREE — THE UNDERWORLD

INT. HADES' PALACE - NIGHT

HADES (dark, patient) sits on a throne of bone. PERSEPHONE beside him.

HADES
I don't test. I don't tempt. I judge. (beat) You've taken much. What have you given?

JASON
We gave ourselves. Our safety. Our—

HADES
(interrupting)
Wrong. You've taken. But you haven't given. Not really. Not yet.

PERSEPHONE
(softly)
What will you give?

JASON
(quietly)
My certainty.

HADES
(nodding)
You passed. Not because you were strong. Because you were willing to be weak.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE EARTH

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - NIGHT

GAIA (ageless, mother of everything) tends the earth loom.

GAIA
Before me — there was chaos. And I chose. The first choice. To weave order from chaos. (beat) And that choice created everything. The gods. The world. You.

JASON
Why?

GAIA
Because I was lonely. Chaos is free — but it's alone. I wanted connection. (beat) So I wove. And the weaving created — everything.

MEDEA
(writing)
And the suffering?

GAIA
(sadly)
The cost. Every thread I wove — excluded every other thread. Order is beautiful. But it's built on the graves of all the possibilities I didn't choose.

---

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

THE FIRST WEAVER (ageless, the one who made the first choice):

THE FIRST WEAVER
You carry six threads. You seek the seventh. (beat) The seventh thread makes you the center. The consciousness. The weaver of weavers.

JASON
And if I refuse?

THE FIRST WEAVER
The pattern continues. Without a center. Beautiful. Unfinished.

MEDEA
(writing)
Jason. No.

JASON
(quietly)
I choose both. (beat) I choose to become the center. But I choose to remember. I choose to hold the pattern — but I choose to hold you too.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(sharply)
That's not possible. The center can't—

JASON
(interrupting)
Watch me.

He takes the seven threads. Light. Blinding. He screams — not in pain, in becoming.

JASON (CONT'D)
(quietly)
I am the pattern. And I am Jason. Both. Always both.

THE FIRST WEAVER
(smiling)
You did it. You became the center — and remained yourself. (beat) The center can love. The pattern is love.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo returns. The crowd gathered. Jason steps onto the dock. The seven threads glowing.

JASON
Don't kneel. I'm not a god. I'm not a king. I'm just Jason.

PELIAS
(pushing through the crowd)
You carry the pattern. (beat) Then I surrender. The throne is yours.

JASON
(helping him up)
I don't want the throne. (beat) But I need your help. You know people. I know patterns. Together — we could serve well.

PELIAS
(together)
We could serve well.

---

EXT. TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason and Medea's wedding. Simple. Joyful.

PRIESTESS
Jason of Iolcus. Do you take this woman? Not as property. Not as prize. But as partner. As equal?

JASON
I do.

PRIESTESS
Medea of Colchis. Do you take this man? Not as hero. Not as center. But as partner. As equal?

MEDEA
(speaking — her voice returned)
I do.

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)
The pattern provides.

They kiss. The crowd cheers.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - ROOFTOP - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. The stars above.

MEDEA
(quietly)
It's not over.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
There are still threads that need mending. Still patterns that need healing.

JASON
Where to next?

MEDEA
(smiling)
The pattern will tell us. (beat) But not tonight.

JASON
What happens tonight?

MEDEA
Tonight — we rest. We love. We live.

JASON
And tomorrow?

MEDEA
Tomorrow — the pattern continues.

---

## ACT SIX — THE MENDING

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - VILLAGE - DAY

An ELDER speaks broken Greek.

ELDER
You are the thread-bearer. The center. (betrayal) The pattern told us you would come.

JASON
Why?

ELDER
(seriously)
The last lesson.

---

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - HUT - DAY

Medea finds her mother's loom. A half-finished tapestry. Mother and child weaving.

MEDEA
(writing, tears falling)
She was making this. When she had to run.

ELDER
She left something. For you.

He hands her a letter. She reads silently. Shows Jason.

MEDEA
(writing)
She says she's sorry. She says she loved me. She says she wove me into the pattern so I'd find my way here. So I'd finish what she started.

JASON
What did she start?

MEDEA
(writing)
The mending. The pattern is fraying. It's been fraying since the beginning. And she tried to mend it. But she was too alone.

---

EXT. UNKNOWN LAND - VILLAGE - SUNSET

Medea weaves. In the air. The threads visible. Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black. Green. And the seventh — all colors.

Jason holds the pattern. Steady. Strong.

MEDEA
(weaving, speaking)
The pattern isn't fate. It's choice. And choice is love.

The last thread weaves into place. The pattern — whole. Complete. Mended.

The world BREATHES.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(collapsing)
It's done.

JASON
(catching her)
Medea!

MEDEA
(smiling, weak)
I'm okay. Just tired.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails home. The seven threads gleaming.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Home. Iolcus. Two weeks.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
What will we do? When we get there?

JASON
(smiling)
Live. Love. Be.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's enough?

JASON
(smiling)
That's everything.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo docks. The crew disembarks. Jason and Medea last.

JASON
(to the crew)
Rest. Recover. We sail in one week.

ATALANTA
Where to?

JASON
(smiling)
Wherever the pattern leads.

MEDEA
(writing)
Together.

CREW
(together)
Always!

The sun rises. The pattern holds.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END OF ITERATION 4
