# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 10: REFUSAL OF RETURN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 10 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE CRISIS

INT. PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

A MESSENGER arrives. Breathless.

MESSENGER
My lord! The Fleece! It's gone!

JASON
I know. I returned it.

MESSENGER
No, my lord. The TEMPLE. The sacred oak. The Fleece has been STOLEN.

Jason's face. The Fleece was returned to the gods. Who took it?

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE INVESTIGATION

EXT. SACRED GROVE - DAY

Jason, Medea, and Heracles examine the oak. The FLEECE is gone. Only a scrap of cloth remains.

MEDEA
This is Hecate's cloth.

JASON
Hecate took it?

MEDEA
Or someone who serves her.

HERACLES
Who?

MEDEA
(quietly)
Someone who wants the power back.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE SUSPECT

INT. PALACE - DUNGEONS - NIGHT

Jason interrogates a PRISONER. A priest of Hecate.

JASON
Where is the Fleece?

PRIEST
I don't know.

JASON
You serve Hecate. She took it.

PRIEST
The goddess takes what she wants. (beat)
But she didn't take the Fleece.

JASON
Then who did?

PRIEST
(afraid)
Someone worse.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE REVELATION

INT. PALACE - JASON'S CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Medea performs a ritual. Smoke. Fire. Divination.

MEDEA
I see it. (beat)
The Fleece. In the hands of—

She stops. Her face WHITES.

JASON
Who?

MEDEA
Your uncle. (beat)
Pelias. He's alive.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE TRUTH

INT. PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason addresses the court.

JASON
Pelias lives.

GASPS. The court MURMURS.

JASON (CONT'D)
He faked his death. He's been hiding. And now he has the Fleece.

HERACLES
Where?

JASON
Colchis. (beat)
He's going to use the Fleece to forge a new covenant. One that makes him king of both Colchis and Iolcus.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE DECISION

INT. PALACE - PRIVATE CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Jason paces. Medea watches.

MEDEA
You have to go after him.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
You just got peace. You just got the throne. You just got everything.

JASON
And I have to give it up. (beat)
To finish this.

MEDEA
Then I'm coming with you.

JASON
No. You stay. Protect the kingdom.

MEDEA
Jason—

JASON
I need to do this alone.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE REFUSAL

EXT. PALACE - COURTYARD - DAWN

Jason prepares to leave. The heroes gather.

HERACLES
You're going alone?

JASON
Yes.

ATALANTA
That's suicide.

JASON
Maybe. (beat)
But it's my suicide.

HERACLES
Then I'm coming.

JASON
No.

HERACLES
You don't get to refuse. (beat)
We're heroes. We go where the story takes us.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

Jason boards a small boat. Alone.

Medea watches from the shore. Tears on her face.

AESON
(appearing beside her)
He'll return.

MEDEA
How do you know?

AESON
Because he's my son. (beat)
And we always return.

The boat sails. Jason looks back. Iolcus grows small.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE JOURNEY

EXT. AEGEAN SEA - DAY

Jason sails alone. The sea is calm. Too calm.

He speaks to the wind.

JASON
I know you're there.

POSEIDON (V.O.)
You speak to the sea?

JASON
I speak to the god of the sea.

POSEIDON appears. Massive. Trident in hand.

POSEIDON
You returned the Fleece. Why?

JASON
Because it wasn't mine.

POSEIDON
And now Pelias has it.

JASON
Yes.

POSEIDON
And you're going to take it back.

JASON
Yes.

POSEIDON
Alone?

JASON
Yes.

POSEIDON
(laughs)
You're either very brave or very stupid.

JASON
Both.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE STORM

EXT. AEGEAN SEA - NIGHT

A STORM rises. Not natural. Poseidon's test.

Jason's boat is tossed. Waves crash. Lightning strikes.

JASON
(shouting into the storm)
I'm not afraid!

The storm INTENSIFIES.

JASON (CONT'D)
I'm not afraid of you!

The storm CALMS. Poseidon appears.

POSEIDON
You should be.

JASON
Maybe. (beat)
But I'm more afraid of not trying.

Poseidon smiles.

POSEIDON
Then pass.

The sea goes flat. The way forward is clear.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE ARRIVAL

EXT. COLCHIS - HARBOR - DAY

Jason docks. The city is DIFFERENT. Darker. The FLEECE's absence has changed it.

PELIAS stands on the walls. He's ALIVE. Older. Harder.

PELIAS
(shouting)
Jason! You've come!

JASON
(shouting back)
I've come for the Fleece!

PELIAS
It's mine now! The covenant is MINE!

JASON
(shouting)
Covenants can't be owned!

PELIAS
(shouting)
Watch me!

The gates OPEN. Pelias's army emerges.

Jason stands alone. One man against an army.

He draws his sword.

JASON
(to himself)
For Iolcus. For Medea. For the truth.

He CHARGES.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 10
