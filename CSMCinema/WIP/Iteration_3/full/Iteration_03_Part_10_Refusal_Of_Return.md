# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 10: REFUSAL OF RETURN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 10 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE OFFER

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

A ship approaches. Greek colors. Athenian flag.

The ship pulls alongside. ATHENA (appearing as geometry, not words — pure mathematical intuition) stands at the prow.

ATHENA
Jason of Iolcus.

JASON
Lady Athena.

ATHENA
The gods have watched you. With interest. (beat) You refused the Persian king. You chose mercy over revenge. You touched the loom and returned.

JASON
I had help.

ATHENA
You always do. (beat) The gods offer you a choice.

JASON
What choice?

ATHENA
Stay. In Greece. Rule Iolcus. We will make you king. Not just of Iolcus — of all Greece. Every city. Every throne. Every—

JASON
(interrupting)
No.

ATHENA
(surprised)
You haven't heard the full offer.

JASON
I don't need to. (beat) The answer is no.

ATHENA
You would refuse the gods?

JASON
I would refuse ownership. Again. (beat) The Persian king offered the same thing. Different words. Same trap.

ATHENA
This is not a trap. This is a gift.

JASON
Gifts from the gods always have strings. (beat) I prefer my strings visible.

---

# PIECE 2: THE THREAT

EXT. OPEN SEA - CONTINUOUS

Athena's geometry shifts. Sharper. More angular.

ATHENA
You refuse us. That is... unwise.

JASON
I've been called worse.

ATHENA
The gods do not forget slights.

JASON
I'm not slighting you. I'm choosing my own path.

ATHENA
Your path leads through the gods. (beat) We can make it easy. Or we can make it hard.

JASON
(quietly)
Then make it hard. (beat) I've done hard before.

ATHENA
(considering)
You're either very brave or very stupid.

JASON
My crew says both.

ATHENA
(almost smiling)
Your crew is wise. (beat) Very well. The path will be hard. But — we will not stop you. (beat) The gods are curious. We want to see what you become.

JASON
And what's that?

ATHENA
(smiling)
If we knew — it wouldn't be interesting.

She fades. The Athenian ship turns. Sails away.

---

# PIECE 3: THE DOUBT

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - NIGHT

Jason sits alone. The crew sleeps. He stares at the golden thread.

ATALANTA
(entering)
You refused the gods.

JASON
Word travels fast.

ATALANTA
The crew is talking. (beat) Some think you're brave. Some think you're crazy.

JASON
What do you think?

ATALANTA
I think you're consistent. (beat) You refused the Persian king. You refused Pelias. Now you refuse the gods. (beat) You have a pattern.

JASON
The pattern again.

ATALANTA
No. Your pattern. Not fate. Not the gods. Yours. (beat) You refuse to be owned. By anyone. (beat) That's not the pattern. That's you.

JASON
(quietly)
What if I'm wrong? What if I should have accepted?

ATALANTA
Then you'd be someone else. (beat) And we didn't follow someone else. We followed you.

---

# PIECE 4: THE NIGHTMARE

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. Dreams.

The DARKNESS speaks. Not a voice. Not a presence. Just — absence.

DARKNESS
You refuse everyone. The Persian king. The gods. Your uncle. Your destiny.

JASON
(in the dream)
I refuse ownership. Not connection.

DARKNESS
What's the difference?

JASON
Ownership is control. Connection is choice.

DARKNESS
And if the choice is taken away?

JASON
Then it was never choice.

DARKNESS
(closer)
You'll lose everything. The crew. The quest. The girl. (beat) Everyone you love — gone. Because you refused to bend.

JASON
Maybe. (beat) But I'll still be me.

DARKNESS
Is that enough?

JASON
It has to be.

The darkness recedes. Jason wakes.

---

# PIECE 5: THE MORNING

EXT. ARGO - DAWN

Jason wakes. The crew is already up. Working. Laughing. Living.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Bad dreams?

JASON
The usual.

ORPHEUS
The darkness?

JASON
(surprised)
How did you—

ORPHEUS
It comes to all of us. (beat) The fear that refusing means losing. That choosing yourself means being alone.

JASON
Does it?

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Look around.

Jason looks. The crew. His crew. His family. Still here. Still following. Still choosing him. Every day.

ORPHEUS (CONT'D)
You're not alone. You never were. (beat) The darkness lies.

---

# PIECE 6: THE MESSAGE

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

A dove lands on the mast. A message tied to its leg.

ATALANTA
(from above)
From Medea.

Jason reads. His face falls.

ZETES
What is it?

JASON
The loom. It's — destabilizing. (beat) Medea is holding it together. But she can't hold it forever.

CALAIS
What happens if she lets go?

JASON
Everything she wove — comes undone. (beat) The world. Time. The gods. Everything.

PERICLYMENUS
How long does she have?

JASON
(reading)
Days. Maybe less. (beat) She needs help. She needs— (stopping) —she needs us.

---

# PIECE 7: THE CHOICE

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAY

The crew gathers. Jason stands before them.

JASON
The loom is failing. Medea is holding it together. But she can't hold it alone. (beat) We have to go back.

ZETES
Back to the loom?

JASON
Yes.

CALAIS
We just left.

JASON
I know. (beat) But she needs us. And we don't leave family.

ATALANTA
(sharply)
We were heading north. To the Hyperboreans. To the next loom.

JASON
I know. (beat) But this is more important.

ATALANTA
(angry)
More important than the quest?

JASON
Yes. (beat) The quest is the pattern. But the pattern is people. (beat) Without Medea — there is no pattern.

Silence.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Then we go back.

ZETES
We go back.

CALAIS
All of us.

ATALANTA
(sighing)
We go back. (beat) But you owe us a really good story after this.

JASON
(smiling)
I'll give you the best one yet.

---

# PIECE 8: THE TURN

EXT. OPEN SEA - SUNSET

The Argo turns. South. Back toward the loom. Back toward Medea.

The crew rows. Not complaining. Not questioning. Just — rowing.

Jason stands at the stern. Looking north. Toward the Hyperboreans. Toward the next quest. Toward everything they're giving up.

MEDEA
(from the loom, distant, weak)
*You're coming back.*

JASON
(whispering)
Always.

MEDEA
*You shouldn't. (beat) The quest—*

JASON
The quest can wait. (beat) You can't.

MEEDA
*Jason—*

JASON
I'm coming. Hold on. (beat) Just hold on.

---

# PIECE 9: THE ARRIVAL

EXT. THE LOOM - NIGHT

The Argo arrives. The loom flickers. Unstable. The threads dimming.

Medea stands at the center. Translucent. Fading. Holding everything together through will alone.

JASON
(running to her)
Medea!

MEDEA
(fading)
You came back.

JASON
Of course I came back.

MEDEA
The quest—

JASON
Doesn't matter. (beat) You matter.

He grabs her hand. Solid. Barely. The threads respond. Brightening. Stabilizing.

MEDEA
(whispering)
I can't hold it alone.

JASON
You don't have to.

The crew gathers. Each placing a hand on the loom. Each adding their strength.

The threads brighten. The pattern stabilizes. The cosmos breathes.

MEDEA
(whole again)
Thank you.

JASON
Always.

---

# PIECE 10: THE NEW PATTERN

EXT. THE LOOM - DAWN

The crew rests. The loom glows. Stable. Whole.

Medea stands with Jason. The threads weave around them both.

MEDEA
The pattern changed.

JASON
How?

MEDEA
Because you came back. (beat) The pattern expected you to continue north. To choose the quest over the person. (beat) You didn't.

JASON
Was that wrong?

MEDEA
(smiling)
It was new. (beat) The pattern doesn't like new. But it needs it. (beat) Without new — the pattern becomes fate. Static. Dead.

JASON
And now?

MEDEA
Now the pattern is alive. (beat) Because you chose. Not because you had to. Because you wanted to.

JASON
I'll always choose you.

MEDEA
I know. (beat) That's why the pattern works.

---

# PIECE 11: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. Again. North this time. Toward the Hyperboreans. Toward the next loom.

Medea stands at the bow. Whole. Real. The threads still visible around her — but she's here. Present. With Jason.

MEDEA
The next loom will be harder.

JASON
They're all harder.

MEDEA
This one is different. (beat) The Hyperboreans don't like visitors. They don't like change. They don't like—

JASON
Us?

MEDEA
(smiling)
Anyone. (beat) But especially us.

JASON
Then we'll be especially charming.

MEDEA
You don't know how to be charming.

JASON
I'll learn.

MEDEA
(leaning against him)
You already have.

The Argo sails north. The crew at the oars. The pattern continuing.

END OF PART 10
