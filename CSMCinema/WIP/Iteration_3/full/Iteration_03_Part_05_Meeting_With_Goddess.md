# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 05: MEETING WITH GODDESS
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 05 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE TEMPLE OF HERA

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HERA - DAWN

Jason enters the temple alone. The FLEECE is wrapped in cloth. The temple is empty except for the statue of HERA, queen of the gods.

Jason kneels.

JASON
I have the Fleece. I kept my promise.

The statue EYES glow. HERA (ageless, powerful, beautiful and terrible) appears.

HERA
You kept your promise. But you broke a greater one.

JASON
What promise?

HERA
The covenant of the Fleece. It was not meant for mortals.

JASON
Then why make it achievable?

HERA
To test you. (beat) And you passed. But the test is not over.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE GODDESS'S OFFER

INT. TEMPLE OF HERA - CONTINUOUS

Hera circles Jason. Her presence fills the temple.

HERA
I can give you the throne. Without war. Without blood. Pelias will die in his sleep. You will rule peacefully.

JASON
What's the price?

HERA
Medea. She is a priestess of Hecate. My enemy. Give her to me.

JASON
No.

HERA
(surprised)
No? You barely know her.

JASON
She saved my life. She betrayed her father for me. I won't betray her.

HERA
Then you choose the hard path. (beat) War. Blood. The death of everyone you love.

JASON
I choose the right path.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: MEDEA'S DREAM

INT. JASON'S HOUSE - NIGHT

Medea sleeps. She DREAMS. Hecate appears — three-faced, terrible, beautiful.

HECATE
Daughter. You have chosen a mortal over a goddess.

MEDEA
(in the dream)
I chose love over power.

HECATE
Love is power. The most dangerous kind. (beat) But you have made an enemy of Hera. She will destroy you.

MEDEA
Then I'll fight her.

HECATE
You cannot fight a goddess.

MEDEA
I can try.

HECATE
(smiles)
That's why I chose you.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE CONFRONTATION

INT. JASON'S HOUSE - DAY

Jason tells Medea about Hera's offer.

MEDEA
You refused?

JASON
Of course.

MEDEA
She'll kill us both.

JASON
Then we die together.

MEDEA
(touches his face)
You're a fool.

JASON
I'm a king.

MEDEA
Not yet.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE PLOT

INT. PELIAS'S PALACE - NIGHT

Pelias meets with his daughters. Three of them: ALCESTIS, PISIDICE, and the youngest, also named ALCIMEDE.

PELIAS
Jason has the Fleece. He'll come for the throne.

ALCESTIS
Then give it to him. He's earned it.

PELIAS
I don't give what I can take. (beat) And I don't surrender what's mine.

PISIDICE
What do you want us to do?

PELIAS
Kill him.

Silence. The daughters stare.

ALCESTIS
He's our cousin.

PELIAS
He's my enemy. (beat) And he'll be your problem when I'm gone.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE POISON

INT. JASON'S HOUSE - NIGHT

Pelias's youngest daughter, ALCIMEDE, arrives at Jason's door. She carries a gift: wine.

ALCIMEDE
From my father. A peace offering.

Jason takes the cup. Medea watches from the shadows.

MEDEA
(quietly)
Don't drink it.

JASON
(to Alcimede)
Tell your father I accept his peace.

He drinks. Medea's face FALLS.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE ANTIDOTE

INT. JASON'S HOUSE - LATER

Jason COLLAPSES. The poison works fast. Medea catches him.

MEDEA
You fool! You absolute fool!

She runs to her supplies. Mixes herbs. Her hands shake.

HERACLES
(entering)
What happened?

MEDEA
Poison. Pelias's daughter brought it.

HERACLES
Will he live?

MEDEA
If I'm fast enough.

She pours the antidote down Jason's throat. He CHOKES. Gasps. Opens his eyes.

JASON
(weakly)
Did it work?

MEDEA
(crying)
You're alive, you idiot.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE DAUGHTER'S CHOICE

INT. PELIAS'S PALACE - NIGHT

Alcimede returns. Pelias waits.

PELIAS
Is he dead?

ALCESTIS
(entering)
No. The witch saved him.

Pelias's face DARKENS.

PELIAS
Then we do this the old way.

ALCESTIS
Father, stop. He has the Fleece. He has the goddess's favor. He has the people.

PELIAS
He has nothing. (beat) Tomorrow, I challenge him. Single combat. King to king.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE CHALLENGE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The square is packed. Pelias stands in armor. Jason approaches, still weak from poison.

PELIAS
Single combat. Winner takes the throne.

JASON
You're an old man.

PELIAS
I'm a king. (beat) And you're a boy who got lucky.

They circle each other. The crowd watches.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE DUEL

EXT. MARKET SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Pelias ATTACKS. Fast for an old man. His sword cuts Jason's arm.

Jason DEFENDS. He's weak. Slow. The poison still in his blood.

Pelias PRESSES. Drives Jason back.

PELIAS
You should have died from the poison!

JASON
I don't die easily.

PELIAS
You'll die today!

He strikes. Jason PARRIES. Their swords LOCK.

JASON (CONT'D)
(quietly)
My father. Where is he?

PELIAS
Dead. I killed him myself. Twenty years ago.

Jason's face. The truth. His father is dead.

Something BREAKS in Jason. And something ELSE ignites.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE KILLING

EXT. MARKET SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Jason ROARS. Strikes with force Pelias didn't expect. Their swords clash.

Jason DISARMS Pelias. The old king's sword flies.

Pelias falls. Jason stands over him.

PELIAS
(quietly)
Do it. Kill me. Become me.

Jason looks at the sword. At Pelias. At the crowd.

JASON
No.

He drops the sword.

JASON (CONT'D)
I'm not you.

He turns. Walks away. The crowd parts.

Pelias lies in the dirt. Defeated. Alive. Worse than dead.

HERACLES
(quietly, to Jason)
You should have killed him.

JASON
I know. (beat) But I'm not a murderer. Not yet.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 05
