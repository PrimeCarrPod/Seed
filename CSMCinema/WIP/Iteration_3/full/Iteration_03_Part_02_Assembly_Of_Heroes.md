# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 02: ASSEMBLY OF HEROES
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 02 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE HERALDS GATHER

FADE IN:

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE GATES - DAWN

The square is now CROWDED. Word has spread. Young men from every corner of Greece gather — heroes, warriors, dreamers. Some carry swords. Some carry nothing but hope.

JASON stands at the center, the Golden Fleece promise hanging over him like a curse.

HERACLES (30, massive, scarred, the greatest hero of his generation) approaches. The crowd parts.

HERACLES
You're the one who wants to die in Colchis?

JASON
I'm the one who wants to reclaim my father's throne.

HERACLES
(thrown sandal on the ground)
Same thing. (beat) I'm coming.

Jason looks at him. Something shifts.

JASON
Why?

HERACLES
Because I'm bored. And because one-sandal fools who walk into death are the only honest men left.

Behind them, more heroes gather. CASTOR and POLLUX (twin brothers, mirror images) step forward.

CASTOR
Our sister Helen speaks of you.

POLLUX
She says you're either very brave or very stupid.

JASON
Which do you think?

CASTOR and POLLUX (together)
Yes.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE SHIPBUILDER

EXT. ATHENS - SHIPYARDS - DAY

ARGOS (60, blind in one eye, greatest shipbuilder alive) runs his hand over timber. Around him, apprentices work in silence.

Jason enters with Heracles.

ARGOS
I know why you're here.

JASON
Can you build a ship that survives the Clashing Rocks?

ARGOS
I can build a ship that survives anything. (beat) But I need fifty oarsmen. The best in Greece.

JASON
They're gathering.

ARGOS
Then I'll build your ship. But I sail with you.

JASON
You're blind.

ARGOS
One eye sees the world. The other sees what's coming. (beat) I see what's coming.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE ORACLE SPEAKS

EXT. DELPHI - TEMPLE OF APOLLO - NIGHT

The PYTHIA (ageless, eyes white with prophecy) stands over smoking fissures. Jason approaches alone.

PYTHIA
Jason, son of Aeson. You seek the Fleece.

JASON
I seek my father's throne.

PYTHIA
Your father is alive. (beat) In Colchis. Imprisoned by Aeëtes. The Fleece is his chains.

Jason's face changes. Everything he believed shifts.

JASON
My uncle said he died in exile.

PYTHIA
Your uncle lies. (beat) The Fleece is not gold. It is truth. And truth will destroy you or make you king.

She turns away. The smoke thickens.

PYTHIA (CONT'D)
Beware the king's daughter. She will save you. She will betray you. She will be both.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE HEROES ASSEMBLE

EXT. ATHENS - HARBOR - SUNSET

FIFTY HEROES stand before the unfinished ship. The ARGOS. Named for its builder.

ORPHEUS (25, lyre in hand, voice that calms storms) plays. The heroes listen.

ORPHEUS (singing)
We who are about to sail
Into the jaws of the unknown
Carry Greece on our backs
And Greece carries us

ASCLEPIUS (40, healer, hands that cure death) counts supplies.

ATALANTA (20, fastest woman alive, bow on back) sharpens arrows.

THESEUS (25, slayer of the Minotaur) sharpens his sword.

Jason stands before them. The weight of command visible.

JASON
I won't lie to you. Most of us won't return.

HERACLES
We know.

JASON
Then why come?

HERACLES
Because we're heroes. And heroes die for stories worth telling.

The heroes CHEER. The ship is launched.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE FAREWELL

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE - NIGHT

PELIAS watches from his balcony as the torches of the Argos light the harbor.

ADVISOR
They'll never return.

PELIAS
(quietly)
That's what I'm counting on.

PELIAS turns to his advisor. Something cruel in his eyes.

PELIAS (CONT'D)
If they do return — kill Jason. Not the others. Just him.

ADVISOR
The heroes won't allow it.

PELIAS
Heroes die. (beat) I should know. I've killed enough of them.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE ARGOS SAILS

EXT. AEGEAN SEA - DAY

The ARGOS cuts through blue water. Fifty oars in perfect rhythm. Jason stands at the prow.

Heracles joins him.

HERACLES
You've been quiet since Delphi.

JASON
The oracle told me my father is alive.

HERACLES
That's good news.

JASON
The oracle told me the Fleece is not what I think it is.

HERACLES
It never is.

ATALANTA approaches.

ATALANTA
The men are restless. They want to know the plan.

JASON
Sail east. Survive the Clashing Rocks. Find the Fleece.

ATALANTA
That's not a plan. That's a prayer.

JASON
Then pray with me.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE FIRST TRIAL

EXT. AEGEAN SEA - ISLAND OF LEMNOS - DAY

The Argos anchors at an island of WOMEN. All women. No men.

The QUEEN HYPPSIPYLE (40, regal, wary) approaches in a boat.

HYPPSIPYLE
No men have set foot on this island in a generation.

JASON
We need supplies.

HYPPSIPYLE
We have everything we need. (beat) Including answers to questions you haven't asked.

JASON
What questions?

HYPSSIPYLE
Why you're really sailing east.

The women on shore watch. The heroes shift uncomfortably.

HERACLES
(quietly, to Jason)
This island is cursed. Take your supplies and go.

JASON
(to Hypsipyle)
We'll trade. Work for supplies.

HYPPSIPYLE
Work? (laughs) The last men who came here wanted more than supplies.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE CLASHING ROCKS

EXT. BOSPORUS - THE SYMPLEGades - DAY

The Clashing Rocks. Two cliffs that CRASH together, crushing anything between.

Jason stands at the prow. The rocks THUNDER.

JASON
Orpheus. Play.

Orpheus plays. The music fills the air. The rocks pause — just for a heartbeat.

JASON (CONT'D)
NOW!

The heroes ROW. The Argos shoots forward. The rocks CRASH behind them — missing the ship by inches.

The ship emerges into calm water. The Black Sea.

HERACLES
(breathing hard)
That was too close.

JASON
That was the easy part.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE BLACK SEA

EXT. BLACK SEA - DAY

The Argos sails into dark water. The heroes are silent. Everything has changed.

ORPHEUS
The songs change here. Listen.

The heroes listen. The wind sounds different. The waves speak a different language.

ASCLEPIUS
We're in the world's edge now.

JASON
No. We're in the world's beginning.

Atalanta points ahead.

ATALANTA
Land.

On the horizon: COLCHIS. Golden domes. Towers of bronze. The kingdom of Aeëtes.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE APPROACH

EXT. COLCHIS - HARBOR - SUNSET

The Argos anchors in a foreign harbor. Guards watch from the walls. The heroes prepare.

JASON
We go ashore under truce. Request audience with the king.

HERACLES
He'll kill you.

JASON
He'll try. (beat) But he won't. Not yet.

ATALANTA
How do you know?

JASON
Because I'm going to offer him something he wants more than my life.

HERACLES
What's that?

JASON
Me.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE GATES OF COLCHIS

EXT. COLCHIS - PALACE GATES - NIGHT

The bronze gates OPEN. Jason walks through alone. The heroes watch from the ship.

The gates CLOSE behind him.

HERACLES
(picking up his club)
He's dead.

ATALANTA
Not yet.

HERACLES
Give it time.

On the ship, the heroes wait. The torches of Colchis burn like eyes in the dark.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 02
