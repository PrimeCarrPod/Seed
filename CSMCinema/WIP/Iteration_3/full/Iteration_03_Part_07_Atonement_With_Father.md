# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 07: ATONEMENT WITH FATHER
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 07 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE TOMB

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF POSEIDON - DAWN

Jason stands before a TOMB. His father's name carved in stone. But the tomb is EMPTY.

MEDEA
He's not here.

JASON
Pelias said he killed him.

MEDEA
Pelias lied. (beat) Your father is alive.

JASON
How do you know?

MEDEA
Hecate told me. (beat) He's in Athens. Aegeus has been hiding him.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE JOURNEY

EXT. ROAD TO ATHENS - DAY

Jason and Medea ride toward Athens. Behind them: Heracles, Orpheus, Atalanta.

HERACLES
Aegeus won't give him up peacefully.

JASON
I know.

ATALANTA
So we fight?

JASON
No. We talk. (beat) And if talking fails, we fight.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: ATHENS

EXT. ATHENS - PALACE - DAY

AEGEUS (50, king of Athens, weary, heirless) receives Jason in his throne room.

AEGEUS
Jason of Iolcus. The Fleece-retriever.

JASON
Aegeus of Athens. The father-hider.

AEGEUS
(sharp)
I hide no one.

JASON
My father. Aeson. He's here.

Silence. Aegeus's face changes.

AEGEUS
Who told you?

JASON
The gods. (beat) And the truth.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE TRUTH

INT. ATHENS - PRIVATE CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Aegeus and Jason alone.

AEGEUS
Your father came to me twenty years ago. Broken. Exiled. Pelias had taken everything.

JASON
Why didn't you help him?

AEGEUS
I did. I hid him. Protected him. (beat) And I used him.

JASON
How?

AEGEUS
As leverage. Against Pelias. Against Iolcus. (beat) Your father was my insurance policy.

JASON
Where is he?

AEGEUS
Safe. (beat) But I won't give him up without a price.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE PRICE

INT. ATHENS - THRONE ROOM - DAY

Aegeus addresses Jason before the Athenian court.

AEGEUS
I have your father. He is safe. He is healthy. (beat) And I will return him to you.

JASON
The price?

AEGEUS
The Fleece. (beat) Give me the Fleece, and you can have your father.

The court murmurs. Jason's heroes tense.

JASON
The Fleece is not mine to give. It belongs to the gods.

AEGEUS
Then get it from the gods. (beat) Or your father dies.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE DECISION

INT. ATHENS - GUEST CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Jason paces. Medea watches.

MEDEA
You can't give him the Fleece.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
And you can't let your father die.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
Then what will you do?

JASON
Something stupid. (beat) I'm going to get my father out.

MEDEA
How?

JASON
I'll break into Aegeus's dungeons. Tonight.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE BREAKOUT

INT. ATHENS - DUNGEONS - NIGHT

Jason, Heracles, and Atalanta sneak through the dungeons. Guards sleep — Orpheus's music from outside.

They find AESON (70, gray, broken but alive) in a cell.

JASON
Father.

AESON
(looking up)
Jason?

JASON
I'm getting you out.

AESON
I'm too old to run.

JASON
Then we'll walk.

Heracles BREAKS the cell door. They lift Aeson.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE ESCAPE

EXT. ATHENS - HARBOR - NIGHT

The Argos (brought from Iolcus) waits. They carry Aeson aboard.

Aegeus appears with guards.

AEGEUS
Thief! You steal from a king!

JASON
I reclaim what's mine. (beat)
My father. My blood. My right.

AEGEUS
I'll hunt you to the ends of the earth!

JASON
Then hunt. (beat)
But I'm taking my father home.

The ship sails. Aegeus watches from the dock.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE REUNION

INT. ARGOS - DAY

Jason sits with his father. Aeson is weak but alert.

AESON
You have your mother's eyes.

JASON
I don't remember her.

AESON
She died giving you life. (beat)
Pelias killed her. Slowly. To hurt me.

JASON
Pelias is dead.

AESON
Good. (beat)
But killing him doesn't bring her back.

JASON
No. (beat)
But it brings justice.

AESON
Justice. (laughs bitterly)
There's no justice. Only power.

JASON
Then I'll have power. And I'll use it better than Pelias.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE WISDOM

EXT. ARGOS - NIGHT

Aeson and Jason at the prow. Stars above.

AESON
The Fleece. What is it really?

JASON
A covenant. Between gods and men.

AESON
And you broke it.

JASON
I fulfilled it.

AESON
Same thing. (beat)
The gods don't like when mortals fulfill covenants. It makes them obsolete.

JASON
Then let them be obsolete.

AESON
(looks at his son)
You're braver than I was. Or more foolish.

JASON
Maybe both.

AESON
Good. (beat)
Kings need both.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - SUNSET

The Argos docks. The crowd cheers. Jason helps his father ashore.

AESON
(looking at Iolcus)
I never thought I'd see it again.

JASON
You're home.

AESON
No. (looks at Jason)
You're home. I'm just visiting.

Jason kneels before his father.

JASON
Teach me. How to be a king.

AESON
(lifts him up)
You already know. (beat)
You just don't trust it yet.

They walk together toward the palace. The FLEECE glows behind them.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 07
