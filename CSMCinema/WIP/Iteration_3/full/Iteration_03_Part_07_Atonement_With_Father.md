# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 07: ATONEMENT WITH FATHER
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 07 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE HARBOR

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo docks. Silent. The harbor is empty. No welcome. No celebration.

The crew disembarks. Jason and Medea last. The fleece wrapped in cloth.

ATALANTA
Where is everyone?

ORPHEUS
Hiding.

ZETES
Or running.

CALAIS
Or arming.

JASON
(to the crew)
Stay with the ship. (to Medea) Come with me.

She writes:

*ALWAYS.*

They walk toward the palace. The streets are empty. Doors closed. Windows shuttered.

MEDEA writes:

*THEY'RE AFRAID.*

JASON
Of what?

She writes:

*OF YOU. (beat) YOU COME BACK DIFFERENT. EVERYONE CAN SEE IT.*

JASON
I'm still me.

She writes:

*NO. YOU'RE NOT. (beat) AND THAT'S GOOD.*

---

# PIECE 2: THE PALACE

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

The throne room. Fifty warriors line the walls. PELIAS sits on the throne. Older. Grayer. But the same paranoid eyes.

Jason enters. Medea beside him. The fleece in his hands.

Silence.

PELIAS
Nephew. You're alive.

JASON
Disappointed?

PELIAS
Surprised. (beat) The fleece. You actually retrieved it.

JASON
I actually retrieved it.

PELIAS
(showing nothing)
Then you've fulfilled the quest. The throne is—

JASON
(interrupting)
Before we discuss the throne. I want to talk about my father.

PELIAS
(carefully)
Your father died in exile. Tragic.

JASON
My father was murdered. By you.

The warriors tense. Hands on weapons.

PELIAS
(standing)
That's a serious accusation.

JASON
It's a true one.

PELIAS
Prove it.

JASON
I don't need to prove it. You know it. I know it. The gods know it. (beat) And that's enough.

---

# PIECE 3: THE CONFESSION

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pelias looks at his warriors. At Jason. At Medea.

PELIAS
(to the guards)
Leave us.

GUARD CAPTAIN
My lord—

PELIAS
Leave. Us.

The guards withdraw. The doors close. Just Jason, Medea, and Pelias.

PELIAS
(sitting back down)
What do you want?

JASON
Justice.

PELIAS
Justice? (laughing bitterly) You want justice? From me?

JASON
From the universe. From the pattern. From whatever makes things right.

PELIAS
Nothing makes things right, boy. Things just are. (beat) Your father was weak. The kingdom needed strength. I took it. That's all.

JASON
That's all? You stole his throne. Sent him to die in exile. Stole my childhood.

PELIAS
And gave you something better. I gave you a quest. A purpose. A story. (beat) Without me — you'd be nothing. A prince without a throne. A footnote. Instead — you're the man who stole the fleece from Colchis. The man who sailed the Symplegades. The man who—

JASON
The man who had to become a hero because you made him one.

Silence.

PELIAS
(quietly)
Yes. (beat) And you hate me for it.

JASON
(long pause)
I don't hate you.

PELIAS
(surprised)
No?

JASON
You stole from me. But you also made me. (beat) I don't know how to feel about that.

---

# PIECE 4: THE CHOICE

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

MEDEA writes:

*JASON. YOU DON'T HAVE TO FORGIVE HIM.*

JASON
I know.

She writes:

*BUT YOU DON'T HAVE TO KILL HIM EITHER.*

JASON
I know.

PELIAS
(to Medea)
The priestess advises mercy?

She writes:

*THE PRIESTESS ADVISES CHOICE.*

PELIAS
Choice. (bitterly) I never had choice. The throne demanded I take it. The kingdom demanded I hold it. The gods demanded I— (stopping) —demanded I become a monster.

JASON
You had choice. Every day. Every moment. You chose the throne over your brother. Over your nephew. Over your soul.

PELIAS
And you? What will you choose? The throne? Or your soul?

JASON
(long pause)
I choose... neither.

PELIAS
(confused)
Neither?

JASON
The throne is yours. Keep it.

MEDEA writes:

*WHAT?*

PELIAS
(shocked)
You're... giving it to me?

JASON
I'm not giving you anything. You already took it. I'm just — not taking it back. (beat) I came here for revenge. For justice. For my father. But the truth is — killing you won't bring him back. It won't give me my childhood. It won't make anything right.

PELIAS
Then what was the point?

JASON
The point was the quest. The becoming. The— (looking at Medea) —the living.

---

# PIECE 5: THE FATHER'S RESPONSE

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pelias stares. Then — slowly — laughs. Not mocking. Broken.

PELIAS
You're better than me. Better than your father. Better than any of us.

JASON
I'm just tired.

PELIAS
No. You're wise. (beat) The throne has made me old and paranoid and alone. You walked away from it and you're free.

JASON
I have something better than a throne.

PELIAS
The fleece?

JASON
(looking at Medea)
Purpose.

MEDEA writes:

*AND LOVE.*

PELIAS
(seeing them together)
Yes. That. (beat) I never had that. I had the throne. And the throne was enough. Until it wasn't.

JASON
It's not too late.

PELIAS
(shaking his head)
It's been too late for twenty years. (beat) But maybe — for you — it's not.

He stands. Removes the crown. Looks at it.

PELIAS (CONT'D)
I'm tired, Jason. Tired of sitting. Tired of suspecting. Tired of being alone. (beat) Take it.

JASON
No.

PELIAS
I'm not offering. I'm begging. (beat) Take the throne. Rule. Be better than me.

JASON
(quietly)
I have somewhere else to be.

PELIAS
Where?

JASON
Wherever the pattern leads.

---

# PIECE 6: THE BLESSING

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pelias looks at Jason. At Medea. At the fleece.

PELIAS
Then take my blessing. (beat) You have it. Freely given. (beat) Jason of Iolcus — you are more king than I ever was. Not because of the throne. Because of what you chose.

JASON
Thank you.

PELIAS
No. Thank you. (beat) You could have killed me. You had the right. The reason. The— (stopping) —you had everything except the will. And that's what makes you better.

MEDEA writes:

*HE'S RIGHT. (beat) YOU CHOSE MERCY. THAT'S HARDER THAN REVENGE.*

JASON
(reading)
I learned from someone.

She writes:

*WHO?*

JASON
You.

She writes:

*I NEVER TAUGHT YOU MERCY.*

JASON
You taught me everything else. Mercy was implied.

---

# PIECE 7: THE THRONE ROOM DOORS OPEN

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

The doors burst open. The guard captain enters.

GUARD CAPTAIN
My lord! The people — they're gathering. Outside. They want to see the fleece. They want to see—

He stops. Sees Pelias without the crown. Jason standing where a conqueror should stand.

GUARD CAPTAIN (CONT'D)
What happened?

PELIAS
The Greek chose mercy. (beat) And I chose to accept it.

GUARD CAPTAIN
But — the throne—

PELIAS
Stays mine. For now. (beat) But the Greek has my blessing. And my gratitude. Treat him as you would treat me.

GUARD CAPTAIN
(confused but nodding)
Yes, my lord.

---

# PIECE 8: THE HARBOR

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The entire town has gathered. The Argo in the harbor. The crew on deck.

Jason and Medea emerge from the palace. The crowd parts.

Jason unwraps the fleece. Gold light floods the harbor.

The crowd gasps. Then — silence. Then — cheers.

PELIAS
(from the palace steps, calling)
Jason of Iolcus! You sailed to the ends of the earth! You stole the fleece from the dragon! You returned! (beat) You are the greatest hero Iolcus has ever known!

The crowd roars.

Jason looks at Medea. She writes:

*THIS IS YOUR MOMENT.*

JASON
(reading)
Ours.

She writes:

*I DON'T NEED MOMENTS. (beat) I NEED YOU.*

JASON
You have me.

She writes:

*THEN THAT'S ENOUGH.*

---

# PIECE 9: THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

The celebration continues. Wine. Music. Dancing.

Jason sits with the crew. His crew. His family.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
What now?

JASON
I don't know. The quest is complete. The fleece is home. Pelias is— (surprised) —alive.

ATALANTA
Disappointed?

JASON
Relieved.

ZETES
What will you do?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever comes next.

CALAIS
That's not an answer.

JASON
It's the only one I have.

MEDEA writes:

*THE PATTERN CONTINUES.*

JASON
(reading)
Yes. It does.

ATALANTA
So we just — wait? For the next adventure?

JASON
We don't wait. We live. (beat) The pattern will find us.

---

# PIECE 10: THE PATTERN SPEAKS

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - LATER

The celebration winds down. Jason and Medea walk the harbor alone.

JASON
I thought I'd feel different. After the fleece. After Pelias. After everything.

MEDEA writes:

*DIFFERENT HOW?*

JASON
Complete. Finished. (beat) But I don't. I feel like — this is just the beginning.

She writes:

*BECAUSE IT IS. (beat) THE FLEECE WAS ITERATION TWO. THERE ARE FIVE MORE. SEVEN ITERATIONS. ELEVEN PARTS EACH. SEVENTY-SEVEN PARTS TOTAL.*

JASON
(smiling)
You sound like Hecate.

She writes:

*I'LL TAKE THAT AS A COMPLIMENT.*

JASON
It is one.

She writes:

*JASON. (beat) WHAT WE HAVE — THIS — IT'S NOT JUST THE PATTERN.*

JASON
I know.

She writes:

*I NEED YOU TO KNOW. I DIDN'T CHOOSE YOU BECAUSE THE GODDESS COMMANDED. I CHOSE YOU BECAUSE— (stopping, unable to write it)

JASON
Because?

She writes:

*BECAUSE WHEN I SAW YOU — FOR ONE MOMENT — THE PATTERN WASN'T FATE. IT WAS CHOICE. AND I CHOSE.*

JASON
(quietly)
So did I.

She writes:

*THEN WE'RE BOTH FREE.*

JASON
And both bound.

She writes:

*SAME THING.*

---

# PIECE 11: THE HORIZON

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAWN

The celebration is over. The crew sleeps. Jason and Medea sit at the water's edge.

MEDEA writes:
*WHAT HAPPENS NOW?*

JASON
We rest. We heal. We prepare.

She writes:
*FOR WHAT?*

JASON
For iteration three.

She writes:
*THIS IS ITERATION THREE.*

JASON
(smiling)
Then for iteration four.

She writes:
*YOU'RE SERIOUS. (beat) YOU WANT TO CONTINUE?*

JASON
Don't you?

She writes:
*I ASKED FIRST.*

JASON
Yes. I want to continue. (beat) The fleece was the beginning. The quest was the becoming. But there's more. I can feel it.

She writes:
*THE PATTERN.*

JASON
The pattern.

They sit in silence. The sun rises. The harbor wakes. The crew stirs.

ORPHEUS
(from the ship)
Jason! Medea! You should see this!

They walk to the Argo. The crew is gathered around—

A MAP. Old. Faded. Showing lands beyond Colchis. Beyond Greece. Beyond anything they've seen.

ZETES
Where did you find this?

ORPHEUS
In the fleece. (beat) It was folded into the fleece. Hidden.

CALAIS
Hidden by who?

ORPHEUS
By whoever put the fleece in Colchis. (beat) It's a map. To the next quest.

ATALANTA
(sharply)
There's always a next quest.

JASON
(looking at Medea)
Is that okay?

She writes:
*ASK ME AFTER WE FINISH THIS ONE.*

JASON
We finished it last night.

She writes:
*THEN ASK ME NOW.*

JASON
Okay?

She writes:
*YES. (beat) ALWAYS YES. (beat) THE PATTERN CONTINUES.*

JASON
(to the crew)
Rest. Recover. We sail in one week.

The crew cheers.

The sun rises higher. The Argo waits. The map spreads across the deck.

The pattern continues.
