# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 11: MAGIC FLIGHT RETURN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 11 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE BATTLE

EXT. COLCHIS - OUTSKIRTS - DAY

Jason fights Pelias's army. Alone. Desperate.

He's outnumbered. But not outmatched.

HERACLES (bursting from the trees)
You didn't think we'd let you have all the fun?

ATALANTA
(arriving with arrows)
We're heroes. We go where the story takes us.

ORPHEUS
(playing his lyre)
And the song isn't over yet.

The heroes CRASH into the army. The tide turns.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE PALACE

INT. COLCHIS - THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

Jason bursts in. Pelias waits. The FLEECE behind him.

PELIAS
You came.

JASON
I came for the Fleece.

PELIAS
It's mine. The covenant is mine. The power is mine.

JASON
The Fleece doesn't belong to anyone. (beat)
That's what you never understood.

PELIAS
Then I'll destroy it!

He grabs the Fleece. It BURNS. He SCREAMS.

The Fleece is NOT a weapon. It's a covenant. And covenants cannot be taken by force.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE FALL

INT. THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pelias FALLS. The Fleece GOLDS around him. Consuming him.

PELIAS
What's happening?!

JASON
The covenant is judging you.

PELIAS
I did everything for power!

JASON
That's why you failed.

Pelias DISSOLVES. Into light. Into nothing.

The FLEECE falls to the ground. Gently. Waiting.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE CHOICE

INT. THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Jason stands before the Fleece. He could take it. Become invincible. Become a god.

MEDEA (O.S.)
Don't.

Medea enters. She followed him.

MEDEA
Don't take it. Not again.

JASON
I wasn't going to.

MEDEA
Good. (beat)
Because we have a kingdom to get back to.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE RENEWAL

EXT. SACRED GROVE - DAY

Jason returns the Fleece to the sacred oak. But this time, he does it RIGHT.

He speaks the words Medea taught him. The old words. The true words.

The FLEECE BLAZES. The covenant is renewed. Not broken. Not stolen. RENEWED.

The light SPREADS. Across Colchis. Across Greece. Across the world.

A new age begins.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE RETURN

EXT. AEGEAN SEA - DAY

The heroes sail home. Jason at the prow. Medea beside him.

HERACLES
What now?

JASON
We go home. (beat)
We rule. We live.

ATALANTA
That's it? No more adventures?

JASON
(laughing)
There are always more adventures. (beat)
But first, home.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE HOMECOMING

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - SUNSET

The ship docks. The people CHEER. Aeson waits on the dock.

Jason steps ashore. His father embraces him.

AESON
You did it.

JASON
We did it.

AESON
No. (beat)
You did. (beat)
I'm proud of you.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE THRONE

INT. THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason sits the throne. Medea at his side. The heroes before him.

HERACLES
So. What's next?

JASON
Next? (beat)
We build. We grow. We protect.

ATALANTA
That sounds boring.

JASON
It is. (beat)
Boring is what heroes fight for.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE CELEBRATION

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - NIGHT

The people CELEBRATE. Music. Dance. Joy.

Jason and Medea walk among them. Not above them. Among them.

MEDEA
You're a good king.

JASON
I'm trying.

MEDEA
That's what makes you good.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE FUTURE

EXT. PALACE BALCONY - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. Stars above.

MEDEA
What do you see? In the future?

JASON
Children. (beat)
Grandchildren. (beat)
A Greece united not by war, but by covenant.

MEDEA
That's a nice dream.

JASON
It's not a dream. (beat)
It's a promise.

MEDEA
To whom?

JASON
To everyone. (beat)
To the gods. To the people. To us.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE FINAL IMAGE

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE - DAWN

The sun rises over Iolcus. The city GLOWS.

Jason stands on the balcony. Medea beside him. The ring on his finger GLOWS softly.

HERA (V.O.)
The hero returns. Not with gold. Not with glory.

The sun rises higher.

HERA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
But with something far more valuable.

Jason takes Medea's hand.

HERA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
Understanding.

They stand together. Watching the sun rise on a new age.

HERA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
The Fleece was never the prize. The journey was the prize. The transformation was the prize.

The sun FILLS the screen.

HERA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
And the story continues.

FADE TO BLACK.
