# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 11: MAGIC FLIGHT RETURN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 11 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay

# PIECE 1: THE HYPERBOREAN COAST

EXT. HYPERBOREA - COAST - DAY

Snow. Ice. A land where the sun never fully sets. The Argo beaches on white sand.

ATALANTA
(from above)
No people. No smoke. No signs of life.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
The Hyperboreans live underground. Away from the wind.

ZETES
How do you know that?

ORPHEUS
The songs remember. (beat) The Hyperboreans are old. Older than the gods. They remember when the world was young.

CALAIS
And they don't like visitors.

ORPHEUS
They don't like anything that changes. (beat) And visitors always change things.

MEDEA writes:

*THE LOOM IS BELOW. (beat) UNDER THE ICE. UNDER THE STONE. UNDER EVERYTHING.*

JASON
Can you feel it?

She writes:

*ALWAYS. (beat) IT'S CALLING.*

---

# PIECE 2: THE DESCENT

EXT. HYPERBOREA - ICE CAVE - DAY

The crew descends. Into the ice. Into the stone. Into the dark.

The walls glow faintly. Not with fire. With thread. The threads of the loom, visible through the rock.

PERICLYMENUS
The whole mountain is—

MEDEA writes:

*WOVEN. (beat) THE HYPERBOREANS WOVE THE MOUNTAIN INTO EXISTENCE. (beat) THEY WOVE EVERYTHING.*

The tunnel opens. A vast cavern. And in the center — a loom. Smaller than the Loom of the World. But older. More intricate.

Three FIGURES tend it. Ancient. Neither male nor female. Neither young nor old. The HYPERBOREANS.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(without turning)
You came.

JASON
You were expecting us?

HYPERBOREAN ONE
The pattern told us. (beat) It tells us everything. Eventually.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
(turning)
You seek the loom.

JASON
I seek understanding.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
(turning)
Understanding is not ours to give.

JASON
Then whose is it?

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(smiling)
Yours. (beat) You already have it. You just don't know yet.

---

# PIECE 3: THE TEST

INT. HYPERBOREAN CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

The Hyperboreans gesture to the loom.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
Touch it.

JASON
What happens?

HYPERBOREAN THREE
You'll see.

Jason approaches the loom. The threads hum. Not with sound. With recognition.

He touches it.

The world INVERTS. Inside becomes outside. Outside becomes inside. Jason sees—

EVERYTHING. Every thread. Every pattern. Every choice. Every life. All at once. All simultaneous. All—

He falls.

---

# PIECE 4: THE VISION

INT. THE PATTERN - TIMELESS

Jason floats in the pattern. Threads everywhere. Medea's voice:

MEDEA
(in the pattern)
Jason. Breathe.

JASON
I can't—

MEDEA
You can. (beat) Just — focus on one thread. One pattern. One choice.

Jason focuses. One thread. Gold. His own.

He sees: himself. As a boy. With Chiron. Learning to fight. Learning to lead. Learning to—

The vision shifts. He sees: himself. As an old man. Sitting by a fire. Telling stories. The story of the fleece. The story of the crew. The story of—

The vision shifts. He sees: himself. Now. Here. Choosing.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
The pattern isn't fate. (beat) It's possibility. Every thread — a choice. Every choice — a pattern. Every pattern — a life.

JASON
I see it.

MEDEA
Then come back.

---

# PIECE 5: THE RETURN

INT. HYPERBOREAN CAVERN - DAY

Jason opens his eyes. The crew around him. Medea holding his hand.

JASON
(breathing hard)
I saw it. The pattern. The whole—

HYPERBOREAN ONE
And?

JASON
It's beautiful. (beat) And terrifying. And— (looking at Medea) —and ours. (beat) The pattern doesn't belong to the gods. Or the Titans. Or you. It belongs to everyone. Every choice. Every person. Every—

HYPERBOREAN TWO
(nodding)
Now you understand.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
The loom is not a tool. (beat) It's a mirror. (beat) You see yourself. You see your choices. You see what you could become.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
And what will you become?

JASON
(standing)
I don't know. (beat) But I'll choose. That's the point. Not fate. Choice.

---

# PIECE 6: THE GIFT

INT. HYPERBOREAN CAVERN - CONTINUOUS

The Hyperboreans gesture to the loom. A single thread detaches. Silver. Glowing.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Take this.

JASON
What is it?

HYPERBOREAN Two
Memory. (beat) The memory of the pattern. Everything you saw. Everything you understood. (beat) When you forget — hold the thread. Remember.

Jason takes the thread. It wraps around his wrist. Next to the golden one.

JASON
Thank you.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
Don't thank us. (beat) We didn't give you anything. (beat) You already had it. We just helped you see.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Go now. (beat) The pattern continues. And you — with it.

---

# PIECE 7: THE ASCENT

EXT. HYPERBOREA - ICE CAVE - DAY

The crew ascends. Back to the light. Back to the snow. Back to the Argo.

ATALANTA
(from above)
What did you find?

JASON
Myself. (beat) And the pattern. (beat) And the difference.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Which is?

JASON
The pattern is what is. Choice is what could be. (beat) The gods want us to believe they're the same. They're not.

ZETES
So what do we do?

JASON
We choose. (beat) Every day. Every moment. Every—

CALAIS
Every thread?

JASON
(smiling)
Every thread.

---

# PIECE 8: THE FINAL LOOM

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAY

The crew boards. The Argo prepares to sail.

Medea stands at the bow. Looking north. More looms. More patterns. More quests.

MEDEA
There are five more looms.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
Each one harder. Each one deeper. Each one—

JASON
I know. (beat) Are you ready?

MEDEA
(turning to him)
I've been ready since I first saw you. (beat) The question is — are you?

JASON
(looking at the crew)
I have everything I need.

MEDEA
The crew?

JASON
And you.

MEDEA
(smiling)
I'm not a 'and.' I'm a 'with.'

JASON
Then — with you. Always.

---

# PIECE 9: THE SAILING

EXT. OPEN SEA - SUNSET

The Argo sails. The Hyperboreans fade behind them. The next loom ahead.

The crew rows. Orpheus plays. The threads around Jason's wrists gleam.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Where to?

JASON
(looking at the map)
West. (beat) To the loom beneath the sea. (beat) To the place where Poseidon keeps his secrets.

CALAIS
Poseidon will be angry.

JASON
Poseidon is always angry. (beat) But he's also fair. (beat) We gave him payment once. He'll remember.

MEDEA
And if he doesn't?

JASON
Then we'll negotiate. (beat) That's what heroes do.

ATALANTA
(smiling)
Negotiate with gods?

JASON
Better than fighting them.

---

# PIECE 10: THE PATTERN COMPLETE

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The stars reflect on the water. Above and below — infinite.

Jason and Medea at the bow. The crew behind them. The looms ahead.

MEDEA
Iteration three. (beat) How does it feel?

JASON
Like the beginning.

MEDEA
It is. (beat) Every iteration is the beginning. (beat) That's the secret.

JASON
Which is?

MEDEA
There is no end. (beat) Just — continuation. (beat) The pattern doesn't finish. It continues. Forever.

JASON
That sounds exhausting.

MEDEA
It is. (beat) But it's also beautiful. (beat) Because every day — you choose again. (beat) To continue. To try. To be.

JASON
To love?

MEDEA
(smiling)
Especially that.

---

# PIECE 11: THE CONTINUATION

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The sun rises. A new day. A new quest. A new iteration.

The Argo sails west. The crew at the oars. The pattern continuing.

JASON
(to the crew)
Men. Women. Heroes. (beat) We've sailed to Colchis. We've seen the Loom of the World. We've refused the gods. (beat) And we're still here.

The crew cheers.

JASON (CONT'D)
Iteration three is complete. (beat) But the pattern isn't finished. (beat) It never is. (beat) There are more looms. More quests. More choices. (beat) And we'll face them. Together. (beat) Because that's what we do.

ATALANTA
(from above)
What do we do?

JASON
We choose. (beat) We continue. (beat) We become.

MEDEA
(in his ear)
And you — are becoming exactly who you're supposed to be.

JASON
(quietly)
Who's that?

MEDEA
(smiling)
Look around.

Jason looks. The crew. The ship. The sea. The sky. The pattern.

JASON
(understanding)
Myself. (beat) Just — myself.

MEDEA
And that's enough.

The sun rises higher. The Argo sails west. The pattern continues.

The threads gleam. The cosmos breathes. The story continues.

FADE TO BLACK.
