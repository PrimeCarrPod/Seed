# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 04: ROAD OF TRIALS
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 04 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE PURSUIT

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The Argo flies across dark water. Behind them: Colchis burns. Ahead: the unknown.

The crew rows in silence. Medea sits at the bow, the fleece wrapped in cloth beside her. Jason stands at the stern, looking back.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
Ships. Three of them. Colchian colors.

JASON
How far?

ATALANTA
Two miles. Gaining.

JASON
(to the crew)
Faster.

The oars dig deeper. The ship accelerates. But behind them, the Colchian ships are faster.

MEDEA
(quietly)
My brother commands the lead ship. Absyrtus. He's faster than me. Smarter than me. And he'll never stop.

JASON
Then we don't stop either.

MEDEA
You don't understand. He's my brother. I— (stopping herself) —I left him behind.

JASON
You chose.

MEDEA
That doesn't make it easier.

---

# PIECE 2: THE TRICK

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - NIGHT

The crew gathers. Jason, Medea, Orpheus, Atalanta, the twins.

JASON
They're faster. We can't outrun them.

ZETES
We could fight.

MEDEA
Three ships. Fifty men each. We'd lose.

CALAIS
Then what?

Medea looks at the fleece. At Jason. At the crew.

MEDEA
We split them up. Make them choose.

JASON
How?

MEDEA
(standing)
Absyrtus wants me. The fleece is secondary. If I surrender — he'll take me home. Leave you alone.

JASON
No.

MEDEA
It's the only way.

JASON
I said no. (beat) There's another way.

He looks at the crew.

JASON (CONT'D)
We don't surrender. We don't fight. We— (looking at Medea) —we make them think we surrendered. And when they come close—

MEDEA
(understanding)
A trap.

JASON
You said Absyrtus is smart. Smart men think they're smarter than they are.

---

# PIECE 3: THE AMBUSH

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The Argo slows. Dramatically. As if the rowers are exhausted.

The Colchian ships close. The lead ship — Absyrtus' vessel — pulls alongside.

ABSYRTUS (20s, Medea's brother, golden armor, thinks three moves ahead) stands at the prow.

ABSYRTUS
Sister. You've had your adventure. Come home.

MEDEA
(calling across)
I can't. The fleece—

ABSYRTUS
Father doesn't care about the fleece. He cares about you. (beat) Come home. Bring the Greek if you want. Father will negotiate.

MEDEA
(looking at Jason)
Negotiate?

ABSYRTUS
The Greek lives if you return. Simple.

Jason steps forward. Visible. Letting Absyrtus see him.

JASON
(calling)
I'm coming aboard. To discuss terms.

ABSYRTUS
(cautious)
Alone?

JASON
Alone.

JASON
(to Medea, quietly)
When I'm aboard — you know what to do.

Medea nods. Pale.

A rope ladder drops. Jason climbs. Reaches Absyrtus' deck.

The brothers-in-arms stare at each other.

ABSYRTUS
You're younger than I expected.

JASON
You're older than I expected.

ABSYRTUS
(almost smiling)
My sister chose you. I'm trying to understand why.

JASON
So am I.

Absyrtus studies him. Then—

ABSYRTUS
The fleece. Give it up. I'll let you live.

JASON
And Medea?

ABSYRTUS
Comes home. To her family. To her kingdom.

JASON
Even though she doesn't want to?

ABSYRTUS
Want is irrelevant. She's my sister. She's Colchian. She comes home.

JASON
(quietly)
Then we have a problem.

---

# PIECE 4: THE BLADE

EXT. ABSYRTUS' SHIP - CONTINUOUS

Jason looks back at the Argo. Medea stands at the bow. Their eyes meet.

He nods. Once.

Jason draws his sword — fast — presses it to Absyrtus' throat.

JASON
Call off your ships.

ABSYRTUS
(not moving)
You'll never leave this deck alive.

JASON
Neither will you.

ABSYRTUS
(calmly)
Then we both die. And my ships destroy yours. And the fleece burns. And Medea returns home anyway.

JASON
Maybe.

ABSYRTUS
Definitely. (beat) You're not a killer, Jason. I can see it. You won't—

Medea's voice from the Argo:

MEDEA
(calling)
Brother!

Absyrtus turns.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
I'm sorry.

She throws something. A bundle. It lands on the deck between them.

It's the fleece. Wrapped in cloth. Glowing.

ABSYRTUS
(startled)
The fleece—

Jason shoves Absyrtus backward. Grabs the fleece. Jumps.

The water is cold. He surfaces. The Argo is there. Hands pull him up.

Behind him: chaos. Absyrtus' ship circling. The other two Colchian ships closing.

ATALANTA
(from above)
They're confused. They don't know which way to go.

JASON
Row! Now!

The Argo shoots forward.

---

# PIECE 5: THE BROTHER

EXT. OPEN SEA - LATER

The Colchian ships fall behind. The Argo escapes.

Jason sits at the bow. Wet. Cold. The fleece beside him.

Medea sits apart. Staring back at where her brother was.

JASON
(gently)
Medea.

MEDEA
I left him.

JASON
You chose.

MEDEA
You keep saying that. Like it makes it easier.

JASON
Does it?

MEDEA
(long pause)
No. (beat) He was kind to me. When we were children. Before Father made him into a soldier. He used to— (stopping) —it doesn't matter.

JASON
It matters.

MEDEA
Why? He'll hunt us now. He'll never stop. Because I humiliated him. Because I chose a stranger over my own blood.

JASON
You chose the pattern.

MEDEA
(bitterly)
The pattern. The pattern. I'm so tired of the pattern.

She stands. Walks to the stern. Sits with her back to the crew.

Orpheus approaches Jason.

ORPHEUS
(quietly)
Give her time.

JASON
How much time?

ORPHEUS
As much as she needs. (betrayal like hers — it doesn't heal. It scars.)

JASON
Have you—

ORPHEUS
(quietly)
I know what it costs to choose between love and everything else. (beat) The cost doesn't go away. You just learn to carry it.

---

# PIECE 6: THE LAND OF THE PHAEACIANS

EXT. PHAEACIAN ISLAND - DAY

The Argo beaches on a strange shore. Green hills. Olive trees. A palace in the distance.

MEDEAELA
The land of the Phaeacians. King Alcinous. Queen Arete. They're known for hospitality.

JASON
And for sending travelers home.

PERICLYMENUS
If they like you.

ATALANTA
And if they don't?

MEDEAELA
They drown you. Politely. With a feast first.

The crew disembarks. Medea stays on the ship.

JASON
(approaching her)
Come with us.

MEDEA
I'm a traitor to my father's house. A kidnapper of princes. A thief. (beat) What king welcomes that?

JASON
Then we don't ask for welcome. We ask for passage.

MEDEA
You don't understand. I can't— (struggling) —I did something terrible. To my brother. To my father. And I'd do it again. What does that make me?

JASON
Human.

MEDEA
(almost laughing)
Human. Is that supposed to comfort me?

JASON
No. It's supposed to be true.

She looks at him. Then — slowly — stands.

MEDEA
If they reject me—

JASON
Then we find another way.

MEDEA
You always say that.

JASON
Because it's always true.

They walk toward the palace together.

---

# PIECE 7: THE PHAEACIAN COURT

INT. PHAEACIAN PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

KING ALCINOUS (60s, generous face, eyes that miss nothing) sits beside QUEEN ARETE (60s, sharp, the real power).

The Argonauts enter. Medea among them. Heads high.

ALCINOUS
Travelers. From where?

JASON
Colchis. By way of Iolcus. Seeking passage home.

ALCINOUS
And the girl?

MEDEA
(stepping forward)
Medea. Daughter of Aetes. Priestess of Hecate. Traitor to my father's house.

Silence. The court murmurs.

ARETE
(leaning forward)
You admit it freely?

MEDEA
I did what I did. I won't hide from it.

ARETE
Your father wants you back.

MEDEA
My father wants me dead. There's a difference.

ALCINOUS
And the fleece?

JASON
We retrieved it. As you can see.

He unwraps the fleece. The court gasps. The gold light fills the room.

ARETE
(to Medea)
You gave this to him?

MEDEA
I gave him more than that. (beat) I gave him everything. My home. My family. My name. Everything.

ARETE
(standing)
Why?

MEDEA
Because the pattern demanded it. Because the goddess commanded it. Because— (looking at Jason) —because I chose.

Arete studies her husband. Some silent communication passes.

ARETE
(to Medea)
You're brave. Or foolish. I haven't decided which.

MEDEA
Both. Definitely both.

ALCINOUS
(laughing)
I like her. (beat) You'll have your passage. Both of you. But—

JASON
But?

ALCINOUS
Stay one night. Feast with us. Let us hear your story. (beat) It's not every day we meet people who stole the fleece and lived.

---

# PIECE 8: THE FEAST

INT. PHAEACIAN PALACE - BANQUET HALL - NIGHT

The feast is generous. Wine. Music. Dancing.

Medea sits beside Jason. Quiet. Watching.

Arete approaches. Sits across from Medea.

ARETE
You're not eating.

MEDEA
I'm not hungry.

ARETE
You're afraid.

MEDEA
(looking up)
Shouldn't I be?

ARETE
Of what?

MEDEA
That you'll give us to my father. That this feast is a trap. That—

ARETE
(gently)
That you don't deserve kindness.

MEDEA
(silence)

ARETE
I know what it's like. To choose between your blood and your heart. Between what you're supposed to do and what you must do.

MEDEA
You do?

ARETE
I was not born here. I came from across the sea. I left my family for Alcinous. (beat) It's been forty years. And some nights — I still wonder if I chose right.

MEDEA
How do you live with the wondering?

ARETE
You wake up each morning. And you choose again. Every day. The same choice. (beat) Until one day — the wondering stops. And you realize: you didn't choose wrong. You just chose. And choosing is always both.

MEDEA
(quietly)
Both?

ARETE
Painful and necessary. Loss and gain. Ending and beginning. (smiling) That's what choosing means.

---

# PIECE 9: THE WARNING

INT. PHAEACIAN PALACE - BALCONY - NIGHT

Jason stands on the balcony. Medea joins him. The sea below.

MEDEA
Arete says we can stay. As long as we want.

JASON
Do you want to stay?

MEDEA
I want to stop running. (beat) But we can't. Not yet.

JASON
Why not?

MEDEA
Because the fleece isn't home. Because your throne isn't won. Because— (looking at him) —because the pattern isn't finished.

JASON
The pattern.

MEDEA
You think I'm obsessed with it. But you don't understand. The pattern isn't fate. The pattern is choice. Every choice we made — to come here, to take the fleece, to leave Colchis — those were choices. The pattern is just — the shape those choices make.

JASON
And what shape are we making?

MEDEA
(quietly)
I don't know yet. But it's not finished.

ATALANTA
(from the doorway)
Ship approaching. Colchian colors.

JASON
Absyrtus?

ATALANTA
No. Smaller. Faster. (beat) It's a messenger.

---

# PIECE 10: THE MESSAGE

INT. PHAEACIAN PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAWN

A COLCHIAN MESSENGER (30s, exhausted) kneels before Alcinous.

MESSENGER
King Alcinous. I bring word from Aetes, king of Colchis.

ALCINOUS
Speak.

MESSENGER
Aetes demands the return of his daughter. And the fleece. And the Greek who stole both.

ALCINOUS
And if I refuse?

MESSENGER
Then Colchia will consider it an act of war.

The court murmurs. Jason steps forward.

JASON
Give me to Aetes. Let Medea and the fleece go.

MESSENGER
(shaking his head)
Aetes doesn't want you, Greek. He wants his daughter. (beat) He's sworn an oath. If she returns — she'll face trial for treason. The penalty is death.

Silence.

MESSENGER (CONT'D)
But if she doesn't return — he'll send his fleet. Every ship. Every soldier. He'll burn this island to the floor of the sea.

ALCINOUS
(to Jason)
This is your choice. Give me the girl. Or face war.

JASON
There's a third option.

ALCINOUS
Which is?

JASON
We leave. Now. Before your island burns. We take the fleece. We go home.

MEDEA
And Colchis follows.

JASON
Then we'll face them. But not here. Not on someone else's shore.

ALCINOUS
(considering)
You'd leave to protect us?

JASON
We already brought enough trouble.

ARETE
(to Alcinous)
Let them go. Give them a ship. Give them supplies. (beat) And give them our blessing.

ALCINOUS
(nodding)
So it shall be. (to Jason) You leave at dawn. With our ship. Our provisions. Our respect.

JASON
Thank you.

ALCINOUS
Don't thank me. Just survive.

---

# PIECE 11: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. PHAEACIAN ISLAND - HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo is loaded. New provisions. New sail. The Phaeacians gather to watch.

Arete approaches Medea.

ARETE
Take this.

She gives Medea a small cloth bundle.

MEDEA
What is it?

ARETE
Seeds. From my garden. Plant them wherever you land. (beat) So something of this place goes with you.

MEDEA
(taking it)
Thank you. For everything.

ARETE
Choose well. (beat) And choose again tomorrow. And the day after. (smiling) That's the secret.

The Argo sails. The Phaeacians wave. The island fades.

Jason and Medea stand at the bow. The fleece between them.

JASON
We made it this far.

MEDEA
We made it this far.

JASON
What now?

MEDEA
Now we go home. (beat) And face whatever waits.

JASON
Pelias.

MEDEA
Pelias. Your father's ghost. My father's fleet. (beat) Everything we left behind.

JASON
Ready?

MEDEA
(smiling)
No. But I'm going anyway.

The Argo sails toward Iolcus. Toward home. Toward the ending that is a beginning.
