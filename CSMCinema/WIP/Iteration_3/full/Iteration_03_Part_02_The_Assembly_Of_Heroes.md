# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 02: ASSEMBLY OF HEROES
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 02 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE SHIP TUNES ITSELF

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo cuts through calm water. The storm has passed. Heracles is gone. The crew rows in silence.

ORPHEUS sits at the bow, tuning his lyre. Each string finds a frequency. The hull begins to hum.

ZETES
(to Calais)
Did the ship just—

CALAIS
Yes.

ZETES
Ships don't hum.

CALAIS
This one does.

Orpheus plays a melody. The waves flatten to match. The wind shifts to fill the sail at exactly the right angle.

JASON
(from the stern)
Can you calm a storm with that?

ORPHEUS
(not looking up)
I can make the storm calm itself.

JASON
That's not an answer.

ORPHEUS
It's the only one I have.

ATALANTA climbs down from the masthead.

ATALANTA
The ship responds to him.

JASON
How?

ATALANTA
Same way it responds to you. It knows who its crew is.

The hull hums louder. Orpheus smiles.

---

# PIECE 2: THE HUNTER AND THE BARD

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The crew sleeps. ATALANTA keeps watch at the masthead. ORPHEUS plays softly at the bow.

ATALANTA
(calling down)
You don't sleep?

ORPHEUS
(calling up)
Music is my sleep.

ATALANTA
That doesn't make sense.

ORPHEUS
Nothing worth doing does.

Silence. Then:

ATALANTA
Why are you here? Really.

ORPHEUS
(long pause)
I'm looking for the end of music.

ATALANTA
The end?

ORPHEUS
Where sound becomes silence. Where the last note fades. I want to hear it.

ATALANTA
That sounds like death.

ORPHEUS
(smiling up at her)
You hunt. You know the difference between death and the end of the chase.

ATALANTA
(considering)
The end of the chase is better prey. Harder to find. More satisfying when you do.

ORPHEUS
Exactly.

ATALANTA
(looking out at the sea)
Land tomorrow. Lemnos. We're not stopping.

ORPHEUS
I know.

ATALANTA
But after that—

ORPHEUS
After that, Colchis. And whatever waits.

ATALANTA
You're not afraid?

ORPHEUS
I'm terrified. (beat) That's how I know it's worth doing.

---

# PIECE 3: THE TWINS ARGUE

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAY

CASTOR and POLLUX face each other. The crew watches. This is the first time they've disagreed.

CASTOR
You went alone.

POLLUX
Someone had to.

CASTOR
We're supposed to be together.

POLLUX
We're supposed to be effective. There's a difference.

CASTOR
(to the crew)
My brother scouted the island without me. Alone. While I slept.

POLLUX
I didn't wake you because you needed rest.

CASTOR
I don't need rest. I need my brother.

JASON
(stepping between them)
What did you find?

POLLUX
A harbor. Abandoned. Safe to anchor.

CASTOR
He also found tracks. Large ones. Something lives there.

POLLUX
Something that left.

CASTOR
Or something that's still there and patient.

The twins stare at each other. Some unspoken communication passes.

CASTOR/POLLUX
(together)
We go together next time.

The crew relaxes. Jason nods.

JASON
Together. Always.

---

# PIECE 4: THE LESSON

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

JASON sits with ORPHEUS at the bow. The bard plays. Jason listens.

JASON
Teach me.

ORPHEUS
Teach you what?

JASON
The lyre. I want to understand what you do.

ORPHEUS
(handing him the lyre)
Hold it like this. The strings face out. Your fingers here.

Jason plucks a string. It sounds wrong.

JASON
I'm terrible.

ORPHEUS
You're beginning. There's a difference. (beat) Again.

Jason plucks. Still wrong. He tries again. Worse.

JASON
I don't have the gift.

ORPHEUS
The gift is patience. You have other gifts. (beat) The crew doesn't need another musician. They need a leader who understands music.

JASON
Why?

ORPHEUS
Because music is pattern. Pattern is fate. And you — (smiling) — you're trying to understand fate.

JASON
(struggling with the strings)
I'm trying to understand why the gods made it so complicated.

ORPHEUS
They didn't. We did.

---

# PIECE 5: THE ISLAND

EXT. LEMNOS - HARBOR - DAY

The Argo anchors offshore. The island is quiet. Too quiet.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
No smoke. No movement. No birds.

JASON
Abandoned?

ATALANTA
No. They're hiding. Watching us.

MEDEAELA
(approaching Jason)
The women here killed their men. All of them. Husbands. Fathers. Sons.

JASON
Why?

MEDEAELA
The men took concubines from Thrace. Brought them home. Forgot their wives. The wives remembered.

PERICLYMENUS
So we're anchoring at an island of murderers.

MEDEAELA
We're anchoring at an island of women who were wronged and responded badly.

ATALANTA
(from above)
They're coming. Boats. Dozens of them.

JASON
(to the crew)
Nobody leaves the ship. Nobody boards. We wait.

The boats approach. Women armed with kitchen knives. Farm tools. One holds a loom shuttle like a spear.

HYPSIPYLE (30s, queen, bearing that doesn't match her weapon) stands in the lead boat.

HYPSIPYLE
Turn back.

JASON
We mean no harm. We need only water and rest.

HYPSIPYLE
Rest here becomes permanent. (beat) Your men will stay. You will go. Or you will all stay.

JASON
(quietly, to Orpheus)
Play something.

ORPHEUS
What?

JASON
Something that says "we're not a threat."

Orpheus plays. A simple melody. A lullaby. The kind mothers sing.

The women lower their weapons. Just slightly.

HYPSIPYLE
(softening)
You have a bard.

JASON
We have a crew. All different. All necessary.

HYPSIPYLE
(still guarded)
And what do you seek?

JASON
The Golden Fleece. In Colchis.

HYPSIPYLE
(long pause)
Then you seek death. (beat) But you're welcome to water. And rest. One night.

JASON
One night.

---

# PIECE 6: THE LOOM AND THE SWORD

EXT. LEMNOS - SHORE - NIGHT

The crew camps on shore. Jason walks the perimeter. He finds HYPSIPYLE at a loom.

HYPSIPYLE
Can't sleep?

JASON
Captain's habit.

HYPSIPYLE
Weaving helps me think. (beat) You're wondering if we're dangerous.

JASON
I'm wondering if you're like me.

HYPSIPYLE
How's that?

JASON
Trying to rebuild something that was broken. Trying to make it whole again.

HYPSIPYLE
(stopping her work)
We didn't break anything. They broke us. We just... responded.

JASON
I know the feeling.

HYPSIPYLE
Do you? (beat) Your men look at you the way my women looked at me. Like you're supposed to have answers.

JASON
Do you?

HYPSIPYLE
No. But I weave anyway. And I wait for the pattern to emerge.

JASON
(looking at the loom)
The pattern. Everyone keeps saying that.

HYPSIPYLE
Because it's true. The pattern is already there. We just reveal it.

Jason stares at the threads. For a moment, he sees what Medea will see. What Hecate sees. The cloth as cosmos.

JASON
(quietly)
Thank you.

HYPSIPYLE
For what?

JASON
For the reminder.

He walks back to camp. Hypsipyle watches him go. Returns to her loom.

---

# PIECE 7: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. LEMNOS - HARBOR - DAWN

The crew boards the Argo. The women watch from shore.

HYPSIPYLE
(calling)
The Symplegades wait ahead. The clashing rocks. No ship passes through.

JASON
We'll find a way.

HYPSIPYLE
(calling)
If you do — remember us. Remember that we chose to let you pass.

JASON
I will.

The Argo sails. The women fade behind them.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
The clashing rocks ahead.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
I'll need a louder song.

ZETES
(to Calais)
We fly over if it goes wrong.

CALAIS
Agreed.

JASON
(to the crew)
The Symplegades. Two rocks that clash together. Crush anything between. We send a dove through first. If it passes, we follow. If it doesn't—

MEDEAELA
If it doesn't?

JASON
We find another way.

---

# PIECE 8: THE SYMPLESGADES

EXT. SYMPLADES - DAY

Two massive cliffs face each other. The water between them churns. The rocks clash — BOOM — open — close — BOOM. Each crash sends spray fifty meters high.

The crew stares.

PERICLYMENUS
No ship survives that.

ATALANTA
No ship has survived that.

JASON
Release the dove.

ZETES releases a white dove. It flies toward the rocks.

The crew watches. The rocks clash. The dove flies through — barely — losing only a tail feather.

JASON
Row. Now.

The crew rows. Fifty oars. The Argo shoots forward.

The rocks begin to close.

ORPHEUS
(playing, loud)
Faster!

The oars dig deeper. The ship accelerates. The rocks close—

The Argo passes through. The rocks clash behind them. The stern post is clipped — splinters — but the ship is through.

The crew cheers.

JASON
(breathing hard)
We made it.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
The feather. The dove lost a feather.

JASON
What does that mean?

ATALANTA
It means the rocks take payment. Even from doves.

ORPHEUS
(quietly)
Everything has a price.

---

# PIECE 9: THE COST

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The crew celebrates. Wine from Lemnos. Songs from Orpheus.

Jason sits apart. Orpheus joins him.

ORPHEUS
You're not celebrating.

JASON
The rocks took a feather. What will Colchis take?

ORPHEUS
Whatever it needs to.

JASON
That's not comforting.

ORPHEUS
It's not meant to be. It's meant to be true.

JASON
(long pause)
Heracles left. We're down one hero. The rocks nearly took the ship. The dragon waits. And I'm supposed to lead.

ORPHEUS
You are leading.

JASON
I'm pretending.

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
That's what leadership is. Confident pretending until it becomes real.

JASON
Does it become real?

ORPHEUS
For some. (beat) For those who care more about the crew than about looking brave.

JASON
(looking at the crew)
I do care.

ORPHEUS
Then you'll be fine.

---

# PIECE 10: THE DREAM

EXT. ARGO - BOW - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. Dreams. HECATE appears.

HECATE
You passed the rocks.

JASON
(in the dream)
Barely.

HECATE
Barely is enough. (beat) Colchis is close now. Medea prays for you.

JASON
She doesn't know me.

HECATE
She knows enough. She's known since birth. The priestess who would betray her father. The weaver who would choose love over kingdom. She's known and she's terrified.

JASON
Why are you telling me this?

HECATE
Because when you meet her, you'll think it's your idea. Your quest. Your choice. It's not. It's hers.

JASON
Then what do I do?

HECATE
Respect it. Respect her. Respect the pattern.

JASON
The pattern. Always the pattern.

HECATE
Because the pattern is all there is.

She fades. Jason wakes.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
Land. Colchis.

---

# PIECE 11: THE WHALE OPENS

EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo glides through mist that doesn't burn off. Gold-laced. Volatile. Poisonous.

The crew gathers at the rails. The cliffs rise like teeth.

JASON
(quietly)
We've entered the whale.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
Then let's not be digested.

The harbor opens like a mouth. The ship drifts forward. Mist swallows the stern.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Archers on the cliffs. Watching. Not attacking.

JASON
Not yet.

MEDEAELA
They're waiting.

PERICLYMENUS
For what?

JASON
For us to make the first mistake.

The Argo docks. The crew stands ready. The cliffs watch. The mist swirls.

JASON
(to the crew)
We're here. Colchis. The fleece is somewhere ahead. And somewhere ahead — Medea waits.

ATALANTA
How do you know?

JASON
Because the pattern says so.

The crew disembarks. Fifty heroes. One purpose. The quest enters its next phase.
