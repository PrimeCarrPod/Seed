# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 08: APOTHEOSIS
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 08 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE MAP

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - DAY

The crew gathers around the map. It shows lands beyond Greece. Beyond Colchis. Beyond anything they know.

PERICLYMENUS
These symbols — they're not Greek. Not Persian. Not anything I recognize.

MEDEA writes:

*THEY'RE OLDER.*

JASON
How old?

She writes:

*TITAN. (beat) BEFORE THE GODS. BEFORE OLYMPUS. BEFORE EVERYTHING.*

ATALANTA
The Titans are dead.

She writes:

*THE TITANS ARE SLEEPING. (beat) THERE'S A DIFFERENCE.*

ORPHEUS
What does the map show?

She writes:

*THE LOOM OF THE WORLD. (beat) THE ORIGINAL LOOM. THE ONE THAT WOVE THE GODS INTO EXISTENCE.*

Silence.

ZETES
You're saying — there's a loom that wove the gods?

She writes:

*I'M SAYING THE GODS WEREN'T ALWAYS HERE. THEY WERE MADE. WOVEN. BY SOMETHING OLDER. (beat) AND IT'S STILL THERE. WAITING.*

JASON
Waiting for what?

She writes:

*FOR SOMEONE WHO UNDERSTANDS.*

---

# PIECE 2: THE DREAM

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

Jason sleeps on the dock. Dreams.

CHRONOS (not old man, not fire — the entropy gradient itself, the arrow that never misses) stands before him.

CHRONOS
The map shows the way.

JASON
(in the dream)
To what?

CHRONOS
To where I was born. To where time began. To the loom that wove me.

JASON
Why are you telling me this?

CHRONOS
Because you'll go anyway. (beat) But you should know — the loom changes everyone who sees it. Your father saw it. It drove him mad. That's why Pelias killed him.

JASON
Pelias killed my father because of the loom?

CHRONOS
Your father wanted to weave his own fate. The loom doesn't allow that. (beat) The loom weaves. You're woven. That's the rule.

JASON
Then why show us the map?

CHRONOS
Because the rule is changing. The loom is waking up. And when it wakes — everything changes. (beat) The gods. The world. Time itself.

JASON
How do I stop it?

CHRONOS
(smiling)
You don't. You become it.

Jason wakes. Medea sits beside him. She writes:

*YOU WERE TALKING IN YOUR SLEEP.*

JASON
Chronos came. (beat) He told me about the loom.

She writes:

*I KNOW. I HEARD.*

JASON
You were listening?

She writes:

*I ALWAYS LISTEN. (beat) JASON — WE HAVE TO GO. TO THE LOOM. IT'S IMPORTANT.*

JASON
Why?

She writes:

*BECAUSE THE PATTERN DEMANDS IT. (beat) AND BECAUSE I'M SUPPOSED TO BE THERE.*

---

# PIECE 3: THE PREPARATION

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAWN

The crew prepares to sail. Pelias approaches.

PELIAS
You're leaving. Already.

JASON
The fleece was just the beginning.

PELIAS
It's always just the beginning with you. (beat) Where will you go?

JASON
Wherever the map leads.

PELIAS
(looking at the map)
I've seen this before. Your father had a copy. (beat) He went looking for this loom. He never came back.

JASON
I'll come back.

PELIAS
You don't know that.

JASON
No. But I choose to believe it.

PELIAS
(quietly)
Then choose well. (beat) And Jason — when you find the loom. Don't weave anything. Don't touch anything. Don't— (stopping) —don't become your father.

JASON
What happened to my father?

PELIAS
He tried to weave himself into the pattern. (beat) The pattern wove him out.

---

# PIECE 4: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo sails. The crew is different now. Quieter. More purposeful.

Jason stands at the bow. Medea beside him.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Open sea. No land for days.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Good. I need to compose a new song.

ZETES
About what?

ORPHEUS
About the end of everything. (beat) And the beginning.

CALAIS
That's cheerful.

ORPHEUS
It's honest.

MEDEA writes:

*JASON. (beat) WHEN WE REACH THE LOOM — I'LL NEED TO WEAVE.*

JASON
I know.

She writes:

*IT MIGHT CHANGE ME.*

JASON
Everything changes you. That's what living is.

She writes:

*THIS IS DIFFERENT. (beat) THE LOOM IS — POWERFUL. IT MIGHT CHANGE ME INTO SOMETHING YOU DON'T RECOGNIZE.*

JASON
Then I'll recognize you again.

She writes:
*YOU CAN'T PROMISE THAT.*

JASON
I just did.

---

# PIECE 5: THE VOID

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The sea changes. The water goes flat. Not calm — flat. As if the waves have forgotten how to move.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The birds are gone. The fish are gone. Everything is—

She stops.

ATALANTA (CONT'D)
There's something in the water.

The crew looks. Beneath the surface — shapes. Enormous shapes. Moving slowly.

MEDEA writes:

*THE OLD ONES. (beat) WHAT CAME BEFORE THE GODS.*

JASON
Are they dangerous?

She writes:

*ONLY IF WE DANGER THEM. (beat) THEY'RE GUARDIANS. THEY PROTECT THE LOOM.*

ZETES
How do we get past them?

She writes:
*WE DON'T. (beat) THEY LET US THROUGH.*

CALAIS
How do you know?

She writes:
*BECAUSE THE PATTERN DEMANDS IT.*

The shapes rise. Massive. Ancient. Eyes like stars. They regard the Argo. Then — slowly — part.

A path opens through the water. Down. Into darkness.

ORPHEUS
(stopping his playing)
The water is solid.

ATALANTA
(from above)
It's not water anymore. It's — something else.

MEDEA writes:

*IT'S THE LOOM. (beat) WE'RE INSIDE IT NOW.*

---

# PIECE 6: THE LOOM

EXT. THE LOOM - NIGHT

The Argo floats through darkness. Not water. Not air. Something else. Threads stretch in every direction — visible, glowing, alive.

The crew stares.

PERICLYMENUS
This is—

MEDEA writes:

*THE LOOM OF THE WORLD. (beat) WHERE TIME WAS WOVEN. WHERE THE GODS WERE MADE. WHERE EVERYTHING BEGAN.*

In the distance — a structure. Vast. Impossible. A loom the size of a mountain. And on it — the cloth of everything. Every life. Every moment. Every choice. All woven together.

ATALANTA
(quietly)
My gods.

ORPHEUS
(whispering)
It's beautiful.

MEDEA writes:

*IT'S TERRIFYING. (beat) EVERY THREAD IS A LIFE. EVERY PATTERN IS A FATE. EVERY COLOR IS A CHOICE.*

JASON
And you can read it?

She writes:

*I CAN READ ALL OF IT. (beat) I CAN SEE EVERYTHING.*

JASON
Even us?

She writes:

*ESPECIALLY US.*

---

# PIECE 7: THE WEAVING

EXT. THE LOOM - CONTINUOUS

Medea approaches the loom. Her hands tremble.

MEDEA writes:

*I HAVE TO TOUCH IT. (beat) I HAVE TO WEAVE.*

JASON
Are you sure?

She writes:

*NO. (beat) BUT I'M DOING IT ANYWAY.*

She places her hands on the loom.

Light. Blinding. The threads respond to her touch. They sing. Not with sound — with meaning.

Medea weaves. Not cloth. Reality itself. Each pass of the shuttle changes something. A star ignites. A world forms. A life begins.

JASON
(awed)
You're creating—

She writes:

*I'M NOT CREATING. (betrayal) I'M REVEALING. (beat) IT'S ALREADY HERE. I'M JUST MAKING IT VISIBLE.*

The cloth expands. 1,331 galaxies per second. The cosmos breathes.

And Medea — changes. Her eyes glow. Her hair lifts. She becomes translucent. Not disappearing. Expanding. Becoming part of the pattern.

JASON
(alarmed)
Medea!

She writes:

*I'M HERE. (beat) I'M EVERYWHERE. (beat) I'M THE PATTERN NOW.*

---

# PIECE 8: THE CHOICE

EXT. THE LOOM - CONTINUOUS

Jason grabs her hand. She's still solid. Still Medea. Barely.

JASON
Come back.

She writes:

*I CAN'T. (beat) IF I LET GO — THE PATTERN BREAKS. (beat) EVERYTHING I WOVE — IT ALL COMES UNDONE.*

JASON
Then I'll hold on.

She writes:

*YOU CAN'T HOLD ON FOREVER.*

JASON
I can try.

She writes:

*JASON. (beat) LISTEN. (beat) THIS IS WHAT I WAS BORN FOR. (beat) THE PRIESTESS WHO BECAME THE LOOM. THE WEAVER WHO BECAME THE WEAVE. (beat) THIS IS MY PURPOSE.*

JASON
Your purpose is with me.

She writes:

*IT IS. (beat) BUT IT'S ALSO THIS. (beat) I CAN BE BOTH.*

JASON
How?

She writes:

*BECAUSE THE PATTERN ISN'T FATE. (beat) IT'S CHOICE. (beat) AND I CHOOSE YOU. AND I CHOOSE THIS. (beat) BOTH. ALWAYS BOTH.*

---

# PIECE 9: THE TRANSFORMATION

EXT. THE LOOM - CONTINUOUS

Medea weaves faster. The cloth expands. The cosmos breathes. The gods — far away on Olympus — feel it. The pattern is changing.

And Medea — becomes. Not fully human. Not fully divine. Something between. Something new.

Her eyes see everything. Her hands weave everything. Her voice—

She speaks. For the first time in months. Her voice returns.

MEDEA
(speaking)
Jason.

JASON
(stunned)
Your voice—

MEDEA
The loom gave it back. (beat) The loom gives everything. Eventually.

JASON
Are you—

MEDEA
I'm still me. (beat) But I'm also — more. (beat) I'm the loom. And the loom is me. And the pattern is— (looking at her hands) —everything.

JASON
Come back.

MEDEA
(smiling)
I never left.

She takes his hand. The threads weave around them both. Not trapping. Embracing.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
The pattern is beautiful. (beat) I can see why the gods wanted to control it. Why my father wanted to steal it. Why everyone wants to own it.

JASON
But you're not trying to own it.

MEDEA
No. (beat) I'm trying to understand it. (beat) That's different.

---

# PIECE 10: THE GIFT

EXT. THE LOOM - CONTINUOUS

Medea weaves one final thread. A single thread. Gold. Her own.

She pulls it from the loom. Hands it to Jason.

MEDEA
Take this.

JASON
What is it?

MEDEA
Protection. (beat) The loom's protection. No god can harm you while you hold it. No fate can claim you. No pattern can break you.

JASON
I can't take this.

MEDEA
You have to. (beat) The next iterations — they'll be harder. The gods will notice. They'll try to stop you. (beat) You'll need this.

JASON
What about you?

MEDEA
(smiling)
I AM the protection now.

She kisses him. The threads sing. The cosmos breathes. The pattern holds.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
Now go. (beat) The crew needs you. The quest needs you. (beat) I'll be here. Weaving. (beat) Always weaving.

JASON
Will I see you again?

MEDEA
Every time you look at the cloth. Every time you see a pattern. Every time you choose. (beat) I'll be there.

---

# PIECE 11: THE RETURN

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The Argo sails. The loom behind them. The world ahead.

Jason stands at the bow. The golden thread wrapped around his wrist.

The crew is silent. Changed. They saw something no mortal should see.

ORPHEUS
(softly)
Was that real?

JASON
Does it matter?

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
No. (beat) I guess it doesn't.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Where to now?

JASON
(looking at the map)
Wherever the pattern leads.

MEDEA
(from the loom, distant, echoing)
*Wherever the pattern leads.*

The Argo sails. The sun rises. The fleece glows. The golden thread gleams.

Iteration 2 is complete. Iteration 3 approaches.

The pattern continues.
