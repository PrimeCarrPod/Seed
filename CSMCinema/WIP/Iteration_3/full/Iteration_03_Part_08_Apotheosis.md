# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 08: APOTHEOSIS
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 08 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE TRANSFORMATION

INT. IOLCUS - PALACE - DAY

Jason stands before a mirror. Medea adjusts his royal robes.

MEDEA
You look like a king.

JASON
I feel like an impostor.

MEDEA
All kings are impostors. (beat)
The trick is believing your own lie.

JASON
Is that what we're doing? Lying?

MEDEA
We're doing something harder. (beat)
We're telling a new truth.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE COURT

INT. THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason sits the throne. The FLEECE behind him, now BRIGHT. His father sits at his side.

The court presents petitions. Jason listens. Judges. Decides.

HERACLES
(whispering)
You're good at this.

JASON
(whispering)
I'm terrified.

HERACLES
Good. Terrified kings make careful decisions.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE WARNING

EXT. PALACE BALCONY - NIGHT

Aeson and Jason alone. The city below.

AESON
Aegeus will come for you.

JASON
Let him.

AESON
He has an army.

JASON
I have the Fleece. I have the gods. I have the people.

AESON
The gods are fickle. The people are fickle. (beat)
Only power is constant.

JASON
Then I'll have power.

AESON
No. (beat)
You'll have something better. You'll have love.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE CHALLENGE

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

Athens's fleet appears. Aegeus's flagship at the front.

Aegeus stands at the prow.

AEGEUS
(shouting)
Jason! Return what you stole!

JASON
(shouting back)
I stole nothing! My father is mine by blood!

AEGEUS
Then blood will decide!

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE BATTLE

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

The Athenian fleet attacks. Jason's defenders meet them at the docks.

Heracles fights at the front. His club CRUSHES Athenian soldiers.

Atalanta's arrows FLY. Each one finds its mark.

Orpheus plays. The music CALMS the defenders, CONFUSES the attackers.

Jason fights beside his people. Not above them. Beside them.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE DUEL

EXT. DOCK - DAY

Jason and Aegeus face each other. Swords drawn.

AEGEUS
You could have given me the Fleece!

JASON
It wasn't mine to give!

AEGEUS
Everything is yours to give! You're a king!

JASON
And you're a father who hides behind armies!

They fight. Aegeus is skilled. Jason is desperate.

Aegeus DISARMS Jason. Sword at his throat.

AEGEUS
Yield.

JASON
Never.

Aegeus raises his sword.

An ARROW hits his shoulder. He falls.

Atalanta on the wall. Bow drawn.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE MERCY

EXT. DOCK - DAY

Jason stands over Aegeus. The Athenian king defeated.

JASON
Yield.

AEGEUS
Never.

JASON
(quietly)
I'm not going to kill you.

AEGEUS
Why?

JASON
Because I'm not Pelias. (beat)
Because I'm not you.

He offers his hand. Aegeus stares. Takes it.

AEGEUS
You're a fool.

JASON
Maybe. (beat)
But I'm a living fool.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE PEACE

INT. THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason and Aegeus negotiate. Medea present. Aeson present.

JASON
You wanted an heir. I'll give you one.

AEGEUS
How?

JASON
My firstborn son. He'll be heir to Athens AND Iolcus.

AEGEUS
And what do you get?

JASON
Peace. (beat)
And an alliance.

Aegeus considers.

AEGEUS
Done.

They shake hands. The alliance is sealed.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE MARRIAGE

EXT. PALACE GARDENS - NIGHT

Jason and Medea marry. The heroes celebrate. The people cheer.

AESON
(to Medea)
Take care of him.

MEDEA
I will.

AESON
He's not easy.

MEDEA
I know.

AESON
But he's worth it.

MEDEA
I know that too.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE GODDESS

EXT. PALACE - NIGHT

Hera appears. Alone. Before Jason.

HERA
You've won.

JASON
I have.

HERA
You defied me. You refused my offer. You chose a priestess over a goddess.

JASON
I chose love over power.

HERA
And yet you have both. (beat)
How?

JASON
Because love IS power. The only kind that lasts.

HERA
(quietly)
You remind me of someone.

JASON
Who?

HERA
Myself. (beat)
When I was young.

She fades. Jason stands alone under the stars.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE KING

EXT. PALACE BALCONY - DAWN

Jason stands alone. The sun rises over Iolcus.

Medea joins him.

MEDEA
What are you thinking?

JASON
That I'm not ready.

MEDEA
No one ever is.

JASON
But I have to be.

MEDEA
Then be. (beat)
The kingdom needs you.

JASON
I need you.

MEDEA
You have me. (beat)
Forever.

They stand together. Watching the sun rise.

The FLEECE glows behind them. The covenant fulfilled. The hero transformed.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 08
