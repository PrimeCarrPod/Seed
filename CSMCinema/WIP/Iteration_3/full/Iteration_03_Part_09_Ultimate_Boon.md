# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 09: ULTIMATE BOON
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 09 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo docks. Again. But this time — the crew is different. Changed. They've seen the loom. They've seen Medea become the pattern.

The people of Iolcus gather. Silent. Afraid.

PELIAS
(from the palace steps)
You came back.

JASON
We came back.

PELIAS
And the loom?

JASON
We found it.

PELIAS
(quietly)
And your father's fate?

JASON
I know what happened. He tried to weave himself into the pattern. The pattern wove him out.

PELIAS
(nodding)
I tried to stop him. He wouldn't listen. (beat) The loom doesn't care about want. It only cares about pattern.

JASON
I know. (beat) But I also know — the pattern can be understood. Not controlled. Understood.

---

# PIECE 2: THE BOON

INT. IOLCUS PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason stands before Pelias. The crew behind him. The fleece — no longer glowing — hangs in the temple.

JASON
The fleece was never the boon.

PELIAS
(confused)
Then what was?

JASON
The becoming. The quest. The crew. The choices. (beat) The fleece was just — the excuse. The reason to leave. The reason to become.

PELIAS
Become what?

JASON
Who I was supposed to be.

PELIAS
And who is that?

JASON
(looking at his crew)
A leader. Not because I command. Because I serve. (beat) The boon isn't the fleece. The boon is understanding what matters.

PELIAS
Which is?

JASON
Not the throne. Not the gold. Not the glory. (beat) The people. The choices. The pattern.

---

# PIECE 3: THE GIFT

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The crew prepares to leave. Again. Pelias approaches Jason.

PELIAS
You're leaving again.

JASON
The pattern continues.

PELIAS
Where?

JASON
Wherever it leads. (beat) The map showed other looms. Other patterns. Other quests.

PELIAS
You could stay. Rule. Rest.

JASON
(smiling)
I don't know how to rest.

PELIAS
(sighing)
Your father said the same thing. (beat) Take this.

He hands Jason a small knife. Old. The handle worn smooth.

PELIAS (CONT'D)
Your father's. He left it when he went to the loom. (beat) I kept it. For you. For when you were ready.

JASON
(taking it)
Thank you.

PELIAS
Don't thank me. Just — come back. (beat) Someone should.

JASON
I will.

PELIAS
You can't promise that.

JASON
I just did.

---

# PIECE 4: THE CREW DECIDES

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAY

The crew gathers. Jason stands before them.

JASON
The quest continues. But I won't force anyone. You've given enough. You've seen enough. (beat) If you want to stay — stay. No judgment. No shame.

Silence.

ATALANTA
(standing)
I'm coming.

ZETES
(standing)
We're coming.

CALAIS
(standing)
All of us.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Where you go, we go.

MEDEA
(from the loom, distant, echoing)
*Where you go, we go.*

JASON
(quietly)
Thank you.

ATALANTA
Don't thank us. Just — lead.

---

# PIECE 5: THE SAILING

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - SUNSET

The Argo sails. Again. The crew at the oars. Jason at the bow.

The golden thread around his wrist gleams. The fleece hangs in the temple behind them. Pelias watches from the palace steps.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Open sea. No land for weeks.

JASON
Good.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
What's the next quest?

JASON
(looking at the map)
The second loom. In the land of the Hyperboreans. Beyond the North Wind.

ZETES
Beyond the North Wind?

CALAIS
That's impossible.

JASON
(smiling)
So was the fleece.

---

# PIECE 6: THE PATTERN SPEAKS

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

Jason stands at the bow. The stars above. The golden thread gleaming.

MEDEA
(from the loom, distant)
*Jason.*

JASON
(whispering)
I hear you.

MEDEA
*The pattern is pleased.*

JASON
The pattern has feelings?

MEDEA
*The pattern has purpose. (beat) And its purpose — is you.*

JASON
That's a lot of pressure.

MEDEA
*You can handle it.*

JASON
How do you know?

MEDEA
*Because I wove you that way.*

JASON
(smiling)
You wove me?

MEDEA
*Not alone. But — I helped. (beat) The pattern is collaboration. Not control.*

JASON
Then we're collaborators.

MEDEA
*We always were.*
