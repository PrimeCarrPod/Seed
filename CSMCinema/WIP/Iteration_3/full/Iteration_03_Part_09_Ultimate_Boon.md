# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 09: ULTIMATE BOON
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 09 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE BOON

INT. PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason sits the throne. The FLEECE glows behind him. A YEAR has passed. Peace. Prosperity.

But Jason is restless.

MEDEA
You have everything. Why are you unhappy?

JASON
Because everything isn't enough.

MEDEA
It never is. (beat)
What do you want?

JASON
To understand. (beat)
The Fleece. The covenant. What it all means.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE TEMPLE

EXT. DELPHI - TEMPLE OF APOLLO - DAY

Jason returns to Delphi. The Pythia waits.

PYTHIA
You've returned.

JASON
I need answers.

PYTHIA
You have the throne. The girl. The Fleece. What answers do you need?

JASON
Why me? Why was I chosen?

PYTHIA
You weren't. (beat)
You chose yourself.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE TRUTH

INT. TEMPLE - CONTINUOUS

PYTHIA
The Fleece is not a prize. It's a test.

JASON
I passed.

PYTHIA
No. (beat)
You're still taking it. (beat)
The test is: what do you do with power?

JASON
I've used it well.

PYTHIA
Have you? (beat)
Or have you used it safely?

JASON
What's the difference?

PYTHIA
Safe kings preserve. Great kings transform. (beat)
Which are you?

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE VISION

INT. TEMPLE - NIGHT

Jason sleeps in the temple. He DREAMS.

He sees Greece. UNITED. Not by conquest. By covenant.

He sees the FLEECE. Not gold. But a SYMBOL. Of what mortals can achieve.

He sees HIMSELF. Not a king. But a BRIDGE. Between gods and men.

He wakes.

JASON
(to himself)
I understand now.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE - DAY

Jason returns. Medea waits.

MEDEA
What did you learn?

JASON
That the Fleece was never mine. (beat)
It belongs to everyone.

MEDEA
Then give it back.

JASON
I will. (beat)
To the gods. To the people. To everyone.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE OFFERING

EXT. SACRED GROVE - DAY

Jason places the FLEECE on the sacred oak. The heroes watch.

HERACLES
You're giving it up?

JASON
I'm returning it. (beat)
To where it belongs.

ATALANTA
What will you live on?

JASON
The same thing as everyone else. (beat)
Work. Love. Hope.

The FLEECE GLOWS. Then the light SPREADS. Across Iolcus. Across Greece.

The people feel it. A warmth. A promise.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE COVENANT

EXT. SACRED GROVE - CONTINUOUS

HERA appears. But different. Softer.

HERA
You've done what no hero has done.

JASON
What's that?

HERA
Let go. (beat)
Power. Glory. The Fleece. You let it all go.

JASON
Because it was never mine.

HERA
(quietly)
Now you understand. (beat)
The covenant was never about the Fleece. It was about you.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE GIFT

EXT. SACRED GROVE - NIGHT

Hera offers Jason a GIFT. A simple ring.

HERA
This ring. It has no power. No magic. (beat)
It only reminds you of what you've learned.

JASON
What have I learned?

HERA
That the boon is not the prize. (beat)
The boon is the journey.

Jason takes the ring. Puts it on.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE CELEBRATION

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - NIGHT

The people CELEBRATE. The Fleece is gone. But something else remains.

HOPE.

Jason and Medea dance. The heroes laugh. AESON watches from the side.

AESON
(to Heracles)
My son did it.

HERACLES
He did.

AESON
He's a better man than I ever was.

HERACLES
He had a good teacher.

AESON
No. (beat)
He had a good heart.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE PROPHECY

EXT. PALACE BALCONY - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. Stars above.

MEDEA
What happens now?

JASON
We live. (beat)
We rule. We love.

MEDEA
And when we die?

JASON
Then we die. (beat)
But the story continues.

MEDEA
What story?

JASON
The story of the Fleece. (beat)
The story of the covenant. (beat)
The story of what mortals can become.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE ULTIMATE BOON

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE - DAWN

Jason stands on the balcony. The sun rises. The ring on his finger GLOWS softly.

He is not just a king. He is not just a hero.

He is the BRIDGE. Between what was and what will be.

The FLEECE is gone. But its light remains. In the people. In the land. In him.

HERA (V.O.)
The ultimate boon is not power. It is not glory. It is not even love.

The sun rises higher.

HERA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
The ultimate boon is understanding.

Jason smiles.

JASON
(to himself)
I understand.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 09
