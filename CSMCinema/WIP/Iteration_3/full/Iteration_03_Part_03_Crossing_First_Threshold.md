# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 03: CROSSING FIRST THRESHOLD
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 03 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay

# PIECE 1: THE THRONE ROOM

INT. COLCHIS - THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

AEËTES (60, king of Colchis, golden crown, eyes like a serpent) sits on a throne of bronze. Behind him: the GOLDEN FLEECE, hanging from a sacred oak, glowing with its own light.

Jason stands before him. Guards on every side.

AEËTES
You come for the Fleece.

JASON
I come for my father.

AEËTES
(surprised)
Your father is dead. Pelias told you so.

JASON
Pelias lies.

AEËTES
(leaning forward)
Yes. He does. (beat) Your father lives. In my dungeons. For twenty years.

Jason's face. The confirmation of the oracle.

AEËTES (CONT'D)
The Fleece is not gold. It is the soul of this kingdom. It keeps the land alive. The crops grow because of it. The rivers flow because of it.

JASON
Then why show it to me?

AEËTES
Because you cannot take it. (beat) No man can.

# PIECE 2: THE KING'S DAUGHTER

INT. COLCHIS - PALACE GARDENS - NIGHT

MEDEA (20, daughter of Aeëtes, priestess of Hecate, eyes that see through walls) walks among poisonous plants. She touches them. They bloom.

Jason finds her.

JASON
You're the king's daughter.

MEDEA
You're the fool who wants to die.

JASON
I want the Fleece.

MEDEA
Everyone wants the Fleece. No one understands it. (beat) It's not an object. It's a covenant.

JASON
A covenant with whom?

MEDEA
With the gods. With the land. With death itself. (beat) Break the covenant, and Colchis dies.

JASON
Then why are you telling me this?

MEDEA
Because I read the stars. And the stars say you will succeed. (beat) And I must decide whether to help you or stop you.

# PIECE 3: THE CHALLENGE

INT. COLCHIS - THRONE ROOM - DAY

Aeëtes addresses the court. Jason stands before him.

AE�ëTES
You want the Fleece? Then earn it. (beat) My challenge: yoke the bronze-footed bulls. Plow the field of Ares. Sow the dragon's teeth. What rises — you must kill.

The court MURMURS. This is a death sentence.

JASON
And if I succeed?

AEËTES
The Fleece is yours. And your father goes free.

JASON
And if I fail?

AEËTES
You die. Your heroes die. Your ship burns.

Jason looks at the Fleece. It pulses like a living thing.

JASON
I accept.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: MEDEA'S CHOICE

INT. MEDEA'S CHAMBER - NIGHT

Medea stands over a CAULDRON. Her nurse watches.

NURSE
Don't do this.

MEDEA
He'll die without my help.

NURSE
He's a foreigner. A Greek. Your father's enemy.

MEDEA
He's the man the stars chose. (beat) And I will not let the stars be wrong.

She adds ingredients to the cauldron. The liquid GLOWS.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
The ointment of invulnerability. One application. One day. (beat) Enough to survive the bulls.

NURSE
If your father finds out, he'll kill you.

MEDEA
If my father finds out, I'll already be gone.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE SECRET MEETING

EXT. COLCHIS - SACRED GROVE - NIGHT

Jason waits. Medea emerges from shadows.

MEDEA
Take this. (hands him the ointment)
Rub it on your skin. The fire won't burn you. The horns won't pierce you.

JASON
Why are you helping me?

MEDEA
Because my father is a tyrant. Because the Fleece should not be hoarded. Because—

She stops. Looks at him.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
Because I see what you will become. And I want to be part of it.

JASON
What will I become?

MEDEA
A king. Or a corpse. (beat) The stars are unclear on which.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE FIELD OF ARES

EXT. COLCHIS - FIELD OF ARES - DAWN

A vast field. Bronze plow waiting. In the distance: the BRONZE-FOOTED BULLS, breathing fire.

The heroes watch from a distance. Heracles grips his club.

HERACLES
I should be down there.

ATALANTA
You'd die. The bulls kill everything.

HERACLES
Jason will die too.

ATALANTA
Maybe. (beat) But he chose this.

Jason anoints himself with Medea's ointment. The liquid SHimmers on his skin.

JASON
(to himself)
For my father. For my throne. For the truth.

He steps into the field.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE BULLS

EXT. FIELD OF ARES - CONTINUOUS

The BULLS charge. Fire from their nostrils. Bronze hooves that crack stone.

Jason stands still. The fire hits him — and parts around him. The ointment GLOWS.

The bulls TRY to gore him. Their horns slide off.

Jason grabs the yoke. The bulls ROAR. But he holds.

The crowd falls silent. This has never happened.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE PLOWING

EXT. FIELD OF ARES - CONTINUOUS

Jason yokes the bulls. The plow cuts deep into the earth of Ares. The ground BLEEDS.

Behind him, he sows the DRAGON'S TEETH — sacred, ancient, cursed.

The earth RUMBLES.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE WARRIORS RISE

EXT. FIELD OF ARES - CONTINUOUS

From the planted teeth, ARMED WARRIORS rise. Fully armed. Fully armored. The SPARTOI — the sown men.

They turn toward Jason. Spears raised.

Jason stands alone. Exhausted. The ointment is fading.

SPARTOI LEADER
You sow death. You reap death.

JASON
I sow warriors. I reap allies.

SPARTOI LEADER
We serve only the king who planted us.

JASON
Then serve me. For I planted you.

The Spartoi hesitate. This has never been spoken.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE TRICK

EXT. FIELD OF ARES - CONTINUOUS

Jason picks up a stone. Throws it among the Spartoi.

The stone hits one warrior. The warrior turns — attacks his neighbor.

CHAOS. The Spartoi turn on each other. Swords clash. Armor rings.

Jason watches as they destroy each other. One by one they fall.

The last Spartoi stands. Looks at Jason. Falls.

Silence.

Jason stands alone in the field of the dead.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE FLEECE

INT. COLCHIS - THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

Aeëtes stands. His face unreadable.

AEËTES
You have succeeded.

JASON
The Fleece. And my father.

AEËTES
(quietly)
You have succeeded where all others failed. (beat) But the Fleece is guarded by a dragon that never sleeps.

JASON
Then I'll kill the dragon.

AEËTES
No man can kill it.

JASON
I'm not just a man. (beat) I'm the man who yoked your bulls.

Aeëtes looks at him. Something like respect. Something like fear.

AEËTES
Tomorrow night. The sacred grove. The dragon waits. (beat) And so does my daughter.

Jason understands. Medea will be there.

JASON
Then tomorrow night, this ends.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 03
