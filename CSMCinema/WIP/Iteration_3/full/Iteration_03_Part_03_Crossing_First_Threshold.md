# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 03: CROSSING FIRST THRESHOLD
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 03 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE KING RECEIVES

INT. AETES' PALACE - THRONE ROOM - DAY

The throne room of Colchis. Walls of hammered gold. Torches that burn without smoke. At the far end: AETES (60s, king, eyes like his daughter's, a throne made of knives).

The Argonauts stand before him. Jason at the front.

AETES
Greeks. In my hall. I should have you killed.

JASON
You could try.

AETES
(almost amused)
Confident. Or stupid. (beat) Why have you come?

JASON
For the Golden Fleece.

Laughter from the court. Aetes doesn't laugh.

AETES
Every year. Someone comes for the fleece. They all die. (beat) What makes you different?

JASON
I accepted the quest before I knew what it meant.

AETES
That's not different. That's just younger.

JASON
Then I'm younger. And I'm here. And I won't leave without it.

AETES
(leaning forward)
You want the fleece? Prove you're worthy. Yoke my fire-breathing bulls. Plow the field of Ares. Sow the dragon's teeth. Kill the warriors that spring from the earth. Do this — and we'll discuss the fleece.

JASON
When?

AETES
Tomorrow. Dawn. (beat) Tonight, you feast with us. Guests of Colchis. Under my protection.

He raises his hand. Guards move to surround the Argonauts — not threatening, but present.

AETES (CONT'D)
And under my watch.

---

# PIECE 2: THE FEAST

INT. AETES' PALACE - BANQUET HALL - NIGHT

A feast. Roasted lamb. Wine from Colchis — darker than Greek wine. Fruit that doesn't grow in Greece.

The Argonauts eat carefully. The Colchian court watches them like exhibits.

Jason sits at the high table. Alone. The Colchians keep their distance.

MEDEA (19, black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes, moves like she's afraid of being seen) enters with a pitcher of wine. She pours for the court. Avoids Jason's table.

Their eyes meet. Just for a moment.

She looks away first. Moves to the next table.

ORPHEUS
(quietly, to Jason)
That's her.

JASON
How do you know?

ORPHEUS
Because you haven't breathed since she entered.

Jason realizes he's been holding his breath. Exhales.

JASON
I can't talk to her. Not here. Not with her father watching.

ORPHEUS
Then don't talk. Listen.

Orpheus plays. A simple melody. The court quiets.

Medea, pouring wine at the far end of the hall, stops. Listens. Her hand trembles. Wine spills.

Aetes notices. His eyes narrow.

AETES
(to Medea)
Daughter.

MEDEA
(startled)
Father.

AETES
You're spilling.

She looks down. The wine darkens the cloth.

MEDEA
Forgive me.

She retreats. As she passes Jason's table, something falls from her robe. A small vial. It rolls to Jason's foot.

He picks it up. Looks at her back as she disappears through a side door.

ORPHEUS
(softly)
Don't open it here.

JASON
(turning the vial)
What is it?

ORPHEUS
The beginning.

---

# PIECE 3: THE POTION

INT. AETES' PALACE - GUEST CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Jason sits on a stone bed. The vial in his hand. Orpheus watches from the door.

JASON
She gave me this deliberately.

ORPHEUS
Obviously.

JASON
Why?

ORPHEUS
Because she chose you. The moment she saw you. The moment you saw her. The pattern recognized itself.

JASON
I don't even know her name.

ORPHEUS
Yes, you do.

JASON
(quietly)
Medea.

ORPHEUS
Hecate told you.

JASON
Hecate tells me everything and nothing.

Orpheus smiles. Sits across from Jason.

ORPHEUS
Open it.

Jason uncorks the vial. The scent: herbs. Something else. Something that makes his fingers tingle.

JASON
What is it?

ORPHEUS
A gift. Or a test. Or both. (beat) Medea is a priestess of Hecate. She knows herbs. Poisons. Medicines. This — (smelling it) — this is protection.

JASON
From what?

ORPHEUS
From what tomorrow brings.

JASON
The bulls.

ORPHEUS
The bulls. The fire. The teeth. The warriors. (beat) She's giving you a way to survive.

JASON
Why would she help me? She doesn't know me.

ORPHEUS
(standing)
She knows enough. The pattern told her.

Jason looks at the vial. Drinks. The effect is immediate — warmth spreading through his veins. His hands stop shaking.

ORPHEUS (CONT'D)
Sleep. Tomorrow you face fire.

---

# PIECE 4: THE TEMPLE

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

Medea kneels before the altar. Three torches burn. She prays in a language Jason wouldn't understand.

MEDEA
(whispering)
Goddess. I saw him. The Greek. The one from the prophecy.

The torches flicker.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
I gave him the draught. As you commanded. (beat) But goddess — I don't know him. How can I betray everything for someone I don't know?

The flames rise.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(whispering)
Because you know him. Because the pattern demands it. Because I was born for this.

She lowers her head.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
I understand. (beat) I don't accept it. But I understand.

She rises. Turns. JASON stands in the doorway.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(startled)
You shouldn't be here.

JASON
You gave me the vial.

MEDEA
I gave you medicine. For the bulls tomorrow.

JASON
You gave me more than that.

MEDEA
(stepping back)
You don't know what I gave you.

JASON
Then tell me.

MEDEA
(afraid)
I can't. If I tell you, it becomes real. And if it becomes real—

JASON
It becomes real anyway.

MEDEA
(almost crying)
You don't understand. If I help you — I betray my father. My kingdom. Everything I've ever known.

JASON
Then don't help me.

MEDEA
(staring at him)
You'd fail.

JASON
Probably.

MEDEA
You'd die.

JASON
Likely.

MEDEA
And you're telling me not to help you?

JASON
I'm telling you it's your choice. Not the goddess's. Not the pattern's. Yours.

MEDEA
(whispering)
If it were that simple.

JASON
It is that simple. It's just not easy.

She looks at him. Really looks at him. Seeing him for the first time.

MEDEA
What's your name?

JASON
Jason.

MEDEA
I know your name. (beat) I mean — who are you? Who is the man who came to Colchis? Who is the man who accepted a death quest without hesitation? Who is the man who tells a priestess not to help him even though he'll die without her?

JASON
(thinking)
I'm someone who's trying to finish what his father started. Someone who believes the gods recognize rather than choose. Someone who's terrified but keeps walking forward anyway.

MEDEA
(quietly)
That's the first honest thing anyone has said to me in years.

---

# PIECE 5: THE CHOICE

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

They stand in the torchlight. The goddess watches.

MEDEA
If I help you — I can never come back here. My father will disown me. My kingdom will reject me. My people will call me traitor.

JASON
I know.

MEDEA
You don't know. You can't know. You're not—

JASON
I'm not a woman who has to choose between love and family? Between her heart and her home? Between who she is and who she's supposed to be?

MEDEA
(surprised)
You do know.

JASON
I know what it's like to lose everything for a choice. My father lost his throne. I grew up in exile. I chose to come here knowing I'd never be the same.

MEDEA
And was it worth it?

JASON
I don't know yet. Ask me after the bulls.

MEDEA
(a small smile)
After the bulls. After the teeth. After the dragon. After everything. (beat) If we survive — then ask me.

JASON
We?

MEDEA
(taking a breath)
I choose the pattern. I choose the choice. I choose—

She stops. Can't say it.

JASON
You don't have to say it.

MEDEA
I know. (beat) But I need to. I choose to help you. I choose to betray my father. I choose to leave my kingdom. I choose— (looking at him) — you.

---

# PIECE 6: THE PLAN

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

Medea draws a map in the dust.

MEDEA
The bulls are kept in underground pens. Bronze hooves. Fire breath. They melt stone.

JASON
I noticed.

MEDEA
The field of Ares is four hundred meters square. Volcanic soil. You need to plow it, sow the dragon's teeth, and kill the warriors that spring up. All before sunset.

JASON
How do I yoke the bulls?

MEDEA
The draught I gave you — it makes you fireproof. For one hour. Enough to approach them. To touch them. To make them recognize you as master.

JASON
And the warriors?

MEDEA
When they spring from the earth, they'll be confused. Disoriented. You have maybe ten seconds before they organize. Throw a stone into their midst. They'll fight each other. The Spartoi — dragon's teeth men — they attack whatever moves nearest.

JASON
Including each other.

MEDEA
Exactly.

JASON
And the fleece?

MEDEA
(looking away)
The fleece is in the grove of Ares. Guarded by a dragon. Sleepless. Immortal. It has never closed its eyes.

JASON
How do I kill something immortal?

MEDEA
You don't. You put it to sleep.

She produces a second vial. Larger. Darker.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
This is stronger than the first. Made from the poppy, the mandrake, and — other things. Things only Hecate knows. Pour it into the dragon's water. It will sleep for one hour.

JASON
One hour to take the fleece and escape.

MEDEA
One hour to take the fleece, find me, and run. My father will know immediately. His army will follow. We'll have to move fast.

JASON
We?

MEDEA
I'm coming with you. (beat) I told you. I chose.

---

# PIECE 7: THE KING'S SUSPICION

INT. AETES' PRIVATE CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Aetes stands at a window. His advisor, CHONOR, approaches.

CHONOR
The Greek — Jason — he went to the temple.

AETES
Which temple?

CHONOR
Your daughter's.

AETES
(turning)
Medea?

CHONOR
They talked. For some time.

AETES
(considering)
My daughter helps him.

CHONOR
You don't know that.

AETES
I know my daughter. She has her mother's heart. Soft. Easily turned. (beat) Watch her. If she gives him anything — herbs, potions, advice — report to me.

CHONOR
And if she does?

AETES
Then we'll know the Greeks have a traitor in my house. (beat) And traitors must be punished.

---

# PIECE 8: THE DAWN

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - DAWN

The field stretches before them. Black earth, cracked like dragon hide. In the distance: two bronze pens.

Aetes sits on his throne of knives. The Colchian court watches from a pavilion. Medea stands beside her father. Face blank.

JASON stands at the field's edge. Alone. The Argonauts watch from a distance.

AETES
(calling)
Greek. You have until sunset. Yoke the bulls. Plow the field. Sow the teeth. Kill the warriors. Fail — and you die. Succeed — and we'll discuss the fleece.

JASON
(calling back)
I understand.

AETES
Release the bulls.

The pens open. Two creatures emerge. Not cattle. Forged. Bronze hooves spark on stone. Their nostrils glow red. The air shimmers with heat.

Jason walks forward. Empty-handed.

ATALANTA
(from the distance)
He's going to die.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
He's going to try.

The bulls see Jason. Lower their heads. Fire builds in their throats.

Jason doesn't stop walking.

---

# PIECE 9: THE YOKE

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - CONTINUOUS

Jason approaches the bulls. The heat is immense. The ground smolders under his feet.

But the draught holds. The fire doesn't touch him.

He reaches the first bull. Puts his hand on its muzzle.

The bull snarls. Breathes fire.

Jason doesn't burn.

JASON
(quietly)
I'm not your enemy. I'm your new master.

The bull's eyes narrow. Then — recognition. It kneels.

The second bull watches. Jason approaches. Same gesture. Same result.

The second bull kneels.

The Colchian court murmurs. Aetes stands.

Jason yokes both bulls. They rise. Waiting.

He takes the plow. The bulls pull. The black earth turns. The field is plowed.

---

# PIECE 10: THE TEETH

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - CONTINUOUS

Jason sows the dragon's teeth. Each one a black diamond. Each one pulsing with something ancient.

The earth trembles.

Warriors spring from the soil. Armored. Armed. Confused. Angry. They number — ten — twenty — thirty — more.

Jason picks up a stone. Throws it into their midst.

The nearest warrior strikes at the movement. Hits another warrior. That warrior strikes back. Within seconds — they're fighting each other.

Jason watches. Waits. The warriors destroy each other.

One remains. It turns to Jason. Raises its sword.

Jason picks up a broken sword from a fallen warrior. They fight.

The warrior is strong but clumsy. Born minutes ago. No training. No instinct beyond violence.

Jason is trained. Chiron taught him. Twenty years of training.

He wins. The warrior falls. Dissolves into earth.

Silence.

Jason stands in the field. Covered in ash. Alive.

The Colchian court is silent.

AETES
(quietly, to Chonor)
My daughter helped him.

CHONOR
Yes.

AETES
Bring her to me.

---

# PIECE 11: THE GROVE

EXT. GROVE OF ARES - NIGHT

Jason runs. Behind him: chaos. Aetes' guards searching. Medea — gone. Taken by her father.

The grove appears through the trees. And there — the dragon. Vast. Coiled. Eyes that have never closed.

The Golden Fleece hangs from a branch. Glowing. Warm. Alive.

Jason pulls out the second vial. Approaches the dragon's water — a pool at the base of the tree.

The dragon watches. Doesn't move. Doesn't blink.

JASON
(whispering)
Just drink. Just sleep.

He pours the vial into the pool. The dragon drinks.

Nothing happens.

Then — the dragon's eyes close. Just for a moment. Just for—

Jason climbs. Fast. Reaches the fleece. Pulls it from the branch.

The moment it comes free — the dragon wakes. ROAR.

Jason jumps. Lands. Runs.

The dragon follows. Fire. Teeth. Rage.

Jason runs toward the harbor. Toward the Argo. Toward—

MEDEA
(from the shadows)
Here!

She's bleeding. Her father hit her. But she's free.

JASON
The fleece!

MEDEA
I see it. Run!

They run together. The dragon behind them. The harbor ahead. The Argo waiting.

ORPHEUS
(from the ship)
Row!

The Argonauts row. The ship moves. Jason and Medea dive into the water. Climb aboard.

The dragon reaches the shore. Stops. It won't enter the water.

It watches them go. Eyes open. Always open.

MEDEA
(holding the fleece)
We did it.

JASON
We did it.

MEDEA
(looking at him)
Now comes the hard part.

JASON
What's that?

MEDEA
Living with the choice.

The Argo sails. Colchis burns behind them. The fleece glows between them.

END OF PART 03
