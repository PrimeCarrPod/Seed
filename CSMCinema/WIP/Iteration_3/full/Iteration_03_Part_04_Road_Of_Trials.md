# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 04: ROAD OF TRIALS
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 04 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE SACRED GROVE

EXT. COLCHIS - SACRED GROVE - NIGHT

Ancient oaks. Moonlight through leaves. In the center: the GOLDEN FLEECE on its oak, and coiled around it — the DRAGON. Massic. Scales like bronze. Eyes open. Never sleeping.

Jason approaches alone. Medea waits in the shadows.

MEDEA
(whispering)
The dragon has watched the Fleece for a thousand years. It knows every trick.

JASON
What does it not know?

MEDEA
Music.

She hands him a branch of a sacred tree.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
Hecate's branch. The dragon fears it. But only for a moment.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE DRAGON

EXT. SACRED GROVE - CONTINUOUS

Jason steps forward. The dragon's EYES open. All of them. It sees everything.

DRAGON
(in a voice like grinding stone)
Another thief.

JASON
Another hero.

DRAGON
Heroes die faster than thieves. They have more to lose.

The dragon STRIKES. Jason rolls. The branch of Hecate GLOWS. The dragon RECOILS.

Jason runs for the Fleece. The dragon's tail WHIPS — catches him. He flies. Hits a tree.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE HEROES ARRIVE

EXT. SACRED GROVE - CONTINUOUS

HERACLES bursts through the trees. Orpheus behind him. Atalanta with an arrow nocked.

HERACLES
Jason!

The dragon turns. Sees them. LAUGHS.

DRAGLE
More heroes. More bones for my grove.

Heracles charges. His club hits the dragon's skull. The dragon doesn't flinch.

Orpheus plays. The music fills the grove. The dragon PAUSES — confused, disoriented.

ATALANTA
(shoots)
Now!

The arrow hits the dragon's eye. It ROARS.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE FLEECE

EXT. SACRED GROVE - CONTINUOUS

Jason reaches the Fleece. Touches it. It BURNS with golden light.

The dragon SCREAMS. Turns. Fire from its mouth.

Medea steps forward. Speaks words in a language older than Greek. The fire PARTS around her.

MEDEA
Take it! NOW!

Jason pulls the Fleece from the oak. The grove SHAKES. The dragon WRITHES.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE ESCAPE

EXT. COLCHIS - HARBOR - NIGHT

The heroes RUN. The Argos is waiting. Behind them: the dragon, the guards, the king's army.

Jason carries the Fleece. It glows like a sun.

They reach the ship. The heroes PULL the oars. The Argos shoots forward.

Aeëtes appears on the walls. His face RAGE.

AEËTES
MEDEA! YOU BETRAYED YOUR FATHER!

Medea stands on the ship. Looks back at him.

MEDEA
No, father. I chose a better one.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE PURSUIT

EXT. BLACK SEA - DAY

The Argos sails. Behind them: the Colchian fleet. Faster. Gaining.

HERACLES
They'll catch us.

JASON
Not if we're faster.

HERACLES
We're not faster. We're desperate.

ATALANTA
Desperation is faster than speed.

Jason holds the Fleece. It pulses. He understands something.

JASON
The Fleece. It wants to go home.

MEDEA
It wants to go where it belongs.

JASON
Then show me.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE STORM

EXT. BLACK SEA - NIGHT

A STORM rises. Not natural. Aeëtes has called it. Waves like mountains. Wind like knives.

The Argos climbs a wave. Crests. Falls.

ORPHEUS
We're going to die!

JASON
No. We're going to live. (holds up the Fleece)
This is not just gold. It's a covenant. And covenants have power.

The Fleece GLOWS. The storm CALMS. The sea goes flat.

The heroes stare.

HERACLES
What did you do?

JASON
I made a promise.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE FATHER

INT. COLCHIS - DUNGEONS - NIGHT

Aeëtes stands before a cell. Inside: AESON (70, Jason's father, broken but alive).

AEËTES
Your son has the Fleece.

AESON
(weakly)
Then he's coming for me.

AEËTES
He's coming. But he won't reach you. (beat) I'll kill him at the docks.

AESON
You can't kill him. He's already survived the bulls. The dragon. The Spartoi.

AEËTES
Then I'll survive him. (beat) As I always have.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE RETURN

EXT. AEGEAN SEA - DAY

The Argos sails into familiar waters. Greece ahead. The heroes are exhausted. Wounded. But alive.

Jason stands at the prow. The Fleece in his hands. Medea beside him.

MEDEA
What will you do with it?

JASON
Return it to the gods. End the covenant. Free my father.

MEDEA
And then?

JASON
Then I take my throne.

MEDEA
And me?

JASON
(looks at her)
You come with me.

MEDEA
As what? Wife? Prisoner? Witch?

JASON
As the woman who saved my life.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE APPROACH

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - SUNSET

The Argos approaches Iolcus. The harbor is CROWDED. Word has spread.

Pelias watches from his balcony. His face pale.

ADVISOR
He has the Fleece.

PELIAS
I can see that.

ADVISOR
What do we do?

PELIAS
What I should have done twenty years ago. (beat) Kill them all.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE DOCK

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - NIGHT

The Argos docks. Jason steps onto Greek soil. The Fleece GLOWS.

The crowd parts. Pelias approaches with guards.

PELIAS
Jason. You've returned.

JASON
I have the Fleece. I've come for my father's throne.

PELIAS
Your father is dead.

JASON
No. He's in your dungeons. (beat) And you're going to release him.

Pelias smiles. It doesn't reach his eyes.

PELIAS
Or what?

JASON
Or I tell Greece what you did. The exile. The lies. The murder attempts.

PELIAS
(quietly)
Greece doesn't care about truth. Greece cares about power.

JASON
Then let me show you power.

He holds up the Fleece. The crowd GASPS. The light is blinding.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 04
