# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 06: TEMPTATION AWAY FROM PATH
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 06 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE THRONE

INT. IOLCUS - THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason sits on the throne. The FLEECE hangs behind him, dimmed. Medea stands at his side.

The COURT kneels. All except Pelias's supporters, who watch from the shadows.

HERACLES
(whispering)
You're a king now. Act like one.

JASON
(whispering back)
I don't know how.

HERACLES
None of us do. We fake it.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 2: THE TEMPTATION

INT. THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

Jason alone. The court has left. APATE (goddess of deceit, beautiful, dangerous) appears.

APATE
You've won. The throne. The Fleece. The girl.

JASON
Who are you?

APATE
I am every choice you didn't make. (beat) And I'm here to offer you one more.

JASON
I don't want more.

APATE
You want peace. I can give it. Medea can be your queen. But she'll always be Hecate's priestess. Always serve another master.

JASON
She serves me now.

APATE
Does she? (beat) Or do you serve Hecate through her?

CUT TO:

# PIECE 3: THE DOUBT

INT. JASON'S CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Jason can't sleep. Medea watches him.

MEDEA
What's wrong?

JASON
Are you mine? Or Hecate's?

MEDEA
(quietly)
Both. (beat) I'm a priestess. That doesn't change.

JASON
I need to know you're loyal to me.

MEDEA
I betrayed my father for you. I defied Hera for you. What more proof do you need?

JASON
I don't know.

MEDEA
(stands)
Then you don't know me at all.

She leaves. Jason sits alone.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 4: THE COUNSEL

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE GARDENS - DAY

Heracles finds Jason brooding.

HERACLES
You're pushing her away.

JASON
She serves a goddess.

HERACLES
You served Hera. You carry the Fleece of the gods. We all serve something.

JASON
What do you serve?

HERACLES
My own glory. (beat) It's simpler. And lonelier.

JASON
Which would you choose? Love or glory?

HERACLES
I chose glory. Look at me. (beat) Don't be like me.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 5: THE PLOTTERS

INT. PELIAS'S OLD CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Pelias's supporters meet in secret. Led by AUTOLYCUS (40, cunning, cousin of Hermes).

AUTOLYCUS
Pelias is broken. But we're not.

ALCESTIS
What do you propose?

AUTOLYCUS
Jason's weakness is the woman. Remove her, and he falls.

ALCESTIS
How?

AUTOLYCUS
Hera wants her dead. We give Hera what she wants. And Hera gives us what we want.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 6: THE WARNING

INT. JASON'S CHAMBERS - NIGHT

Orpheus enters, lyre in hand.

ORPHEUS
I heard something. In the music.

JASON
What?

ORPHEUS
A plot. Against Medea. (beat) They're going to kill her.

JASON
Who?

ORPHEUS
Pelias's people. With Hera's blessing.

Jason stands. Grabs his sword.

JASON
Where?

ORPHEUS
The temple. Tonight.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 7: THE TEMPLE

EXT. TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Medea prays alone. Around her: torches, smoke, sacred knives.

AUTOLYCUS and armed men enter.

AUTOLYCUS
The goddess Medea. Priestess of Hecate. Enemy of Hera.

MEDEA
(standing)
I'm no enemy of Hera.

AUTOLYCUS
You chose a mortal over a goddess. That makes you an enemy.

He raises his sword.

JASON (O.S.)
Stop.

Jason enters. Heracles behind him. Atalanta with an arrow nocked.

JASON (CONT'D)
She's under my protection.

AUTOLYCUS
She's under Hera's judgment.

JASON
Hera has no jurisdiction in Iolcus. (beat) This is my kingdom. My rules.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 8: THE CHOICE

EXT. TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

Autolycus hesitates. His men look to him.

AUTOLYCUS
You can't protect her forever.

JASON
I can try.

Autolycus lowers his sword. Leaves.

Jason turns to Medea.

MEDEA
You came.

JASON
Always.

MEDEA
Even when I serve another?

JASON
(looks at her)
You serve love. That's the same god I serve.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 9: THE VOW

INT. TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. The sacred fire burns.

MEDEA
I need to tell you something.

JASON
What?

MEDEA
Hecate came to me. In a dream. She offered me power. Real power. To be her high priestess. To rule Colchis through her.

JASON
What did you say?

MEDEA
I said no. (beat) But the offer stands. And I'm tempted.

JASON
Then take it.

MEDEA
(surprised)
What?

JASON
If that's what you want, take it. I won't trap you.

MEDEA
You'd let me go?

JASON
I'd let you choose. (beat) That's what love is.

Medea looks at him. Something shifts.

MEDEA
I choose you. (beat) I'll always choose you.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 10: THE BETRAYAL

INT. PALACE - NIGHT

Alcimde enters Jason's chambers. She carries a scroll.

ALCIMEDE
A message from Athens. Your uncle is coming.

JASON
My uncle?

ALCIMEDE
Aegeus. King of Athens. He wants to meet the new king of Iolcus.

Jason reads the scroll. His face changes.

JASON
He's bringing an army.

ALCIMEDE
He says it's a diplomatic visit.

JASON
No. It's a conquest. (beat) He wants the Fleece.

CUT TO:

# PIECE 11: THE ALLIANCE

INT. THRONE ROOM - DAY

Jason addresses his heroes.

JASON
Aegeus comes with an army. He wants the Fleece. He wants my throne.

HERACLES
Then we fight.

JASON
No. We negotiate. (beat) I'll give him what he wants.

ATALANTA
The Fleece?

JASON
No. Something better. (beat) An alliance. Athens and Iolcus, united.

HERACLES
He won't accept.

JASON
He will. Because I'm going to offer him what he really wants.

HERACLES
What's that?

JASON
A son. (beat) Heirless king meets the hero who retrieved the Fleece. It's the beginning of a beautiful friendship.

Medea watches. She sees what Jason is becoming. Not just a hero. A king.

FADE TO BLACK.

END OF PART 06
