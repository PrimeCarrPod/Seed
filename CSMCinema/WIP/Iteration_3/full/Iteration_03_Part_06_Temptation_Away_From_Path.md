# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 06: TEMPTATION AWAY FROM PATH
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 06 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE PERSIAN EMISSARY

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

A ship approaches. White flag. No weapons visible.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
One ship. Persian colors. Emissary flag.

JASON
Persian?

MEDEA writes:

*LET THEM COME.*

The Persian ship pulls alongside. An EMISSARY (50s, silk robes, gold, speaks Greek with an accent) boards.

EMISSARY
Jason of Iolcus. I bring greetings from the Great King.

JASON
I didn't ask for greetings.

EMISSARY
(smiling)
No. But you'll want these. (beat) The Great King offers alliance. Your fleece for our gold. Your courage for our armies. Together — you could rule the world.

JASON
I don't want the world.

EMISSARY
Everyone says that. Until they see what the world offers. (beat) The throne of Iolcus is small. Pelias holds it. You'd have to kill him. Kill his supporters. Kill everyone who owes him loyalty. How many deaths, Jason? How many murders for a small throne?

JASON
That's my problem.

EMISSARY
Make it ours. The Great King's armies remove Pelias. Place you on the throne. All you need do — acknowledge him as overlord. Pay tribute. Small price.

MEDEA writes:

*NO.*

EMISSARY
(to Medea)
The priestess speaks.

MEDEA writes:

*I DON'T NEED TO SPEAK TO KNOW A TRAP.*

EMISSARY
(laughing)
A trap? No. An opportunity. (beat) Think about it. You have until we reach Iolcus.

He returns to his ship. Sails ahead.

JASON
(to Medea)
You think it's a trap?

She writes:

*I KNOW IT IS.*

JASON
Then why did you say no for me?

She writes:

*BECAUSE YOU WEREN'T SAYING NO FOR YOURSELF.*

---

# PIECE 2: THE TEMPTATION

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - NIGHT

Jason sits alone. The fleece beside him. He stares at it.

ATALANTA
(entering)
You're thinking about it.

JASON
About what?

ATALANTA
The Persian offer. (beat) Don't lie. I can see it on your face.

JASON
(sighing)
It would be easier.

ATALANTA
Easier isn't better.

JASON
Isn't it? Pelias has fifty warriors. I have forty-nine heroes. The math is close. People will die. My people.

ATALANTA
And the Persian offer?

JASON
No one dies. Pelias falls. I take the throne. Everyone lives.

ATALANTA
Except you become the Persian king's puppet.

JASON
Better a puppet than a corpse.

ATALANTA
(sitting)
You don't believe that.

JASON
How do you know?

ATALANTA
Because cowards don't steal fleece from dragons. (beat) You're not afraid of dying. You're afraid of killing.

JASON
(quietly)
Is that wrong?

ATALANTA
No. It's human. (beat) But it's also a luxury. Sometimes — killing is necessary.

JASON
When?

ATALANTA
When the alternative is worse. (beat) The Persian king doesn't offer alliance. He offers ownership. You'll owe him. Forever. And debts to kings — they never end.

---

# PIECE 3: THE COUNCIL

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAWN

The crew gathers. Jason stands before them.

JASON
The Persian king offered me an army. To take Iolcus. To remove Pelias.

Silence.

JASON (CONT'D)
All I have to do — is bow.

ZETES
And if you do?

JASON
We all live. Pelias falls without a fight.

CALAIS
And if you don't?

JASON
We fight. Some of us die. Maybe all of us.

The crew murmurs.

MEDEAELA
What do you want to do?

JASON
I want to know what you think.

PERICLYMENUS
We followed you for the fleece. Not for a throne.

ZETES
But the throne is the point. Without it — the fleece is just a trophy.

ORPHEUS
The fleece was never the point. The quest was the point. The becoming was the point.

CASTOR
So what do we do?

POLLUX
We choose.

ATALANTA
(standing)
I'll tell you what I think. The Persian king doesn't give. He lends. And his interest is your soul. (beat) I've seen men take his gold. They all regret it.

ORPHEUS
And I'll tell you what I think. The pattern doesn't bend to convenience. You can take the easy path. But you'll arrive somewhere else.

JASON
Where?

ORPHEUS
Nowhere you want to be.

---

# PIECE 4: THE VOICE

EXT. ARGO - BOW - NIGHT

Jason and Medea. The stars above. The fleece glows softly between them.

JASON
I need your advice. Really need it.

She writes:

*THEN HONESTLY. (beat) THE PERSIAN OFFER IS A TRAP. NOT BECAUSE IT'S FALSE. BECAUSE IT'S TRUE. THEY WILL GIVE YOU THE THRONE. AND YOU WILL SPEND YOUR LIFE PAYING FOR IT.*

JASON
So I refuse?

She writes:

*I DIDN'T SAY THAT.*

JASON
Then what?

She wrote:

*REFUSING IS EASIER. ACCEPTING IS HARDER. BUT EASIER ISN'T ALWAYS RIGHT. (beat) IF YOU REFUSE — YOU FIGHT. PEOPLE DIE. BUT YOU'RE FREE. (beat) IF YOU ACCEPT — NO ONE DIES. BUT YOU'RE OWNED.*

JASON
Which would you choose?

She writes:

*ALREADY CHOSE. I CHOSE YOU. I CHOSE THE HARD PATH. THE PATH WHERE PEOPLE GET HURT AND NOTHING IS SIMPLE. (beat) BUT I'M FREE.*

JASON
Even without a voice?

She writes:

*ESPECIALLY WITHOUT A VOICE. (beat) BECAUSE NOW EVERYTHING I SAY — I MEAN. I CAN'T AFFORD WASTED WORDS.

JASON
(smiling)
You've never wasted a word.

She writes:

*EXACTLY.*

---

# PIECE 5: THE DECISION

EXT. ARGO - DECK - DAWN

The Persian ship waits ahead. The Emissary watches from his deck.

Jason stands at the prow. The crew behind him.

JASON
(calling)
Emissary!

EMISSARY
(calling back)
Yes?

JASON
Tell your king — no.

EMISSARY
(calling)
No to alliance?

JASON
No to ownership. No to debt. No to easy paths that lead nowhere.

EMISSARY
(calling)
You're a fool, Greek.

JASON
(calling back)
Maybe. But I'm a free fool.

The Emissary shakes his head. The Persian ship turns. Sails away.

The crew exhales.

ATALANTA
(to Jason)
That was stupid.

JASON
I know.

ATALANTA
(grinning)
I'm proud of you.

---

# PIECE 6: THE WARNING

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - NIGHT

Medea writes. Jason watches.

MEDEA writes:

*HE'LL REMEMBER. THE PERSIAN KING DOESN'T FORGET REFUSALS.*

JASON
Let him remember.

She writes:

*YOU DON'T UNDERSTAND. HE'LL WAIT. YEARS IF NECESSARY. AND WHEN YOU'RE VULNERABLE —*

JASON
Then we'll face it.

She writes:

*YOU CAN'T FACE EVERYTHING.*

JASON
Watch me.

She writes:

*YOU'RE IMPOSSIBLE.*

JASON
I learned from you.

She writes:

*I NEVER TAUGHT YOU STUBBORNNESS.*

JASON
You taught me everything else. Stubbornness was implied.

She almost smiles. Writes:

*THANK YOU. FOR REFUSING.*

JASON
You already thanked me.

She writes:

*NOT FOR YOU. FOR ME. (beat) IF YOU'D ACCEPTED — I'D HAVE LOST YOU TO THE PERSIAN KING. AND I'D HAVE LOST MYSELF.*

JASON
You'd still be Medea.

She writes:

*WOULD I? OR WOULD I BE SOMEONE'S PROPERTY AGAIN? THE PERSIAN KING OWNS EVERYTHING HIS SUBJECTS LOVE. (beat) I CHOSE FREEDOM. YOU JUST PROVED YOU DID TOO.*

---

# PIECE 7: THE GHOST

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. Dreams.

ARISTAEUS (50s, Jason's father, dead these twenty years) stands at the bow. Transparent. Ghost or dream.

ARISTAEUS
Son.

JASON
(in the dream)
Father?

ARISTAEUS
You refused the Persian offer.

JASON
How do you know?

ARISTAEUS
I've been watching. (beat) I'm proud of you.

JASON
I'm going to have to kill Pelias.

ARISTAEUS
I know.

JASON
He's your brother.

ARISTAEUS
He's a usurper. A thief. A murderer. (beat) He stole my throne. He sent you away. He stole your childhood.

JASON
So I kill him?

ARISTAEUS
(hesitating)
I don't know. (beat) I wanted revenge. For years. But now — now that you're close — I wonder if revenge is worth what it costs.

JASON
What does it cost?

ARISTAEUS
Your innocence. (beat) Once you kill — even justly — you're different. Forever.

JASON
Then what do I do?

ARISTAEUS
I can't tell you. (beat) But I can tell you this — the throne isn't worth your soul. If you become king by becoming a murderer — you've already lost.

JASON
Then how do I win?

ARISTAEUS
(smiling)
You already have. You stole the fleece. You chose love over convenience. You refused the easy path. (beat) The throne is just metal. What you've built — that's real.

The ghost fades.

Jason wakes.

---

# PIECE 8: THE APPROACH

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The coast of Greece appears. Home waters.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Iolcus. Two hours.

The crew gathers. Some excited. Some afraid. All ready.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Home.

ZETES
If it's still home.

CALAIS
It's been twenty years for some of us.

MEDEA writes:

*IS IT STILL HOME IF EVERYONE YOU KNEW IS GONE?*

JASON
(reading)
I don't know. (beat) But we'll find out together.

She writes:

*TOGETHER.*

---

# PIECE 9: THE FLEET

EXT. OPEN SEA - LATER

ATALANTA
(from above)
Ships. Dozens. Colchian colors.

The crew freezes.

JASON
Absyrtus?

ATALANTA
No. Aetes himself. His entire fleet.

MEDEA writes:

*HE CAME.*

JASON
How many ships?

ATALANTA
Fifty. Maybe more.

JASON
We can't fight fifty ships.

ORPHEUS
We can't outrun them either.

ATALANTA
(from above)
They're forming a line. Across the harbor entrance. We can't reach Iolcus without passing through.

JASON
(thinking)
Then we don't go through.

MEDEA writes:

*WE GO AROUND.*

JASON
There is no around. They're blocking the only entrance.

She writes:

*THEN WE MAKE A NEW ENTRANCE.*

---

# PIECE 10: THE TRICK

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Colchian fleet blocks the harbor. The Argo approaches slowly.

JASON
(to the crew)
Lower the sail. Raise a white flag.

ZETES
We're surrendering?

JASON
We're pretending to surrender.

The Argo approaches the center of the Colchian line. Aetes' flagship pulls alongside.

AETES
(calling)
Greek! You stole my daughter! You stole the fleece! There's nowhere to run!

JASON
(calling back)
I know! I surrender! Let me aboard! Let's talk!

AETES
(calling)
Talk? You want to talk?

JASON
(calling)
What I took — I took for love! Not for war! Let me explain!

A long pause. Then:

AETES
(calling)
Come aboard! Alone! And bring my daughter!

JASON
(to Medea, quietly)
You don't have to—

She writes:

*I'M COMING.*

---

# PIECE 11: THE FATHER

EXT. AETES' FLAGSHIP - DECK - DAY

Jason and Medea board. Aetes stands before them. Guards surround them.

AETES
(to Medea)
Daughter. Come home.

Medea writes:

*NO.*

AETES
You'd choose this Greek over your father? Over your kingdom? Over your blood?

She writes:

*I CHOOSE MYSELF.*

AETES
(angry)
Yourself? You are nothing without me! Without Colchis! Without—

MEDEA writes:

*I AM EVERYTHING. (beat) I AM THE LOOM. I AM THE PATTERN. I AM THE PRIESTESS. (beat) I WAS NEVER YOURS. I WAS ALWAYS MY OWN.*

AETES
(to Jason)
You did this. You poisoned her against me.

JASON
She poisoned herself. With truth.

AETES
(draws his sword)
Then truth will kill you.

Medea steps between them. Writes:

*YOU HAVE TO GO THROUGH ME.*

AETES
(trembling)
Move, daughter.

She writes:

*NO.*

A long moment. Aetes stares at his daughter. She stares back. Unflinching.

AETES
(lowering the sword)
Go. Both of you. Before I change my mind.

MEDEA writes:

*FATHER—

AETES
(turning away)
You're not my daughter. Not anymore.

Medea's face falls. Jason takes her hand. They return to the Argo.

The Colchian fleet parts. Just enough.

JASON
(quietly)
I'm sorry.

She writes:

*DON'T BE. (beat) HE CHOSE THRONE OVER DAUGHTER. (beat) I CHOSE SELF OVER FATHER. (beat) THE PATTERN DEMANDS SACRIFICE.

JASON
It shouldn't have to.

She writes:

*BUT IT DOES.*

The Argo passes through the Colchian line. Iolcus appears ahead.

Home.
