# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 01: CALL TO ADVENTURE

**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 01 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay

# PIECE 1: THE ORDINARY WORLD

FADE IN:

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

The market is empty. Fish scales glisten in early light. Smoke rises from brick ovens, curling like thread. A DOG crosses the square. A CART rattles on cobblestones.

PELIAS (50s, usurper king, eyes that haven't slept in twenty years) stands on his balcony. Below, the ordinary world breathes.

ADVISOR
He comes, my lord.

PELIAS
I know.

ADVISOR
One sandal. Just as the oracle said.

PELIAS
I know what the oracle said. (beat) Let him come.

Below, JASON (20, golden hair, river mud on his ankle, one sandal) enters the market. Alone. The market people part. Not from fear — from recognition. Something in his walk. Something in his face.

Jason looks up at the balcony.

JASON
Uncle. I've come for my father's throne.

PELIAS
Your father died in exile. Tragic. But the throne requires more than birthright. It requires proof.

JASON
What proof?

PELIAS
The Golden Fleece. Retrieve it from Colchis. Bring it here. Then we'll discuss your birthright.

The market murmurs. Everyone knows this is a death sentence.

JASON
(without hesitation)
I accept.

Pelias smiles. It doesn't reach his eyes.

PELIAS
Then go with the gods, nephew.

Jason turns. Walks out of the square. The dog follows him.

ADVISOR
He accepted.

PELIAS
Of course he did. (beat) The fleece is guarded by a sleepless dragon. Jason will die in Colchis. And I will have killed him without getting my hands dirty.

# PIECE 2: THE GODDESS APPEARS

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

Jason sleeps in a fisherman's hut. Moonlight through the cracks. The sea is absolutely still.

A presence. Not sound. Not movement. Just — presence.

HECATE (30s, three faces — maiden, mother, crone — three torches — green, blue, red) stands at the foot of his bed.

Jason opens his eyes. Doesn't flinch.

JASON
You're the old woman from Anaurus.

HECATE
I am Hecate. Maiden, Mother, Crone. And I have come to collect.

JASON
Collect what? I carried you across. The debt is paid.

HECATE
That was the down payment.

She raises her torches. The hut fills with triple light.

HECATE (CONT'D)
The fleece you seek — it's mine. The grove — mine. The priestess — mine. When you reach Colchis, you'll need me. Remember the river. Remember the debt.

JASON
What priestess?

Hecate smiles. Three smiles. One for each face.

HECATE
Her name is Medea.

The light shifts. Jason sees:

A SHIP — fifty oars, timber from Olympus, waiting to be born.

A CREW — heroes gathering from across Greece.

A DRAGON — sleepless, vast, coiled around gold.

And a WOMAN — black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes, holding torches identical to Hecate's.

HECATE (CONT'D)
She will love you. She will destroy you. She will save you. She will betray her father for you. And you will break her heart.

JASON
Why are you telling me this?

HECATE
Because knowing doesn't change anything.

JASON
Then why tell me at all?

HECATE
Because you asked.

The light fades. Hecate fades. Jason lies alone in the dark. The moon is still full. The sea is still still.

JASON
(to himself)
Then let the pattern continue.

---

# PIECE 3: THE SHIPWRIGHT

EXT. MOUNT PELION - DAY

ARGUS (40s, shipwright, hands like oak roots, listens more than he speaks) kneels at the base of an ancient pine. Eyes closed. Hands on bark.

Silence. Then — not words. Not sound. Geometry. Pure mathematical intuition transmitted through grain.

Argus opens his eyes.

ARGUS
Fifty oars. One hull. One purpose.

He raises the axe. Hesitates. The tree seems to lean toward him.

ARGUS (CONT'D)
(whispering)
I'm not cutting you. I'm freeing you. You've been growing toward this shape for three hundred years.

The first axe falls. The timber cracks like bone.

CHIRON (centaur, Jason's teacher, watching from the valley below) nods once. Approval.

ARGUS (CONT'D)
(to the tree)
Thank you.

He begins to work. The keel takes shape. Oak from Dodona — oracle wood. It speaks in winds.

ARGUS (CONT'D)
(to himself)
The ship already exists. I'm just revealing it.

A HERON lands nearby. Watches. Doesn't fly away.

ARGUS (CONT'D)
(to the heron)
You'll see. This ship will sail where no ship has sailed. And it will come back. (beat) Ships like this always come back.

The heron tilts its head. Argus returns to work. The keel lengthens. The Argo begins.

---

# PIECE 4: THE DEMIGOD

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

Footsteps. Not human rhythm. Each step compresses the needle bed by three centimeters. The ground resonates at 40 Hz.

HERACLES (30s, 340 kg, demigod, skin glowing faintly from the Nemean lion pelt he wore for thirteen years) ascends. He carries no weapon. The club stays at base camp.

He stops ten meters from Argus. Looks at the keel. Looks at the trees. Looks at the space between what is and what will sail.

HERACLES
I heard the mountain speak.

Argus doesn't look up.

ARGUS
The ship needs an anchor who can row.

HERACLES
The ship needs no anchor. It needs a storm that knows when to calm.

ARGUS
(almost smiling)
Then we need the same thing.

HERACLES
Then I volunteer.

Heracles laughs. The laugh shakes pine needles from branches thirty meters up. A cascade of green. The heron finally flies away.

ARGUS
(looking up for the first time)
You're loud.

HERACLES
I'm strong. There's a difference.

ARGUS
Not on this mountain.

HERACLES
(sitting on a boulder that groans)
Then I'll be quiet. (beat) Quietly strong.

The boulder cracks. Heracles doesn't notice. Argus shakes his head. Returns to work.

HERACLES (CONT'D)
How many more?

ARGUS
Forty-nine.

HERACLES
They'll come. The ship called them.

ARGUS
Ships don't call. People do.

HERACLES
(smiling)
Then this one does both.

---

# PIECE 5: THE BARD

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

Sound. Not footsteps. Music. A lyre played at the edge of hearing. Each note falls like water on water.

ORPHEUS (20s, bard, carries a lyre, speaks so quietly you lean in to hear) ascends playing. His fingers move but the music seems to come from the mountain itself.

The trees lean. The stone vibrates.

Argus pauses. The first interruption since dawn.

Orpheus plays a tune that predates the lyre. A tune that predates string. A tune that the ocean knows.

He stops at the hull's edge. Plays one final chord — a major seventh that resolves to nothing. The sound hangs in the air like smoke.

ORPHEUS
The ocean taught me this. It said the ship would need a voice when the wind stops. I am that voice.

ARGUS
The wind never stops.

ORPHEUS
Then I'll sing with it.

The hull resonates at a new frequency. Orpheus didn't just arrive. He tuned the ship.

HERACLES
(from his boulder)
He's good.

ARGUS
(to Orpheus)
What's your name?

ORPHEUS
Orpheus. Son of Calliope. I play the lyre. I seek the place where music ends and silence begins.

ARGUS
You'll find it. (beat) Welcome aboard.

Orpheus sits at the bow. Continues playing. The notes now harmonize with the wind.

---

# PIECE 6: THE TWINS

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

Two voices at once. Not echo. Not harmony. Identical pitch from two throats.

CASTOR AND POLLUX (20s, twins, move as one, biological synchronization) ascend side by side. Neither ahead. Neither behind.

CASTOR
We heard the ship call twice.

POLLUX
Once for each of us.

They speak in unison after — not planned.

CASTOR/POLLUX
The voyage requires both the blade and the fist. We are both. We are one. We volunteer.

ARGUS
(looking up)
The blueprint said two as one. I thought it meant a marriage.

CASTOR
Twice the weapons.

POLLUX
Half the conversation.

They board. The ship doesn't sink deeper. It rises — as if the weight is nothing. As if the ship has been waiting for exactly this mass.

HERACLES
(to Castor)
You fence.

CASTOR
You club.

HERACLES
(to Pollux)
You box.

POLLUX
You crush.

HERACLES
(grinning)
We'll get along.

CASTOR
If you stay on your side.

POLLUX
And we stay on ours.

The twins move to the stern. Perfect balance. Argus measures the hull. Exactly level.

ARGUS
(to himself)
Two as one. I finally understand.

---

# PIECE 7: THE HUNTER

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

ATALANTA (20s, hunter, fastest mortal woman, carries a bow, needs no invitation) ascends alone.

ATALANTA
I heard the call.

ARGUS
I didn't send one.

ATALANTA
The ship did.

HERACLES
There's room for two hunters.

ATALANTA
There's room for one. The other can watch.

Heracles laughs.

HERACLES
I like her.

ATALANTA
(to Heracles)
I didn't ask what you like.

(to Argus)
I hunt my own quarry. The fleece is mine to chase. I don't follow. I don't serve. I don't owe.

ARGUS
What do you do?

ATALANTA
I run. I track. I kill. And I don't miss.

ARGUS
Then welcome aboard. (beat) We'll need someone who doesn't miss.

ATALANTA
(to the ship)
This better be worth it.

The hull hums. Atalanta almost smiles.

---

# PIECE 8: THE CREW ASSEMBLES

EXT. MOUNT PELION - DAY

More heroes arrive. ZETES and CALAIS (the Boreads, winged sons of the North Wind). IDAS and LYNCEUS (one strong, one sharp-sighted). MEDEAELA and PERICLYMENUS (one cunning, one immortal). TELEMON and OILEUS (fathers of future heroes). And more. And more.

Fifty in total. The Argonauts.

JASON arrives last. He looks at the half-built ship. At the crew gathering. At Argus working in silence.

JASON
Will it float?

ARGUS
Will you?

JASON
I don't know how to sail.

ARGUS
Then you'll learn. (beat) The ship doesn't need a captain. It needs a purpose. You have one?

JASON
The fleece.

ARGUS
That's a goal. Purpose is different.

JASON
(thinking)
To restore what was stolen. To finish what my father started. To prove that the gods don't choose — they recognize.

ARGUS
(nodding)
That'll do.

He returns to work. Jason watches the crew. Fifty heroes. One ship. One purpose.

JASON
(to himself)
The pattern continues.

---

# PIECE 9: THE LAUNCH

EXT. MOUNT PELION - DAY

The ship is complete. Timber from Olympus. Keel from Dodona. Hull from Pelion. Mast from the summit. Sails from Thrace.

ARGUS
(staring at his work)
She's ready.

JASON
What's her name?

ARGUS
You tell me.

JASON
(looking at the ship)
Argo.

ARGUS
Why?

JASON
Because argos means "swift." And swift is what we need to be.

The crew gathers. Fifty heroes. The ship waits in its cradle of ropes.

CHIRON
(from below)
Jason!

Jason looks down. Chiron raises a skin of wine.

CHIRON (CONT'D)
For the journey. (beat) Come back.

JASON
I will.

CHIRON
I know. That's why I'm worried.

The crew pushes. The ship slides. The cradle breaks. The Argo touches water for the first time.

And floats.

The crew cheers. Jason stands at the bow. The fleece is somewhere ahead. Colchis is somewhere ahead. Medea is somewhere ahead.

JASON
(to the crew)
Men. We sail for Colchis.

---

# PIECE 10: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. Fifty oars in perfect rhythm. Orpheus plays. The waves calm to match his tuning.

Jason stands at the bow. Behind him: fifty heroes. Ahead: the unknown.

MEDEAELA
(to Periclymenus)
What do you think?

PERICLYMENUS
I think we're either going to be legends or dead.

MEDEAELA
Which do you prefer?

PERICLYMENUS
Legends. Dead is boring.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
Land ahead.

JASON
Where?

ATALANTA
Lemnos. All women. No men. They killed them.

HERACLES
All of them?

ATALANTA
All.

HERACLES
(grinning)
I'm staying on the ship.

ORPHEUS
(from the bow)
The island calls.

JASON
We keep sailing. No stops until Colchis.

HERACLES
(whispering to Orpheus)
He's learning.

ORPHEUS
(whispering back)
Fast.

The Argo sails past Lemnos. Past Samothrace. Past the Thracian coast. The oars pull. The wind fills the sail. The ship flies.

---

# PIECE 11: THE STORM

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The storm comes without warning. Black waves. Wind that tears the sail. Lightning that turns the world white.

JASON
(holding the prow)
Hold!

The crew rows against the storm. Orpheus plays — not to calm the waves but to keep the rhythm. Fifty oars. One beat. Survival.

HERACLES
(rowing)
This is nothing!

Waves crash over him. He keeps rowing.

ATALANTA
(from the masthead)
Land! Ahead!

JASON
Where?

ATALANTA
Mysia! Heracles — your place!

Heracles stops rowing. Stares at the approaching shore.

HERACLES
Hyalus. (beat) I have to go.

JASON
We all have to go.

HERACLES
No. I have to go. (beat) The storm will follow me. You'll be safe.

JASON
Heracles—

HERACLES
(rowing harder)
I'll find you. At Colchis. Or after. (beat) The fleece is yours, Jason. Don't let the dragon have it.

The Argo beaches. Heracles jumps into the surf. Disappears into the storm.

The storm follows him.

Jason watches the lightning retreat. The waves calm. The night clears.

ORPHEUS
(softly)
He'll be back.

JASON
How do you know?

ORPHEUS
Because the pattern demands it.

The crew drags the ship back into the water. The oars find their rhythm. The Argo sails on.

Jason looks back at Mysia. Lightning in the distance. Heracles is out there. Fighting. Surviving. Being Heracles.

JASON
(to himself)
The crew diminishes. The quest continues.

He turns forward. Colchis awaits. The fleece awaits. Medea awaits.

The pattern continues.
