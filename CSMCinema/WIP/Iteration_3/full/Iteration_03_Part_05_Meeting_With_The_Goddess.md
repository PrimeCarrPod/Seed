# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 3 — PART 05: MEETING WITH THE GODDESS
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 3 of 7  
**Part:** 05 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE STORM RETURNS

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

Storm. Black waves. Wind that tears the sail.

The Argo battles. The crew rows. Orpheus plays to keep the rhythm.

Jason at the helm. Medea at the mast, tied to it, watching the waves.

ATALANTA
(from above, shouting)
This is no natural storm!

JASON
What do you mean?

ATALANTA
The wind comes from nowhere! The waves have no pattern!

Medea's eyes widen.

MEDEA
(shouting to Jason)
It's my father! He's sent the winds!

JASON
Can you stop them?

MEDEA
Not me! Hecate!

Medea pulls herself along the mast. Reaches the bow. Kneels.

MEDEA
(praying)
Goddess! I know I disobeyed! I know I chose my own path! But please — not the crew! They're innocent! Punish me! Not them!

The storm intensifies.

---

# PIECE 2: THE GODDESS ANSWERS

EXT. OPEN SEA - CONTINUOUS

Lightning. And in the lightning — HECATE. Three faces. Three torches. Standing on the water.

HECATE
Innocent? None of you are innocent. The thief. The traitor. The crew that followed. All guilty. All mine.

MEDEA
Then take me! Let them go!

HECATE
It's not me you need to convince.

The storm parts. A figure walks across the waves. POSEIDON (40s, bearded, trident, god of the sea).

POSEIDEN
Hecate. You're in my domain.

HECATE
I'm everywhere, Sea King. You know that.

POSEIDON
The girl prays. I listen.

HECATE
And?

POSEIDEN
(looking at Medea)
She's sincere. (beat) But sincerity doesn't calm storms.

HECATE
What does?

POSEIDEN
Payment.

---

# PIECE 3: THE PRICE

EXT. OPEN SEA - CONTINUOUS

Poseidon stands before Medea. The crew watches from the ship.

POSEIDON
You want my sea calm? Give me something in return.

MEDEA
What do you want?

POSEIDON
Your voice.

Silence.

MEDEA
My voice?

POSEIDON
For one year. You'll speak no word. You'll sing no song. You'll make no sound. And my sea will carry you safely home.

MEDEA
(looking at Jason)
One year of silence?

POSEIDON
One year. Starting now.

Medea looks at the crew. At Jason. At the fleece.

MEDEA
(whispering)
If it saves them—

JASON
(shouting from the ship)
Don't! We'll find another way!

MEDEA
(shouting back)
There is no other way!

(to Poseidon)
I accept.

Poseidon touches her throat. Medea opens her mouth — nothing comes out.

The storm stops. Instantly. Flat calm.

POSEIDON
One year. No sooner. No later. (to Jason) Take care of her, Greek. The voiceless are sacred. Harm her — and my sea will swallow you.

Poseidon sinks into the waves. Hecate fades.

The Argo floats on glass.

---

# PIECE 4: THE SILENT ONES

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - NIGHT

Medea sits alone. Silent. Jason approaches.

JASON
I'm sorry.

Medea shakes her head. Writes in the dust with her finger:

*NOT YOUR FAULT*

JASON
I should have found another way.

She writes:

*NO OTHER WAY. THE PATTERN.*

JASON
The pattern demanded your voice?

She writes:

*THE PATTERN DEMANDS EVERYTHING. EVENTUALLY.*

JASON
(sitting beside her)
Then I'll speak for both of us.

She looks at him. Writes:

*YOU CAN'T SPEAK FOR ME. NO ONE CAN.*

JASON
I can try.

She almost smiles. Writes:

*THEN TRY.*

Orpheus enters. Sits across from them.

ORPHEUS
(to Medea)
I understand. (beat) Music isn't just sound. It's the space between sounds. The silence. (playing a single note, then stopping) The silence after the note is part of the music.

Medea watches his fingers. Writes:

*TEACH ME.*

ORPHEUS
Teach you what?

She writes:

*TO HEAR THE SILENCE.*

Orpheus smiles. Plays. A melody made of spaces. Of breaths. Of nothing.

Medea closes her eyes. Listens.

---

# PIECE 5: THE ISLAND OF THE SIRENS

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails through still water. Too still.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Island ahead. Strange. No birds. No movement.

ORPHEUS
(stopping his playing)
The Sirens.

ZETES
Sirens?

ORPHEUS
They sing. Their song is... irresistible. Sailors steer toward them. Crash on the rocks. Die.

CALAIS
Then we plug our ears.

ORPHEUS
It won't work. The song isn't heard with ears. It's heard with—

He looks at Medea.

ORPHEUS (CONT'D)
With the heart.

JASON
Can you counter it?

ORPHEUS
(considering)
Maybe. If I play louder than they sing. (beat) But I'll need silence from the crew. Complete silence. Any voice — any cry — and the Sirens will find the gap.

JASON
(to the crew)
No one speaks. No one cries out. No matter what you hear.

The crew nods.

JASON (CONT'D)
(to Medea)
You already have the advantage.

She writes:

*I'LL WATCH.*

---

# PIECE 6: THE SIREN SONG

EXT. OPEN SEA - APPROACHING THE ISLAND - DAY

The island appears. Rocky. Beautiful. And on the rocks — the SIRENS (three women, beautiful, terrible, mouths open in song).

The song begins. Not heard with ears. Felt. In the chest. In the blood.

The crew freezes. Eyes glazing. Hands reaching toward the island.

Orpheus plays. Counter-melody. His music weaving through the Siren song. Disrupting it. Replacing it.

But the Sirens are old. Powerful. Their song adapts.

One crewman stands. Walks toward the rail. JASON grabs him.

JASON
(whispering)
No.

The crewman fights. Jason holds him.

Another crewman. Then another. The Sirens' song intensifies.

Medea watches. Silent. Her hands move — weaving in the air. Threads only she can see.

She weaves a counter-pattern. Not music. Something else. The visual equivalent of Orpheus's melody.

The crew sees her hands. Sees the pattern. The Siren song breaks.

Orpheus plays the final note. The Sirens scream. Fall silent.

The island passes. The Argo sails on.

The crew collapses. Exhausted.

ORPHEUS
(breathing hard)
That was close.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The Sirens are screaming. At us. At the sea.

ORPHEUS
Good. Let them.

---

# PIECE 7: THE GIFT

INT. ARGO - BELOW DECK - NIGHT

Medea sits with Orpheus. He plays. She weaves — real threads now, a small loom.

ORPHEUS
Your weaving. It's like music. Visual music.

Medea writes:

*MUSIC IS PATTERN. WEAVING IS PATTERN. SAME THING.*

ORPHEUS
Hecate teaches you?

She writes:

*HECATE TEACHES EVERYTHING. SHE IS THE PATTERN.*

ORPHEUS
And Jason?

She writes. Stops. Writes again. Stops.

ORPHEUS (CONT'D)
(gently)
You don't have to write it.

She writes:

*I DON'T KNOW YET. IS THAT TERRIBLE? I LEFT EVERYTHING FOR HIM AND I DON'T KNOW WHY.*

ORPHEUS
That's not terrible. That's honest.

She writes:

*IS HONESTY ENOUGH?*

ORPHEUS
It's the only thing that is.

---

# PIECE 8: THE COAST OF CRETE

EXT. CRETE - COAST - DAY

The Argo approaches a great island. A palace in the distance. A metal giant walks the shore.

ATALANTA
Talos. The bronze giant. He guards Crete. Throws rocks at any ship that approaches.

ZETES
We go around.

ATALANTA
There is no around. He walks the entire coast.

JASON
Then we talk to him.

ATALANTA
He's bronze. He doesn't talk. He throws rocks.

Medea tugs Jason's sleeve. Writes:

*TALOS HAS A VEIN. IN HIS ANKLE. SEALED WITH A BRONZE NAIL. IF YOU REMOVE IT—*

JASON
He dies?

She writes:

*HE'S ALREADY DEAD. HE'S A CONSTRUCT. MAGIC. THE NAIL HOLDS THE MAGIC.*

JASON
How do you know this?

She writes:

*HECATE TAUGHT ME.*

JASON
(to the crew)
We need to get close to him. Close enough to reach his ankle.

ATALANTA
He'll kill us.

JASON
Not if we distract him.

---

# PIECE 9: THE BRONZE GIANT

EXT. CRETE - SHORE - DAY

The Argo beaches. Talos approaches. Thirty feet tall. Bronze. Eyes like burning coals.

JASON
(standing at the bow)
Talos! Guardian of Crete! We seek passage!

Talos doesn't speak. Picks up a boulder the size of a man. Throws.

The crew dives. The boulder splinters the rail.

JASON
(again)
We don't want to fight!

Another boulder. Closer.

ATALANTA
(from the ship)
This isn't working!

Medea pulls Jason down. Points. There — at Talos' ankle. A bronze nail. Glowing.

JASON
(understanding)
I need to get to his ankle.

ATALANTA
He'll crush you.

JASON
Then I'll be fast.

Jason runs. Across the beach. Toward the giant.

Talos sees him. Stomps. The ground shakes.

Jason dodges. Rolls. Reaches the ankle.

The nail is hot. Glowing. Jason grabs it. Pulls.

Nothing.

Talos raises his foot. To crush.

Jason pulls harder. The nail shifts. A quarter turn. A half—

The nail comes free.

Light pours from the ankle. Talos freezes. The bronze cracks. The giant falls.

Silence.

Jason stands in the sand. The bronze nail in his hand.

ATALANTA
(from the ship)
You did it.

JASON
(to Medea)
Thank you.

She writes:

*THE PATTERN PROVIDES.*

---

# PIECE 10: THE WARNING

INT. ARGO - NIGHT

The crew celebrates. Another danger passed.

But Medea sits apart. Staring at the bronze nail.

Jason approaches.

JASON
What's wrong?

She writes:

*THAT NAIL WAS POWERFUL. OLD. NOT MADE BY MORTALS. NOT MADE BY GODS.*

JASON
Then who?

She writes:

*THE TITANS. BEFORE THE GODS. BEFORE EVERYTHING. (beat) THE PATTERN GOES DEEPER THAN I THOUGHT.*

JASON
How deep?

She writes:

*DEEPER THAN HECATE. DEEPER THAN CHRONOS. (beat) JASON — WE'RE NOT JUST RETRIEVING A FLEECE. WE'RE UNCOVERING SOMETHING.*

JASON
Uncovering what?

She writes:

*THE FIRST PATTERN. THE ONE THAT CAME BEFORE THE GODS. THE ONE THAT MADE THE GODS POSSIBLE.*

JASON
(quietly)
And we're part of it.

She writes:

*WE ARE IT.*

---

# PIECE 11: THE HORIZON

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The Argo sails. Crete behind them. The bronze nail secured below deck.

Jason and Medea at the bow.

JASON
How much further?

ATALANTA
(from above)
Iolcus. Two days. If the wind holds.

JASON
Two days.

MEDEA writes:

*THEN WHAT?*

JASON
Then we face Pelias. Then we take the throne. Then—

She writes:

*THEN?*

JASON
(smiling)
I don't know. The pattern will tell us.

She writes:

*STOP HIDING BEHIND THE PATTERN.*

JASON
I'm not hiding.

She writes:

*YOU ARE. YOU SAY 'THE PATTERN' WHEN YOU MEAN 'I DON'T KNOW.' YOU SAY 'THE GODS' WHEN YOU MEAN 'I'M AFRAID.' YOU SAY 'FATE' WHEN YOU MEAN 'I'M CHOOSING.'

JASON
(stung)
Is that wrong?

She writes:

*NO. IT'S HUMAN. (beat) BUT HONESTY IS BETTER.*

JASON
(long pause)
I'm afraid. (beat) I'm afraid of Pelias. I'm afraid of the throne. I'm afraid that when this is over — you'll realize you gave up everything for someone who wasn't worth it.

She writes:

*I KNOW. (beat) I'M AFRAID TOO.*

JASON
Of what?

She writes:

*THAT YOU'RE RIGHT.*

They stand in silence. The sea ahead. The fleece between them. The pattern unfolding.

JASON
Whatever happens—

She writes:

*WHATEVER HAPPENS.*
