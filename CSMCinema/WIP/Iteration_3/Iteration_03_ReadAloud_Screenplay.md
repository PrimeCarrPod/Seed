# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## An Iteration 3 Read-Aloud Screenplay
### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE — THE CALL

EXT. IOLCUS MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

Empty market. Fish scales glisten. PELIAS (50s, paranoid king) on his balcony. JASON (20, golden hair, one sandal) enters below.

PELIAS
You came.

JASON
I've come for my father's throne.

PELIAS
Your father died in exile. But the throne requires proof. The Golden Fleece. Retrieve it from Colchis.

JASON
(without hesitation)
I accept.

PELIAS
Then go with the gods, nephew.

Jason walks away. Pelias watches.

PELIAS (CONT'D)
(to advisor)
The fleece is guarded by a sleepless dragon. Jason will die in Colchis.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. HECATE (three faces, three torches) appears.

HECATE
Boy who carried me. I've come to collect.

JASON
I carried you across. The debt is paid.

HECATE
That was the down payment. The fleece you seek is mine. The grove is mine. The priestess is mine. When you reach Colchis, you'll need me.

JASON
What priestess?

HECATE
Her name is Medea. She will love you. She will destroy you. She will save you. She will betray her father for you. And you will break her heart.

JASON
Why tell me this?

HECATE
Because knowing doesn't change anything.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - DAY

ARGUS (40s, shipwright) kneels at an ancient pine. HERACLES (30s, demigod) ascends.

HERACLES
The trees told me the ship needs a storm that knows when to calm. I volunteer.

ARGUS
We need no anchor.

HERACLES
Then I am that storm.

ORPHEUS (20s, bard) ascends playing his lyre.

ORPHEUS
The ocean taught me a song. It said the ship would need a voice when the wind stops.

ARGUS
The wind never stops.

ORPHEUS
Then I'll sing with it.

CASTOR AND POLLUX (20s, twins) ascend in unison.

CASTOR/POLLUX
We heard the ship call twice. Once for each of us. We are both. We are one. We volunteer.

ATALANTA (20s, hunter) ascends alone.

ATALANTA
I hunt my own quarry. The fleece is mine to chase.

HERACLES
There's room for two hunters.

ATALANTA
There's room for one. The other can watch.

---

EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo arrives. AETES (60s, king of Colchis) receives them.

AETES
Greeks. In my hall. Why?

JASON
For the Golden Fleece.

AETES
Yoke my fire-breathing bulls. Plow the field. Sow the dragon's teeth. Kill the warriors. Do this — and we'll discuss the fleece.

JASON
When?

AETES
Tomorrow. Dawn.

---

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

MEDEA (19, priestess, black hair, gold eyes) prays. Jason enters.

MEDEA
You shouldn't be here.

JASON
You gave me the vial.

MEDEA
I gave you medicine. For the bulls tomorrow.

JASON
You gave me more than that. (beat) What's your name?

MEDEA
Medea. (beat) If I help you — I betray my father. My kingdom. Everything.

JASON
Then don't help me.

MEDEA
(startled)
You'd fail. You'd die.

JASON
Probably. Likely. (beat) But it's your choice. Not the goddess's. Not the pattern's. Yours.

MEDEA
(quietly)
I choose to help you. I choose to betray my father. I choose... you.

---

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - DAY

Two FIRE-BREATHING BULLS charge. Jason walks forward. Empty-handed. The bulls breathe fire. Jason doesn't burn. He touches their muzzles. They kneel.

He yokes them. Plows. Sows the teeth. Warriors spring from earth. He throws a stone — they fight each other. One remains. Jason kills it.

Silence. Aetes stands.

AETES
(quietly)
My daughter helped him.

---

EXT. GROVE OF ARES - NIGHT

Medea and Jason before the sleeping dragon. The fleece glows.

MEDEA
The draught will hold for one hour. Take the fleece. Find me. Run.

JASON
We.

MEDEA
(smiling)
We.

They take the fleece. The dragon wakes. They run.

---

## ACT TWO — THE RETURN

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

ABSYRTUS (20s, Medea's brother) pursues with three ships.

MEDEA
He'll never stop.

JASON
Then we make him choose.

Jason boards Absyrtus' ship. Presses blade to his throat.

JASON (CONT'D)
Call off your ships.

ABSYRTUS
You'll never leave this deck alive.

JASON
Neither will you.

MEDEA
(calling)
Brother! I'm sorry!

She throws the fleece. Absyrtus turns. Jason shoves him. Grabs the fleece. Jumps.

---

EXT. PHAEACIAN ISLAND - NIGHT

QUEEN ARETE (60s, wise) speaks with Medea.

ARETE
You chose between your blood and your heart.

MEDEA
I chose myself.

ARETE
That's harder. (beat) I left my family for love. Forty years ago. Some nights I still wonder if I chose right.

MEDEA
How do you live with the wondering?

ARETE
You wake up each morning. And you choose again. Every day. Until one day — the wondering stops.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

A storm. POSEIDON appears on the waves.

POSEIDON
You want my sea calm? Give me something in return.

MEDEA
What do you want?

POSEIDON
Your voice. For one year.

JASON
Don't!

MEDEA
(to Poseidon)
I accept.

Poseidon touches her throat. She opens her mouth — nothing. The storm stops.

POSEIDON
One year. Harm her — and my sea will swallow you.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

Orpheus plays. Medea weaves in the air — visible patterns.

ORPHEUS
Your weaving is like music. Visual music.

MEDEA
(writing in dust)
Music is pattern. Weaving is pattern. Same thing.

ORPHEUS
And Jason?

MEDEA
(stops writing, then)
I don't know yet. Is that terrible? I left everything for him and I don't know why.

ORPHEUS
That's not terrible. That's honest.

---

EXT. CRETE - DAY

ATALANTA
Talos. The bronze giant. He throws rocks at any ship.

MEDEA
(writing)
He has a vein in his ankle. Sealed with a bronze nail. Remove it — he dies.

JASON
How do you know this?

MEDEA
Hecate taught me.

Jason runs. Dodges boulders. Pulls the nail. Talos falls.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

Medea stares at the bronze nail. Jason approaches.

MEDEA
(writing)
That nail was old. Not made by mortals. Not made by gods.

JASON
Then who?

MEDEA
The Titans. Before the gods. Before everything. (beat) Jason — we're not just retrieving a fleece. We're uncovering something.

JASON
Uncovering what?

MEDEA
The first pattern. The one that made the gods possible.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

Jason returns. Pelias waits.

PELIAS
You're alive.

JASON
Disappointed?

PELIAS
Surprised. (beat) The fleece. You actually retrieved it.

JASON
Before we discuss the throne. I want to talk about my father.

PELIAS
(carefully)
Your father died in exile.

JASON
My father was murdered. By you.

PELIAS
(standing)
That's a serious accusation.

JASON
It's a true one.

---

INT. THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pelias dismisses the guards. Just the two of them.

PELIAS
What do you want?

JASON
Justice.

PELIAS
Justice? Your father was weak. The kingdom needed strength. I took it. That's all.

JASON
You stole his throne. Sent him to die. Stole my childhood.

PELIAS
And gave you something better. A quest. A purpose. A story. Without me — you'd be nothing.

JASON
(quietly)
I choose... neither.

PELIAS
Neither?

JASON
The throne is yours. Keep it. Killing you won't bring him back.

PELIAS
(long pause)
You're better than me. Better than your father. Better than any of us.

JASON
I'm just tired.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

Celebration. Jason and Medea walk alone.

JASON
I thought I'd feel different. After the fleece. After Pelias.

MEDEA
(writing)
Different how?

JASON
Complete. Finished. But I don't. I feel like this is just the beginning.

MEDEA
Because it is. The fleece was iteration two. There are five more. Seven iterations. Seventy-seven parts total.

JASON
(smiling)
You sound like Hecate.

MEDEA
I'll take that as a compliment.

---

## ACT THREE — THE LOOM

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

A map found inside the fleece. Lands beyond Greece. Beyond Colchis.

MEDEA
(writing)
The Loom of the World. Where time was woven. Where the gods were made.

ATALANTA
The gods were made?

MEDEA
The gods weren't always here. They were woven. By something older.

---

EXT. THE LOOM - NIGHT

The Argo floats through darkness. Threads everywhere. A loom the size of a mountain.

MEDEA
(writing)
I have to touch it. I have to weave.

JASON
Are you sure?

MEDEA
No. But I'm doing it anyway.

She places her hands on the loom. Light. She weaves — reality itself. Galaxies form. Stars ignite. Medea changes. Becomes translucent.

JASON
Medea!

MEDEA
(writing)
I'm here. I'm everywhere. I'm the pattern now.

---

EXT. THE LOOM - LATER

Medea speaks. Her voice returned.

MEDEA
The loom gave it back. The loom gives everything.

JASON
Are you—

MEDEA
I'm still me. But I'm also more. I'm the loom. And the loom is me.

JASON
Come back.

MEDEA
(smiling)
I never left. (beat) Take this.

She hands him a golden thread.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
Protection. No god can harm you while you hold it.

JASON
What about you?

MEDEA
I am the protection now.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

ATHENA (appearing as geometry) offers Jason kingship of Greece.

ATHENA
The gods offer you a choice.

JASON
No.

ATHENA
You haven't heard the full offer.

JASON
I don't need to. The answer is no.

ATHENA
You would refuse the gods?

JASON
I would refuse ownership. Again.

ATHENA
(almost smiling)
You're either very brave or very stupid.

JASON
My crew says both.

---

EXT. THE LOOM - NIGHT

The loom destabilizes. Medea fades.

MEDEA
(weak)
Jason. The pattern is breaking.

JASON
What do you need?

MEDEA
Help. I can't hold it alone.

The crew gathers. Each places a hand on the loom. The threads brighten. The pattern stabilizes.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
(whole again)
Thank you.

JASON
Always.

---

EXT. HYPERBOREA - DAY

Snow. Ice. The crew descends underground. Three HYPERBOREANS tend an ancient loom.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
You came.

JASON
You were expecting us?

HYPERBOREAN ONE
The pattern told us.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
Touch it.

Jason touches the loom. Sees everything. Every thread. Every choice. Every life.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
And what will you become?

JASON
I don't know. But I'll choose.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Now you understand.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The Argo sails west. Jason and Medea at the bow.

MEDEA
Iteration three. How does it feel?

JASON
Like the beginning.

MEDEA
It is. Every iteration is the beginning. There is no end. Just continuation.

JASON
That sounds exhausting.

MEDEA
It is. But it's also beautiful. Because every day — you choose again.

JASON
To love?

MEDEA
Especially that.

The sun rises. The Argo sails west. The pattern continues.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END OF ITERATION 3
