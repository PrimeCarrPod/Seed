# GLOBAL PARALLELS — 15 CULTURES MIRRORING GATEKEEPER TESTS:

1. **CIRCE (Greek)** — The original gatekeeper who transformed men into beasts. Medea passes where Odysseus merely survived. **[DEEPENING: *IN *THE *ODYSSEY, *CIRCE *TRANSFORMED *ODYSSEUS' *MEN *INTO *SWINE. *ODYSSEUS *HIMSELF *WAS *PROTECTED *BY *THE *HERB *MOLY *(GIVEN *BY *HERMES). *HE *DID *NOT *PASS *CIRCE'S *TEST — *HE *RESISTED *IT. *MEDEA *PASSES *CIRCE'S *TEST *BY *NOT *RESISTING. *ODYSSEUS *USED *MAGIC *(MOLY). *MEDEA *USES *HONESTY *(EMPTINESS). *ODYSSEUS *LEFT *CIRCE'S *ISLAND *AFTER *A *YEAR. *MEDEA *PASSES *THROUGH *CIRCE *IN *MOMENTS. *THE *DIFFERENCE: *ODYSSEUS *WAS *TESTING *CIRCE *(CAN *I *ESCAPE?). *MEDEA *IS *BEING *TESTED *BY *CIRCE *(AM *I *READY?). *ONE *IS *ADVERSARIAL. *THE *OTHER *IS *INITIATORY. *BOTH *ENCOUNTER *THE *SAME *GATEKEEPER. *BUT *ONLY *ONE *PASSES.]**

2. **YAMA'S GUARDIAN (Hindu)** — The god of death who tests all who enter the underworld. Two dogs guard the gate. **[DEEPENING: *YAMA, *HINDU *GOD *OF *DEATH, *IS *GUARDED *BY *TWO *DOGS *(SHYAMA *AND *SHABALA) — *THE *SAME *DOGS *THAT *GUARD *HECATE. *THE *PARALLEL *IS *EXACT: *BOTH *TRADITIONS *USE *CANINE *GUARDIANS *AT *THE *THRESHOLD *BETWEEN *LIFE *AND *DEATH, *BETWEEN *MORTAL *AND *DIVINE. *YAMA *TESTS *THE *DEAD *BY *ASKING: *"WHAT *DID *YOU *DO *WITH *YOUR *LIFE?" *CIRCE *TESTS *THE *LIVING *BY *ASKING: *"WHAT *DO *YOU *HAVE *NOW?" *BOTH *TESTS *ARE *ABOUT *ACCOUNTABILITY. *BOTH *TESTS *ARE *ABOUT *HONESTY. *BOTH *TESTS *CANNOT *BE *FAILED *BY *THOSE *WHO *TRULY *KNOW *THEMSELVES.]**

3. **THE SPHINX (Greek)** — "What walks on four legs in the morning, two at noon, three in the evening?"
