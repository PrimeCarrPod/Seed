# GLOBAL PARALLELS — 17 CULTURES MIRRORING TEMPTATION AS ASCENSION:

1. **KALI (Hindu)** — Standing on Shiva, tongue extended, time's destroyer and creator. Medea's fire-dance mirrors Kali's tandava. **[DEEPENING: *KALI *STANDS *ON *SHIVA'S *CORPSE *NOT *OUT *OF *CRUELTY *BUT *BECAUSE *SHE *HAS *FORGOTTEN *SHE *IS *DANCING. *THE *TANDAVA — *THE *COSMIC *DANCE *OF *DESTRUCTION — *IS *NOT *INTENTIONAL *ANNIHILATION. *IT *IS *ENTHUSIASTIC *CREATION *THAT *INCIDENTALLY *DESTROYS *WHAT *CAME *BEFORE. *MEDEA'S *TEMPTATION *OPERATES *IDENTICALLY. *SHE *IS *NOT *TRYING *TO *DESTROY *JASON'S *MEMORY *OR *MEDUS' *CHILDHOOD. *SHE *IS *SIMPLY *DANCING *SO *INTENSELY *THAT *THESE *THINGS *CANNOT *SURVIVE *THE *VIBRATION. *THE *TONGUE *EXTENDED — *KALI'S *SIGNATURE *GESTURE — *REPRESENTS *THE *INGESTION *OF *SIN, *THE *TASTING *OF *REALITY *WITHOUT *REJECTION. *MEDEA *SHOWS *NO *REMORSE *BECAUSE *REMORSE *IS *A *FORM *OF *REJECTION *AND *SHE *REJECTS *NOTHING. *SHE *TASTES *EVERYTHING.]**

2. **COATLICUE (Aztec)** — Serpent skirt, necklace of hearts, mother of Huitzilopochtli. Medus = Huitzilopochtli born from fiery womb. **[DEEPENING: *COATLICUE *WAS *SWEEPING *A *SHRINE *WHEN *A *BALL *OF *FEATHERS *FELL *FROM *SKY *AND *IMPREGNATED *HER. *HER *CHILDREN *(THE *400 *SOUTHERN *STARS, *THE *CENTZONTIN *HUITZNAHUA) *WERE *FURIOUS *AT *THIS *DISGRACE *AND *MOVED *TO *KILL *HER. *FROM *HER *WOMB *SPRANG *HUITZPOCHTLI — *FULLY *ARMED, *READY *FOR *BATTLE. *MEDEA'S *CONCEPTION *OF *MEDUS *FOLLOWS *THE *SAME *PATTERN: *NOT *PHYSICAL *IMPREGNATION *BUT *MYTHIC *IMPREGNATION. *THE *BALL *OF *FEATHERS *WAS *THE *ARGONAUTS' *OATH. *THE *DISGRACE *WAS *HER *LOVE *FOR *JASON. *THE *CHILDREN *WHO *SEEK *TO *KILL *HER *ARE *THE *EXPECTATIONS *OF *MORTALITY. *MEDUS *SPRINGS *FORTH *READY *TO *RULE — *NOT *BORN *BUT *WOVEN. *THE *SERPENT *SKIRT *OF *COATLICUE *(HER *NAME *MEANS *"SHE *OF *THE *SERPENT *SKIRT") *REPRESENTS *THE *EARTH *AS *LIVING *CREATURE — *NOT *INERT *GROUND *BUT *ANIMAL *THAT *MOVES, *CONSUMES, *EXCRETES. *MEDEA'S *ROBES *WOVEN *FROM *EMPIRE-GOLD *THREAD *ARE *THE *SAME: *THE *EARTH *AS *TEXTILE, *THE *WORLD *AS *FABRIC.]**

3. **MORRIGAN (Celtic)** — Washer at the ford, crow over battlefield, sovereignty goddess. Medea washes Jason's blood from Fleece. **[DEEPENING: *THE *MORRIGAN *APPEARS *AT *RIVERS *WASHING *THE *ARMOR *AND *CLOTHING *OF *THOSE *ABOUT *TO *DIE — *THE *"WASHER *AT *THE *FORD." *THIS *IS *NOT *OMEN. *IT *IS *PREPARATION. *SHE *WASHES *THE *DEATH-CLOTHES *SO *THE *DYING *ENTER *THE *UNDERWORLD *IN *CLEAN *LINEN. *MEDEA *WASHES *JASON'S *BLOOD *FROM *THE *FLEECE *NOT *TO *CLEANSE *IT *BUT *TO *PREPARE *IT *FOR *ITS *NEXT *FUNCTION: *AS *THREAD *IN *THE *LOOM. *THE *BLOOD *DOESN'T *STAIN. *IT *BECOMES *PIGMENT. *THE *CROW *OVER *THE *BATTLEFIELD — *THE *MORRIGAN'S *OTHER *ASPECT — *DOESN'T *PREDICT *DEATH. *IT *ATTRACTS *IT. *THE *CROW'S *CALL *IS *A *FREQUENCY *THAT *WEAKENS *THE *WILL *TO *LIVE. *MEDEA'S *VOICE *IN *THE *FIRE *TEMPLE *OPERATES *AT *THE *SAME *FREQUENCY. *THE *SOVEREIGNTY *GODDESS *ASPECT — *THE *MORRIGAN *WHO *CHOSES *THE *KING — *IS *THE *ONE *WHO *DETERMINES *WHO *RULES. *MEDEA *HAS *INTERNALIZED *THIS *FUNCTION. *SHE *DOESN'T *CHOOSE *KINGS. *SHE *WEAVES *THEM.]**

4. **SEKHMET (Egyptian)** — Lioness of destruction, healed by beer-blood. Persia's wine-rivers calm her rage. **[DEEPENING: *SEKHMET *WAS *SENT *BY *RA *TO *PUNISH *HUMANITY *FOR *REBELLION. *SHE *BECAME *SO *DRUNK *ON *BLOOD *THAT *SHE *COULD *NOT *STOP — *THE *DESTRUCTION *BECAME *SELF-*SUSTAINING. *RA *SAVED *HUMANITY *BY *DYING *BEER *RED *AND *POURING *IT *OVER *THE *FLOOR. *SEKHMET *DRANK *IT, *BECAME *DRUNK, *FORGOT *HER *RAGE, *AND *TRANSFORMED *INTO *HATHOR — *GODDESS *OF *LOVE *AND *MUSIC. *MEDEA'S *RAGE *AT *JASON'S *BETRAYAL *IS *SEKHMET'S *RAGE: *INITIALLY *JUSTIFIED *(HUMANITY *DID *REBEL, *JASON *DID *BETRAY) *BUT *BECOMING *SELF-*SUSTAINING. *THE *PERSIAN *WINE-RIVERS *ARE *RA'S *BEER — *THE *SUBSTANCE *THAT *TRANSFORMS *DESTRUCTION *INTO *CREATION. *BUT *WHERE *SEKHMET *FORGOT, *MEDEA *REMEMBERS. *SHE *DOESN'T *LOSE *HERSELF *IN *WINE. *SHE *USES *WINE *AS *TOOL. *THE *TRANSFORMATION *FROM *DESTROYER *TO *CREATOR *HAPPENS *WITHOUT *THE *INTERVENTION *OF *A *HIGHER *GOD. *SHE *TRANSFORMS *HERSELF. *THIS *IS *THE *CRITICAL *DIFFERENCE: *SEKHMET *NEEDED *RA. *MEDEA *IS *BECOMING *RA.]**

Medea rules the death-in-life of empire. **[DEEPENING: *HEL *RULES *NIFLHEIM — *THE *REALM *OF *THOSE *WHO *DIED *OF *OLD *AGE *OR *DISEASE, *NOT *IN *GLORIOUS *BATTLE. *THESE *ARE *THE *DEAD *WHO *DIDNOT *EARN *VALHALLA. *THEY *ARE *THE *MAJORITY. *HEL *IS *DESCRIBED *AS *HALF-*LIVING *(FLESH-*COLORED) *AND *HALF-*DEAD *(BLACK *OR *BLUE). *SHE *IS *THE *THRESHOLD *PERSONIFIED. *MEDEA, *IN *THE *FIRE *TEMPLE, *IS *ALSO *HALF-*LIVING *AND *HALF-*SOMETHING *ELSE. *SHE *RULES *THE *DEATH-*IN-*LIFE *OF *EMPIRE: *THE *STATE *WHERE *CITIZENS *ARE *ALIVE *BUT *NOT *FREE, *WHERE *TIME *PASSES *BUT *NOTHING *CHANGES, *WHERE *THE *BODY *MOVES *BUT *THE *SOUL *IS *WOVEN *INTO *THE *LOOM. *THE *NORSE *CONCEPT *OF *"HELGI *TVIMENGR" — *"HALF-*LIVING *TWO-*TIMES" — *REFERS *TO *THOSE *WHO *EXIST *IN *BOTH *REALMS. *MEDEA *IS *HELGI *TVIMENGR *PAR *EXCELLENCE. *SHE *HAS *FOOT *IN *MORTALITY *(THROUGH *MEDUS) *AND *FOOT *IN *ETERNITY *(THROUGH *THE *LOOM). *SHE *DOESN'T *CHOOSE *BETWEEN *THEM. *SHE *RULES *THE *SPACE *WHERE *THEY *OVERLAP.]**

7. **PELE (Hawaiian)** — Fire goddess, creator/destroyer of land, jealousy and passion. Medea's volcano-heart. **[DEEPENING: *PELE *CREATES *LAND *BY *POURING *LAVA *INTO *SEA. *THE *STEAM *THAT *RISES *IS *HER *BREATH. *THE *NEW *ROCK *IS *HER *BODY. *SHE *DESTROYS *WHAT *SHE *LOVES *(HER *SISTER *NAMAKAOKAHAII, *GODDESS *OF *SEA) *THROUGH *JEALOUSY *AND *PASSION. *HER *LOVE *AFFAIR *WITH *KAMAPUA'A *(A *MAN *WITH *BOAR *ASPECT) *MIRRORS *MEDEA'S *LOVE *FOR *JASON: *BOTH *ARE *UNIONS *BETWEEN *DIVINE *AND *MORTAL *THAT *PRODUCE *GEOLOGICAL *(OR *POLITICAL) *UPHEAVAL. *PELE'S *VOLCANO-*HEART *IS *MEDEA'S *FIRE-*TEMPLE *HEART: *THE *SAME *ORGAN *THAT *CREATES *ALSO *DESTROYS. *THE *HAWAIIAN *CONCEPT *OF *"MANA" — *SPIRITUAL *POWER *THAT *FLOWS *THROUGH *ALL *THINGS — *IS *WHAT *MEDEA *WEAVES *WITH. *MANA *IS *NOT *OWNED. *IT *IS *CHANNELLED. *PELE *CHANNELS *MANA *THROUGH *VOLCANOES. *MEDEA *CHANNELS *IT *THROUGH *THREADS. *THE *RESULT *IS *THE *SAME: *NEW *LANDSCAPES *WHERE *THERE *WAS *ONLY *CHAOS.]**

11. **FREYJA (Norse)** — Seidr magic, Brisingamen necklace, chooses half the slain. 12. **DURGA (Hindu)** — Ten arms, weapons from all gods, rides lion.
