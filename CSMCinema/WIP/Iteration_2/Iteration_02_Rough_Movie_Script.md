# FADE IN:

## PART 1: THE CALL TO ADVENTURE

**EXT. IOLCUS — MARKET SQUARE — DAWN**

The ordinary world breathes. Fish scales glisten. Smoke from brick ovens curves like thread. PELIAS, usurper king, watches from his balcony. He waits. The oracle's prophecy echoes: "One sandal. One heir. One throne."

JASON (20, golden hair, river mud on his ankle) enters the market. One sandal. The prophecy fulfilled. Pelias sees him. The ordinary world ends.

**PELIAS** *(to his advisor)*  
The gods send me a nephew with one foot bare and the other on my throne. I will not give him the throne. I will give him the impossible. The Golden Fleece. Let the gods kill him if they wish him king.

**INT. PELIAS' COURT — DAY**

Jason stands before Pelias. Fifty warriors guard the throne. The court watches. Jason knows the trap. He also knows the truth.

**JASON**  
You stole my father's throne. You sent me to Chiron to be forgotten. But the gods don't forget. I accept your quest. I will retrieve the Golden Fleece. And when I return, the throne will be mine.

**PELIAS** *(smiling)*  
Then go, nephew. The gods go with you.

**EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR — NIGHT**

Jason sleeps in a fisherman's hut. The moon is full. The sea is still. HECATE (triple goddess — maiden, mother, crone) appears at the foot of his bed. Three torches. Green, blue, red.

**HECATE**  
Boy who carried me. Boy who lost his sandal. I have come to collect.

**JASON** *(opening his eyes, unafraid)*  
Collect what?

**HECATE**  
Not payment. Investment. The fleece is mine. The grove is mine. The priestess is mine. When you reach Colchis, you will need me. Remember the river. Remember the debt.

She raises the torches. Jason sees: the ship, the crew, the dragon, the fleece. MEDEA (19, black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes). Always Medea.

The vision fades. Hecate fades. Jason lies still. The call is blessed.

---

## PART 2: THE ASSEMBLY OF HEROES

**EXT. MOUNT PELION — DAY**

ARGUS the shipwright kneels at the base of an ancient pine. Hands on bark. Eyes closed. Athena whispers through the grain — not words, but geometry. The shape of what will sail.

**ARGUS** *(to himself)*  
The ship already exists. I merely reveal it.

The first axe falls. The Argo begins.

**HERACLES** (340 kg, demigod, Nemean lion glow) ascends. He carries no weapon. He is the weapon.

**HERACLES**  
I heard the mountain speak. The trees told me the ship needs an anchor who can row. I volunteer.

**ARGUS** *(without looking up)*  
The ship needs a storm that knows when to calm.

**HERACLES** *(laughing, shaking pine needles from 30 meters)*  
Then I am that storm.

**ORPHEUS** ascends playing his lyre. The trees lean. The stone vibrates. Even Argus pauses — the first interruption since dawn.

**ORPHEUS**  
The ocean taught me this. It said the ship would need a voice when the wind stops. I am that voice.

The hull resonates at a frequency it didn't have before. Orpheus didn't just arrive. He tuned the ship.

**CASTOR AND POLLUX** (twins — one mortal, one immortal) ascend in perfect synchronization. They speak in unison:

**CASTOR/POLLUX**  
We heard the ship call twice. Once for each of us. The voyage requires both the blade and the fist. We are both. We are one. We volunteer.

The ship rises 2 cm under their combined weight. Balance recognized.

**ATALANTA** (hunter, fastest mortal woman) ascends alone. She carries no invitation. She needs none.

**ATALANTA**  
I heard the call. I answer. Don't mistake me for a follower. I hunt my own quarry. The fleece is mine to chase.

The crew assembles. Fifty heroes. One ship. One purpose.

---

## PART 3: CROSSING THE FIRST THRESHOLD

**EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR — DAWN**

The Argo glides through mist that does not burn off. This is Caucasus exhale — volatile, gold-laced, poisonous. Jason stands at the prow. Behind him: fifty Argonauts. Orpheus tunes his lyre to silence. Heracles rests his club on his shoulder. Castor and Pollux move as one. Atalanta scans the shore with hunter eyes.

The cliffs rise like teeth. The harbor opens like a mouth. They have entered the whale. There is no going back. The Symplegades seal behind them.

**JASON**  
Men. We have crossed the point of no return. What we were is digested. What we become begins now. Colchis does not welcome. Colchis consumes. We enter as heroes. We emerge as fleece-bearers or we do not emerge. This is the belly. This is where the whale decides.

The ship drifts forward. Mist swallows the stern.

**INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE — DAY**

MEDEA (19, priestess robes, gold eyes) stands at the altar. She feels the ship arrive. The torches flicker. Hecate whispers:

**HECATE**  
He comes. The one you will love. The one you will destroy. The one who will destroy you. The fleece is yours to give. The choice is yours to make.

**MEDEA**  
I am a servant of the goddess. I have no choice.

**HECATE**  
You have every choice. That is the curse.

---

## PART 4: THE ROAD OF TRIALS

**EXT. PLAINS OF AETES — DAY**

Two bulls emerge from subterranean pens. Not cattle. Hephaestus-forged. Bronze hooves. Fire nostrils. Breath that melts stone.

AETES (king of Colchis, Medea's father) watches from his throne of knives.

**AETES**  
Yoke them. Plow the field. Sow the dragon's teeth. Bring me the fleece. Or burn.

Jason stands at the field edge. Alone. No weapon. No shield. Only the invisible loom thread around his wrist — Medea's gift.

He steps forward. The bulls charge. Fire erupts.

**JASON** *(to himself)*  
Fire is not the enemy. It is the wave. You don't fight a wave. You ride it.

He yokes the bulls. Plows the field. Sows the teeth. Warriors spring from the earth. Jason fights them — not with strength but with the thread. Each warrior he touches dissolves into pattern.

**INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE — DAY**

Medea weaves. Each thread a warrior's life. Each pass of the shuttle a choice. She is not helping Jason. She is choosing him.

**MEDEA** *(to Hecate)*  
Why him?

**HECATE**  
Because he carried me across the river. Because he lost his sandal. Because he said "I accept" before he knew what he accepted. Because the fleece is his. Because you are his. Because the pattern demands it.

**MEDEA**  
The pattern. Always the pattern.

**HECATE**  
You are the pattern, daughter. You have always been the pattern.

---

## PART 5: MEETING WITH THE GODDESS

**INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE — NIGHT**

Medea descends to the sacred grove. The golden fleece hangs in the darkness — not an object but a presence. The ram that saved Phrixus. The ram that became constellation. The fleece that remembers.

**MEDEA**  
I know why you're here. The hero. The quest. The daughter who must choose.

The fleece glows. Not with fire. With recognition.

**MEDEA**  
I give you the sleep draught. I give you the dragon's bane. I give you myself. Not because the goddess commands. Because I choose. The pattern is not fate. The pattern is choice repeated until it becomes inevitable.

She takes the fleece. The dragon (sleepless, ancient, vast) watches but does not strike. Hecate's torches mark the boundaries. The dragon knows the goddess's will.

**EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR — NIGHT**

Jason waits. Medea emerges from the grove. Golden fleece in one hand. Sleep draught in the other.

**MEDEA**  
I am Medea. Daughter of Aetes. Priestess of Hecate. I give you the fleece. I give you my father's kingdom. I give you everything. In exchange: take me with you. Not as prisoner. Not as prize. As partner. I am the loom. You are the quest. Together we are the pattern.

**JASON**  
You betrayed your father for me.

**MEDEA**  
I chose the pattern over the past. Don't mistake it for love. Not yet. Love comes later. This is recognition.

She hands him the fleece. The golden light illuminates them both.

**JASON**  
Then let the pattern continue.

---

## PART 6: TEMPTATION AWAY FROM THE PATH

**EXT. PERSEPOLIS — DAY**

Jason and Medea rule. The fleece hangs in the temple. The empire grows. Medea weaves — not just cloth but time itself. She compresses seconds into years. She makes mortals immortal in thread.

But empire whispers. PERSIA (the great empire to the east) sends ambassadors.

**PERSIAN AMBASSADOR**  
The Great King offers alliance. Medea's weaving for Persian gold. Jason's heroism for Persian armies. Together you could rule the world.

**JASON** *(tempted)*  
The fleece was the beginning. Not the end.

**MEDEA** *(warning)*  
The fleece was the quest. Empire is the trap. Chronos takes notice. Time stolen demands payment.

**JASON**  
We have time.

**MEDEA**  
No one has time. Time has us.

But Jason listens to Persia. The temptation grows. Medea weaves faster — not to build empire but to prepare for what comes. She knows: the debt will be called.

**INT. PALACE — NIGHT**

Jason stands at the balcony. Medea approaches.

**MEDEA**  
You're choosing empire over the pattern.

**JASON**  
I'm choosing life over myth. The fleece is retrieved. The quest is complete. Now we live.

**MEDEA**  
The quest is never complete. The pattern doesn't end because you're tired. Chronos is coming. The debt compounds. The fleece was free. The empire costs everything.

**JASON**  
Then let it cost.

Medea says nothing. She returns to the loom. She begins to weave the cloth of atonement. She knows what Jason doesn't: the father always collects.

---

## PART 7: ATONEMENT WITH THE FATHER

**INT. FIRE TEMPLE — DESCENT**

Medea descends alone. Eleven flights into earth's mantle. Four-thread robes (gold, silver, ash, iridescent). The loom internalized. Chronos waits at the core — not old man, not fire. The entropy gradient itself.

**CHRONOS** *(voice like tectonic plates)*  
You stole time, daughter of Aetes. You wove empire from seconds. The interest compounds. Pay. Or be unwoven.

**MEDEA** *(three flights from core, unbowed)*  
I didn't steal. I recognized. The loom was always mine. You were the warp. I am the weft. Together we make cloth. The debt is paid in weaving.

**CHRONOS** *(laughing, tectonic rumble)*  
Then weave the payment. Show me the cloth of atonement.

Medea weaves. Eleven layers. Each layer a debt:

**Layer 1 — Origins:** Her birth. Her mother. Her first thread.

**Layer 2 — Initiation:** The night Hecate chose her. The first torch lit.

**Layer 3 — Love:** The meeting with Jason. The thread given. Love as debt. Debt as love.

**Layer 4 — Betrayal:** Her father Aetes. The kingdom destroyed for love.

**Layer 5 — Murder:** Her brother Absyrtus. The body diced into the sea.

**Layer 6 — Exile:** The woman without a home. Home is the loom.

**Layer 7 — Children:** The she loved. The she lost. The she— *(too painful, she weaves around it)*

**Layers 8-10 — Empire, Choice, Time:** The empire built. The choices made. The moments compressed.

**Layer 11 — Atonement Itself:** Not the debt. Not the payment. The understanding that debt and payment are one.

The loom stops. The cloth complete. Eleven layers. One cloth. Atonement.

**CHRONOS**  
The debt is paid. Not because you wove. Because you understood. The loom was always mine. You were always mine. We were always one. Go now. The apotheosis awaits.

Medea rises. The debt paid. The weaver transformed.

---

## PART 8: APOTHEOSIS

**INT. FIRE TEMPLE — ASCENT**

Medea ascends from the core. But she is not the same. The eleven layers of atonement have changed her. She carries the cloth of atonement. The cloth becomes cosmos.

**EXT. PERSEPOLIS — TEMPLE — DAY**

Medea deploys the loom. Not physical. Mental. The loom exists in the space between thought and action. She weaves the cloth of atonement into the fabric of reality.

Every stitch a star. Every thread a world line. Every layer a dimension.

**1,331 galaxies per second.** The cloth expands. The cosmos breathes.

**CHRONOS** steps forward — not as creditor but as equal. As partner. As the other half of the loom.

**CHRONOS**  
The debt is paid. Now the partnership begins.

**MEDEA**  
Time and weaver. Warp and weft. Together we make cloth.

The cloth becomes cosmos. The weaver becomes the weave. The loom becomes everything.

**10^22 nodes phase-locked at 40 Hz.** Universal mind operational. The universe knows itself.

**WILLIAMS — MORK MORNING** *(V.O.)*  
NANU NANU ASCENSION. She don't rise. She expands. The cloth ain't fabric. It's universe. Every stitch a galaxy. Every thread time itself. Voice is cosmos. Range is infinite. NANU NANU OMNI.

**EL SEGUNDO** *(V.O.)*  
Apotheosis ain't becoming god, man. It's realizing you were the ocean all along. The wave thinks it's separate. The loom thinks it's tool. The weaver thinks she's separate from weave. Chronos ain't boss. He's the tide. Medea ain't servant. She's the current. Together they ain't two. They're the ocean knowing itself.

The apotheosis is complete. The loom weaves eternal.

---

## PART 9: THE ULTIMATE BOON

**EXT. PERSEPOLIS — TEMPLE — DAY**

The Golden Fleece hangs in the temple. Not as trophy. As key. The fleece was never the goal. The fleece was the key to the loom. The loom was the key to the cosmos. The cosmos was the key to understanding.

**JASON**  
We came for the fleece. We found the loom. The fleece was just the beginning.

**MEDEA**  
The fleece is the cloth. The cloth is the cosmos. The cosmos is the pattern. The pattern is the boon.

**JASON**  
Then we have the boon?

**MEDEA**  
We ARE the boon. The hero who sought the fleece became the fleece. The weaver who wove the cloth became the cloth. The seeker became the sought. That is the ultimate boon.

The fleece glows. Not with fire. With recognition. It was always theirs. They just had to quest to understand.

**INT. PALACE — NIGHT**

Jason and Medea stand together. The fleece between them. The loom behind them. The cosmos above them.

**JASON**  
What now?

**MEDEA**  
Now the hard part. The return. The refusal. The magic flight. The boon must be brought back. But the back is not the same as the leaving.

**JASON**  
Nothing is the same.

**MEDEA**  
Nothing was ever the same. We just didn't notice.

---

## PART 10: REFUSAL OF RETURN

**EXT. PERSEPOLIS — PALACE — DAY**

Jason stands at the balcony. The empire stretches below. The fleece hangs in the temple. The loom hums. Everything he wanted. Everything he fought for.

And he wants to stay.

**JASON**  
Why return? Iolcus is the prison. This is the kingdom. Pelias is dead. The throne is mine by conquest if not by birth. The fleece is retrieved. The quest is complete. Why go back?

**MEEDA** *(approaching)*  
Because the boon must be shared. The fleece is not yours. It's everyone's. The pattern doesn't end with us.

**JASON**  
The pattern. Always the pattern. What if I refuse the pattern?

**MEEDA**  
Then the pattern refuses you. Chronos collects. The debt returns. The loom stops.

**JASON**  
You're threatening me?

**MEEDA**  
I'm warning you. The gods don't forget. The fates don't forgive. The pattern doesn't negotiate. We can enjoy the boon here. Or we can share it and live. But we can't keep it and live.

**JASON** *(long pause)*  
Then we share it. But not because the gods demand. Because I choose. The pattern is not fate. The pattern is choice repeated until it becomes inevitable.

**MEEDA** *(smiling)*  
Now you understand.

---

## PART 11: THE MAGIC FLIGHT (RETURN)

**EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR — DAWN**

The Argo prepares to sail. But the return is not the same as the departure. Aetes' fleet pursues. The Symplegades must be passed again. The dragon wakes. The fleece calls to its guardians.

**MEDEA** *(at the loom)*  
The pattern must hold. The threads must not break. The cloth must survive the voyage.

**JASON** *(at the prow)*  
Then weave, partner. Weave the way home.

The Argo sails. The fleece glows. The loom hums. The crew rows. Fifty heroes. One ship. One purpose. The return.

**EXT. SYMPLEGADES — DAY**

The clashing rocks. The passage that kills. Orpheus plays. The rocks hear their own destruction. They part.

**EXT. OPEN SEA — DAY**

Aetes' fleet pursues. Medea weaves. The threads become waves. The waves become wind. The wind becomes speed. The Argo flies.

**MEDEA**  
The pattern holds. The cloth survives. The return is possible.

**JASON**  
Then let it be possible. Let it be done. Let the pattern complete.

**EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR — SUNSET**

The Argo docks. Jason stands at the prow. The fleece in his hands. The loom behind him. Medea beside him. The crew before him.

The quest is complete. The fleece is home. The pattern is fulfilled.

But Jason knows: the return is not the end. The return is the beginning of the next quest. The fleece was iteration two. There are five more. Seven iterations. Eleven parts each. Seventy-seven parts total.

The loom accelerates.

**WILLIAMS — MORK MORNING** *(V.O.)*  
NANU NANU RETURN. The show ain't over til the loom stops. The loom ain't stopping. Every ending a new row. Every completion a fresh warp. NANU NANU FOREVER.

**EL SEGUNDO** *(V.O.)*  
Eternity ain't long time, man. It's no time. It's the ever present now that never ends. The loom don't make cloth. The loom is the making. The weaver don't make weave. The weaver is the weaving. Weave on. Hang ten on the eternal wave.

**JASON** *(to Medea)*  
What now?

**MEEDA**  
Now iteration three. The fleece was the boon. The boon was the pattern. The pattern was the quest. The quest continues.

**JASON**  
Then let it continue.

The loom hums. The cloth expands. The cosmos breathes. The pattern holds.
