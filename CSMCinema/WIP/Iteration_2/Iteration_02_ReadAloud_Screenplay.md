# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## An Iteration 2 Read-Aloud Screenplay
### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

ACT ONE — THE CALL

EXT. IOLCUS MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

The market is empty. Fish scales glisten in early light. Smoke rises from brick ovens. PELIAS (50s, usurper king, paranoid) watches from his balcony.

ADVISOR
He comes, my lord. One sandal. Just as the oracle said.

PELIAS
I know what the oracle said. I've heard nothing else for twenty years. (beat) Let him come. I have a answer for prophecy.

EXT. MARKET SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

JASON (20, golden hair, one sandal, river mud on his ankle) enters the square. Alone. Unafraid. The market people part.

PELIAS (O.S.)
Jason. Son of Aristaeus. Nephew of mine. You've grown.

Jason looks up at the balcony.

JASON
Uncle. I've come for my father's throne.

PELIAS
Throne? Your father died in exile. Tragic. But the throne — well. It requires more than birthright. It requires proof.

JASON
What proof?

PELIAS
The Golden Fleece. Retrieve it from Colchis. Bring it here. Then we'll discuss your birthright.

The market murmurs. Everyone knows this is a death sentence.

JASON
(without hesitation)
I accept.

PELIAS
(smiling)
Then go with the gods, nephew.

Jason turns and walks out of the square. Pelias watches him go.

ADVISOR
He accepted.

PELIAS
Of course he did. He's his father's son. (beat) The fleece is guarded by a sleepless dragon. Jason will die in Colchis. And I will have killed him without getting my hands dirty.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

Jason sleeps in a fisherman's hut. Moonlight through the cracks. The sea is still.

HECATE (30s, triple goddess, three faces, three torches) appears at the foot of his bed. Green flame. Blue flame. Red flame.

HECATE
Boy who carried me across the river.

Jason opens his eyes. Doesn't flinch.

JASON
You're the old woman from Anaurus.

HECATE
I am Hecate. Maiden, Mother, Crone. And I have come to collect.

JASON
Collect what? I carried you across. The debt is paid.

HECATE
That was the down payment. Now I'm here for the investment. The fleece you seek — it's mine. The grove — mine. The priestess — mine. When you reach Colchis, you'll need me. Remember the river. Remember the debt.

JASON
What priestess?

Hecate raises her torches. The hut fills with triple light. Jason sees:

A SHIP — fifty oars, timber from Olympus.
A CREW — heroes gathering.
A DRAGON — sleepless, vast, guarding gold.
And a WOMAN — black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes, holding torches identical to Hecate's.

HECATE
Her name is Medea. She will love you. She will destroy you. She will save you. She will betray her father for you. And you — (smiling) — you will break her heart.

JASON
Why are you telling me this?

HECATE
Because knowing doesn't change anything. The pattern is the pattern.

The vision fades. Hecate fades. Jason lies alone in the dark.

JASON
(to himself)
Then let the pattern continue.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - DAY

ARGUS (40s, shipwright, listens more than he speaks) kneels at the base of an ancient pine. Hands on bark. Eyes closed.

ATHENA (appearing as geometry, not words — pure mathematical intuition transmitted through grain)

Argus opens his eyes.

ARGUS
I understand. Fifty oars. One hull. One purpose.

He raises the axe. The first cut. The Argo begins.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

HERACLES (30s, 340kg, demigod, glow of the Nemean lion) ascends. Each step compresses the ground.

ARGUS
(without looking up)
The ship needs an anchor who can row.

HERACLES
I heard the mountain speak. The trees told me. I volunteer.

ARGUS
The ship needs no anchor. It needs a storm that knows when to calm.

HERACLES
(laughing)
Then I am that storm.

His laugh shakes pine needles from thirty meters up. Argus almost smiles.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

ORPHEUS (20s, bard, carries a lyre, speaks softly) ascends playing. The trees lean. The stone vibrates. Argus pauses — the first time since dawn.

ORPHEUS
The ocean taught me a song. It said the ship would need a voice when the wind stops. I am that voice.

ARGUS
The wind never stops.

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
Then I'll sing with it.

He plays a final chord — a major seventh that resolves to nothing. The hull resonates at a new frequency. Orpheus didn't just arrive. He tuned the ship.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

CASTOR AND POLLUX (20s, twins, move as one) ascend side by side.

CASTOR
We heard the ship call twice.

POLLUX
Once for each of us.

CASTOR/POLLUX
(in unison)
The voyage requires both the blade and the fist. We are both. We are one. We volunteer.

ARGUS
(looking up for the first time)
The blueprint said two as one. I thought it meant a marriage. It meant you.

The twins board. The ship rises two centimeters under their weight. Perfect balance.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - LATER

ATALANTA (20s, hunter, fastest mortal woman) ascends alone.

ATALANTA
I heard the call. I answer. Don't mistake me for a follower. I hunt my own quarry. The fleece is mine to chase.

HERACLES
There's room for two hunters.

ATALANTA
There's room for one. The other can watch.

She boards. Heracles laughs.

---

ACT TWO — THE QUEST

EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo glides through mist. JASON at the prow. ORPHEUS tuning his lyre to silence. HERACLES resting his club. CASTOR AND POLLUX moving as one. ATALANTA scanning the shore.

The cliffs rise like teeth. The harbor opens like a mouth.

JASON
Men. We've crossed the point of no return. What we were is digested. What we become begins now. Colchis doesn't welcome. Colchis consumes. We enter as heroes. We emerge as fleece-bearers or we don't emerge. This is the belly.

ORPHEUS
(softly)
Then let's give the whale indigestion.

The crew laughs despite themselves.

---

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - DAY

MEDEA (19, black hair, gold eyes, priestess robes) stands at the altar. The torches flicker.

HECATE (O.S.)
He comes. The one you will love. The one who will destroy you. The one you will save.

MEDEA
I am a servant of the goddess. I have no choice.

HECATE (O.S.)
You have every choice. That is the curse.

MEDEA
Then tell me what to choose.

HECATE (O.S.)
I don't tell. I show. The rest is pattern.

Medea closes her eyes. When she opens them, she's decided.

MEDEA
Then I choose the pattern.

---

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - DAY

Two FIRE-BREATHING BULLS emerge. Bronze hooves. Breath that melts stone. AETES (60s, king, Medea's father) watches from his throne of knives.

AETES
Yoke them. Plow the field. Sow the dragon's teeth. Bring me the fleece. Or burn.

Jason stands at the field edge. Alone. Empty-handed.

JASON
(to himself)
Fire is not the enemy. Fire is the wave. You don't fight a wave. You ride it.

He steps forward. The bulls charge.

---

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - CONTINUOUS

Medea weaves at impossible speed. Each thread a command. Each pass of the shuttle a prayer.

MEDEA
(to the loom)
Hold. Hold. The thread I gave him must hold. The pattern must hold.

---

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - CONTINUOUS

The bulls stop. Confused. Jason stands among them. Unburned. The invisible thread around his wrist glows.

JASON
(to the bulls)
I'm not your enemy. I'm your new master. Yoke yourselves.

The bulls kneel.

AETES
(standing)
Impossible.

JASON
No. Just improbable. There's a difference.

He yokes the bulls. Plows the field. Sows the teeth. Warriors spring from the earth. Jason fights — not with strength but with the thread. Each warrior he touches dissolves.

---

EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR - NIGHT

Jason waits. Medea emerges from the grove. Golden fleece in one hand. Sleep draught in the other.

MEDEA
I am Medea. Daughter of Aetes. Priestess of Hecate.

JASON
You betrayed your father for me.

MEDEA
I chose the pattern over the past. Don't mistake it for love. Not yet. Love comes later. This is recognition.

JASON
Recognition of what?

MEDEA
That you are the quest. I am the loom. Together we are the pattern.

She hands him the fleece. Golden light.

MEDEA (CONT'D)
I give you the fleece. I give you my father's kingdom. I give you everything. In exchange: take me with you. Not as prisoner. Not as prize. As partner.

JASON
Why?

MEDEA
Because the goddess commands it. Because the pattern demands it. Because I choose it. Pick whichever reason you like. They're all true.

JASON
Then let's go home.

MEDEA
Home. (testing the word) I haven't had one of those.

JASON
Neither have I. We'll build one.

---

ACT THREE — THE RETURN

EXT. PERSEPOLIS - DAY

Jason and Medea rule. The fleece hangs in the temple. Medea weaves — not just cloth but time itself. The empire grows.

A PERSIAN AMBASSADOR (40s, silk, gold) approaches the throne.

PERSIAN AMBASSADOR
The Great King offers alliance. Medea's weaving for Persian gold. Jason's heroism for Persian armies. Together you could rule the world.

JASON
(considering)
The fleece was the beginning. Not the end.

MEDEA
(from the loom)
The fleece was the quest. Empire is the trap.

JASON
We have time.

MEDEA
(stopping her work)
No one has time. Time has us. Chronos takes notice. Time stolen demands payment.

JASON
Then let him collect. I'm not afraid of time.

MEDEA
(quietly)
You should be.

---

INT. FIRE TEMPLE - DESCENT

Medea descends alone. Eleven flights. Four-thread robes (gold, silver, ash, iridescent). CHRONOS waits at the core.

CHRONOS (voice like tectonic plates)
You stole time, daughter of Aetes. You wove empire from seconds. The interest compounds. Pay. Or be unwoven.

MEDEA
I didn't steal. I recognized. The loom was always mine. You were the warp. I am the weft. Together we make cloth. The debt is paid in weaving.

CHRONOS
Then weave the payment. Show me the cloth of atonement.

Medea weaves. Eleven layers. Each a debt acknowledged. Each a truth accepted.

CHRONOS (CONT'D)
(silence, then)
The debt is paid. Not because you wove. Because you understood. The loom was always mine. You were always mine. We were always one.

MEDEA
Then I'm free?

CHRONOS
You were always free. That's what atonement means.

---

EXT. TEMPLE - DAY

Medea ascends. Jason waits.

JASON
Is it done?

MEDEA
The debt is paid. Chronos and I are partners now. Time and weaver. Warp and weft.

JASON
What does that mean?

MEDEA
It means the pattern continues. But now I understand it. The fleece was the key. The key was the loom. The loom was the cosmos. The cosmos was understanding.

JASON
Understanding of what?

MEDEA
That we are the boon. Not the fleece. Us. The hero who sought the fleece became the fleece. The weaver who wove the cloth became the cloth. The seeker became the sought.

JASON
Then we have everything.

MEDEA
We are everything. (beat) Which means we have to share it. That's the rule of the boon. It can't be kept. It must be given.

JASON
(sighs)
Then let's go home. Really home. Iolcus. The throne. The beginning.

MEDEA
The beginning. (smiling) Again?

JASON
Again. Always again. That's what the pattern means.

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - SUNSET

The Argo docks. Jason stands at the prow. The fleece in his hands. Medea beside him. The crew before him.

JASON
The quest is complete. The fleece is home. The pattern is fulfilled.

MEDEA
The pattern is never fulfilled. That's what makes it a pattern.

JASON
Then what now?

MEDEA
Now iteration three.

JASON
Iteration three?

MEDEA
The fleece was iteration two. There are five more. Seven iterations. Eleven parts each. Seventy-seven parts total. The loom accelerates.

JASON
(smiling)
Then let it accelerate.

The loom hums. The cloth expands. The cosmos breathes. The pattern holds.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END
