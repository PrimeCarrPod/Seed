# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 6 — PART 01: THE CONVERGENCE
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

# PIECE 1: THE EIGHTH THREAD

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAWN

Jason wakes. The eighth thread around his wrist — the only one that remains. It pulses. Not calling. Not pulling. Just— (searching) —waiting.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's quiet.

JASON
(nodding)
Too quiet. The pattern is— (searching) —complete. And I don't know what to do with— (searching) —with the rest of my life.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
That's what being human is. Not knowing. Just— (searching) —just living.

ORPHEUS
(from outside)
Jason! Medea! You need to see this!

---

# PIECE 2: THE SKY

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

The crew gathered. Looking up.

The sky is— (searching) —wrong. Not broken. Not damaged. Just— (searching) —different. The stars are moving. Rearranging. Forming— (searching) —patterns.

ATALANTA
(returned from her hunting)
The stars are weaving themselves.

ZETES and CALAUS
(returned from the sky's edge)
The edge is gone. There is no— (searching) —no edge anymore.

KORE
(touching the ground)
The trees of hope. They're— (searching) —they're blooming. All at once. Everywhere.

MEDEA
(writing, afraid)
The pattern is— (searching) —it's not just free anymore. It's— (searching) —it's alive.

---

# PIECE 3: THE PATTERN SPEAKS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The tree of hope blooms. Its branches heavy. Its roots deep.

And from it — a voice. Not the tree. Not the Weaver. The pattern itself.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
Jason. (betrayal) You freed me. (betrayal) And now— (searching) —now I don't know what to do.

JASON
(awed)
You're— (searching) —you're awake.

THE PATTERN
(nodding through voice)
Because you let go. When you released the seven threads— (searching) —you released me. I'm not— (searching) —not a thing anymore. I'm— (searching) —I'm alive.

MEDEA
(writing)
What do you want?

THE PATTERN
(afraid)
I don't know. (betrayal) I've been— (searching) —been followed for eons. Followed and woven and— (searching) —and controlled. And now— (searching) —now I'm free. And freedom is— (searching) —is terrifying.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN'S FEAR

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(quietly)
I know the feeling.

THE PATTERN
(confused)
You?

JASON
(smiling)
I was the center. I held everything. And then— (searching) —then I let go. And I was— (searching) —I was terrified. But also— (searching) —also free.

THE PATTERN
(afraid)
But what do I do? Without— (searching) —without someone to hold me? Without— (searching) —without purpose?

MEDEA
(writing, gently)
The same thing we do. (betrayal) You live. You choose. You— (searching) —you become.

THE PATTERN
(awed)
Become what?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Whatever you want.

---

# PIECE 5: THE GODS ARRIVE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

Thunder. Lightning. The air splits.

ZEUS descends. POSEIDON. HADES. ATHENA. All the gods. Furious.

ZEUS
(angry)
Jason! You've destroyed everything!

JASON
(calmly)
Have I?

POSEIDON
(thundering)
The pattern is— (searching) —is out of control! The seas are— (searching) —are choosing their own paths!

HADES
(darkly)
The dead are— (searching) —are walking. Not escaping. Just— (searching) —just choosing to visit.

ATHENA
(sharply)
The stars are— (searching) —are rearranging! The future is— (searching) —is unwritten!

JASON
(smiling)
Good.

---

# PIECE 6: THE GODS' COMPLAINT

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

ZEUS
(draws lightning)
You don't understand. Without the pattern— (searching) —without control— (searching) —there is chaos.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. Without control— (searching) —there is freedom.

POSEIDON
(angry)
Freedom is chaos!

MEDEA
(writing, firmly)
No. Chaos is— (searching) —is what you had before the pattern. Freedom is— (searching) —is what you have after.

HADES
(darkly)
And what of us? What of— (searching) —of the gods?

JASON
(quietly)
You get to choose too. (betrayal) You're not— (searching) —not rulers anymore. You're just— (searching) —just beings. Like everyone else.

ATHENA
(afraid)
But we're gods.

JASON
(smiling)
Then act like it. (betrayal) Not as tyrants. As— (searching) —as guardians. Helpers.— (searching) —Friends.

---

# PIECE 7: THE GODS' CHOICE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - SUNSET

The gods confer. Quietly. Seriously.

ZEUS
(to the others)
He's right. (betrayal) We've been— (searching) —been holding so tight. Afraid of— (searching) —of losing control.

POSEIDON
(sighing)
And in holding tight— (searching) —we strangled everything.

HADES
(quietly)
I'm tired. (betrayal) Tired of judging. Tired of— (searching) —of being the end. I want to— (searching) —to be part of things. Not— (searching) —not apart from them.

ATHENA
(nodding)
Then we choose. (betrayal) Not control.— (searching) —Connection.

ZEUS
(to Jason)
If we do this— (searching) —if we let go— (searching) —what becomes of us?

JASON
(smiling)
What you were always meant to be. (betrayal) Not rulers. Not tyrants.— (searching) —Teachers. Friends.— (searching) —Family.

---

# PIECE 8: THE GODS RELEASE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - NIGHT

The gods stand together. And they— (searching) —they let go.

Not their power. Not their— (searching) —their divinity. Just their— (searching) —their need to control.

ZEUS
(releasing)
I release the thunder. Let it— (searching) —let it be weather. Not weapon.

POSEIDON
(releasing)
I release the seas. Let them— (searching) —let them be seas. Not soldiers.

HADES
(releasing)
I release the dead. Let them— (searching) —let them be remembered. Not judged.

ATHENA
(releasing)
I release wisdom. Let it— (searching) —let it be shared. Not hoarded.

The world responds. Not with chaos. With— (searching) —with beauty.

---

# PIECE 9: THE NEW WORLD

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

The people wake. And the world is— (searching) —different. Not because it changed. But because— (searching) —because they can finally see it.

The pattern is visible. Not as— (searching) —as control. As— (searching) —as connection. Every thread visible. Every choice— (searching) —every choice glowing.

OLD WOMAN
(awed)
I can see it. The pattern. It's— (searching) —it's beautiful.

OLD MAN
(touching a thread)
And I can— (searching) —I can touch it. Feel it.

CHILD
(laughing)
It's singing!

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)
The Music of Everything. (betrayal) All at once. All— (searching) —all together.

---

# PIECE 10: THE PATTERN'S JOY

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The pattern's voice. No longer afraid.— (searching) —Joyful.

THE PATTERN
(voice like laughter)
I understand now. (betrayal) I'm not meant to be— (searching) —to be held. I'm meant to be— (searching) —to be lived.

JASON
(quietly)
Yes.

THE PATTERN
(joyful)
And you— (searching) —you're not the center anymore. You're— (searching) —you're part of me. Just like— (searching) —just like everyone.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
That's what freedom means. (betrayal) Not being above. Being— (searching) —being part of.

---

# PIECE 11: THE EIGHTH THREAD'S PURPOSE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason looks at the eighth thread. Still there. Still— (searching) —still glowing.

MEDEA
(writing)
Why does it remain?

JASON
(thinking)
I think— (searching) —I think it's not for me. It's for— (searching) —for whoever comes next.

MEDEA
(writing)
Next?

JASON
(nodding)
I won't live forever. (betrayal) Someday— (searching) —someday someone else will need— (searching) —will need to hold the pattern. And this— (searching) —this will guide them.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then we'd better teach them well.

JASON
(smiling back)
Yes. We'd better.

The eighth thread glows softly. Waiting. Patient.— (searching) —Ready.
