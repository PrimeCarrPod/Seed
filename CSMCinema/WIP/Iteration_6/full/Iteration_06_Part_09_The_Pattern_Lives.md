# PIECE 1: THE MORNING AFTER

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Medea beneath the tree. Alone. But— (searching) —but not alone.

MEDEA
(whispering)
Jason?

A breeze. Warm. Gentle. Like— (searching) —like a hand on her cheek.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like everything)
I'm here. (betrayal) I'm— (searching) —I'm everywhere.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
I miss you.

THE PATTERN
(softly)
I miss you too. (betrayal) But I'm— (searching) —I'm also— (searching) —also more present than— (searching) —than I ever was. I'm in— (searching) —in every choice. Every— (searching) —every love. Every— (searching) —every moment.

# PIECE 2: THE NEW CENTER

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The eighth thread remains. Glowing. Waiting.

KORE
(touching it)
It's for me. (betrayal) Isn't it?

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason)
If you— (searching) —if you choose.

KORE
(afraid)
I don't know if I'm— (searching) —if I'm ready.

THE PATTERN
(softly)
No one ever is. (betrayal) But you— (searching) —you've been forgotten. You know— (searching) —know what it means. You'd— (searching) —you'd be compassionate.

KORE
(looking at Medea)
What do you think?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
I think— (searching) —I think Jason chose well. (betrayal) And I think— (searching) —I think you will too.

---

# PIECE 3: KORE'S CHOICE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

Kore holds the eighth thread. The crew— (searching) —the crew around her.

KORE
(quietly)
If I take this— (searching) —do I become like Jason? Do I— (searching) —do I disappear?

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason)
You become— (searching) —become more yourself. Not— (searching) —not less. More.

KORE
(thinking)
Will it hurt?

MEDEA
(writing)
Yes. (betrayal) But the hurt means— (searching) —means you care. And caring is— (searching) —is the point.

KORE
(nodding)
Then I choose. (betrayal) I choose to— (searching) —to hold the pattern. To— (searching) —to remember. To— (searching) —to love.

She wraps the thread around her wrist. Light— (searching) —light fills her. Not— (searching) —not consuming. Completing.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN RENEWED

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Kore stands. Changed. The same. Both.

KORE
(awed)
I can feel them. All of them. Every— (searching) —every person. Every— (searching) —every choice. Every— (searching) —every love.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
Welcome to— (searching) —to the center.

KORE
(smiling)
It's not a center. It's— (searching) —it's a web. And I'm— (searching) —I'm just one— (searching) —one thread. But I'm— (searching) —I'm connected to— (searching) —to everything.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
The pattern lives. (betrayal) Not because— (searching) —because it's held. Because it's— (searching) —because it's loved.
