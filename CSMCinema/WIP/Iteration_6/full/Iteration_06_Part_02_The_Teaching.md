# PIECE 1: THE SCHOOL

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

A school. Not for warriors. Not for— (searching) —for heroes. For— (searching) —for everyone.

Jason and Medea teach. Children. Adults.— (searching) —Anyone who wants to learn.

JASON
(to the students)
The pattern isn't something you— (searching) —you follow. It's something you— (searching) —you create. Every choice. Every— (searching) —every moment.

STUDENT
But how do we know if we're— (searching) —if we're choosing right?

MEDEA
(writing)
You don't. (betrayal) That's the beauty of it. (betrayal) Right and wrong aren't— (searching) —aren't written in the stars. They're— (searching) —they're written in your heart.

STUDENT
(timidly)
What if my heart is— (searching) —is wrong?

JASON
(gently)
Your heart isn't wrong. It's— (searching) —it's learning. And learning means— (searching) —means making mistakes. And mistakes mean— (searching) —mean growing.

# PIECE 2: THE GODS AS STUDENTS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The gods arrive. Not as teachers. As— (searching) —as students.

ZEUS
(timidly)
We want to learn. (betrayal) How to— (searching) —how to be part of things. Not— (searching) —not above them.

JASON
(smiling)
Sit. Listen.— (searching) —Choose.

POSEIDON
(asking)
How do we— (searching) —how do we let go of— (searching) —of being gods?

MEDEA
(writing)
You don't stop being gods. You just— (searching) —just stop being only gods. You're also— (searching) —also friends. Neighbors.— (searching) —Teachers. Learners.

HADES
(quietly)
I've been the end for so long. I don't know how to be— (searching) —to be anything else.

JASON
(softly)
Then start small. (betrayal) Smile at someone. Help someone.— (searching) —Be someone's friend.

ATHENA
(awed)
That's it?

JASON
(smiling)
That's everything.

# PIECE 3: THE FIRST LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

Zeus walks through the market. Trying.— (searching) —Trying to be human.

A CHILD drops her doll. Zeus picks it up. Hands it to her.

CHILD
Thank you!

ZEUS
(startled, then smiling)
You're— (searching) —you're welcome.

The child runs off. Zeus stands there.— (searching) —Transformed.

ZEUS
(to himself)
That's all it took. (betrayal) A small kindness. (betrayal) And I feel— (searching) —I feel more alive than I have in— (searching) —in millennia.

# PIECE 4: THE SECOND LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

Poseidon helps a fisherman. Not with— (searching) —with waves or wind. With— (searching) —with nets.

POSEIDON
(awkwardly)
Like this. You— (searching) —you pull here. And— (searching) —and tie there.

FISHERMAN
(grateful)
Thank you. I've been— (searching) —been doing it wrong for years.

POSEIDON
(awed)
I've been making waves for eons. Never— (searching) —never occurred to me to help with— (searching) —with nets.

FISHERMAN
(laughing)
Waves are nice. But nets— (searching) —nets feed my family.

POSEIDON
(quietly)
I understand now.

# PIECE 5: THE THIRD LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - GARDEN - DAY

Hades sits with an OLD WOMAN. She's dying. Not— (searching) —not scared. Just— (searching) —just ready.

OLD WOMAN
I'm not afraid.

HADES
(surprised)
You're not?

OLD WOMAN
(shaking her head)
I've lived. I've loved. I've— (searching) —I've chosen. (betrayal) What comes next is just— (searching) —just another choice.

HADES
(awed)
You're braver than I am.

OLD WOMAN
(laughing)
You're the god of death. How are you— (searching) —how are you afraid?

HADES
(quietly)
Because I've only ever been the end. I've never— (searching) —never understood the middle. The— (searching) —the living.

OLD WOMAN
(taking his hand)
Then learn. (betrayal) It's not— (searching) —it's not too late.

# PIECE 6: THE FOURTH LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - SCHOOL - DAY

Athena teaches. Not— (searching) —not from above. From— (searching) —from beside.

ATHENA
(to a student)
I don't know the answer. (betrayal) Let's— (searching) —let's find out together.

STUDENT
(amazed)
You don't know?

ATHENA
(smiling)
I know many things. But not— (searching) —not everything. And that's— (searching) —that's okay. Because not knowing means— (searching) —means learning. And learning means— (searching) —means growing.

STUDENT
(awed)
You're different than I— (searching) —than I thought gods would be.

ATHENA
(softly)
I'm different than I thought— (searching) —I thought I'd be too.

# PIECE 7: THE TEACHERS LEARN

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The crew gathered. Tired. Happy.

JASON
(to the crew)
They're learning. The gods. The people.— (searching) —Everyone.

ATALANTA
(returned from hunting)
And you? What are you— (searching) —what are you learning?

JASON
(thinking)
That I don't have to— (searching) —to know everything. That I can— (searching) —can be part of things. Not— (searching) —not above them.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The Music of Togetherness. (betrayal) It's— (searching) —it's the most beautiful thing I've ever heard.

KORE
(happy)
Even better than the Music of the Future?

ORPHEUS
(smiling)
Different. The future is— (searching) —is possibility. This is— (searching) —this is actuality. And actuality is— (searching) —is sweeter.
