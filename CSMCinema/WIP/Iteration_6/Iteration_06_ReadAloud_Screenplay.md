# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## An Iteration 6 Read-Aloud Screenplay
### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE — THE CONVERGENCE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The tree of hope blooms. JASON at the bow, the eighth thread around his wrist. MEDEA beside him.

THE PATTERN (voice like everything):
Jason. You freed me. And now I don't know what to do.

JASON
(quietly)
I know the feeling.

THE PATTERN
You?

JASON
I was the center. I held everything. And then I let go. And I was terrified. But also free.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The gods descend. Furious.

ZEUS
Jason! You've destroyed everything!

JASON
(calmly)
Have I?

POSEIDON
The pattern is out of control! The seas are choosing their own paths!

JASON
(smiling)
Good.

ZEUS
(draws lightning)
You don't understand. Without the pattern—there is chaos.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. Without control—there is freedom.

ATHENA
And what of us? What of the gods?

JASON
You get to choose too. Not as tyrants. As guardians. Helpers. Friends.

---

## ACT TWO — THE TEACHING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The gods arrive as students.

ZEUS
(timidly)
We want to learn. How to be part of things. Not above them.

JASON
(smiling)
Sit. Listen. Choose.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

Zeus walks through the market. A CHILD drops her doll. Zeus picks it up.

CHILD
Thank you!

ZEUS
(startled, then smiling)
You're welcome.

ZEUS (CONT'D)
(to himself)
That's all it took. A small kindness. And I feel more alive than I have in millennia.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

Poseidon helps a fisherman with nets.

POSEIDON
Like this. You pull here. And tie there.

FISHERMAN
Thank you. I've been doing it wrong for years.

POSEIDON
(awed)
I've been making waves for eons. Never occurred to me to help with nets.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - GARDEN - DAY

Hades sits with an OLD WOMAN. She's dying.

OLD WOMAN
I'm not afraid.

HADES
You're not?

OLD WOMAN
I've lived. I've loved. I've chosen. What comes next is just another choice.

HADES
(awed)
You're braver than I am.

OLD WOMAN
Then learn. It's not too late.

---

## ACT THREE — THE FRAYING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Jason wakes. The eighth thread is flickering.

MEDEA
(writing, afraid)
The thread. It's fraying.

ATALANTA
(from outside, urgent)
Jason! The trees! The trees of hope!

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

The tree of hope. Its leaves browning. Falling.

THE TREE
(voice weak)
The pattern is fraying. It's too free. Without something to hold it—it's coming apart.

MEDEA
(writing)
But Jason released it. He let it go.

THE TREE
And that was right. But freedom without structure is just chaos. The pattern needs both. Freedom and form.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Everyone weaves. Not with thread. With choice. With intention. With love.

ZEUS
(awed)
It's working. The pattern is holding.

POSEIDON
Not because we're forcing it. Because we're part of it.

HADES
I've never felt so connected.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE TRANSFORMATION

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The Keeper of the Forgotten arrives. But lighter. Not burdened.

THE KEEPER
Jason. The forgotten are remembered. Because of what you did.

JASON
I didn't do anything. I just let go.

THE KEEPER
You did more than that. You made room. For all of us.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - SCHOOL - DAY

Jason's final class.

JASON
(to the students)
The pattern isn't something you hold. It's something you are. Every choice you make. Every person you love. Every moment you live.

STUDENT
But what if we fail?

JASON
(smiling)
Then you fail. And you try again. That's the pattern. Not perfection. Persistence.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE FINAL CHOICE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The eighth thread speaks.

THE EIGHTH THREAD
Jason. It's time. To choose. To stay. Or to go into the pattern.

MEDEA
(writing, afraid)
I don't want to lose you.

JASON
(taking her hand)
You won't. I'll be in every thread. Every choice. Every moment. I'll be the pattern. And the pattern is love.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

Jason stands before the tree of hope. The eighth thread glowing.

JASON
(to everyone)
I'm not dying. I'm becoming. Becoming what I've always been. Part of the pattern. Part of you.

He steps forward. Into the light. Into the pattern. Into everything.

And he's gone. Not dead. Just different. Everywhere. Nowhere. Everything.

MEDEA
(whispering)
I feel him. He's here. Everywhere.

---

## ACT SIX — THE PATTERN LIVES

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Kore holds the eighth thread.

KORE
It's for me. Isn't it?

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason)
If you choose.

KORE
(looking at Medea)
What do you think?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
I think Jason chose well. And I think you will too.

KORE
Then I choose. I choose to hold the pattern. To remember. To love.

She wraps the thread around her wrist. Light fills her. Not consuming. Completing.

KORE (CONT'D)
(awed)
I can feel them all. Every person. Every choice. Every love.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
Welcome to the center.

KORE
(smiling)
It's not a center. It's a web. And I'm just one thread. But I'm connected to everything.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Medea alone beneath the tree. But not alone.

MEDEA
(whispering)
Jason. Are you there?

A breeze. Warm. Gentle. Like arms around her.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason)
Always. I'm in every thread. Every choice. Every moment.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
I love you.

THE PATTERN
I love you too. And that never ends.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

Kore. Medea. The crew. The gods. All together.

KORE
(to Medea)
What happens now?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Now we live. We love. We choose.

KORE
And iteration seven?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Iteration seven will come when we're ready.

KORE
I'm not ready.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
No one ever is. But the pattern provides.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The stars above. Each one a choice. Each one a love. Each one a possibility.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
I am the pattern. And I am love.

MEDEA
(whispering)
And love is everything.

KORE
(smiling)
Then we're home.

The pattern continues. Forever.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END OF ITERATION 6
