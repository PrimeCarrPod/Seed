# PIECE 1: THE BOOK COMPLETE

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAY

Jason finishes writing. The book—complete.

MEDEA
(writing)

JASON
(nodding)
The story is—done.

MEDEA
(writing)
What will you—do with it?

JASON
(smiling)

# PIECE 2: THE SHARING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason shares the story. The people—listen.

JASON
(reading)
"And so—the pattern was complete. Not because—it was perfect. Because—it was loved. And love is—the thread that holds—without strangling. The thread that—connects without—controlling. The thread that—frees without—abandoning."

CHILD
(awed)
That's—beautiful.

JASON
(smiling)
It's—true.

# PIECE 3: THE STORY SPREADS

EXT. GREECE - VARIOUS - DAY (MONTAGE)

The story spreads. Across—Greece. Across—the world. Across—time.

A MOTHER reads to her child. The child—dreams.

A TEACHER reads to her students. The students—choose.

A BARD sings in a tavern. The people—hope.

The story—continues. Forever.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN REMEMBERS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The pattern remembers. Every—story. Every—choice. Every—love.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
I remember—Jason. I remember—Medea. I remember—Kore. I remember—everyone.

KORE
(awed)
And you'll—remember—forever?

THE PATTERN
(softly)
Forever. (betrayal) Because you—mattered. You—all mattered.

---

# PIECE 5: THE CONTINUATION

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The new keeper—the child. Learning. Growing.—Becoming.

CHILD
(to Kore)
Will I—do it right?

KORE
(smiling)
There's no—right. There's only—love.

CHILD
(nodding)
Then I'll—love.

KORE
(smiling)
That's—everything.
