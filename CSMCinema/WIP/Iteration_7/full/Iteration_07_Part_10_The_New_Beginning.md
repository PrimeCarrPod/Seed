# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 10: THE NEW BEGINNING
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 10 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE MORNINGS

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAWN

Jason and Medea. Together.—Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's—over.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. It's—beginning.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then let's—begin.

---

# PIECE 2: THE SCHOOL CONTINUES

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The school continues. Teachers. Not—students.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The Music of—Everything.

ATALANTA
(returned from hunting)
And the Music of—the Hunt.

ZETES and CALAUS
(returned from the sky)
And the Music of—Flight.

KORE
(smiling)
And the Music of—Remembering.

MEDEA
(writing)
All the musics.—Together.

---

# PIECE 3: THE GODS AMONG US

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The gods live among the people. Not—above. Just—with.

ZEUS
(helping build a house)
I never knew—building was so—satisfying.

POSEIDON
(teaching a child to swim)
I never knew—teaching was so—rewarding.

HADES
(sitting with the dying)
I never knew—comfort was so—beautiful.

ATHENA
(learning from a student)
I never knew—not knowing was so—freeing.
