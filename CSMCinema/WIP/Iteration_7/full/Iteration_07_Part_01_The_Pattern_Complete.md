# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 01: THE PATTERN COMPLETE
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 01 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE EIGHTH THREAD CALLS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

KORE at the center. The eighth thread around her wrist. It pulses. Not calling. Not pulling. Just—waiting.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's quiet.

KORE
(nodding)
Too quiet. The pattern is—complete. And I don't know what to do with the rest of my life.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
That's what being human is. Not knowing. Just living.

THE PATTERN (voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything):
The pattern is complete. But not finished.

KORE
(confused)
What's the difference?

THE PATTERN
(softly)
Complete means whole. Finished means—over. The pattern is whole. But it's never over.

---

# PIECE 2: THE VISITOR

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

A figure approaches. Old. Familiar. But changed.

MEDEA
(awed, writing)
Jason?

JASON (older, human, mortal)
Hello, Medea.

MEDEA
(running to him)
You're—you're here. You're—real.

JASON
(taking her hands)
I'm here. I'm real. I'm—me again.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
How?

JASON
(smiling)
The pattern released me. I gave it everything. And it—gave me back.

---

# PIECE 3: THE PATTERN'S GIFT

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(to Kore)
You've done well. Holding the pattern. Remembering. Loving.

KORE
(timidly)
I'm not you.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. You're better. You've been forgotten. You know what it means. You're—compassionate.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
The pattern is complete. Because of both of you. Because of—everyone.

JASON
(quietly)
What happens now?

THE PATTERN
(smiling through voice)
Now—the story ends. And begins. Forever.

---

# PIECE 4: THE FINAL LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

Jason, Medea, Kore. The crew. The gods. Everyone.

JASON
(to everyone)
The pattern is complete. Not because it's perfect. Because it's—loved.

ZEUS
(awed)
And what of us? What of the gods?

JASON
(smiling)
You get to choose. As you always have. Not as rulers. Not as tyrants. As—family.

POSEIDON
(smiling)
Family. I like that.

HADES
(quietly)
We'll stay. And visit. And—be part of things.

ATHENA
(awed)
The pattern is in good hands.

---

# PIECE 5: THE EIGHTH THREAD'S PURPOSE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

KORE holds the eighth thread. Glowing. Waiting.

KORE
(to Jason)
What do I do with this?

JASON
(quietly)
Pass it on. When the time comes. To the one who—needs it next.

KORE
(afraid)
What if I choose wrong?

JASON
(smiling)
You won't. The pattern—always chooses well.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
And we'll be here. To help.

---

# PIECE 6: THE STORY ENDS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The pattern complete. The story whole.

JASON
(to Medea)
I'm home.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
You're home.

JASON
(smiling)
And I'm never leaving again.

MEDEA
(writing)
Promise?

JASON
(taking her hand)
Promise.

---

# PIECE 7: THE PATTERN CONTINUES

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

The stars above. Each one a choice. Each one a love. Each one a possibility.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
I am the pattern. And I am—love.

JASON
(whispering)
And love is—everything.

KORE
(smiling)
Then we're—home.

The pattern continues. Forever.
