# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 03: THE LEGACY
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 03 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE BOOK

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAY

Jason writes. Not—weaving. Not—choosing. Just—writing.

MEDEA
(writing)
What are you writing?

JASON
(smiling)
Our story. So—people remember. So—the pattern continues.

MEDEA
(writing)
What will you call it?

JASON
(thinking)
The Golden Fleece. (betrayal) The story of—a quest. A crew. A—love.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
And the pattern?

JASON
(nodding)
And the pattern. Because—the pattern is—everything.

---

# PIECE 2: THE FIRST PAGE

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(reading aloud)
"In the beginning—there was chaos. And from chaos—came choice. And from choice—came pattern. And from pattern—came everything."

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
It's beautiful.

JASON
(smiling)
It's true.

---

# PIECE 3: THE STORY SPREADS

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The story spreads. From—person to person. From—voice to voice. From—heart to heart.

OLD WOMAN
(to her grandchildren)
And Jason said—the pattern isn't fate. It's choice.

CHILD
(awed)
I can choose?

OLD WOMAN
(smiling)
You always could.

OLD MAN
(to his friends)
And the gods—they learned to be—human.

FRIEND
(awed)
The gods—human?

OLD MAN
(smiling)
The gods—family.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN REMEMBERS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The pattern remembers. Not—because it has to. Because—it wants to.

Every thread. Every choice. Every love. All—held. All—cherished. All—remembered.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
I remember everything. (betrayal) Every choice. Every love. Every—moment.

KORE
(awed)
And you'll remember—forever?

THE PATTERN
(softly)
Forever. (betrayal) Because forever is—all there is.

---

# PIECE 5: THE LEGACY

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The crew gathered. The gods. The—people. All—together.

JASON
(to everyone)
The story doesn't end. It—continues. Through—you. Through—your choices. Through—your love.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
And that's—everything.

KORE
(awed)
Then we'll—continue it. The story. The—pattern. The—love.

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. You will.

---
