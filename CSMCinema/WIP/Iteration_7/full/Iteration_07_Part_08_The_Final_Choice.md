# PIECE 1: THE CHOICE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea beneath the tree. The stars above.

MEDEA
(writing)
What do you—want to do? Now that—it's over?

JASON
(thinking)
I want to—live. With you. Here.—Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
No more—quests?

JASON
(shaking his head)
The quests are—done. The pattern is—complete. Now—we just—live.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then let's—live.

---

# PIECE 2: THE FINAL UNDERSTANDING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Jason and Medea. The pattern—whole.

JASON
(quietly)
I understand—now. Why the pattern—needed me. Why it—needed all of us.

MEDEA
(writing)
Why?

JASON
(smiling)
Because the pattern—isn't something you—hold. It's something you—are. And being—means choosing. Loving.—Living.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
Yes. It—does.
