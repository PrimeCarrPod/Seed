# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 07: THE FINAL LESSON
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 07 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE LAST CLASS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason's last class. The students—gathered. Knowing.

JASON
(to the students)
This is my—last lesson. Not because—I'm leaving. But because—you don't need me anymore.

STUDENT
(afraid)
But we do—need you!

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. You need—each other. You need—yourselves. The pattern isn't—in me. It's in—you.

MEDEA
(writing)
It always was.

---

# PIECE 2: THE LAST LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(thinking)
The last lesson is—this: The pattern doesn't need—to be perfect. It needs to be—lived. Messy. Beautiful.—Real.

STUDENT
(timidly)
But what if we—fail?

JASON
(smiling)
Then you fail. And you—try again. That's—the pattern. Not—perfection. Persistence. Not—certainty. Faith. Not—control. Love.

---

# PIECE 3: THE GODS' GRATITUDE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The gods gather. Thanking—thanking Jason.

ZEUS
(serious)
You taught us—taught us how to be—more than tyrants.

POSEIDON
(smiling)
How to be—friends.

HADES
(quietly)
How to be—part of things.

ATHENA
(awed)
How to be—human.

JASON
(smiling)
You were always—more than tyrants. You just—forgot.

---

# PIECE 4: THE CREW'S FAREWELL

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAWN

The crew gathered. The Argo—waiting.

ATALANTA
(returned from hunting)
I'm staying. (betrayal) This is—home now.

ZETES and CALAUS
(returned from the sky)
We're staying too. (betrayal) The sky can—wait.

KORE
(hugging Jason)
I'll remember. For—everyone.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The music continues. (betrayal) It always—does.

---

# PIECE 5: THE PATTERN'S PROMISE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

The pattern speaks. One—last time. To—Jason.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
Thank you. (betrayal) For—holding me. For—releasing me. For—loving me.

JASON
(quietly)
Thank you for—everything.

THE PATTERN
(softly)
The story—continues. Through—you. Through—your choices. Through—your love.

JASON
(smiling)
Then we'll—continue it.
