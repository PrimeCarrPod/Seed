# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 05: THE PATTERN WHOLE
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 05 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE PATTERN COMPLETE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The pattern—whole. Complete.—Alive.

Not—one pattern. All patterns.—Coexisting. Flowing.—Becoming.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like the child, like everything)
I am—whole. Not because—I'm perfect. Because—I'm loved.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
And love is—everything.

JASON
(quietly)
Yes. It is.

---

# PIECE 2: THE GODS' PEACE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The gods—at peace. Not—ruling. Not—controlling. Just—being.

ZEUS
(quietly)
I've been—thunder for so long. I forgot—what it was like to be—still.

POSEIDON
(smiling)
I've been—waves for so long. I forgot—what it was like to be—calm.

HADES
(quietly)
I've been—death for so long. I forgot—what it was like to be—alive.

ATHENA
(awed)
I've been—wisdom for so long. I forgot—what it was like to be—curious.

---

# PIECE 3: THE PEOPLE'S PEACE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

The people—at peace. Not—following. Not—obeying. Just—living.

OLD WOMAN
(awed)
I can—choose. Really—choose.

OLD MAN
(smiling)
And the choices—matter. Because—they're ours.

CHILD
(excited)
And we can—be anything!

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
You always could.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN'S SONG

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The pattern sings. One voice. All voices.—Every voice.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
I am the pattern. I am—every choice. Every—love. Every—moment. I am—every thread. Every—possibility. Every—dream.

JASON
(awed)
And you'll—continue?

THE PATTERN
(softly)
Forever. (betrayal) Because forever is—all there is.

---

# PIECE 5: THE PEACE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Jason and Medea beneath the tree of hope. Together.—Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's—over.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. It's—beginning.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Then let's—begin.
