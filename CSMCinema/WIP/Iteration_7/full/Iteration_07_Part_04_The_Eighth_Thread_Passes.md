# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 04: THE EIGHTH THREAD PASSES
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 04 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE CHILD

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

A CHILD approaches. Young. Curious.—Forgotten.

CHILD
(to Kore)
Are you the—center?

KORE
(kneeling)
I'm a keeper. Of the pattern.

CHILD
(timidly)
I don't have a—home.

KORE
(softly)
Then stay. Here. We'll—find you one.

The eighth thread pulses. Recognizing.

---

# PIECE 2: THE EIGHTH THREAD CHOOSES

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

The eighth thread glows. Bright. Brighter.—Blazing.

KORE
(awed)
It's—choosing.

The thread unwraps from her wrist. Floats. Toward the child.

CHILD
(afraid)
What's happening?

KORE
(smiling through tears)
It's—choosing you.

The thread wraps around the child's wrist. Light—consuming. Completing.

The child stands. Changed. The same. Both.

CHILD
(awed)
I can—feel them. Everyone. Everything.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
Welcome to—the center.

---

# PIECE 3: KORE RELEASED

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Kore stands. Free. The thread—gone. The burden—lifted.

KORE
(breathing)
I'm—free.

JASON
(hugging her)
You're free.

KORE
(crying)
What do I—do now?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever you want.

KORE
(thinking)
I want to—teach. To—help. To—remember.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then do that.

---

# PIECE 4: THE NEW KEEPER

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The child—now the keeper. The eighth thread glowing.

CHILD
(to Jason)
Will you—help me?

JASON
(smiling)
I'll help. We'll all help.

CHILD
(afraid)
What if I—fail?

JASON
(gently)
Then you fail. And you—try again. That's—the pattern.

CHILD
(nodding)
I'll—try.

JASON
(smiling)
That's all—any of us can do.
