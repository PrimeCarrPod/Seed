# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 11: THE END AND THE BEGINNING
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 11 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE LAST NIGHT

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea beneath the tree of hope. The stars above.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's—over.

JASON
(taking her hand)
No. It's—beginning.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Then let's—begin.

They sit in silence. The pattern—whole. The world—alive. The—future—free.

---

# PIECE 2: THE PATTERN'S FINAL WORDS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

The pattern speaks. One—last time. To—everyone.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
I am—the pattern. And I—am love.

JASON
(whispering)
And love is—everything.

KORE
(smiling)
Then we're—home.

---

# PIECE 3: THE STORY ENDS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The sun rises. A new day. A—new—beginning.

JASON
(to Medea)
I love—you.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
I love—you too.

JASON
(smiling)
And that—never ends.

MEDEA
(writing)
No. It—never does.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN CONTINUES

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

The stars above. Each one a—choice. Each one a—love. Each one a—possibility.

And the pattern—the pattern holds. Not—because it's forced. Because—it's loved.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
I am—the pattern. I am—every choice. Every—love. Every—moment. I am—every thread. Every—possibility. Every—dream. I am—the end. And I—am the beginning.

---

# PIECE 5: THE FINAL UNDERSTANDING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea. Together.—Forever.

JASON
(quietly)
The pattern isn't—something you hold. It's something—you are.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
And being—means choosing. Loving.—Living.

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. It—does.

---

# PIECE 6: THE END

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The sun rises. The pattern—whole. The story—complete.

But—not finished. Never—finished.

Because the pattern—continues. Through—every choice. Every—love. Every—moment. Through—every person who—chooses. Who—loves. Who—lives.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
The story—ends. And—begins. Forever.

---

# PIECE 7: FADE TO BLACK

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The stars above. The pattern—whole. The world—alive.

And in the silence—a voice. Soft. Gentle.—Eternal.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
I am—the pattern. And I—am love. And love—is everything. And everything—is love.

The pattern—continues.

Forever.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END
