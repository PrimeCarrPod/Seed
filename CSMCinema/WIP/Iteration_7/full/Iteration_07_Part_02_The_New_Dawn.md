# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 7 — PART 02: THE NEW DAWN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 7 of 7  
**Part:** 02 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE MORNING

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAWN

Jason and Medea. Together. Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's been so long.

JASON
(nodding)
Too long.

MEDEA
(writing)
What do we do now?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever we want. The pattern doesn't—call anymore. It's—complete.

MEDEA
(writing)
And Kore?

JASON
(quietly)
She'll hold it. Until—someone else needs to. And then—she'll pass it on.

---

# PIECE 2: THE SCHOOL

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

The school continues. But now—teachers. Not students.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The Music of the Future. The Music of the Past. The Music of—Everything.

ATALANTA
(returned from hunting)
And the Music of the Hunt.

ZETES and CALAUS
(returned from the sky)
And the Music of Flight.

KORE
(smiling)
And the Music of Remembering.

MEDEA
(writing)
All the musics. Together.

---

# PIECE 3: THE GODS' NEW LIFE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The gods live among the people. Not above. Not apart. Just—with.

ZEUS
(helping build a house)
I never knew—building was so—satisfying.

POSEIDON
(teaching a child to swim)
I never knew—teaching was so—rewarding.

HADES
(sitting with the dying)
I never knew—comfort was so—beautiful.

ATHENA
(learning from a student)
I never knew—not knowing was so—freeing.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PATTERN'S SONG

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The pattern sings. Not with words. With—feeling.

Every thread. Every choice. Every love. All—held. All—cherished. All—free.

THE PATTERN
(voice like Jason, like Kore, like everything)
I am not a cage. I am a garden. Grow freely. Love deeply. Choose bravely.

KORE
(awed)
And I'll tend it. Not control it. Tend it.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
That's all any of us can do.

---

# PIECE 5: THE FUTURE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The future is—open. Not—controlled. Not—predicted. Just—possible.

JASON
(to Medea)
What do you want to do?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Everything. Nothing. This.

JASON
(smiling)
This?

MEDEA
(writing)
You. Me. Home.

JASON
(taking her hand)
Then let's do this.
