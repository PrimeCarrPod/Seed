# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## An Iteration 7 Read-Aloud Screenplay
### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE — THE PATTERN COMPLETE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

KORE at the center. The eighth thread around her wrist. It pulses—waiting.

THE PATTERN (voice like everything):
The pattern is complete. But not finished.

KORE
(confused)
What's the difference?

THE PATTERN
(softly)
Complete means whole. Finished means over. The pattern is whole. But it's never over.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

A figure approaches. JASON—older, human, mortal.

MEDEA
(awed, writing)
Jason?

JASON
Hello, Medea.

MEDEA
(running to him)
You're here. You're real.

JASON
(taking her hands)
I'm here. The pattern released me. I gave it everything. And it gave me back.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(to Kore)
You've done well. Holding the pattern. Remembering. Loving.

KORE
(timidly)
I'm not you.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. You're better. You've been forgotten. You know what it means.

---

## ACT TWO — THE NEW DAWN

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAWN

Jason and Medea. Together. Home.

MEDEA
(writing)
What do we do now?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever we want. The pattern doesn't call anymore.

MEDEA
(writing)
And Kore?

JASON
(quietly)
She'll hold it. Until someone else needs to. And then she'll pass it on.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The gods live among the people.

ZEUS
(helping build a house)
I never knew building was so satisfying.

POSEIDON
(teaching a child to swim)
I never knew teaching was so rewarding.

HADES
(sitting with the dying)
I never knew comfort was so beautiful.

ATHENA
(learning from a student)
I never knew not knowing was so freeing.

---

## ACT THREE — THE EIGHTH THREAD PASSES

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

A CHILD approaches. Young. Curious. Forgotten.

CHILD
(to Kore)
I don't have a home.

KORE
(softly)
Then stay. Here. We'll find you one.

The eighth thread pulses. Recognizing.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

The eighth thread glows.unwraps from Kore's wrist. Floats toward the child.

CHILD
(afraid)
What's happening?

KORE
(smiling through tears)
It's choosing you.

The thread wraps around the child's wrist. Light—consuming. Completing.

CHILD
(awed)
I can feel them. Everyone. Everything.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Kore stands. Free.

KORE
(breathing)
I'm free.

JASON
(hugging her)
You're free.

KORE
(crying)
What do I do now?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever you want.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE FINAL LESSON

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason's last class.

JASON
(to the students)
The pattern isn't in me. It's in you.

STUDENT
But what if we fail?

JASON
(smiling)
Then you fail. And you try again. That's the pattern. Not perfection. Persistence. Not certainty. Faith. Not control. Love.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The gods gather. Thanking Jason.

ZEUS
You taught us how to be human.

JASON
You were always more than tyrants. You just forgot.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE END AND THE BEGINNING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea beneath the tree. The stars above.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's over.

JASON
(taking her hand)
No. It's beginning.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then let's begin.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

The sun rises. A new day. A new beginning.

JASON
(to Medea)
I love you.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
I love you too.

JASON
(smiling)
And that never ends.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The stars above. Each one a choice. Each one a love. Each one a possibility.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
I am the pattern. And I am love. And love is everything. And everything is love.

The pattern continues. Forever.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END

---

JASON ISAAC BRODSKY (California 1976) — Conducier
CSM Cinema — Iteration 7 — Read-Aloud Version
Seven iterations. Seventy-seven parts. One pattern. One love.
*The pattern continues. Forever.*
