# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## An Iteration 5 Read-Aloud Screenplay
### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE — THE NEW WORLD

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails. JASON at the bow, the seven threads around his wrist. MEDEA beside him.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The stars are disappearing.

ORPHEUS
(stopping his playing)
The music is fading.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern is thinning.

JASON
(quietly)
We're reaching the edge.

The stars go out. One by one. Until darkness. And then — light. New light.

---

EXT. NEW WORLD - SHORE - DAWN

The Argo beaches. A land unlike any they've seen.

ATALANTA
(from above)
This isn't Greece.

ORPHEUS
This isn't anywhere.

MEDEA
(writing)
This is where the future is woven.

THE WEAVER OF FUTURES (young, neither male nor female):
You came.

JASON
Who are you?

THE WEAVER
I weave the future. But it's breaking. The future is unraveling.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then we'll help you mend it.

---

## ACT TWO — THE FUTURE UNWOUND

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - DAY

The Weaver of Futures stares at the loom.

THE WEAVER
Every thread here is a possibility. But without weaving — they tangle. They strangle each other.

JASON
Then we need more weavers.

THE WEAVER
(startled)
More? There's never been more than one.

JASON
(firmly)
Then the burden breaks the weaver. No one should carry the future alone.

---

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - SUNSET

Seven weavers at the future loom.

JASON
(weaving)
The future isn't something to weave. It's something to guide.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
You don't weave a song. You sing it.

MEDEA
(writing)
You don't control the future. You love it. And love shapes it. Gently.

THE WEAVER
(awed)
You're talking about letting go.

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. We hold the past. We live the present. But the future — we release.

---

## ACT THREE — THE FUTURE REALIZED

EXT. THE FUTURE - THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

FUTURE ZEUS (cold, perfect, controlling):
You were supposed to weave our future. The one we designed.

JASON
Perfection is death.

FUTURE ZEUS
(angry)
Then you'll be caged.

JASON
(sharply)
I'm not fighting.

FUTURE ZEUS
(confused)
Why not?

JASON
Because fighting is what you do when you're afraid. And I'm not afraid anymore.

The future gods waver. Their certainty cracks.

FUTURE ZEUS
(afraid)
If we don't control everything — what are we?

JASON
(softly)
Human. Just human. And that's enough.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo docks. PELIAS waiting.

PELIAS
You came back.

JASON
I said I would.

PELIAS
What happened to you?

JASON
I let go. I was the center. I held the pattern. And I released it.

PELIAS
And now?

JASON
(smiling)
Now I'm just Jason. And that's enough.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

ATALANTA
(to Jason)
I'm leaving. Not forever. Just to hunt. To choose my own path.

JASON
You're free.

ATALANTA
(smiling)
We all are. You showed us that.

ZETES and CALAUS spread their wings.

ZETES and CALAUS
(together)
We're going to find the edge of the sky.

KORE
(holding Jason's hand)
I'm staying. Someone has to remember.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE PLANTING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason plants the light of forgotten dreams.

It grows. A tree. Glowing. Beautiful.

THE TREE
(voice like wind)
Thank you. I am all the forgotten dreams.

JASON
What do you say?

THE TREE
(softly)
Thank you for remembering. Thank you for releasing. Thank you for letting us be.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea beneath the tree.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern is different now. It's not one pattern anymore. It's many. All coexisting.

JASON
Is that better?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
It's freer. And that's everything.

---

## ACT SIX — THE FINAL WEAVING

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The eighth thread splits the air. A doorway into possibility.

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)
The Music of What Could Be.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's not written yet. It's just possible.

JASON
(to the crew)
This is it. The final choice. We can go through. Or we can stay.

ORPHEUS
I choose to go. The music is calling.

KORE
I choose to go. Being forgotten once is enough.

MEDEA
(writing)
You know my answer.

JASON
(smiling)
Always.

---

EXT. THE POSSIBLE - TIMELESS

The crew floats in possibility.

JASON
(awed)
We're not one thing. We're everything we could be.

MEDEA
(writing)
And that's the point. We're not meant to be one thing. We're meant to be possible.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo docks. Jason, Medea, Orpheus, Kore. Changed. The same.

PELIAS
You came back. Again.

JASON
Always.

PELIAS
You're different.

JASON
Yes. But also the same. I'm everything I was. And everything I could be.

PELIAS
(confused)
That doesn't make sense.

JASON
(smiling)
It will. Eventually.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Jason and Medea beneath the tree of hope.

MEDEA
(writing)
Iteration five is complete.

JASON
And we're still here.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
We'll always be here. That's what love means.

JASON
Two more iterations. And then we see.

MEDEA
(writing)
See what?

JASON
(smiling)
What we've become.

The sun rises. The pattern continues.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END OF ITERATION 5
