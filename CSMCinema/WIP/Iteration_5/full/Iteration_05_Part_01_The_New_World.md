# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 01: THE NEW WORLD
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 01 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE MESSAGE

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - DAWN

Jason and Medea at home. Peaceful. The seven threads quiet around his wrist.

A STRANGER arrives. Young. Urgent.

STRANGER
Jason of Iolcus. I bring word from the pattern.

JASON
(tired)
Already? We just returned.

STRANGER
(sadly)
The pattern doesn't rest. (beat) There's a new world. Across the western ocean. A land that doesn't exist on any map. And in it — a loom that weaves the future.

MEDEA
(writing)
The future loom?

STRANGER
(nodding)
Where the pattern hasn't been woven yet. Where everything is still — possible.

JASON
(standing)
Then we go.

STRANGER
(shaking his head)
Not yet. (beat) The path is dangerous. The sea is — wrong. The stars don't shine there. The pattern is — thin.

JASON
How thin?

STRANGER
(quietly)
So thin — you might fall through.

---

# PIECE 2: THE PREPARATION

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

The crew gathers. Jason and Medea before them.

JASON
The pattern calls. Again. (beat) A new world. A loom that weaves the future. (beat) But the path is dangerous. The pattern is thin. We might not come back.

Silence.

ATALANTA
(standing)
When do we leave?

JASON
(surprised)
You don't want to think about it?

ATALANTA
(shaking her head)
No. (beat) I've followed you this far. I'm not stopping now.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The music doesn't stop. It continues.

ZETES and CALAUS
(together)
We're coming.

KORE
(standing)
Me too. (beat) The pattern forgot me once. I won't let it forget anyone else.

JASON
(looking at Medea)
And you?

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Always.

---

# PIECE 3: THE FAREWELL

EXT. IOLCUS - PALACE - DAY

Pelias watches them prepare.

PELIAS
Again?

JASON
Again.

PELIAS
(sighing)
You'll never stop, will you?

JASON
(quietly)
Not while there are threads to mend. Not while there are people to remember. Not while— (searching) —not while the pattern needs me.

PELIAS
(serious)
And if it kills you?

JASON
(smiling)
Then I'll die doing what matters.

PELIAS
(taking his hand)
Then come back. (beat) Someone should.

JASON
I will.

PELIAS
You can't promise that.

JASON
I just did.

---

# PIECE 4: THE SAILING

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - SUNSET

The Argo sails. The crew at the oars. The seven threads gleaming.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Open sea. No land for days.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The Music of the New World. (beat) A song that hasn't been written yet.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's what makes it beautiful. (betrayal) And terrifying.

JASON
(quietly)
Everything worth doing is both.

---

# PIECE 5: THE THINNING

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The sea changes. Becoming transparent. The stars dim.

ATALANTA
(from above)
The stars are disappearing.

ORPHEUS
(stopping his playing)
The music is fading.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern is thinning. (betrayal) We're reaching the edge.

KORE
(afraid)
What happens if it disappears?

JASON
(quietly)
We find out.

The stars go out. One by one. Until — darkness. Complete. Absolute.

And then — light. Not stars. Something else. Something new.

---

# PIECE 6: THE NEW WORLD

EXT. NEW WORLD - SHORE - DAWN

The Argo beaches. A land unlike any they've seen. The trees glow faintly. The sand is white. The air tastes like possibility.

ATALANTA
(from above)
This isn't Greece.

ORPHEUS
(playing softly)
This isn't anywhere.

MEDEA
(writing)
This is where the future is woven. (betrayal) Where everything is still possible.

JASON
(looking around)
It's beautiful.

KORE
(touching a glowing tree)
It's alive.

A VOICE from the forest:

VOICE
You came.

---

# PIECE 7: THE WEAVER OF FUTURES

EXT. NEW WORLD - FOREST - CONTINUOUS

A figure emerges. Young. Neither male nor female. Eyes that see everything and nothing.

THE WEAVER OF FUTURES
Jason. Medea. (betrayal) I've been waiting.

JASON
Who are you?

THE WEAVER
(smiling)
I weave the future. What hasn't happened yet. What might happen. What— (searching) —what could be.

MEDEA
(writing)
The future loom?

THE WEAVER
(nodding)
Yes. (betrayal) But it's breaking. (betrayal) The future is — unraveling. And when the future unravels— (stopping) —everything unravels.

JASON
Why?

THE WEAVER
(sadly)
Because the pattern forgot to weave it. (betrayal) The past was woven. The present is woven. But the future— (searching) —the future was left undone.

---

# PIECE 8: THE UNRAVELING

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - DAY

The future loom. Vast. Incomplete. Threads hanging loose. Unwoven.

THE WEAVER
Every thread here is a possibility. A choice that hasn't been made yet. A future that hasn't happened. (betrayal) But without weaving — they tangle. They knot. They— (searching) —they strangle each other.

JASON
(showing his threads)
Can these help?

THE WEAVER
(awed)
Seven threads. Past. Present. Sea. Sky. Underworld. Earth. Center. (betrayal) Yes. They can help. (betrayal) But you'll have to give them up.

MEDEA
(writing, afraid)
Give them up?

THE WEAVER
(quietly)
To weave the future — you need to release the past. (betrayal) The seven threads anchor you to what was. To weave what could be— (searching) —you have to let go.

---

# PIECE 9: THE CHOICE

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(afraid)
If I give up the threads — I lose the center. I lose the pattern. I lose— (stopping) —I lose everything I've become.

THE WEAVER
(softly)
Yes.

MEDEA
(writing)
Jason. No. (betrayal) We'll find another way.

JASON
(gently)
There is no other way. (betrayal) The future needs weaving. And only the center can weave it.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
But if you give up the threads — you might not come back. The pattern might— (stopping) —the pattern might forget you.

JASON
(taking her hand)
Then you'll remember for me. (betrayal) That's what love does. It remembers.

---

# PIECE 10: THE RELEASE

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - SUNSET

Jason stands before the future loom. The seven threads gleaming.

He unwraps them. One by one. Gold. Silver. Blue. White. Black. Green. And the seventh.

Each thread he releases — he feels it go. A piece of himself. A piece of the pattern. A piece of—

JASON
(whispering)
I was the center. (betrayal) I was the pattern. (betrayal) I was—

The last thread falls.

He's just — Jason. Human. Mortal. Ordinary.

And free.

THE WEAVER
(awed)
You did it. (betrayal) You let go.

JASON
(smiling weakly)
Now what?

THE WEAVER
(smiling back)
Now — you weave.

---

# PIECE 11: THE FUTURE

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - NIGHT

Jason weaves. Not as the center. Not as the pattern. As — himself.

And the future responds. Threads weaving themselves. Possibilities forming. Futures taking shape.

MEDEA
(watching, writing)
He's doing it. (betrayal) He's weaving the future.

ATALANTA
(awed)
Without the threads?

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The threads were never the power. (betrayal) He was.

The loom completes. The future — woven. Whole. Possible.

JASON
(collapsing)
It's done.

MEDEA
(running to him)
Jason!

JASON
(smiling)
I'm okay. (betrayal) Just— (searching) —just tired.

THE WEAVER
(placing something in his hand)
Take this.

A single thread. New. Unknown. Full of possibility.

THE WEAVER (CONT'D)
The eighth thread. (betrayal) Memory of the future. What could be. What might be. What— (searching) —what we choose.

JASON
(taking it)
The eighth thread.

THE WEAVER
(smiling)
The first of a new pattern.
