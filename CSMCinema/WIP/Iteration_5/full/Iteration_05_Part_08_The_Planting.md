# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 08: THE PLANTING
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 08 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE GARDEN

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason and Medea stand before the temple. The light of forgotten dreams in Jason's hands.

MEDEA
(writing)
Here. (betrayal) Where it all began. Where— (searching) —where you first heard Hecate. Where— (searching) —where the pattern first spoke to you.

JASON
(nodding)
It's right.

He plants the light in the earth before the temple.

It grows. Instantly. A tree. Small. Glowing. Beautiful.

And on its branches — lights. Each one a forgotten dream. Each one a possibility. Each one— (searching) —each one remembered.

---

# PIECE 2: THE TREE OF HOPE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

The people gather. Staring. Awed.

CHILD
What is it?

JASON
(kneeling)
Hope. (betrayal) All the futures that never were. All the dreams that— (searching) —that were forgotten. All the possibilities that— (searching) —that were released.

WOMAN
(touching the tree)
It's warm.

MAN
(awed)
I can feel them. The dreams. They're— (searching) —they're alive.

MEDEA
(writing)
They always were. They just needed— (searching) —they needed to be remembered.

---

# PIECE 3: THE FIRST BLOOM

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

A flower blooms on the tree. Golden. Glowing.

And from it — a voice. Soft. Gentle. Full of— (searching) —full of possibility.

THE TREE
(voice like wind)
Thank you.

JASON
(startled)
You speak?

THE TREE
(smiling through voice)
I am all the forgotten dreams. All the released futures. All the— (searching) —all the possibilities. And I— (searching) —I speak for them.

MEDEA
(writing)
What do you say?

THE TREE
(softly)
Thank you for remembering. Thank you for— (searching) —for releasing. Thank you for— (searching) —for letting us be.

---

# PIECE 4: THE GIFT OF THE TREE

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The tree grows taller. Its branches reach. Its roots deepen.

THE TREE
I have a gift. For you. For— (searching) —for all of you.

A seed detaches. Falls. Jason catches it.

THE TREE (CONT'D)
Plant this wherever you go. (betrayal) Wherever hope is needed. Wherever— (searching) —wherever dreams are forgotten. Wherever— (searching) —wherever the future needs— (searching) —needs freeing.

JASON
(taking it)
We will.

THE TREE
(smiling through voice)
I know. (betrayal) That's why I chose you.

---

# PIECE 5: THE SPREADING

EXT. IOLCUS - VARIOUS - DAY (MONTAGE)

The tree's seeds spread. Carried by wind. By birds. By— (searching) —by hope.

They land in fields. In forests. In— (searching) —in hearts.

And they grow. Trees of hope. Across Greece. Across— (searching) —across the world.

Each one remembering. Each one— (searching) —each one freeing. Each one— (searching) —each one hoping.

---

# PIECE 6: THE PEOPLE'S RESPONSE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The people gather. Changed. Hopeful. Free.

OLD MAN
Jason. The trees. They're— (searching) —they're everywhere.

JASON
(nodding)
Hope spreads. That's what— (searching) —that's what it does.

OLD WOMAN
What do we do with it?

JASON
(smiling)
Whatever you want. (betrayal) That's the point. (betrayal) Hope isn't— (searching) —isn't something you're given. It's something you— (searching) —you grow.

---

# PIECE 7: THE PATTERN SHIFTS

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea sit beneath the tree of hope. The stars above.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern is different now.

JASON
(looking at the stars)
How?

MEDEA
(writing)
It's not— (searching) —it's not one pattern anymore. It's— (searching) —it's many. All coexisting. All— (searching) —all possible.

JASON
(quietly)
Is that better?

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
It's freer. (betrayal) And that's— (searching) —that's everything.
