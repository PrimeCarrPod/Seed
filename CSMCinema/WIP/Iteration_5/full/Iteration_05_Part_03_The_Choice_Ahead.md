# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 03: THE CHOICE AHEAD
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 03 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE MORNING

EXT. NEW WORLD - CAMPSITE - DAWN

The crew gathers. The future loom behind them. The Weaver watching.

JASON
The future is— (searching) —is free. We released it. (betrayal) Now we have a choice.

ATALANTA
What choice?

JASON
Stay. Help the Weaver guide the future. Or— (searching) —or go home. Back to Greece. Back to— (searching) —back to our lives.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Those aren't the only choices.

JASON
(surprised)
No?

ORPHEUS
(shaking his head)
We could go forward. (betrayal) Into the future we wove. See what we created. See— (searching) —see what's possible.

MEDEA
(writing)
The future is uncharted. No one's ever— (searching) —no one's ever gone into the future before.

JASON
(quietly)
Then maybe it's time someone did.

---

# PIECE 2: THE DIVISION

EXT. NEW WORLD - CAMPSITE - DAY

The crew debates.

ZETES
We should go home. We've done enough.

CALAUS
Have we? (betrayal) The future is free. But it's also— (searching) —it's also dangerous. Someone should guide it.

ATALANTA
We can't guide the future by standing still. (betrayal) We have to move. Explore. See what's out there.

KORE
I want to see the future. (betrayal) I want to see what the world becomes when— (searching) —when people remember.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The music is calling. Forward. Not back.

MEDEA
(writing)
Whatever we choose— (searching) —we choose together.

JASON
(nodding)
Together. Always.

---

# PIECE 3: THE WEAVER'S WARNING

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - DAY

The Weaver approaches. Serious.

THE WEAVER
If you go into the future— (searching) —you might not come back.

JASON
Why?

THE WEAVER
The future isn't fixed. It changes. Constantly. (betrayal) If you go in— (searching) —you might change with it. You might become— (stopping) —something else.

MEDEA
(writing)
Something else?

THE WEAVER
(afraid)
The future doesn't have a pattern yet. No structure. No— (searching) —no rules. You could become anything. Or— (stopping) —or nothing.

JASON
(quietly)
You're saying we might lose ourselves.

THE WEAVER
(nodding)
Yes.

JASON
(looking at Medea)
Worth it?

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Ask me when we get there.

---

# PIECE 4: THE PREPARATION

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - SUNSET

The crew prepares. Not to leave the new world. To go deeper. Into the future itself.

THE WEAVER
I can open a path. But I can't guarantee— (searching) —I can't guarantee where it leads.

JASON
We don't need guarantees. Just— (searching) —just possibility.

THE WEAVER
(smiling)
Then you're ready.

The Weaver weaves. The air splits. A doorway. Not physical. Temporal. Leading— (searching) —leading forward.

Through it: glimpses. Cities that don't exist. People who haven't been born. Choices that haven't been made.

ATALANTA
(awed)
The future.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Beautiful.

KORE
(afraid)
Terrifying.

JASON
(taking Medea's hand)
Together?

MEDEA
(writing)
Always.

---

# PIECE 5: THE THRESHOLD

EXT. NEW WORLD - TEMPORAL DOORWAY - NIGHT

The crew stands before the doorway. The future beyond.

JASON
(to the Weaver)
Will we see you again?

THE WEAVER
(smiling)
I'm in every future. Every possibility. Every— (searching) —every choice. (betrayal) So yes. You'll see me. In every moment. Every— (searching) —every thread.

JASON
(nodding)
Then goodbye. For now.

THE WEAVER
(shaking his head)
Not goodbye. (betrayal) In the future— (searching) —there are no goodbyes. Only— (searching) —only see you later.

---

# PIECE 6: THE CROSSING

EXT. THE FUTURE - TIMELESS

The crew steps through. The world— (searching) —the world changes.

Not a place. Not a time. Just— (searching) —possibility.

They see: themselves. Older. Younger. Different. The same.

They see: the world. Changed. Unchanged. Better. Worse. Both. Neither.

They see: the pattern. Not woven. Not unwoven. Just— (searching) —becoming.

JASON
(awed)
This is the future.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's not one future. It's— (searching) —it's all of them.

ATALANTA
(tracking)
Which one is real?

ORPHEUS
(playing)
All of them. None of them. (betrayal) The future isn't real until— (searching) —until we choose it.

---

# PIECE 7: THE FIRST FUTURE

EXT. THE FUTURE - POSSIBILITY ONE - DAY

A future where the gods are gone. Where humans rule themselves. Where— (searching) —where the pattern is their own.

KORE
(awed)
No gods?

JASON
(looking around)
No gods. Just— (searching) —just people. Choosing. Living. Being.

MEDEA
(writing)
Is it better?

JASON
(thinking)
Different. Not better. Not worse. Just— (searching) —just different.

ATALANTA
(sharply)
I like it. No gods means— (searching) —no gods means no one telling us what to do.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
But also— (searching) —also no one to guide. No one to— (searching) —to catch us if we fall.

JASON
(quietly)
That's what being human means. Falling. And getting up. And— (searching) —and choosing again.

---

# PIECE 8: THE SECOND FUTURE

EXT. THE FUTURE - POSSIBILITY TWO - NIGHT

A future where the gods never existed. Where the pattern wove itself. Where— (searching) —where everything is different.

MEDEA
(writing)
This is— (searching) —this is strange.

JASON
(looking around)
No Greece. No Colchis. No— (searching) —no us.

ATALANTA
(afraid)
We don't exist here?

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Not yet. Not in this— (searching) —in this possibility.

KORE
(quietly)
Which future is real?

JASON
(thinking)
All of them. Until we choose.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then let's choose wisely.

---

# PIECE 9: THE THIRD FUTURE

EXT. THE FUTURE - POSSIBILITY THREE - DAWN

A future where they never left Iolcus. Where Jason never sought the fleece. Where— (searching) —where everything stayed the same.

JASON
(seeing himself)
There I am. The other me. The one who— (searching) —the one who stayed.

The other Jason sits on a throne. Alone. Powerful. Miserable.

MEDEA
(writing)
He looks sad.

JASON
(quietly)
He is. (betrayal) He has everything he thought he wanted. And— (searching) —and nothing that matters.

ATALANTA
Would you trade? (betrayal) Your quest for— (searching) —for his peace?

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. (betrayal) Peace without purpose is— (searching) —is just slow death.

---

# PIECE 10: THE CHOICE

EXT. THE FUTURE - CENTER - TIMELESS

The crew gathers. The futures swirling around them.

JASON
We've seen three futures. There are— (searching) —there are billions more. But we can't see them all. We have to— (searching) —we have to choose.

MEDEA
(writing)
Choose what?

JASON
Which future to— (searching) —to bring back. Which possibility to make real.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
We can't choose for everyone.

JASON
(shaking his head)
No. But we can choose for— (searching) —for ourselves. And trust others to choose for themselves.

ATALANTA
(sharply)
That's chaos.

JASON
(smiling)
That's freedom.

---

# PIECE 11: THE DECISION

EXT. THE FUTURE - CENTER - CONTINUOUS

JASON
(looking at each of them)
I choose— (searching) —I choose the future where we keep choosing. Where no one decides for everyone. Where— (searching) —where freedom matters more than certainty.

MEDEA
(writing)
I choose the same.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
I choose the music. Wherever it leads.

ATALANTA
(tracking)
I choose the hunt. Whatever I find.

ZETES and CALAUS
(together)
I choose the sky. Forever.

KORE
(standing tall)
I choose remembrance. Always.

JASON
(taking Medea's hand)
Then let's make it real.

They weave. Not controlling. Not forcing. Just— (searching) —just suggesting. Gently. With love.

The future responds. Not one future. All futures. Coexisting. Possible. Free.

The pattern— (searching) —the pattern breathes.
