# PIECE 1: THE JOURNEY NORTH

EXT. NORTHERN SEA - DAY

The Argo sails north. Jason, Medea, Orpheus, Kore, and a small crew.

ATALANTA returned to her hunting. Zetes and Calaus to their flying. But the core remains.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
The Music of Return. (betrayal) It's different from— (searching) —from the Music of Departure.

KORE
(happy)
Because we're different.

MEDEA
(writing)
And so is the— (searching) —the destination.

JASON
(quietly)
We left Kore in the ice. Now we— (searching) —now we return. To see— (searching) —to see what grew.

# PIECE 2: THE HYPERBOREANS

EXT. HYPERBOREA - COAST - DAY

The three Hyperboreans wait. But they're not alone.

Behind them — people. Hundreds. Thousands. The forgotten. The lost. The— (searching) —the ones the pattern let go.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Jason. You returned.

JASON
(awed)
What is this?

HYPERBOREAN TWO
This is what happens when— (searching) —when hope spreads. When the trees bloom. When— (searching) —when people choose.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
The forgotten are— (searching) —are returning. Remembering. Becoming— (searching) —becoming real.

KORE
(running to the forgotten children)
You're alive!

FORGOTTEN CHILD
(hugging her)
We remember. We— (searching) —we remember you.

# PIECE 3: THE FORGOTTEN RETURN

EXT. HYPERBOREA - VILLAGE - DAY

The forgotten walk among the living. Not ghosts. Not memories. Just— (searching) —just people. Real. Whole. Remembered.

OLD WOMAN
(to Jason)
You did this. (betrayal) You freed the future. And— (searching) —and we came back.

JASON
(shaking his head)
I didn't— (searching) —I didn't do anything. I just— (searching) —I just let go.

OLD WOMAN
(smiling)
That's everything.

# PIECE 4: THE HYPERBOREANS' REQUEST

EXT. HYPERBOREA - CAVERN - NIGHT

The three Hyperboreans gather. Serious.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
We have a request.

JASON
What?

HYPERBOREAN TWO
Plant the tree. Here. (betrayal) Where the— (searching) —where the first winter was woven. Where— (searching) —where cold was born.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
Let hope grow— (searching) —grow where once there was only— (searching) —only ice.

JASON
(looking at the seed)
It will change things.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(smiling)
That's the point.

---

# PIECE 5: THE PLANTING

EXT. HYPERBOREA - DEEP ICE - DAY

Jason plants the seed in the deepest ice. Where Kore was once— (searching) —was once frozen.

It grows. A tree of hope. But different. Its bark is ice. Its leaves are snow. Its fruit is— (searching) —is warmth.

MEDEA
(awed, writing)
A tree of hope. Made of— (searching) —of winter. Growing in— (searching) —in ice.

KORE
(touching it)
It's beautiful.

THE TREE
(voice like winter wind)
Thank you. (betrayal) I am hope in— (searching) —in hopeless places. Warmth in— (searching) —in cold places. Life in— (searching) —in death places.

---

# PIECE 6: THE TRANSFORMATION

EXT. HYPERBOREA - VILLAGE - SUNSET

The tree transforms the land. Not destroying the ice. Not— (searching) —not ending winter. Just— (searching) —just adding warmth. Hope. Life.

The Hyperboreans watch. Crying.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
We've been cold so long.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
We forgot what— (searching) —what warmth felt like.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
(softly)
Thank you.

JASON
(quietly)
The pattern provides.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
(shaking his head)
No. (betrayal) You provide. The pattern just— (searching) —just shows the way.

---

# PIECE 7: THE CELEBRATION

EXT. HYPERBOREA - VILLAGE - NIGHT

A celebration. The forgotten and the remembered. Together. Dancing. Singing.— (searching) —Being.

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)
The Music of Return. (betrayal) It's— (searching) —it's the most beautiful thing I've ever— (searching) —ever heard.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Because it's real. (betrayal) Not planned. Not— (searching) —not controlled. Just— (searching) —just people. Choosing. Living.— (searching) —Being.

JASON
(quietly)
This is what the pattern wanted. All along. (betrayal) Not to control. Just to— (searching) —to connect.

---

# PIECE 8: THE DEPARTURE

EXT. HYPERBOREA - SHORE - DAWN

The Argo prepares to sail. The Hyperboreans gather.

HYPERBOREAN ONE
Where will you go?

JASON
(looking at the eighth thread)
Wherever it leads.

HYPERBOREAN TWO
(smiling)
Home?

JASON
(considering)
Maybe. (betrayal) Or maybe— (searching) —maybe somewhere new.

HYPERBOREAN THREE
The pattern isn't finished with you.

JASON
(smiling)
It never is.
