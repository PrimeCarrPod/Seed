# PIECE 1: THE EIGHTH THREAD

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

Jason at the bow. The eighth thread glowing. Pulsing.— (searching) —Calling.

(writing)

# PIECE 2: THE DOORWAY

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAWN

The eighth thread splits the air. A doorway. Not physical. Not temporal. Just— (searching) —possible.

Through it: glimpses. Not of places. Of— (searching) —of choices. Choices not yet made. Futures not yet woven.— (searching) —Possibilities not yet born.

ATALANTA would have seen: herself. Still hunting. Still free. Still— (searching) —still choosing.

ZETES and CALAUS would have seen: the edge of the sky. And beyond it— (searching) —beyond it, everything.

ORPHEUS
(playing, crying)

MEDEA
(writing)

# PIECE 3: THE FINAL CHOICE

EXT. OPEN SEA - DOORWAY - DAY

JASON
(looking at his crew)
This is it. (betrayal) The final choice. (betrayal) We can go through. Into— (searching) —into the unknown. Into the— (searching) —into the future that hasn't been woven.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Or?

JASON
(smiling)
Or we can stay. Here. In the— (searching) —in the present. In the— (searching) —in the world we've built.

KORE
(afraid)
What happens if we go through?

JASON
(quietly)
I don't know. (betrayal) We might— (searching) —we might become something new. We might— (searching) —we might lose ourselves. We might— (searching) —we might find everything.

MEDEA
(writing)
And if we stay?

JASON
(smiling)
We live. We love. We— (searching) —we are.

# PIECE 4: THE DECISION

EXT. OPEN SEA - DOORWAY - SUNSET

JASON
(looking at each of them)

ORPHEUS
(playing)

KORE

MEDEA
(writing)

JASON
(smiling)
Always.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling back)
Always.

# PIECE 6: THE BECOMING

EXT. THE POSSIBLE - TIMELESS

The crew floats. Not in space. Not in time. Just— (searching) —just in possibility.

They see: themselves. Every version. Every choice. Every— (searching) —every possibility.

And they choose. Not one future. Not— (searching) —not one version. But— (searching) —but all of them. Simultaneously. Coexisting. Free.

JASON
(awed)

MEDEA
(writing)

EXT. THE POSSIBLE - BECOMING

The pattern— (searching) —the pattern completes. Not ending. Not— (searching) —not finishing. Just— (searching) —just becoming what it always was. Possibility. Choice.— (searching) —Love.

# PIECE 8: THE RETURN

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo docks. Jason, Medea, Orpheus, Kore. Changed. The same. Both.

(waiting)

JASON
(stepping onto the dock)
Always.

PELIAS
(looking at him)

JASON
(thinking)

JASON
(smiling)
It will. Eventually.
