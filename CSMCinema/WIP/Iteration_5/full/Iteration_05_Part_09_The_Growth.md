Seasons change. The tree of hope grows. Blooms. Seeds. Spreads.

Spring: flowers bloom. Children play beneath the branches.

Summer: the tree tall. Its shade welcome. Its presence— (searching) —its presence comforting.

Autumn: seeds spread. New trees growing. Hope— (searching) —hope multiplying.

# PIECE 3: THE STORY

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - CONTINUOUS

Jason tells the story. The children listen. Medea writes.

JASON
In the beginning— (searching) —in the beginning, there was chaos. And from chaos— (searching) —from chaos came choice. And from choice— (searching) —from choice came pattern.

CHILD
And from pattern?

JASON
(smiling)
From pattern came— (searching) —came everything. The gods. The world. The— (searching) —the seas. The sky. The— (searching) —the underworld. The earth.

CHILD
And us?

JASON
(quietly)
And you. (betrayal) The most important part. (betrayal) Because without— (searching) —without people to choose. Without people to— (searching) —to love. Without people to— (searching) —to be. The pattern is— (searching) —is just threads. Empty. Meaningless.

# PIECE 5: THE RETURN OF ATALANTA

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAY

A ship. Not the Argo. Smaller. Faster. And on it— (searching) —Atalanta.

ATALANTA
(stepping onto the dock)
Jason! Medea!

JASON
(running to her)
You came back!

ATALANTA
(laughing)
I said I would. (beat) And I brought— (searching) —I brought stories.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Stories?

ATALANTA
(excited)
The world is changing. The trees of hope— (searching) —they're everywhere. And people— (searching) —people are choosing. Really choosing.

MEDEA
(writing)
What kind of choosing?

ATALANTA
(smiling)
Freedom. Love.— (searching) —Hope. Everything you— (searching) —everything you wove. It's— (searching) —it's real.
