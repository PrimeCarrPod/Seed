# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 07: THE HOMECOMING
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 07 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay

# PIECE 1: THE ARRIVAL

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo docks. The harbor full. The people gathered.

But different from before. Not afraid. Not silent.— (searching) —hopeful.

PELIAS
(waiting)
You came back.

JASON
(stepping onto the dock)
I said I would.

PELIAS
(smiling)
I know. But— (searching) —but I've learned not to count on promises. (beat) What happened to you?

JASON
(thinking)
I let go. (betrayal) I was the center. I held the pattern. And I— (searching) —I released it.

PELIAS
(quietly)
And now?

JASON
(smiling)
Now I'm just— (searching) —just Jason. And that's— (searching) —that's enough.

# PIECE 2: THE PEOPLE

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The people gather. Not for a king. For— (searching) —for one of them.

WOMAN
Jason. We heard— (searching) —we heard you freed the future.

JASON
(shaking his head)
I didn't free it. I just— (searching) —I just stopped holding it. It was always— (searching) —it was always free.

MAN
What does that mean? For us?

JASON
(thinking)
It means— (searching) —it means your choices matter. Not because the gods decide. Not because— (searching) —not because the pattern demands. But because— (searching) —but because you choose.

CHILD
(awed)
We can choose anything?

JASON
(kneeling)
Anything. (betrayal) And that's— (searching) —that's terrifying. And beautiful. And— (searching) —and everything.

---

# PIECE 3: THE CHANGED CREW

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - NIGHT

The crew gathers. Quiet. Changed.

ATALANTA
(to Jason)
I'm leaving.

JASON
(startled)
What?

ATALANTA
(smiling)
Not forever. Just— (searching) —just to hunt. To see what's out there. To— (searching) —to choose my own path.

JASON
(understanding)
You're free.

ATALANTA
(nodding)
We all are. (betrayal) You showed us that. (betrayal) Now— (searching) —now I want to see what I can do with it.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
I'm staying. (betrayal) For now. The music is— (searching) —the music is here.

ZETES and CALAUS
(together)
We're going to fly. Really fly. Beyond— (searching) —beyond anything we've known.

KORE
(holding Jason's hand)
I'm staying too. (betrayal) Someone has to— (searching) —someone has to remember.

---

# PIECE 4: THE FAREWELL

EXT. IOLCUS - HARBOR - DAWN

Atalanta prepares to leave. The crew sees her off.

ATALANTA
(to Jason)
Thank you.

JASON
For what?

ATALANTA
(smiling)
For showing me that— (searching) —that the hunt isn't about the prey. It's about— (searching) —it's about the choosing. The freedom. The— (searching) —the being.

JASON
(hugging her)
Come back.

ATALANTA
(laughing)
I always do.

She walks away. Into the forest. Into— (searching) —into her own future.

---

# PIECE 5: THE ZETES AND CALAUS

EXT. IOLCUS - SKY - DAY

Zetes and Calaus spread their wings. Real wings now. Grown from— (searching) —from choice. From freedom. From— (searching) —from love.

ZETES
(to Jason)
We're going to find the edge of the sky.

CALAUS
And then— (searching) —and then we're going past it.

JASON
(awed)
Be safe.

ZETES and CALAUS
(together, laughing)
We'll be free!

They fly. Higher. Higher. Until— (searching) —until they're specks. And then— (searching) —and then they're gone.

# PIECE 6: THE QUIET

EXT. IOLCUS - JASON'S HOUSE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea alone. The first time in— (searching) —in so long.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's quiet.

JASON
(nodding)
Too quiet.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
We can leave again. If you want. If the— (searching) —if the pattern calls.

JASON
(thinking)
The pattern doesn't call anymore. (betrayal) I'm not— (searching) —I'm not the center. I'm just— (searching) —just me.

MEDEA
(writing)
(smiling)
Then what do you want?

JASON
(taking her hand)
This. (betrayal) You. Home. (betrayal) Quiet.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's enough?

JASON
(smiling)
That's everything.

# PIECE 7: THE NEXT MORNING

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

Jason and Medea walk through the market. Not as heroes. Not as— (searching) —as legends. Just as— (searching) —as people.

OLD WOMAN
Jason. Will you stay?

JASON
(smiling)
For now.

OLD WOMAN
And then?

JASON
(looking at the eighth thread)
And then— (searching) —and then we'll see. (betrayal) The future is— (searching) —the future is free. And so am I.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
And so are we.
