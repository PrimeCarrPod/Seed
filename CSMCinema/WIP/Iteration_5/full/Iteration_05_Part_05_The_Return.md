# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 05: THE RETURN
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 05 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE WAY BACK

EXT. THE FUTURE - CENTER - DAWN

The crew prepares to return. The Weaver of Futures approaches.

THE WEAVER
You're leaving.

JASON
We have to. (betrayal) The past needs us. The present needs us. (betrayal) And— (searching) —and we need them.

THE WEAVER
(sadly)
The future will miss you.

JASON
(smiling)
The future will be fine. (betrayal) It's free now. It doesn't need— (searching) —it doesn't need us to hold it.

THE WEAVER
(nodding)
But it will remember you. (betrayal) Every choice you made. Every— (searching) —every thread you wove.

MEDEA
(writing)
That's all we can ask.

---

# PIECE 2: THE DOORWAY HOME

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - DAY

The Weaver opens a doorway. Back to the past. Back to— (searching) —back to home.

Through it: glimpses of Greece. Iolcus. The Argo in harbor. Pelias waiting.

ATALANTA
(awed)
Home.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
It looks the same.

KORE
(quietly)
Will we?

JASON
(looking at his crew)
No. (betrayal) We're different. We've seen the future. We've— (searching) —we've become something new.

MEDEA
(writing)
Better?

JASON
(smiling)
Different. And that's— (searching) —that's enough.

---

# PIECE 3: THE CROSSING BACK

EXT. THE FUTURE - THRESHOLD - SUNSET

The crew stands before the doorway. The future behind them. The past ahead.

JASON
(to the Weaver)
Thank you. For— (searching) —for showing us. For trusting us. For— (searching) —for letting go.

THE WEAVER
(smiling)
Thank you for teaching me. (betrayal) I spent eons trying to control the future. (betrayal) You showed me— (searching) —you showed me how to live it.

JASON
(taking Medea's hand)
Together?

MEDEA
(writing)
Always.

They step through. The doorway closes. The future— (searching) —the future breathes.

---

# PIECE 4: THE NEW WORLD AGAIN

EXT. NEW WORLD - SHORE - NIGHT

The crew emerges. Not in Greece. Still in the new world.

ATALANTA
(confused)
This isn't home.

JASON
(looking around)
No. (betrayal) But it's— (searching) —it's where we need to be. For now.

MEDEA
(writing)
The pattern isn't finished with us.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
It never is.

---

# PIECE 5: THE MESSAGE

EXT. NEW WORLD - CAMPSITE - DAWN

A STRANGER waits. Old. Familiar.

STRANGER
Jason. Medea. (betrayal) You've returned.

JASON
Do I know you?

STRANGER
(smiling)
Not yet. But you will. (betrayal) I'm from the future you wove. The one where— (searching) —the one where you succeed.

MEDEA
(writing)
Succeed at what?

STRANGER
(seriously)
At becoming what you're meant to be. (betrayal) Not heroes. Not gods. Not— (searching) —not centers. Just— (searching) —just human. Fully. Completely. Freely.

JASON
(quietly)
And what are we meant to be?

STRANGER
(smiling)
You'll see. (betrayal) Soon.

---

# PIECE 6: THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

EXT. NEW WORLD - SHORE - DAY

The Argo prepares to sail. Not home yet. Somewhere else. Somewhere— (searching) —somewhere new.

ATALANTA
(from above)
Where to?

JASON
(looking at the eighth thread)
Wherever this leads.

MEDEA
(writing)
The eighth thread. Memory of the future.

JASON
(nodding)
It's calling. (betrayal) Not home. Not yet. (betrayal) Somewhere— (searching) —somewhere we need to be.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
Then let's find it.
