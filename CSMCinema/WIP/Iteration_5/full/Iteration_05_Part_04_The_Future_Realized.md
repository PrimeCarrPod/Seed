# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 04: THE FUTURE REALIZED
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 04 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE WEAVING

EXT. THE FUTURE - CENTER - TIMELESS

Seven weavers weave the future. Not one future. All futures. Possibilities coexisting.

JASON
(weaving)
I see it. The future where— (searching) —where the pattern holds. Where people remember. Where— (searching) —where love matters.

MEDEA
(weaving, writing)
I see it too. The future where no one is forgotten. Where— (searching) —where everyone matters.

ORPHEUS
(weaving, playing)
I hear it. The music of the future. Not one song. All songs. (betrayal) Together.

ATALANTA
(weaving, tracking)
I hunt it. The future where freedom is— (searching) —is the rule. Not the exception.

The futures weave together. Not competing. Complementing. Different melodies in the same symphony.

---

# PIECE 2: THE RESISTANCE

EXT. THE FUTURE - DARKNESS - CONTINUOUS

A presence. Not the Weaver. Something else. Something— (searching) —something that doesn't want the future to be free.

THE RESISTANCE (voice like breaking threads):
You're destroying everything.

JASON
(stopping)
Who are you?

THE RESISTANCE
I am the future that was. The one that was planned. The one that was— (searching) —the one that was controlled. (betrayal) And you're ruining it.

MEDEA
(writing)
Controlled by whom?

THE RESISTANCE
By the ones who know better. By the ones who— (searching) —by the ones who see the big picture. By— (stopping) —by the gods.

---

# PIECE 3: THE GODS OF THE FUTURE

EXT. THE FUTURE - THRONE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

The crew sees: the future gods. Not Zeus. Not Poseidon. New gods. Gods of— (searching) —of control. Of order. Of— (searching) —of certainty.

FUTURE ZEUS (cold, calculating, perfect):
You were supposed to weave our future. The one we designed. The one we— (searching) —the one we perfected.

JASON
(sharply)
Perfection is death.

FUTURE ZEUS
(angry)
Perfection is peace. Perfection is— (searching) —perfection is safety.

MEDEA
(writing)
Perfection is a cage.

FUTURE ZEUS
(drawing power)
Then you'll be caged.

---

# PIECE 4: THE BATTLE

EXT. THE FUTURE - BATTLEFIELD - CONTINUOUS

The future gods attack. Not with lightning. With— (searching) —with inevitability. With the weight of a future that has no choice.

JASON
(holding the eighth thread)
We need to— (searching) —we need to show them.

MEDEA
(writing)
Show them what?

JASON
That the future isn't— (searching) —isn't something to control. It's something to— (searching) —to live.

He weaves. Not fighting. Not resisting. Just— (searching) —just being. Being human. Being free. Being— (searching) —being alive.

The future gods hesitate.

FUTURE ZEUS
(confused)
You're not fighting.

JASON
(smiling)
No.

FUTURE ZEUS
(angry)
Why not?

JASON
Because fighting is— (searching) —fighting is what you do when you're afraid. And I'm not— (searching) —I'm not afraid anymore.

---

# PIECE 5: THE TRANSFORMATION

EXT. THE FUTURE - BATTLEFIELD - CONTINUOUS

The future gods waver. Their certainty— (searching) —their certainty cracks.

FUTURE ZEUS
(afraid)
If we're not perfect— (searching) —if we don't control everything— (stopping) —what are we?

JASON
(softly)
Human. (betrayal) Just human. (betrayal) And that's— (searching) —that's enough.

The future gods change. Their cold perfection— (searching) —their cold perfection warms. They become— (searching) —they become themselves. Not gods of control. Just— (searching) —just beings. Choosing. Living. Being.

FUTURE ZEUS
(awed)
We forgot. (betrayal) We forgot what it was like. To— (searching) —to choose. To not know. To— (searching) —to be free.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's not too late.

FUTURE ZEUS
(smiling)
No. (betrayal) It's not.

---

# PIECE 6: THE NEW FUTURE

EXT. THE FUTURE - DAWN - CONTINUOUS

The future changes. Not one future. All futures. Not controlled. Not perfected. Just— (searching) —just free.

The crew stands together. Exhausted. Changed. Whole.

ATALANTA
(awed)
We did it.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
We showed them.

KORE
(holding Jason's hand)
The future is free.

JASON
(quietly)
The future is— (searching) —the future is ours. Not to control. To— (searching) —to live.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Together.
