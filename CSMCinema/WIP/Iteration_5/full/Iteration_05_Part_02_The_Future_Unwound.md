# CSM CINEMA — ITERATION 5 — PART 02: THE FUTURE UNWOUND
## Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier
**Iteration:** 5 of 7  
**Part:** 02 of 11  
**Format:** Standard Screenplay  

---

# PIECE 1: THE MORNING AFTER

EXT. NEW WORLD - CAMPSITE - DAWN

Jason wakes. Medea beside him. The eighth thread glowing softly around his wrist.

MEDEA
(writing)
How do you feel?

JASON
(thinking)
Different. Lighter. (beat) I'm not the center anymore.

MEDEA
(writing)
How does that feel?

JASON
(afraid)
Terrifying. (beat) And liberating.

ATALANTA
(approaching)
The Weaver wants to see you.

JASON
(standing)
Then let's see what the future holds.

---

# PIECE 2: THE UNRAVELING

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - DAY

The Weaver of Futures stares at the loom. Something's wrong.

THE WEAVER
(afraid)
It's unraveling again.

JASON
I thought I wove it.

THE WEAVER
You wove one future. But there are — billions. Trillions. Every choice creates new possibilities. Every possibility creates new— (searching) —new futures.

MEDEA
(writing)
And they're all unraveling?

THE WEAVER
(shaking his head)
Not unraveling. Multiplying. Faster than I can weave them. Faster than anyone can— (stopping) —the future is becoming chaos.

JASON
Then we need more weavers.

THE WEAVER
(startled)
More? There's never been more than one. The burden is too— (searching) —too great.

JASON
(sharply)
Then the burden breaks the weaver. (beat) No one should carry the future alone.

---

# PIECE 3: THE CREW CHOOSES

EXT. NEW WORLD - CAMPSITE - DAY

Jason stands before the crew.

JASON
The future is unraveling. The Weaver can't hold it alone. (beat) I need help. (beat) But the burden is — heavy. Heavier than the past. Heavier than— (searching) —heavier than anything we've faced.

Silence.

ORPHEUS
(standing)
I'll help. (beat) The music has always been about the future. About what comes next. (beat) This is what I was meant for.

ATALANTA
(standing)
I'll help. (betrayal) I've spent my life hunting what is. Maybe it's time to hunt what could be.

ZETES and CALAUS
(together)
We'll help. (betrayal) Together.

KORE
(standing)
I'll help. (betrayal) The future forgot me once. I won't let it forget anyone again.

MEDEA
(writing)
You know I'm in.

JASON
(quietly)
I know. (beat) But Medea — the burden might— (searching) —it might change you.

MEDEA
(writing, firmly)
Everything changes me. That's what living is.

---

# PIECE 4: THE WEAVING BEGINS

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - SUNSET

Seven weavers at the future loom. Each touching a thread. Each weaving a different future.

Jason weaves futures of peace. Futures where the gods don't war. Futures where people remember.

Medea weaves futures of connection. Futures where no one is forgotten. Futures where love wins.

Orpheus weaves futures of music. Futures where songs heal. Futures where sound becomes— (searching) —becomes meaning.

Atalanta weaves futures of freedom. Futures where people choose. Futures where no one is caged.

Zetes and Calaus weave futures of flight. Futures where humans soar. Futures where the sky is home.

Kore weaves futures of remembrance. Futures where children are never forgotten. Futures where— (searching) —where everyone matters.

The futures multiply. Possibilities expanding. The future— (searching) —the future breathes.

---

# PIECE 5: THE COST

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - NIGHT

The weavers collapse. Exhausted. Changed.

JASON
(quietly)
It's too much. (betrayal) The futures keep multiplying. Every choice we weave creates ten more. Every possibility creates— (searching) —creates infinite others.

THE WEAVER
(tired)
That's what I've been trying to tell you. (betrayal) The future isn't a tapestry. It's an ocean. You can't weave an ocean.

MEDEA
(writing)
Then what do we do?

THE WEAVER
(sadly)
You let it flow. (betrayal) You can't control the future. You can only— (searching) —only suggest it.

---

# PIECE 6: THE NEW WAY

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - DAWN

JASON
(thinking)
We've been thinking about this wrong. (betrayal) The future isn't something to weave. It's something to— (searching) —to guide. To suggest. To—

ORPHEUS
(playing)
To sing. (betrayal) You don't weave a song. You sing it. And it becomes what it becomes.

ATALANTA
(tracking)
You don't hunt the future. You track it. You follow where it leads.

MEDEA
(writing)
You don't control the future. You— (searching) —you love it. And love shapes it. Gently. Without force.

THE WEAVER
(awed)
You're talking about— (searching) —about letting go.

JASON
(smiling)
Yes. (betrayal) We hold the past. We live the present. But the future— (searching) —the future we release.

---

# PIECE 7: THE RELEASE

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - SUNSET

The seven weavers release their threads. Not abandoning — releasing. Letting the future flow.

The loom shimmers. The futures multiply. Not chaos— (searching) —not anymore. Possibility. Beautiful. Terrible. Free.

THE WEAVER
(crying)
I've been trying to control the future for eons. (betrayal) And you— (searching) —you let it go. And it's more beautiful than anything I ever wove.

JASON
(quietly)
That's the secret. (betrayal) Control is death. Release is life.
