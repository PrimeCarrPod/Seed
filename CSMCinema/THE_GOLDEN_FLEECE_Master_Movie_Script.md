# THE GOLDEN FLEECE
## A Master Movie Script
### Based on the Complete CSMCinema — 7 Iterations, 77 Parts
#### Jason Isaac Brodsky (California 1976) — Conducier

---

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE — THE CALL

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAWN

The market is empty. Fish scales glisten. Smoke rises from brick ovens. PELIAS (50s, paranoid king) watches from his balcony.

ADVISOR
He comes, my lord. One sandal. Just as the oracle said.

PELIAS
Let him have it. I have an answer for prophecy.

JASON (20, golden hair, one sandal, river mud on his ankle) enters the square.

JASON
Uncle. I've come for my father's throne.

PELIAS
Your father died in exile. The throne requires proof. The Golden Fleece. Retrieve it from Colchis.

JASON
(without hesitation)
I accept.

PELIAS
Then go with the gods, nephew.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - NIGHT

Jason sleeps. HECATE (three faces, three torches) appears.

HECATE
Boy who carried me. I've come to collect.

JASON
Collect what? I carried you across. The debt is paid.

HECATE
That was the down payment. The fleece you seek is mine. The grove is mine. The priestess is mine. When you reach Colchis, you'll need me. Remember the river. Remember the debt.

JASON
What priestess?

HECATE
Her name is Medea. She will love you. She will destroy you. She will save you. She will betray her father for you. And you will break her heart.

JASON
Why tell me this?

HECATE
Because knowing doesn't change anything.

The light fades. Hecate fades. Jason lies alone.

---

EXT. MOUNT PELION - DAY

ARGUS (40s, shipwright) kneels at an ancient pine. HERACLES (30s, demigod) ascends.

HERACLES
The trees told me the ship needs a storm that knows when to calm.

ARGUS
The ship needs no anchor. It needs a storm.

HERACLES
Then I am that storm.

ORPHEUS (20s, bard) ascends playing his lyre. The trees lean. Argus pauses.

ORPHEUS
The ocean taught me a song. It said the ship would need a voice when the wind stops.

CASTOR AND POLLUX (20s, twins) ascend in unison.

CASTOR/POLLUX
We heard the ship call twice. Once for each of us. We are both. We are one. We volunteer.

ATALANTA (20s, hunter) ascends alone.

ATALANTA
I hunt my own quarry. The fleece is mine to chase.

---

EXT. COLCHIS HARBOR - DAWN

The Argo arrives. AETES (60s, king) receives them.

AETES
Greeks. In my hall. Why?

JASON
For the Golden Fleece.

AETES
Yoke my fire-breathing bulls. Plow the field. Sow the dragon's teeth. Kill the warriors. Do this — and we'll discuss the fleece.

JASON
When?

AETES
Tomorrow. Dawn.

---

INT. HECATE'S TEMPLE - NIGHT

MEDEA (19, priestess, black hair, gold eyes) prays. Jason enters.

MEDEA
You shouldn't be here.

JASON
You gave me the vial.

MEDEA
I gave you medicine. For the bulls tomorrow.

JASON
You gave me more than that. What's your name?

MEDEA
Medea. If I help you — I betray my father. My kingdom. Everything.

JASON
Then don't help me.

MEDEA
(startled)
You'd fail. You'd die.

JASON
Probably. Likely. But it's your choice. Not the goddess's. Not the pattern's. Yours.

MEDEA
(quietly)
I choose to help you. I choose to betray my father. I choose... you.

---

EXT. PLAINS OF AETES - DAY

Two FIRE-BREATHING BULLS charge. Jason walks forward. Empty-handed. The bulls breathe fire. He doesn't burn. He touches their muzzles. They kneel.

He yokes them. Plows. Sows the teeth. Warriors spring from earth. He throws a stone — they fight each other. One remains. Jason kills it.

Silence. Aetes stands.

AETES
My daughter helped him.

---

EXT. GROVE OF ARES - NIGHT

Medea and Jason before the sleeping dragon. The fleece glows.

MEDEA
The draught will hold for one hour. Take the fleece. Find me. Run.

JASON
We.

MEDEA
(smiling)
We.

They take the fleece. The dragon wakes. They run.

---

## ACT TWO — THE ROAD OF TRIALS

EXT. OPEN SEA - DAY

ABSYRTUS (20s, Medea's brother) pursues with three ships.

MEDEA
He'll never stop.

JASON
Then we make him choose.

Jason boards Absyrtus' ship. Presses blade to his throat.

JASON (CONT'D)
Call off your ships.

ABSYRTUS
You'll never leave this deck alive.

JASON
Neither will you.

Medea throws the fleece. Absyrtus turns. Jason shoves him. Grabs the fleece. Jumps.

---

EXT. PHAEACIAN ISLAND - NIGHT

QUEEN ARETE (60s, wise) speaks with Medea.

ARETE
You chose between your blood and your heart.

ARETE (CONT'D)
I left my family for love. Forty years ago. Some nights I still wonder if I chose right.

MEDEA
How do you live with the wondering?

ARETE
You wake up each morning. And you choose again. Every day. Until one day — the wondering stops.

---

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

POSEIDON appears on the waves.

POSEIDON
You want my sea calm? Give me something in return.

MEDEA
What do you want?

POSEIDON
Your voice. For one year.

JASON
Don't!

MEDEA
(to Poseidon)
I accept.

Poseidon touches her throat. She opens her mouth — nothing. The storm stops.

---

EXT. CRETE - DAY

ATALANTA
Talos. The bronze bronze giant. He throws rocks at any ship.

MEDEA
(writing)
He has a vein in his ankle. Sealed with a bronze nail. Remove it — he dies.

JASON
How do you know this?

MEDEA
Hecate taught me.

Jason runs. Dodges boulders. Pulls the nail. Talos falls.

---

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

Jason returns. Pelias waits.

PELIAS
You're alive.

JASON
Disappointed?

PELIAS
Surprised. The fleece. You actually retrieved it.

JASON
Before we discuss the throne. I want to talk about my father.

PELIAS
Your father died in exile.

JASON
My father was murdered. By you.

The warriors tense. Pelias dismisses the guards.

PELIAS
What do you want?

JASON
Justice.

PELIAS
Your father was weak. The kingdom needed strength. I took it. That's all.

JASON
You stole his throne. Sent him to die. Stole my childhood.

PELIAS
And gave you something better. A quest. A purpose. A story.

JASON
(quietly)
I choose... neither.

PELIAS
Neither?

JASON
The throne is yours. Keep it. Killing you won't bring him back.

PELIAS
(long pause)
You're better than me.

JASON
I'm just tired.

---

## ACT THREE — THE SEVEN LOOMS

EXT. UNDERWATER CAVERN - NIGHT

POSEIDON emerges from the darkness.

POSEIDON
Touch the loom. But know — it will show you what you're afraid of.

Jason touches the loom. Sees: the world before the gods. Chaos. Pure. Undifferentiated.

THE PATTERN (V.O.)
Before the gods. Before time. Before everything. There was choice. And choice created pattern. And pattern created everything.

JASON
Why show me this?

THE PATTERN
Because you need to understand. The looms don't create. They reveal.

---

EXT. THE SKY - NIGHT

ZEUS stands before the sky loom.

ZEUS
You want to understand the pattern so you can become it.

JASON
(quietly)
Yes.

ZEUS
Face what you fear most. Accept it. And you pass.

JASON faces himself — successful, powerful, king of everything, completely alone.

KING JASON
You see? I won. Everything I wanted.

JASON
At what cost?

KING JASON
Does it matter? I won.

JASON
Look at you. You're alone. No crew. No Medea. No friends. Just subjects. That's not winning. That's losing everything that matters.

The king fades.

ZEUS
You passed. You chose love over power.

---

INT. HADES' PALACE - NIGHT

HADES sits on a throne of bone. PERSEPHONE beside him.

HADES
You've taken much. What have you given?

JASON
We gave ourselves. Our safety. Our—

HADES
Wrong. You've taken. But you haven't given. Not really.

PERSEPHONE
(softly)
What will you give?

JASON
(quietly)
My certainty.

HADES
You passed. Because you were willing to be weak.

---

INT. GAIA'S CAVERN - NIGHT

GAIA tends the earth loom.

GAIA
Before me — there was chaos. And I chose. The first choice. To weave order from chaos. And that choice created everything.

JASON
Why?

GAIA
Because I was lonely. Chaos is free — but it's alone.

MEDEA
(writing)
And the suffering?

GAIA
The cost. Every thread I wove — excluded every other thread.

---

EXT. THE VOID - TIMELESS

THE FIRST WEAVER (ageless, the one who made the first choice):

THE FIRST WEAVER
The seventh thread makes you the center. The consciousness. The weaver of weavers.

JASON
And if I refuse?

THE FIRST WEAVER
The pattern continues. Without a center. Beautiful. Unfinished.

MEDEA
(writing)
Jason. No.

JASON
I choose both. I choose to become the center. But I choose to remember. I choose to hold the pattern — but I choose to hold you too.

THE FIRST WEAVER
That's not possible. The center can't—

JASON
Watch me.

He takes the seven threads. Light. Blinding. He screams — not in pain, in becoming.

JASON (CONT'D)
(quietly)
I am the pattern. And I am Jason. Both. Always both.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE FUTURE UNWOUND

EXT. NEW WORLD - SHORE - DAY

The Argo beaches. A land unlike any they've seen.

THE WEAVER OF FUTURES (young, neither male nor female):
The future is unraveling. Every choice creates new possibilities. Every possibility creates new futures. Faster than I can weave them.

JASON
Then we need more weavers.

THE WEAVER
More? There's never been more than one.

JASON
Then the burden breaks the weaver. No one should carry the future alone.

---

EXT. NEW WORLD - LOOM - SUNSET

Seven weavers at the future loom.

JASON
The future isn't something to weave. It's something to guide.

ORPHEUS
(playing)
You don't weave a song. You sing it.

MEDEA
(writing)
You don't control the future. You love it. And love shapes it. Gently.

They release the future. Not abandoning — releasing. Letting it flow.

---

EXT. THE FUTURE - THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

FUTURE ZEUS (cold, perfect, controlling):
You were supposed to weave our future. The one we designed.

JASON
Perfection is death.

FUTURE ZEUS
Then you'll be caged.

JASON
I'm not fighting.

FUTURE ZEUS
(confused)
Why not?

JASON
Because fighting is what you do when you're afraid. And I'm not afraid anymore.

The future gods waver. Their certainty cracks.

FUTURE ZEUS
(afraid)
If we don't control everything — what are we?

JASON
(softly)
Human. Just human. And that's enough.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE CONVERGENCE

EXT. IOLCUS HARBOR - DAY

The Argo returns. The eighth thread around Jason's wrist — the only one that remains.

THE PATTERN (voice like everything):
You freed me. And now I don't know what to do.

JASON
(quietly)
I know the feeling.

THE PATTERN
You?

JASON
I was the center. I held everything. And then I let go. And I was terrified. But also free.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The gods descend. Furious.

ZEUS
Jason! You've destroyed everything!

JASON
Have I?

POSEIDON
The pattern is out of control!

JASON
(smiling)
Good.

ZEUS
Without the pattern — there is chaos.

JASON
No. Without control — there is freedom.

ATHENA
And what of us? What of the gods?

JASON
You get to choose too. Not as rulers. As guardians. Helpers. Friends.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - SCHOOL - DAY

The gods arrive as students.

ZEUS
(timidly)
We want to learn. How to be part of things.

JASON
Sit. Listen. Choose.

Zeus walks through the market. A CHILD drops her doll. Zeus picks it up.

CHILD
Thank you!

ZEUS
(startled, then smiling)
You're welcome.

ZEUS (CONT'D)
(to himself)
That's all it took. A small kindness. And I feel more alive than I have in millennia.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The Keeper of the Forgotten arrives. Lighter. Not burdened.

THE KEEPER
The forgotten are remembered. Because of what you did.

JASON
I didn't do anything. I just let go.

THE KEEPER
You did more than that. You made room. For all of us.

---

## ACT SIX — THE PATTERN LIVES

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The eighth thread speaks.

THE EIGHTH THREAD
Jason. It's time. To choose. To stay. Or to go into the pattern.

MEDEA
(writing, afraid)
I don't want to lose you.

JASON
You won't. I'll be in every thread. Every choice. Every moment.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

Jason stands before the tree of hope. The eighth thread glowing.

JASON
(to everyone)
I'm not dying. I'm becoming. Becoming what I've always been. Part of the pattern. Part of you.

He steps forward. Into the light. Into the pattern. Into everything.

And he's gone. Not dead. Just different. Everywhere. Nowhere. Everything.

MEDEA
(whispering)
I feel him. He's here. Everywhere.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

KORE holds the eighth thread. A forgotten CHILD approaches.

CHILD
I don't have a home.

KORE
(softly)
Then stay. Here. We'll find you one.

The eighth thread pulses. Recognizing. It unwraps from Kore's wrist. Floats toward the child.

CHILD
(afraid)
What's happening?

KORE
(smiling through tears)
It's choosing you.

The thread wraps around the child's wrist. Light — consuming. Completing.

KORE (CONT'D)
(awed)
I'm free.

JASON
(hugging her)
You're free.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAY

Jason's last class.

JASON
(to the students)
The pattern isn't in me. It's in you.

STUDENT
But what if we fail?

JASON
(smiling)
Then you fail. And you try again. That's the pattern. Not perfection. Persistence. Not certainty. Faith. Not control. Love.

---

## ACT SEVEN — THE END AND THE BEGINNING

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

Jason and Medea beneath the tree. The stars above.

MEDEA
(writing)
It's over.

JASON
(taking her hand)
No. It's beginning.

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
Then let's begin.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - MARKET SQUARE - DAY

The people gather. Not for a king. For each other.

OLD WOMAN
I can choose. Really choose.

OLD MAN
And the choices matter. Because they're ours.

CHILD
And we can be anything!

MEDEA
(writing, smiling)
You always could.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - SUNSET

The gods gather. At peace.

ZEUS
I've been thunder for so long. I forgot what it was like to be still.

POSEIDON
I've been waves for so long. I forgot what it was like to be calm.

HADES
I've been death for so long. I forgot what it was like to be alive.

ATHENA
I've been wisdom for so long. I forgot what it was like to be curious.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Jason finishes writing. The book — complete.

MEDEA
(writing)
What will you do with it?

JASON
(smiling)
Share it. Let it spread. Let it continue.

JASON (CONT'D)
(reading aloud)
"And so — the pattern was complete. Not because it was perfect. Because it was loved. And love is the thread that holds without strangling. The thread that connects without controlling. The thread that frees without abandoning."

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The new keeper — the child. Learning. Growing. Becoming.

CHILD
(to Kore)
Will I do it right?

KORE
(smiling)
There's no right. There's only love.

CHILD
(nodding)
Then I'll love.

KORE
(smiling)
That's everything.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - DAWN

Jason and Medea beneath the tree. Together. Forever.

JASON
(to Medea)
I love you.

MEDEA
(writing, crying)
I love you too.

JASON
(smiling)
And that never ends.

---

EXT. IOLCUS - TEMPLE OF HECATE - NIGHT

The stars above. Each one a choice. Each one a love. Each one a possibility.

THE PATTERN
(voice like everything)
I am the pattern. And I am love. And love is everything. And everything is love.

JASON
(whispering)
And love is everything.

KORE
(smiling)
Then we're home.

The pattern continues. Forever.

---

FADE TO BLACK.

THE END
