## LOCATION 03.1: THE SYMPLAEGADES — MIDDAY

### Overview
The Clashing Rocks. Two massive limestone cliffs face each other across a channel 20 meters wide. The rocks clash. BOOM. Open. Close. BOOM. Each crash sends spray 50 meters high. The sound is 18 Hz infrasound — felt in bones, not heard in ears. The rocks have been clashing for ten thousand years. The rocks have crushed a thousand ships. The rocks are patient. The rocks are waiting.

### Physical Layout
- **Channel Width:** 20m (when open), 2m (when closed)
- **Cliff Height:** 30m each
- **Cliff Width:** 20m each
- **Material:** Limestone, white (#F5F5F5 with grey veins #A0A0A0)
- **Texture:** Carved by water, smooth, ancient
- **Water:** White water (#FFFFFF), churning, violent, waves 2m height
- **Spray:** 50m height, volumetric, density 0.03
- **Cycle:** Open → Close → Open, every 5 seconds
