### MATERIAL DESCRIPTION
**LBFRP-001** — Monolithic laminate fabricated by Automated Fiber Placement (AFP):
- **Fiber:** Continuous basalt fiber (volcanic rock melt-extruded), 12K tows
- **Matrix:** Elium thermoplastic resin (Arkema), processable at 200°C, self-healing via reflow
- **Reinforcement:** Lonsdaleite nanodiamonds (hexagonal carbon, 58% harder than cubic diamond), 3% by weight
- **Thermal Management:** Integrated pyrolytic graphite substrates (1500 W/m·K in-plane)
- **Density:** 1.6 g/cm³ (vs. Al 2219 at 2.7, Ti 6-4 at 4.5)
- **Tensile:** 1.8 GPa | **Compressive:** 1.2 GPa | **Shear:** 0.9 GPa

### INTENDED APPLICATION
Primary load-bearing structures on NASA CLPS lunar landers:
- Lander legs (crush structure replacement)
- Propellant tanks / pressure vessels
- Payload adapter rings
- Habitat module panels (future missions)

**Replaces:** Aluminum 2219, Titanium 6Al-4V, Carbon fiber T700/epoxy, Aluminum honeycomb HRH-10 — **four material systems with one monolithic laminate.**
