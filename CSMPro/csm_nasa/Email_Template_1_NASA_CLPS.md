### THE PROBLEM
CLPS landers have experienced two consecutive mission-compromising anomalies in primary structure and landing systems:
- **Peregrine Mission One (TO-19C):** Propulsion anomaly — valve failure led to oxidizer leak, loss of attitude control, mission termination before lunar arrival
- **IM-1 / Nova-C (TO-19D):** Landing gear collapse — aluminum honeycomb crush core exceeded design limits, lander tipped over, degraded science return

Both failures trace to **traditional aerospace integration methods**: bolted joints in primary load paths, discrete crush structures with single-point failure modes, and multi-system architectures (structure + radiation + thermal + micrometeoroid as separate subsystems) that accumulate mass, complexity, and interface risk.

### THE SOLUTION: LBFRP-001
LBFRP-001 integrates **four critical functions into ONE monolithic laminate**:
1. **Primary Structure** — 1.8 GPa tensile, 1.2 GPa compressive, ρ = 1.6 g/cm³ (vs. Al 2219 at 2.7 g/cm³)
2. **Radiation Shielding** — Lonsdaleite nanodiamonds (58% harder than cubic diamond) + hydrogen-rich Elium thermoplastic stop GCR/SEP better than polyethylene at 1/3 the mass
3. **Thermal Management** — Integrated pyrolytic graphite substrates (1500 W/m·K in-plane) spread lunar day/night thermal loads laterally; structure IS the thermal control system
4. **Micrometeoroid/Regolith Protection** — Self-healing Elium thermoplastic (reflow at 200°C) closes micro-cracks; lonsdaleite blunts crack propagation at nanoscale

**Fabrication:** Automated Fiber Placement (AFP) — robotic, repeatable, digital-twin-verified. Every tow placement logged. Zero bolted joints in primary load path. Distributed manufacturing via partner AFP facilities — no dedicated factory required.
