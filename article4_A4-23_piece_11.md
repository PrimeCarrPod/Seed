m_{N}(d) = ℏ / (κ · d)

For d = 8: m_N ≈ 1.4 MeV
For d = 10: m_N ≈ 1.1 MeV
For d = 12: m_N ≈ 0.94 MeV

ΔN_eff = (43/7) (g_{s}/g_{ν})^{4/3} Σ_i (T_{ν_i}/T_ν)^4
