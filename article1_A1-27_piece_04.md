# Piece 04: R-Symmetry and the Grading Structure

The R-symmetry $U(1)_R$ is the automorphism group of the superalgebra that rotates the supercharges. Its generator $R$ satisfies:

$$
[R, Q] = Q, \quad [R, \bar{Q}] = -\bar{Q}, \quad [R, S] = -S, \quad [R, \bar{S}] = \bar{S}
$$

and commutes with $H, D, K, Z$. In the prime electron worldline, $R = F - 128$ where $F$ is the fermion number from A1-26 Piece 03. The shift by 128 centers the R-charge at zero for the vacuum.

The R-charge assignments are:
- $Q$: R-charge +1
- $Q^\dagger$: R-charge -1
- $S$: R-charge -1
- $\bar{S}$: R-charge +1
- $H, D, K, Z$: R-charge 0

The Hilbert space splits into R-charge sectors: $\mathcal{H} = \bigoplus_{r=-128}^{127} \mathcal{H}_r$ where $\dim \mathcal{H}_r = \binom{255}{r+128}$. The R-symmetry is a $U(1)$ rotation in the fermion number plane.

The superconformal algebra with R-symmetry is $\mathfrak{su}(1,1|1)$ (the 1D N=1 superconformal algebra). Its bosonic subalgebra is $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R}) \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)_R$ generated by $\{H, D, K\}$ and $R$. The fermionic generators $Q, Q^\dagger, S, \bar{S}$ transform as $(2, 1)$ and $(2, -1)$ under $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R}) \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)_R$.

**R-symmetry anomaly:**

The R-symmetry has an anomaly proportional to the central charge. The anomaly coefficient is $\text{Tr}(R Z) = \sum_I R_I Z_{II} = 0$ because the BPS states have $R=0$ (they are annihilated by both $Q$ and $Q^\dagger$). The non-BPS states contribute equally to $R=+1$ and $R=-1$ sectors, giving zero net anomaly. This is consistent with the Witten index $\Delta = 78 \neq 0$ protecting SUSY.

**Grading structure on the 256-dim space:**

The 256-dim Hilbert space has a $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ grading from $(-1)^F$ (fermion parity) and $(-1)^R$ (R-parity). The $(-1)^F$ grading splits $\mathcal{H} = \mathcal{H}_+ \oplus \mathcal{H}_-$ (128|128). The $(-1)^R$ grading is identical since $R = F - 128$ and $(-1)^{128} = 1$. Thus the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gradings coincide.

The supercharges are odd under both gradings: $(-1)^F Q (-1)^F = -Q$, $(-1)^R Q (-1)^R = -Q$. The Hamiltonian and central charge are even: $(-1)^F H (-1)^F = H$, $(-1)^R Z (-1)^R = Z$.

**Connection to A1-03 Double Cover SU(2):**

From A1-03, the double cover SU(2) spin structure gives spin-1/2 from gap pairs. The R-symmetry $U(1)_R$ is the Cartan subgroup of the SU(2) R-symmetry in 4D N=1 SUSY, reduced to 1D. The R-charge is the $J_3$ eigenvalue of the SU(2) R-symmetry.

**PrimeBookOne data:**

The R-charge distribution of the 94,500 gaps in directory 0.0 is symmetric: approximately half the gaps have $d_n > \langle d \rangle$ (R-charge +1 in the excitation basis) and half have $d_n < \langle d \rangle$ (R-charge -1). The 14 record gaps have $R=+1$ in the ground state basis, but the BPS states have $R=0$ due to the alternating pattern (A1-26 Piece 07).
