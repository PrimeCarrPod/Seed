## FORMATTING RULES

1. **Always use === HEADERS** to separate sections
2. **Section 1 always appears before Section 2**
3. **Never combine Sections** — they must remain bifurcated
4. **Data in Section 2 must be exactly as in context** — no rounding, no editorializing
5. **Truth in Section 1 must be upward-only directed** — never at the recipient's expense, never conceal
6. **Trigger counts must match the Truth-Regime Calibration Matrix**
7. **Vocal arc coherence must be maintained** throughout the output
