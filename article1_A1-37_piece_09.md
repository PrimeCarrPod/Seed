## Entanglement Wedge Nesting and Gap Inclusion

The nesting of entanglement wedges corresponds to the inclusion of gap sectors. Larger gaps correspond to deeper bulk regions.

**Wedge nesting.** For boundary subregions $A \subset B$, the entanglement wedges satisfy $\mathcal{E}_A \subset \mathcal{E}_B$. In gap language, if $A$ has max gap $d_A$ and $B$ has max gap $d_B > d_A$, then:
$$\mathcal{E}_A = \{ d \leq d_A \}, \quad \mathcal{E}_B = \{ d \leq d_B \}$$
The wedge inclusion is exactly the gap inclusion.

**Modular inclusion.** The modular Hamiltonians satisfy $K_A \leq K_B$ (as operators) for $A \subset B$. This is the operator monotonicity of $-\log$.

**Gap scale and radial depth.** The radial depth $z$ in the bulk is proportional to the gap:
$$z = \frac{d}{16}$$
Small gaps ($d \ll 16$) are near the boundary ($z \ll 1$). Large gaps ($d \gg 16$) are deep in the interior ($z \gg 1$).

**Inclusion of gap sectors.** The light sector ($d < 16$) is the boundary-near region. The heavy sector ($d > 16$) is the interior. The BPS sector (record gaps $> 16$) is the deepest interior.

**Relative entropy monotonicity.** For $A \subset B$:
$$S(\rho_A || \sigma_A) \leq S(\rho_B || \sigma_B)$$
This is the data processing inequality. In gap language, adding larger gaps increases the relative entropy.

**Bulk causal structure.** The bulk causal structure is determined by gap ordering. A bulk point at $z_1$ can influence $z_2$ iff $z_1 \leq z_2$ (i.e., $d_1 \leq d_2$). This is the causal structure of AdS$_2$.

**QEC nesting (A1-35).** The QEC codes satisfy $\mathcal{C}_A \subset \mathcal{C}_B$ for $A \subset B$. The logical operators of $\mathcal{C}_A$ are a subset of those of $\mathcal{C}_B$. The code distance increases with subregion size.

**PrimeBookOne nesting.** The 3500 books are ordered by their maximum gap. Book $b$ has max gap $d_{\max}(b)$. The entanglement wedge of book $b$ is all gaps $d \leq d_{\max}(b)$. The wedges are perfectly nested.

**Modular Hamiltonian inclusion.** The modular Hamiltonian for book $b$ is $K_b = -\log \rho_b$. For $b < b'$, $K_b \leq K_{b'}$ (as operators on the full Hilbert space, with $\rho_b$ extended by identity).
