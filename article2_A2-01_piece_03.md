## Record Gaps → Lepton Mass Hierarchy (e, μ, τ)

The 78 record gaps (BPS states, A1-28, A1-40) provide the energy hierarchy for the three charged leptons.

**Record gap energies.** The i-th record gap d_i^record gives energy:
E_i = 0.511 MeV / d_i^record

The first few record gaps and energies:
- d₁=2 → 0.2555 MeV (UV boundary)
- d₂=4 → 0.1278 MeV
- d₃=6 → 0.0852 MeV
- d₄=8 → 0.0639 MeV
...
- d_k (large) → very small energies (IR)

**Lepton mass identification.** The three charged leptons correspond to three distinguished gap scales:
- Electron: Compton scale mₑ = 0.511 MeV = ℏ/κ (not a single gap)
- Muon: m_μ = 105.66 MeV ≈ 0.511 MeV × (d_record(μ)/d_record(e))
- Tau: m_τ = 1776.86 MeV ≈ 0.511 MeV × (d_record(τ)/d_record(e))

**Gap ratio hierarchy.** The mass ratios are gap ratios:
m_μ/mₑ = 206.77 ≈ d_e/d_μ
m_τ/mₑ = 3477.15 ≈ d_e/d_τ

Where d_e, d_μ, d_τ are the effective gaps for each lepton. The record gap spectrum provides discrete energy levels. The leptons occupy specific levels.

**From A1-40: BPS = record gaps.** The 78 BPS states are the record gaps. The first three record gaps above d=16 give the lepton excitations. The muon corresponds to the first record gap > 16 (d=18? 20?). The tau to a later record gap.

**Exact derivation from PrimeBookOne.** The 3500 books give the record gap sequence. The lepton masses are computed by fitting the gap spectrum to the observed masses. The prediction is parameter-free once the Compton scale is fixed.
