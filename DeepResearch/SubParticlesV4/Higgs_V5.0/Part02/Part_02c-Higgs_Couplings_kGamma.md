## Part 2 of 14 — Higgs Couplings: Loop Couplings $\kappa_\gamma, \kappa_g$ ($h \to \gamma\gamma, gg$)

---

### Abstract

Higgs loop-induced couplings $\kappa_\gamma$ ($h \to \gamma\gamma$), $\kappa_g$ ($gg \to h$). Top, $W$ loops. BSM sensitivity. $h \to Z\gamma$.

---

### 1. $h \to \gamma\gamma$: Clean Mass Peak

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to \gamma\gamma) \approx 0.23\%$
**Amplitude**: $A_{\gamma\gamma} = A_W + \sum_f N_c Q_f^2 A_f$
- $W$ loop: $A_W \approx -8.3$ (dominant, opposite sign)
- Top loop: $A_t \approx +1.8$
- Interference: Destructive

**Measurement**: 
- ATLAS: $\mu_{\gamma\gamma} = 1.10 \pm 0.07$
- CMS: $\mu_{\gamma\gamma} = 1.12 \pm 0.09$
- Combined: $\kappa_\gamma = 1.10 \pm 0.07$

**BSM sensitivity**: Charged particles in loop → $\delta\kappa_\gamma$

---

### 2. $gg \to h$: Gluon Fusion Production

**Cross section**: $\sigma_{gg\to h} \approx 48.6$ pb at 13 TeV
**Amplitude**: Top loop dominant ($A_t \approx 1$)
**N3LO QCD**: Complete 2024, $\delta\sigma/\sigma \sim 2\%$
**$h \to gg$**: Not directly measurable (use $gg \to h$ rate)

**Measurement**: $\kappa_g = 1.00 \pm 0.05$ (from $gg \to h$)

---

### 3. $h \to Z\gamma$: Rare Loop Channel

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to Z\gamma) \approx 0.15\%$
**Amplitude**: $W$ + top loops (interference)
**Significance**: ATLAS $2.2\sigma$, CMS $2.7\sigma$ (2023)
**Combined**: $\kappa_{Z\gamma} = 1.0 \pm 0.5$

**HL-LHC**: $\delta\kappa_{Z\gamma} \sim 20\%$

---

### 4. BSM in Loops

**Charged scalars**: SUSY $\tilde{t}$, 2HDM $H^\pm$ → $\delta\kappa_\gamma$
**Vector-like fermions**: Top partners → $\delta\kappa_g, \delta\kappa_\gamma$
**Extra dimensions**: KK modes in loops

**Constraints**: $\kappa_\gamma, \kappa_g$ precision → BSM mass reach
