### 1. BSM Contribution to a_e

Generic dimension-6 operator:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{BSM}} = \frac{c}{\Lambda^2} \bar{e} \sigma^{\mu\nu} e F_{\mu\nu} \to \delta a_e \sim \frac{m_e^2}{\Lambda^2}$$

Current precision (0.13 ppb) → Λ > 10 TeV for c ~ 1.

**Worldline picture**: BSM vertex = new kink type on worldline with coupling g_BSM and mass M_BSM. Proper-time integral suppressed by (m_e/M_BSM)².

### 2. Electron vs Muon g-2: Complementary Probes

| Observable | Precision | BSM Reach | Status |
|------------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **a_e** | 0.13 ppb | Λ ~ 10 TeV | SM consistent |
| **a_μ** | 0.20 ppm | Λ ~ 1 TeV | **4.2σ tension** |

**Key**: a_e constrains **flavor-universal** BSM (same coupling to e, μ); a_μ tension suggests **flavor-violating** or chiral-enhanced BSM.

**Prime-Electron view**: 
- Electron = ground state (twin prime gaps d=2)
- Muon = 1st excitation (gap=4 record)
- Different gap moments → different BSM operator sensitivity

### 3. Dark Photon (A') Portal

**Lagrangian**: ε e A'_μ ēγ^μe + m_A'² A'_μ A'^μ

**Contribution**: δa_e = (α/2π) ε² F(m_A'/m_e)

**Constraints from a_e**:
- m_A' < 10 MeV: ε < 10⁻³
- m_A' ~ 100 MeV: ε < 10⁻⁴
- m_A' > 1 GeV: ε < 10⁻⁵

**Worldline**: A' emission/absorption = new kink type with mass gap m_A'.

### 4. Supersymmetry (MSSM)

**Dominant**: Chargino-sneutrino, neutralino-selectron loops

**δa_e^SUSY** ≈ (α/4π) (m_e²/m_SUSY²) tanβ × sign(μ)

**Constraints**:
- m_SUSY > 500 GeV (tanβ = 10)
- Electron g-2 stronger than muon for large tanβ

**Worldline**: SUSY partners = worldline excursions into supersymmetric sector.
