### Abstract

Neutrinos from astrophysical sources. Core-collapse supernovae, gamma-ray bursts, active galactic nuclei, tidal disruption events. Multi-messenger astronomy with neutrinos.

---

### 1. Core-Collapse Supernovae (Detailed)

**Emission**: $E_{\text{tot}} \sim 3 \times 10^{53}$ erg in neutrinos
**Timescale**: 
- Neutronization burst: $\sim 10$ ms ($\nu_e$ dominated)
- Accretion: $\sim 100-500$ ms (all flavors)
- Cooling: $\sim 10$ s (all flavors, equipartition)

**Spectra** (pinched thermal):
- $\nu_e$: $\langle E \rangle \sim 11$ MeV, $\alpha \sim 3$
- $\bar{\nu}_e$: $\langle E \rangle \sim 16$ MeV, $\alpha \sim 3$
- $\nu_x$: $\langle E \rangle \sim 22$ MeV, $\alpha \sim 2.5$

**Flavor conversion**: Collective effects, MSW, fast flavor conversion

---

### 2. Gamma-Ray Bursts (GRBs)

**Fireball model**: Relativistic jet ($\Gamma \sim 100-1000$)
**Neutrino production**: 
- $p\gamma \to \Delta^+ \to \pi^+ \to \nu_\mu$ (internal shocks)
- $pp \to \pi^+ \to \nu_\mu$ (external shocks)
- $E_\nu \sim 10^{14}-10^{17}$ eV

**Prompt emission**: $E_\nu \sim 10^{15}$ eV, coincident with γ
**Afterglow**: $E_\nu \sim 10^{17}$ eV, delayed
**IceCube limits**: $\Phi_{\nu}^{\text{GRB}} < 1\%$ of diffuse flux

---

### 3. Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN)

**Blazars**: Jet aligned with line of sight
**TXS 0506+056**: First identified ν source (IceCube, 2017)
- $E_\nu \sim 290$ TeV
- Coincident with γ-ray flare
- $p\gamma$ or $pp$ in jet

**NGC 1068**: Seyfert 2, steady ν source (IceCube, 2022)
- $E_\nu \sim 10-100$ TeV
- Corona/jet base production

**Future**: 
- IceCube-Gen2: More sources
- Multi-messenger: ν + γ + X-ray + optical

---

### 4. Tidal Disruption Events (TDEs)

**TDE**: Star disrupted by SMBH ($M \sim 10^6-10^7 M_\odot$)
**Neutrino production**: 
- Relativistic jet → $p\gamma$ on UV/X-ray photons
- Disk corona → $pp$, $p\gamma$

**AT2019dsg**: Possible ν coincidence (IceCube, 2019)
- $E_\nu \sim 200$ TeV
- TDE peak + ν arrival coincidence

**Future**: 
- ZTF/LSST TDE discovery rate $\sim 100$/yr
- Multi-messenger: ν + optical + X-ray + radio

---

### 5. Starburst Galaxies & Galaxy Clusters

**Starbursts**: High SFR → high CR density → $pp \to \pi \to \nu$
**M82, NGC 253**: Potential ν sources
**Galaxy clusters**: CR confinement → cumulative ν flux
**Perseus, Coma**: Cluster ν limits

---

### 6. Worldline Picture: Astrophysical Sources as Worldline Accelerators

**SNe** = Worldline core collapse
**GRBs** = Worldline relativistic jet
**AGN/TDEs** = Worldline SMBH accretion
**Cosmic rays** = Worldline high-energy folds
