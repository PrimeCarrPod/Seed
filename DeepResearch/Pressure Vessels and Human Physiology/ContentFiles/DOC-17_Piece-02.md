#### 17.2 RES Phagocytosis & Lipid Encapsulation Pathways

**17.2.1 RES Uptake Mechanism — From Alveoli to Liver/Spleen**

**Pathway:**
1. **Alveolar Macrophages (AMs):** Phagocytose PFC droplets (0.5–5 µm) in alveoli
2. **Migration:** AMs → lymphatics → hilar nodes → thoracic duct → bloodstream
3. **Systemic Circulation:** PFC-laden AMs/monocytes → liver (Kupffer cells) / spleen
4. **Hepatic/Splenic Sequestration:** Kupffer cells (liver), splenic macrophages → phagocytosis

**Phagocytosis Kinetics (Alveolar Macrophages):**
$$\frac{dN_{PFC}}{dt} = k_{phag} \cdot N_{AM} \cdot C_{PFC} - k_{clear} \cdot N_{PFC}$$

**Rate Constants (Rat AMs, In Vitro):**
- $k_{phag} \approx 0.1 \text{ mL/cell·hr}$
- $k_{clear} \approx 0.05 \text{ hr}^{-1}$ (migration to lymphatics)

**AM Capacity:** ~50 PFC droplets/cell (saturation)

**17.2.2 Lipid Encapsulation — The "Trojan Horse" Mechanism**

**Mechanism:**
1. **PFC droplets** in cytoplasm → recognized as lipid
2. **Lipid droplet biogenesis proteins** (PLIN2, DGAT1) → coat PFC with phospholipid monolayer
3. **Apolipoproteins** (ApoE, ApoA-I) → adsorb to surface → lipoprotein-like particle
4. **VLDL-like secretion** → enters lymphatic → bloodstream

**Lipid Coating Composition (Mass Spec):**
| Component | % of Coating Mass |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Phosphatidylcholine (PC)** | 45% |
| **Phosphatidylethanolamine (PE)** | 20% |
| **Cholesterol** | 15% |
| **Triglycerides** | 10% |
| **Apolipoproteins (ApoE, ApoA-I)** | 10% |

**Resulting Particle:**
- **Diameter:** 30–100 nm (lipoprotein-sized)
- **Density:** ~1.0 g/cm³ (near plasma)
- **Circulation Half-life:** 4–8 hr (like VLDL)

**17.2.3 RES Sequestration — Liver & Spleen Kinetics**

**Hepatic Uptake (Kupffer Cells):**
- **Blood Flow:** 1.5 L/min (25% cardiac output)
- **Extraction Fraction:** 0.6–0.8 (first pass)
- **Uptake Rate:** $k_{liver} \approx 0.3 \text{ hr}^{-1}$

**Splenic Uptake:**
- **Blood Flow:** 0.3 L/min
- **Extraction Fraction:** 0.4–0.6
- **Uptake Rate:** $k_{spleen} \approx 0.15 \text{ hr}^{-1}$

**Organ Distribution at 24 hr (IV Dose, Rat):**
| Organ | % Injected Dose | PFC Concentration |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Liver** | 45–50% | 20–30% w/w |
| **Spleen** | 15–20% | 10–15% w/w |
| **Lung** | 5–10% | 2–5% w/w |
| **Blood** | 2–5% | 0.5–1% w/w |
| **Kidney** | < 1% | < 0.5% |
| **Brain** | < 0.05% | < 0.01% (BBB intact) |
| **Fat** | 5% | 5% w/w |

**17.2.4 Intracellular Fate — Lysosomal Sequestration**

**Intracellular Trafficking:**
1. **Phagosome Formation** → PFC droplet enclosed
2. **Phagosome Maturation** → Rab5 → Rab7 → LAMP1
3. **Lysosomal Fusion** → pH 4.5–5.0, hydrolytic enzymes
4. **PFC Stability:** **NO degradation** — C-F bonds resist lysosomal enzymes
5. **Storage:** PFC droplets persist in lysosomal vacuoles

**Lysosomal Burden:**
- **Kupffer Cell Capacity:** ~200 PFC droplets/cell
- **Lysosomal Volume Fraction:** Up to 30% of cell volume
- **Cellular Stress:** Mild (no apoptosis at clinical doses)

**Long-Term Storage (Months–Years):**
- **Liver/Spleen:** PFC droplets persist in lysosomal residual bodies
- **Turnover:** Extremely slow (macrophage turnover ~years)
- **Release:** Only upon cell death → re-phagocytosis by new macrophages
