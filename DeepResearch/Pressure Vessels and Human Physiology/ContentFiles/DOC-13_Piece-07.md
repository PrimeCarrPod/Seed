**13.7.2 Susceptibility Screening (Operational)**

**Pre-Dive Assessment (USN/Commercial):**
1. **HPNS History:** Prior Grade II+ → restrict depth
2. **Neurological Exam:** Tremor, reflexes, coordination
3. **EEG Baseline:** θ/α ratio, spike activity
3. **Psychological:** Anxiety scale (STAI), stress resilience
4. **Genetic (Optional):** HPNS-PRS panel (emerging)

**13.7.3 Acclimatization (The "Saturation Effect")**

**Repeated Exposure Reduces HPNS:**
- **Mechanism:** Homeostatic plasticity — channel expression changes
- **Time Course:** 3–5 saturation exposures
- **Magnitude:** 30–50% reduction in tremor/EEG at same depth

**Operational Use:**
- **Novice Divers:** Conservative depth limits
- **Experienced Sat Divers:** Full depth range (with Trimix)
- **"Saturation Season":** Spring training dives → fall project dives
