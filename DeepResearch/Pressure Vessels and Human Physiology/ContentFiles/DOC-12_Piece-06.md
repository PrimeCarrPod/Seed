**12.6.6 Operational Mitigation: The "END" Calculation**

**Equivalent Narcotic Depth (END):**
$$\text{END} = \left( \frac{P_{N_2} + P_{Ar} + \dots}{0.79} \right) \times 10 \quad \text{[msw]}$$

**Example (Trimix 18/45 at 100 msw):**
- $P_{tot} = 11$ bar
- $F_{He} = 0.45$, $F_{N_2} = 0.18$, $F_{O_2} = 0.37$
- $P_{N_2} = 0.18 \times 11 = 1.98$ bar
- $\text{END} = (1.98 / 0.79) \times 10 = \mathbf{25 \text{ msw}}$

**Operational Rule: Keep END ≤ 30–40 msw for complex tasks.**
