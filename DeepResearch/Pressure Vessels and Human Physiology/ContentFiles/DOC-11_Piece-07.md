#### 11.7 Commercial vs. Naval Saturation Systems

**11.7.1 US Navy Saturation Fly-Away System (SATFADS)**

**Capability:**
- **Depth:** 0–450 msw (1,500 fsw)
- **Divers:** 6–12
- **Deployment:** C-5/C-17 airlift, ship-deployed
- **Setup Time:** 72 hours

**Key Components:**
- **Chambers:** 2 × Living (6-man), 1 × Transfer, 1 × Medical
- **Gas Storage:** 50,000 scf He, 10,000 scf O₂, 5,000 scf N₂
- **Compressors:** 2 × 1,000 scfm (He/O₂ boost)
- **Scrubber:** Central soda lime (1,000 kg)
- **Hot Water:** 500 kW diesel heater
- **Power:** 2 × 500 kW generators
- **Control:** VANGuard saturation control system

**Operational Differences (Naval vs. Commercial):**
| Aspect | **US Navy** | **Commercial (Oceaneering, TechnipFMC)** |
|--------|-------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Max Depth** | 450 msw | 300 msw (typically) |
| **Divers** | 6–12 | 12–24 |
| **Mission** | Rescue, salvage, war | Construction, inspection, maintenance |
| **Duration** | 7–30 days | 14–28 days (project-based) |
| **Gas Mix** | Heliox / Trimix | Heliox / Trimix / Nitrox |
| **Decompression** | USN 6/5/4/3 | USN 6/5/4/3 or COMEX |
| **Medical** | Full med lock, DMO | Contract medic, telemedicine |
| **Regulation** | USN DISSUB/NAVSEA | IMCA D 018, DNV, Flag State |

**11.7.2 Commercial Saturation Systems (Oceaneering SAT 10/20/30)**

**Oceaneering SAT 30 (Latest Generation):**
- **Divers:** 18–24
- **Depth:** 300 msw
- **Chambers:** 3 × Living (8-man), 1 × Transfer, 1 × Sanitary, 1 × Medical
- **Gas Management:** Automated (PLC-controlled)
- **CO₂ Scrubbing:** Dual regenerable amine beds (no soda lime)
- **Trace Gas:** Catalytic oxidation + carbon
- **Thermal:** Heat pump (reclaims scrubber heat)
- **Control:** SATLink® (touchscreen, remote monitoring)

**Automation Features:**
- **Gas Blending:** Automated He/O₂/N₂ mixing to setpoints
- **CO₂ Control:** Amine beds cycle every 30 min (auto)
- **Thermal:** Heat pump maintains 31°C ±0.5°C
- **Data:** Real-time to shore (satellite link)

**11.7.3 COMEX (French) Saturation Systems**

**COMEX Hydra / Saga Systems:**
- **Decompression Tables:** COMEX 30 (proprietary)
- **Gas:** Heliox primary, Trimix for > 180 msw
- **Decompression:** 12/24-hr day (12 hr deco / 12 hr hold)
- **Thermal:** 30–32°C (slightly lower than USN)
- **Medical:** COMEX 30 medical protocols

**COMEX 30 Decompression (Example, 300 msw):**
| Segment | Rate [msw/hr] | Comparison to USN |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| 300–60 | 2.0 | USN: 1.8 (faster) |
| 60–30 | 1.5 | USN: 1.5 (same) |
| 30–15 | 1.2 | USN: 1.2 (same) |
| 15–0 | 0.9 | USN: 0.91 (same) |

**Slightly faster deep, same shallow.**

**11.7.4 Norwegian (DNV) Saturation Standards**

**DNV-ST-E271 Requirements:**
- **Chamber Design:** 1.5× safety factor on collapse
- **Gas System:** Redundant supply, auto-switch
- **Scrubber:** Dual regenerable (amine or soda lime)
- **Medical:** Hyperbaric chamber on-site (2-lock)
- **Decompression:** Approved tables (USN, COMEX, or proprietary)
- **Monitoring:** Continuous Doppler, video, gas analysis

**Norwegian Sector Practice (North Sea):**
- **Depths:** 100–180 msw (mostly)
- **Duration:** 14–21 days
- **Contractors:** TechnipFMC, Subsea 7, DeepOcean
- **Decompression:** USN 6/5/4/3 standard

**11.7.5 Chinese (COSL / CNOOC) Saturation Development**

**COSL Saturation System (2020+):**
- **Depth:** 300 msw target
- **Divers:** 12–18
- **Technology:** Licensed from Oceaneering/COMEX
- **Decompression:** USN 6/5/4/3
- **Medical:** Chinese Navy hyperbaric protocols
- **Status:** Operational in South China Sea (2022+)
