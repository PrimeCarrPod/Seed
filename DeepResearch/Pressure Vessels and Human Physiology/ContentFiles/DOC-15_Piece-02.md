| Gas | C₁₀F₂₀ | C₁₀F₁₈ | C₈F₁₇Br | C₈F₁₈ | C₆F₁₄ |
|-----|--------|--------|---------|-------|-------|

**Lower k_H = Higher Solubility**

**15.2.2 Solubility Temperature Dependence (van't Hoff)**

$$\ln \frac{k_H(T_2)}{k_H(T_1)} = -\frac{\Delta H_{\text{sol}}}{R} \left(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}\right)$$

**Enthalpy of Solution (ΔH_sol) [kJ/mol]:**
| Gas | C₁₀F₂₀ | C₁₀F₁₈ | C₈F₁₇Br | C₈F₁₈ |
|-----|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| **O₂** | -12 | -11 | -12 | -12 |
| **CO₂** | -18 | -17 | -18 | -18 |
| **N₂** | -10 | -9 | -10 | -10 |

**Exothermic dissolution — solubility DECREASES with temperature.**
