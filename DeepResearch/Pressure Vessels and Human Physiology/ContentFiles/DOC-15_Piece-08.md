#### 15.8 Selection Guide by Application

**15.8.1 Decision Matrix — PFC Selection by Clinical Scenario**

| Clinical Need | Primary Requirement | Recommended PFC | Alternative | Rationale |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Neonatal TLV (RDS, < 2 kg)** | Low η, lowest γ, fast alveolar recruitment | **PFOB (Perflubron)** | Perfluoro-n-octane | Lowest η (1.7 cP), lowest γ (7.3 mN/m) |
| **Adult ARDS TLV (Severe)** | Max CO₂ clearance, moderate η | **Perfluorodecalin (cis)** | Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane | Highest CO₂ (160 mL), η manageable (5.4 cP) |
| **Adult TLV (Prolonged, > 24 hr)** | High O₂/CO₂, low VP (low loss) | **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | Perfluorodecalin | Highest gas capacity, low VP (3.8 mmHg) |
| **IV O₂ Delivery (Trauma, Stroke)** | Emulsifiable, low η, long circulation | **PFOB** or **Perfluoro-n-octane** | Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane | Emulsifiable, IV compatible, PFOB proven |
| **Organ Preservation (Liver/Pancreas)** | High O₂ diffusion, stable, low cost | **Perfluorodecalin** | Perfluoro-n-octane | Proven clinical track record, UW/PFC method |
| **Organ Preservation (Lung EVLP)** | Emulsifiable, low γ, O₂ carrier | **Perfluorodecalin emulsion (10%)** | PFOB emulsion | Proven in Toronto EVLP protocol |
| **Deep Dive TLV (> 500 msw)** | Max gas capacity, low VP, thermal stability | **Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane** | Perfluorodecalin | Highest gas capacity, lowest VP (3.8 mmHg) |
| **Organ Preservation (Heart/Kidney)** | O₂ carrier in perfusate | **Perfluorodecalin emulsion (5%)** | PFOB emulsion | Compatible with machine perfusion |

**15.8.2 TLV Ventilator Compatibility Matrix**

| Ventilator Type | Preferred PFC | Reason |
|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Volume-Control (Constant Flow)** | Perfluoro-n-octane (low η) | Low pressure drop in circuit |
| **Pressure-Control (Constant P)** | Perfluorodecalin (high CO₂) | Maximizes CO₂ clearance at set P |
| **High-Frequency Oscillatory (HFOV)** | PFOB (low η, low γ) | Low impedance at high frequency |
| **Liquid Ventilator (Dedicated)** | Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane | Matches design specs (high gas, low VP) |

**15.8.3 Emulsion Formulation Compatibility**

| Emulsifier System | Compatible PFCs | Incompatible | Notes |
|-------------------|-----------------|--------------|-------|
| **Egg Yolk Phospholipid (Pluronic F-68)** | Perfluorodecalin, PFOB, C₈F₁₈ | C₆F₁₄ (too volatile) | Classic (Fluosol) |
| **Egg Phospholipid + DSPE-PEG2000** | All (C₆F₁₄–C₁₀F₂₀) | None | Modern (Oxygent, Oxycyte) |
| **DSPE-PEG/Cholesterol** | All | None | High stability (NanO₂) |
| **Perfluorinated Surfactant (Zonyl)** | All | None | Specialized, expensive |
