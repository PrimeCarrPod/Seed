**17.7.1 Mechanical Counterpressure (MCP) Suits — The "Bio-Suit" Evolution**

**Current State (MIT Bio-Suit, NASA Z-2 Prototype):**
- **Principle:** Elastic compression garment (30 kPa mechanical) replaces gas pressure
- **Materials:** Spandex + Dyneema + Nitinol active elements
- **Pressure Delivery:** 25–30 kPa mechanical compression
- **Mobility:** 85% of unsuited range (vs. 30% for EMU)
- **Weight:** 15 kg (vs. 60 kg EMU)

**Next-Gen MCP (2035–2045):**
| Generation | Pressure | Mobility | Key Tech |
|------------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Bio-Suit 2.0** | 30 kPa | 90% | Shape-memory alloy (SMA) tendons |
| **Bio-Suit 3.0** | 35 kPa | 95% | Electroactive polymer (EAP) muscle |
| **Bio-Suit 4.0 (MCP-Exo)** | 40 kPa | 100% | Soft exoskeleton + MCP hybrid |

- **Shape-Memory Alloys (NiTi):** Thermal activation → 5% strain, 300 MPa stress

- **Magnetorheological Fluids:** Field-controlled viscosity → variable stiffness joints

**Power Requirements (MCP-Exo):**
- **Actuation:** 50–100 W (intermittent)
- **Sensors/Control:** 10 W continuous
- **Battery:** 2 kg Li-S (500 Wh/kg) → 8 hr EVA

**17.7.2 Synthetic Biology — Living PVHO Membranes**

**Concept:** Engineered biological membranes for gas exchange, CO₂ capture, O₂ generation

**Approach 1: Synthetic Chloroplasts (Artificial Photosynthesis)**
- **Chassis:** Liposome or polymerosome
- **Components:** Photosystem II mimics, water-splitting catalysts, CO₂ fixation enzymes
- **Reaction:** $2\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{CO}_2 \xrightarrow{h\nu} \text{O}_2 + \text{CH}_2\text{O}$
- **Target:** 1 mL O₂/cm²/hr at 1 sun equivalent

**Approach 2: Engineered Extremophile Biofilms**
- **Organism:** *Deinococcus radiodurans* (radiation resistant) + *Synechocystis* (photosynthetic)
- **Function:** Living membrane on PVHO inner surface
- **Functions:** O₂ generation, CO₂ scrubbing, toxin degradation, self-repair
- **Nutrient Feed:** Minimal (CO₂, H₂O, trace minerals, light)

**Approach 3: Engineered Protein Channels (Designer Ion Channels)**
- **Platform:** *E. coli* expression → purified protein → reconstituted in lipid bilayer
- **Targets:** O₂ channels (high flux), CO₂ channels (carbonic anhydrase fusion), H⁺ pumps
- **Integration:** Artificial lipid bilayer on PVHO inner surface
