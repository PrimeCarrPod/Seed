#### 15.4 Biocompatibility, Toxicology & Clearance Kinetics

**15.4.1 Acute Toxicity (Animal Studies)**

| PFC | LD₅₀ (IV, rat) [mL/kg] | LD₅₀ (IP, rat) [mL/kg] | NOAEL (IV, 28-day) [mL/kg/day] | Primary Toxicity |
|-----|------------------------|------------------------|--------------------------------|------------------|
| **C₁₀F₂₀ (Oxycyte)** | > 20 | > 50 | 2.0 | Mild hepatic vacuolation |
| **C₁₀F₁₈ (Decalin)** | > 15 | > 30 | 1.5 | Hepatic, splenic enlargement |
| **C₈F₁₇Br (PFOB)** | > 15 | > 30 | 1.0 | Hepatic, mild hemolysis |
| **C₈F₁₈ (n-Octane)** | > 10 | > 20 | 0.5 | Hepatic, testicular (male rats) |
| **C₆F₁₄** | > 5 | > 10 | 0.2 | CNS depression, hepatic |

**All PFCs: Low acute toxicity — primary effect is physical (organ enlargement from accumulation).**

**15.4.4 Pulmonary Clearance Kinetics (TLV / Inhalation)**

**Clearance Model (Two-Compartment):**
$$C(t) = C_0 \left[ f_1 e^{-k_1 t} + f_2 e^{-k_2 t} \right]$$

**Clearance Parameters (Rat, 6-hr TLV):**
| PFC | f₁ (Fast) | k₁ [hr⁻¹] | t₁/₂,fast [hr] | f₂ (Slow) | k₂ [hr⁻¹] | t₁/₂,slow [hr] |
|-----|-----------|-----------|----------------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| **C₁₀F₂₀** | 0.70 | 0.8 | 0.87 | 0.30 | 0.05 | 14 |
| **C₁₀F₁₈** | 0.65 | 0.6 | 1.15 | 0.35 | 0.04 | 17 |
| **C₈F₁₇Br** | 0.60 | 0.5 | 1.4 | 0.40 | 0.03 | 23 |
| **C₈F₁₈** | 0.75 | 1.0 | 0.69 | 0.25 | 0.06 | 11 |
| **C₆F₁₄** | 0.95 | 2.0 | 0.35 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 7 |

**Human Extrapolation (Allometric Scaling):**
$$t_{1/2,\text{human}} \approx t_{1/2,\text{rat}} \times \left(\frac{70}{0.25}\right)^{0.25} \approx t_{1/2,\text{rat}} \times 7$$

**Human Half-Lives (Estimated):**
- **Fast phase:** 5–10 hr
- **Slow phase:** 3–5 days (up to 2 weeks for PFOB)
