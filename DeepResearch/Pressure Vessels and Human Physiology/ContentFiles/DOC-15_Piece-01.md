### ABSTRACT

This document provides comprehensive comparative tables of **biomedical perfluorocarbon (PFC) variants** — the liquid fluorocarbons optimized for Total Liquid Ventilation (TLV), intravenous oxygen delivery, organ preservation, and blood substitution. Each variant is characterized by its molecular structure, synthesis route, and complete physic-chemical profile including gas solubility, thermophysical properties, transport coefficients, biocompatibility metrics, and regulatory status. The document enables rational selection of PFCs for specific TLV, IV, and organ preservation applications through quantitative multi-parameter comparison.
