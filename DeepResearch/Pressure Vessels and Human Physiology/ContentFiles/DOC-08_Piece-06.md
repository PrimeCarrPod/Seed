#### 8.6 Historical Evolution: Mercury → Gemini → Apollo → Shuttle → ISS

**8.6.1 Project Mercury (1961–1963)**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Cabin Atmosphere** | 100% O₂ at 34.5 kPa (5.0 psi) |
| **Suit Pressure** | 34.5 kPa (5.0 psi) — **same as cabin** |
| **Pre-breathe** | None required (isobaric) |
| **Fire Risk** | High (Apollo 1 precursor) |
| **Rationale** | Simplicity: single atmosphere |

**Key Issue:** Cabin at 34.5 kPa 100% O₂ on ground = **Apollo 1 fire hazard**.

**8.6.2 Project Gemini (1965–1966)**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Cabin Atmosphere** | 100% O₂ at 34.5 kPa (5.0 psi) |
| **Suit Pressure** | 25.5 kPa (3.7 psi) — G4C/G5C |
| **Pre-breathe** | Required (cabin 34.5 → suit 25.5) |
| **EVA Protocol** | 30 min 100% O₂ pre-breathe |
| **Rationale** | Lower suit pressure for mobility (first EVA) |

**Gemini 4 (White EVA):** 23-min EVA, 3.7 psi suit — **first US EVA**.

**8.6.3 Project Apollo (1968–1972)**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Launch Cabin** | 60% O₂ / 40% N₂ at 101.3 kPa (sea level) |
| **Orbit Cabin** | 100% O₂ at 34.5 kPa (5.0 psi) |
| **Suit Pressure (A7L)** | 25.5 kPa (3.7 psi) |
| **Pre-breathe (Launch)** | None (mixed gas cabin) |
| **Pre-breathe (Orbit)** | 30 min 100% O₂ (cabin 34.5 → suit 25.5) |
| **Lunar Surface** | Suit at 25.5 kPa, cabin depressurized |

**Post-Apollo 1 Change:** Launch at sea-level mixed gas → **eliminated ground fire risk**.

**8.6.4 Skylab (1973–1974)**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Cabin** | 70% O₂ / 30% N₂ at 34.5 kPa (5.0 psi) |
| **EVA Suit** | Modified A7L at 25.5 kPa (3.7 psi) |
| **Pre-breathe** | 30 min (70% O₂ cabin → 100% O₂ suit) |
| **Innovation** | First long-duration mixed-gas habitat |

**8.6.5 Space Shuttle (1981–2011)**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Cabin** | 100% O₂ / N₂ at 101.3 kPa (14.7 psi) — **sea level** |
| **EMU Suit** | 29.6 kPa (4.3 psi) — **lower than Apollo!** |
| **Pre-breathe (Standard)** | 4 hr @ 101.3 kPa + 45 min suit |
| **Pre-breathe (Campout)** | 24 hr @ 70.3 kPa (10.2 psi) + 45 min |
| **Rationale** | Sea-level cabin for science, passengers; suit optimized for EVA work |

**Shuttle Innovation:** **Two pre-breathe protocols** for operational flexibility.

**8.6.6 International Space Station (1998–Present)**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Cabin** | 101.3 kPa, 21% O₂ / 79% N₂ — **sea level air** |
| **EMU Suit** | 29.6 kPa (4.3 psi) |
| **Pre-breathe (Standard)** | 4 hr 100% O₂ + 45 min suit |
| **Pre-breathe (Campout)** | 24 hr @ 70.3 kPa + 45 min |
| **Russian Segment** | 101.3 kPa air; Orlan 40 kPa, 30 min pre-breathe |

**ISS Dual Protocol:** Allows EVA from both US and Russian airlocks.

**8.6.7 Pressure Evolution Summary**

| Program | Cabin P [kPa] | Cabin Gas | Suit P [kPa] | Pre-breathe |
|---------|---------------|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Mercury** | 34.5 | 100% O₂ | 34.5 | None |
| **Gemini** | 34.5 | 100% O₂ | 25.5 | 30 min |
| **Apollo** | 101.3/34.5 | Mixed/100% O₂ | 25.5 | 30 min |
| **Skylab** | 34.5 | 70% O₂ | 25.5 | 30 min |
| **Shuttle** | 101.3 | Air | **29.6** | 4 hr / 24 hr |
| **ISS** | 101.3 | Air | **29.6** | 4 hr / 24 hr |
| **Orlan** | 101.3 | Air | 40.0 | 30 min |
| **Future (Gateway)** | **55** | **30% O₂** | **29.6/40** | **0 min** |

**Trend:** Cabin pressure ↑ (sea level for science), Suit pressure optimized for **EVA work capacity**.
