**15.5.2 Fluosol-DA 20 — The First Approved Blood Substitute**

**Composition:** 20% Perfluorodecalin, 80% water, Pluronic F-68 (emulsifier)
**Indication:** Adjunct to PCI (reduce ischemic complications)
**Dose:** 10–20 mL/kg IV
**O₂ Delivery:** 2.5 mL O₂/100 mL (at 1 atm O₂)
**Advantages:** Reduced angina, improved ST segments
**Failures:**
- **Complement activation** → flu-like symptoms, pulmonary hypertension
- **Low O₂ capacity** (20% PFC) → marginal benefit
- **Short half-life** (2–4 hr) → repeated dosing
- **Cost:** $1,500/dose (1990s) vs. $200 for blood
**Withdrawn:** 1994 (commercial failure)

**15.5.3 Oxygent — The Phase III Failure**

**Composition:** 60% Perfluoro-n-octane, egg phospholipid emulsifier
**Indication:** Coronary artery bypass (CABG), trauma
**Dose:** 15–30 mL/kg (up to 2 L total)
**O₂ Delivery:** 27 mL O₂/100 mL (at 100% O₂)
**Phase III Results (CABG, n=600):**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Allogeneic blood avoidance — **NOT MET**
- **Stroke Rate:** **Oxygent 6.8% vs. Control 2.1% (p=0.007)**
- **Mechanism:** Microemboli from droplets? Platelet activation? Complement?
**Outcome:** **Program terminated 1998**

**15.5.4 Oxycyte — Current Hope for Trauma**

**Composition:** 60% Perfluoro-tert-butylcyclohexane, phospholipid/PEG
**Indication:** Traumatic brain injury (TBI), ischemic stroke
**Mechanism:** IV O₂ delivery to ischemic penumbra
**Phase II Results (TBI, n=120):**
- **Mortality:** Oxycyte 18% vs. Control 28% (p=0.08)
- **GOS-E at 6 mo:** Favorable trend
- **Safety:** No stroke signal (droplet size 0.2 µm, PEGylated)
**Status:** **Phase IIb/III planning (2024–2025)**

**15.5.5 Perfluoron — Ophthalmic Success**

**Indication:** Retinal detachment repair (vitreous tamponade)
**Advantages:** High O₂ solubility → prevents retinal hypoxia
**Properties:** Pure Perfluoro-n-octane, 100% liquid
**Duration:** Temporary (removed after 1–2 weeks)
**Status:** **FDA Approved (1999), Standard of Care**
