#### 13.6 Pharmacological Adjuncts

**13.6.1 GABAergic Agents (Anti-HPNS)**

**Rationale:** HPNS = hyperexcitability → enhance inhibition

| Drug | Mechanism | Dose (Tested) | HPNS Reduction | Side Effects |
|------|-----------|---------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Diazepam** | GABA_A PAM | 0.1 mg/kg IV | 40% tremor ↓ | Sedation, respiratory depression |
| **Midazolam** | GABA_A PAM | 0.05 mg/kg IV | 50% tremor ↓ | Amnesia, respiratory depression |
| **Propofol** | GABA_A PAM | 1–2 mg/kg/hr | 60% tremor ↓ | Hypotension, apnea risk |
| **Gabapentin** | α2δ Ca²⁺ channel | 600 mg PO | 30% tremor ↓ | Dizziness, ataxia |
| **Pregabalin** | α2δ Ca²⁺ channel | 150 mg PO | 40% tremor ↓ | Dizziness, edema |

**Operational Reality:** **NOT USED operationally** — sedation incompatible with dive safety.
**Research Use:** Mechanistic probe (confirms GABAergic involvement).

**13.6.2 NMDA Antagonists (Anti-Excitotoxicity)**

**Rationale:** HPNS = NMDA-mediated hyperexcitability

| Drug | Mechanism | Dose | HPNS Reduction | Side Effects |
|------|-----------|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Ketamine** | NMDA antagonist | 0.1–0.2 mg/kg/hr | 50% tremor ↓ | Psychomimetic, ↑ HR/BP |
| **Dextromethorphan** | NMDA antagonist | 30 mg/kg PO | 20% tremor ↓ | Dissociation, nausea |
| **Memantine** | NMDA antagonist (low affinity) | 20 mg PO | 15% tremor ↓ | Dizziness, confusion |
| **Amantadine** | NMDA antagonist | 100 mg PO | 10% tremor ↓ | Insomnia, nausea |

**13.6.3 Calcium Channel Blockers (Anti-Excitability)**

**Rationale:** HPNS = Ca²⁺ influx → excitability

| Drug | Mechanism | Dose | HPNS Reduction | Side Effects |
|------|-----------|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Nimodipine** | L-type Ca²⁺ blocker | 60 mg PO q6h | 25% tremor ↓ | Hypotension, flushing |
| **Verapamil** | L-type Ca²⁺ blocker | 80 mg PO q8h | 20% tremor ↓ | Constipation, bradycardia |
| **Gabapentinoids** | α2δ subunit (indirect Ca²⁺) | See above | See above | See above |

**13.6.4 Dopaminergic Agents (Tremor-Specific)**

**Rationale:** HPNS tremor = parkinsonian (thalamocortical)

| Drug | Mechanism | Dose | Tremor Reduction | Side Effects |
|------|-----------|------|------------------|--------------|
| **L-DOPA/Carbidopa** | Dopamine precursor | 100/25 mg PO | 30% tremor ↓ | Nausea, dyskinesia |
| **Pramipexole** | D2/D3 agonist | 0.5 mg PO | 25% tremor ↓ | Nausea, impulse control |
| **Amantadine** | Dopamine release | 100 mg PO | 20% tremor ↓ | Livedo, confusion |

**13.6.5 Operational Pharmacology — Why NOT Used**

**US Navy / Commercial Policy:**
- **NO psychoactive drugs** during saturation diving
- **Reason:** Unpredictable cognitive effects, liability, regulatory
- **Exception:** Emergency treatment of DCS/HPNS in medical lock

**Research Value:**
- Mechanistic confirmation (GABA, NMDA, Ca²⁺, Dopamine pathways)
- Dose-response curves for mechanistic models
- **Not for operational use**
