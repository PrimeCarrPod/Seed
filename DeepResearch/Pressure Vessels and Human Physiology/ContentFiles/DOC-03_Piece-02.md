#### 3.2 Comet 1 Disaster Sequence (1954)

Three catastrophic in-flight structural disintegrations occurred within 14 months, all exhibiting **explosive decompression from the cabin roof/window area**, leading to complete airframe breakup.

**3.2.1 Accident 1: G-ALYP (BOAC Flight 781) — 10 January 1954**

- **Route:** Rome → London (departed Ciampino)
- **Phase:** Climb through 8,200 m (27,000 ft)
- **Time:** 10:31 UTC, 20 min after takeoff
- **Witnesses:** Fishermen off Elba Island saw "fire in sky" / "explosion"
- **Wreckage:** Scattered over 15 km² seabed (depth 150 m)
- **Recovery:** 60% by weight (Italian Navy, March–May 1954)
- **Key Finding:** **Forward fuselage roof skin** fractured longitudinally; **square window corners** at ADF (Automatic Direction Finder) antenna apertures showed fatigue cracking

**3.2.2 Accident 2: G-ALYY (BOAC Flight 783) — 8 April 1954**

- **Route:** Rome → Johannesburg (departed Ciampino)
- **Phase:** Cruise at 10,700 m (35,000 ft)
- **Time:** 18:32 UTC, 40 min after takeoff
- **Witnesses:** None (night, over Mediterranean)
- **Wreckage:** Scattered over 25 km² seabed (depth 700 m)
- **Recovery:** Minimal (deep water); **no structural fragments recovered**
- **Significance:** **Second identical failure** → fleet grounded (12 April 1954)

**3.2.3 Accident 3: G-ALYV (SAA Flight 201) — 8 April 1954 (same day as G-ALYY!)**

- **Route:** Rome → Cairo (departed Ciampino)
- **Phase:** Climb through 10,400 m (34,000 ft)
- **Time:** 19:07 UTC, 35 min after takeoff
- **Wreckage:** Scattered over 20 km² near Naples (depth 500 m)
- **Recovery:** 40% by weight (Italian Navy, May–June 1954)
- **Key Finding:** **Identical fracture pattern** to G-ALYP — forward roof, ADF window corners
- **Significance:** **Third failure in 3 months** → definitive proof of systemic design flaw

**3.2.4 Common Failure Signature (All Three):**

| Feature | G-ALYP | G-ALYY | G-ALYV |
|---------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Flight Phase** | Climb | Cruise | Climb |
| **Altitude** | 8,200 m | 10,700 m | 10,400 m |
| **Cabin ΔP** | ~20 kPa | ~27.6 kPa | ~27 kPa |
| **Fracture Origin** | ADF window corner (forward roof) | Presumed identical | ADF window corner (forward roof) |
| **Crack Direction** | Longitudinal (along fuselage) | Presumed | Longitudinal |
| **Failure Mode** | Explosive decompression → dynamic breakup | Same | Same |
| **Cycles to Failure** | ~1,300 | ~1,100 | ~1,200 |

**Critical Insight:** All three failed at **~1,200 pressurization cycles** — **less than 12% of the assumed 10,000-cycle design life**. The square window corners with rivet holes created a **fatigue crack initiation site** that propagated to critical length in ~1,200 cycles.
