### 12. ATOMIC AND CELLULAR MANIFESTATIONS OF EXTREME PRESSURIZATION

#### 12.1 The Meyer-Overton Hypothesis and Inert Gas Narcosis

**12.1.1 Historical Foundation (Meyer 1899, Overton 1901)**

**Meyer's Observation (1899):**
> "The narcotic potency of an anesthetic gas is proportional to its solubility in lipids."

**Overton's Extension (1901):**
> "The olive oil/gas partition coefficient ($\lambda_{oil/gas}$) predicts narcotic potency across diverse chemical species."

**The Meyer-Overton Correlation:**
$$\text{ED}_{50} \propto \frac{1}{\lambda_{oil/gas}}$$

Where $\text{ED}_{50}$ = partial pressure producing 50% narcotic effect (immobility in tadpoles, loss of righting reflex).

**12.1.2 Olive Oil/Gas Partition Coefficients (Key Data)**

| Gas | $\lambda_{oil/gas}$ (25°C) | Relative Potency (1/$\lambda$) | Narcotic Depth Equivalence |
|-----|---------------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Helium (He)** | 0.015 | **1.0** (baseline) | Non-narcotic |
| **Neon (Ne)** | 0.045 | 0.33 | 3× He |
| **Hydrogen (H₂)** | 0.055 | 0.27 | 3.7× He |
| **Nitrogen (N₂)** | 0.067 | **0.22** | **1.0 (reference)** |

**Key Insight:** **6 orders of magnitude range** in potency, perfectly correlated with lipid solubility ($R^2 > 0.99$).

**12.1.3 Narcotic Potency of Nitrogen — The "Rapture of the Deep"**

**Nitrogen at Depth:**
- **Partial Pressure at 30 msw (4 bar):** $P_{N_2} = 0.78 \times 4 = 3.12$ bar
- **At 60 msw (7 bar):** $P_{N_2} = 5.46$ bar
- **At 90 msw (10 bar):** $P_{N_2} = 7.8$ bar

**Meyer-Overton Prediction:**
$$\text{Narcotic Effect} \propto P_{N_2} \times \lambda_{oil/gas}$$

**Equivalent Narcotic Depth (END) for Trimix:**
$$\text{END} = \frac{P_{N_2} + P_{Ar} + \dots}{0.79} \quad \text{(at surface, air = 0.79 N₂)}$$

**12.1.4 The Critical Assumption: Lipid Phase Partitioning**

**Meyer-Overton Implies:**
1. Narcosis is a **physical** (not chemical) interaction
2. **Site of action = lipid bilayer** of neuronal membranes
3. **No specific receptor** — generic membrane perturbation
4. **Cutoff effect:** Very long-chain alkanes (high $\lambda$) lose narcotic effect → **size exclusion** from lipid bilayer

**Cutoff Evidence:**
- n-Alkanes: Potency increases with chain length (C1–C10)
- **C11–C12:** Peak potency
- **C13+:** **Sharp drop** in potency (despite higher $\lambda$)
- **Interpretation:** Molecules too large to partition into lipid bilayer core
