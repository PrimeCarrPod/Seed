#### 13.5 Hydrogen as Alternative (Hydreliox)

**13.5.1 Hydrogen Properties vs. Helium**

| Property | Hydrogen (H₂) | Helium (He) | Advantage |
|----------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Molecular Weight** | 2 g/mol | 4 g/mol | **2× lighter** |
| **Density (at 300 msw)** | 0.016 g/L | 0.032 g/L | **Half the density** |
| **Viscosity** | 9 μPa·s | 20 μPa·s | **Lower WOB** |
| **Thermal Conductivity** | 0.18 W/m·K | 0.15 W/m·K | Better cooling |
| **Lipid Solubility (λ)** | 0.055 | 0.015 | **Higher → more narcotic** |
| **Narcotic Potency** | 3.7× He | Baseline | **Mild narcosis** |
| **HPNS Suppression** | **Better than He** | Baseline | **Less HPNS** |
| **Flammability** | **Explosive (4–75% in O₂)** | Inert | **Major hazard** |

**13.5.2 Hydreliox (H₂/He/O₂) — The "Holy Grail" Mix**

**Concept:** Replace part of He with H₂ to:
1. **Reduce density** → lower Work of Breathing (WOB)
2. **Reduce thermal conductivity** → less heat loss
3. **Add mild narcosis** → counteracts HPNS (like N₂ but different)

**Optimal Hydreliox (Experimental, COMEX/NEDU):**
- **H₂:** 10–15%
- **He:** 50–60%
- **O₂:** 0.45 bar ppO₂
- **Balance:** He (and sometimes N₂)

**Hydreliox at 450 msw (COMEX Hydra 10, 1992):**
- **Mix:** H₂ 12% / He 58% / O₂ 30% (at 46 bar)
- **Depth:** 450 msw (46 bar)
- **Duration:** 8 hours at depth
- **Result:** **No HPNS, WOB 40% lower than He, mild euphoria only**

**13.5.3 Hydrogen Biophysics — Narcosis vs. HPNS**

**H₂ Lipid Solubility:** λ = 0.055 (between N₂ 0.067 and He 0.015)
**Bilayer Effect: MODERATE EXPANSION (between N₂ and He)**

**Bilayer Effect Comparison:**
| Gas | λ | ΔA/A₀ (per 100 bar) | Effect |
|-----|---|---------------------|--------|
| **N₂** | 0.067 | +18% | Strong expansion |
| **H₂** | 0.055 | +15% | Moderate expansion |
| **He** | 0.015 | -2% | Slight compression |

**H₂ provides ~85% of N₂'s bilayer expansion!**
**Excellent HPNS counter with lower narcotic potency than N₂.**

**13.5.3 Hydrogen Safety — The Flammability Challenge**

**Flammability Limits (in O₂):**
- **Lower Explosive Limit (LEL):** 4% H₂ in O₂
- **Upper Explosive Limit (UEL):** 75% H₂ in O₂
- **Autoignition Temp:** 500°C (but catalytic surfaces lower it)

**At Depth (High Pressure):**
- **Flammability range WIDENS** with pressure
- **At 50 bar:** LEL ~2%, UEL ~80%
- **Any H₂ > 4% is potentially explosive in O₂!**

**Safety Protocols (COMEX/NEDU):**
1. **H₂ < 4% in O₂-rich zones** (loop, helmet)
2. **H₂ > 4% ONLY in He-diluted mix** (total mix < 4% in O₂)
3. **Catalytic Recombiners** (Pd/Pt) in gas loop
4. **Explosion-proof Electronics** (intrinsically safe)
5. **Continuous H₂ Monitoring** (catalytic bead, thermal conductivity)

**Hydreliox Safety Record:**
- **COMEX Hydra 10 (1992):** 450 msw, 12% H₂, **zero incidents**
- **NEDU H₂ Studies (1980s–90s):** Multiple dives to 450 msw, **zero incidents**
- **Key:** Strict gas management, catalytic recombiners, no ignition sources
