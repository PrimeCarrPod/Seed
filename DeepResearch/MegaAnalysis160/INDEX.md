## Aegis C Integration

All 160 parts operate under the **Aegis C** classification framework — the third-generation advanced fabrication protocol defined in CSMFAB001. Aegis C specifies:

- **Dielectric-First Construction:** All structural materials must be electrically non-conductive to survive Carrington-class electromagnetic events and lightning strikes during crustal-displacement storms. The LBFRP-001 composite satisfies this with basalt fiber (inherently dielectric) plus Elium thermoplastic resin.
- **Defense-in-Depth:** No single component may be the sole barrier between occupants and the external environment. Every habitation module, power system, and communication link must have at least three independent failure modes before catastrophic loss.
- **Phoenix Protocol Compliance:** All materials and assemblies must be recoverable, recyclable, or safely consumed at end-of-life, with ≥90% material recovery by mass. The Elium thermoplastic matrix enables re-melting and re-forming; lonsdaleite microcrystals are recovered via electrostatic separation.
- **S-Bus Beacon Standard:** Every deployed asset must carry an S-Bus RF beacon transmitting location, structural integrity, and occupancy data through ≥15 meters of debris coverage, operating on the 433 MHz ISM band with LoRa modulation for 5+ km range.
- **FEATHER Mesh Interoperability:** All systems must join the FEATHER wireless mesh network upon deployment, enabling autonomous coordination, resource sharing, and emergency failover between pods, vessels, and surface assets.
