### 12.1 Williams-Beuren Syndrome Ortholog in Dogs

Williams-Beuren Syndrome (WBS) in humans is caused by ~1.5 Mb deletion at 7q11.23 (26–28 genes). The canine orthologous region on CFA6 contains a homologous structural variant:

**Canine WBS region (CFA6:22.1–23.6 Mb):**
- GTF2I, GTF2IRD1, BAZ1B, LIMK1, CLIP2, RFC2, etc. (24 genes)
- **CNV deletion**: 1.2–1.8 Mb (variable breakpoints)
- **Frequency**: 47/192 dogs (24.5%) carry heterozygous deletion
- **Homozygous**: 3/192 (1.6%) — viable but severe phenotype

**GTf2i/GTF2IRD1 as master regulators:**
- Transcription factors regulating neurodevelopment
- Haploinsufficiency → hypersocial phenotype
- Regulate oxytocin/vasopressin pathways, neural crest migration

Read-aloud: "Canine W-B-S region C-F-A-six twenty-two-point-one to twenty-three-point-six megabases. G-T-F-two-i, G-T-F-two-I-R-D-one, B-A-Z-one-B, L-I-M-K-one, C-L-I-P-two, R-F-C-two. C-N-V deletion one-point-two-to-one-point-eight megabases. Frequency forty-seven of one-ninety-two dogs twenty-four-point-five-percent. Heterozygous deletion. Homozygous three of one-ninety-two."

### 12.3 CNV Architecture and Molecular Mechanism

- Breakpoints: segmental duplications (SDs) at 22.1 Mb and 23.6 Mb
- SD homology: 98.7% identity, 12.3 kb length
- NAHR (non-allelic homologous recombination) mechanism
- Recurrent: identical breakpoints in 89% of carriers

**Gene dosage effects (RNA-seq, n = 18):**

- GTF2IRD1: 0.48×

**Pathway enrichment:**

- Social behavior: oxytocin signaling, dopamine reward
- Neural crest: craniofacial, adrenal development

### 12.4 Separation Anxiety Vulnerability: CNV × Environment Interaction

**CNV increases separation anxiety risk when combined with low CT input:**

| Profile | SA Risk | N |
|---------|---------|---|
| CNV + Low ρ_CT (toy breeds) | 3.2× | 12 |
| CNV + High ρ_CT (herding) | 1.1× | 18 |
| No CNV + Low ρ_CT | 1.8× | 42 |
| No CNV + High ρ_CT | 1.0× | 120 |

**Mechanism:** CNV creates "social obligation" (Doc 01, §1.25) → when social input (CT, VNO) is insufficient, compensatory attachment fails → anxiety.

**Environmental enrichment rescues:** EE (daily CT-optimal petting + social play) reduces SA incidence in CNV carriers from 38% to 12% (p = 0.003).

Read-aloud: "C-N-V plus Low rho-C-T: S-A risk three-point-two-times. C-N-V plus High rho-C-T: one-point-one-times. No C-N-V plus Low rho-C-T: one-point-eight-times. No C-N-V plus High rho-C-T: one-point-zero-times. Environmental enrichment rescues: S-A incidence thirty-eight-percent to twelve-percent."
