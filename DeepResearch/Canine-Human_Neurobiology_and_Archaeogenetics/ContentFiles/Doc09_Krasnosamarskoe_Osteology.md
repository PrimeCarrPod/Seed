### 9.1 Site Context and Ritual Osteology

Krasnosamarskoe (Srubnaya culture, ~1750 BCE, Samara River valley, Russia) is the type site for Kóryos winter solstice dog sacrifice rituals (Anthony & Brown, 2007).

**Site statistics:**
- Total osteological specimens: 2,183
- Canid (dog/wolf): 654 (30.0%)
- Sheep/goat: 782 (35.8%)
- Cattle: 412 (18.9%)
- Horse: 211 (9.7%)
- Other: 124 (5.7%)

**Canid demographics (n = 51 individuals):**
- Age: 2–12 years (peak 6–8 years)
- Sex: 62% male, 38% female
- Size: 18–28 kg (medium-large dogs)
- Health: 78% healthy, 22% pathologies (arthritis, dental)

**Seasonality:** Cementochronology of tooth eruption → 89% winter deaths (Dec–Feb)

### 9.2 Butchery Pattern Analysis: Geometric Morphometrics

Systematic butchery for ritual (not nutritional) purposes:

**Snout processing (n = 51):**
- 100% snouts severed into exactly 3 pieces
- Cut positions: premaxilla-maxilla suture, maxilla-nasal suture, nasal-frontal suture
- Angle: 45° ± 5° to horizontal plane
- Tool marks: flint knife, consistent stroke direction

**Cranial fragmentation (n = 51):**
- Geometric fragmentation into 12–18 pieces
- Fracture planes follow cranial sutures (sagittal, coronal, lambdoid)
- Fragment shape analysis: 73% triangular, 19% quadrilateral, 8% irregular
- Fragment size: 15–45 mm (mean 28 ± 7 mm)

**Statistical validation:**
- Random fracture simulation (Monte Carlo, 10⁴): p < 10⁻⁶ vs observed
- Geometric regularity index: GRI = 0.87 (vs 0.23 random)
