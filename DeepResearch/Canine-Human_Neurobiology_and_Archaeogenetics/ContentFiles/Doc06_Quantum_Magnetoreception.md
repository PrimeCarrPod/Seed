### 6.1 Cryptochrome Photophysics and Radical Pair Formation

Cryptochrome 1a (Cry1a) is a blue-light photoreceptor (peak sensitivity 450 nm) expressed in the outer segments of UV/blue-sensitive S1 cone photoreceptors in canine retina. Upon blue photon absorption:

FAD + hν (450 nm) → FAD* (excited singlet)
FAD* + Trp (conserved tryptophan triad) → [FAD•⁻ Trp•⁺] (spin-correlated radical pair)   (1)

The radical pair (RP) is born in a singlet state (S). The electron spins evolve under the spin Hamiltonian:

Ĥ = J Ŝ₁⋅Ŝ₂ + Σᵢ Ŝᵢ⋅Aᵢ⋅Îᵢ + γₑ B₀⋅(Ŝ₁ + Ŝ₂)   (2)

where J = exchange interaction (~1 mT), Aᵢ = hyperfine coupling tensors, γₑ = electron gyromagnetic ratio, B₀ = geomagnetic field (50 μT). The singlet-triplet interconversion rate k_ST is exquisitely sensitive to B₀ alignment.
