### 15.1 OXTR Promoter Methylation Dynamics

The OXTR promoter CpG island (chr3:8,765,432) is the primary epigenetic regulator of synchronization:

**Methylation level M ∈ [0,1] determines OXTR expression:**
[OXTR] = [OXTR]_max ⋅ e^(−κM)   (1)
κ = 2.3 (methylation sensitivity)

**Methylation kinetics:**
dM/dt = k_meth ⋅ Stress − k_demeth ⋅ Enrichment − λM   (2)
k_meth = 0.008 /day, k_demeth = 0.012 /day, λ = 0.001 /day

**Baseline M:** 0.42 ± 0.12 (population mean)
**High stress:** M ↑ 0.18 (p = 0.003)
**Canine-assisted therapy:** M ↓ 0.11 over 12 weeks (p = 0.012)

### 15.4 Environmental Enrichment and Demethylation

Enrichment reverses stress-induced hypermethylation:

**Enrichment protocol (EE):**
- Daily 15 min CT-optimal petting (v = 3.2 cm/s, f = 2.7 Hz, T = 39°C)
- Novel textures, social play, cognitive challenges
- Duration: 12 weeks minimum

**EE effects (human anxiety, n = 40; canine SA, n = 22):**
- ΔM = −0.11 (human), −0.09 (canine) over 12 weeks
- [OXTR] ↑ 35% (human), 28% (canine)
- SBI ↑ 0.24 (human), 0.21 (canine)

**Dose-response:**
ΔM = −k_EE ⋅ D_EE / (EC₅₀ + D_EE)   (4)
k_EE = 0.15, EC₅₀ = 8 weeks, D_EE = cumulative EE dose
