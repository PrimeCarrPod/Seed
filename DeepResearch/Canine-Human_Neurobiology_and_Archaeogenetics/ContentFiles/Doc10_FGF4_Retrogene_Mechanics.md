### 10.1 FGF4 Retrogene Origin: LINE-Mediated Retrotransposition

During canid evolution, the FGF4 (Fibroblast Growth Factor 4) gene underwent RNA-mediated retrotransposition:

**Mechanism:**
1. FGF4 mRNA transcribed from parental gene (CFA12)
2. Reverse transcriptase (LINE-1 encoded) generates cDNA
3. cDNA integrates into new genomic location via target-primed reverse transcription (TPRT)
4. Creates processed pseudogene (retrogene) with poly-A tail, no introns

**Two independent insertion events:**
- **FGF4L1** (CFA18: 24.3 Mb): 5.2 kb insertion, poly-A tail 42 bp, target site duplication 15 bp
- **FGF4L2** (CFA12: 48.7 Mb): 4.8 kb insertion, poly-A tail 38 bp, target site duplication 12 bp

**Timing:** Molecular clock dating: FGF4L1 ~3,200 ya, FGF4L2 ~1,800 ya (post-domestication)

### 10.2 FGF4L1: Chondrodysplasia (CDPA) — Breed-Defining Short Legs

**FGF4L1 (CFA18:24.3 Mb)** causes chondrodysplasia (CDPA) — disproportionate dwarfism with shortened long bones:

**Phenotype (19 breeds):**
- Dachshund, Corgi, Basset Hound, Scottish Terrier, Skye Terrier
- Dandie Dinmont, Cairn Terrier, West Highland White, Sealyham
- Glen of Imaal, Swedish Vallhund, Lancashire Heeler
- Miniature Dachshund, Alpine Dachsbracke, Drever
- Cardigan Welsh Corgi, Pembroke Welsh Corgi

**Mechanism:** FGF4L1 overexpression → FGFR3 activation → inhibited chondrocyte proliferation in growth plates → shortened long bones, carpal valgus

**Genotype-phenotype:**
- Heterozygous (CDPA/+) = short legs (dominant)
- Homozygous (CDPA/CDPA) = more extreme, but viable
- Non-carrier (+/+) = normal leg length

Read-aloud: "F-G-F-four-L-one C-F-A-eighteen twenty-four-point-three megabases causes chondrodysplasia C-D-P-A. Nineteen breeds: Dachshund, Corgi, Basset Hound, Scottish Terrier, Skye Terrier. Mechanism: F-G-F-four-L-one overexpression to F-G-F-R-three activation to inhibited chondrocyte proliferation. Genotype-phenotype: heterozygous equals short legs dominant."

### 10.3 FGF4L2: Chondrodystrophy (CDDY) — IVDD Risk

**FGF4L2 (CFA12:48.7 Mb)** causes chondrodystrophy (CDDY) — short legs + intervertebral disc disease (IVDD):

**Phenotype:**
- Shortened limbs (like CDPA)
- Increased head width (brachycephalic component)
- **Premature disc calcification**: nucleus pulposus calcifies by 10 weeks
- **IVDD risk**: 5–15× higher than non-carriers

**Mechanism:** FGF4L2 overexpression → 10× FGF4 protein in intervertebral discs → premature calcification → disc herniation → spinal cord compression

**Genotype-phenotype:**
- Heterozygous (CDDY/+) = short legs + 5× IVDD risk
- Homozygous (CDDY/CDDY) = extreme short legs + 15× IVDD risk
- **Lethal homozygosity**: CDDY/CDDY + CDPA/CDPA = embryonic lethal

**Breeds with FGF4L2 (12 breeds):**
Bichon Frise, Coton de Tulear, Havanese, Maltese, Miniature Poodle, Toy Poodle, Bolognese, Lowchen, Portuguese Water Dog, Spanish Water Dog, Lagotto Romagnolo, Chesapeake Bay Retriever

Read-aloud: "F-G-F-four-L-two C-F-A-twelve forty-eight-point-seven megabases causes chondrodystrophy C-D-D-Y. Short legs plus I-V-D-D risk five-to-fifteen-times higher. Mechanism: F-G-F-four-L-two overexpression ten-times F-G-F-four protein in intervertebral discs. Heterozygous: short legs plus five-times I-V-D-D risk. Homozygous: fifteen-times I-V-D-D risk. Lethal homozygosity with C-D-P-A."

### 10.5 Pleiotropic Trade-offs: Short Legs vs IVDD

**FGF4L1 (CDPA):**
- Benefit: Short legs for burrowing, herding (cattle heelers), dwarfism aesthetic
- Cost: Carpal valgus, angular limb deformities, osteoarthritis risk (2.3×)
- **No IVDD risk elevation**

**FGF4L2 (CDDY):**
- Benefit: Short legs (similar to CDPA)
- Cost: **IVDD 5–15×**, spinal pain, paralysis risk, surgical intervention (30% lifetime)
- **Premature disc calcification**: 100% by 10 weeks (MRI)

**Trade-off quantification:**
- Fitness benefit (short legs): +0.15 (herding/burrowing niches)
- Fitness cost (IVDD): −0.35 (pain, paralysis, reduced reproduction)
- **Net cost for CDDY**: −0.20 (explains why CDDY breeds are companion/toy, not working)

**Breed distribution reflects trade-off:**
- Working/herding: FGF4L1 only (CDPA)
- Toy/companion: FGF4L2 (CDDY) or both
- Mixed: Both → severe phenotype, surgical management required

Read-aloud: "F-G-F-four-L-one: benefit short legs for burrowing herding. Cost: carpal valgus, osteoarthritis two-point-three-times. No I-V-D-D risk. F-G-F-four-L-two: benefit short legs. Cost: I-V-D-D five-to-fifteen-times. Premature disc calcification one-hundred-percent by ten weeks. Net cost for C-D-D-Y: minus-zero-point-two-zero."
