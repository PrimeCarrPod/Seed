# Document 3: Extracellular Marine Hemoglobin - M101/HEMO2life
## Piece 10/18: Hemoxycarrier — Systemic Intravenous Development and Preclinical Efficacy

Building on the organ preservation success of HEMO2life, Hemarina is advancing Hemoxycarrier® — a systemic intravenous formulation of M101 for acute indications including hemorrhagic shock, ischemic stroke, and entrapment rescue. This piece details the preclinical development, formulation challenges, and efficacy data for systemic M101.

**Formulation Development — Hemoxycarrier**:
*Concentration*: 10 g/dL (100 mg/mL) — 100× HEMO2life concentration.
*Buffer*: Modified lactated Ringer's (NaCl 130, KCl 4, CaCl₂ 1.5, Na lactate 28, pH 7.4) with 5 mM ascorbate (stabilizer).
*Osmolarity*: 290 mOsm/kg (iso-osmotic).
*Viscosity at 10 g/dL*: 4.5 cP (37°C) — higher than Hemopure (3.5 cP) due to larger size, but shear-thinning.
*Colloidal Stability*: No aggregation at 37°C, 1000 s⁻¹ shear, 6 months at 25°C.
*Sterility*: 0.22 μm filtration, endotoxin <0.25 EU/mL.
*Container*: 100 mL, 250 mL, 500 mL PVC-free bags (EVA multilayer).
*Shelf Life Target*: 24 months at 2-8°C, 12 months at 25°C (ongoing stability studies).

**Concentration Challenge**: 
M101 solubility limit ~150 mg/mL (15 g/dL) due to high charge density and hydration shell. At 10 g/dL, viscosity manageable; at 15 g/dL, viscosity >10 cP (impairs infusion). Hemarina developing:
1. **Lyophilized formulation**: 20 g/dL equivalent on reconstitution (like ErythroMer).
2. **High-concentrate liquid**: 15 g/dL with viscosity modifiers (low MW PEG).
3. **Powder-in-bag**: Dual-chamber with diluent.

**Preclinical Efficacy — Hemorrhagic Shock Models**:

*Rat Model (Controlled Hemorrhage, 40% Blood Volume, 60 min Shock)*:
- Groups: Sham, Shock+Saline, Shock+Hemopure (10 mL/kg), Shock+Hemoxycarrier (10 mL/kg of 10 g/dL).
- Resuscitation to MAP 60 mmHg.
- **6h Survival**: Saline 20%, Hemopure 55%, Hemoxycarrier 85% (p<0.01 vs Hemopure).
- **Lactate at 6h**: Saline 12 mM, Hemopure 6 mM, Hemoxycarrier 2.5 mM (p<0.01).
- **Tissue PO₂ (muscle, microelectrode)**: Saline 8 mmHg, Hemopure 18 mmHg, Hemoxycarrier 32 mmHg.
- **MAP Response**: Hemopure +25 mmHg (transient hypertension), Hemoxycarrier +3 mmHg (stable).
- **Renal Function (Creatinine 24h)**: Hemopure 1.8 mg/dL, Hemoxycarrier 0.6 mg/dL (baseline 0.4).
- **Mechanism**: Hemoxycarrier's high O₂ affinity loads in lungs (even at low PaO₂), SOD prevents reperfusion oxidative burst, no vasoconstriction allows microvascular perfusion.

*Rat Model (Uncontrolled Hemorrhage, Liver Laceration, No Resuscitation Fluid)*:
- Hemoxycarrier (5 mL/kg) vs Saline vs Hemopure.
- **Time to Death**: Saline 45 min, Hemopure 75 min, Hemoxycarrier >180 min (censored).
- **Hemorrhage Volume**: Hemoxycarrier reduced ongoing bleeding 40% vs saline (preserved coagulation, no platelet activation).
- **Coagulation (TEG)**: Hemopure MA -20%, Hemoxycarrier MA unchanged.

*Pig Model (Class III Hemorrhage, 30% Blood Volume, 2h Shock, then Resuscitation)*:
- Hemoxycarrier 15 mL/kg (1.5 g/kg Hb) vs Hemopure 15 mL/kg vs Blood (autologous).
- **24h Survival**: Blood 90%, Hemoxycarrier 80%, Hemopure 50%.
- **Organ Dysfunction (SOFA score)**: Blood 2, Hemoxycarrier 3, Hemopure 6.
- **Inflammatory Cytokines (IL-6, 6h)**: Blood 50 pg/mL, Hemoxycarrier 80 pg/mL, Hemopure 300 pg/mL.
- **Microvascular Perfusion (Sublingual SDF imaging)**: Hemoxycarrier near-baseline; Hemopure heterogeneous.

**Preclinical Efficacy — Ischemic Stroke (MCAO Model)**:
- Rat transient MCAO (90 min occlusion, reperfusion).
- Hemoxycarrier (5 mL/kg IV at reperfusion) vs Saline vs Hemopure.
- **Infarct Volume (TTC, 24h)**: Saline 180 mm³, Hemopure 150 mm³, Hemoxycarrier 70 mm³ (p<0.01).
- **Neurological Score (mNSS)**: Saline 14, Hemopure 11, Hemoxycarrier 5 (0=normal).
- **BBB Permeability (Evans Blue)**: Saline 1.0, Hemopure 0.8, Hemoxycarrier 0.3 (μg/g).
- **Mechanism**: M101's high affinity loads in penumbra (PaO₂ low), SOD protects against reperfusion oxidative injury, no vasoconstriction preserves collateral flow.

**Preclinical Efficacy — Crush Syndrome/Compartment Syndrome**:
- Rat hindlimb compression 4h, then release + resuscitation.
- Hemoxycarrier vs Hemopure vs Saline.
- **Rhabdomyolysis (CK 24h)**: Saline 50,000, Hemopure 30,000, Hemoxycarrier 12,000 U/L.
- **AKI (Creatinine 48h)**: Saline 2.5, Hemopure 1.8, Hemoxycarrier 0.7 mg/dL.
- **Survival 7d**: Saline 40%, Hemopure 60%, Hemoxycarrier 90%.
- **Mechanism**: M101 penetrates compressed microvasculature (small size, no deformation needed), SOD prevents myoglobin/heme oxidative synergy, no vasoconstriction.

**Entrapment-Specific Model — Combined Hypoxia/Hypercapnia/Ischemia**:
*Custom Rat Model*: 
1. Tracheal occlusion (apnea) + 10% O₂ breathing (hypoxia) + CO₂ rebreathing (PaCO₂ 80 mmHg) for 30 min.
2. Simultaneous femoral artery occlusion (limb ischemia) for 60 min.
3. Resuscitation with Hemoxycarrier (10 mL/kg) vs Hemopure vs Saline at minute 30.
4. Reperfusion at minute 90.

*Results*:
- **Survival to 24h**: Saline 0%, Hemopure 30%, Hemoxycarrier 80%.
- **Neurological Outcome**: Hemoxycarrier near-normal; Hemopure severe deficits.
- **Lactate Clearance**: Hemoxycarrier normalized by 6h; Hemopure persistent >5 mM.
- **Lung Injury (Wet/Dry, BAL protein)**: Hemopure 2× Hemoxycarrier (vasoconstriction → pulmonary hypertension).

**Safety Pharmacology (GLP, Dog and Rat)**:
- **Cardiovascular (Dog, telemetry)**: 3× dose (30 mL/kg) — MAP +5 mmHg, HR -10 bpm, QT unchanged. No arrhythmias.
- **Respiratory (Rat, plethysmography)**: No respiratory depression (unlike THAM).
- **CNS (Rat, Irwin screen)**: No behavioral changes at 10× dose.
- **Renal (Rat, 28-day repeat dose)**: No tubular casts, no creatinine rise at 5 g/kg/week.
- **Coagulation (Dog)**: PT, aPTT, fibrinogen, platelets normal at 3× dose.
- **Immunogenicity (Rat/Dog, 4 weekly doses)**: No anti-M101 antibodies.
- **Local Tolerance**: Perivascular injection — no necrosis (pH 7.4, non-alkaline).

**Regulatory Pathway — IND Application (Projected 2026)**:
- IND for hemorrhagic shock (trauma) with entrapment as secondary.
- Animal Rule applicability: Efficacy in pig hemorrhage + rat entrapment model.
- Safety database: GLP tox (rat 28-day, dog 14-day), safety pharmacology, genotoxicity.
- CMC: Aquaculture GMP, viral clearance validation, stability.
- Clinical Phase 1: Healthy volunteers (safety, PK), then trauma patients (expanded access).

**Comparison with ErythroMer for Entrapment**:
| Feature | Hemoxycarrier (M101) | ErythroMer |
|---------|---------------------|------------|
| O₂ Affinity (P50) | 8-12 mmHg (high) | 25-30 mmHg (tunable) |
| Bohr Effect | Minimal | Full (KC1003) |
| SOD Activity | Intrinsic (Cu/Zn) | Formulation antioxidants |
| Sulfide Resistance | High | None |
| NO Scavenging | Minimal (intrinsic) | None (lipid barrier) |
| Size | 25 nm (molecular) | 200 nm (particle) |
| Microvascular Access | Excellent (small) | Excellent (deformable) |
| Shelf Life (liquid) | 24 months at 2-8°C | 36 months lyophilized |
| Manufacturing | Aquaculture (biological) | Microfluidics (synthetic) |
| Development Stage | IND-enabling | Phase 1 ready |

**Conclusion**: Hemoxycarrier demonstrates superior survival and organ protection in preclinical entrapment-relevant models compared to Hemopure and saline. Its intrinsic SOD activity, sulfide resistance, and minimal NO scavenging address the core pathophysiological triad of entrapment (hypoxia, oxidative stress, toxic gas accumulation). The main limitation — high O₂ affinity with minimal Bohr effect — is mitigated in entrapment where tissue PO₂ is extremely low (<10 mmHg), allowing even high-affinity Hb to unload oxygen. For scenarios with less severe hypoxemia but significant acidosis, combination with ErythroMer or allosteric modification of M101 would be optimal.
