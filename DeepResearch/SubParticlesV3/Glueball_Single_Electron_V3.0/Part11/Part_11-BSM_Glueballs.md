## 1. Supersymmetric Glueballs

### 1.1 SU(N|N) Supermultiplet

The supersymmetric glueball spectrum includes fermionic partners:

- **Scalar glueball** $G_S$: $J^{PC} = 0^{++}$
- **Dilatino** $\tilde{G}$: $J^P = 0^-$ (Majorana fermion)
- **Tensor glueball** $G_T$: $J^{PC} = 2^{++}$
- **Gravitino** $\tilde{G}$: $J^P = 3/2^-$

### 1.2 Mass Relations from Superalgebra

The supersymmetry algebra:

$$\{ Q_\alpha, \bar{Q}_{\dot{\beta}} \} = 2(\sigma^\mu)_{\alpha\dot{\beta}} P_\mu$$

implies the mass relation for the supermultiplet:

$$M_{\tilde{G}}^2 = M_G^2 + 2m_{\text{D}}\left(m_{\text{D}} - M_G\right)$$

where $m_{\text{D}}$ is the dilaton mass.

## 2. Extra-Dimensional Glueballs

### 2.1 Kaluza-Klein Tower

In a 5D spacetime with compactification radius $R$:

$$M_n^2 = M_0^2 + \left( \frac{n}{R} \right)^2$$

for $n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$

### 2.2 Warped Geometry

In Randall-Sundrum model with warp factor $e^{-2ky}$:

$$M_G^{(n)} = k \left( 1 + kR \right) e^{-k\pi R} n$$
