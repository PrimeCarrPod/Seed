## 1. The Microbial Loop Theory

### 1.1 Recycled Carbon Pathway

$$\text{DOM} \rightarrow \text{Bacteria} \rightarrow \text{Bacterial Biomass} \rightarrow \text{Protozoa} \rightarrow \text{Dissolved Organic Carbon}$$

The loop efficiency:

$$\eta = \frac{\text{Bacterial biomass}_{\text{recycled}}}{\text{Total DOM input}} \approx 0.67$$

### 1.2 Microbial Biomass C:N:P Ratios

Redfield ratio: C:N:P = 106:16:1

Dissolved organic matter to biomass conversion:

$$Y = \frac{\text{Biomass produced}}{\text{Substrate consumed}} \approx 0.3$$

## 2. Saprophytic Network Topology

### 2.1 Hyphal Network Dynamics

Mycelial network growth rate:

$$\frac{dL}{dt} = \lambda \cdot v_{\text{tip}}$$

where $L$ is hyphal length, $\lambda$ colonization density, $v_{\text{tip}}$ tip extension velocity.

### 2.2 Enzyme Production and Diffusion

$$\frac{\partial C}{\partial t} = D \nabla^2 C - k_{\text{cat}} E C$$

with Michaelis-Menten kinetics for extracellular enzymes.
