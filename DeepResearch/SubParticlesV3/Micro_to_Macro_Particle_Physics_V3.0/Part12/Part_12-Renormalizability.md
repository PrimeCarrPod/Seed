# V3.0 Deep Dive Series: Micro to Macro Particle Physics
## Part 12 of 14 — Renormalizability and Critical Dimensions

---

### Abstract

This installment examines renormalizability criteria and critical dimensions in quantum field theories, connecting ultraviolet behavior to macroscopic observables.

---

## 1. Power Counting and Superficial Degrees of Freedom

### 1.1 Engineering Dimension

For an interaction $\mathcal{L}_{\text{int}} \sim g \phi^n$:

$$[\phi] = \frac{d-2+\sqrt{(d-2)^2 + 4m}}{2}$$

### 1.2 Dimless Coupling

The coupling is dimensionless when:

$$d_{\text{crit}} = \frac{4}{n-2}$$

For $\phi^4$: $d_{\text{crit}} = 4$.

---

## 2. Epsilon Expansion

### 2.1 Expansion Around Upper Critical Dimension

Near $d = 4-\epsilon$:

$$[\lambda] = \epsilon + O(\epsilon^0)$$

The beta function order by order:

$$\beta(\lambda) = -\epsilon \lambda + \frac{3\lambda^2}{16\pi^2} + O(\epsilon^0, \lambda^3)$$

---

## 3. Wilson-Fisher Fixed Point

The non-trivial fixed point:

$$\lambda^* = \frac{16\pi^2 \epsilon}{3} + O(\epsilon^2)$$

with critical exponents:

$$\nu = \frac{1}{2\epsilon} + O(\epsilon^0)$$
