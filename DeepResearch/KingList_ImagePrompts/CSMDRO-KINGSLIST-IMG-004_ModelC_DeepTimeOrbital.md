### CLUSTER 1 (Left): MILANKOVITCH ORBITAL ARCHITECTURE — THE CELESTIAL CLOCK

**Center Hex (C1-Hex-0,0):** THREE ORBITAL PARAMETERS — 3D PHASE SPACE VISUALIZATION
- **Eccentricity (e):** Shape of Earth's orbit — 0.005 to 0.058 — periods: 413 kyr (primary), 95 kyr, 125 kyr
  - Current: e = 0.0167 — near circular — low seasonal contrast
  - At MPT (~900 kyr): e modulation shifted — 413 kyr cycle dominant
- **Obliquity (ε):** Axial tilt — 22.1° to 24.5° — period: 41 kyr
  - Current: ε = 23.44° — decreasing — less seasonal contrast
  - At MPT: 41 kyr cycle dominated pre-MPT glacial cycles
- **Precession (ω):** Axial wobble — period: 23 kyr + 19 kyr = 21 kyr average
  - Modulates seasonal insolation at given latitude
  - Precession index: e·sin(ω) — combines eccentricity + precession
- **3D Phase Space:** (e, ε, e·sinω) — Lissajous trajectory — 1.2 Myr shown
- Micro-hex: 7 orbital states at key epochs (MPT, YDIH, Holocene, LGM, Eemian, MIS 11, Present)

**C1-Hex-1,0:** ECCENTRICITY — THE WEAK PACER
- 413 kyr cycle: Jupiter-Saturn great inequality — most stable — "metronome"
- 95 & 125 kyr: Inner planet perturbations — weaker — but 100 kyr glacial cycle emerges!
- **PARADOX:** 100 kyr glacial cycle NOT in eccentricity spectrum (too weak) — UCSB 2025 solves this

- Micro-hex: 7 eccentricity maxima/minima in last 1 Myr — dates, e values, insolation anomaly

**C1-Hex-1,1:** OBLIQUITY — THE 41 KYR PACER (PRE-MPT DOMINANT)
- 41 kyr cycle: Planetary torque on equatorial bulge — stable
- High obliquity → more summer insolation at poles → ice melt → interglacial
- Pre-MPT: Glacial cycles locked to 41 kyr obliquity — "linear response"
- Post-MPT: 41 kyr signal still present but SUBORDINATE to 100 kyr
- Micro-hex: 7 obliquity maxima — correlation with pre-MPT terminations

**C1-Hex-1,2:** PRECESSION — THE 23/19 KYR PACER — SEASONAL REDISTRIBUTOR

- When e high → precession strong → large seasonal contrast at perihelion/aphelion

**C1-Hex-1,3:** INSOLATION AT 65°N — THE GLACIAL THERMOSTAT

**C1-Hex-1,4:** UCSB 2025 THRESHOLD MECHANISM — THE 100 KYR RIDDLE SOLVED
- **Core discovery:** Ice sheet volume threshold ~30 million km³
- Below threshold: Linear response to 41 kyr obliquity — small ice sheets, fast response
- Above threshold: Nonlinear feedback loops activate:
  1. Ice-albedo feedback: More ice → higher albedo → cooler → more ice
  2. CO₂ feedback: Cold ocean absorbs CO₂ → lower greenhouse → cooler
  3. Ocean circulation: AMOC slowdown → heat transport reduction → cooler poles
  4. Bedrock depression: Ice weight depresses crust → lower surface elevation → warmer base → faster flow
  5. Dust-ice albedo: Glacial dust on ice → lower albedo → faster melt at termination
- **Result:** Weak 100 kyr eccentricity signal AMPLIFIED by nonlinear system into dominant 100 kyr cycle
- **MPT = Crossing of ~30M km³ threshold** — ~900–800 kyr ago
- Micro-hex: 7 feedback loops — equations, timescales, magnitudes, proxies, modeling, validation, references

**C1-Hex-1,5:** SILICATE DUST FEEDBACK — ANTARCTIC CORE 2025 CONFIRMATION

- Glacial: Dust 20–50× interglacial — Patagonian source — fertilizes Southern Ocean → CO₂ drawdown
- Interglacial: Low dust — CO₂ release
