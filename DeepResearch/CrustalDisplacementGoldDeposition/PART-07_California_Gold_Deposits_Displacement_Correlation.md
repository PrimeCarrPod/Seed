## 7.2 Mother Lode Quartz Vein System: Displacement Reactivation

### 7.2.1 Mother Lode Geometry and Gold Endowment

**Mother Lode Belt:** 190 km long, 1–5 km wide, **NW-SE striking** (parallel to Melones Fault Zone)
**Total historical production:** ~3,000 tonnes Au (1848–2000)
**Major districts (NW to SE):** Alleghany, Downieville, Washington, Nevada City, Grass Valley, Coloma, Placerville, Jackson, Angels Camp, Sonora, Jamestown, Coulterville

**Vein characteristics:**
- **Host:** Melones Fault Zone (MFZ) — **crustal-scale suture** (Smartville Complex ophiolite vs. continental margin)

- **Gold:** Native Au (850–950 fine), **5–50 g/t** in shoots, **0.1–1 g/t** background

## 7.3 Tertiary Auriferous Gravels: Mega-Tsunami Recession Deposits

### 7.3.3 Key Locality Data: Tuolumne Table Mountain (Type Section)

**Coordinates:** UTM 10N: 745,210 E, 4,189,320 N (Sonora 7.5' quad)
**Elevation:** 450 m (1,475 ft)
**Age:** 9.5 Ma (K-Ar on capping basalt, Wagner et al., 1956)
**Thickness:** 60–100 m gravel, capped by 30 m latite

**Stratigraphy (bottom to top):**
1. **Basal lag:** 2–5 m, **boulders 1–3 m, NO GOLD** (inundation stripping surface)

- **Foraminifera:** *Globigerina*, *Bolivina* (planktonic/benthic marine)
- **Shark teeth:** *Carcharodon*, *Isurus* (Eocene–Miocene marine)

**IMPOSSIBLE for fluvial deposit at 450 m elevation, 200 km from paleo-coast.**
