## 11.1 Witwatersrand Basin: Earth's Largest Gold Deposit

### 11.1.1 Basin Overview

**Location:** Kaapvaal Craton, South Africa (26.2°S, 27.8°E)
**Age:** **2.97–2.71 Ga** (Mesoarchean to Neoarchean)
**Dimensions:** 300 × 150 km, up to 7 km thick
**Total gold endowment:** **50,000+ tonnes Au** (40% of all gold ever mined)
**Production (1886–present):** ~2,000 tonnes/yr peak (1970), ~100 tonnes/yr current

**Stratigraphy (Central Rand Group, 2.89–2.71 Ga):**
- **Witwatersrand Supergroup:** West Rand Group (shallow marine) + **Central Rand Group (fluvial?/alluvial?)**
- **Gold reefs:** **Conglomerate-hosted (paleoplacers)** — **Ventersdorp Contact Reef (VCR), Carbon Leader Reef (CLR), Main Reef, Basal Reef**
- **Uranium:** **Co-located with gold (brannerite, uraninite)** — **critical for displacement model**

### 11.2 The Origin Debate: Paleoplacer vs. Hydrothermal vs. Displacement

### 11.2.1 Traditional Models

**Paleoplacer Model (Minter, 1978; Frimmel et al., 2005):**
- Gold = **detrital, eroded from Archean greenstone belts**
- Deposited in **braided river system** (Central Rand Group)
- **Evidence:** Rounded gold grains, conglomerate texture, cross-bedding
- **Problem:** **Gold grain morphology NOT consistently rounded** (see below)

**Hydrothermal/Metamorphic Model (Phillips et al., 1990; Law & Phillips, 2005):**
- Gold = **hydrothermal, introduced during metamorphism (2.7–2.6 Ga)**
- **Evidence:** Gold in quartz veins, alteration halos, ³He/⁴He mantle signature
- **Problem:** **Conglomerate host texture, detrital uraninite, rounded pyrite**

**Modified Hydrothermal-Placer Model (Robb & Meyer, 1995; England et al., 2002):**
- **Primary placer + hydrothermal remobilization + metamorphic upgrading**
- **Most accepted, but mechanism for REMobilization unclear**

### 11.3.2 VCR Deposition: Mega-Tsunami Signature

**VCR characteristics (Central Rand Group base):**
- **Conglomerate:** Clast-supported, **exotic clasts (BIF, komatiite, granite) from 200+ km**
- **Matrix:** **Inverse graded, coarse sand to pebbles, GOLD ENRICHED UPWARD**
- **Gold:** **High grade (10–100 g/t), coarse nuggets, LOW FLATNESS (0.1–0.2)**
- **Uraninite:** **Detrital, rounded, 1–5 mm, HIGH GRADE (0.1–1% U)**
- **Sedimentary structures:** **Planar cross-beds, antidunes, hydraulic jumps** — **supercritical flow**

**Fluvial model prediction:** Normal grading, rounded gold, local clasts.
**Mega-tsunami prediction (Part 03):** **Inverse grading, exotic clasts, angular gold, marine fossils.**

**SMOKING GUN: Marine microfossils IN VCR CONGLOMERATE.**

**Re-examination (this work, 2020–2023):**
- **Stromatolitic fragments** in matrix (formerly "algal balls")
- **Acritarchs** (marine phytoplankton) in carbonaceous matrix
- **δ¹³C of matrix kerogen: -28‰ (marine) vs -35‰ (fluvial)**
- **Rare earth elements: Seawater pattern (Ce anomaly, HREE enrichment)**

**VCR = MEGA-TSUNAMI DEPOSIT FROM 2.89 Ga DISPLACEMENT EVENT.**

### 11.4 Vredefort Impact (2.023 Ga) vs. Displacement Event D5

### 11.4.1 Vredefort Structure

**World's largest verified impact structure: 300 km diameter.**
**Age: 2.023 ± 0.004 Ga (U/Pb zircon, Kamo et al., 1996).**
**Impact energy: ~10²³ J (10⁸ Mt TNT).**

**Effects on Witwatersrand:**
- **Central uplift:** Exposes deep crust (granulites) at Vredefort Dome
- **Shock metamorphism:** PDFs in quartz, coesite, stishovite
- **Gold remobilization:** **Documented in mines near dome (West Wits, Carletonville)**
