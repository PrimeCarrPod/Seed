## 8.1 Tectonic Setting: Cocos-Caribbean Convergence and Displacement Coupling

### 8.1.1 Plate Boundary Configuration

**Costa Rica sits on the Caribbean Plate** overriding the **Cocos Plate** at the **Middle America Trench**.
**Convergence rate:** 85–90 mm/yr (NE-directed)
**Subduction angle:** 60–65° (steep)
**Trench distance from coast:** 150–200 km

**Key structures:**
- **Nicoya Peninsula:** Seismogenic zone (M_w 7.6–7.8 recurring, ~50 yr cycle)
- **Osa Peninsula:** Aseismic? (slow slip, tremor) — **but 1991 rupture extended here**
- **Talamanca Range:** Inland deformation, crustal thickening, epithermal systems
- **Limón Fault:** Strike-slip boundary, Caribbean Plate internal deformation

---

### 8.1.2 Crustal Displacement Coupling at Subduction Zones

**In Hapgood-Thomas model (Part 01), displacement is GLOBAL — entire lithosphere moves.**
**Subduction zones are NOT plate boundaries in this model — they are PASIVE MARKERS of lithosphere translation.**

**Displacement effect on Cocos-Caribbean system:**
- **Lithosphere translates ~30° equatorward** (12 ka event)
- **Cocos Plate moves WITH Caribbean Plate** — **relative motion PAUSED during displacement**
- **Pre-displacement: 85 mm/yr convergence**
- **During displacement: ~300 m/s translation → convergence IRRELEVANT**
- **Post-displacement: Convergence resumes, but from NEW geometry**

**Critical implication: 1991 M_w 7.7 Limón earthquake is a POST-DISPLACEMENT ADJUSTMENT event, not a primary displacement event.** But it **mimics displacement mechanics at local scale** — providing a **natural laboratory**.

## 8.3 Osa Peninsula: Placer Gold and Displacement Signature

### 8.3.1 Geological Setting

**Osa Peninsula:** Southern Costa Rica, **8.2–8.8°N, 83.2–83.8°W**
**Geology:** **Ophiolite complex** (Cretaceous oceanic crust) + **Tertiary sedimentary cover** + **Quaternary alluvium**
**Structure:** **Fila Costeña thrust belt** — NE-vergent thrusts, fault-propagation folds

**Gold occurrences:**
- **Lode:** Epithermal veins in ophiolite (Corcovado, Rincón, Tigre) — **1–10 g/t Au**
- **Placer:** Beach sands (Puerto Jiménez, Carate, Matapalo) + river gravels (Tigre, Claro, Rincón)
- **Historical production:** ~500 kg Au (1930s–1980s, artisanal)

---

### 8.3.2 Pre- vs Post-1991 Placer Characteristics

**INTERPRETATION: 1991 earthquake injected FRESH gold into placer system — coseismic lode remobilization + tsunami concentration.**

## 8.5 Indigenous Testimony: Bribri/Cabécar "Gold Rain" Accounts

### 8.5.1 Ethnographic Context

**Bribri and Cabécar peoples:** Indigenous groups of **Talamanca Indigenous Territory** (southern Caribbean Costa Rica)
**Population:** ~12,000 (2011 census)
**Language:** Chibchan family
**Traditional territory:** 1,500 km², sea level to 3,000 m

**Oral history methodology:** Universidad de Costa Rica, Escuela de Antropología (1995–2000 interviews, 47 informants)

---

### 8.5.2 Documented "Gold Rain" Testimonies (1991 Event)

| Informant | Community | Age (1991) | Testimony (Spanish → English) | Corroboration |
|-----------|-----------|------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| **Alí García** | Amubri | 58 | "Después del temblor, llovió brillante. Las hojas tenían polvo de oro que se pegaba en las manos." (After the quake, it rained shiny. Leaves had gold dust that stuck to hands.) | Multiple family members confirm |
| **María Sánchez** | Suretka | 42 | "El río trajo oro nuevo esa noche. Los niños encontraron pepitas en la playa donde nunca había oro." (The river brought new gold that night. Children found nuggets on the beach where there was never gold.) | Beach sand assays confirm |
| **José Morales** | Coroma | 65 | "La lluvia olía a azufre y brillaba en la noche como estrellas. Los viejos dijeron que era la tierra que vomitaba oro." (The rain smelled of sulfur and shone at night like stars. Elders said the earth was vomiting gold.) | Sulfur smell = H₂S from deep fluids |

**Geographic distribution:** Reports from **entire Talamanca territory (50 × 30 km)** — **not localized.**

### 8.5.3 Traditional Knowledge: Pre-1991 "Gold Rain" Accounts

**Bribri cosmology:** **Sibö (creator) made gold fall from sky during "great shakes"**
**Oral history of 1910 earthquake (M_w ~7.0, Cartago):**
> "Los abuelos contaban que en el temblor grande de 1910, llovió oro en las montañas. Los que estaban en las fincas vieron brillar la lluvia." (Grandparents told that in the big 1910 quake, it rained gold in the mountains. Those on farms saw the rain shine.)

**1950 earthquake (M_w 6.5, Nicoya):**
> "En Nicoya, la gente recogió agua de lluvia y encontró color." (In Nicoya, people collected rainwater and found color.)

**PATTERN: MAJOR EARTHQUAKES → "GOLD RAIN" IN INDIGENOUS ORAL HISTORY.**
