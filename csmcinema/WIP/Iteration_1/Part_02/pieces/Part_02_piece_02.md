Hypsipyle. 

**Hypsipyle** — "She who unlooses horses." *Hypso* (high, lofty) + *pyle* (gate, release). The name is a prophecy she never asked for. Daughter of Thoas. Thoas — "Swift." The king who *should* have died the night of the androcide. But Hypsipyle *hid him*. In a chest. On a ship. Sent him to Tauris. *Treason*. *Mercy*. *Both*. 

**Williams Register — Awakenings Clarity:** She was seventeen. The night the knives came out. She stood at her father's door. The women — *her* women, her *subjects* — demanded the old king's blood. "He brought the Thracians!" they screamed. "He *is* the betrayal!" 

Hypsipyle said: "No."

One word. Seventeen. The crown weighed nothing then. It is *lead* now.
