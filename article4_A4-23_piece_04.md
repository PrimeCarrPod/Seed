# 5. Running Gravitational Coupling and RG Flow

## 5.1 Gravitational RG Flow from Gap Distribution

The running gravitational coupling is:

α_G(μ) = α_G(m_e) × [1 + (2/3π) log(μ/m_e) + ...]_{gravity} + Δα_G^{gap}(μ)

The gap contribution comes from integrating out gap modes between the electron scale (gap 2) and the scale μ:

Δα_G^{gap}(μ) = (1/254²) Σ_{d=2}^{d(μ)} A_{d,254}^{grav} log(μ/E_d)

where d(μ) is the gap corresponding to energy scale μ, and A_{d,254}^{grav} is the gravitational gap correlation.

## 5.2 Asymptotic Safety and Gap 254

α_G*(μ → E_{Pl}) = 1/254² ≈ 1.55 × 10⁻⁵ (in Planck units)

This is the asymptotic safety fixed point for gravity in the Prime Electron framework. The fixed point value is determined entirely by the 8-bit Hilbert space dimension (256 states, maximum gap 254).
