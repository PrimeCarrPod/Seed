# PIECE 01: Introduction — N=1 Supersymmetry from Prime Gap Pairs

The prime electron worldline, constructed from the sequence of prime gaps $d_n = p_{n+1} - p_n$, exhibits a hidden supersymmetric structure. From A1-24 (Worldline_Index_Theorem), the analytical index of the proper-time Dirac operator is $\text{Index}(D_\tau) = 78$, the number of record gaps up to the UV cutoff (PrimeBookOne directory 3.0, $p \sim 10^{18}$). From A1-07 (Pair_Creation_Annihilation), forward/backward proper-time propagation corresponds to electron/positron pairs — a natural particle/antiparticle doubling. From A1-17 (Worldline_Hamiltonian), the Hamiltonian is $H = \frac{\hbar}{\kappa} \sum_n d_n^{-1}$ acting on the 8-bit Hilbert space $\mathcal{H} = \mathbb{C}^{256}$.

This piece introduces **N=1 supersymmetric quantum mechanics** on the prime electron worldline. The supersymmetry emerges from the **pairing of consecutive prime gaps** $(d_n, d_{n+1})$ into supercharge components. The central charge $Z$ in the supersymmetry algebra $\{Q, Q^\dagger\} = H + Z$ is precisely the index theorem integer: $Z = \text{Index}(D_\tau) = 78$.

**Key Structures:**
1. **Supercharges**: $Q = \sum_{n=1}^{N-1} \psi_n d_n$, $Q^\dagger = \sum_{n=1}^{N-1} \psi_n^\dagger d_n$ where $\psi_n$ are fermionic operators on $\mathcal{H}$.
2. **Hamiltonian**: $H = \{Q, Q^\dagger\} = \sum_n d_n^2 \psi_n^\dagger \psi_n + \text{cross terms}$ — matches A1-17 up to gap statistics.
3. **Central Charge**: $Z = \frac{1}{2} [Q, Q^\dagger]_{\text{topological}} = \text{Index}(D_\tau) = 78$ — topological, gap-independent.
4. **SUSY Hilbert Space**: $\mathcal{H}_{\text{SUSY}} = \mathcal{H}_B \oplus \mathcal{H}_F$ with $\dim \mathcal{H}_B = \dim \mathcal{H}_F = 128$ (chiral split of 256).
5. **Witten Index**: $\text{Tr}(-1)^F e^{-\beta H} = \text{Index}(D_\tau) = 78$ — independent of $\beta$, computes the number of supersymmetric ground states.

**Physical Interpretation:** The 78 supersymmetric ground states correspond to the 78 record gaps. Each record gap creates a BPS state (from A1-28, to be derived) that saturates the BPS bound $M = |Z|$. The electron (forward time) and positron (backward time) are superpartners related by the SUSY transformation generated by $Q$.
