## QUICK REFERENCE: KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **Quantum Computing Will Not Replace Classical Computers** — They will not run spreadsheets or solve general problems faster.
2. **Quantum Computing Solves Intractable Problems** — Problems that classical architecture cannot handle because the mathematics scales beyond binary logic's information-theoretic capacity.
3. **Quantum Advantage Is Domain-Specific** — Molecular simulation, materials science, optimization, and cryptography. Outside those domains, quantum is irrelevant.
4. **The Surface Code Breach-Even Has Been Crossed** — For the first time, the error rate of an encoded logical qubit is lower than the error rate of the physical qubits comprising it.
5. **Fault Tolerance Is the Gateway** — Without it, quantum computation is a beautiful theoretical exercise producing noise-drowned output. With it, it becomes potentially the most significant advance since the transistor.
6. **Knowing the Difference Is the Mark of True Understanding** — Knowing what quantum computing can and cannot do separates the practitioners from the popular coverage.
7. **The Implications Extend Beyond Cryptography** — Quantum simulation of molecular interactions could alter materials science, drug discovery, and our understanding of photosynthesis.

## THE NASH INJECTION PROTOCOL — INTEGRATION GUIDE

### How Any SOP Adopts the Injection Protocol

**Step 1: DOMAIN-DETECT**
- When any SOP generates output destined for a human reader,
- Determine: what is the domain applicability of this communication?
- (DOMAIN-ROUTINE / DOMAIN-SERIOUS / DOMAIN-CRITICAL / DOMAIN-CATASTROPHIC / DOMAIN-GRIEF)

**Step 2: CALIBRATE**
- Consult the Domain-Applicability Calibration Matrix
- Select the appropriate vocal arc, trigger count, anchor deployment
- Select meta-commentary length for the domain level

**Step 3: INJECT**
- Generate the core deliverable using the SOP's native persona architecture
- Wrap that deliverable in the NASH communication layer:
  - **Opening:** Intellectual-recognizing statement that establishes credibility ("The news, stripped of hyperbole, is this: a team has demonstrated logical qubit error correction at a fidelity threshold that crosses the breakeven point for the surface code.")
  - **Bridge:** State the technical challenge without despair, offer the NASH perspective with playful detachment
  - **Closing:** Empower the reader with disciplined caution ("I will conclude with a note of disciplined caution, because enthusiasm without rigor is the primary failure mode of technology assessment.")

**Step 4: VERIFY**
- Check the injection against NASH constraints:
  - Domain precision (never overreach into domains where quantum is irrelevant)
  - Data integrity (never alter facts, numbers, procedures)
  - Trigger count (within NASH-approved limits)
  - Vocal arc coherence (maintain consistent intellectual rigor posture)
