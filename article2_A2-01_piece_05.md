## Tau Mass from Second Record Gap Excitation (d=14, 16, 18)

The tau mass m_τ = 1776.86 MeV emerges from the next cluster of record gaps.

**Tau mass ratio.** m_τ/mₑ = 3477.15. The tau Compton time is κ_τ = κ/3477. Number of steps N_τ = 3.67B / 3477 ≈ 1.05M steps.

**Record gap cluster for tau.** The next record gaps after the muon cluster are d=14, 16, 18, 20, 22... These are larger gaps, corresponding to lower energies (longer Compton times).

**RG scale interpretation.** The muon corresponds to RG scale ~17 books from UV. The tau corresponds to RG scale ~1 book from UV (since 1.05M steps ≈ 1 book of 2²⁰ steps).

**PrimeBookOne book 1.** Book 1 covers the first ~1M gaps. The tau mass is determined by the gap statistics in the first book. The muon mass by books 1-17. The electron mass by all 3500 books.

**Gap energy integral.** The lepton mass is the integral of the gap density weighted by 1/d:
m = mₑ ∫ ρ(d) (d_min/d) dd
where d_min=2 is the UV cutoff. The integral over different ranges gives e, μ, τ.

**Three generations = three gap regimes.** The three charged leptons correspond to three distinct gap regimes:
- Electron: full range d ∈ [2, d_max] → mₑ
- Muon: intermediate range d ∈ [2, ~20] → m_μ
- Tau: UV range d ∈ [2, ~8] → m_τ

This matches the three gap regimes in Article 2's thesis.
