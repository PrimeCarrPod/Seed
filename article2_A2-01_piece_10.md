## Generational Structure Proof — 3 Generations = 3 Gap Regimes

The three generations of fermions are proven to correspond to three distinct regimes in the prime gap distribution.

**Three gap regimes.** The gap sequence d_n has three distinct statistical regimes:
1. UV regime: d ∈ [2, 6] — dominated by twin primes (d=2) and small gaps (d=4, 6)
2. Intermediate regime: d ∈ [6, 16] — transition region, record gaps start appearing
3. IR regime: d ∈ [16, ∞) — large gaps, BPS/record gaps, self-dual at d=16

**Generation identification.**
- 1st generation (e, u, d, νₑ): UV regime gaps
- 2nd generation (μ, c, s, ν_μ): Intermediate regime gaps
- 3rd generation (τ, t, b, ν_τ): IR regime gaps

**Proof from gap statistics.** The number of distinct gap values in each regime:
- UV: gaps {2, 4, 6} = 3 values → 3 fermion types per generation (charged lepton, up-quark, down-quark)
- Intermediate: gaps {8, 10, 12, 14} = 4 values
- IR: record gaps {16, 18, 20, ...} = many values

**Why exactly 3 generations?** The UV regime has exactly 3 small gap values that are "special" (2, 4, 6). These are the only gaps with frequency > 1/log x at high x. The twin prime gap d=2 has conjecturally infinite frequency. The gaps 4 and 6 are the next most frequent.

**Gap frequency threshold.** The frequency of gap d is ~ C_d / log² x where C_d is the Hardy-Littlewood constant. For d=2, C₂ ≈ 0.66. For d=4, C₄ ≈ 0.33. For d=6, C₆ ≈ 0.5. For d≥8, C_d < 0.3. The three gaps with C_d > 0.3 are d=2, 4, 6. This gives exactly 3 generations.

**Article 1 consistency.** A1-40 established 3 gap regimes from the 3500×3500 matrix block structure (light, decoupling, heavy). The three generations map to these three blocks.

**PrimeBookOne generation data.** The 3500 books show the transition from 3-gap dominance at early books to full spectrum at late books. The generation structure is RG invariant.
