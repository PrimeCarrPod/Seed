#### 15.6 Organ Preservation Applications

**15.6.1 Two-Layer UW/PFC Method (Standard for Liver/Pancreas)**

**Principle:** UW solution (aqueous) + PFC layer (oxygenated) → diffusion gradient

**Standard Protocol (Liver):**
- **Bottom Layer:** 500 mL UW solution (4°C)
- **Top Layer:** 100 mL Perfluorodecalin (oxygenated, 4°C)
- **Organ:** Immersed at interface
- **Gas Phase:** 100% O₂ headspace
- **Storage Time:** Up to 24 hr (vs. 12 hr UW alone)

**O₂ Diffusion Physics:**
$$J_{O_2} = \frac{D_{PFC} \cdot S_{PFC} \cdot (P_{O_2,gas} - P_{O_2,tissue})}{h_{PFC}}$$

**Typical Parameters:**
- $D_{PFC} \approx 2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ cm}^2/\text{s}$
- $S_{PFC} \approx 45 \text{ mL/100mL/atm}$
- $h_{PFC} \approx 0.5 \text{ cm}$
- $J_{O_2} \approx 0.5 \text{ mL O}_2/\text{min/cm}^2$ (sufficient for liver)

**15.6.2 Clinical Outcomes (Liver Transplant)**

| Outcome | UW Alone | UW + PFC (Perfluorodecalin) |
|---------|----------|----------------------------|
| **Primary Non-Function** | 8% | **2%** |
| **Early Graft Dysfunction** | 25% | **10%** |
| **1-Year Graft Survival** | 85% | **92%** |
| **Biliary Complications** | 15% | **8%** |
| **Peak AST (IU/L)** | 1,200 | **600** |
| **Peak Bilirubin (mg/dL)** | 12 | **6** |

**Mechanism:** Continuous O₂ supply → prevents anaerobic metabolism → preserves ATP → reduces reperfusion injury.

**15.6.3 Pancreas & Lung Preservation**

**Pancreas (Islet Transplant):**
- **PFC:** Perfluorodecalin (oxygenated)
- **Benefit:** ↑ Islet yield 30%, ↑ viability 20%
- **Mechanism:** Prevents hypoxia-induced apoptosis during isolation

**Lung (Ex Vivo Lung Perfusion - EVLP):**
- **Perfusate:** Steen solution + 10% Perfluorodecalin emulsion
- **Benefit:** ↓ Edema, ↑ gas exchange, extends preservation to 12 hr
- **Clinical:** Toronto protocol — 20% increase in transplantable lungs

**15.6.4 Heart & Kidney Preservation**

**Heart (Ex Vivo Perfusion):**
- **Challenge:** High O₂ demand (coronary flow needed)
- **PFC Role:** Oxygenate perfusate → ↓ lactate, ↑ ATP
- **Status:** Experimental (porcine models, promising)

**Kidney (Machine Perfusion):**
- **Standard:** Hypothermic machine perfusion (HMP)
- **PFC Addition:** 5% Perfluorodecalin in perfusate
- **Benefit:** ↓ Delayed graft function (DGF) 30% → 15%
- **Mechanism:** O₂ delivery to medulla (hypoxic zone)
