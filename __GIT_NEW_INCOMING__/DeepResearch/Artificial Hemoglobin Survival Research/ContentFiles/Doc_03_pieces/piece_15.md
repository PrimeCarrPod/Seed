# Document 3: Extracellular Marine Hemoglobin - M101/HEMO2life
## Piece 15/18: Entrapment-Specific Deployment Protocols for M101/Hemoxycarrier

Translating M101's unique properties into practical entrapment rescue protocols requires integration with existing emergency medical systems, consideration of austere environment constraints, and definition of clear decision algorithms for first responders. This piece provides detailed deployment protocols tailored to entrapment scenarios.

**Entrapment Rescue Phases and M101 Role**:

| Phase | Time | Physiology | M101 Role | Adjuncts |
|-------|------|------------|-----------|----------|
| **Access** | 0-30 min | Victim located, borehole/umbilical established. Hypoxia, hypercapnia, acidosis. | LOMs (if no ventilation) → M101 if IV access. | THAM, airway management. |
| **Stabilization** | 30 min - 6h | IV/IO access secured. Ongoing hypoxia, rising CO₂, shock. | **M101 primary carrier** (sustained O₂ + SOD). | THAM/IPRAC (CO₂), fluids, pressors. |
| **Prolonged Support** | 6-48h | Awaiting extrication. Reperfusion injury risk, infection risk. | M101 continued (IRI prevention, sulfide resistance). | Antibiotics, nutrition, monitoring. |
| **Extrication/Rescue** | 48h+ | Reperfusion, transport to hospital. | M101 weaned as native Hb recovers. | Blood products, ICU care. |

**M101 Dosing Protocol — Hemoxycarrier 10 g/dL**:

*Patient Weight Estimation*: 
- Adult: 70 kg (adjust ±20% for build).
- Pediatric: Broselow tape or weight-based (10 mL/kg).

*Loading Dose*:
- **Adult**: 500 mL (50 g M101, 1 g/kg) over 30 min via 18G IV/IO.
- **Pediatric**: 10 mL/kg over 30 min.
- **Target Plasma [M101]**: 1.0-1.5 g/dL (achievable Cmax with Vd 60 mL/kg).
- **O₂ Delivery Increase**: +5.6 mL O₂/dL plasma (at 1.2 g/dL M101, 140 μM heme × 1.34).

*Maintenance Dosing*:
- **Interval**: Every 12 hours (t½ 18-24h).
- **Dose**: 250 mL (25 g M101, 0.5 g/kg) over 30 min.
- **Adjustment**: If plasma [M101] < 0.8 g/dL at 6h post-dose → give 125 mL early.
- **Maximum 48h Total**: 1.5 L (150 g M101, 3 g/kg cumulative).

*Concurrent Infusions*:
- **Crystalloid**: 1:1 with M101 (500 mL NS/RL per 500 mL M101) — maintains intravascular volume, urine output.
- **THAM**: 0.3 M, 500 mL over 4h, then 250 mL q6h (per CO₂ mitigation protocol).
- **Vasopressors**: Norepinephrine if MAP <65 despite fluids (M101 does not cause vasoconstriction).
- **Antibiotics**: If entrapment >6h or open wounds (ceftriaxone 2g IV q24h).

**Administration Requirements**:
- **IV Access**: 18G or larger (250 mL/30 min = 500 mL/h = 8.3 mL/min = 138 drops/min macro-drip). Pressure bag (300 mmHg) if gravity insufficient.
- **IO Access**: Proximal tibia/humerus (approved for Hemoxycarrier viscosity 4.5 cP).
- **Filter**: Standard blood filter (170-260 μm) — M101 25 nm passes freely.
- **Warming**: Not required (room temp stable), but warm to 37°C if available (improves O₂ release).
- **Compatibility**: Compatible with NS, RL, Plasmalyte, THAM, vasopressors, antibiotics. **Incompatible**: D5W (hemolysis risk), blood products (separate line).

**Monitoring Protocol** (Minimal, Austere-Compatible):
| Parameter | Frequency | Target | Action if Abnormal |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| SpO₂ (pulse ox) | Continuous | >90% | Increase FiO₂, check M101 flow |
| MAP (non-invasive) | q15min × 1h, then q1h | >65 mmHg | Fluids, vasopressors |
| HR | Continuous | 60-100 | Treat cause |
| Urine Output | q1h (via catheter) | >0.5 mL/kg/h | Fluid bolus, check renal |
| Capillary Refill | q30min | <3 sec | Perfusion assessment |
| Mental Status | q1h (GCS) | Baseline or improving | Neuro check |
| Plasma Color | q6h (visual) | Pink-red | If brown → metHb >20%, replace bag |
| Lactate (if portable) | q2h | Decreasing >10%/h | Increase O₂ delivery |
| MetHb (if co-oximeter) | q6h | <10% | Methylene blue 1-2 mg/kg |

**Field Monitoring Tools** (Prioritized for Austere):
1. **Pulse Oximeter** (Masimo Radical-7 or equivalent — SpO₂, PI, PVI).
2. **Non-invasive BP** (automated cuff).
3. **Portable Lactate** (Lactate Scout, Lactate Plus — fingerstick, 13 sec).
4. **Handheld Co-oximeter** (Radical-7 with Rainbow SET — SpHb, SpMet, SpCO). **Gold standard**.
5. **Urine Dipstick** (hemoglobinuria, myoglobinuria).
6. **Point-of-Care Ultrasound** (cardiac, IVC, lung — if trained provider).

**Decision Algorithm — M101 vs ErythroMer vs LOMs**:

```
ENTRAPMENT VICTIM CONFIRMED
    ↓
AIRWAY PATENT? → NO → LOMs IMMEDIATE (500 mL IV over 15 min) → Reassess
    ↓ YES
SULFIDE ENVIRONMENT? (Mine, sewer, manure) → YES → M101 PRIMARY (500 mL load)
    ↓ NO
SEVERE ACIDOSIS? (pH <7.2, lactate >6) → YES → ErythroMer PRIMARY (Bohr effect)
    ↓ NO
HEMORRHAGE/CRUSH? → YES → ErythroMer + M101 COMBINATION
    ↓ NO
PROLONGED (>12h) ANTICIPATED? → YES → M101 (SOD, sustained)
    ↓ NO
SHORT-TERM (<6h) EXTRICATION LIKELY? → YES → ErythroMer or Hemopure
```

**Special Populations**:
- **Pediatric**: 10 mL/kg load, 5 mL/kg q12h. Adjust for weight. IO preferred if IV difficult.
- **Pregnancy**: M101 crosses placenta? Unknown. Likely yes (25 nm). Benefit > risk in maternal hypoxia. Dose by maternal weight.
- **Elderly**: Reduced renal reserve — monitor urine output closely. M101 no renal toxicity but fluids may overload.
- **Cardiac Disease**: M101 no vasoconstriction — safer than Hemopure. Monitor for volume overload (crackles, JVD).
- **Sickle Cell**: M101 does not sickle. May improve microvascular flow. No contraindication.

**Complication Management**:
1. **Hypertension** (MAP >110): Rare with M101. If occurs: Reduce rate, nitroprusside/nitro.
2. **Volume Overload** (crackles, JVD, SpO₂ drop): Stop M101/crystalloid, furosemide 20-40 mg IV, consider CPAP.
3. **Allergic Reaction** (theoretical): Epinephrine 0.3 mg IM, antihistamine, steroid. No pre-existing immunity expected.
4. **MetHb >20%**: Methylene blue 1-2 mg/kg IV (reduces M101 metHb same as Hb).
5. **Line Occlusion**: M101 25 nm — should not clot. Flush with 20 mL NS.
6. **Bag Discoloration** (brown/green): Oxidation/contamination. Discard, replace.

**Training Requirements for First Responders**:
- **Level 1 (All miners/submariners)**: Recognize entrapment, call for help, apply LOMs if available (2-hour course).
- **Level 2 (Rescue medics)**: IV/IO access, M101 administration, monitoring (16-hour course + annual refresher).
- **Level 3 (Physicians/Advanced providers)**: Full protocol, combination therapies, complication management (40-hour course).

**Documentation and Handoff**:
- **Entrapment Resuscitation Record**: Time, doses, vitals, complications.
- **Hospital Handoff**: M101 total dose, last dose time, plasma color, metHb%, lactate trend.
- **Blood Bank Notification**: M101 interferes with Hb electrophoresis, co-oximetry (known spectrum). Provide M101 spectrum file.

**Conclusion**: M101/Hemoxycarrier deployment in entrapment is feasible with current emergency medical capabilities. The protocol leverages M101's unique sulfide resistance, SOD activity, and NO compatibility while providing clear decision logic for integration with LOMs (immediate) and ErythroMer (acidosis/hemorrhage). Training and equipment caches at high-risk sites enable rapid deployment within the critical 30-minute access window.
