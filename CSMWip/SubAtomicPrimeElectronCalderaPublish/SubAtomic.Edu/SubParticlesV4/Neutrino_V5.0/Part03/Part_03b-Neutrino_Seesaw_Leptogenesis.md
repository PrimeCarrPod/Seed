### 1. Type I Seesaw: Details & Parameterization

**Casas-Ibarra parameterization**:
$$Y_\nu = \frac{i}{v} U \sqrt{m_\nu} R \sqrt{M_R}$$
where $R$ is complex orthogonal ($R^T R = 1$)

**3 RH neutrinos**: $R \in O(3, \mathbb{C})$ → 3 complex angles
**Degeneracy**: $R$ not fixed by low-energy data

**Minimal seesaw** (2 RH neutrinos): $m_1 = 0$, predicts $\sum m_\nu$

---

### 2. Dirac vs Majorana

**Dirac neutrinos**: $m_\nu = Y_\nu v / \sqrt{2}$, $Y_\nu \sim 10^{-12}$
**Symmetry**: $U(1)_{B-L}$ or $Z_2$ (lepton number)
**Test**: $0\nu\beta\beta$ (Dirac → no signal)

**Majorana**: $m_\nu = m_\nu^T$, $0\nu\beta\beta$ possible
**Seesaw**: Natural Majorana masses

### 3. Low-Scale Seesaw & LHC Signatures

**Inverse seesaw**: 
$$\mathcal{M} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & m_D & 0 \\ m_D^T & 0 & M_R \\ 0 & M_R^T & \mu \end{pmatrix}$$
$\mu \ll m_D \ll M_R$ → $m_\nu \sim m_D M_R^{-1} \mu M_R^{-1} m_D^T$

**Linear seesaw**: $\mu$ term from symmetry breaking

**LHC signatures**:
- $pp \to W^* \to \ell N$ → displaced vertices
- $pp \to Z^* \to N N$ → same-sign dileptons
- $M_N \sim 100$ GeV – 1 TeV testable

### 4. Leptogenesis Variants

**Thermal (hierarchical)**: $M_1 > 10^9$ GeV, $\eta_B \sim 10^{-10}$
**Resonant**: $M_1 \sim$ TeV, $\Delta M \sim \Gamma$ → TeV leptogenesis
**ARS (Akhmedov-Rubakov-Smirnov)**: Oscillations in early universe
**Low-scale**: $N_1 \sim$ GeV, GeV-scale leptogenesis

**Flavored leptogenesis**: $\eta_B \sim 10^{-10}$ for $M_1 \sim 10^{10-12}$ GeV

---

### 5. Dirac Leptogenesis

**Dirac neutrinos**: $L$ asymmetry stored in $N_R$ (not $L$)
**Mechanism**: $N_R$ decay → $L$ asymmetry in $N_R$ sector
**Sphalerons**: Don't wash out (B-L conserved)
**Late decay**: $N_R$ decays after sphaleron freeze-out
