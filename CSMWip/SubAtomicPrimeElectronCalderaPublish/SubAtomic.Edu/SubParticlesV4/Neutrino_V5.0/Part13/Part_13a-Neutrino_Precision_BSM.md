### 1. PMNS Unitarity Tests

**Unitarity condition**: $U U^\dagger = 1$
**Constraints**:
- $|U_{e1}|^2 + |U_{e2}|^2 + |U_{e3}|^2 = 1.00 \pm 0.02$
- $|U_{\mu1}|^2 + |U_{\mu2}|^2 + |U_{\mu3}|^2 = 1.00 \pm 0.05$
- $|U_{\tau1}|^2 + |U_{\tau2}|^2 + |U_{\tau3}|^2 = 1.00 \pm 0.1$

**Deviations**: Could indicate sterile neutrinos, NSI, or heavy neutrinos

**Future**: DUNE + JUNO + T2HK → unitarity at 1% level

### 2. Electroweak Precision Observables (EWPO)

**Neutrino couplings**: $g_L^\nu = 1/2$, $g_R^\nu = 0$ (SM)
**$Z$ invisible width**: $\Gamma_{\text{inv}} = N_\nu \Gamma_{\nu\bar{\nu}}$
- $N_\nu = 2.984 \pm 0.008$ (LEP)
- **Constraints**: $N_\nu = 3$ light neutrinos

**$W$ mass & $\sin^2\theta_W$**: 
- $m_W$ depends on $\Delta r \propto \sum |U_{\ell i}|^2$
- $\sin^2\theta_W$ from $\nu$-N, $e^+e^-$, APV

**SMEFT**: $\mathcal{O}_{HD}, \mathcal{O}_{HWB}$ shift $m_W$, $\sin^2\theta_W$

### 3. Neutrino Magnetic Moments

**Dirac**: $\mu_\nu = 3.2 \times 10^{-19} \mu_B (m_\nu/1 \text{eV})$
**Majorana**: $\mu_\nu$ transition only (diagonal = 0)
**Limits** (90% CL):
- $\mu_{\nu_e} < 2.9 \times 10^{-11} \mu_B$ (GEMMA)
- $\mu_{\nu_\mu} < 6.8 \times 10^{-10} \mu_B$ (LSND)
- $\mu_{\nu_\tau} < 3.9 \times 10^{-7} \mu_B$ (DONUT)

**BSM**: $\mu_\nu \sim 10^{-11} \mu_B$ possible in some models
