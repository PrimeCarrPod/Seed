### 2. CUPID (CUORE Upgrade with Particle ID)

**Detector**: $^{100}$Mo-enriched Li₂MoO₄ scintillating bolometers
**Mass**: 250 kg (240 kg $^{100}$Mo)
**Background**: $< 10^{-4}$ cts/(keV·kg·yr) with PID
**Sensitivity**: $m_{\beta\beta} \sim 0.01$ eV (10 yr)
**Status**: CUPID-Mo demonstrator running, CUPID construction 2025+

---

### 3. NEXT (Neutrino Experiment with Xenon TPC)

**Technology**: High-pressure Xe gas TPC + electroluminescence
**NEXT-100**: 100 kg $^{136}$Xe, running at LSC
**NEXT-HD**: 1 tonne, $m_{\beta\beta} \sim 0.01$ eV
**Key**: Topological signal (track + blob) → background rejection
**Ba⁺ tagging**: Single Ba⁺ ion detection → ultimate background-free

---

### 3. PTOLEMY: CνB Detection via $^3$H Capture

**Principle**: $\nu_e + {}^3\text{H} \to {}^3\text{He} + e^-$ (no threshold)
**Target**: 100 g $^3$H (tritium)
**Energy resolution**: $\Delta E \sim 0.1$ eV → CνB peak at $E_0 + m_\nu$
**Sensitivity**: $N_{\text{events}} \sim 10$ per year for $m_\nu = 0.1$ eV
**CνB detection**: Direct detection of cosmic neutrino background
