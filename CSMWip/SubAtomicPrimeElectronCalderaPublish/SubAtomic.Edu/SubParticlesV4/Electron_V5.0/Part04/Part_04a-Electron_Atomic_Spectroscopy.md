### 1. Lamb Shift: Bound-State QED

**Definition**: ΔE_Lamb = E(2S₁/₂) - E(2P₁/₂) in hydrogen = 1057.844 MHz

**QED contributions**:
- Self-energy (dominant): electron emits/reabsorbs virtual photon in Coulomb field
- Vacuum polarization: Uehling potential (e⁺e⁻ loop)
- Two-photon exchange, recoil, radiative-recoil

**Worldline in Coulomb field**: Electron worldline bound to proton — proper-time intervals Δτ_n modified by 1/r potential. Prime gaps in external potential ↔ shifted gap sequence.

**Prime-Electron**: Lamb shift measures **worldline self-interaction in external field** — the "prime gap fluctuation spectrum" in 1/r potential.

### 3. Hyperfine Splitting

**Ground state H**: ΔE_HFS = 1,420,405,751.768(1) Hz (21 cm line)

**QED corrections**: α/π, α², radiative-recoil

**Muonium (μ⁺e⁻)**: Purely leptonic — no hadronic uncertainty
- Δν_Mu = 4,463,302,880(30) Hz
- Determines α, m_μ/m_e

**Worldline**: Hyperfine = worldline spin-flip in proton magnetic field.

### 4. Muonic Atoms: Proton & Deuteron Radii

**Muonic hydrogen (μp)**: 2S-2P Lamb shift → proton charge radius
- r_p = 0.84087(39) fm (CREMA 2010/2013)
- **5σ smaller** than CODATA 2010 (0.8775 fm) — "proton radius puzzle"

**Muonic deuterium (μd)**: 2S-2P → deuteron radius
- r_d = 2.12562(78) fm

**Muonic helium-4 (μ⁴He)**: 2S-2P → α-particle radius
- r_α = 1.67824(83) fm

**Resolution (2024)**: Electronic H 2S-4P (Paris) and 2S-2P (York) agree with **muonic value** — puzzle resolved in favor of smaller radius.

### 5. Muonic Atoms & Prime Gaps

**Muon = 1st worldline excitation** (gap=4 record prime gap). Bound muon in Coulomb field:
- Proper-time intervals Δτ_μ = 4 × Δτ_e (scale factor)
- Lamb shift enhanced by (m_μ/m_e)³ ~ 10⁷
- Worldline kinks scaled by mass ratio

**PrimeBookOne**: Directory 0.1 (muon threshold) gap statistics → muonic atom energy levels.

### 6. Helium & Few-Electron Systems

**He⁺ (hydrogen-like)**: 1S-2S, 2S-2P — test QED in Z=2 field
**He (neutral)**: 2³S-2¹S, 2³P-2³S — QED + correlation
**Li⁺, Be²⁺**: Higher Z — test strong-field QED

**Worldline**: Multi-electron = multiple worldlines with exchange symmetry (Pauli).
