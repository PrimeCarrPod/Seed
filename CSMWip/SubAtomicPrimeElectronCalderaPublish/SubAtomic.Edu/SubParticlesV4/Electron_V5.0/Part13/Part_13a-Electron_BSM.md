### 1. Supersymmetry (MSSM & Beyond)

**Electron sector**: Selectron (ẽ_L, ẽ_R), neutralino (χ⁰), chargino (χ±)

**Key processes**:
- ẽ production: e⁺e⁻ → ẽ⁺ẽ⁻ (FCC-ee, ILC)
- χ⁰χ⁰ production: missing energy + e⁺e⁻
- **g-2 & EDM**: SUSY loops (Parts 2, 3)

**Constraints**:
- m_ẽ > 500 GeV (LHC)
- m_χ⁰ > 200 GeV (LHC)
- Phases φ_μ, φ_A < 0.01 (EDM)

**Worldline**: SUSY partners = worldline excursions into SUSY sector.

### 2. Extra Dimensions

**ADD (Large flat)**:
- KK gravitons: e⁺e⁻ → γ + G_KK (missing E_T)
- Virtual KK: e⁺e⁻ → e⁺e⁻ via G_KK exchange
- Constraints: M_D > 10 TeV (n=2), > 5 TeV (n=6)

**Randall-Sundrum (Warped)**:
- KK gluons/Z: e⁺e⁻ → KK → e⁺e⁻
- Constraints: m_KK > 5-10 TeV

**Worldline**: Extra dimensions = additional worldline directions (compactified).

### 4. Dark Photon (A') & Dark Sector

**Kinetic mixing**: ε F_μν F'^μν → ε e A'_μ ēγ^μe

**Signatures**:
- A' → e⁺e⁻ (prompt or displaced)
- e⁻ beam dump: e⁻ Z → e⁻ Z A' (missing E)
- g-2 contribution (Part 2)

**Parameter space**:
- m_A' = 1 MeV - 1 GeV: ε = 10⁻⁴ - 10⁻³
- m_A' > 1 GeV: ε < 10⁻⁵

**Experiments**: NA64, LDMX, BDX, DarkQuest, LHCb

### 6. Leptoquarks (LQ)
