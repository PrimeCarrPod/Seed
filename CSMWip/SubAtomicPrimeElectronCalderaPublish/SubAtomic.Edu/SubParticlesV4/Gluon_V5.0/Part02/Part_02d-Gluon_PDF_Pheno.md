### Abstract

### 1. Higgs Production: Gluon Fusion Dominance

**gg→H**: σ(gg→H) = 48.6 pb at 13 TeV (NNLO+NNLL)
**gg→HH**: σ = 31 fb at 13 TeV (NNLO)
**Gluon luminosity**: L_gg = ∫ dx₁dx₂ g(x₁)g(x₂) δ(x₁x₂s - M_H²)

**Precision**: δσ/σ ~ 5% (theory) → gluon PDF uncertainty dominant at high M_HH.

**HL-LHC**: 3 ab⁻¹ → δκ_g/κ_g ~ 2-3% (Higgs coupling to gluons)

### 3. Small-x Gluon & Saturation

**Current reach**: x ~ 10⁻⁴ (LHC forward jets, UPCs)
**Saturation scale**: Q_s²(x=10⁻⁴) ~ 1-2 GeV² (CGC fits)

**EIC projections** (10 fb⁻¹ e+p):
- g(x,Q²) at x=10⁻⁴, Q²=10 GeV²: 5% precision
- Saturation onset: Q_s²(x) measurement to 10%
