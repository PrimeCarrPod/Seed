### 1. $W'$ and $Z'$ Bosons

**Sequential SM**: $W'_{\text{SSM}}, Z'_{\text{SSM}}$ (same couplings as W/Z)
**Mass limits**: $m_{W'} > 6$ TeV, $m_{Z'} > 5$ TeV (LHC dilepton/dijet)

**Left-Right symmetric**: $SU(2)_L \times SU(2)_R \times U(1)_{B-L}$
- $W_R$: $m_{W_R} > 4-5$ TeV
- $Z'$: $m_{Z'} > 5-6$ TeV

**$B-L$ gauge**: $Z'_{B-L}$ couples to $B-L$
**$L_\mu - L_\tau$**: $Z'$ explains $g-2$ muon anomaly

**Effective Lagrangian**:

- $q\bar{q} \to \ell\ell, q\bar{q} \to q\bar{q}$

### 3. Extra Dimensions

**ADD (Large flat)**: 
- KK gravitons: $G_{\text{KK}} \to \ell\ell, \gamma\gamma$
- Virtual KK: $q\bar{q} \to \ell\ell$ via $G_{\text{KK}}$
- $M_D > 5-10$ TeV ($n=2-6$)

**Randall-Sundrum (Warped)**:
- KK gluons/Z/W: $m_{\text{KK}} > 5-10$ TeV
- $pp \to G_{\text{KK}} \to WW, ZZ, hh$

### 4. SUSY & Extended Gauge Sectors

**MSSM**: 
- $Z'$ from $U(1)'$ breaking
- $W'$ from $SU(2)'$ breaking
- Constraints: $m_{Z'} > 3-5$ TeV

**Gauge-Higgs Unification**: 
- $W/Z$ as KK modes of 5D gauge field
- $m_{W/Z} \sim 1/R$

**Twin Higgs**: 
- Mirror sector with $W', Z'$
- $m_{W'} \sim 1$ TeV

### 5. $W/Z$ in Dark Sector

**Dark $Z'$**: $U(1)_d$ kinetic mixing with $Z$
**Dark $W'$**: $SU(2)_d$ with mixing
**Dark matter**: $\chi$ couples via $Z'$

**Signatures**:
- $Z' \to \chi\chi$ (MET + mono-X)
- $Z' \to \ell\ell$ (dilepton resonance)
- $W' \to \ell\nu$ (transverse mass)
