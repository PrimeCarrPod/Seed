### Abstract

W/Z at finite temperature and chemical potential. Electroweak phase transition (EWPT). Sphalerons. Early universe cosmology. Baryogenesis. Lattice EWPT.

### 1. Finite Temperature EW Theory

**Matsubara frequencies**: $\omega_n = 2n\pi T$ (bosons)
**Propagators**: $D_{\mu\nu}(\omega_n, k)$ with thermal self-energies
**Hard Thermal Loops (HTL)**:
- $m_D^2 = (g^2 + g'^2) T^2/8$ (Debye mass)
- Magnetic mass: $m_{\text{mag}} \sim g^2 T$

**Resummed propagators**: HTL effective theory

---

### 2. Electroweak Phase Transition (EWPT)

**Order parameter**: $\langle \phi \rangle = v(T)$
**SM**: Crossover for $m_h = 125$ GeV
**Critical temperature**: $T_c \approx 159$ GeV
**Order**: Crossover (no latent heat)
**Lattice**: 4D lattice confirms crossover

**BSM for 1st order**:
- Singlet: $S^2 \Phi^\dagger \Phi$ → barrier
- 2HDM: Extra scalars → 1st order
- Dim-6: $(\Phi^\dagger \Phi)^3$ → barrier

---

### 3. Sphalerons & B+L Violation

**Sphaleron**: Static, unstable $SU(2)$ solution
**Energy**: $E_{\text{sph}}(T) \approx 4\pi v(T)/g$
**Rate**: $\Gamma_{\text{sph}} \sim T^4 \exp(-E_{\text{sph}}/T)$

**B+L violation**: Sphalerons violate $B+L$, conserve $B-L$
**Washout**: In symmetric phase ($T > T_c$), $\Gamma_{\text{sph}} \gg H$ → washout
**Baryogenesis**: Requires 1st order PT → bubbles turn off sphalerons inside

---

### 4. Early Universe & EWPT

**Timeline**:
- $T \sim 100$ GeV: EWPT (crossover in SM)
- $T \sim 1$ GeV: QCD crossover
- $T \sim 1$ MeV: BBN
- $T \sim 0.1$ MeV: CMB

**Cosmological GW**: EWPT → stochastic GW background
**LISA**: Sensitive to BSM 1st order EWPT

---

### 4. EW Baryogenesis (EWBG)

**Sakharov conditions**:
1. B violation: Sphalerons
2. C/CP violation: CKM + BSM
3. Departure from equilibrium: 1st order PT

**SM fails**: Crossover, insufficient CPV
**BSM**: 2HDM, MSSM, singlet, dim-6 → 1st order + CPV

---

### 5. Lattice EWPT at Finite T

**Method**: 4D lattice, physical $m_h = 125$ GeV
**Results**: 
- Crossover confirmed
- $T_c = 159.5 \pm 1.5$ GeV
- No 1st order in SM

**BSM on lattice**: Emerging studies for singlet, dim-6

---

### 6. Worldline Picture: Finite T as Worldline Periodicity

**Matsubara**: $\omega_n = 2\pi n T$ → Worldline periodicity $\beta = 1/T$
**$T > T_c$**: Worldline EW fold symmetric (no stiffness)
**$T < T_c$**: Worldline EW fold acquires stiffness $v(T)$
**Sphaleron** = Worldline EW fold topology change
**EWPT** = Worldline EW fold stiffness acquisition
