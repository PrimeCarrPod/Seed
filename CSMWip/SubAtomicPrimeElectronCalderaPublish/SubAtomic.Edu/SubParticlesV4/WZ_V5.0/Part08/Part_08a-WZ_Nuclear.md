## Part 8 of 14 — W/Z in Nuclear/Hadron Physics: DIS, EIC, Nuclear PDFs, Coherent Scattering

### Abstract

W/Z in nuclear and hadron physics. Deep inelastic scattering (DIS). Electron-Ion Collider (EIC). Nuclear PDFs. Coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering (CEvNS). W/Z in nuclei.

---

### 1. Deep Inelastic Scattering (DIS)

**Neutral current**: $e + p \to e + X$ ($\gamma/Z$ exchange)
**Charged current**: $e + p \to \nu + X$ ($W$ exchange)
**Structure functions**: $F_2, F_L, xF_3$
**PDFs**: $q(x,Q^2), \bar{q}(x,Q^2), g(x,Q^2)$

**CC DIS**: $\nu_\mu + p \to \mu^- + X$ ($W^+$ exchange)
**NC DIS**: $\nu_\mu + p \to \nu_\mu + X$ ($Z$ exchange)

**EIC**: $e+p$ at $\sqrt{s}=140$ GeV → $x=10^{-4}-0.1$, $Q^2=1-10^4$ GeV²

---

### 2. W/Z in Nuclear Targets

**Nuclear PDFs**: $f_i^A(x,Q^2) = R_i^A(x,Q^2) A f_i^p(x,Q^2)$
**Shadowing**: $R < 1$ at $x < 0.01$ (saturation)
**Antishadowing**: $R > 1$ at $0.01 < x < 0.1$
**EMC effect**: $R < 1$ at $0.3 < x < 0.7$
**Fermi motion**: $R > 1$ at $x > 0.7$

**Coherent scattering**: $\nu + A \to \nu + A$ (CEvNS)
**$W$ in nuclei**: $\beta$ decay, muon capture

---

### 3. Electron-Ion Collider (EIC)

**Status**: CD-1 approved (2024), construction 2025-2033
**Beams**: $e+p$ (20-140 GeV), $e+d$, $e+He$, $e+Au/Pb$
**Luminosity**: $10^{33}-10^{34}$ cm⁻²s⁻¹
**Polarization**: 70% electron, 70% proton/light ions

**W/Z measurements at EIC**:
- **NC DIS**: $F_2, F_L, xF_3$ at small $x$
- **CC DIS**: $W^+$ exchange → $d$ quark PDF
- **W production**: $e+p \to \nu_e + X$ (CC)
- **Z production**: $e+p \to e+Z+X$ (NC)
- **Nuclear**: $R_i^A$ for $W/Z$ couplings

---

### 4. Coherent Elastic Neutrino-Nucleus Scattering (CEvNS)

**Process**: $\nu + A \to \nu + A$ (coherent, $Q^2 \sim 0$)
**Cross section**: $\sigma \propto N^2$ (coherent enhancement)
**COHERENT (SNS)**:
- CsI (2017): $134 \pm 22$ events, SM consistent
- Ar (2021): First Ar measurement
- Ge (2023): Low-threshold

**Applications**:
- $\sin^2\theta_W$ at low $Q^2$
- NSI constraints
- Neutron radius
- Supernova $\nu$ detection

---

### 5. W/Z in Heavy-Ion Collisions

**UPCs**: $\gamma + A \to W/Z + A$ (photon-induced)
**Pb+Pb**: $W/Z$ production in UPCs at LHC
**QGP**: $W/Z$ as probes (no strong interaction)
**$W$ asymmetry**: $W^+/W^-$ in p+Pb (nuclear PDF)
