### Abstract

Future gravitational wave facilities. LISA (space), Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer (ground), μAres (atomic), matter-wave interferometers. BSM reach projections.

---

### 1. LISA (Laser Interferometer Space Antenna)

**Launch**: 2037 (ESA L3)
**Arms**: 2.5M km, triangular
**Band**: 0.1 mHz - 1 Hz
**Sources**:
- **MBHB**: $10^4-10^7 M_\odot$ at $z=1-10$
- **EMRI**: $10 M_\odot$ into $10^6 M_\odot$ SMBH (precision GR tests)
- **Galactic binaries**: $10^4+$ WD/NS binaries (foreground)
- **Stochastic**: Inflation, phase transitions, cosmic strings

**Precision GR tests**:
- No-hair theorem: $\delta Q/M^3 \sim 10^{-3}$
- Ringdown overtones: $\delta f/f \sim 10^{-3}$
- Tidal deformability: $\delta \Lambda \sim 10^2$

---

### 2. Einstein Telescope (ET)

**Design**: Underground, 10 km arms, triangular (xylophone)
**Band**: 1 Hz - 10 kHz (10× LIGO at low-f)
**Sensitivity**: 
- BNS to $z \sim 2$ (10⁵/yr)
- BBH to $z \sim 10$
- Stochastic: $\Omega_{\text{GW}} \sim 10^{-12}$

**Key science**:
- NS equation of state to 1%
- Hubble constant to 1%
- BSM: phase transitions, cosmic strings, PBH

---

### 3. Cosmic Explorer (CE)

**Design**: 40 km arms, L-shaped, surface
**Band**: 5 Hz - 5 kHz
**Sensitivity**: 
- BNS to $z \sim 5$
- BBH to $z \sim 20$ (full cosmic history)
- Stochastic: $\Omega_{\text{GW}} \sim 10^{-13}$

**Key science**:
- Primordial BH population
- Inflationary GWs
- Dark sirens for cosmology

---

### 4. Atomic & Matter-Wave GW Detectors

**μAres** (Micro-Arcsecond Reference):
- Atom interferometer, 1 km baseline
- Band: 0.1-10 Hz
- Target: $\Delta h/h \sim 10^{-20}$

**MIGA** (Matter-wave Interferometer Gravitational Antenna):
- Cold atoms, 100 m baseline
- Band: 0.1-1 Hz

**MAGIS** (Matter-wave Atomic Gradiometer Interferometric Sensor):
- 100 m baseline, Stanford
- Band: 0.03-10 Hz

**Advantage**: Different systematics, complementary to optical interferometers
