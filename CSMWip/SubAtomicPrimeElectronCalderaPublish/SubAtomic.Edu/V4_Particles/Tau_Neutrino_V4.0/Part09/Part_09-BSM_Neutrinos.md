### 3. Lorentz Violation and CPT

#### 3.1 Lorentz Violation from Gap Anisotropy

The prime gap sequence is **isotropic** (no preferred direction in 1D sequence).

**Prediction**: No intrinsic Lorentz violation from gap sequence.

**Experimental limit**: $|a_L| < 10^{-23}$ GeV — **consistent**.

#### 3.2 CPT Violation

CPT violation would require **differences between neutrino and antineutrino gap sequences**.

In the prime gap picture, $\bar{\nu}$ = time-reversed gap sequence. Sequence is statistically symmetric.

**Prediction**: No CPT violation.

**Experimental limit**: $|\Delta m^2_{\nu} - \Delta m^2_{\bar{\nu}}| < 10^{-4} \text{ eV}^2$ — **consistent**.
