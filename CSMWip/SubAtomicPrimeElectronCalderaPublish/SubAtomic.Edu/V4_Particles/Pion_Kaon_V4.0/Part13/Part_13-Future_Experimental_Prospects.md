# Pion-Kaon Chiral Dynamics — V4.0 Deep Dive Series
## Part 13 of 14 — Future Experimental Prospects: BESIII, LHCb, Belle II, J-PARC, PANDA, EIC

---

### Abstract

This V4.0 installment maps out the experimental landscape for pion and kaon physics over the next decade (2025-2035). We detail the physics reach of BESIII (BEPCII), LHCb (Run 3 and Upgrade II), Belle II (SuperKEKB), J-PARC (HIHR, E70), PANDA (FAIR), and the Electron-Ion Collider (EIC), focusing on precision measurements of decays, form factors, spectroscopy, and BSM searches.

---

### 1. BESIII (BEPCII, 2024-2028+)

#### 1.1 Machine Parameters

- $e^+e^-$ collider at $\sqrt{s} = 2.0-4.95$ GeV
- Peak luminosity: $1.0 \times 10^{33} \text{ cm}^{-2}\text{s}^{-1}$ at $3.77$ GeV
- Integrated luminosity goal: $20 \text{ fb}^{-1}$ (2024-2028)

#### 1.2 Key Physics Programs

| Channel | Observable | Current Precision | BESIII Goal |
|---------|------------|-------------------|-------------|
| $J/\psi \to \gamma \eta_c$ | $M_{\eta_c}, \Gamma_{\eta_c}$ | 1-2% | 0.5% |
| $J/\psi \to \gamma \pi^0, \eta, \eta'$ | Transition form factors | 5-10% | 2% |
| $\psi(3770) \to D\bar{D}$ | $f_+(0)$ for $D \to \pi\ell\nu$ | 1% | 0.5% |
| $e^+e^- \to \pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$ | $\sigma(e^+e^- \to 3\pi)$ | 3% | 1% |
| $\tau \to \pi\pi\nu$ | Spectral function | 5% | 2% |

#### 1.3 Precision Pion/Kaon Physics

**$\pi^0$ transition form factor:**
$$F_{\pi^0\gamma^*\gamma^*}(q_1^2, q_2^2)$$
Single-tag $e^+e^- \to e^+e^- \pi^0$ at $\sqrt{s} = 3.77$ GeV.
Goal: $Q^2$ up to $30 \text{ GeV}^2$ for $F(Q^2)$.

**$\eta, \eta'$ decays:**
- $\eta \to \pi^0\gamma\gamma$: ChPT $\mathcal{O}(p^6)$ test
- $\eta' \to \eta\pi\pi$: Glueball content
- $\eta \to \mu^+\mu^-$: BSM search

**Light quark masses:**
$\tau \to \pi\pi\nu$ spectral function $\to$ $m_s$ via FESR.

#### 1.4 BSM Searches

- Dark photon: $e^+e^- \to \gamma A'$, $A' \to \pi^+\pi^-$
- Axion-like particles: $J/\psi \to \gamma a$, $a \to \gamma\gamma$
- LFV: $J/\psi \to e\mu, e\tau, \mu\tau$

### 2. LHCb (Run 3: 2022-2025, Upgrade II: 2030s)

#### 2.1 Run 3 Upgrades

- Trigger: Software-only, 30 MHz inelastic rate
- Luminosity: $2 \times 10^{33} \text{ cm}^{-2}\text{s}^{-1}$
- Integrated: $23 \text{ fb}^{-1}$ (Run 3), $300 \text{ fb}^{-1}$ (Upgrade II)

#### 2.2 Kaon Physics Program

| Decay | Observable | Current | Run 3 | Upgrade II |
|-------|------------|---------|-------|------------|
| $K^+ \to \pi^+\nu\bar{\nu}$ | BR | 30% (NA62) | 15% | 5% |
| $K_S \to \pi^0\nu\bar{\nu}$ | BR | — | — | First obs. |
| $K_L \to \pi^0\ell^+\ell^-$ | BR, $A_{CP}$ | — | First obs. | 10% |
| $K \to \pi\pi\gamma$ | Form factors | 10% | 3% | 1% |

#### 2.3 Charm and Strange Spectroscopy

- $D_s^+ \to \pi^+\pi^-\pi^+$: Dalitz plot, $a_0(980)$
- $\Lambda_c \to p K^-\pi^+$: Baryon resonances
- $\Xi_c, \Omega_c$ spectroscopy
- $P_c$ pentaquarks in $\Lambda_b \to J/\psi p\pi^-$

#### 2.4 CP Violation

- $\phi_s$ from $B_s \to J/\psi \phi$: $0.003$ rad precision
- $\gamma$ from $B \to DK$: $< 1^\circ$ precision
- $\Delta A_{CP}$ in $D \to K^+K^-, \pi^+\pi^-$

### 3. Belle II (SuperKEKB, 2019-2030s)

#### 3.1 Machine Parameters

- Asymmetric $e^+e^-$ at $\Upsilon(4S)$: $7 \text{ GeV} \times 4 \text{ GeV}$
- Nano-beam scheme: Luminosity $6 \times 10^{35} \text{ cm}^{-2}\text{s}^{-1}$
- Target: $50 \text{ ab}^{-1}$ by 2030s

#### 3.2 Precision Pion/Kaon Physics

| Channel | Observable | Current | Belle II (50 ab$^{-1}$) |
|---------|------------|---------|------------------------|
| $B \to \pi\ell\nu$ | $|V_{ub}|$ | 4% | 1.5% |
| $B \to D\ell\nu$ | $|V_{cb}|$ | 2% | 1% |
| $\tau \to \pi\nu$ | $|V_{ud}|$ | 0.1% | 0.05% |
| $K_S \to \pi^0\nu\bar{\nu}$ | BR | — | First obs. |
| $K_L \to \pi^0\ell^+\ell^-$ | BR, $A_{CP}$ | — | 20% |
| $\pi^0 \to e^+e^-$ | BR | 5% | 1% |

#### 3.3 Spectroscopy

- $B \to K\pi\pi$: $K^*$ resonances, $\kappa$
- $B \to D^{(*)}\pi$: $D^{**}$ spectroscopy
- $\Upsilon(5S) \to B_s\bar{B}_s$: $B_s$ decays to $\phi\pi$, $\phi K$

#### 3.4 BSM

- LFV: $\tau \to \ell\gamma, \ell\ell\ell, \pi\ell$
- Dark sector: $e^+e^- \to \gamma + \text{invisible}$
- $B \to K^{(*)}\nu\bar{\nu}$: $Z'$ constraints

### 4. J-PARC (HIHR, E70, KOTO Step 2)

#### 4.1 Hadron Facility (HIHR, 2028-2032)

- 30 GeV proton synchrotron
- High-intensity $K^-$, $\pi^-$, $\bar{p}$ beams
- $10^{11}$ protons/pulse, 1.3 s cycle

#### 4.2 Key Experiments

**E70 (KOTO Step 2): $K_L \to \pi^0\nu\bar{\nu}$**
- Target: SM sensitivity ($3 \times 10^{-11}$)
- New CsI calorimeter, upgraded veto
- Running 2024-2028

**E42: $H$-dibaryon search**
- $(K^-, K^+)$ on $^{12}\text{C}$ at 1.8 GeV/c
- Sensitivity: $B_H < 1$ MeV

**E50: $\Xi$ hypernuclei**
- $(K^-, K^+)$ for $\Xi$ production
- $_{\Xi}^{15}\text{C}$, $_{\Xi\Xi}^6\text{He}$

**E07: $\Xi$ nuclear physics**
- $\Xi^- p \to \Xi\Xi$ dibaryon
- $\Xi$ optical potential

#### 4.3 HIHR Future

- $K_L$ beam for $K_L \to \pi^0\ell^+\ell^-$
- $\bar{p}p \to$ charmonium exotics
- $\Lambda \Lambda$ correlation for $H$-dibaryon

### 5. PANDA (FAIR, $\bar{p}p$ Annihilation, 2028+)

#### 5.1 Unique Capabilities

- $\bar{p}$ beam: 1.5-15 GeV/c
- Formation experiments: $\bar{p}p \to X$ (not production)
- High resolution: $\Delta p/p \sim 10^{-4}$
- Full acceptance detector

#### 5.2 Pion/Kaon Physics

| Channel | Observable | Significance |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| $\bar{p}p \to \pi^0\pi^0$ | $\pi\pi$ scattering lengths | ChPT test |
| $\bar{p}p \to \eta\pi^0$ | $\eta$ transition FF | Glueball mixing |
| $\bar{p}p \to \phi\phi$ | $\phi\phi$ threshold | $X_{s1}$ search |
| $\bar{p}p \to \Lambda_c\bar{\Lambda}_c$ | $\Lambda_c$ form factors | Charm spectroscopy |
| $\bar{p}p \to J/\psi \phi\phi$ | $X_{s1}$ production | Tetraquark $c\bar{c}s\bar{s}$ |

#### 5.3 Exotic Hadron Factory

- $\bar{p}p \to \chi_{c1} \pi$ for $P_c$ formation
- $\bar{p}p \to D_s\bar{D}^*$ for $Z_{cs}$ formation
- $\bar{p}p \to \eta_c \phi$ for $X_{s1}$ formation

Formation = known initial state $\to$ clean quantum numbers.

### 6. EIC (Electron-Ion Collider, 2030s)

#### 6.1 Machine Parameters

- $e^-$ (5-18 GeV) $\times$ $p/A$ (50-275 GeV)
- Luminosity: $10^{34} \text{ cm}^{-2}\text{s}^{-1}$
- Polarized beams: $e$ (80%), $p$ (70%)

#### 6.2 Pion/Kaon Structure

**GPDs and TMDs:**
- $\pi, K$ GPDs from $ep \to e' \pi^+ n$
- $\pi, K$ TMDs from SIDIS
- Gluonic content: $xg_\pi(x)$, $xg_K(x)$

**Form Factors:**
- $F_\pi(Q^2)$ up to $Q^2 = 50 \text{ GeV}^2$
- $F_K(Q^2)$ up to $Q^2 = 30 \text{ GeV}^2$
- Transition FFs: $\gamma^*\pi \to \pi\pi$

**Chiral Dynamics:**
- $\pi\pi$ scattering from $ep \to e' \pi^+\pi^- n$
- $\sigma$ pole from GPDs

#### 6.3 Kaon-Specific

- $K^+$ structure via $ep \to e' K^+ \Lambda$
- $K^0$ via $ep \to e' K^0 \Lambda$
- Strangeness GPDs: $H^s, E^s$

#### 6.4 3D Imaging

- Wigner distributions: $W(x, \vec{b}_\perp, \vec{k}_\perp)$
- Orbital angular momentum: $L_q = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\Delta\Sigma - L_g$
- Pion/kaon mechanical properties (pressure, shear forces)

### 8. One-Electron Universe: Experiments as Worldline Probes

In the one-electron universe, each experiment probes a different aspect of the worldline.

#### 8.1 $e^+e^-$ Colliders (BESIII, Belle II) = Worldline Pair Production

$e^+e^- \to$ hadrons = worldline loop creation from vacuum.

Resonance scans = worldline proper-time spectroscopy.

#### 8.2 Fixed Target (NA62, KOTO, J-PARC) = Worldline Scattering

$p + N \to$ strange hadrons = worldline kicked by external field.

Rare decays = worldline tunneling observed in flight.

#### 8.3 $\bar{p}p$ (PANDA) = Worldline Annihilation

$\bar{p}p \to X$ = worldline-antworldline annihilation.

Formation = controlled worldline topology creation.

#### 8.4 DIS (EIC) = Worldline Tomography

$e p \to e' X$ = photon probes worldline internal structure.

GPDs/TMDs = 3D worldline density maps.
