### 3. Baryon Mass Formula from Gap Statistics

#### 3.1 General Mass Formula

For a baryon with quark content $q_1 q_2 q_3$:
$$M_B = \sum_i m_{q_i} - B_B$$

where:
- $m_{q_i}$ from record gaps (Part 1 of One-Quark V4.0)
- Binding energy $B_B \approx 8-20$ MeV depending on strangeness

#### 4.2 Mass Formula from Gap Gaps

For a baryon with quark gap indices $d_1, d_2, d_3$:
$$M_B = m_e \left[ \left( \frac{d_1}{2} \right)^\delta + \left( \frac{d_2}{2} \right)^\delta + \left( \frac{d_3}{2} \right)^\delta \right] \times \mathcal{S} - B_B$$

where:
- $d_i$ are the record/near-twin gaps for each quark
- $\delta = 2.315$ (mass exponent from Electron V4.0)
- $\mathcal{S}$ is the binding energy suppression factor
