## 6.7 Mathematical Details of Knot Theory

### 6.7.1 Gauss Linking Integral

The linking number between two closed curves C₁ and C₂ is:

Lk(C₁, C₂) = (1/4π) ∮_{C₁} dx^μ ∮_{C₂} dy^ν ε_{μνρσ} (x-y)^ρ / |x-y|³

For a self-linking number of a single curve with framing:

SL = (1/4π) ∮ dx^μ ∮ dy^ν ε_{μνρσ} (x-y)^ρ / |x-y|³ + Twist

where the twist depends on the framing.

### 6.7.2 Calugareanu-White-Fuller Theorem

SL = Wr + Tw

where Wr is the writhe (geometric property of the curve) and Tw is the twist (framing-dependent). The writhe is:

Wr = (1/4π) ∮ dx^μ ∮ dy^ν ε_{μνρσ} (x-y)^ρ / |x-y|³

The twist is:

Tw = (1/2π) ∮ (d\mathbf{N}/ds · \mathbf{B}) ds

where \mathbf{N} is the normal vector and \mathbf{B} is the binormal.

### 6.7.3 Skein Relations

The Alexander polynomial satisfies:

Δ(L_+) - Δ(L_-) = (t^{1/2} - t^{-1/2}) Δ(L_0)

The Jones polynomial satisfies:

q V(L_+) - q⁻¹ V(L_-) = (q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2}) V(L_0)

The HOMFLY-PT polynomial satisfies:

l P(L_+) + l⁻¹ P(L_-) + m P(L_0) = 0

These skein relations allow recursive computation of the invariants.
