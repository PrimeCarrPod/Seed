### 1. GR Non-Linearities: Einstein Equations

**Einstein-Hilbert**: $\mathcal{L} = \frac{2}{\kappa^2} \sqrt{-g} R$
**Expansion**: $g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + \kappa h_{\mu\nu}$
**Vertices from $\sqrt{-g} R$**:
- 3-graviton: $\kappa \partial h \partial h h$
- 4-graviton: $\kappa^2 \partial h \partial h h h$
- n-graviton: $\kappa^{n-2} \dots$

**Gauge invariance**: Diffeomorphism symmetry → Ward identities

### 2. 3-Graviton Vertex in Momentum Space
