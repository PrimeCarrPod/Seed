### 2. Higgs-Gravity Coupling

**Non-minimal coupling**: $\xi H^\dagger H R$
**Beta function**: $\beta_\xi = \frac{1}{16\pi^2} (\xi + \frac{1}{6}) (6\lambda_H + \dots)$
**Fixed point**: $\xi_* \sim 0$ or $\xi_* \sim 1/6$ (conformal)

**Inflation**: $\xi \sim 10^4$ (Higgs inflation) — not at fixed point
