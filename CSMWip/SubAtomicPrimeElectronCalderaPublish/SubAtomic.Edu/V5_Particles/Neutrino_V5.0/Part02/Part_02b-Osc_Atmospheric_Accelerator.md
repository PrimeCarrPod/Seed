### 1. Atmospheric Neutrinos

**Source**: Cosmic rays → π/K → ν_μ, ν_e
**Zenith angle**: Upward (L ~ 13000 km) vs Downward (L ~ 20 km)
**Oscillation**: ν_μ → ν_τ (disappearance), ν_e appearance
**Super-K**: 
- ν_μ deficit at upward → $\Delta m^2_{31}$, $\sin^2\theta_{23} \approx 0.5$
- Multi-GeV e-like excess → ν_τ appearance
- 3-flavor analysis → $\theta_{23}$, $\delta_{CP}$, ordering hints

**KM3NeT/ORCA**: 
- 3-10 GeV atmospheric ν in Mediterranean
- Ordering sensitivity: 3-5σ (2025-2030)
- $\theta_{23}$ octant, $\delta_{CP}$

### 2. Accelerator Long-Baseline: T2K, NOvA

**T2K**: 295 km, ν_μ beam (0.6 GeV peak)
- ν_μ → ν_e appearance → $\theta_{13}$, $\delta_{CP}$
- ν_μ disappearance → $\Delta m^2_{31}$, $\theta_{23}$
- **Result**: $\delta_{CP} \approx 1.2\pi$ (favors CPV), NO preferred

**NOvA**: 810 km, ν_μ beam (2 GeV)
- ν_μ → ν_e appearance (higher E)
- ν_μ disappearance
- **Result**: Consistent with T2K, NO preferred

### 4. Mass Ordering: Normal vs Inverted

**Normal (NO)**: $m_1 < m_2 < m_3$, $\Delta m^2_{31} > 0$
**Inverted (IO)**: $m_3 < m_1 < m_2$, $\Delta m^2_{31} < 0$

**Current preference**: NO at $\sim 3\sigma$ (global fit)
**Decisive measurements**:
- **JUNO**: Reactor $\bar{\nu}_e$ spectrum → ordering at 3-4σ
- **DUNE**: Matter effect in 1300 km baseline → ordering at 5σ
- **KM3NeT**: Atmospheric ν matter effect → ordering at 3-5σ
- **IceCube/DeepCore**: High-E atmospheric ν
