### Abstract

Solar and reactor neutrino oscillations. $\Delta m^2_{21}$, $\theta_{12}$, $\theta_{13}$. MSW effect in Sun. JUNO precision. Worldline fold mixing.

### 1. Solar Neutrinos & MSW Effect

**pp chain**: pp, pep, $^7$Be, $^8$B, hep neutrinos
**Fluxes**: $pp \approx 6 \times 10^{10}$ cm⁻²s⁻¹, $^8$B $\approx 5 \times 10^6$ cm⁻²s⁻¹

**MSW effect**: 
- Vacuum: $P_{ee} \approx 1 - \sin^2 2\theta_{12} \sin^2(\Delta m^2 L/4E)$
- Matter: $V_{CC} = \sqrt{2} G_F n_e$ modifies Hamiltonian
- Adiabatic: $P_{ee} \approx \sin^2 \theta_{12}$ (high E), $\cos^2 \theta_{12}$ (low E)

**Borexino**: pp, $^7$Be, pep, CNO flux measurements
**Super-K**: $^8$B spectrum, day/night asymmetry
**SNO**: CC/NC separation → flavor conversion proof

---

### 2. Reactor Neutrinos: $\theta_{13}$ & $\Delta m^2_{21}$

**Reactors**: $\bar{\nu}_e$ from $^{235}$U, $^{238}$U, $^{239}$Pu, $^{241}$Pu
**Near/Far detectors**: Cancel flux/systematics
**Daya Bay, RENO, Double Chooz**: $\theta_{13}$ discovery (2012)
**JUNO (2024+)**: 20 kt LS, 53 km baseline → $\Delta m^2_{21}$, $\Delta m^2_{31}$, ordering

**Precision**: 
- $\sin^2 2\theta_{13} = 0.085 \pm 0.003$ (3%)
- $\Delta m^2_{21}$ to 0.5% (JUNO)

---

### 3. $\theta_{12}$ from Solar + Reactor

**Global fit**: $\theta_{12} = 33.4^\circ \pm 0.8^\circ$
**Solar**: MSW → $\sin^2 \theta_{12} \approx 0.31$
**Reactor (KamLAND)**: $\Delta m^2_{21}$ confirmation
**Tension**: Slight ($\sim 1\sigma$) between solar and reactor $\theta_{12}$

---

### 4. Worldline Picture: Solar/Reactor as Fold Mixing

**Solar ν** = Electron fold → μ/τ fold via MSW
**Reactor ν̄** = Antineutrino backward fold
**$\theta_{12}$** = Electron fold ↔ μ/τ fold mixing angle
**$\theta_{13}$** = Electron fold ↔ 3rd fold small mixing
