### 1. Matter Effects & MSW Resonance

**Hamiltonian in matter**: $H = \frac{1}{2E} U \text{diag}(0, \Delta m^2_{21}, \Delta m^2_{31}) U^\dagger + \text{diag}(V_{CC}, 0, 0)$

**MSW resonance** (2-flavor):
$$\Delta m^2 \cos 2\theta = 2E V_{CC}$$
$$V_{CC} = \sqrt{2} G_F n_e$$

**Solar**: Adiabatic MSW for $^8$B neutrinos
**Earth**: Day/night effect (regeneration in Earth)
**Supernova**: Collective effects, fast flavor conversion

### 3. Non-Standard Interactions (NSI)

**Effective Lagrangian**:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{NSI}} = -2\sqrt{2} G_F \varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{fP} (\bar{\nu}_\alpha \gamma^\mu P_L \nu_\beta) (\bar{f} \gamma_\mu P f)$$

**Parameters**: $\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{fP}$ (flavor $\alpha,\beta$, fermion $f$, chirality $P$)

**Effects**:
- Modify matter potential: $V_{CC} \to V_{CC}(1 + \varepsilon_{ee})$
- New CPV phases: $\varepsilon_{e\mu}, \varepsilon_{e\tau}$
- Degeneracies with $\delta_{CP}, \theta_{23}$

**Constraints** (90% CL):
- $\varepsilon_{ee} < 0.1$, $\varepsilon_{\mu\mu} < 0.01$
- $\varepsilon_{e\mu}, \varepsilon_{e\tau} < 0.01$

### 4. Sterile Neutrinos (3+1)

**LSND/MiniBooNE anomaly**: $\bar{\nu}_\mu \to \bar{\nu}_e$ at $\Delta m^2 \sim 1$ eV²
**Reactor anomaly**: Flux deficit at short baselines
**Gallium anomaly**: $^{51}$Cr, $^{37}$Ar sources → deficit

**3+1 model**: $m_4 \sim 1$ eV, $|U_{e4}|^2 \sim 0.02$, $|U_{\mu4}|^2 \sim 0.01$

**Status**: 
- IceCube/DeepCore: Excludes most 3+1 parameter space
- MicroBooNE: No low-E excess
- Global fit: Tension between appearance/disappearance
