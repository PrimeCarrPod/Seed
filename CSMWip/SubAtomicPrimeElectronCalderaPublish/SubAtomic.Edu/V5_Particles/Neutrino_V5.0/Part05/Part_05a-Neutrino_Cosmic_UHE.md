### 1. Cosmic Neutrino Background (CνB)

**Relic neutrinos**: Decoupled at $T \sim 1$ MeV, $t \sim 1$ s
**Temperature**: $T_\nu = (4/11)^{1/3} T_\gamma = 1.95$ K
**Density**: $n_\nu = 336$ cm⁻³ per flavor (56 cm⁻³ per $\nu + \bar{\nu}$)
**Energy**: $E_\nu \sim 10^{-4}$ eV (non-relativistic today for $m_\nu > 0.1$ eV)

**Detection**: 
- **PTOLEMY**: $\nu_e + {}^3\text{H} \to {}^3\text{He} + e^-$ (no threshold)
- **CνB capture**: Monoenergetic peak at $Q + m_\nu$
- **Sensitivity**: $N \sim 10$ events/yr for 100 g $^3$H, $m_\nu = 0.1$ eV

---

### 2. Ultra-High-Energy (UHE) Neutrinos

**Sources**: 
- **Cosmogenic**: $p + \gamma_{\text{CMB}} \to \Delta^+ \to \pi^+ \to \nu_\mu$ (GZK)
- **Astrophysical**: AGN, GRB, TDE, starburst galaxies
- **New physics**: Top-down (superheavy DM, cosmic strings)

**Energy range**: $10^{15} - 10^{20}$ eV
**Flux**: $E^2 \Phi \sim 10^{-8}$ GeV cm⁻² s⁻¹ sr⁻¹ (IceCube)

---

### 3. IceCube & High-Energy Neutrino Astronomy

**IceCube**: 1 km³ at South Pole
**Discoveries**:
- **Astrophysical flux**: $E^2 \Phi \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$ GeV cm⁻² s⁻¹ sr⁻¹
- **First source**: TXS 0506+056 (blazar, 2017) — $\gamma$ + ν coincidence
- **Glashow resonance**: $\bar{\nu}_e + e^- \to W^-$ at 6.3 PeV (2021)
- **Steady sources**: NGC 1068 (Seyfert), TXS 0506+056

**IceCube-Gen2**: 8× volume, radio array for >10 PeV
