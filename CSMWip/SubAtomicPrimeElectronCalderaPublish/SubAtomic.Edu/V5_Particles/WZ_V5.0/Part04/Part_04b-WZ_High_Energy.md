## Part 4 of 14 — W/Z at High Energy: VBF, $VV, Vh$, Unitarity, High-$p_T$

### Abstract

High-energy W/Z production. VBF, $VV, Vh$. Longitudinal $W/Z$ scattering unitarity. Higgs as unitarizer. High-$p_T$ tails. BSM sensitivity.

---

### 1. VBF at High Energy

**Process**: $qq \to qq V$ (forward jets)
**Amplitude**: $W_L W_L \to V$ fusion
**Longitudinal $W$**: $\epsilon_L^\mu \approx p^\mu / m_W$

**Unitarity**: $W_L W_L \to W_L W_L$ violates unitarity at $\sqrt{s} \sim 1.2$ TeV
**Higgs exchange**: $W_L W_L \to h \to W_L W_L$ restores unitarity
**$V$ exchange**: $W_L W_L \to V \to W_L W_L$ (cancels bad growth)

**High-$p_T$ VBF**: $p_T^V > 500$ GeV → probes $\kappa_V$ at high scale

---

### 2. $VV$ at High Energy

**$VV$** ($VV = WW, WZ, ZZ$): High $m_{VV}$ → sensitive to aTGCs, EFT
**VBF $VV$**: $m_{VV} > 1$ TeV → pure gauge couplings at high scale
**Unitarity**: $W_L W_L \to VV$ violates unitarity without Higgs

**High-energy theorems**: 
- $W_L W_L \to V$ amplitude $\to \kappa_V$
- $W_L W_L \to VV$ amplitude $\to$ gauge couplings

---

### 3. $Vh$ at High Energy

**Process**: $qq \to qq Vh$ (VBF $Vh$)
**Amplitude**: $W_L W_L \to Vh$ fusion
**$Vh$ as unitarizer**: $W_L W_L \to Vh$ cancels $W_L W_L \to VV$ growth

**High-$p_T$ $Vh$**: 
- $p_T^h > 500$ GeV → $\kappa_V, \kappa_t$ at high scale
- $p_T^V > 500$ GeV → gauge couplings at high scale

**SMEFT**: $\mathcal{O}_{HG}, \mathcal{O}_{HW}$ modify $Vh$ at high $p_T$

---

### 4. Longitudinal $W/Z$ Scattering

**$W_L W_L \to W_L W_L$**: $\mathcal{A} \sim s/v^2$ (violates unitarity)
**Higgs exchange**: $\mathcal{A}_h \sim -s/v^2$ (cancels)
**$V$ exchange**: $\mathcal{A}_V \sim -s/v^2$ (cancels)
**Total**: $\mathcal{A}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{O}(1)$ (unitary)

**LHC measurement**: 
- $W^\pm W^\pm$ scattering (VBF same-sign $WW$)
- $\sigma_{\text{fid}} = 2.9 \pm 0.5$ fb (ATLAS)
- Consistent with SM unitarization

---

### 5. Higgs as Unitarizer: Worldline Picture

**Without Higgs**: Worldline EW folds scatter strongly → unitarity violation
**With Higgs**: Stiffness exchange cancels bad high-energy behavior
**$V$ exchange**: EW vertex self-interaction cancels growth
**$Vh$**: Stiffness + gauge vertex combination

**Unitarity bound**: $\sqrt{s} < 4\pi v \sim 3$ TeV without Higgs

---

### 6. High-$p_T$ $V$ as BSM Probe

**SMEFT at high $p_T$**:
- $\mathcal{O}_{HG}$: $h VV$ contact → $p_T^h$ enhancement
- $\mathcal{O}_{HW}$: $W_L W_L \to V$ at high $p_T$
- $\mathcal{O}_{HWB}$: $\delta \kappa_V$ → high-$p_T$ VBF

**Reach**: $p_T^V > 500$ GeV → $\Lambda \sim 1-2$ TeV
