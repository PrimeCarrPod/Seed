# W/Z Boson Physics — V5.0 Deep Dive Series
## Part 5 of 14 — W/Z Width & Rare Decays: $\Gamma_W, \Gamma_Z$, $Z \to \ell\ell, \nu\nu$, $W \to \ell\nu$, Exotics

---

### Abstract

W/Z total and partial widths. Rare decays: $Z \to \ell\ell, \nu\nu$, $W \to \ell\nu$, flavor-changing, invisible. BSM width contributions. Future precision.

---

### 1. Z Total & Partial Widths

**$\Gamma_Z = 2.4952 \pm 0.0023$ GeV** (LEP)
**Partial widths**:
- $\Gamma_{\text{had}} = 1.7444 \pm 0.0020$ GeV
- $\Gamma_{\ell\ell} = 83.984 \pm 0.086$ MeV ($\ell = e, \mu, \tau$)
- $\Gamma_{\nu\nu} = 167.1 \pm 0.4$ MeV (per $\nu$, $N_\nu = 2.984$)
- $\Gamma_{b\bar{b}} = 377.3 \pm 1.1$ MeV
- $\Gamma_{c\bar{c}} = 299.8 \pm 1.5$ MeV

**FCC-ee**: $\Gamma_Z$ to **0.025 MeV**, partial widths to **0.1%**

---

### 2. W Width & Branching Ratios

**$\Gamma_W = 2.085 \pm 0.042$ GeV** (LEP + LHC)
**Branching ratios**:
- $W \to \ell\nu$: $10.86 \pm 0.09\%$ per $\ell = e, \mu, \tau$
- $W \to \text{hadrons}$: $67.41 \pm 0.27\%$
- $W \to c\bar{s}$: $95\%$ of $W \to \text{hadrons}$ (CKM)

**LHC**: $W \to \ell\nu$ lineshape → $\Gamma_W$ to $\sim 2\%$
**FCC-ee**: $\Gamma_W$ from $WW$ threshold → **1 MeV**

---

### 3. Rare Z Decays

**$Z \to \ell\ell$**: $3.3658 \pm 0.0023\%$ per $\ell$
**$Z \to \nu\nu$**: $20.00 \pm 0.06\%$ per $\nu$ (invisible)
**$Z \to b\bar{b}$**: $15.12 \pm 0.05\%$
**$Z \to c\bar{c}$**: $12.03 \pm 0.06\%$

**Rare SM**:
- $Z \to \gamma\gamma$: $BR \sim 10^{-5}$ (loop)
- $Z \to J/\psi \gamma$: $BR \sim 10^{-6}$
- $Z \to \gamma\gamma\gamma$: $BR \sim 10^{-7}$

---

### 4. Rare W Decays

**$W \to \ell\nu$**: $10.86\%$ per $\ell$
**$W \to \pi\nu$**: $BR \sim 10^{-4}$ (hadronic)
**$W \to D_s \nu$**: $BR \sim 10^{-2}$

**Rare SM**:
- $W \to \gamma\nu$: $BR \sim 10^{-5}$ (loop)
- $W \to \pi\gamma$: $BR \sim 10^{-6}$

---

### 5. Flavor-Changing & Exotic Decays

**$Z \to e\mu, e\tau, \mu\tau$**: $BR < 10^{-6}$ (LFV)
**$Z \to q_i \bar{q}_j$ ($i \neq j$)**: $BR < 10^{-5}$ (FCNC)
**$W \to e\nu_\mu, \mu\nu_e$**: $BR < 10^{-6}$ (LFV)

**BSM**: 
- $Z \to \text{DM DM}$ (invisible)
- $Z \to \text{hidden sector}$
- $W \to \text{exotic}$

---

### 6. Invisible Width & BSM

**$Z \to \text{inv}$**: $\Gamma_{\text{inv}} = N_\nu \Gamma_{\nu\bar{\nu}} + \Gamma_{\text{BSM}}$
- $N_\nu = 2.984 \pm 0.008$ (SM: 3)
- $\Gamma_{\text{inv}} < 2.0$ MeV (95% CL)

**$W \to \text{inv}$**: Not directly measurable

**BSM**: 
- $Z \to \chi\chi$ (DM)
- $Z \to \text{hidden}$
- $W \to \text{exotic}$

---

### 7. Worldline Picture: Widths as EW Vertex Lifetimes

**$\Gamma_Z$** = Neutral EW vertex lifetime
**$\Gamma_W$** = Charged EW vertex lifetime
**Partial widths** = EW vertex decay channels
**Rare decays** = Suppressed EW vertex channels
**Invisible** = EW vertex decay to hidden sector
