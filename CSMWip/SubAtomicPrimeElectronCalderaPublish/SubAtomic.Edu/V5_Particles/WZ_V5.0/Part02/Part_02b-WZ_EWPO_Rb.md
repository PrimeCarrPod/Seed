### 3. $R_b$ & $R_c$: Heavy Quark Couplings

**$R_b = \Gamma(Z \to b\bar{b})/\Gamma_{\text{had}}$**: 
- Sensitive to $m_t$ (vertex correction)
- Sensitive to BSM: $Z \to b\bar{b}$ vertex corrections

**$R_c = \Gamma(Z \to c\bar{c})/\Gamma_{\text{had}}$**:
- Less sensitive to $m_t$
- Complementary to $R_b$

**SMEFT**: $\mathcal{O}_{Hq}^{(3)}, \mathcal{O}_{Hb}$ modify $Zb\bar{b}$ vertex

---

### 4. $A_{FB}^{0,f}$ & Polarization Asymmetries

**Forward-backward**: $A_{FB}^{0,f} = \frac{3}{4} A_e A_f$
**Left-right**: $A_{LR} = A_e = 2g_V^e g_A^e / (g_V^2 + g_A^2)$

**SLD**: $A_{LR} = 0.1513 \pm 0.0021$ (polarized beams)
**LEP**: $A_{FB}^{0,b} = 0.0996 \pm 0.0016$

**Polarization**: $\tau$ polarization at LEP → $A_\tau = 0.1439 \pm 0.0043$
