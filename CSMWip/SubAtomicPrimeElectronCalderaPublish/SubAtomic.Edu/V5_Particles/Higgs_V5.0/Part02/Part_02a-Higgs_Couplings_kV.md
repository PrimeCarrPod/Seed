# Higgs Physics — V5.0 Deep Dive Series
## Part 2 of 14 — Higgs Couplings: Gauge Couplings $\kappa_V$ ($h \to ZZ^*, WW^*$)

---

### Abstract

Higgs gauge couplings $\kappa_V = g_{hVV}/g_{hVV}^{\text{SM}}$. $h \to ZZ^* \to 4\ell$, $h \to WW^* \to \ell\nu\ell\nu$. VBF, VH production. N3LO predictions. Global $\kappa_V$ fit.

---

### 1. $h \to ZZ^* \to 4\ell$: Golden Channel

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to ZZ^*) \approx 2.6\%$ at $m_h=125$ GeV
**Cross section**: $\sigma(pp \to h \to ZZ^*) \approx 1.2$ pb at 13 TeV
**Signature**: 4 isolated leptons ($e/\mu$), narrow mass peak
**Background**: $q\bar{q} \to ZZ$, $gg \to ZZ$ (irreducible)

**Measurement**: 
- ATLAS: $\mu_{ZZ} = 1.02 \pm 0.07$
- CMS: $\mu_{ZZ} = 1.05 \pm 0.08$
- Combined: $\kappa_Z = 1.03 \pm 0.04$

---

### 2. $h \to WW^* \to \ell\nu\ell\nu$: High Statistics

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to WW^*) \approx 21.5\%$
**Signature**: 2 leptons + $E_T^{\text{miss}}$, no mass peak
**Background**: $WW$, $t\bar{t}$, $Z/\gamma^* \to \tau\tau$

**Measurement**:
- ATLAS: $\mu_{WW} = 1.08 \pm 0.08$
- CMS: $\mu_{WW} = 1.00 \pm 0.07$
- Combined: $\kappa_W = 1.03 \pm 0.05$

---

### 3. VBF & VH Production: Direct $\kappa_V$ Probe

**VBF**: $qq \to qq h$ (forward jets, rapidity gap)
- $\sigma_{\text{VBF}} \approx 3.8$ pb at 13 TeV
- Pure $\kappa_V$ sensitivity

**VH**: $q\bar{q} \to Wh, Zh$ (leptonic $W/Z$)
- $\sigma_{WH} \approx 1.4$ pb, $\sigma_{ZH} \approx 0.9$ pb
- $\kappa_V$ from production × decay

**Combined $\kappa_V$**: $1.03 \pm 0.05$ (ATLAS+CMS Run 2)

---

### 4. N3LO QCD + NLO EW for $h \to VV$

**gg→h→VV**: N3LO QCD (2024) + NLO EW
**VBF/VH**: NNLO QCD + NLO EW
**Differential**: $p_T^h$, $y_h$, jet veto

**Scale uncertainty**: $\delta \sigma/\sigma \sim 1-2\%$ (N3LO)

---

### 5. Worldline Picture: Gauge Couplings as EW Fold-Stiffness

**EW fold** (W/Z) + **Stiffness** (Higgs) = Mass
**Coupling $\kappa_V$** = EW fold response to stiffness
**VBF** = EW fold scattering via stiffness exchange
