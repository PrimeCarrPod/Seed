### 2. Forward Physics: FASER, FASERν, FORMOSA

**FASER** (LHC Run 3): 480 m downstream of ATLAS
- Dark photons, axion-like particles, millicharged particles
- ν detection (FASERν) — first collider neutrinos

**FORMOSA**: Forward detector for millicharged particles
