### 1. Gluon Condensates at T>0

**⟨G²⟩_T**: 
$$\langle \frac{α_s}{π} G² \rangle_T = \langle \frac{α_s}{π} G² \rangle_0 \left(1 - \frac{T^4}{T_c^4}\right) \text{ for } T < T_c$$

**Lattice**: Sudden drop at T_c (crossover for physical masses)

**Polyakov loop**: ⟨L⟩ = 0 (confined) → ⟨L⟩ > 0 (deconfined)
**Gluonic**: ⟨tr F_{μν}F^{μν}⟩ → order parameter for pure gauge

---

### 2. QCD Phase Diagram

**T-μ plane**:
- T=0, μ=0: Hadron gas
- T>T_c, μ=0: QGP (crossover)
- T=0, μ>μ_c: Color superconductor (CFL, 2SC)
- **Critical point**: (T_c, μ_c) — endpoint of 1st order line

**Gluon role**: 
- Deconfinement: Gluon liberation (Polyakov loop)
- Chiral: Gluon-mediated quark interaction

**Lattice at μ>0**: Sign problem — det M(μ) not real
**Methods**: Taylor expansion, imaginary μ, reweighting, complex Langevin

---

### 3. Gluon at Finite μ: Color Superconductivity

**Diquark condensation**: ⟨qq⟩ ≠ 0
**CFL phase** (μ ≫ Λ_QCD): All quarks paired, gluons massive (Meissner)
**2SC phase**: Two flavors paired, one unpaired

**Gluon masses**: 
- Meissner: m_M² ~ g²μ²/π²
- Debye: m_D² ~ g²μ²/π²

**Worldline**: Finite μ = chemical potential = worldline temporal winding number.

### 4. Lattice QCD at μ>0 (2024 Progress)

**Taylor expansion**: P/T⁴ = Σ c_n(T) (μ/T)²ⁿ — c_n from lattice at μ=0
**Imaginary μ**: μ = iμ_I — no sign problem, analytic continuation
**Functional renormalization**: FRG with gluon/quark vertices

**Results**: Critical point search, T_c(μ) curvature κ = 0.015(3)

### 5. Early Universe: Gluon at Cosmological T

**T ~ 100 GeV**: EW phase transition (gluons in plasma)
**T ~ 1 GeV**: QCD crossover — gluons hadronize
**T ~ 1 MeV**: BBN — gluons gone
**Gravitational waves**: QCD transition → stochastic GW background

**Worldline**: Cosmological expansion = worldline stretching.
