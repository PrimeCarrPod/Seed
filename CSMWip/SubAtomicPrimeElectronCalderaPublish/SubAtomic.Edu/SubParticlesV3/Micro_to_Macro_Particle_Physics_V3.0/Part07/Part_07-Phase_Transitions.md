## 1. Order Parameter and Symmetry Breaking

### 1.1 Landau Free Energy

$$F[\phi] = \int d^dx \left[ \frac{a}{2} \phi^2 + \frac{b}{4} \phi^4 + \frac{c}{2} (\nabla \phi)^2 \right]$$

Critical temperature: $a(T_c) = 0$.

### 1.2 Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking

For $b > 0$:

$$\phi_0 = \pm \sqrt{-\frac{a}{b}} \quad \text{for } a < 0$$
