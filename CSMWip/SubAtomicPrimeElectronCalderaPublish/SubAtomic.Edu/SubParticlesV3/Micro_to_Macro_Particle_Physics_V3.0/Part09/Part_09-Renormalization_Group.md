# V3.0 Deep Dive Series: Micro to Macro Particle Physics
## Part 9 of 14 — Renormalization Group and Scale Invariance

---

### Abstract

This installment develops the renormalization group formalism for understanding how microscopic physics flows to macroscopic scales, including beta functions and fixed points.

---

## 1. Renormalization Group Equation

### 1.1 Scale Dependence

$$\left[ \mu \frac{\partial}{\partial \mu} + \beta(g) \frac{\partial}{\partial g} + \gamma_m m \frac{\partial}{\partial m} + \cdots \right] \mathcal{O} = 0$$

### 1.2 Beta Function

For a gauge coupling:

$$\beta(g) = -\frac{g^3}{16\pi^2} \left( \frac{11}{3} C_2(G) - \frac{4}{3} T(R) n_f \right)$$

In QCD: $b_0 = \frac{11}{3} C_A - \frac{4}{3} T_F n_f$.

---

## 2. Asymptotic Freedom and Infrared Freedom

### 2.1 QCD Asymptotic Freedom

For $N_c = 3$, $n_f = 6$:

$$\beta(g) < 0 \text{ for } g < g^* \approx 1.6$$

### 2.2 Infrared Seduction

$$\alpha_s(Q^2) \sim \frac{1}{\ln(Q^2/\Lambda^2)}$$

as $Q^2 \rightarrow \Lambda^2$: $\alpha_s \rightarrow \infty$.

---

## 3. Fixed Points and Universality Classes

### 3.1 Gaussian Fixed Point

$g^* = 0$: free theory.

### 3.2 Interacting Fixed Point

Non-zero $g^*$ gives the critical behavior of phase transitions.
