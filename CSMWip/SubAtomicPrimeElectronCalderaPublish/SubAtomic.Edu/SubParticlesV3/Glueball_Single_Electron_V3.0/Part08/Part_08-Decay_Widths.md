## 1. Optical Theorem and Total Decay Width

### 1.1 Forward Scattering Amplitude

The total width is related to the imaginary part of the forward scattering amplitude:

$$\Gamma_{\text{total}} = \frac{\text{Im} \mathcal{M}(G \rightarrow \text{all})}{\sqrt{s}}$$

### 1.2 Unitarity Condition

The S-matrix unitarity condition:

$$\text{Im} T = i T^\dagger T$$

For glueball decays:

$$\Gamma_G = \frac{1}{2M_G} \sum_f \int d\Pi_f \, |\mathcal{M}(G \rightarrow f)|^2$$

## 2. Decay to Two Gluons

### 2.1 Matrix Element Squared

The decay $G \rightarrow gg$ amplitude:

$$|\mathcal{M}|^2 = \frac{g^4}{4} \left( \epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta} \varepsilon_1^\mu \varepsilon_2^\nu \varepsilon_G^{\,\alpha} \varepsilon_G^{\,\beta} \right)^2$$

Averaging over colors and spins:

$$\sum |\mathcal{M}|^2 = \frac{9 g^4}{64} \left( k \cdot \varepsilon_1 \right)^2 \left( k \cdot \varepsilon_2 \right)^2$$

### 2.2 Two-Body Phase Space

The phase space integral:

$$\Gamma(G \rightarrow gg) = \frac{1}{8\pi M_G} \int d\Pi_2 = \frac{1}{8\pi M_G} \frac{|\vec{p}|}{M_G^2}$$

with $|\vec{p}| = M_G/2$ for massless decay products.
