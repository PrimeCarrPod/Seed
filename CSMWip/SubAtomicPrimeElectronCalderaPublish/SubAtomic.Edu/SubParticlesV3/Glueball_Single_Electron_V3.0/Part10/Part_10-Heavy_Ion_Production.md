### Abstract

This installment examines glueball production mechanisms in relativistic heavy-ion collisions, including thermal production in the quark-gluon plasma and compressed baryonic matter phases.

## 1. Thermal Glueball Production

### 1.1 Statistical Model

The Cooper-Frye formula for glueball production:

$$\frac{dN_G}{d^4p} = \int d\sigma_\mu p^\mu \left[ f_B(E_p) - f_F(E_p) \right]$$

where $f_B = (e^{E_p/T} - 1)^{-1}$ is the Bose-Einstein distribution and $f_F = (e^{E_p/T} + 1)^{-1}$ is the Fermi-Dirac distribution.

### 1.2 Yield Calculation

The total multiplicity:

$$N_G = \int \frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3} \frac{g_G}{e^{E_p/T} - 1}$$

At freeze-out temperature $T_{\text{fo}} = 155 \, \text{MeV}$ and $\tau_{\text{fo}} = 8 \, \text{fm}/c$:

$$N_G \approx \frac{4\pi g_G (m_G T)^2 K_2(m_G/T)}{h^3} \tau_{\text{fo}} \sigma_{\text{beam}}$$

## 2. Compressed Baryonic Matter Phase

### 2.1 In-Medium Modification

The medium-modified glueball mass:

$$m_G^*(\rho) = m_G \sqrt{1 + \frac{g_A}{m_G^2} \rho}$$

where $\rho$ is the local baryon density and $g_A$ is the coupling to baryonic matter.

### 2.2 Solvent-Surfactant Model

The glueball-Solvent interaction parameter:

$$\chi_G = \frac{g_{\text{GS}}}{\sqrt{g_G g_S}}$$

affects the dissociation temperature:

$$T_d = T_0 \exp(-\chi_G)$$
