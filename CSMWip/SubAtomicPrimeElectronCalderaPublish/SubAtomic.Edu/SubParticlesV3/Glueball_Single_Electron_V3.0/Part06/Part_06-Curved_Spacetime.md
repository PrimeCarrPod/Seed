## 1. Klein-Gordon Equation in Curved Space

### 1.1 General Covariant Formulation

For a scalar glueball with mass $m$:

$$(\Box + m^2 + \xi R) \phi = 0$$

where $\Box = g^{\mu\nu} \nabla_\mu \nabla_\nu$ is the D'Alembertian and $\xi$ is the non-minimal coupling constant.

### 1.2 Schwarzschild Spacetime

In Schwarzschild coordinates:

$$ds^2 = -\left(1 - \frac{2GM}{r}\right) dt^2 + \left(1 - \frac{2GM}{r}\right)^{-1} dr^2 + r^2 d\Omega^2$$

The radial equation for a glueball with angular momentum $l$:

$$\frac{d^2 R}{dr^2} + \frac{2}{r}\left(1 - \frac{2GM}{r}\right)^{-1} \frac{dR}{dr} + \left[ \frac{m^2}{\left(1 - \frac{2GM}{r}\right)} - \frac{l(l+1)}{r^2} - \frac{2GM}{r^2} \left(\xi - \frac{1}{4}\right) \right] R = 0$$

## 2. Hawking Radiation and Glueball Production

### 2.1 Tunneling Probability

The tunneling rate for glueball pair production near a black hole horizon:

$$\Gamma \sim e^{-S_E}$$

where $S_E$ is the Euclidean action difference between instanton configurations.

### 2.2 Thermal Distribution

The glueball number distribution:

$$n(E) = \frac{1}{e^{E/k_B T} - 1}$$

where $T = \frac{\kappa}{2\pi}$ is the Hawking temperature and $\kappa$ is the surface gravity.
