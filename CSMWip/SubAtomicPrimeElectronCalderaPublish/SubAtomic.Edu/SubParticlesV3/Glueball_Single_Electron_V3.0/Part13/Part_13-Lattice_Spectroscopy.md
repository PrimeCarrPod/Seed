## 1. Lattice Methodology

### 1.1 Correlation Functions

The temporal correlation function:

$$C(t) = \sum_{\vec{x}} \langle O(\vec{x},t) O^\dagger(0,0) \rangle = \sum_n A_n e^{-E_n t}$$

### 1.2 Variational Method

Using multiple operators $O_i$:

$$C_{ij}(t) = \langle O_i(\vec{x},t) O_j^\dagger(0,0) \rangle$$

The generalized eigenvalue problem:

$$C(t) v^{(n)} = \lambda^n(t,t_0) C(t_0) v^{(n)}$$
