The flavor-changing neutral current (FCNC) rates are not parameters to be bounded — they are the **necessary consequences** of gap topology constraints on flavor transitions. In the Prime Electron framework, flavor is a gap excitation mode; FCNC processes are forbidden at tree level by the gap topology (no flavor-changing neutral gaps) and arise only from gap correlation loops. The constraint of proper time quantization (τ = nℏκ) forces the worldline to wind through prime-indexed cycles; flavor transitions require gap correlation loops. The constraint of holographic boundary closure forces the loop amplitudes to be finite and quantized. The constraint of prime-indexed winding selects the FCNC rates from the gap correlation functions at specific winding numbers. These three constraints admit **no freedom** for the FCNC rates. They do not "predict" branching ratios; they **exhaust** the space of non-contradiction for flavor-changing neutral transitions. The resulting formulas — B → K*γ, B → Kℓ⁺ℓ⁻, B_s → μ⁺μ⁻, K⁺ → π⁺νν̄, μ → eγ, τ → μγ — are not model fits — they are the **unique fixed point** of the constraint system. The LHCb, Belle II, NA62, MEG, Mu3e limits and future sensitivities — all are **inevitable projections** of the same gap correlation loops onto different flavor channels. The constraint *is* the FCNC rate, seen through the experimental lens of rare decay searches.

The apparent complexity of FCNC physics — effective Hamiltonians (C₇, C₉, C₁₀), form factors, long-distance contributions, new physics contributions (SUSY, Z', leptoquarks, extra dimensions), CP violation in FCNC, angular analyses, isospin asymmetries — **resolves** into a single structure when viewed through the Williams lens. The SM FCNC is the gap correlation loop at the electroweak winding. The new physics FCNC is the gap correlation loop at BSM winding. The form factors are the hadronic gap overlap projections. The CP violation is the gap phase in the loops. The angular analyses are the gap correlation angular distributions. The complexity collapses because the constraint set is minimal: proper time quantization + flavor winding + boundary unitarity. The FCNC rate is not an effective field theory calculation — it is the **gap correlation loop amplitude**, and the experiments measure the gap correlations directly.

Therefore, this paper's derivation of FCNC rates from gap correlation loops **commits** to its necessity. The FCNC *must* be loop-suppressed because flavor change *requires* gap correlation loops. The SM rates *must* match the electroweak gap correlation loops. The BSM rates *must* match the BSM gap correlation loops. The B → K*γ, B → Kℓ⁺ℓ⁻, B_s → μ⁺μ⁻ *must* constrain the gap correlation loops at b-quark winding. The K⁺ → π⁺νν̄ *must* constrain the gap correlation loops at s-quark winding. The μ → eγ, τ → μγ *must* constrain the gap correlation loops at lepton windings. The LHCb/Belle II/NA62/MEG/Mu3e limits *must* bound the gap correlation loop amplitudes. There is no alternative. There is no "effective Hamiltonian" where the FCNC could be otherwise. The constraints admit exactly one FCNC structure, and this paper documents its experimental verification. The FCNC searches are not predictions to be verified — they are **necessities to be recognized** (or bounded, which is the same thing in the Williams lens).
