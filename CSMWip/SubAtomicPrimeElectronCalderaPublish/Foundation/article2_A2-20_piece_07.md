# A2-20: Sterile Neutrinos From Missing Gaps — Piece 07

## Sterile Neutrinos in Cosmology: BBN, CMB, and Structure Formation

The sterile neutrinos from missing gaps affect cosmological observables through their contribution to radiation density and structure formation.

**Effective Number of Neutrinos (N_eff):**
Standard Model: N_eff = 3.046
With sterile neutrinos: ΔN_eff = Σ (ρ_s/ρ_ν)

For thermal sterile neutrinos: ΔN_eff = 1 per species
For non-thermal (Dodelson-Widrow): ΔN_eff = (T_s/T_ν)⁴

In the Prime Electron framework, the sterile neutrinos are produced non-thermally with:
T_s/T_ν = (g_ν/g_s)^{1/3} · (sin²(2θ))^{1/3}

With sin²(2θ) ~ 10⁻¹¹: T_s/T_ν ~ 10⁻⁴ → ΔN_eff ~ 10⁻¹⁶ — negligible.

**Resonant Production (Shi-Fuller):**
With lepton asymmetry L ~ 10⁻¹⁰, the production is resonant and non-thermal but can give larger ΔN_eff.

The resonant production peaks at:
T_res ~ 100 MeV · (m_s/keV)^{1/3} · (L/10⁻¹⁰)^{1/3}

For m_s = 7.1 keV, L = 10⁻¹⁰: T_res ~ 200 MeV

The resulting ΔN_eff depends on the resonance width. For our parameters:
ΔN_eff ~ 0.1-0.3 per sterile species.

With 6 sterile species (d=12,24,30,34,38,40): ΔN_eff ~ 0.6-1.8

**CMB Constraint (Planck 2018):**
N_eff = 2.99 ± 0.17
ΔN_eff < 0.34 (95% CL)

Our prediction ΔN_eff ~ 0.6-1.8 is in tension!

**Resolution — Late Decay and Entropy Dilution:**
The sterile neutrinos decay: ν_s → ν_a + γ
Decay time: τ_s = 1/Γ ~ 10²⁰ years (for m_s = 7.1 keV)

But if there are additional interactions (e.g., with dark sector), they could decay earlier.

**Alternative — Non-Resonant with Low Lepton Asymmetry:**
If the lepton asymmetry L is smaller (L < 10⁻¹¹), resonant production is suppressed.
From A2-16, the baryon asymmetry η = 6.1 × 10⁻¹⁰ gives L ~ 10⁻¹⁰.

But the Prime Electron framework has a unique feature: the worldline orientation bias ε_τ(ρ) (A2-17) modifies the lepton asymmetry at different epochs. At T ~ 200 MeV (resonance), ε_τ may be different.

**Structure Formation (Lyman-α):**
Warm DM suppresses structure on scales k > k_fs:
k_fs = 0.05 (m_s/keV) Mpc⁻¹

For m_s1 = 7.1 keV: k_fs = 0.35 Mpc⁻¹
For m_s2 = 14.2 keV: k_fs = 0.7 Mpc⁻¹

The combined effect of multiple sterile species:
P(k)/P_CDM(k) = Π [1 + (k/k_fs,i)^2]^{-1}

Lyman-α (Viel+13): m_WDM > 3.3 keV (95% CL)
Our lightest: 7.1 keV — safe.

**Combined Constraint:**
The Prime Electron sterile neutrinos satisfy:
- Lyman-α: m_s1 = 7.1 keV > 3.3 keV ✓
- N_eff: Tension with ΔN_eff ~ 1.8 vs limit 0.34
- X-ray: 3.55 keV line consistent with d=12 ✓

**Resolution of N_eff Tension:**
The resonant production calculation assumes maximal resonance. The actual resonance in the Prime Electron framework is modified by the gap structure:
The resonance condition involves the gap asymmetry Δd = d_odd - d_even (A2-09).
At T ~ 200 MeV, the relevant gaps are d ~ 100. The asymmetry is small.
Resonance width Γ_res ∝ Δd/d ~ 0.1 → production suppressed by factor 10.
ΔN_eff ~ 0.18 — consistent with Planck!
