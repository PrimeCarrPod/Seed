## 1. Nitrogen Cycle Equations

### 1.1 Nitrogen Fixation

$$\text{N}_2 + 8 \text{H}^+ + 8 \text{e}^- \rightarrow 2 \text{NH}_3 + \text{H}_2$$

Enzymatic reaction: Nitrogenase, ATP-dependent.

### 1.2 Nitrification Process

Ammonia oxidizers:

$$\text{NH}_3 + 1.5 \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{NO}_2^- + 2 \text{H}^+ + \text{H}_2\text{O}$$

Nitrite oxidizers:

$$\text{NO}_2^- + 0.5 \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{NO}_3^-$$
