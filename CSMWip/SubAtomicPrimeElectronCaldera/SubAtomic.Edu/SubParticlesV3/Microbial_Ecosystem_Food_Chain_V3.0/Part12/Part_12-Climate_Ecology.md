## 1. Methane Cycling

### 1.1 Methanogenesis Pathway

$$\text{CO}_2 + 4 \text{H}_2 \rightarrow \text{CH}_4 + 2 \text{H}_2\text{O} \quad \Delta G^\circ = -131 \, \text{kJ/mol}$$

### 1.2 Methanotrophy

$$\text{CH}_4 + 2 \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_2 + 2 \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{Energy}$$

## 2. Soil Carbon Sequestration

### 2.1 Microbial Carbon Use Efficiency

$$\text{CUE} = \frac{\text{Biomass C}}{\text{Substrate C consumed}}$$

Typical values: 0.3-0.6 for terrestrial microbes.
