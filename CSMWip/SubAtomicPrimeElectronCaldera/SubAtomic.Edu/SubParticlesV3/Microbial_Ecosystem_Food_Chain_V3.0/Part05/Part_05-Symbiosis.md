### Abstract

## 1. Types of Symbiosis

### 1.1 Mutualism

Both partners benefit:

$$\frac{dN_1}{dt} = r_1 N_1 + \alpha N_1 N_2$$
$$\frac{dN_2}{dt} = r_2 N_2 + \beta N_1 N_2$$

### 1.2 Commensalism

One benefits, other unaffected:

$$N_1^* = \frac{r_1}{\alpha}, \quad N_2 \text{ at equilibrium}$$

## 2. Mycorrhizal Networks

### 2.1 Carbon-Nitrogen Trade

$$\text{Plant} \xrightarrow{\text{C}} \text{Fungus} \xrightarrow{\text{Pi, N}} \text{Plant}$$

Nutrient exchange efficiency:

$$\epsilon_{\text{myco}} = \frac{\text{Nutrient gained by plant}}{\text{Carbon allocated to fungus}}$$
