### Abstract

Electroweak phase transition (EWPT). Finite temperature field theory. EW baryogenesis. Vacuum stability. Higgs potential at high T. Lattice EWPT.

### 1. Electroweak Phase Transition

**Order parameter**: $\langle \phi \rangle = v(T)$
**SM**: Crossover for $m_h = 125$ GeV
**Critical temperature**: $T_c \approx 159$ GeV
**Order**: 
- $m_h < 72$ GeV: 1st order
- $72 < m_h < 100$ GeV: Weak 1st order
- $m_h > 100$ GeV: Crossover (SM)

**Lattice**: 4D lattice at $T > 0$ confirms crossover for $m_h = 125$ GeV

---

### 2. EW Baryogenesis (EWBG)

**Sakharov conditions**:
1. B violation: Sphalerons ($B+L$ violation)
2. C/CP violation: CKM + BSM
3. Departure from equilibrium: 1st order PT

**SM fails**: Crossover (no 1st order), insufficient CPV
**BSM required**: 2HDM, MSSM, singlet, dimension-6 operators

**BSM for 1st order**:
- **Singlet**: $S^2 \Phi^\dagger \Phi$ → barrier
- **2HDM**: Extra scalars → 1st order
- **Dim-6**: $\mathcal{O}_6 = (\Phi^\dagger \Phi)^3$ → barrier

### 3. Gravitational Waves from EWPT

**Sources**: Bubble collisions, sound waves, turbulence
**Spectrum**: 
$$h^2 \Omega_{\text{GW}}(f) \sim 10^{-11} \left(\frac{H_*}{\beta}\right)^2 \left(\frac{\kappa \alpha}{1+\alpha}\right)^2 \left(\frac{g_*}{100}\right)^{-1/3}$$

**Parameters**:
- $\alpha$: Latent heat / radiation
- $\beta/H_*$: Inverse duration
- $v_w$: Wall velocity
- $\kappa$: Efficiency

**LISA reach**: $\alpha \sim 0.1$, $\beta/H \sim 100$ → detectable

### 4. Vacuum Stability at High T

**Effective potential**: 
$$V_{\text{eff}}(\phi, T) = V_0(\phi) + V_1(\phi) + V_T(\phi, T)$$
**Thermal corrections**: $V_T \sim T^2 \phi^2$ (restores symmetry at high T)

**High T behavior**: $\langle \phi \rangle = 0$ for $T \gg T_c$
**Restoration**: $SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$ symmetry restored

**Lattice**: Confirms symmetry restoration at $T \sim 200$ GeV

---

### 5. Sphalerons & B+L Violation

**Sphaleron**: Static, unstable $SU(2)$ solution
**Energy**: $E_{\text{sph}} \approx 4\pi v(T)/g \sim 9$ TeV (at $T=0$)
**Rate**: $\Gamma_{\text{sph}} \sim \exp(-E_{\text{sph}}/T)$

**B+L violation**: Sphalerons violate $B+L$, conserve $B-L$
**Washout**: Sphalerons in symmetric phase wash out $B+L$ asymmetry

**EWBG requirement**: 1st order PT → bubbles → sphalerons off inside bubbles

---

### 6. Lattice EWPT (2024)

**Method**: 4D lattice, physical $m_h = 125$ GeV
**Results**: 
- Crossover confirmed (no latent heat)
- $T_c = 159.5 \pm 1.5$ GeV
- No 1st order in SM

**BSM on lattice**: 
- Singlet extension: 1st order possible
- Dim-6 operators: Lattice studies emerging

---

### 7. Worldline Picture: EWPT as Worldline Symmetry Change

**$T > T_c$**: Worldline EW fold symmetric (no stiffness)
**$T < T_c$**: Worldline EW fold acquires stiffness ($v = 246$ GeV)
**1st order** = Worldline fold stiffness with barrier
**Crossover** = Smooth stiffness acquisition
**Sphaleron** = Worldline fold topology change
