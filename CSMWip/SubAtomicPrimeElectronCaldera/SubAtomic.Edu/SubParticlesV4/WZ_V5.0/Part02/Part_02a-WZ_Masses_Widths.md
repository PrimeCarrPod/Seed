### 1. W Mass: The CDF Tension

**SM prediction**: $m_W = 80.357 \pm 0.006$ GeV (from $m_Z, \alpha, G_F, m_t, m_h$)
**CDF (2022)**: $m_W = 80.433 \pm 0.009$ GeV (**$7\sigma$ above SM**)
**ATLAS (2023)**: $m_W = 80.360 \pm 0.016$ GeV (consistent with SM)
**LHCb (2024)**: $m_W = 80.354 \pm 0.023$ GeV (consistent with SM)
**World avg (w/o CDF)**: $80.377 \pm 0.012$ GeV

**Worldline**: $m_W$ = charged EW fold vertex energy. CDF = possible vertex energy anomaly.
