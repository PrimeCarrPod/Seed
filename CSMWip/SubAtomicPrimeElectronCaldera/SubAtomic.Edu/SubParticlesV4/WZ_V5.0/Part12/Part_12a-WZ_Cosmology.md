### Abstract

W/Z in early universe cosmology. Electroweak phase transition (EWPT), sphalerons, baryogenesis, gravitational waves from EWPT. Vacuum stability.

### 1. Electroweak Phase Transition in Early Universe

**Timeline**: $T \sim 100$ GeV, $t \sim 10^{-11}$ s after Big Bang
**SM**: Crossover for $m_h = 125$ GeV
**Critical temperature**: $T_c \approx 159$ GeV
**Order parameter**: $v(T)$ drops from 246 GeV to 0 at $T_c$

**Lattice**: Crossover confirmed (no latent heat)

---

### 2. Sphalerons & Baryogenesis

**Sphaleron**: $B+L$ violating $SU(2)$ configuration
**Energy**: $E_{\text{sph}}(T) \approx 4\pi v(T)/g$
**Rate**: $\Gamma_{\text{sph}} \sim T^4 \exp(-E_{\text{sph}}/T)$

**B+L violation**: $\Delta(B+L) = N_f \Delta N_{\text{CS}}$
**Washout**: $T > T_c$ → $\Gamma_{\text{sph}} \gg H$ → $B+L$ washed out

**EW baryogenesis requires**:
1. 1st order EWPT (bubbles)
2. CP violation (CKM + BSM)
3. Departure from equilibrium (bubble walls)

**SM fails**: Crossover, insufficient CPV
**BSM works**: 2HDM, MSSM, singlet, dim-6

---

### 3. Gravitational Waves from EWPT

**Sources**: Bubble collisions, sound waves, turbulence
**Spectrum**: 
$$h^2 \Omega_{\text{GW}}(f) \sim 10^{-11} \left(\frac{H_*}{\beta}\right)^2 \left(\frac{\kappa \alpha}{1+\alpha}\right)^2 \left(\frac{g_*}{100}\right)^{-1/3}$$

**Parameters**:
- $\alpha$: Latent heat / radiation energy
- $\beta/H_*$: Inverse duration
- $v_w$: Bubble wall velocity
- $\kappa$: Efficiency factor

**LISA reach**: $\alpha \sim 0.1$, $\beta/H \sim 100$ → detectable for BSM 1st order

---

### 4. W/Z in Thermal Bath

**$W/Z$ at $T > T_c$**: Massless, in thermal equilibrium
**$W/Z$ at $T < T_c$**: Massive, $m_W = g v(T)/2$, $m_Z = \sqrt{g^2+g'^2} v(T)/2$
**Debye masses**: $m_D^W \sim g T$, $m_D^Z \sim \sqrt{g^2+g'^2} T$

**Thermal rates**: 
- $\Gamma_W \sim \alpha_W T$
- $\Gamma_Z \sim \alpha_Z T$

### 5. Vacuum Stability & Higgs Potential

**Effective potential**: $V_{\text{eff}}(\phi, T) = V_0(\phi) + V_1(\phi) + V_T(\phi, T)$
**High T**: $V_T \sim T^2 \phi^2$ → symmetry restoration
**$T=0$**: $V_0(\phi) = \frac{1}{4} \lambda \phi^4 - \frac{1}{2} \mu^2 \phi^2$

**RG running**: $\lambda(\mu)$ at 3-loop
**Instability**: $\lambda(\mu) < 0$ at $\mu > 10^{10}$ GeV (metastable)
**BSM**: Stabilization via SUSY, 2HDM, portal

### 6. Worldline Picture: Cosmology as Worldline History

**$T > T_c$** = Worldline EW fold symmetric (no stiffness)
**$T < T_c$** = Worldline EW fold acquires stiffness $v(T)$
**Sphaleron** = Worldline EW fold topology change
