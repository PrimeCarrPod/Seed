### 1. Gluon Jet Tomography in Heavy Ions

**Jet R_AA**: Suppression factor — gluon vs quark
**R_AA^g < R_AA^q** at same p_T (larger color charge)

**Jet v₂**: Elliptic flow of jets — path-length dependence
**Gluon jet v₂ > Quark jet v₂**

**Jet-hadron correlations**: Trigger jet, associated hadron
**Medium response**: Mach cone, diffusion wake

**Tomographic power**: 
- q̂(x) profile from jet R_AA vs p_T, η
- Initial gluon density from jet v₂

### 2. EIC Gluon Jet Measurements

**Kinematics**: e+p at √s=140 GeV, Q²=10-1000 GeV²
**Current fragmentation**: γ* → q (quark jet) — standard
**Target fragmentation**: γ*+p → jet+X — gluon-initiated

**Gluon jet channels**:
- **Dijet**: γ*+g → q+q̄ (gluon from proton)
- **Photon+jet**: γ*+g → γ+q (Compton)
- **Charm+jet**: γ*+g → c+c̄ (gluon dominance)

**Projections**: Gluon jet fraction measured to 5% at x=0.01.

### 4. Gluon Jet as Worldline Probe

**Jet** = worldline branching (parton shower)
**Splitting** = worldline kink multiplication
**Grooming** = worldline pruning (removing soft kinks)
**Medium** = worldline in external field

**Gluon vs Quark**: 
- Gluon = color octet worldline fold (more self-interaction)
- Quark = color triplet worldline fold

**C_A/C_F = 9/4** = ratio of worldline self-interaction strengths.
