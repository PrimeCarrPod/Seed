### Abstract

Higgs self-coupling $\lambda_{hhh} = 3m_h^2/v$. Double Higgs production $gg \to hh$, VBF $hh$, $t\bar{t}hh$. Trilinear coupling extraction. HL-LHC and future collider reach.

---

### 1. Higgs Self-Coupling in SM

**Potential**: $V = \frac{1}{2} m_h^2 h^2 + \lambda_{hhh} h^3 + \frac{1}{4} \lambda_{hhhh} h^4$
**Trilinear**: $\lambda_{hhh} = 3 m_h^2 / v = 190$ GeV
**Quartic**: $\lambda_{hhhh} = 3 m_h^2 / v^2 = 0.13$

**Deviation**: $\kappa_\lambda = \lambda_{hhh} / \lambda_{hhh}^{\text{SM}}$

### 3. VBF $hh$: Pure $\lambda_{hhh}$ Probe

**Process**: $qq \to qq hh$ (forward jets)
**Cross section**: $\sigma_{\text{VBF}} \approx 1.7$ fb at 13 TeV
**Advantage**: Direct $\lambda_{hhh}$ vertex, no top loop
**HL-LHC**: $\delta\kappa_\lambda/\kappa_\lambda \sim 50\%$ (combined)

---

### 4. $t\bar{t}hh$: Top Yukawa + Self-Coupling

**Cross section**: $\sigma_{t\bar{t}hh} \approx 0.7$ fb at 13 TeV
**Signature**: $t\bar{t} + 4b$ or $t\bar{t} + \gamma\gamma bb$
**Combined**: $t\bar{t}hh + VBF hh$ → $\kappa_t$ and $\kappa_\lambda$ simultaneously
