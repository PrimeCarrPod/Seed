### Abstract

BSM graviton physics. Large extra dimensions (ADD), warped extra dimensions (Randall-Sundrum), KK graviton towers. String theory graviton resonances. Collider and astrophysical constraints.

---

### 1. ADD Large Extra Dimensions

**Setup**: $n$ flat extra dimensions, radius $R$
**Fundamental Planck**: $M_D^{n+2} = M_{\text{Pl}}^2 / R^n$
**KK gravitons**: $m_k = |k|/R$, $k \in \mathbb{Z}^n$
**Coupling**: $1/M_{\text{Pl}}$ per KK mode, but $\sum_k \sim (M_D R)^n = M_{\text{Pl}}^2/M_D^2$

**Signatures**:
- Mono-jet + MET (graviton emission)
- Dilepton/diphoton resonance (KK graviton exchange)
- Virtual KK: contact interactions

**Constraints**: $M_D > 5-10$ TeV (LHC, $n=2-6$)

---

### 2. Randall-Sundrum Warped Extra Dimension

**Metric**: $ds^2 = e^{-2kr_c|\phi|} \eta_{\mu\nu} dx^\mu dx^\nu + r_c^2 d\phi^2$
**Two branes**: UV (Planck) at $\phi=0$, IR (TeV) at $\phi=\pi$
**Hierarchy**: $M_{\text{weak}} = M_{\text{Pl}} e^{-kr_c\pi}$ → $kr_c \sim 12$

**KK gravitons**: $m_n = x_n k e^{-kr_c\pi} \sim \text{TeV} \times x_n$
**Couplings**: IR-localized fields couple strongly ($\sim \text{TeV}^{-1}$)

**Signatures**: 
- $pp \to G_{\text{KK}} \to \gamma\gamma, ZZ, WW, tt̄, hh$
- Narrow resonances at TeV scale

**Constraints**: $m_1 > 4-5$ TeV (LHC)

---

### 3. Clockwork / Linear Dilaton

**Clockwork**: Exponential coupling hierarchy from discrete sites
**Linear dilaton**: $ds^2 = e^{2k|y|} \eta_{\mu\nu} dx^\mu dx^\nu + dy^2$
**KK modes**: Quasi-continuous, soft mass gap
**Phenomenology**: Similar to RS but softer resonances

---

### 4. String Theory Graviton Resonances

**Regge trajectory**: $J = \alpha_0 + \alpha' M^2$
**Graviton excitations**: Higher-spin string states
**Mass scale**: $M_s = 1/\sqrt{\alpha'}$
**Coupling**: $g_s = e^{\langle \phi \rangle}$

**Signatures**:
- String balls at $E \gg M_s$
- Regge recurrence production
- Amplitude behavior: $A(s,t) \sim \beta(-1-\alpha's)$

---

### 5. Astrophysical & Cosmological Constraints

**Supernova cooling**: KK graviton emission → $M_D > 50$ TeV ($n=2$)
**Neutron star heating**: KK graviton capture → $M_D > 1000$ TeV ($n=2$)
**Cosmology**: KK graviton overproduction, BBN
**GW dispersion**: Extra dimensions → modified GW propagation
