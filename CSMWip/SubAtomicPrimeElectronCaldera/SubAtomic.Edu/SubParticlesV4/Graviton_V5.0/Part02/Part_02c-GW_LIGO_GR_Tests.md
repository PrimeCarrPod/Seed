### Abstract

Parametrized post-Einsteinian (ppE) framework for testing GR with GWs. Constraints on graviton mass, Lorentz violation, dipole radiation, modified dispersion. Worldline consistency tests.

---

### 1. ppE Framework

**Waveform deformation**: 
$$h(f) = h_{\text{GR}}(f) \left[1 + \alpha u^\beta \right] e^{i \delta u^\epsilon}$$
where $u = (\pi \mathcal{M} f)^{1/3}$, $\mathcal{M}$ = chirp mass

**Parameters**: 
- $\alpha, \beta$: Amplitude corrections
- $\delta, \epsilon$: Phase corrections

**GR limit**: $\alpha = \delta = 0$

---

### 2. Graviton Mass & Dispersion

**Massive graviton**: $E^2 = p^2 + m_g^2$ → modified dispersion
**Phase correction**: $\delta \Psi = -\frac{\pi D m_g^2}{\lambda_g^2 (1+z) f}$ where $\lambda_g = h/m_g c$

**LIGO constraint**: $m_g < 1.3 \times 10^{-23}$ eV (GW170817)
**Compton wavelength**: $\lambda_g > 1.6 \times 10^{13}$ km

**Worldline**: Massive graviton = worldline with finite correlation length.

---

### 3. Lorentz Violation

**Modified dispersion**: $E^2 = p^2 + \xi p^4/M_{\text{Pl}}^2 + \dots$
**GW speed**: $c_g^2 = 1 + 2\xi E^2/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$

**GW170817+GRB**: $|c_g - c|/c < 10^{-15}$
**LIGO O4**: Improved constraints on $\xi$

### 4. Dipole Radiation & Scalar-Tensor

**Scalar-tensor theories**: Additional scalar polarizations
**Dipole radiation**: $\dot{E}_{\text{dipole}} \propto (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2)^2$
**Constraints**: $\omega_{\text{BD}} > 4 \times 10^4$ (Cassini), GWs give comparable
