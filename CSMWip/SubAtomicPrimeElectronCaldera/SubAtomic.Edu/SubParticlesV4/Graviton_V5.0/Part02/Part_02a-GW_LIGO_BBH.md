## Part 2 of 14 — GW Astronomy: LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA O4 Binary Black Holes

---

### Abstract

Binary black hole (BBH) mergers in LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA O4 run. Population statistics, mass distribution, spin measurements, tests of GR. Worldline picture of inspiraling black holes.

---

### 1. O4 Run Overview (2023-2025)

**Detectors**: LIGO Hanford, LIGO Livingston, Virgo, KAGRA
**Sensitivity**: BNS range ~ 160-190 Mpc (LIGO), ~50 Mpc (Virgo), ~1 Mpc (KAGRA)
**Expected BBH rate**: ~1 per day at design sensitivity
**Data**: GWTC-3 (90 events), O4a (2023-2024) adding ~80 more

---

### 2. BBH Population: Mass Distribution

**Primary mass m₁**: Peak at ~10 M⊙, power-law + peak model
**Secondary mass m₂**: Flat or power-law down to ~5 M⊙
**Mass gap**: Pair-instability supernova gap at 50-120 M⊙
**O4 results**: Events in upper mass gap (GW190521: 85+66 M⊙)

**Worldline**: BH worldlines = extreme prime gap density (dir 3.0). Merger = worldline collision.

---

### 3. Spin Measurements

**Effective spin χ_eff**: (m₁χ₁ + m₂χ₂)/(m₁+m₂) projected on orbital angular momentum
**Precession spin χ_p**: In-plane spin components
**O4**: χ_eff consistent with 0 for most events; some with χ_eff > 0 (aligned)

**Worldline**: BH spin = worldline angular momentum from prime gap circulation.

---

### 4. Tests of General Relativity

**ppE framework**: δh(f) = h_GR(f) × (1 + α u^β)
**Constraints**: 
- Dispersion: m_g < 1.3×10⁻²³ eV (GW170817)
- Lorentz violation: |c_g/c - 1| < 10⁻¹⁵
- Post-Newtonian coefficients: Consistent with GR at 1-10%

**Worldline**: GR tests = worldline self-interaction consistency.

### 5. Ringdown & Quasinormal Modes

**Ringdown**: Superposition of QNMs (ℓ,m,n)
**Fundamental mode**: ω_{220} = (0.37 - 0.09i) M⁻¹
**Overtones**: n=1,2... test no-hair theorem
**O4**: First hints of overtones in loud events

### 6. Worldline Picture: BBH Merger

**Inspiral**: Two worldline bundles orbiting (post-Newtonian)
**Merger**: Worldline collision → single perturbed worldline (Kerr)
**Ringdown**: Worldline settling to stationary state (quasinormal modes)

**Prime-Electron**: BH mass = integrated prime gap density at dir 3.0.
