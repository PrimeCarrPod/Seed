### Abstract

### 1. Neutrino Masses & Cosmology

**Free-streaming**: Neutrinos decouple at $T \sim 1$ MeV, free-stream until non-relativistic
**Free-streaming scale**: $k_{\text{fs}} \approx 0.018 \Omega_m^{1/2} (m_\nu/1 \text{eV})^{1/2} h \text{Mpc}^{-1}$
**Suppression**: $P(k) \to P(k) (1 - 8 f_\nu)$ for $k \gg k_{\text{fs}}$
**$f_\nu$**: $\Omega_\nu / \Omega_m \approx \sum m_\nu / 93 h^2 \text{eV}$

**CMB**: 
- Early ISW effect (high $\ell$)
- Lensing (smooths peaks)
- $\sum m_\nu$ from $C_\ell^{\phi\phi}$

---

### 2. Sum of Masses $\sum m_\nu$

**Current limits** (95% CL):
- Planck 2018 + BAO: $\sum m_\nu < 0.12$ eV
- Planck + BAO + Pantheon: $\sum m_\nu < 0.09$ eV
- Lyman-$\alpha$: $\sum m_\nu < 0.15$ eV

**Minimum**: 
- NO: $\sum m_\nu > 0.06$ eV
- IO: $\sum m_\nu > 0.10$ eV

**Future**:
- CMB-S4 + DESI: $\sigma(\sum m_\nu) \sim 0.02$ eV
- EUCLID + CMB-S4: $\sigma(\sum m_\nu) \sim 0.01$ eV
- **Discrimination**: $\sum m_\nu > 0.06$ eV → NO vs IO

---

### 3. Effective Number of Neutrinos $N_{\text{eff}}$

**Definition**: $\rho_{\text{rad}} = \frac{\pi^2}{30} \left( 2 + \frac{7}{8} N_{\text{eff}} \right) T_\gamma^4$
**SM**: $N_{\text{eff}} = 3.044$ (slight heating from $e^\pm$ annihilation)
**Current**: $N_{\text{eff}} = 2.99 \pm 0.17$ (Planck)
**Future**: CMB-S4 $\sigma(N_{\text{eff}}) \sim 0.03$

**BSM**: 
- Light sterile: $\Delta N_{\text{eff}} \sim 1$
- Dark radiation: $\Delta N_{\text{eff}} > 0$
- Non-thermal: $\Delta N_{\text{eff}}$ from decays

### 4. Structure Formation & BAO

**Free-streaming**: Suppresses small-scale power
**BAO**: Neutrinos shift BAO scale slightly
**Lyman-$\alpha$**: Probes $k \sim 1$ Mpc⁻¹ → $m_\nu$ sensitivity
**21 cm**: Future (SKA) → $P(k)$ at high $z$, $k$

**RSD**: Redshift-space distortions → $f\sigma_8$, neutrino suppression

### 5. CMB-S4 Projections

**CMB-S4**: 500k detectors, 7 telescopes, 7 years
**Projections** (with BAO):
- $\sigma(\sum m_\nu) = 0.015-0.02$ eV
- $\sigma(N_{\text{eff}}) = 0.02-0.03$
- $\sigma(r) = 0.0005$ (inflation)

**Hierarchy discrimination**: 
- If $\sum m_\nu > 0.06$ eV → NO vs IO at $>3\sigma$
- If $\sum m_\nu \approx 0.06$ eV → NO confirmed
