### Abstract

Neutrinoless double beta decay. Majorana mass $m_{\beta\beta}$. Nuclear matrix elements (NME). nEXO, LEGEND, KamLAND-Zen, CUPID. Mechanisms.

---

### 1. 0νββ Basics

**Process**: $(A,Z) \to (A,Z+2) + 2e^-$ (no neutrinos)
**Requirement**: Majorana neutrino, $\Delta L = 2$
**Rate**: 
$$\Gamma^{0\nu} = G^{0\nu} |M^{0\nu}|^2 \frac{|m_{\beta\beta}|^2}{m_e^2}$$
**$m_{\beta\beta}$**: 
$$m_{\beta\beta} = \left| \sum_i U_{ei}^2 m_i \right| = |c_{12}^2 c_{13}^2 m_1 + s_{12}^2 c_{13}^2 m_2 e^{i\alpha} + s_{13}^2 m_3 e^{i\beta}|$$

**Majorana phases**: $\alpha, \beta$ (unmeasurable in oscillations)

### 3. Nuclear Matrix Elements (NME)

**Methods**: QRPA, IBM, EDF, ab initio (CC, IMSRG)
**Spread**: $\sim 2-3\times$ between methods
**Ab initio progress** (2024): 
- $^{48}$Ca, $^{76}$Ge, $^{130}$Te with CC/IMSRG
- Uncertainty $\sim 20-30\%$

**Key**: NME uncertainty dominates $m_{\beta\beta}$ extraction

---

### 4. Mechanisms Beyond Light ν Exchange

**Heavy RH neutrino**: $m_{\beta\beta}^{N_R} \sim \frac{m_p}{M_R} \frac{m_W^4}{M_R^2} \sim 10^{-8}$ eV
**SUSY (RPV)**: $\lambda'_{111}$ → $m_{\beta\beta}^{\text{SUSY}}$
**Left-right**: $W_R$ exchange → $m_{\beta\beta}^{LR}$
**Extra dimensions**: KK neutrinos

**Discrimination**: Isotope dependence, angular correlation
