### 1. Inflation & Primordial Gravitational Waves

**Tensor modes**: $h_{ij} \sim \frac{H}{M_{\text{Pl}}}$ during inflation
**Power spectrum**: $\mathcal{P}_h(k) = \frac{2}{\pi^2} \frac{H^2}{M_{\text{Pl}}^2} \left(\frac{k}{k_*}\right)^{n_t}$
**Tensor-to-scalar ratio**: $r = \mathcal{P}_h/\mathcal{P}_\zeta = 16\epsilon$

**BICEP/Keck 2024**: $r_{0.05} < 0.036$ (95% CL)
**Implication**: $V_{\text{inf}}^{1/4} < 1.6 \times 10^{16}$ GeV

**Models**:
- Starobinsky: $r \sim 0.003$
- Higgs inflation: $r \sim 0.003$
- Natural inflation: $r \sim 0.01-0.1$
- Monomial: $r \sim 0.1$ (excluded)

### 2. Reheating & GW Spectrum

**Reheating**: Inflaton decay → radiation
**GW from preheating**: Parametric resonance → $h^2 \Omega_{\text{GW}} \sim 10^{-10}-10^{-6}$
**Frequency today**: $f \sim 10^6-10^9$ Hz (LIGO band for low-scale inflation)

### 3. Electroweak Phase Transition

**Order**: Crossover (SM) vs 1st order (BSM)
**GW from 1st order PT**: Bubble nucleation, collision, sound waves, turbulence
**Spectrum**: 
$$h^2 \Omega_{\text{GW}} \sim 10^{-5} \left(\frac{H_*}{\beta}\right)^2 \left(\frac{\kappa \alpha}{1+\alpha}\right)^2 \left(\frac{g_*}{100}\right)^{-1/3}$$

**LISA reach**: $\alpha \sim 0.1$, $\beta/H \sim 100$ → detectable

**BSM for 1st order**: 2HDM, singlet extension, MSSM

### 5. Cosmic Strings

**Formation**: U(1) breaking at $T \sim \eta$
**Tension**: $G\mu = (\eta/M_{\text{Pl}})^2$
**GW from loops**: 
$$h^2 \Omega_{\text{GW}}(f) \sim (G\mu)^{1/2} \left(\frac{f}{f_{\text{eq}}}\right)^{1/3} \text{ (cusps)}$$
$$ \sim (G\mu) \left(\frac{f}{f_{\text{eq}}}\right)^{-1} \text{ (kinks)}$$

**PTA constraint**: $G\mu < 10^{-11}$ (NANOGrav 15yr)
**LISA**: Probes $G\mu \sim 10^{-16}-10^{-11}$
