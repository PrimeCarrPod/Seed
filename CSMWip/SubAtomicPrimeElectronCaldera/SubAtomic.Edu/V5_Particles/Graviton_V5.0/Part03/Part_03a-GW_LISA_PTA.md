## Part 3 of 14 — GW Astronomy: LISA, PTA, Future Detectors

---

### Abstract

Space-based GW astronomy (LISA), pulsar timing arrays (PTA), future ground-based detectors (Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer). mHz to nHz bands. Stochastic backgrounds.

---

### 1. LISA (Laser Interferometer Space Antenna)

**Launch**: 2037 (ESA-led)
**Orbit**: Heliocentric, 2.5M km arms
**Band**: 0.1 mHz - 1 Hz
**Sources**:
- **MBHB**: $10^4-10^7 M_\odot$ at $z=1-10$
- **EMRI**: $10 M_\odot$ BH into $10^6 M_\odot$ SMBH
- **Galactic binaries**: $10^4$+ WD/NS binaries
- **Stochastic**: Inflation, phase transitions, cosmic strings

**Precision**: $\Delta h/h \sim 10^{-3}$, sky localization $\sim 10$ deg²

---

### 2. Pulsar Timing Arrays (PTA)

**Principle**: Millisecond pulsars as GW detectors
**Band**: nHz ($1-100$ nHz)
**Arrays**: NANOGrav, EPTA, PPTA, IPTA
**15-year results (2023)**: Evidence for stochastic GWB
- $A_{\text{GWB}} \sim 2 \times 10^{-15}$ at $f = 1/\text{yr}$
- Spectrum consistent with SMBHB population

**Future**: SKA (2030s) → 100+ pulsars, $\Delta h/h \sim 10^{-4}$

---

### 3. Einstein Telescope (ET)

**Design**: Underground, 10 km arms, triangular
**Band**: 1 Hz - 10 kHz (10× LIGO at low-f)
**Sensitivity**: BNS to $z \sim 2$, BBH to $z \sim 10$
**Timeline**: CDR 2020, construction 2030s

---

### 4. Cosmic Explorer (CE)

**Design**: 40 km arms, L-shaped, surface
**Band**: 5 Hz - 5 kHz
**Sensitivity**: BNS to $z \sim 5$, BBH to $z \sim 20$
**Timeline**: CDR 2021, construction 2030s
