### 2. Nuclear Modification Regions

| x range | Effect | R_g^A | Mechanism |
|---------|--------|-------|-----------|

### 3. Coherent vs Incoherent Diffraction (EIC)

**Coherent**: γ*+A → J/ψ+A (nucleus intact)
- Probes **average** gluon density ⟨g^A⟩
- t-dependence → transverse gluon profile

**Incoherent**: γ*+A → J/ψ+A* (nucleus breaks up)
- Probes **fluctuations** in gluon density
- Var(g^A) = ⟨g²⟩ - ⟨g⟩²

**Ratio**: σ_incoh/σ_coh = Var(g^A)/⟨g^A⟩² — measures gluon lumpiness

---

### 4. EIC Nuclear Program (Approved)

**Beams**: e+p, e+d, e+He, e+Au, e+Pb
**Luminosity**: 10³³-10³⁴ cm⁻²s⁻¹
**Key measurements**:
1. **F₂^A/F₂^D** at x=10⁻⁴-0.1 — gluon shadowing
2. **Exclusive J/ψ** — nuclear gluon GPD, saturation
3. **Di-hadron** — gluon TMD in nuclei
4. **Dijet** — gluon nPDF at x>0.01

**Projections**: Gluon shadowing R_g^A to 5% at x=10⁻³.

### 5. Gluon Saturation in Nuclei

**Nuclear saturation scale**: Q_s,A² = A^{1/3} Q_s,p²
**Centrality dependence**: Q_s,A²(b) = Q_s,p² T_A(b)/T_p(0)

**CGC prediction**: 
- Geometric scaling: N_A(r,b) = N_p(r Q_s,A(b))
- Diffractive cross section enhanced in nuclei
