# Higgs Physics — V5.0 Deep Dive Series
## Part 11 of 14 — Higgs Rare & Exotic Decays: $h \to \mu\mu, Z\gamma, \text{inv}, \text{exotic}$

---

### Abstract

Rare and exotic Higgs decays. $h \to \mu\mu$ (Yukawa), $h \to Z\gamma$ (loop), invisible (DM), flavor-violating ($h \to e\tau, \mu\tau$), lepton flavor violation, displaced vertices.

---

### 1. $h \to \mu\mu$: Second-Generation Yukawa

**BR**: $BR(h \to \mu\mu) = 2.2 \times 10^{-4}$
**Significance**: ATLAS $3.0\sigma$, CMS $3.0\sigma$ (2021)
**Coupling**: $\kappa_\mu = 1.1 \pm 0.3$
**HL-LHC**: $\delta\kappa_\mu \sim 10-15\%$
**FCC-ee**: $\delta\kappa_\mu \sim 1-2\%$

**Importance**: First 2nd-gen Yukawa, tests flavor structure

---

### 2. $h \to Z\gamma$: Rare Loop Decay

**BR**: $BR(h \to Z\gamma) = 1.5 \times 10^{-3}$
**Amplitude**: $W$ + top loops (constructive interference)
**Status**: ATLAS $2.2\sigma$, CMS $2.7\sigma$ (2023)
**Combined**: $\kappa_{Z\gamma} = 1.0 \pm 0.5$
**HL-LHC**: $\delta\kappa_{Z\gamma} \sim 20\%$

---

### 3. Invisible Decays: $h \to \text{inv}$

**Channels**: $h \to \nu\bar{\nu}$ (SM: $BR \sim 10^{-3}$), $h \to \text{DM DM}$, $h \to \text{hidden sector}$

**Searches**:
- VBF + $E_T^{\text{miss}}$: $BR_{\text{inv}} < 0.11$ (95% CL)
- $Zh$ with $Z \to \ell\ell$: $BR_{\text{inv}} < 0.19$
- $t\bar{t}h$: $BR_{\text{inv}} < 0.35$

**DM interpretation**: $h \to \chi\chi$ → $\sigma_{\text{SI}}$ bounds from direct detection

---

### 4. Flavor-Violating Decays

**$h \to e\tau, \mu\tau$**: 
- $BR(h \to e\tau) < 0.22\%$ (CMS)
- $BR(h \to \mu\tau) < 0.15\%$ (ATLAS)
- SUSY, 2HDM Type X, leptoquarks

**$h \to e\mu$**: 
- $BR < 3.5 \times 10^{-4}$ (CMS)
- Strongly constrained by $\mu \to e\gamma$

---

### 5. Exotic & Displaced Decays

**$h \to aa \to 4\gamma, 4b, 2\gamma 2b$**: 
- Light pseudoscalar $a$ (2HDM, NMSSM)
- Displaced vertices if $a$ long-lived

**$h \to Z_d Z_d \to 4\ell$**: 
- Dark photon $Z_d$ (portal)
- Displaced if $Z_d$ long-lived

**$h \to \text{long-lived} \to \text{displaced}$**: 
- MATHUSLA, CODEX-b, FASER proposals
- Lifetime $c\tau \sim 1$ m - 1 km

---

### 6. Worldline Picture: Rare Decays as Exotic Fold Channels

**$h \to \mu\mu$** = 2nd-gen fold coupling to stiffness
**$h \to Z\gamma$** = Loop fold channel
**$h \to \text{inv}$** = Fold into hidden sector
**Flavor violation** = Fold crossing
**Displaced** = Slow fold relaxation
