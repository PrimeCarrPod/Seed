# Higgs Physics — V5.0 Deep Dive Series
## Part 2 of 14 — Higgs Couplings: Fermion Couplings $\kappa_f$ ($h \to \tau\tau, bb, \mu\mu$)

---

### Abstract

Higgs Yukawa couplings $\kappa_f = y_f/y_f^{\text{SM}}$. $h \to \tau\tau$, $h \to bb$, $h \to \mu\mu$. $t\bar{t}h$ for top Yukawa. $h \to cc$ projections.

---

### 1. $h \to \tau\tau$: Cleanest Fermion Channel

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to \tau\tau) \approx 6.3\%$
**Channels**: $\tau_{\text{lep}}\tau_{\text{had}}$, $\tau_{\text{had}}\tau_{\text{had}}$
**Background**: $Z \to \tau\tau$, $t\bar{t}$, $W+jets$

**Measurement**:
- ATLAS: $\mu_{\tau\tau} = 1.09 \pm 0.11$
- CMS: $\mu_{\tau\tau} = 0.95 \pm 0.10$
- Combined: $\kappa_\tau = 1.02 \pm 0.07$

---

### 2. $h \to bb$: Dominant but Challenging

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to bb) \approx 58\%$
**Production**: 
- VBF: $h \to bb$ with forward jets
- VH: $h \to bb$ with $W/Z \to \ell\nu/\ell\ell$
- $t\bar{t}h$: $h \to bb$ with $t\bar{t}$

**Background**: QCD $b\bar{b}$, $t\bar{t}$, $V+jets$

**Measurement**:
- VBF: $\mu_{bb}^{\text{VBF}} = 1.0 \pm 0.2$
- VH: $\mu_{bb}^{\text{VH}} = 1.0 \pm 0.15$
- $t\bar{t}h$: $\mu_{bb}^{t\bar{t}h} = 1.1 \pm 0.2$
- Combined: $\kappa_b = 1.00 \pm 0.07$

---

### 3. $h \to \mu\mu$: Rare, Yukawa Precision

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to \mu\mu) \approx 0.022\%$
**Significance**: First observation 2020-2021
**ATLAS**: $3.0\sigma$, $\mu_{\mu\mu} = 1.2 \pm 0.6$
**CMS**: $3.0\sigma$, $\mu_{\mu\mu} = 1.19 \pm 0.40$
**Combined**: $\kappa_\mu = 1.1 \pm 0.3$

**HL-LHC**: $\delta\kappa_\mu/\kappa_\mu \sim 10-15\%$

---

### 4. Top Yukawa: $t\bar{t}h$ Production

**Cross section**: $\sigma_{t\bar{t}h} \approx 0.5$ pb at 13 TeV
**Channels**: $h \to bb, \gamma\gamma, \tau\tau, WW^*, ZZ^*$
**Background**: $t\bar{t}+jets$, $t\bar{t}V$, $t\bar{t}H$

**Measurement**: $\kappa_t = 1.00 \pm 0.07$ (combined)

---

### 5. Charm Yukawa: $h \to cc$ Projections

**Branching ratio**: $BR(h \to cc) \approx 2.9\%$
**Challenge**: $c$-tagging, QCD background
**HL-LHC**: $\delta\kappa_c/\kappa_c \sim 50\%$
**FCC-ee**: $\delta\kappa_c/\kappa_c \sim 5\%$

---

**Yukawa $\kappa_f$** = Fold coupling to stiffness
