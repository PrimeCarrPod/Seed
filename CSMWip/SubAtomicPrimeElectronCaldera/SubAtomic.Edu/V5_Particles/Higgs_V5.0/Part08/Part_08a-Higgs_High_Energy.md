### Abstract

High-energy Higgs production: VBF, $hh$, $t\bar{t}h$. Longitudinal $W/Z$ scattering unitarity. Higgs as unitarizer. High-$p_T$ tails. BSM sensitivity.

---

### 1. VBF at High Energy

**Process**: $qq \to qq h$ (forward jets)
**Amplitude**: $W_L W_L \to h$ fusion
**Longitudinal $W$**: $\epsilon_L^\mu \approx p^\mu / m_W$

**Unitarity**: $W_L W_L \to W_L W_L$ violates unitarity at $\sqrt{s} \sim 1.2$ TeV
**Higgs exchange**: $W_L W_L \to h \to W_L W_L$ restores unitarity

**High-$p_T$ VBF**: $p_T^h > 500$ GeV → probes $\kappa_V$ at high scale

---

### 2. $hh$ at High Energy

**$gg \to hh$**: High $m_{hh}$ → sensitive to $c_g, c_t, \lambda_{hhh}$
**VBF $hh$**: $m_{hh} > 1$ TeV → pure $\lambda_{hhh}$ at high scale
**Unitarity**: $W_L W_L \to hh$ violates unitarity without Higgs self-coupling

**High-energy theorems**: 
- $W_L W_L \to h$ amplitude $\to \kappa_V$
- $W_L W_L \to hh$ amplitude $\to \lambda_{hhh}$

---

### 3. $t\bar{t}h$ at High Energy

**Process**: $gg/q\bar{q} \to t\bar{t}h$
**High $p_T^h$**: $p_T^h > 300$ GeV → top Yukawa at high scale
**$t\bar{t}h$ vs $t\bar{t}$**: Ratio probes $\kappa_t$ directly

**High-$p_T$ tail**: Sensitive to $c_t, c_g$ in SMEFT

---

### 4. Longitudinal $W/Z$ Scattering

**$W_L W_L \to W_L W_L$**: $\mathcal{A} \sim s/v^2$ (violates unitarity)
**Higgs exchange**: $\mathcal{A}_h \sim -s/v^2$ (cancels)
**$W_L W_L \to Z_L Z_L$**: Similar cancellation

**LHC measurement**: $W^\pm W^\pm$ scattering (VBF same-sign $WW$)
- $\sigma_{\text{fid}} = 2.9 \pm 0.5$ fb (ATLAS)
- Consistent with SM unitarization

---

### 5. Higgs as Unitarizer: Worldline Picture

**Without Higgs**: Worldline folds (W/Z) scatter strongly → unitarity violation
**With Higgs**: Stiffness exchange cancels bad high-energy behavior
**$h$** = Stiffness propagator
**$hh$** = Double stiffness exchange for $hh$ final state

**Unitarity bound**: $\sqrt{s} < 4\pi v \sim 3$ TeV without Higgs

---

### 6. High-$p_T$ Higgs as BSM Probe

**SMEFT at high $p_T$**:
- $\mathcal{O}_{HG}$: $h gg$ contact → $p_T^h$ enhancement
- $\mathcal{O}_{tH}$: $t\bar{t}h$ at high $p_T$
- $\mathcal{O}_H$: $\delta\kappa_V$ → high-$p_T$ VBF

**Reach**: $p_T^h > 500$ GeV → $\Lambda \sim 1-2$ TeV
