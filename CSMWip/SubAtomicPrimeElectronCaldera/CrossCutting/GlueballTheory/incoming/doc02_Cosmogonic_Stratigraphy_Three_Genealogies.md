Nyx is the thermodynamic ground state — the maximum entropy reservoir from which all order emerges and to which all order returns. Her parthenogenetic generation of the Moirai (no father named) signifies that destiny requires no external agency; it is the spontaneous symmetry-breaking of the void. The Moirai as Nyx-daughters are the intrinsic tendentialities of the dark: Moros (Doom), Thanatos (Death), Hypnos (Sleep), Nemesis (Retribution), Apate (Deceit), Geras (Old Age), Eris (Strife), the Keres (Violent Death). This sibling-set is the complete catalog of termination-pathways. The Moirai do not cause these; they are the allotment-mechanism that assigns each entity its specific pathway from the menu.

The Nyx-stratum reflects a pre-Olympian, matriarchal cosmology where power flows from the chthonic feminine. The Moirai here are not subject to Zeus; they are his senior. In the Iliad, even Zeus consults the scales — he does not command the Fates. The Nyx-genealogy preserves this priority: the Moirai are the operating system; Zeus is an application. The chthonic character manifests in the epithets: adrésteiai (inescapable), krataié (strong), oloé (destructive). These are not moral qualifications; they are physical specifications. The Moirai are as inescapable as gravity, as strong as the nuclear force, as destructive as entropy. The "lame" epithet (chōlē) encodes temporal asymmetry: the operators move forward only, dragging the severed thread. Lameness is the gait of the second law.

The Linear B tablets record moira as rations — wheat, oil, cloth, labor — assigned by central authority to named individuals. The palace is the cosmic order; the ration is the moira; the scribe is Clotho; the storekeeper is Lachesis; the consumption is Atropos. The theological abstraction is the palace economy scaled to cosmos: the universe is a palace; every entity receives its ration; the ration is finite; the storehouse is Nyx. The "terrifying" aspect of the Nyx-Moirai is the terror of the ration-recipient: the portion is fixed, the storehouse is dark, the scribe is deaf to pleas. The Mycenaean Wanax (king) controls the distribution but not the total — the total comes from the land (Nyx). The Wanax is Zeus; the land is Nyx. The Moirai are the distribution-mechanism.
