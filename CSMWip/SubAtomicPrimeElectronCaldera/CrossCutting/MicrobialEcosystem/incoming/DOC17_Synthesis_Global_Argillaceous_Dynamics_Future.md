## 17.3 Clay as Planetary Thermostat: The CO₂-Clay Connection

### 17.3.1 The Urey Reaction: Clay as CO₂ Thermostat

**The fundamental weathering reaction:**
$$\text{CaSiO}_3 + 2\text{CO}_2 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Ca}^{2+} + 2\text{HCO}_3^- + \text{H}_4\text{SiO}_4 \xrightarrow{\text{clay}} \text{Clay} + \text{SiO}_2$$

**Clay's role:**
1. **Product sink** — Clay formation consumes Si, drives reaction forward
2. **Catalyst** — Clay surfaces accelerate dissolution 10–100×
3. **Cation exchange** — Clay buffers pH, maintains reactivity
4. **Physical protection** — Clay coatings slow dissolution (negative feedback)

### 17.3.2 Quantitative CO₂ Drawdown

| Process | CO₂ Consumption (Gt C/yr) | Clay Role |
