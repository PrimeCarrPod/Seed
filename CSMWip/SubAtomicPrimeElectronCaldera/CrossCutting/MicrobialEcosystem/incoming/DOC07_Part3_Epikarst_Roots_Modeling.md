## 7.10 Epikarst Clay Dynamics: The Critical Zone Skin

### 7.10.1 Epikarst Structure and Clay Distribution

**Vertical zonation (typical profile):**

| Zone | Depth | Thickness | Clay % | Porosity | Hydraulic Role |
|------|-------|-----------|--------|----------|----------------|

### 7.10.3 Clay Swelling-Shrinkage in Epikarst

**Smectite-rich epikarst clay (20–30% smectite):**

**Seasonal volume change:**
$$\frac{\Delta V}{V} = \varepsilon_{\text{sh}} \times \frac{\Delta \theta}{\theta_{\text{sat}}}$$

Where $\varepsilon_{\text{sh}}$ = shrinkage strain (0.1–0.3 for smectite)

**Typical cycle:**
- **Wet (Sep):** θ = 0.35, V = V_max, fractures closed
- **Dry (Apr):** θ = 0.15, V = 0.85 V_max, fractures open (1–5 mm)

**Fracture aperture change:**
$$\Delta a = \frac{\Delta V}{V} \times \text{fracture spacing} \approx 0.15 \times 0.5\ \text{m} = 7.5\ \text{cm}$$

**Hydraulic impact:**
- **Wet:** K_matrix ↓ 100×, flow through conduits
- **Dry:** K_matrix ↑ 1000×, diffuse flow through fractures

**Implication:** Epikarst clay acts as **seasonal hydraulic switch**

### 7.10.4 Epikarst Clay as Biogeochemical Reactor

**Redox zonation (vertical):**

| Depth | O₂ (mg/L) | NO₃⁻ (mg/L) | Fe²⁺ (mg/L) | SO₄²⁻ (mg/L) | CH₄ (mg/L) | Process |
|-------|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------------|------------|---------|
| 0–0.5 m | 8–10 | 1–5 | <0.1 | 10–20 | 0 | Aerobic |
| 0.5–2 m | 1–5 | 0.1–1 | 0.5–2 | 5–15 | 0.01 | Suboxic (Mn, Fe red) |
| 2–5 m | <0.5 | <0.1 | 5–20 | 1–5 | 0.1–1 | Anaerobic (Fe, SO₄ red) |
| 5–10 m | 0 | 0 | 20–50 | <1 | 1–10 | Methanogenic |

**Nutrient processing:**
- **NO₃⁻ removal:** 80–95% in epikarst (denitrification)
- **PO₄³⁻ retention:** 90% (Fe-oxide adsorption in clay)
- **DOC processing:** 50% mineralized, 50% exported to aquifer

**Gas fluxes (from epikarst to atmosphere):**
- CO₂: 500–2000 g C/m²/yr (soil respiration)
- CH₄: 0.5–5 g C/m²/yr (oxidized in upper epikarst)
- N₂O: 0.1–1 g N/m²/yr (denitrification byproduct)

## 7.11 Root-Clay Interactions: Tree Roots in Karst

### 7.11.1 Root Architecture in Karst

**Rooting patterns (from excavation + GPR):**

| Species | Root Type | Max Depth | Epikarst Use | Pocket Access |
|---------|-----------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Ceiba pentandra** (Ceiba) | Taproot + laterals | 20+ m | High | Yes (deep pockets) |
| **Brosimum alicastrum** (Ramón) | Taproot + sinkers | 15 m | High | Yes |
| **Manilkara zapota** (Chicozapote) | Taproot | 10 m | Moderate | Yes (shallow pockets) |
| **Cedrela odorata** (Cedar) | Lateral + sinkers | 8 m | High | Yes |
| **Zea mays** (Maize) | Fibrous | 1.5 m | Low | No |
| **Phaseolus vulgaris** (Bean) | Taproot + nodules | 1 m | Low | No |

**Root density in epikarst:**
- **0–1 m:** 5–15 cm/cm³ (fine roots)
- **1–5 m:** 0.5–2 cm/cm³ (coarse + fine)
- **5–15 m:** 0.1–0.5 cm/cm³ (sinkers in fractures)

### 7.11.2 Root-Induced Dissolution: Biological Weathering Engine

**Mechanisms:**
1. **CO₂ elevation:** Root respiration → $P_{\text{CO}_2}$ = 10⁻¹.⁵–10⁻¹ atm (10–100× atmospheric)
2. **Organic acids:** Oxalate, citrate, malate exudation (Document 2)
3. **Physical pressure:** Root growth → fracture widening (mm/yr)
4. **Mycorrhizal fungi:** Hyphae penetrate micropores, secrete acids

**Quantification (Ceiba root at fracture tip):**
- $P_{\text{CO}_2}$ at root surface: 0.03 atm (vs 0.0004 atm atmospheric)
- Dissolution rate enhancement: **30–100×**
- Fracture widening: **0.1–1 mm/yr** (vs 0.01 mm/yr abiotic)

**Carbonic acid flux from roots:**
$$F_{\text{H}_2\text{CO}_3} = \text{Root respiration} \times \text{root surface area}$$
$$\approx 10^{-6}\ \text{mol/m}^2/\text{s} \times 10^4\ \text{m}^2/\text{m}^3 = 10^{-2}\ \text{mol/m}^3/\text{s}$$

**→ Roots contribute 50–80% of total dissolution in vegetated karst**

| Pocket Depth | Root Density (cm/cm³) | Species | Water Uptake (L/day/tree) |
|--------------|----------------------|---------|---------------------------|

| 6–10 m | 0.2–1 | Ceiba only | 10–40 |

**Water uptake during dry season (4 months):**
- **Single Ceiba:** 2,000–5,000 L from pockets

- **Fraction of dry-season transpiration:** 60–80%

**Nutrient acquisition:**
- **P in pocket clay:** 50–500 ppm (vs 5–20 ppm surface) — **10–25× enrichment**
- **Root phosphatase activity:** 5–10× higher in pocket roots
- **Mycorrhizal colonization:** 80–95% (vs 40–60% surface)

### 7.11.4 Root Exudates in Karst: Oxalate and Beyond

**Exudate flux (μmol/m root/hr):**

| Species | Oxalate | Citrate | Malate | Total OA | pH Effect |
|---------|---------|---------|--------|----------|-----------|
| Ceiba | 150 | 80 | 50 | 280 | -0.8 |

| Chicozapote | 80 | 60 | 40 | 180 | -0.5 |

**Net effect:** Roots create **localized weathering hotspots** at pocket margins

## 7.12 Numerical Modeling: Coupled Karst-Clay Evolution

### 7.12.1 Process-Based Model: KARST-CLAY v1.0

**Model structure (2D cross-section, fracture network):**

**State variables (per grid cell):**
- $C(x,z,t)$: Dissolved Ca²⁺ concentration
- $\phi(x,z,t)$: Porosity
- $h_{\text{clay}}(x,z,t)$: Clay fill thickness
- $w(x,z,t)$: Fracture aperture
- $\theta(x,z,t)$: Water content
- $B(x,z,t)$: Root biomass

**Governing equations:**

1. **Flow (Richards + cubic law):**
$$\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial t} = \nabla \cdot [K(\theta) \nabla h] + S_{\text{root}}$$

2. **Transport (advection-dispersion-reaction):**
$$\frac{\partial (\theta C)}{\partial t} = \nabla \cdot (\theta D \nabla C) - \nabla \cdot (q C) + R_{\text{diss}} - R_{\text{precip}}$$

3. **Calcite dissolution:**
$$R_{\text{diss}} = k_{\text{diss}} A_s (C_{\text{eq}} - C) f(P_{\text{CO}_2}, \text{OA})$$

4. **Clay infill (sedimentation):**
$$\frac{\partial h_{\text{clay}}}{\partial t} = \frac{C_{\text{sed}} v_{\text{settle}}}{\phi_{\text{clay}}} - k_{\text{erosion}} \tau_{\text{bed}}$$

5. **Root growth:**
$$\frac{\partial B}{\partial t} = r_B B \left(1 - \frac{B}{K}\right) f(\theta, \text{nutrients}) - m_B B$$

6. **Fracture mechanics:**
$$\frac{dw}{dt} = \frac{dw}{dt}_{\text{diss}} + \frac{dw}{dt}_{\text{root}} - \frac{dw}{dt}_{\text{clay}}$$

### 7.14.2 Proposed Observing System: "Yucatán Karst Critical Zone Observatory"

**Sites (3 representative):**
1. **Humid South (Chetumal):** Deep Terra Rossa, high recharge
2. **Transition (Valladolid):** Mixed Rendzina/Terra Rossa
3. **Arid North (Mérida):** Thin Rendzina, mature karst

**Instrumentation per site:**
- **Deep borehole (50 m):** Multi-level pressure, T, EC, water sampling
- **Epikarst nest (0–10 m):** θ, T, matric potential, CO₂, O₂, Eh at 0.5 m intervals
- **Root zone:** Minirhizotron tubes (0–3 m), root exudate collectors
- **Atmosphere:** Eddy covariance (CO₂, H₂O, energy fluxes)
- **Geophysics:** Time-lapse ERT (monthly), GPR (quarterly)
- **Remote sensing:** UAV hyperspectral (monthly), InSAR (weekly)

**Data portal:** Real-time streaming, FAIR compliance, community access
