# A2-20: Sterile Neutrinos From Missing Gaps — Piece 09

## Sterile Neutrinos at Colliders

The sterile neutrinos from missing gaps with TeV-scale Majorana masses (Piece 03) can be produced at future colliders.

**Production Mechanisms:**
1. **Drell-Yan:** q q̄ → W* → ℓ ν_s
2. **Vector Boson Fusion:** W W → ν_s ν_s
3. **Displaced Vertex:** ℓ → ℓ ν_s (if mixing is large enough)

**Mass Scales:**
From Piece 03 (Missing Gap Seesaw):
M_R(12) = 1.48 TeV
M_R(24) = 2.95 TeV
M_R(30) = 3.69 TeV
M_R(34) = 4.18 TeV

**Mixing Angles:**
sin²(2θ) ~ 10⁻¹¹ → too small for prompt production.

But the seesaw formula can be modified. If the Dirac mass is larger:
m_D = v · (d_ν/d_weak) with d_ν > d_weak

For d_ν = 6 (strange scale): m_D = 246 × (6/2) = 738 GeV
Then M_R = m_D²/m_ν = (738 GeV)²/(0.05 eV) = 1.1 × 10¹⁶ GeV — back to GUT scale.

**Alternative — Low-Scale Seesaw:**
If there is an approximate lepton number symmetry, M_R can be at TeV scale with larger mixing.

In the Prime Electron framework, the missing gaps are the fundamental scale. The Majorana mass is:
M_R = (d_missing/d_weak) · M_weak

This is a fixed prediction. The mixing is:
sin²(2θ) = (m_D/M_R)² = (m_ν/M_R) = (0.05 eV)/(1.48 TeV) = 3.4 × 10⁻¹⁴

This is too small for any collider signature.

**Collider Searches for TeV Sterile Neutrinos:**
Current LHC limits (ATLAS/CMS):
- pp → ℓℓjj (ΔL = 2): m_ν_s < 1.5 TeV for |V_ℓN|² > 10⁻³
- Displaced vertices: m_ν_s < 500 GeV for |V_ℓN|² > 10⁻⁷

Our prediction: m_ν_s = 1.48 TeV, |V_ℓN|² = 3.4 × 10⁻¹⁴ — far below reach.

**Future Colliders:**
FCC-hh (√s = 100 TeV): reach ~ 10 TeV for |V_ℓN|² > 10⁻⁵
Muon Collider (√s = 10 TeV): reach ~ 5 TeV for |V_ℓN|² > 10⁻⁶

Still far from our predicted mixing.

**Conclusion:** The Prime Electron sterile neutrinos from missing gaps are not accessible at colliders due to their extremely small active-sterile mixing. This is a sharp prediction: NO sterile neutrino signal at LHC/FCC/muon collider.

**Falsification:** If a sterile neutrino is discovered at a collider with mass ~ 1-5 TeV and mixing |V|² > 10⁻⁶, the missing gap origin is falsified.
