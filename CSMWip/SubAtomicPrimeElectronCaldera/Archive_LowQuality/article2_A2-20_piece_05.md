# A2-20: Sterile Neutrinos From Missing Gaps — Piece 05

## Neutrino Oscillations With Sterile States

The active neutrino oscillations (solar, atmospheric, reactor, accelerator) are modified by the presence of sterile neutrinos from missing gaps.

**3+3 Neutrino Framework:**
3 active (ν_e, ν_μ, ν_τ) + 3 sterile (ν_s1, ν_s2, ν_s3 from d=12, 24, 30)
The full mixing matrix is 6×6:
U_6×6 = [ U_3×3    Θ_3×3 ]
        [ Θ^T_3×3  1_3×3 ]

where Θ is the active-sterile mixing matrix.

**Active-Sterile Mixing Angles:**
From Piece 02:
sin²(2θ_12) = 5 × 10⁻¹¹ (ν_e ↔ ν_s1, d=12)
sin²(2θ_24) = 1.2 × 10⁻¹¹ (ν_μ ↔ ν_s2, d=24)
sin²(2θ_30) = 0.8 × 10⁻¹¹ (ν_τ ↔ ν_s3, d=30)

These are extremely small — sterile neutrinos are nearly decoupled.

**Oscillation Probabilities:**
The survival probability for solar neutrinos:
P_ee = 1 - ½ sin²(2θ_12) - ½ sin²(2θ_13) - Σ sin²(2θ_s)

With θ_12 = 33.4°, θ_13 = 8.6°, and sterile mixing < 10⁻⁵:
P_ee = 0.55 (standard) + O(10⁻¹⁰) — no observable deviation.

**Short-Baseline Anomalies:**
LSND (ν_μ → ν_e, Δm² ~ 1 eV²)
MiniBooNE (ν_μ → ν_e excess)
Reactor antineutrino anomaly (flux deficit)
Gallium anomaly (source deficit)

These require Δm² ~ 1 eV² and sin²(2θ) ~ 0.01.

**Prime Electron Explanation:**
The missing gaps d = 12, 24, 30 give m_s ~ keV, Δm² ~ 10⁻⁸ eV² — far too small for short-baseline anomalies.

However, there could be additional missing gaps at smaller d. But the record gaps start at d=2,4,6,8,10 — no missing gaps below 12.

**Alternative — Excited Sterile States:**
The worldline can have excited states in the missing gap potential. The radial excitation number n_r gives:
m_s(n_r) = m_s(0) · (1 + n_r · d_missing/d_weak)

For n_r = 1: m_s(12) = 7.1 keV × (1 + 12/2) = 49.7 keV
Δm² = (49.7 keV)² - (7.1 keV)² ≈ 2.5 × 10⁹ eV² — too large.

**Conclusion:** The Prime Electron sterile neutrinos from missing gaps do not explain the short-baseline anomalies. Those anomalies require either:
1. Additional sterile states not from the missing gap sequence
2. Non-standard interactions
3. Systematic errors

The framework predicts NO short-baseline sterile neutrino signal — a falsifiable prediction.
