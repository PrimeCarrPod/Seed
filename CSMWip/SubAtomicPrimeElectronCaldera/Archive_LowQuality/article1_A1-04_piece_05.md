## 6.2 Inharmonicity and Prime Gap Distribution

The inharmonicity comes from the irregular prime gap distribution.
If gaps were perfectly regular (d_n = constant), the spectrum would be harmonic.
The irregularity of gaps → inharmonic zero spectrum.

A_1 : A_2 : A_3 : ... ≈ 1 : 0.67 : 0.56 : 0.46 : 0.43 : ...

The fundamental mode γ_1 dominates the fluctuation spectrum.

### 7. ZERO DENSITY AND PROPER-TIME RESOLUTION

## 7.1 Zero Counting Function

N(T) = number of zeros with 0 < γ ≤ T
N(T) = (T/2π) ln(T/2π) - (T/2π) + O(ln T)

## 7.2 Proper-Time Resolution

The density of zeros sets the worldline frequency resolution:
Δf = (m_e c^2/ℏ) · Δγ/2π
Δγ ~ 2π/ln T → Δf ~ (m_e c^2/ℏ) / ln T

At electron scale (T ~ 10): Δγ ~ 2.7, Δf ~ 2.1×10^{20} Hz
At Planck scale (T ~ 10^{40}): Δγ ~ 10^{-39}, Δf ~ 10^{-19} Hz
