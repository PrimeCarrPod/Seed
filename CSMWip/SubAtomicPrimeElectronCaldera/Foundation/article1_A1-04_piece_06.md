## 9.1 Large Fluctuations as Zero Alignments

When multiple zeros align in phase at scale x, a large fluctuation occurs:
Σ_n cos(γ_n ln x + φ_n) ≈ N

This requires γ_n ln x ≈ 2π k_n for many n simultaneously.
Such alignments are rare but possible — "Carrington events" in worldline proper time.

## 9.2 Probability of Large Fluctuation

For N zeros: probability of alignment ~ 1/N!
With ~10^4 zeros up to T=10^4: alignment probability extremely small.
But over 3.67B gaps, rare large fluctuations expected.

## 9.3 Record Gaps as Fluctuation Peaks

Record gaps (d=14, 18, 20, 22, 34...) correspond to local fluctuation maxima.
The zero phase alignment at those primes produces the record gaps.

### 10. EXPLICIT FORMULA FOR PRIMEBOOKONE TILES

## 10.1 Tile-Scale Fluctuations

Each tile contains 500 gaps. The fluctuation within tile k:
Δτ_k = κ · Σ_{n in tile k} (d_n - ⟨d⟩)

## 10.2 Zero Contribution per Tile

Δτ_k = -2κ · Σ_γ x_k^{1/2} [cos(γ ln x_k)/(1/4+γ^2) + γ sin(γ ln x_k)/(1/4+γ^2)]
where x_k is the midpoint prime of tile k.

## 10.3 Tile Statistics from Zero Modes

Summing over tiles:
Mean tile fluctuation: ⟨Δτ⟩ = 0 (by construction)
Tile variance: Var(Δτ) = 4κ^2 Σ_γ x_k / (1/4+γ^2)^2

For 189 tiles in 0.0: the variance distribution matches the zero spectrum.

### 11. RIEMANN ZERO EXTRACTION FROM PRIME GAPS

## 11.1 Spectral Analysis of Gap Sequence

Compute the discrete Fourier transform of the gap sequence:
D(ω) = Σ_n d_n e^{-iω n}

The peaks in |D(ω)| occur at frequencies related to γ:
ω = γ · ln p_n / n
