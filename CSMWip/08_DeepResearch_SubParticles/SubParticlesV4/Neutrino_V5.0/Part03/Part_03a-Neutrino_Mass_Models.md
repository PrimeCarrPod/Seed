### Abstract

---

### 1. Seesaw Mechanisms

**Type I (canonical)**: Heavy RH neutrinos $N_R$
$$\mathcal{L} \supset -Y_\nu \bar{L} \tilde{\Phi} N_R - \frac{1}{2} \bar{N}_R^c M_R N_R$$
$$m_\nu \approx -Y_\nu^T M_R^{-1} Y_\nu v^2$$
**Scale**: $M_R \sim 10^{10-15}$ GeV for $m_\nu \sim 0.1$ eV

**Type II**: Scalar triplet $\Delta \sim (3, 1)$
$$\mathcal{L} \supset Y_\Delta L^T C i\sigma_2 \Delta L + M_\Delta^2 \Delta^\dagger \Delta$$
$$m_\nu = Y_\Delta v_\Delta, \quad v_\Delta \sim Y_\Phi v^2 / M_\Delta$$

**Type III**: Fermion triplet $\Sigma \sim (3, 0)$
$$m_\nu \approx -Y_\Sigma^T M_\Sigma^{-1} Y_\Sigma v^2$$

**Inverse/Linear seesaw**: Low-scale $M_R \sim$ TeV, small $\mu$ or $Y$

---

### 2. Radiative Mass Models

**Zee model** (1-loop): Charged scalar $h^+$, second Higgs doublet
**Babu-Zee** (2-loop): Two charged scalars $k^{++}, h^+$
**Scotogenic** (1-loop): DM candidate $N_R$, scalar $\eta$
$$m_\nu \sim \frac{Y^2}{(16\pi^2)^n} \frac{v^2}{M}$$
**Testability**: LHC (charged scalars), DM direct detection

### 3. Flavor Symmetries

**Discrete groups**: $A_4, S_4, A_5, \Delta(27), T'$
**Predictions**: 
- $A_4$: Tri-bimaximal ($\theta_{13}=0$, $\theta_{23}=45^\circ$, $\theta_{12}=35^\circ$)
- $S_4$: TM1/TM2 mixing
- $\Delta(27)$: $\theta_{13} \neq 0$, $\delta_{CP}$ predictions

**Breaking**: Flavon fields $\langle \phi \rangle$ → PMNS deviations

**Predictions** (2024):
- $A_4$ + NLO: $\theta_{13} \sim 9^\circ$, $\delta_{CP} \sim \pm \pi/2$
- $S_4$ TM1: $\theta_{12} \approx 35^\circ$, $\theta_{13} \approx 9^\circ$

### 4. Leptogenesis

**Thermal leptogenesis**: $N_1$ decay → $L$ asymmetry → sphalerons → $B$ asymmetry
**DI bound**: $M_1 > 10^9$ GeV (hierarchical)
**Resonant leptogenesis**: $M_1 \sim$ TeV, $\Delta M \sim \Gamma$
**Flavored leptogenesis**: $N_1$ decay to individual flavors

**Baryon asymmetry**: $\eta_B \approx 6 \times 10^{-10}$
