### 1. Reactor Antineutrino Anomaly

**History**: 2011 — 5-6% deficit vs Huber-Mueller model
**Resolution** (2023-2024):
- Improved $^{235}$U, $^{239}$Pu spectra (ILL, LANL)
- $^{238}$U, $^{241}$Pu contributions
- Daya Bay, RENO, Double Chooz: Consistent with new models
- **Status**: Largely resolved by improved flux predictions

**Remaining**: 
- $^{238}$U spectrum uncertainty
- Short-baseline shape (sterile?)

---

### 2. Gallium Anomaly

**Experiments**: SAGE (Cr-51), GALLEX (Cr-51), BEST (Cr-51, Ar-37)
**Deficit**: $R = 0.79 \pm 0.05$ (20-30% deficit)
**BEST (2022)**: Confirmed deficit with two-zone source
- Inner zone: $R = 0.79 \pm 0.08$
- Outer zone: $R = 0.77 \pm 0.08$
- **Status**: Persistent, not explained by known physics

**Interpretations**:
- Sterile neutrino ($\Delta m^2 \sim 1$ eV², $|U_{e4}|^2 \sim 0.03$)
- Cross section error (unlikely)
- New physics (NSI, neutrino decay)

---

### 3. LSND & MiniBooNE Anomalies

**LSND (1995-1998)**: $\bar{\nu}_\mu \to \bar{\nu}_e$ at $\Delta m^2 \sim 1$ eV²
**MiniBooNE (2002-2019)**: $\nu_\mu \to \nu_e$ + $\bar{\nu}_\mu \to \bar{\nu}_e$ excess
- Low-energy excess (200-475 MeV): $4.8\sigma$
- High-energy: consistent with background

**MicroBooNE (2022)**:
- No low-energy excess (wire + TPC)
- Excludes $e^-$ interpretation
- Photon/hadron? Still under study

**icARUS/SBN**: 
- SBND (near) + MicroBooNE + ICARUS (far)
- 3-detector: flux $\times$ cross section cancellation

---

### 4. Coherent Elastic Neutrino-Nucleus Scattering (CEvNS)

**Process**: $\nu + A \to \nu + A$ (coherent, $Q^2 \sim 0$)
**Cross section**: $\sigma \propto N^2$ (coherent enhancement)
**COHERENT (SNS)**: 
- CsI (2017): $134 \pm 22$ events, $SM$ consistent
- Ar (2021): First Ar measurement
- Ge (2023): Low-threshold

**Applications**:
- $\sin^2\theta_W$ at low $Q^2$
- NSI constraints
- Neutron radius
- Supernova ν detection

### 5. Tritium Endpoint & Neutrino Mass

**KATRIN**: 
- $m_\nu < 0.8$ eV (90% CL, 2022)

**Project 8**: Cyclotron Radiation Emission Spectroscopy (CRES)
- Single-electron detection
- $m_\nu \sim 0.04$ eV target

**Project 8 + KATRIN**: Ultimate kinematic mass reach
