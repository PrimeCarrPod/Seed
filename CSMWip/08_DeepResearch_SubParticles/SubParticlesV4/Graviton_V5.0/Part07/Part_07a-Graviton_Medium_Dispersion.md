### Abstract

### 1. Graviton Dispersion Relations

**Modified dispersion**: $\omega^2 = k^2 + m_g^2 + \xi \frac{k^4}{M_{\text{Pl}}^2} + \dots$

**Sources of modification**:
- Graviton mass $m_g$
- Lorentz violation (SME, Hořava-Lifshitz)
- Quantum gravity (LQG, strings)
- Medium effects (plasma, dark matter)

**Group velocity**: $v_g = \frac{\partial \omega}{\partial k} = 1 - \frac{m_g^2}{2\omega^2} - \dots$

### 2. Massive Gravity & Bimetric Theory

**Fierz-Pauli**: $m_g^2 (h_{\mu\nu}h^{\mu\nu} - h^2)$ — ghost-free at linear level
**dRGT (de Rham-Gabadadze-Tolley)**: Non-linear ghost-free massive gravity
**Bimetric**: $g_{\mu\nu}$ and $f_{\mu\nu}$ interacting
**Vainshtein mechanism**: Screening near sources → GR recovered

**Cosmology**: Self-acceleration, $m_g \sim H_0$

### 3. GW170817 Constraint on Graviton Mass

**Dispersion**: $\delta t = \frac{D m_g^2}{2\omega^2}$ for massive graviton
**GW170817**: $\Delta t < 1.7$ s for $D \sim 40$ Mpc, $\omega \sim 100$ Hz
**Bound**: $m_g < 1.3 \times 10^{-23}$ eV (90% CL)
**Compton wavelength**: $\lambda_g > 1.6 \times 10^{13}$ km

---

### 4. LIGO O4 Dispersion Tests

**Parametrized**: $\omega^2 = k^2 + A k^\alpha$
**Constraints**: $\alpha = 0$ (mass) → $m_g < 10^{-23}$ eV
**Lorentz violation**: $\alpha = 2, 4$ → SME coefficients

**Worldline**: Massive graviton = worldline with finite correlation length.

---

### 5. Graviton in Plasma & Dark Matter

**Plasma effect**: $m_{\text{eff}}^2 \sim \omega_p^2$ (negligible for GWs)
**Dark matter**: Scalar DM → oscillating $G_{\text{eff}}$, vector DM → birefringence
**GW propagation**: $h \propto a^{-1} e^{i \int \omega dt}$

---

### 6. Hořava-Lifshitz Gravity

**Anisotropic scaling**: $z=3$ in UV ($t \to b^3 t, x \to b x$)
**Lagrangian**: $R + \frac{1}{M^2} (\nabla^2 R)^2 + \dots$
**Graviton**: $z=3$ dispersion in UV, $z=1$ in IR

**Worldline**: Anisotropic proper-time scaling at high frequency.
