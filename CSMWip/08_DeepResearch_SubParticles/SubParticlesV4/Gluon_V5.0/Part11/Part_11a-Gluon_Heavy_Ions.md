## Part 11 of 14 — Gluon in Heavy Ions: QGP, Jet Quenching, Flow, Gluon Observables

---

### Abstract

Gluons in heavy-ion collisions. QGP formation, jet quenching, elliptic flow, direct photons, J/ψ. Gluon-dominated observables. RHIC, LHC, future EIC e+A.

---

### 1. Initial State: Gluon Saturation → Glasma

**CGC initial condition**: Saturated gluons at x~10⁻³
**Glasma**: Classical gluon fields after collision
**Fields**: Strong longitudinal E, B fields (flux tubes)

**Gluon production**: 
- Classical: dN/dy ~ Q_s²/α_s
- Quantum: small-x evolution

**Worldline**: Glasma = coherent worldline bundle at high density.

---

### 2. QGP Formation & Thermalization

**Bottom-up thermalization** (Baier et al.):
1. τ ~ 1/Q_s: Hard gluons (Q_s), free stream
2. τ ~ α_s^{-3/2}/Q_s: Soft gluons, instabilities
3. τ ~ α_s^{-5/2}/Q_s: Thermal QGP

**Hydrodynamics**: η/s ~ 0.1-0.2 — gluon-dominated viscosity

**Lattice**: ε/T⁴, p/T⁴ — gluons carry ~80% of energy.

---

### 3. Jet Quenching: Gluon Energy Loss

**Mechanisms**:
- **Radiative**: Gluon bremsstrahlung in medium (BDMPS-Z)
- **Collisional**: Elastic scattering with thermal gluons

**q̂ (transport coefficient)**: 
- q̂ = 1-2 GeV²/fm (RHIC), 2-4 GeV²/fm (LHC)
- q̂ ∝ gluon density in medium

**Gluon vs quark**: ΔE_g / ΔE_q = C_A/C_F = 9/4

**Jet R_AA**: 
- R_AA^g < R_AA^q at same p_T
- Gluon jet fragmentation softer in medium

---

### 4. Elliptic Flow: Gluon Response

**v₂(p_T)**: Azimuthal anisotropy
**Gluon contribution**: 
- Initial eccentricity → pressure gradients → flow
- Gluon pressure dominates early time (τ < 1 fm/c)

**Gluon v₂ > Quark v₂** at low p_T (hydro regime)

**Event-by-event**: Initial gluon fluctuations → v_n fluctuations

---

### 5. Direct Photons: Gluon Compton

**Process**: g + q → γ + q (Compton)
**Thermal photons**: q + q̄ → γ + g (annihilation)

**Prompt photons**: Initial hard scattering
**Thermal photons**: QGP emission (T, flow)

**Photon v₂**: Probes early-time gluon dynamics

---

### 6. J/ψ Suppression & Regeneration

**Suppression**: Color screening (Debye mass m_D ~ gT)
**Regeneration**: c + c̄ → J/ψ in QGP (gluon-mediated)

**Sequential suppression**: 
- χ_c, ψ' melt at lower T
- J/ψ survives to higher T

**Gluon role**: Both screening and regeneration via gluons.

---

### 7. Worldline Picture: Heavy Ions as Worldline Collisions

**Two nuclei** = two worldline bundles colliding
**Overlap region** = high worldline density → QGP
**Jet** = probe worldline through medium
**Flow** = collective worldline motion
**Photon** = worldline electromagnetic radiation
