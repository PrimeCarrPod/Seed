## Part 5 of 14 — Higgs Differential: $p_T^h, y_h$, Jet Bins, STXS, EFT

---

### Abstract

Higgs differential cross sections. $p_T^h$, rapidity $y_h$, jet multiplicity. Simplified Template Cross Sections (STXS). EFT interpretation. N3LO predictions.

---

### 1. $p_T^h$ Distribution

**Regions**:
- $p_T^h < 50$ GeV: Non-perturbative, resummation
- $50 < p_T^h < 200$ GeV: Fixed-order (NNLO)
- $p_T^h > 200$ GeV: BSM sensitive, EFT

**NNLO+PS**: $gg \to h$ matched to parton shower
**$p_T^h$ spectrum**: $\sigma \sim 1/p_T^4$ at high $p_T$

**Measurement**: 
- ATLAS/CMS: $p_T^h$ to 400 GeV
- Precision: 5-10% per bin

---

### 2. Rapidity $y_h$ & Jet Bins

**Rapidity**: $y_h$ — probes PDFs, gluon at high $x$
**Jet bins**:
- 0-jet: Inclusive, resummation
- 1-jet: $p_T^h \sim p_T^{\text{jet}}$
- $\ge 2$-jet: VBF-enriched, $h+jets$

**STXS bins** (STage 1.2):
- $p_T^h$: [0, 10, 50, 100, 200, 300, 450, 650, $\infty$] GeV
- $N_{\text{jets}}$: 0, 1, $\ge 2$
- VBF: $m_{jj} > 400$ GeV, $\Delta\eta_{jj} > 2.5$

---

### 3. STXS Framework

**Simplified Template Cross Sections**:
- Model-independent bins
- Minimal theory dependence
- Combine across channels

**STXS 1.2** (2020): 122 bins for $ggF, VBF, WH, ZH, t\bar{t}h, bbH, tH$

**Measurements**: $\mu_i = \sigma_i / \sigma_i^{\text{SM}}$ per bin

---

### 4. EFT Interpretation of Differential Data

**SMEFT effects on $p_T^h$**:
- $\mathcal{O}_{HG}$: Contact $hgg$ → high $p_T$ enhancement
- $\mathcal{O}_{tH}$: Top Yukawa → $p_T^h$ shape
- $\mathcal{O}_H$: $\delta\kappa_V$ → overall normalization

**Global fit**: $p_T^h$ + $y_h$ + $N_{\text{jets}}$ → SMEFT coefficients
