### Abstract

Higgs total width $\Gamma_h$. Off-shell $ZZ^*$ interference method. Lifetime from flight distance. BSM width constraints. Future projections.

### 1. Higgs Total Width in SM

**SM prediction**: $\Gamma_h^{\text{SM}} = 4.07 \pm 0.05$ MeV
**Dominant**: $h \to bb$ ($\sim 58\%$), $h \to WW^*$ ($\sim 21\%$), $h \to gg$ ($\sim 8\%$), $h \to \tau\tau$ ($\sim 6\%$)
**Challenge**: $\Gamma_h \ll$ detector resolution ($\sim 1$ GeV)

---

### 2. Off-Shell $ZZ^*$ Interference Method

**Principle**: $gg \to h^* \to ZZ$ interferes with $gg \to ZZ$ continuum
**Off-shell cross section**: $\sigma_{\text{off-shell}} \propto \Gamma_h \times \kappa_g^2 \kappa_Z^2$
**On-shell**: $\sigma_{\text{on-shell}} \propto \kappa_g^2 \kappa_Z^2 / \Gamma_h$

**Ratio**: $\sigma_{\text{off}}/\sigma_{\text{on}} \propto \Gamma_h^2$

**Measurements**:
- ATLAS: $\Gamma_h < 3.2$ MeV (95% CL)
- CMS: $\Gamma_h < 3.6$ MeV (95% CL)
- Combined: $\Gamma_h = 3.2^{+2.4}_{-1.7}$ MeV (consistent with SM)

---

### 3. Lifetime from Flight Distance

**Lifetime**: $\tau_h = \hbar / \Gamma_h \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-22}$ s
**Flight distance**: $c\tau_h \approx 48$ fm
**Challenge**: Too short for direct vertexing

**Future**: 
- $h \to \gamma\gamma$ vertex at FCC-ee (displaced vertex)
- $h \to bb$ at $\mu$C (impact parameter)

---

### 4. BSM Width Contributions

**Invisible decays**: $h \to \text{inv}$ (DM, neutrinos)
- $BR_{\text{inv}} < 0.11$ (95% CL) → $\Gamma_{\text{inv}} < 0.45$ MeV
- $h \to \tilde{\chi}^0 \tilde{\chi}^0$ (SUSY), $h \to \nu\nu$ (Dirac)

**Undetected**: $h \to c\bar{c}$, $h \to gg$ (if not tagged)
**Exotic**: $h \to aa$ (scalar), $h \to Z_d Z_d$ (dark photon)
