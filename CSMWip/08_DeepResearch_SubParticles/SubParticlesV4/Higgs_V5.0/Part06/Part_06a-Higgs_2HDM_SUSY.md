### 1. Two-Higgs-Doublet Model (2HDM)

**Two doublets**: $\Phi_1, \Phi_2$ with $Y=1/2$
**Physical scalars**: $h^0, H^0, A^0, H^\pm$
**Parameters**: $\tan\beta = v_2/v_1$, $\cos(\beta-\alpha)$ (alignment)

**Types** (Z₂ symmetry):
| Type | $\Phi_1$ couples to | $\Phi_2$ couples to |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **I** | None | All fermions |
| **II** | Down-type | Up-type + $\nu$ |
| **X** (Lepton) | Quarks | Leptons |
| **Y** (Flipped) | Down + leptons | Up |

**Alignment limit**: $\cos(\beta-\alpha) \to 0$ → $h^0$ SM-like

---

### 2. MSSM Higgs Sector

**Supersymmetry**: 2HDM with SUSY relations
**Tree-level**: $m_h^2 \le m_Z^2 \cos^2 2\beta$ → needs radiative corrections
**Radiative**: $\Delta m_h^2 \approx \frac{3m_t^4}{4\pi^2 v^2} \log(m_{\tilde{t}}^2/m_t^2)$
**Parameters**: $m_A, \tan\beta, \mu, A_t, m_{\tilde{t}}$

**Decoupling limit**: $m_A \gg m_Z$ → $h^0$ SM-like, $H^0, A^0, H^\pm$ heavy

---

### 3. Heavy Higgs Searches

**Channels**:
- $H/A \to \tau\tau$ (Type II, large $\tan\beta$)
- $H/A \to t\bar{t}$ (Type I/II, moderate $\tan\beta$)
- $H/A \to ZZ, WW$ (alignment deviation)
- $H^\pm \to tb, \tau\nu$

**Constraints** (95% CL):
- Type II: $m_A > 1$ TeV at $\tan\beta=10$
- Type I: $m_A > 500$ GeV at $\tan\beta=5$
- $H^\pm$: $m_{H^\pm} > 600$ GeV (LHC)

---

### 4. BSM Higgs Couplings in 2HDM/MSSM

**Coupling modifiers** (alignment limit):
- $\kappa_V = \sin(\beta-\alpha) \approx 1 - \frac{1}{2}\cos^2(\beta-\alpha)$
- $\kappa_u = \cos\alpha/\sin\beta$ (Type I: $\cot\beta$, Type II: $\cot\beta$)
- $\kappa_d = -\sin\alpha/\cos\beta$ (Type I: $\cot\beta$, Type II: $\tan\beta$)
- $\kappa_\ell$ (Type X: $\tan\beta$, Type Y: $\cot\beta$)

**Global fit**: $\cos(\beta-\alpha)$ vs $\tan\beta$ from $\kappa$ measurements
