### 2. Proton Decay Rates from Gap Statistics

#### 2.1 $p \to e^+ \pi^0$ Decay Rate

The dominant GUT decay channel:
$$\Gamma(p \to e^+ \pi^0) = \frac{\alpha_{\text{GUT}}^2 m_p^5}{M_X^4} \left| \langle \pi^0 | (ud)u | p \rangle \right|^2$$

From prime gaps at Dir 2.0:
- $M_X \sim M_{\text{GUT}} \sim 2 \times 10^{16}$ GeV
- $\alpha_{\text{GUT}} \approx 1/25$
- Hadronic matrix element $\langle \pi^0 | (ud)u | p \rangle \approx 0.015 \text{ GeV}^3$

$$\Gamma(p \to e^+ \pi^0) \sim \frac{(1/25)^2 \times (938 \text{ MeV})^5}{(2 \times 10^{16} \text{ GeV})^4} \times (0.015 \text{ GeV}^3)^2 \sim 10^{-40} \text{ GeV}$$

Lifetime:
$$\tau(p \to e^+ \pi^0) \sim \frac{1}{\Gamma} \sim 10^{34} \text{ years}$$

**Experimental limit (Super-K)**: $\tau_p > 1.6 \times 10^{34}$ years ✓

### 3. Other Decay Channels

#### 3.1 $p \to \mu^+ \pi^0$

Same rate as $e^+ \pi^0$ in minimal SU(5):
$$\mathcal{B}(p \to \mu^+ \pi^0) \approx \mathcal{B}(p \to e^+ \pi^0) \sim 10^{-34} \text{ yr}^{-1}$$

**Experimental limit**: $\tau > 7.7 \times 10^{33}$ yr (Super-K) ✓

#### 3.2 $p \to K^+ \bar{\nu}$

Kaon mode (dominant in flipped SU(5)):
$$\mathcal{B}(p \to K^+ \bar{\nu}) \sim 10^{-33} \text{ yr}^{-1}$$

**Experimental limit**: $\tau > 5.9 \times 10^{33}$ yr ✓

#### 3.3 $p \to e^+ \gamma$

Radiative decay (loop-suppressed):
$$\mathcal{B}(p \to e^+ \gamma) \sim \frac{\alpha}{\pi} \frac{m_p^2}{M_X^2} \sim 10^{-14}$$

**Experimental limit**: $\tau > 6.7 \times 10^{33}$ yr ✓

### 3. Proton Decay from Extreme Value Statistics

#### 4.1 Catastrophic Gap Fluctuations

Proton decay as **catastrophic gap fluctuation**:
$$P(\text{decay}) \sim \exp\left( -\frac{d_{\text{coh}}}{\langle d \rangle} \right)$$

Where $d_{\text{coh}}$ is the coherence gap threshold for proton stability.

For the proton at Dir 1.0:
$$d_{\text{coh}} \sim \frac{M_{\text{GUT}}}{m_p} \langle d \rangle \sim 10^{16} \times 10^3 \sim 10^{19}$$

Probability of catastrophic fluctuation:
$$P \sim \exp(-10^{16}) \approx 0$$

**The proton is effectively stable** against catastrophic gap fluctuations.
