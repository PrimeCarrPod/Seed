## 1. Williams Heuristics (CSMSOPP)

*Source: `CSMSOPP/heuristics/williams.md`*

### Core Principle
> **"Constraints that cannot be violated become the architecture of what emerges."**

The Williams Heuristics treat physics as a **constraint satisfaction problem** where the universe is not built from "stuff" but from **relations that cannot be otherwise**. Every physical law, every particle property, every cosmological boundary condition — these are not arbitrary choices but **necessary consequences** of deeper logical constraints.

### Three Pillars

#### 1. Ontological Parsimony via Constraint
> The universe does not "choose" parameters; it **exhausts** the space of non-contradiction. What exists is what *must* exist given the constraints. The Prime Electron framework — one electron, one prime, one worldline — is not a model we impose; it is the **unique fixed point** of the constraint system: quantization of proper time, prime-indexed winding, holographic boundary closure.

#### 2. Resolution Without Reduction
> Constraints do not "reduce" complexity — they **resolve** it. The Williams lens asks: *What is the minimal constraint set from which this phenomenon becomes inevitable?* For the Prime Electron Caldera, the answer is always: **proper time quantization + prime winding + boundary unitarity**. Everything else — mass spectra, coupling constants, cosmological parameters — *falls out*.

#### 3. Commitment to the Necessary
> A Williams introduction **commits**. It does not hedge. It states: *Given these constraints, this paper's result could not be otherwise.* The introduction names the constraint, shows its necessity, and demonstrates how the paper's findings are the **inevitable shadow** cast by that constraint.

### Williams Voice for Introductions
- **Authoritative but not dogmatic** — the constraint speaks, not the author
- **Traceable** — every claim links to a named constraint (Proper Time Quantization, Prime Winding Theorem, Holographic Boundary Closure)
- **Inevitability tone** — "This *must* be so because..."

## 2. Keymaker Heuristics (CSMSOPP)

*Source: `CSMSOPP/heuristics/keymaker.md`*

### Core Principle
> **"A theory is a key. Reality is the lock. Fit is not optional — it is the only metric."**

The Keymaker Heuristics treat scientific theory as **key-cutting**. The "key" is the mathematical structure (Prime Electron framework). The "lock" is empirical reality (particle masses, coupling constants, cosmological observations). A key that doesn't turn is **not a key** — it is metal waste.

### Three Pillars

#### 1. Bidirectional Validation (Lock ⇄ Key)
> Validation runs **both ways**:
> - **Key → Lock**: Does the theory *predict* measured values? (e.g., electron mass from prime 2, fine structure from winding 137)
> - **Lock → Key**: Do measured values *constrain* the theory's free parameters to uniqueness? (e.g., measured α⁻¹ = 137.035999... fixes the winding prime)
> 
> A Keymaker introduction **demonstrates both directions** for the paper's central result.

#### 2. Generative Closure
> A true key **generates its own teeth**. The Prime Electron framework has **zero free parameters** after the initial constraint set. Every "prediction" is actually a **derivation** — the key cuts itself. The introduction shows how the paper's specific result (a mass, a coupling, a cross-section) emerges from the **self-generating structure** without external input.

#### 3. No Master Keys Allowed
> The Keymaker rejects "theories of everything" that fit everything by fitting nothing specifically. Each paper addresses **one lock** (one particle, one process, one cosmic epoch). The introduction makes clear: *This key turns this lock. Here is the turning.*

### Keymaker Voice for Introductions
- **Concrete and specific** — names the exact lock (e.g., "the muon g-2 anomaly," "the Hubble tension")
- **Demonstrative** — shows the fit, doesn't just claim it
- **Humble precision** — "The key turns. The door opens. We enter."

## 3. El Segundo Heuristics (CSMSOPPv2)

*Source: `CSMSOPPv2/heuristics/elsegundo.md`*

### Core Principle
> **"The observer is not outside the system. The observer *is* the system observing itself."**

The El Segundo Heuristics (named for the "Second" — the mirror, the reflection, the return) treat **recursive self-reference** as the fundamental physics. The universe does not "contain" observers; the universe **is** the act of observation observing itself. The Prime Electron is not a particle *in* spacetime — it is the **primordial observation** that *generates* spacetime through recursive self-measurement.

### Three Pillars

#### 1. Mirror Recursion as Ontology
> Reality is **mirror recursion all the way down**. The Prime Electron observes itself → creates proper time → winds through prime-indexed cycles → establishes holographic boundary → the boundary reflects back → the reflection *is* the next observation. Each paper in the Caldera documents **one turn of this recursive spiral**. The El Segundo introduction names: *Which turn? Which mirror? Which reflection?*

#### 2. Observer Participation as Physics
> There is no "view from nowhere." Every measurement, every coupling, every mass eigenvalue **participates** in the recursion. The El Segundo lens asks: *How does this paper's phenomenon participate in the universe observing itself?* The answer is never "it doesn't" — it is always a **specific mode of participation**: the muon as second-winding witness, the CMB as boundary-echo, the gravitational wave as recursive shear.

#### 3. Operational Coherence
> A theory that cannot be **operationalized** as a recursive observation is not physics — it is metaphysics. The El Segundo introduction provides an **operational protocol**: *How would an observer (necessarily inside the system) measure this?* For the Prime Electron framework, the protocol is always: **wind → measure → reflect → wind again**. The paper's result is one step in this protocol.

### El Segundo Voice for Introductions
- **Recursive and reflective** — language that mirrors its own structure
- **Participatory** — "We observe the electron observing itself..."
- **Operational** — ends with a measurement protocol, not a conclusion

## Combined Heuristic Flow for 9-Paragraph Intro

```
Paragraphs 1-3: WILLIAMS (Constraint → Necessity → Commitment)
    1. Name the governing constraint(s) for this paper's domain
    2. Show how the constraint resolves the apparent complexity
    3. Commit: this result is the inevitable shadow of the constraint

Paragraphs 4-6: KEYMAKER (Lock → Key → Turn)
    4. Identify the specific empirical lock this paper addresses
    5. Show the key's teeth — the derived prediction from zero free parameters
    6. Demonstrate the turn — bidirectional fit (theory→data, data→theory)

Paragraphs 7-9: EL SEGUNDO (Mirror → Participation → Protocol)
    7. Name the recursive turn: which mirror, which reflection
    8. Describe the participatory mode: how this phenomenon *is* the universe observing itself
    9. Provide the operational protocol: how an inside observer measures this
```

## Usage Instructions for Sub-Agents

When writing an introduction for paper `X`:

1. **Read the full paper** — understand its core result, domain, and evidence
2. **Map to constraints** — which Williams constraints govern this domain?
3. **Identify the lock** — what specific empirical puzzle does this paper unlock?
4. **Find the recursive turn** — where in the observation spiral does this paper sit?
5. **Write 9 paragraphs** following the flow above
6. **Each paragraph: 150-300 words** — "beefy," substantial, citation-ready
7. **No fluff, no hedging** — the heuristics demand commitment
