### Abstract

Higgs in early universe. Electroweak phase transition (EWPT). Baryogenesis via EWBG. Gravitational waves from EWPT. Higgs inflation. Vacuum stability.

### 1. Electroweak Phase Transition (EWPT)

**Order parameter**: $\langle \phi \rangle = v(T)$
**SM**: Crossover (no phase transition) for $m_h = 125$ GeV
**BSM**: 1st order EWPT possible in 2HDM, MSSM, singlet extensions

**Critical temperature**: $T_c \sim 100$ GeV
**Strength**: $\xi = v(T_c)/T_c$
**1st order condition**: $\xi > 1$ (for baryogenesis)

---

### 2. Baryogenesis via EWBG (Electroweak Baryogenesis)

**Sakharov conditions**:
1. B violation: Sphalerons ($B+L$ violation)
2. C/CP violation: CKM + BSM (e.g., 2HDM, MSSM)
3. Departure from equilibrium: 1st order PT bubble walls

**Mechanism**: 
- Bubbles nucleate, expand
- CPV at bubble wall → chiral asymmetry
- Sphalerons in symmetric phase → baryon asymmetry

**BSM required**: SM CPV insufficient, SM crossover

**2HDM/MSSM**: CPV in Higgs sector → EWBG viable

---

### 3. Gravitational Waves from EWPT

**GW sources**: Bubble collisions, sound waves, turbulence
**Spectrum**: $h^2 \Omega_{\text{GW}}(f) \sim 10^{-11} \left(\frac{H_*}{\beta}\right)^2 \left(\frac{\kappa \alpha}{1+\alpha}\right)^2$

**Parameters**:
- $\alpha$: Latent heat / radiation energy
- $\beta/H_*$: Inverse duration
- $v_w$: Bubble wall velocity

**LISA reach**: $\alpha \sim 0.1$, $\beta/H \sim 100$ → detectable

---

### 4. Higgs Inflation

**Non-minimal coupling**: $\xi h^2 R$
**Einstein frame**: $U(\chi) = \frac{\lambda v^4}{4} (1 - e^{-\sqrt{2/3}\chi/M_{\text{Pl}}})^2$
**Predictions**: $n_s \approx 0.967$, $r \approx 0.003$
**Constraints**: $\xi \sim 10^4$, $m_h = 125$ GeV consistent

**Unitarity**: $\Lambda \sim M_{\text{Pl}}/\xi \sim 10^{13}$ GeV

---

### 5. Vacuum Stability

**Higgs potential at high scale**: $V_{\text{eff}}(h) = \frac{1}{4} \lambda_{\text{eff}}(h) h^4$
**RG running**: $\lambda(\mu)$ at 3-loop
**Instability scale**: $\lambda(\mu) < 0$ for $\mu > 10^{10}$ GeV (central)
**Metastability**: $\tau_{\text{vac}} \gg t_{\text{universe}}$

**BSM stabilization**: 
- SUSY: $\lambda$ driven positive
- 2HDM: Extra scalars modify RG
- Portal: $S^2 \Phi^\dagger \Phi$
