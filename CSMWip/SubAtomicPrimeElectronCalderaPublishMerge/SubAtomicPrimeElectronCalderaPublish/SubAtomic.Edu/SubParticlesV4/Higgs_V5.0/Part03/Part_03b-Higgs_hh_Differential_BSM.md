### 1. Differential $hh$ Distributions

**Key observables**:
- $m_{hh}$: Invariant mass — $\lambda_{hhh}$ sensitivity
- $p_T^h$: Higgs $p_T$ — top loop vs triangle

### 2. BSM in $hh$: Anomalous Couplings

**Effective Lagrangian**:
$$\mathcal{L} = \lambda_{hhh} h^3 + \frac{c_g}{v^2} h^2 G^2 + \frac{c_\gamma}{v^2} h^2 F^2 + \frac{c_{t}}{v} h^2 \bar{t}t + \dots$$

**$c_g$**: $gg \to hh$ contact → modifies $m_{hh}$ shape
**$c_t$**: Top Yukawa in $hh$ → modifies $p_T^h$
**$c_\lambda$**: $\lambda_{hhh}$ deviation

**Global fit**: $c_g, c_t, c_\lambda$ from $m_{hh}, p_T^h$ distributions

### 3. Heavy Resonances $\to hh$

**Kaluza-Klein gravitons** (RS): $G_{\text{KK}} \to hh$
**Radion** (stabilized extra dim): $\phi \to hh$
**Extended Higgs sector**: $H, A \to hh$ (2HDM, MSSM)

**Signatures**: Narrow peak in $m_{hh}$ at $m_X$
**Constraints**: $\sigma(pp \to X \to hh) < 10-100$ fb (ATLAS/CMS)

### 5. EFT for $hh$: SMEFT Dimension-6
