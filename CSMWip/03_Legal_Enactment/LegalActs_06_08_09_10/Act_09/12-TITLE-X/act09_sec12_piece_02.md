## SEC. 1005. CIVIL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1005.** Any person who fails to comply with this Act shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation per day.

## SEC. 1006. CRIMINAL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1006.** Any person who willfully violates this Act shall be subject to criminal penalties, including fines up to $500,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years.
