## SEC. 1013. ENFORCEMENT ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 1013.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with enforcement requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with enforcement activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

**SEC. 1014.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
