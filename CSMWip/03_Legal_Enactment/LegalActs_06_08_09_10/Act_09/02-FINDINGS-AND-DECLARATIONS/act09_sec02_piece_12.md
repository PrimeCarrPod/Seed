## CONGRESSIONAL INTENT

**SEC. 222.** It is the intent of Congress that this Act be implemented in a manner that:

**(1)** Maximizes the effectiveness of federal water infrastructure investments.

**(2)** Prioritizes the needs of communities with the greatest water challenges.

**(3)** Promotes innovation and technology adoption.

**(4)** Protects public health and the environment.

**(5)** Advances environmental justice.

**(6)** Strengthens national security through water security.

**(7)** Builds resilience to climate change.

**(8)** Creates good-paying jobs in the water industry.
