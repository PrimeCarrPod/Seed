# TITLE VI — FUNDING MECHANISMS

## SEC. 601. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 601.** This title may be cited as the "Water Infrastructure Funding Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 602. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO FUNDING

**SEC. 602.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Funding Gap**
Current federal water infrastructure funding is insufficient to address the scale of the challenge.

**(2) Dedicated Funding Needed**
A dedicated, sustainable funding mechanism is needed.

**(3) Economic Return**
Every dollar invested in water infrastructure returns $4.30 in economic benefits.

---

## SEC. 603. PURPOSES OF TITLE VI

**SEC. 603.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a Water Infrastructure Fund.

**(2)** Provide tax incentives for water infrastructure investment.

**(3)** Ensure sustainable, long-term funding.

---

## SEC. 604. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VI

**SEC. 604.** In this title:

**(1) "Water Infrastructure Fund"**
The fund established under Sec. 605.

## SEC. 605. WATER INFRASTRUCTURE FUND

**SEC. 605.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Water Infrastructure Fund.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $150,000,000,000 over 10 years.

**(c) ALLOCATION.—**Funds shall be allocated as follows:
(1) Title I (Water Infrastructure): $80,000,000,000
(2) Title II (Drought Resilience): $35,000,000,000
(3) Title III (Flood Management): $45,000,000,000
(4) Title IV (Water Conservation): $20,000,000,000
(5) Title V (Climate Adaptation): $30,000,000,000

---

## SEC. 606. TAX INCENTIVES

**SEC. 606.** (a) WATER INFRASTRUCTURE TAX CREDIT.—**A 25% tax credit for water infrastructure investment.

**(b) WATER CONSERVATION TAX CREDIT.—**A 20% tax credit for water conservation investment.

---

## SEC. 607. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE VI

**SEC. 607.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $150,000,000,000 over 10 years.
