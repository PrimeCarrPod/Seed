## SEC. 1020. REGULATORY REPORTING

**SEC. 1020.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on regulatory activities, including:
(A) Regulatory development progress
(B) Compliance rates
(C) Enforcement actions
(D) Expenditures

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of regulatory activities.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying regulatory data.

## SEC. 1021. REGULATORY SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1021.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
