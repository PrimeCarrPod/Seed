## SEC. 911. IMPLEMENTATION COORDINATION

**SEC. 911.** (a) FEDERAL INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREVENTION COUNCIL.—**There is established a National Wildfire Prevention Council to coordinate implementation across federal agencies.

**(2) MEMBERSHIP.—**The Council shall include:
(A) Secretary of Agriculture (Chair)
(B) Secretary of Energy
(C) Secretary of Interior
(D) Secretary of Homeland Security
(E) Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency
(F) Director of the Federal Emergency Management Agency
(G) Other relevant federal officials

**(3) FUNCTIONS.—**The Council shall:
(A) Coordinate implementation across federal agencies
(B) Resolve interagency conflicts
(C) Develop joint implementation strategies
(D) Report annually to Congress on implementation progress

**(b) STATE COORDINATION.—

**(1) STATE IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**States shall develop state implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(2) STATE COORDINATORS.—**Each state shall designate a state wildfire prevention coordinator.

**(c) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) TRIBAL CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on implementation activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(d) LOCAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Local governments in high fire risk areas shall develop local implementation plans.

**(2) LOCAL COORDINATORS.—**Local governments shall designate local wildfire prevention coordinators.
