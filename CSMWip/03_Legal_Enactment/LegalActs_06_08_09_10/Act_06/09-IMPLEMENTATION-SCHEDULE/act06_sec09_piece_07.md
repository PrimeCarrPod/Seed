## SEC. 913. IMPLEMENTATION PREEMPTION

**SEC. 913.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national implementation standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt implementation standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 914. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 914.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

---

## SEC. 915. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 915.** (a) ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT.—**This title amends the Administrative Procedure Act as provided in Title VIII.

**(b) STATE ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the authority of state administrative agencies.

**(c) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.
