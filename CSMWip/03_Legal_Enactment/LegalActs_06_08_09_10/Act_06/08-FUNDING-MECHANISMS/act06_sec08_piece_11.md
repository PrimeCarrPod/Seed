## SEC. 826. FUND PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 826.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for Fund expenditures, including:

**(1)** Acres treated per dollar spent
**(2)** Structures protected per dollar spent
**(3)** Ignition risk reduction per dollar spent
**(4)** Community preparedness improvement per dollar spent
**(5)** Jobs created per dollar spent
**(6)** Economic return on investment

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on Fund performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

## SEC. 827. FUND EVALUATION

**SEC. 827.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of Fund activities every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of Fund programs
(B) Efficiency of Fund operations
(C) Equity of Fund distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
