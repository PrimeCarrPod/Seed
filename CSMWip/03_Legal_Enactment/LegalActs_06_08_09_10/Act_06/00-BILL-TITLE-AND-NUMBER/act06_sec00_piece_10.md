## SEVERABILITY AND RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

### Severability Clause

If any provision of this Act or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the Act and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

### Relationship to Existing Law

**Amendments to Existing Statutes:**

1. **Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (FLPMA, 43 U.S.C. §1701 et seq.)**
   - Amends §1732 to add wildfire prevention as a primary land use designation
   - Adds §1786 establishing federal vegetation management standards

2. **National Forest Management Act of 1976 (16 U.S.C. §1600 et seq.)**
   - Amends §1604 to require wildfire risk assessment in all forest plans
   - Adds §1615 establishing community firebreak grant program

3. **Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. §791a et seq.)**
   - Amends §824o to add wildfire prevention to FERC reliability standards
   - Adds §824w establishing grid hardening cost recovery mechanism

4. **Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. §5121 et seq.)**
   - Amends §5170a to include wildfire mitigation in disaster declarations
   - Adds §5174b establishing pre-fire mitigation grant program

5. **National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. §4321 et seq.)**
   - Adds §4332a establishing categorical exclusions for emergency vegetation management

### Savings Clause

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to:
(1) Diminish the authority of any federal agency under existing law
(2) Supersede any state or tribal law that provides greater protection than this Act
(3) Affect the rights of any party under any treaty or international agreement
(4) Limit the ability of any state to adopt more stringent standards

### Preemption

This Act does not preempt state or tribal law except where:
(1) Compliance with both state and federal law is impossible
(2) State law stands as an obstacle to the accomplishment of congressional purposes
(3) Express preemption is stated in specific sections of this Act
