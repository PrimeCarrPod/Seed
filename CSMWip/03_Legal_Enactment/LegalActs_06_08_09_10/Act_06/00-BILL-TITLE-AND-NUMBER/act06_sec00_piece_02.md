## BILL LONG TITLE

A BILL

To establish a comprehensive national program for wildfire prevention, grid resilience, and fire-hardened infrastructure, to authorize funding for vegetation management and firebreak construction, to create standards for fire-resistant building materials in the wildland-urban interface, to modernize wildfire response capabilities, and for other purposes.

---

## SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 101.** This Act may be cited as the "Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Act of 2026" or "WPGR Act of 2026".
