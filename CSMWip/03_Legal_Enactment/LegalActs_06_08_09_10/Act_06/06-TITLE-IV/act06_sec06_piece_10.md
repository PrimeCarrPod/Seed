## SEC. 623. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION INCENTIVES

**SEC. 623.** (a) TAX INCENTIVES.—

**(1) TAX CREDIT.—**There is allowed a tax credit of up to $10,000 for taxpayers who retrofit their primary residence to meet fire-resistant construction standards.

**(2) ACCELERATED DEPRECIATION.—**Commercial buildings retrofitted to meet fire-resistant construction standards shall qualify for accelerated depreciation.

**(b) GRANT PROGRAMS.—**

**(1) COMMUNITY GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to communities for fire-resistant construction programs.

**(2) NONPROFIT GRANTS.—**The Secretary shall provide grants to nonprofit organizations for fire-resistant construction programs serving low-income communities.

---

## SEC. 624. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION REPORTING

**SEC. 624.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on fire-resistant construction activities, including:
(A) Building code adoption rates
(B) Compliance rates
(C) Retrofit activity
(D) Structure loss data
(E) Workforce data
(F) Expenditures

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of fire-resistant construction programs.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying fire-resistant construction data.

---

## SEC. 625. FIRE-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 625.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
