## SEC. 1316. APPENDIX L — SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1316.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

## SEC. 1317. APPENDIX M — RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 1317.** (a) FEDERAL STATUTES.—**This Act amends the following federal statutes as provided in Title VIII:
(A) Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976
(B) National Forest Management Act of 1976
(C) Federal Power Act
(D) Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act
(E) National Environmental Policy Act of 1969
(F) Endangered Species Act of 1973
(G) Clean Air Act
(H) Clean Water Act
(I) National Housing Act
(J) Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970
(K) Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938
(L) Administrative Procedure Act
(M) Congressional Budget Act of 1974

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of state or local governments.

**(c) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.

---

## SEC. 1318. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE XI

**SEC. 1318.** (a) IN GENERAL.—There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $500,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) EFFECTIVE DATE.—**This title shall take effect on the date of enactment.
