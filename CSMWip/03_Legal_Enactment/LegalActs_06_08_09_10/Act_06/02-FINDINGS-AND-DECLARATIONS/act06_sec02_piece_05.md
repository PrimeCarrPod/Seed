## SECTION 205. GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE

**SEC. 205.** (a) IN GENERAL.—This Act applies to all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and all U.S. Territories.

**(b) FEDERAL LANDS.**—This Act applies to all lands managed by the Department of Interior, USDA Forest Service, Department of Defense, and other federal land management agencies.

**(c) TRIBAL LANDS.**—This Act applies to Tribal lands subject to federal trust responsibilities, with implementation through government-to-government consultation.

**(d) STATE AND PRIVATE LANDS.**—This Act applies to state and private lands through grant programs, building code requirements, and utility regulation.

**(e) HIGH FIRE RISK AREAS.**—The provisions of Titles I, II, III, and IV apply specifically to areas designated as High Fire Risk Areas by the applicable state fire marshal or federal land management agency.

## SECTION 208. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 208.** (a) IN GENERAL.—This Act does not repeal, supersede, or modify any existing federal law except as expressly provided herein.

**(b) AMENDMENTS.—**This Act amends the following statutes as provided in Title VIII:
