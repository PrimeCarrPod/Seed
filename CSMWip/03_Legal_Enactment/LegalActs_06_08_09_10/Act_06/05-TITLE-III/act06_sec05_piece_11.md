## SEC. 520. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT SAFETY STANDARDS

**SEC. 520.** (a) OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY.—

**(1) OSHA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary of Labor, in coordination with the Secretary of Agriculture, shall establish occupational safety standards for vegetation management workers, including:
(A) Chainsaw safety requirements
(B) Heavy equipment safety requirements
(C) Prescribed fire safety requirements
(D) Heat illness prevention
(E) Personal protective equipment standards
(F) Emergency response procedures

**(2) TRAINING REQUIREMENTS.—**All vegetation management workers shall complete mandatory safety training before working in the field.

**(b) PUBLIC SAFETY.—

**(1) PRESCRIBED FIRE SAFETY.—**The Secretary shall establish safety standards for prescribed fire operations, including:
(A) Burn boss qualifications
(B) Contingency planning
(C) Escape route requirements
(D) Communication protocols

**(2) EQUIPMENT SAFETY.—**The Secretary shall establish safety standards for vegetation management equipment.

## SEC. 521. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE III

**SEC. 521.** (a) IN GENERAL.—There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $25,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036, to be allocated as follows:

**(1) National Vegetation Management Program (Sec. 505)**
$10,000,000,000 over 10 years ($1 billion annually)
- Federal land treatment: $6,000,000,000
- State and private cost-share: $2,500,000,000
- Technical assistance: $1,500,000,000

**(2) Prescribed Fire Program (Sec. 506)**
$5,000,000,000 over 10 years ($500 million annually)
- Federal prescribed fire: $2,500,000,000
- State and private assistance: $1,500,000,000
- Cultural burning: $1,000,000,000

**(3) Biomass Utilization Program (Sec. 507)**
$3,000,000,000 over 10 years ($300 million annually)
- Market development: $1,000,000,000
- Processing infrastructure: $1,500,000,000
- Research and development: $500,000,000

**(4) Workforce Development (Sec. 508)**
$2,000,000,000 over 10 years ($200 million annually)
- Hiring and salaries: $1,000,000,000
- Training programs: $500,000,000
- Youth Corps: $500,000,000

**(5) Tribal and Rural Programs (Sec. 513-514)**
$3,000,000,000 over 10 years ($300 million annually)

**(6) Research and Innovation (Sec. 512, 515)**
$2,000,000,000 over 10 years ($200 million annually)

**(b) EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL.—**There is authorized to be appropriated an additional $5,000,000,000 for emergency vegetation management following wildfire, insect infestation, or disease events.

**(c) EFFECTIVE DATE.—**This title shall take effect 180 days after the date of enactment.
