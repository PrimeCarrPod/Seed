## SEC. 1232. ENFORCEMENT FOR LANDOWNERS

**SEC. 1232.** (a) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce defensible space requirements for landowners in the WUI.

**(b) PENALTIES.—**Landowners that fail to comply with defensible space requirements shall be subject to civil penalties.

**(c) MITIGATION.—**Low-income landowners shall receive assistance to achieve compliance with defensible space requirements.

---

## SEC. 1233. ENFORCEMENT FOR INSURERS

**SEC. 1233.** (a) ENFORCEMENT.—**The Secretary shall enforce insurance requirements for structures in the WUI.

**(b) PENALTIES.—**Insurers that fail to comply with insurance requirements shall be subject to civil penalties.
