## SEC. 513. INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 513.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with international partnership requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with international partnership activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 514. INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 514.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
