## SEC. 505. ALLIED SUPPLY CHAIN AGREEMENTS

**SEC. 505.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—The Secretary of State shall negotiate allied supply chain agreements with trusted partners.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**Agreements shall include:
(1) Technology sharing provisions
(2) Supply chain coordination
(3) Joint research and development
(4) Export control coordination
(5) Investment protections

**(c) PRIORITY NATIONS.—**Agreements shall be prioritized with:
(1) Japan
(2) South Korea
(3) Taiwan
(4) Australia
(5) United Kingdom
(6) European Union member states

---

## SEC. 506. TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER PROTECTIONS

**SEC. 506.** (a) SAFEGUARDS.—**Technology transfer under this title shall include safeguards against unauthorized transfer.

**(b) INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY.—**Intellectual property protections shall be maintained.

**(c) RESEARCH SECURITY.—**Research security requirements shall be included in all technology transfer agreements.

## SEC. 507. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE V

**SEC. 507.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $10,000,000,000 over 10 years.
