## SEC. 320. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 320.** (a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Labor shall develop workforce development programs for pharmaceutical supply chain industries.

**(b) PARTNERSHIPS.—**Workforce development programs shall include partnerships with:
(1) Community colleges
(2) Universities
(3) Industry associations
(4) Labor organizations

**(c) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAMS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for pharmaceutical supply chain careers.

---

## SEC. 321. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 321.** (a) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall coordinate research and development activities for pharmaceutical supply chain.

**(b) NATIONAL LABORATORIES.—**The Secretary shall utilize national laboratories for pharmaceutical supply chain research and development.

**(c) PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall establish public-private research partnerships for pharmaceutical supply chain development.
