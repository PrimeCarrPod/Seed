## Act 08: Critical Technology Supply Chain Act — Section 01
**Piece:** 10 of 12

## INTERAGENCY COORDINATION

**Critical Technology Supply Chain Council**
- Chaired by the Secretary of Commerce
- Includes representatives from all relevant federal agencies
- Meets quarterly to coordinate implementation
- Resolves interagency conflicts

**Regional Implementation Teams**
- Established in each federal region
- Coordinate with state and local governments
- Provide technical assistance to grant recipients
- Monitor implementation progress

---

## TRIBAL CONSULTATION

**Government-to-Government Consultation**
- The Secretary of Commerce shall consult with Tribal nations on all activities affecting Tribal lands or resources
- Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans
- Tribal nations are eligible for grants and other assistance

**Tribal Priority**
- Tribal communities receive priority for critical technology projects
- Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures benefit Tribal communities
- Technical assistance provided to Tribal nations

---

## STATE AND LOCAL COORDINATION

**State Implementation Plans**
- States may develop state implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan
- State implementation plans shall include:
  (A) State-specific goals and objectives
  (B) Resource requirements
  (C) Timeline for implementation
  (D) Performance metrics

**Local Government Coordination**
- Local governments shall be consulted on implementation activities
- Local governments are eligible for grants and other assistance
- Technical assistance provided to local governments
