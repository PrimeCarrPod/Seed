## TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER CONTROLS

**SEC. 19.** (a) EXPORT CONTROLS.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall maintain export controls on critical technologies.

**(b) FOREIGN INVESTMENT REVIEW.—**The Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS) shall review foreign investments in critical technology companies.

**(c) RESEARCH SECURITY.—**Federal research funding under this Act shall include research security requirements.

---

## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PROTECTIONS

**SEC. 20.** (a) PATENT PROTECTIONS.—**Inventions developed with federal funding under this Act shall be subject to applicable patent protections.

**(b) TRADE SECRET PROTECTIONS.—**Trade secrets developed under this Act shall be protected.

**(c) BAYH-DOLE ACT COMPLIANCE.—**Inventions developed with federal funding shall comply with the Bayh-Dole Act.

---

## ANTITRUST CONSIDERATIONS

**SEC. 21.** (a) ANTITRUST EXEMPTION.—**Activities under this Act shall be exempt from antitrust laws to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of this Act.

**(b) OVERSIGHT.—**The Department of Justice shall oversee activities under this exemption.
