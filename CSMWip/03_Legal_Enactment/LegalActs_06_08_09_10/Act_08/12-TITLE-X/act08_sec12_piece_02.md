## SEC. 1005. CIVIL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1005.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person who fails to comply with this Act shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation per day.

**(b) FACTORS.—**In determining the penalty amount, the Secretary shall consider:
(1) The nature and extent of the violation
(2) The violator's ability to pay
(3) The degree of culpability
(4) The economic benefit gained

---

## SEC. 1006. CRIMINAL PENALTIES

**SEC. 1006.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Any person who willfully violates this Act shall be subject to criminal penalties, including:
(1) A fine of up to $500,000 per violation
(2) Imprisonment for up to 5 years
(3) Both fine and imprisonment
