## SEC. 415. STRATEGIC RESERVE FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 415.** The Secretary of Defense shall prioritize strategic reserve releases for defense and critical infrastructure facilities.

---

## SEC. 416. STRATEGIC RESERVE PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 416.** The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for the strategic reserve, including stock levels, rotation frequency, and emergency release readiness.
