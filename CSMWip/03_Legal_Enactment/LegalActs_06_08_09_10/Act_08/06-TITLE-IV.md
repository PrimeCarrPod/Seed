# TITLE IV — STRATEGIC RESERVE

## SEC. 401. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 401.** This title may be cited as the "National Critical Technology Reserve Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 402. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO STRATEGIC RESERVE

**SEC. 402.** The Congress finds the following with respect to strategic reserves:

**(1) Supply Chain Disruptions**
Supply chain disruptions cost the U.S. economy $100 billion annually. A strategic reserve of critical materials would buffer against disruptions.

**(2) National Security**
A strategic reserve is essential for national security. The U.S. military depends on critical technologies that are subject to supply disruptions.

**(3) Economic Security**
A strategic reserve protects economic security by ensuring continued production of critical goods during supply disruptions.

**(4) Pandemic Preparedness**
The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated the need for strategic reserves of critical materials. Future pandemics or other emergencies may require similar reserves.

---

## SEC. 403. PURPOSES OF TITLE IV

**SEC. 403.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a National Critical Technology Reserve.

**(2)** Maintain a 90-day supply of critical materials.

**(3)** Buffer against supply chain disruptions.

**(4)** Ensure national security during emergencies.

**(5)** Protect economic security during supply disruptions.

---

## SEC. 404. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE IV

**SEC. 404.** In this title:

**(1) "National Critical Technology Reserve"**
A stockpile of critical materials or products maintained by the federal government to ensure supply chain resilience.

**(2) "Critical Materials"**
Materials designated as critical by the Secretary of Commerce, including semiconductors, rare earth minerals, and pharmaceuticals.

**(3) "Emergency Release"**
The release of materials from the strategic reserve in response to a supply chain disruption or national emergency.

## SEC. 405. NATIONAL CRITICAL TECHNOLOGY RESERVE

**SEC. 405.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a National Critical Technology Reserve.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**The reserve shall include:
(1) Semiconductors
(2) Rare earth minerals
(3) Active pharmaceutical ingredients
(4) Finished pharmaceutical products
(5) Other critical materials designated by the Secretary

**(c) TARGET LEVELS.—**The reserve shall maintain:
(1) 90-day supply of semiconductors
(2) 90-day supply of rare earth minerals
(3) 90-day supply of active pharmaceutical ingredients
(4) 90-day supply of essential medicines

**(d) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $15 billion over 10 years.

---

## SEC. 406. RESERVE MANAGEMENT

**SEC. 406.** (a) MANAGEMENT.—**The Department of Defense shall manage the reserve.

**(b) ROTATION.—**Reserve materials shall be rotated to prevent obsolescence.

**(c) EMERGENCY RELEASE.—**The President may authorize emergency release of reserve materials.

**(d) REPORTING.—**The Secretary of Defense shall submit an annual report on reserve status.

---

## SEC. 407. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR TITLE IV

**SEC. 407.** There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this title $20,000,000,000 over 10 years.
