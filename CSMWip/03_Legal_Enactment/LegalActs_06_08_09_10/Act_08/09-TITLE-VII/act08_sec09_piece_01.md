# TITLE VII — IMPLEMENTATION SCHEDULE

## SEC. 701. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 701.** This title may be cited as the "Critical Technology Supply Chain Implementation Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 702. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO IMPLEMENTATION

**SEC. 702.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Urgency**
The critical technology supply chain crisis demands immediate action. Every year of delay increases U.S. vulnerability.

**(2) Phased Approach**
A phased implementation approach is necessary to allow for capacity building and technology deployment.

**(3) Coordination Complexity**
Implementation requires coordination across multiple federal agencies, states, and private stakeholders.

---

## SEC. 703. PURPOSES OF TITLE VII

**SEC. 703.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a phased implementation schedule.

**(2)** Define clear roles and responsibilities.

**(3)** Establish milestones and deadlines.

**(4)** Create accountability mechanisms.

---

## SEC. 704. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VII

**SEC. 704.** In this title:

**(1) "Implementation Plan"**
The comprehensive plan developed under Sec. 705.

**(2) "Milestone"**
A specific, measurable achievement that marks progress.
