## SEC. 613. FUNDING MECHANISMS ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 613.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with funding mechanism requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with funding mechanism activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 614. FUNDING MECHANISMS JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 614.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
