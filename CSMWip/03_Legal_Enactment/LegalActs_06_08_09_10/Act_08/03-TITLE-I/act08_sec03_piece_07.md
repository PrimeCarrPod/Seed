## SEC. 112. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN REPORTING

**SEC. 112.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall submit an annual report to Congress on semiconductor supply chain activities, including:
(A) U.S. share of global semiconductor manufacturing
(B) Number of domestic fabrication facilities
(C) Domestic semiconductor production capacity
(D) Semiconductor workforce size
(E) Expenditures by program
(F) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of semiconductor supply chain programs.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying semiconductor supply chain data.

## SEC. 113. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 113.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
