## SECTION 208. RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 208.** (a) IN GENERAL.—This Act does not repeal, supersede, or modify any existing federal law except as expressly provided herein.

**(b) AMENDMENTS.—**This Act amends the following statutes as provided in Title VIII:
(1) CHIPS and Science Act of 2022
(2) Defense Production Act of 1950
(3) Trade Act of 1974
(4) National Defense Authorization Act
(5) Buy American Act

**(c) SAVINGS CLAUSE.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of any federal agency under existing law.

---

## SECTION 209. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS

**SEC. 209.** (a) CRITICAL TECHNOLOGY SUPPLY CHAIN FUND.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the Critical Technology Supply Chain Fund established under Sec. 605, $200,000,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL.—**There is authorized to be appropriated an additional $50,000,000,000 for emergency critical technology supply chain activities.

---

## SECTION 210. RULE OF CONSTRUCTION

**SEC. 210.** Nothing in this Act shall be construed to:

**(1)** Authorize the taking of private property without just compensation.

**(2)** Supersede any existing federal law except as expressly provided.

**(3)** Create a private right of action.

**(4)** Diminish the authority of any federal agency under existing law.

**(5)** Diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.
