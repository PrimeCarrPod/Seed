$$\frac{d[\text{Sericin}]}{dt} = -k \cdot [\text{OH}^-]^n \cdot [\text{Sericin}]^m$$

Where $k = A \exp(-E_a/RT)$, $E_a = 85\text{ kJ/mol}$, $A = 2.3\times10^7\text{ s}^{-1}$, $n=1.2$, $m=0.8$.
