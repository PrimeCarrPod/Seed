  - Crushing: Ball mill to <100μm

  - Sonication: 40 kHz, 100W, 30 min (ice bath)
  - Centrifugation: 3500 rpm, 10 min (collect supernatant)
  - Flake size: 1-5μm lateral, 1-3 layers thick
