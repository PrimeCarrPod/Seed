### 3.2 Edition-Specific Calibration

$$T_{z,e} = T_{z,\text{base}} \cdot \left(1 + \alpha_e \cdot \frac{M_e - M_{\text{ref}}}{M_{\text{ref}}}\right) \cdot \beta_{z,e}$$

**Morphology Factors $\alpha_e$:**

| Edition | $\alpha_e$ | $M_e/M_{\text{ref}}$ |
|---------|------------|---------------------|
| RM-TS | -0.08 | 0.92 |
| RM-TG | +0.15 | 1.15 |
| RM-SS | -0.08 | 0.92 |
| RM-SG | +0.15 | 1.15 |
