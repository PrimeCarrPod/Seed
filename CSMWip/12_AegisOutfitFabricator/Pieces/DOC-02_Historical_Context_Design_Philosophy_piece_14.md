
$$\frac{\partial g_{\text{thickness}}}{\partial t_k} = 1, \quad \sum t_k \leq T_{\text{max}} = 25\text{ mm}$$

$$\frac{\partial h_{\text{LES}}}{\partial T_z} = 1, \quad \sum_{z=1}^{12} T_z \cdot \mathbf{r}_z = \mathbf{0} \quad \text{(moment balance)}$$
