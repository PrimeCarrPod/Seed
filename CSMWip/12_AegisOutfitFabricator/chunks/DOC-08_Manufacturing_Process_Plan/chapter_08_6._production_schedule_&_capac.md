## 6. Production Schedule & Capacity

| Phase | Duration | Parallelization |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Material production | 72 hr | Batch for 4 editions |
| Component fabrication | 96 hr | Parallel lines |
| Assembly & integration | 48 hr | Single line |
