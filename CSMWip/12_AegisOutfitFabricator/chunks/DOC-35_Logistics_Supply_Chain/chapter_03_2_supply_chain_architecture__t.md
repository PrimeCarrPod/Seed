## 2. Supply Chain Architecture — Tiered Three-Level Support

### 2.1 Organizational Level (O-Level) — Field/Unit
**Personnel:** CSM-AIMES-OP (40hr cert) + CSM-AIMES-TECH (80hr cert)  
**Response:** ≤4 hours for critical LRUs, ≤24 hours for non-critical  
**Stock Objective:** 30-day supply + 15-day safety stock  

```
O-LEVEL CAPABILITIES:
  ✅ Ceramic tile replacement (magnetic alignment jig)
  ✅ Seal gasket replacement (8× interfaces)
  ✅ MR fluid top-off (pressure cart, 0.5±0.05 bar)
  ✅ Battery module swap (hot-swap capable)
  ✅ Aerogel panel replacement (quilted, 18-22 SPI)
  ✅ Lacing system repair (Dyneema SK99, Ti₃AlC₂ cleats)
  ✅ BIT execution (PBIT 01-12, CBIT continuous)
  ✅ Local calibration (EMI continuity, NIR reflectance)
```

### 2.2 Intermediate Level (I-Level) — Regional Hubs
**Locations:** 6 Regional Logistics Centers (RLC): CONUS-East, CONUS-West, EUCOM, INDOPAC, CENTCOM, SOUTHCOM  
**Personnel:** CSM-AIMES-TECH (80hr cert) + CSM-AIMES-INST (120hr cert)  
**Response:** ≤72 hours for all LRUs, ≤2 weeks for rebuild  
**Stock Objective:** 90-day supply + 30-day safety stock + 15% fleet war reserve  

```
I-LEVEL CAPABILITIES:
  ✅ MR fluid full service (CIP separation, oil distillation, reformulation)
  ✅ Battery module reconditioning (cell balance, SOC calibration)
  ✅ Aerogel panel re-quilting (recovery ≥90% thermal performance)
  ✅ FSS module repair (MXene thread re-embroidery, ultrasonic)
  ✅ Framework realignment (baleen straightening, hoop re-circularization)
  ✅ Ceramic tile re-sintering (ZrB₂-SiC, 1650°C vacuum furnace)
  ✅ PVDF-TrFE sensor array re-calibration (240 nodes)
  ✅ Seal gasket re-manufacturing (mold + cure, CoAl₂O₄ coating)
```

### 2.3 Depot Level (D-Level) — CSM Facility
**Location:** CSM Advanced Manufacturing Facility (classified location)  
**Personnel:** CSM-AIMES-INST (120hr cert) + Engineering Support  
**Response:** ≤2 weeks for structural rebuild, ≤4 weeks for ASIC/neuromorphic  
**Stock Objective:** 180-day supply + strategic reserve (5% fleet) + DMSMS pipeline  

```
D-LEVEL CAPABILITIES:
  ✅ Structural chassis rebuild (BFRP/Elium®, autoclave)
  ✅ MHD generator channel re-manufacture (electrode re-coat)
  ✅ Neuromorphic ASIC replacement (foundry coordination)
  ✅ Phoenix Protocol processing (full material recovery)
  ✅ Edition reconfiguration (LE-TS ↔ LE-TG ↔ LE-SS ↔ LE-SG)
  ✅ Recertification (full MIL-STD-810H, NIJ IV, NFPA 1971)
  ✅ Obsolescence redesign (form-fit-function replacement)
```
