### The Empirical Lock

The lock this document addresses is the **fabrication fidelity gap** — the persistent failure of modern textile engineering to simultaneously achieve:
- Historical geometric fidelity (Renaissance pattern intelligence)
- Material property optimization (silk fibroin, structural composites, protective layers)
- Zero-waste fabrication (Alcega-level nesting efficiency)
- Parametric adaptability (Garsault-level proportional scaling across bodies)
- Cross-property validation (MAT-04 matrix: every property tested against every other)

For decades, each of these has been a separate specialization: pattern makers know history, material scientists know properties, nesting software knows optimization, CAD knows parameters. The lock is precise: any system claiming to unify textile engineering must produce Alcega layouts, Garsault scaling, and MAT-04 validation from *zero free parameters* — and must explain why the historical masters achieved zero-waste without computers, while modern industry averages 15% waste with them.
