# 01 - Repository Portfolio Evaluation (Revenue Lens)

Source index: `CSMWip/CSMReadMe/legacy_indices/REPO_INDEX_V7.csv` (460 projects) and `REPO_INDEX_V7_SUMMARY.md`.
Method: every index category was assigned to a read-only reviewer that opened each project's README, TODO, and main documents, then verified the index claims against real files.

## How to read the scores

- **Maturity 0-5:** 0 = empty or idea only; 1 = notes or literature; 2 = detailed spec or desk research, no build or test data; 3 = prototype-ready package or finished content; 4 = finished, packaged, sellable content or a working app; 5 = operating business.
- **Monetizable form:** the thing a buyer can actually pay for.

## Ground truth before you rely on the repo

1. **Most README files are generated boilerplate.** The V7 summary says 411 projects "have READMEs." Those files were produced by the README generator (`README_GENERATOR/`, `generate_summaries.awk`). Treat them as navigation, not as evidence of finished work.
2. **Dollar figures are projections, not revenue.** Every TAM, ROI, "$X billion," grant ask, and unit-cost number found in the repo is an internal planning figure. None is backed by invoices, quotes, lab reports, or customers. Do not quote them to a buyer or investor without independent sourcing.
3. **No physical-product CAD exists.** A glob for `.stl`, `.step`, `.kicad_pcb`, and gerber files under CSMFAB found none. Physical products are at specification level.
4. **No test data exists for the shielding claims.** No SE (shielding effectiveness), GIC, or EMP measurements were found anywhere in the repository.
5. **Some content carries real legal or health risk** (see the Risk list in `04_IMMEDIATE_INCOME_TIERS.md`). Examples: medical or EMF-health claims in CSMHuman and DeepResearch, unverified engineering calculations in CSMMetal (bridge analysis), and fictional product catalogs presented as specifications.
6. **Hard-coded credentials exist in the repo.** Details are in section 9. Rotate them before anything is published.

## 10. Android developer verification and signing (affects every app sale)

- Google's Android Developer Verification is already in force from **Sept 30, 2026** in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand, for installs from participating stores including GetApps and OPPO App Market, which are Aptoide Connect partners. Global rollout is planned for 2027. See `02_APK_APTOIDE_PLAYBOOK.md`.
- All release APKs in the repo are signed with debug keys. Google will not let an unregistered package update on certified devices in enforced regions.
