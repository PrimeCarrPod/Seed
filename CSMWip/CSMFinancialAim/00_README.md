## The one-paragraph answer

Start with services you can sell this week (T0: Windows/Mac/M365 support), publish one finished low-risk digital product (T1: the 1920s Radio Design PDF) and an APK build-and-sign service, then approve the apps through the correct store process (T2: Aptoide Connect plus Google developer verification), and start the shielding line by reselling supplier-certified products with your own measured test data (T3). Use the first cash to pay for the next tier. The repo's B2B reports (T4: Chile minerals briefing, model-bill briefs) become sellable as soon as one T1 product proves you can finish and sell something.

## What the evaluation found (short version)

- 460 projects were indexed in V7; every category was reviewed against its actual files.
- **The repo's bottleneck is verification and packaging, not production.** Thousands of finished-looking files exist, but almost none carry test data, CAD, or customer evidence.
- All dollar figures inside the repo are unverified projections. Treat them as planning numbers only.
- All release APKs are debug-signed. No privacy policies, store listings, or screenshots exist. These are the real blockers to app income, not the stores.
- The strongest near-term assets are: DeepResearch/1920s-Radio-Design (maturity 4), the app trio Bounce / LandOLil / TGE E4, `CSMScripts/android-toolkit`, the CSMMinesMagnets 10-document briefing, 03_Legal_Enactment's completed Acts, and the desktop-support skill set itself.
- High-risk content to keep out of sales: EMF-health claims, medical claims, GIC/EMP/Carrington protection claims, unreviewed engineering calculations, and material presented as fact that is actually a speculative framework.
