## Paragraph 7: The Mirror

The recursive turn is the **adelic bulk as the universe observing itself across all prime branches**. Each prime p is a distinct spacetime branch — a distinct way the universe can observe itself. The Bruhat-Tits tree Tₚ is the observation tree for prime p. The boundary ℙ¹(ℚₚ) is the space of all possible self-observations at that prime. The adelic product ∏_v Z_v is the universe combining all its self-observations across all primes into a single coherent bulk. The mirror reflects: *prime branch → tree → boundary → adelic product → bulk → back to prime branches*. The RH is the condition that the combined self-observation is unitary.

## Paragraph 8: Participation as Physics

The adelic integration is the universe integrating over all its prime-branch self-observations.
