## DOC-07: Chilean Regulatory Navigation

### Chapter Outline (9 chapters target)

1. **Mining Code (Código de Minería) — Concessions & Permits**
   - Concession types: Explotación, Exploración, Pedimento
   - Registration: Sernageomin, Conservador de Bienes Raíces
   - Surface rights vs. mineral rights separation
   - Aegis-Forge advantage: No surface disturbance = simplified concessions

2. **Environmental Law 19,300 — SEIA/RCA/EIA/DIA**
   - Project categories: EIA (Art 10) vs DIA (Art 11)
   - RCA (Resolución de Calificación Ambiental) process
   - Community participation: Art 69-71 (observaciones ciudadanas)
   - Aegis-Forge zero-water = DIA category (not EIA) = 6-12 month permit

3. **Indigenous Law 19,253 — Consulta & Territorial Rights**
   - Consulta indígena: Pre-legislative, project-level, FPIC+
   - Territorial rights: Tierras indígenas, áreas de desarrollo
   - Benefit agreement requirements: Law 19,253 Art 13, 14
   - Tardigradia FPIC+: CVO binding veto + FBC equity = exceeds legal minimum

4. **Water Code (Código de Aguas) — Zero-Water Advantage**
   - Water rights: Derechos de aprovechamiento (DAA)
   - Aegis-Forge: Zero aqueous discharge = no water rights needed
   - Atacama aquifer protection: Law 19,300 Art 11 bis
   - CRMA DNSH water criterion: Zero-water = automatic compliance

5. **Royalty Regime Law 21,210 — Ad Valorem vs Operating Margin**
   - Ad valorem: 1-4% on copper, 1-2% on lithium, REE TBD
   - Operating margin: Progressive rate based on margin
   - Municipal distribution: 25% to mining communes
   - Tardigradia modeling: REE ad valorem 1% + operating margin 5-14%

6. **Sernageomin — Concession Registry, Safety, Closure**
   - Concession registry: Catastro minero, pubblicaciones
   - Safety regulations: DS 132, DS 248, DS 594
   - Mine closure: Law 20,551, financial guarantees
   - Aegis-Forge: No tailings = simplified closure plan

7. **Permitting Timeline — Standard vs CRMA Fast-Track**
   - Standard: 5-7 years (EIA + Consulta + Sectoral permits)
   - CRMA Strategic Project: 24 months max (Art 11 CRMA)
   - MinMin MOU: SEA priority review for strategic projects
   - Tardigradia path: RCA fast-track via CRMA + MinMin MOU

8. **Cross-Reference Matrix — DOC-04 Triggers & DOC-05 Capital**
   - Trigger 1: Geological certainty → Concession registration
   - Trigger 2: RCA approved → Construction financing release
   - Trigger 3: Consulta completed → Community trusts funded
   - Trigger 4: Aegis-Forge licensed → Technology transfer begins
   - Trigger 5: CRMA designated → EIB financing committed
   - Trigger 6: Offtake signed → Project finance close
   - Trigger 7: Production → Revenue share to FBC/CDC

9. **Traceability Matrix — Regulatory Claims to Sources**

## DOC-08: Port & Logistics Corridors

### Chapter Outline (9 chapters target)

1. **Chilean Port Infrastructure — Magnetic Mineral Capability**
   - Antofagasta: Bulk terminal, FCAB rail connection, 5M ton/yr capacity
   - Mejillones: Industrial port, LNG, copper concentrate, expanding
   - San Antonio: Container terminal #1, reefer capacity, Panamax
   - Coronel: Forestry/bulk, south-central access, rail link
   - Punta Arenas: Austral logistics, Magallanes mining, Antarctic gateway

2. **Rail Corridors — Mine to Port Connectivity**
   - FCAB (Ferrocarril Antofagasta-Bolivia): 900km, 15M ton/yr, privatized
   - Ferronor: 800km north-south, connects to FCAB, state-owned
   - EFE (Empresa Ferrocarriles del Estado): Central/south network
   - Aegis-Forge cluster rail spur: New build vs. existing siding

3. **Magnet Shipping — IMDG Class 9 & Specialized Handling**
   - NdFeB/SmCo: IMDG Class 9 (miscellaneous dangerous goods)
   - Packaging: UN-certified, temperature-controlled, humidity-controlled
   - Fe16N2: Non-hazardous but oxidation-sensitive, nitrogen blanket
   - Documentation: MSDS, IMDG declaration, CPTPP digital certificates

4. **Customs Procedures — CPTPP Paperless & Blockchain**
   - Paperless customs: CPTPP Art 14 (Digital Trade), Chile Aduanas SIC
   - Blockchain certificates of origin: CPTPP Annex 3-D + digital trade
   - Single Window (VUCE): Integrated permit submission
   - 48-hour clearance target vs. 2-week standard

5. **Logistics Cost Model — Ocean Freight to US/EU/Japan**
   - Antofagasta → Houston: 14 days, $45/ton (bulk), $120/ton (container)
   - Antofagasta → Rotterdam: 21 days, $55/ton (bulk), $150/ton (container)
   - Mejillones → Yokohama: 18 days, $50/ton (bulk), $130/ton (container)
   - Insurance: 0.5-1.5% CIF, war risk surcharge for Magallanes

6. **Supply Chain Resilience — Alternative Routes & Redundancy**
   - Primary: Antofagasta/Mejillones (FCAB rail) → US Gulf/EU/Japan
   - Secondary: San Antonio (EFE rail) → US West Coast/Asia
   - Tertiary: Coronel/Punta Arenas → Austral/Atlantic routes
   - Disruption scenarios: Port strike, rail failure, tsunami, pandemic

7. **CSM Delivery Requirements — Magnet-to-Motor Integration**
   - CSMFAB motor plants: US (Texas), EU (Germany), Japan (Aichi)
   - Just-in-time delivery: 2-week buffer, consignment inventory
   - Quality: MIL-STD-1540 test certificates with each shipment
   - Traceability: Blockchain from mine → Aegis-Forge → Magnet Chile → Port → CSM

8. **Port Community Integration — Local Benefit & Workforce**
   - Antofagasta: 40% local hiring (Codelco model), supplier development
   - Mejillones: Industrial cluster, desalination sharing, workforce training
   - San Antonio: Logistics hub, community infrastructure fund
   - FBC/CDC port trusts: 5% port revenue to community trusts
