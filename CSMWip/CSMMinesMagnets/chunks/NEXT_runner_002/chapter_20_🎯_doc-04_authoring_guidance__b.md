## 🎯 DOC-04 AUTHORING GUIDANCE — Brokerage Protocol

### Scope
Legal and operational framework for transitioning from CSM (facilitator/broker) to Tardigradia (owner/operator) of Chilean magnetic mineral assets. Defines trigger conditions, valuation methodology, execution mechanics, and post-transition governance.

### Key Sections (9 Chapters Target)

1. **Brokerage Mandate & Legal Basis** — CSM facilitator authority, Chilean brokerage law, mandate scope, termination conditions
2. **Facilitator Role Definition & Scope** — What CSM does (deal origination, due diligence, community engagement, government liaison) vs. what Tardigradia does (capital deployment, operations, processing, manufacturing)
3. **Owner Transition Trigger Conditions** — Milestone-based triggers: RCA approved, concessions secured, community agreements signed, financing committed, Aegis-Forge pilot operational
4. **Valuation Methodology for Transition** — DCF of magnetic mineral reserves, Aegis-Forge processing NPV, magnet manufacturing margin, strategic option value (DFARS 2027, Fe₁₆N₂)
5. **Transition Execution Mechanics** — Share purchase agreement, asset contribution, earnout structure, key person provisions, non-compete
6. **Post-Transition Governance** — Tardigradia board composition, CSM ongoing role (royalty, advisory), community trust integration, state observer rights
7. **Dispute Resolution & Escrow** — ICC arbitration, Chilean court enforcement, escrow for earnouts, community veto protection
8. **Cross-Border Enforcement (Chile/US)** — Delaware/Cayman holdco, Chilean SpA opcos, New York law SHA, Chilean law asset agreements, treaty arbitration
9. **Traceability Matrix** — Every clause traced to Chilean law, CSM mandate, DOC-01/02/03, Aegis-Forge specs

### Required Data Points (Per Chapter)

- Trigger definitions: Binary milestones with objective verification sources
