## 6.2 Terbium (Tb) — Heavy REE for $H_{cJ}$ at High Temp

| Specification | **Grade 1** | **Grade 2** | **Test Method** |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Tb/TREO** | ≥ 99.9% | ≥ 99.5% | ICP-MS |
| **Fe, O, C** | Same as Dy | Same as Dy | Same as Dy |

**Sourcing**:
| Source | **Country** | **Qualifying?** | **Capacity** |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Lynas Mt Weld** | Australia | ✅ Yes | 50 t/yr Tb₂O₃ |
| **MP Materials** | USA | ✅ Yes | 20 t/yr (2027+) |
| **China** | China | ❌ No | 95% global |

**Price**: $1,200-1,500/kg (oxide) — used sparingly (0.5-1.5% in N52EH/N48UH).

**Tb vs Dy for High-Temp $H_{cJ}$**:
$$\frac{\partial H_{cJ}}{\partial C_{Tb}} \approx 2\times \frac{\partial H_{cJ}}{\partial C_{Dy}} \quad \text{at } T > 150°C$$
But Tb cost ~4× Dy → **Dy preferred unless extreme temp required**.
