## 8.1 Magnetic Test Methods (Per Lot)

| Test | **Standard** | **Method** | **Parameter** | **Frequency** | **Equipment** |
|------|--------------|------------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Magnetic Moment** | MIL-STD-1540 3.3.1 | Helmholtz coil / Fluxmeter | Dipole moment (A·m²) | 100% | Fluxmeter (0.1% accuracy) |
| **Flux Density (Surface)** | MIL-STD-1540 3.3.2 | Hall probe mapping | $B_r$ at defined points | 100% | 3-axis Hall (0.5% accuracy) |
| **Coercivity ($H_{cJ}$)** | MIL-STD-1540 3.3.3 | Pulse magnetometer | $H_{cJ}$ (kA/m) | 100% | Pulse magnetometer (10 MA/m) |
| **Curie Temperature** | MIL-STD-1540 3.3.4 | Thermomagnetic analysis | $T_c$ (°C) | 1 per lot | Thermomagnetic balance |
| **Reversible Temp Coeff.** | MIL-STD-1540 3.3.5 | -55°C to +150°C | $\alpha_{Br}$, $\alpha_{HcJ}$ | 1 per lot | Thermomagnetic + pulse |
| **Irreversible Loss** | MIL-STD-1540 3.3.6 | Post-thermal cycle | $\Delta B_r/B_r$ | 1 per lot | Fluxmeter before/after |
| **Mechanical Strength** | MIL-STD-1540 3.4.1 | 4-point bend / compression | $\sigma_{ult}$ (MPa) | 1 per lot | Universal test machine |
| **Thermal Shock** | MIL-STD-1540 3.4.2 | -55°C ↔ +150°C, 5 cycles | Visual + $B_r$ check | 1 per lot | Thermal chamber |
| **Vibration (Sine)** | MIL-STD-1540 3.5.1 | 5-2000 Hz, 20G | No degradation | 1 per lot | Electrodynamic shaker |
| **Vibration (Random)** | MIL-STD-1540 3.5.2 | 20-2000 Hz, 0.2G²/Hz | No degradation | 1 per lot | Electrodynamic shaker |
| **Shock (Half-Sine)** | MIL-STD-1540 3.5.3 | 500G, 1ms, 3 axes | No degradation | 1 per lot | Shock machine |
| **Thermal Vacuum** | MIL-STD-1540 3.6.1 | 10⁻⁶ Torr, -55°C/+125°C | Outgassing (TML/CVCM) | 1 per lot (space) | Thermal vacuum chamber |
| **Radiation** | MIL-STD-1540 3.6.2 | 100 krad (Si) | $\Delta B_r/B_r$ | 1 per lot (space) | Co-60 source |
| **Corrosion (Salt Fog)** | MIL-STD-1540 3.7.1 | ASTM B117, 96 hrs | No red rust, <5% $B_r$ loss | 1 per lot | Salt fog chamber |
| **Humidity** | MIL-STD-1540 3.7.2 | 85% RH, 85°C, 168 hrs | No degradation | 1 per lot | Humidity chamber |
