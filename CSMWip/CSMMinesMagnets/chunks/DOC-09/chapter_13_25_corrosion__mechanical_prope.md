| Property | **SmCo 1:5** | **SmCo 2:17** | **CSM Relevance** |
|----------|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Corrosion Resistance** | Excellent (inherent) | Excellent (inherent) | **No coating needed** — cost savings |
| **Fracture Toughness** | ~10 MPa√m | ~12 MPa√m | Brittle — handle per MIL-STD-1540 3.4.1 |
| **Hardness (Vickers)** | 500-600 HV | 550-650 HV | Diamond tooling required |
| **Thermal Conductivity** | 12 W/m·K | 10 W/m·K | Lower than NdFeB — thermal design |
| **Specific Heat** | 350 J/kg·K | 350 J/kg·K | — |
