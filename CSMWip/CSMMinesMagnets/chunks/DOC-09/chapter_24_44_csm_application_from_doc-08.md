## 4.4 CSM Application (from DOC-08 Ch. 7)

| Motor Component | **Alloy** | **Function** | **Benefit vs. Si-Fe** |
|-----------------|-----------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Stator Teeth** | Hiperco 50 (0.2mm lam) | Flux concentration | **+35% flux density** → smaller motor |
| **Rotor Core** | Hiperco 50 (0.2mm lam) | Flux path | Higher torque density |
| **Pole Pieces** | Vacodil 2V (solid) | Field shaping | High $\mu$, low $H_c$ |
| **Magnetic Concentrators** | Fe₃₅Co₃₅Ni₃₀ | Flux guidance | Ultra-high $\mu$ |
| **Shielding** | Fe-Co (solid) | Field containment | Structural + magnetic |

**Chilean Source (DOC-01)**: Biobío Region Fe-Co hydrothermal deposits → Aegis-Forge ordering process → Hiperco 50 equivalent. Annual capacity: 10,000 tons.
