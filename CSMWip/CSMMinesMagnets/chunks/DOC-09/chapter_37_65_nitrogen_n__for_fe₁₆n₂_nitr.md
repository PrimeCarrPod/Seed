## 6.5 Nitrogen (N) — For Fe₁₆N₂ Nitridation

| Specification | **Grade** | **Source** |
|---------------|-----------|------------|
| **N₂ Purity** | ≥ 99.999% (5N) | Air separation (on-site) |
| **O₂** | < 5 ppm | On-site PSA |
| **H₂O** | < 1 ppm | Molecular sieve |
| **Ar** | < 10 ppm | Cryogenic distillation |

**Production**: **On-site air separation unit (ASU)** at Aegis-Forge Chile — zero import dependency, qualifying country by processing location.

**ASU Sizing for Fe₁₆N₂ Pilot (2,000 t/yr)**:
$$\text{N}_2 \text{ consumption} = 2,000 \text{ t Fe} \times 0.111 \times \frac{28}{55.85} \times \frac{1}{0.85} \approx 1,300 \text{ t N}_2/\text{yr}$$
$$\text{ASU Capacity} = \frac{1,300}{8,760 \times 0.9} \approx 165 \text{ kg/hr} \quad \text{(small modular ASU)}$$
