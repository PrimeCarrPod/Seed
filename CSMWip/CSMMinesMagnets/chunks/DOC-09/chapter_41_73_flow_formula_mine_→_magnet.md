## 7.3 Flow Formula (Mine → Magnet)

$$\text{Magnet Output} = \text{Ore Mined} \times \text{Head Grade} \times \text{Mining Recovery} \times \text{Separation Efficiency} \times \text{Metallization Yield} \times \text{Sinter Yield}$$

**Example (Antofagasta → N48SH)**:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Magnet} &= 10^7 \text{ t ore} \times 0.12 \times 0.90 \times 0.94 \times 0.95 \times 0.92 \\
&= 9,400 \text{ t NdFeB/yr} \quad \text{(at 5,000 t REO feed)}
\end{aligned}$$
