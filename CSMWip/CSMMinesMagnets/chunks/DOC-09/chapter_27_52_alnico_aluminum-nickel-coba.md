## 5.2 AlNiCo (Aluminum-Nickel-Cobalt) — Cast/Sintered

| Property | **AlNiCo 5** | **AlNiCo 8** | **AlNiCo 9** | **Status** |
|----------|--------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| **$(BH)_{max}$ (MGOe)** | 5.5 | 8.0 | **10.5** | Mature |
| **$B_r$ (T)** | 1.25 | 0.85 | 1.05 | High $B_r$ |
| **$H_{cJ}$ (kA/m)** | 48 | 120 | 160 | **Low $H_c$** |
| **$T_{max}$ (°C)** | 500 | 500 | 500 | **Excellent** |
| **$\alpha_{Br}$** | -0.02%/°C | -0.02%/°C | -0.02%/°C | Near zero |
| **Shape** | Cast (complex shapes) | Cast | Cast | Near-net |
| **REE Content** | 0% | 0% | 0% | **DFARS ✅** |
| **Critical Material** | **Co (35-40%)** | **Co** | **Co** | Qualifying country |

**AlNiCo Magnetic Structure (Shape Anisotropy Dominated)**:
$$H_{cJ} \approx \frac{2K_{shape}}{M_s} = \frac{(N_\perp - N_\parallel) M_s}{M_s} = N_\perp - N_\parallel$$
Where $N_\perp, N_\parallel$ = demagnetizing factors. **Elongated shapes (high aspect ratio) essential** for usable $H_{cJ}$.

**CSM Role**: **Sensor magnets, temperature-stable references, aerospace** — NOT traction rotors (low $H_c$).
