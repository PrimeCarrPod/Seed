## 5.4 MXene-Enhanced NdFeB — Heavy REE Reduction

| Innovation | **CSMMagTech2026 V2.0** | **Impact** |
|------------|-------------------------|------------|
| **MXene (Ti₃C₂Tₓ) grain boundary diffusion** | +12% $H_{cJ}$ with 50% less Dy | **Dy reduction** |
| **Process** | MXene coating → vacuum diffusion at 900°C | Compatible with sintering |
| **Result** | N48SH → N50SH equivalent with half Dy | Cost + DFARS win |
| **Status** | Lab scale (2024), pilot 2026 | Watch list |

**MXene Diffusion Kinetics**:
$$\frac{\partial C}{\partial t} = D \nabla^2 C \quad D = D_0 \exp\left(-\frac{Q}{RT}\right)$$
For Dy in NdFeB: $D_0 \approx 10^{-4} \text{ m}^2/\text{s}$, $Q \approx 250 \text{ kJ/mol}$.
MXene accelerates: $D_{MXene} \approx 5\times D_{bulk}$ at 900°C.
