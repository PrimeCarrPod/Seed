## 6.4 Gallium (Ga) — Emerging for NdFeB Grain Boundary

| Specification | **Grade** | **Test Method** |
|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Ga** | ≥ 99.99% (4N) | ICP-MS |
| **Fe, Cu, Zn** | < 10 ppm each | ICP-MS |

**Sourcing**: China dominates (80%). **Qualifying**: Canada (Teck), Germany (recycled). **CSM Strategy**: Recycled Ga from semiconductor scrap (EU/US sources).

**Ga Diffusion Efficiency**:
$$\eta_{Ga} \approx 0.5 \times \eta_{Dy} \quad \text{but cost} \approx 0.1 \times \text{Dy}$$
**Promising for cost reduction** — watch 2026-2027 pilot results.
