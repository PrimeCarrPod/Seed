## 8.7 Community Benefit Agreement Template

Standardized template for each port city (customized per community).

```
COMMUNITY BENEFIT AGREEMENT
===========================
Between: Aegis-Forge Chile SpA ("Company")
And:     [Community Organization] ("Community")
For:     [Port City] Aegis-Forge Cluster Deployment
Date:    2026-XX-XX
Term:    20 years (renewable 10-year periods)

1. GOVERNANCE
   - FBC Board: 5 Community, 2 Company, 1 Municipal, 1 Indigenous
   - CDC Board: 5 Community, 2 Company, 1 Municipal, 1 Worker
   - Quorum: 60% including ≥2 Community + ≥1 Indigenous
   - Decisions: Consensus sought; 70% supermajority if consensus fails

2. FINANCIAL FLOWS
   - FBC: 15-20% of Cluster FCF (audited annually)
   - CDC: 15-20% of Cluster FCF (audited annually)
   - Payment: Quarterly, within 30 days of FCF certification
   - Audit: Independent (Big 4), community right to co-audit

3. EMPLOYMENT
   - Local Hiring: ≥60% from host commune
   - Indigenous Reserved: Per §8.5/8.6 (10-15%)
   - Apprenticeships: 50/yr per cluster (funded by Company)
   - Wage Floor: 1.3x regional minimum (indexed to CPI)

4. ENVIRONMENTAL
   - ZLD: Zero aqueous discharge (contractual guarantee)
   - Air Quality: PM10/PM2.5 < 50% national standard at community boundary
   - Noise: < 55 dBA day / 45 dBA night at community boundary
   - Monitoring: Community sentinel program (§8.8), real-time public data

5. CULTURAL (Indigenous-specific)
   - Cultural Site Protection: Chance-find protocol, no-go zones
   - Language/Culture Fund: 2% gross revenue (Indigenous communities)
   - Access Rights: Guaranteed traditional marine/coastal access
   - Representation: Indigenous seats on FBC/CDC boards

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   - Step 1: Community-Company dialogue (30 days)
   - Step 2: FBC/CDC board mediation (30 days)
   - Step 3: Independent mediator (Chilean Arbitration Center)
   - Step 4: Binding arbitration (Spanish/English, Santiago seat)

7. TRANSPARENCY
   - Blockchain Ledger: All flows, decisions, monitoring public
   - Quarterly Reports: Financial, employment, environmental, cultural
   - Annual Assembly: Community review, Company accountability

8. TERM & RENEWAL
   - Initial: 20 years from commercial operation
   - Renewal: 10-year periods, Community consent required
   - Termination: Material breach, 180-day cure, Community vote (60%)

Signed: _________________________  _________________________
        (Aegis-Forge CEO)          (Community Representative)
Date:   2026-XX-XX
```
