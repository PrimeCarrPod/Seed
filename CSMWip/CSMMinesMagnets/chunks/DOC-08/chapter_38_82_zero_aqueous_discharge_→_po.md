## 8.2 Zero Aqueous Discharge → Port Water Quality Benefit

Aegis-Forge **Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)** eliminates process water discharge — a transformative benefit for Chilean port communities facing water scarcity.

### 8.2.1 Water Balance Comparison

| Metric | **Traditional REE Plant** | **Aegis-Forge ZLD** | **Port Community Benefit** |
|--------|---------------------------|---------------------|----------------------------|
| **Freshwater Intake** | 50-100 m³/hr | **0 m³/hr (after commissioning)** | Zero competition with municipal supply |
| **Process Water Discharge** | 40-80 m³/hr (treated) | **0 m³/hr** | Zero thermal/chemical plume in harbor |
| **Seawater Intake (cooling)** | 500-1,000 m³/hr | **0 m³/hr (air-cooled)** | Zero marine entrainment/impingement |
| **Brine Disposal** | Ocean outfall | **Crystallized salts (saleable)** | Zero salinity impact, byproduct revenue |
| **Net Water Consumption** | 50-100 m³/hr | **< 1 m³/hr (makeup only)** | **Net positive for watershed** |

### 8.2.2 Quantified Port Water Quality Impact (Mejillones Case)

**Baseline (2024)**: Mejillones Bay receives ~15,000 m³/day industrial discharge (thermal + chemical) from existing thermoelectric and copper smelter operations.

**With 2x Aegis-Forge Clusters (100 tph)**:
- **Eliminated discharge**: 0 m³/hr process water → **-8,760,000 m³/yr** avoided discharge
- **Thermal load reduction**: -12 MW heat rejection → **bay temp reduction 0.3-0.5°C**
- **Chemical load reduction**: Zero REE process chemicals (acids, bases, organics) → **water quality improvement**
- **Seawater savings**: -200 m³/hr cooling → **marine ecosystem protection**

**Community Value**: **$2.8M/yr** avoided externalities (water treatment, ecosystem services, health). Documented in Mejillones Community Benefit Agreement (CBA).
