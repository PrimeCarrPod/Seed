## 8.1 Aegis-Forge Modular Port Services Deployment

Aegis-Forge separation plants are **containerized, modular, and relocatable** — designed for deployment at port sites to create integrated "mine-to-ship" value chains.

### 8.1.1 Module Specifications

| Module | Footprint | Capacity | Power | Water | Port Integration |
|--------|-----------|----------|-------|-------|------------------|
| **Feed Prep** | 2x 40ft HC | 50 tph | 150 kW | 5 m³/hr (recycled) | Rail siding discharge |
| **Thermal Cascade (17-stage)** | 4x 40ft HC | 50 tph | 850 kW | **0 m³/hr (ZLD)** | Concentrate feed |
| **REE Separation** | 3x 40ft HC | 5 tph REO | 400 kW | 2 m³/hr (recycled) | Product load-out |
| **Metal/Alloy** | 2x 40ft HC | 2 tph alloy | 600 kW | 1 m³/hr (recycled) | Ingot casting |
| **Magnet Mfg** | 3x 40ft HC | 1 tph magnets | 1,200 kW | 1 m³/hr (recycled) | Finished goods |
| **Power/Control** | 1x 40ft HC | 3 MW total | — | — | Microgrid + battery |
| **TOTAL** | **15x 40ft HC** | **50 tph feed** | **3.2 MW** | **9 m³/hr (100% recycled)** | **Port berth adjacent** |

### 8.1.2 Deployment at Chilean Ports

| Port | Aegis-Forge Cluster | Capacity | Status | Community Impact |
|------|---------------------|----------|--------|------------------|
| **Mejillones (TCMN)** | 2 clusters (100 tph) | 10,000 t REO/yr | **Phase 1: 2027** | 350 direct jobs, $12M/yr local spend |
| **Antofagasta (Portezuelo)** | 1 cluster (50 tph) | 5,000 t REO/yr | Phase 2: 2029 | 180 direct jobs, $6M/yr local spend |
| **San Antonio (PESA)** | 1 cluster (50 tph) | 5,000 t REO/yr | Phase 3: 2031 | 180 direct jobs, $6M/yr local spend |
| **Punta Arenas** | 1 cluster (25 tph) | 2,500 t REO/yr | Phase 4: 2033 | 100 direct jobs, $4M/yr local spend |

**Modular Advantage**: Clusters deploy in **6 months** (vs. 3-4 years traditional). Relocatable if port dynamics change. **Zero permanent footprint** — lease port land, not buy.
