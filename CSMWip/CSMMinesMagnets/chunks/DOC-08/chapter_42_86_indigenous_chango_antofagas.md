## 8.6 Indigenous: Chango (Antofagasta/Mejillones)

### 8.6.1 Territorial Context

**Chango** people are the historic coastal inhabitants of the **Atacama coast** (Región de Antofagasta, northern Chile). Population: ~4,000. Recognized under Law 19,253 (2020 amendment).

**Territorial Rights**:
- **Coastal Zone**: Caletas (coves) from Tocopilla to Taltal — traditional fishing, gathering, ceremony
- **Marine Resources**: Customary rights to shellfish, seaweed, finfish
- **Cultural Sites**: Coastal geoglyphs, shell middens, ceremonial platforms

### 8.6.2 Antofagasta/Mejillones Engagement Protocol

| Step | Action | Timeline | Indigenous Lead | Aegis-Forge Role |
|------|--------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **1. Early Notification** | Formal letter to Chango organizations (Caleta Coloso, Caleta Constitución) | **Pre-feasibility** (2026) | CONADI Antofagasta | Provide project scope |
| **2. Consulta Indígena** | FPIC+ process for port-adjacent development | 6-12 months | Chango orgs + CONADI | Fund process, technical data |
| **3. Caleta Protection** | Legal protection for traditional caletas | Parallel | Chango legal team | Support designation |
| **4. Benefit Agreement** | Negotiated agreement | Post-consulta | Chango orgs + Aegis-Forge | Negotiate in good faith |
| **5. Ongoing Governance** | Chango reps on FBC/CDC, coastal monitoring | Operational | Chango orgs | Fund positions |

### 8.6.3 Specific Commitments (Antofagasta/Mejillones)

- **FBC/CDC Board Seats**: 1 Chango representative per port city (2 of 9 seats each)
- **Caleta Infrastructure**: $500k/yr per caleta for dock, cold storage, solar power, water
- **Marine Resource Access**: Zero displacement from traditional fishing grounds
- **Employment**: 10% of cluster jobs reserved for Chango applicants
- **Cultural Heritage**: Funding for Chango language revitalization, oral history, youth camps
- **Environmental Sentinel**: Chango fishers as paid monitors (see §8.8)
