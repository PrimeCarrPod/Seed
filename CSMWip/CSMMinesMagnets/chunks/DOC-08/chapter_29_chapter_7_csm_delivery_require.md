# Chapter 7: CSM Delivery Requirements — Magnet-to-Motor Integration

This chapter defines the precise delivery specifications, quality assurance chain, and contractual terms for magnetic materials flowing from Chilean ports to Carrington Storm Motors (CSM) factories. These requirements flow down from DOC-06 (CSM Magnetic Mineral Integration Strategy) and are enforced through the Tardigradia Holding Company venture structure (DOC-05).
