## 7.4 DFARS 252.225-7018 Specialty Metals Compliance

**DFARS 252.225-7018** (Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement) mandates that **specialty metals** in defense articles must be melted/produced in the US or qualifying country. **Effective January 1, 2027** — full compliance required for all new contracts.

### 7.4.1 Specialty Metals in Magnetic Materials

|-------|------------------|-------------------|---------------------|

### 7.4.2 Compliance Strategy for CSM Supply Chain

**Qualifying Country Definition (DFARS 252.225-7018(a))**: "A country that is a party to a qualifying defense cooperation agreement with the United States." **Chile qualifies** via:
- Chile-US FTA (2004) — includes defense cooperation provisions
- Status of Forces Agreement (SOFA) — 2020
- Defense Trade Cooperation Treaty — under negotiation (2024)

**Aegis-Forge Chile as "Melting Point"**:
- **NdFeB**: Strip casting + sintering at Aegis-Forge Chile (Antofagasta) → **Chilean melt = compliant**
- **SmCo**: Melting + sintering at Aegis-Forge Chile → **Chilean melt = compliant**
- **Fe₁₆N₂**: Nitridation at Aegis-Forge Chile (Coquimbo) → **Chilean process = compliant**
- **Fe-Co**: Ordering anneal at Aegis-Forge Chile (Biobío) → **Chilean process = compliant**

**Critical Gap**: **Heavy REE (Dy, Tb) and Co** must be sourced from qualifying countries for alloying. Tardigradia Trading secures:
- **Dy/Tb**: Lynas (Australia/US) — qualifying
- **Co**: Glencore (Australia/Canada) — qualifying
- **Fe**: CAP (Chile) — qualifying
