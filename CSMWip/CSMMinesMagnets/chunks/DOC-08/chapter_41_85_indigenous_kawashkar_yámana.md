## 8.5 Indigenous: Kawashkar/Yámana (Punta Arenas)

### 8.5.1 Territorial Context

**Kawashkar (Alacaluf)** and **Yámana (Yaghan)** are the Indigenous peoples of the **Magallanes Region** (Chilean Patagonia, Tierra del Fuego, Cape Horn archipelago). Total population: ~2,500 (Kawashkar) + ~1,600 (Yámana).

**Territorial Rights (Law 19,253 Art. 13)**:
- **Maritime Territory**: Customary use of channels, fjords, islands for navigation, fishing, gathering
- **Coastal Territory**: Seasonal camps, shellfish harvesting, cultural sites
- **Consulta Indígena Required**: For any project affecting territorial use (port expansion, Aegis-Forge cluster)

### 8.5.2 Punta Arenas Engagement Protocol

| Step | Action | Timeline | Indigenous Lead | Aegis-Forge Role |
|------|--------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **1. Early Notification** | Formal letter to Kawashkar/Yámana organizations | **Pre-feasibility** (2026) | CONADI Regional | Provide project scope |
| **2. Consulta Indígena** | Free, Prior, Informed Consent (FPIC+) process | 6-12 months | Indigenous orgs + CONADI | Fund process, provide technical data |
| **3. Impact Assessment** | Cultural, environmental, economic | Parallel with SEA | Indigenous technical team | Fund independent assessment |
| **4. Benefit Agreement** | Negotiated agreement (FBC/CDC seats, revenue, jobs, culture) | Post-consulta | Indigenous orgs + Aegis-Forge | Negotiate in good faith |
| **5. Ongoing Governance** | Indigenous reps on FBC/CDC boards, cultural monitoring | Operational | Indigenous orgs | Fund positions, respect autonomy |

### 8.5.3 Specific Commitments (Punta Arenas)

- **FBC/CDC Board Seats**: 1 Kawashkar + 1 Yámana representative (2 of 9 seats)
- **Revenue Share**: 2% of gross revenue → Indigenous cultural fund (language, heritage, youth)
- **Employment**: 15% of cluster jobs reserved for Indigenous applicants (training provided)
- **Cultural Protection**: No disturbance of known cultural sites; chance-find protocol
- **Marine Access**: Guaranteed continued access to customary fishing/gathering areas
- **Environmental Monitoring**: Indigenous sentinel program (see §8.8) co-managed
