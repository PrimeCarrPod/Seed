## 8.3 FBC/CDC Trusts at Port Cities (from DOC-03)

**Foundation Benefit Corporation (FBC)** and **Community Development Corporation (CDC)** structures (DOC-03) are instantiated at each port city hosting Aegis-Forge clusters.

### 8.3.1 Trust Structure per Port City

| Port City | FBC Share | CDC Share | Governance | Annual Flow (Est. 2028) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|-------------------------|
| **Antofagasta** | 15% FCF | 15% FCF | 5 community, 2 Aegis-Forge, 1 municipal, 1 Indigenous (Chango) | $4.2M |
| **Mejillones** | 20% FCF | 20% FCF | 5 community, 2 Aegis-Forge, 1 municipal, 1 CODELCO rep | $8.5M |
| **San Antonio** | 15% FCF | 15% FCF | 5 community, 2 Aegis-Forge, 1 municipal, 1 port worker rep | $4.2M |
| **Punta Arenas** | 20% FCF | 20% FCF | 5 community, 2 Aegis-Forge, 1 municipal, 1 Kawashkar/Yámana rep | $3.0M |

**FCF (Free Cash Flow) Definition**: Revenue - Operating Cost - CAPEX Reserve - Debt Service - Tax. **15-20% of FCF** flows to trusts — **not revenue, not profit — free cash flow**.

### 8.3.2 Trust Investment Mandates (Per DOC-03)

| Trust | Mandate | Prohibited | Measurable Outcomes |
|-------|---------|------------|---------------------|
| **FBC** | Community infrastructure, education, health, environment | Political, religious, private gain | Schools built, clinics funded, water systems, scholarships |
| **CDC** | Local enterprise development, workforce training, supplier diversity | Subsidizing competitors, real estate speculation | Jobs created, local procurement %, certification rates |

**Blockchain Transparency**: All trust flows recorded on Tardigradia ledger — community members can audit in real-time via public explorer.
