## 6. Post-Transition Governance

This chapter defines the governance architecture after transition completion, integrating CSM's ongoing facilitator role, community governance (DOC-03), state partnership, and Tardigradia's operational control.

### 6.1 Governance Architecture Overview

### 6.2 Tardigradia Chile SpA Board — Detailed Mandate

#### 6.2.1 Board Composition & Appointment

| Seat | Appointer | Qualifications | Term | Removal |
|------|-----------|----------------|------|---------|
| Chair | Tardigradia Global | ACS/CSM executive; 10+ yr mining/tech | 3 years | Tardigradia Global |
| Dir 1 (Finance) | Tardigradia Global | CFO/investment banking; capital markets | 3 years | Tardigradia Global |
| Dir 2 (Technology) | Tardigradia Global | CTO/VP Engineering; materials processing | 3 years | Tardigradia Global |
| Dir 3 (Community Elder) | FBC Holding Trustees | Community leader; indigenous/elder status | 2 years | Community assembly (2/3) |
| Dir 4 (Community Youth) | FBC Holding Trustees | Technical degree; <40 yrs; community resident | 2 years | Community assembly (2/3) |
| Dir 5 (Municipal) | Municipal Consortium | Rotating mayors (3 regions); 2-yr rotation | 2 years | Municipal council |
| Dir 6 (Independent) | Dir 3+4+5 consensus | Mining/energy/finance veteran; no conflicts | 3 years | 2/3 board vote |
| Observer (CORFO) | CORFO / MinMin | Government nominee; policy alignment | At pleasure | CORFO/MinMin |

#### 6.2.2 Voting Thresholds

| Decision Type | Threshold | Special Requirements |
|---------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Ordinary business | Simple majority (4/7) | Quorum: 5/7 |
| Capital expenditure > $50M | 2/3 majority (5/7) | Including Dir 6 |
| Community-impacting decisions | 2/3 majority (5/7) | **Including BOTH Dir 3 & Dir 4** |
| Water/land use/permits | 2/3 majority (5/7) | **Including Dir 5 (Municipal veto)** |
| Aegis-Forge technology changes | 2/3 majority (5/7) | Including Dir 2 |
| CEO appointment/removal | 2/3 majority (5/7) | Including Dir 6 |
| Amendment to SHA/Bylaws | 3/4 majority (6/7) | Including Dir 3, 4, 5, 6 |

#### 6.2.3 Board Information Rights

All directors receive:
- Monthly management reports (financial, operational, ESG)
- Quarterly board packages (10 business days before meeting)
- Real-time access to CVO data feeds (SCADA, environmental, production)
- Annual strategy session (off-site, 2 days)

Community directors (3, 4) additionally receive:
- Pre-meeting briefing from CDC Executive Directors (2 days before)
- Direct line to CVO Chair for urgent matters
- Annual community assembly report-back obligation

### 6.4 Community Governance Integration (Per DOC-03 Ch 7)

The post-transition governance **embeds** DOC-03 community structures:

| DOC-03 Structure | Post-Transition Status | Board Interface |
|------------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| FBC Holding Trust | Holds 15% Tardigradia Chile SpA | Appoints Dir 3 & 4 |
| FBC Norte/Centro/Sur | Hold 20% each opco | Appoint 1/5 opco directors |
| CDC Development Corps | Operational; funded 15% FCF | Report to Board ESG Committee |
| CVO Oversight Board | **Binding veto retained** | Monthly reports to Board; escalation to Dir 3,4,5 |

**Critical Principle:** Community veto rights (water, environment, indigenous sites) **cannot be diluted** by board action, shareholder vote, or acquisition. Enshrined in:
- Shareholders Agreement (Tardigradia Chile SpA)
- Opco Shareholders Agreements
- FBC Trust Deeds
- CVO Charter

### 6.5 State Partnership Governance (Per DOC-03 Ch 8)

| State Entity | Mechanism | Rights |
|--------------|-----------|--------|
| **CORFO** | 5-10% equity at holdco (Corfo Ventures) | Board observer; pro-rata participation rights |
| **Ministerio de Minería** | MOU "Chilean Magnet Industry Development" | Policy coordination; permitting support; international promotion |
| **ProChile** | Export promotion agreement | "Chilean Magnets" brand pavilions; co-funded marketing |
| **BancoEstado / Banco Central** | Green bond guarantee program | 4.5% coupon vs 6% market; 20-yr tenor |

**State Rights Protection:**
- CORFO equity: Anti-dilution (participates pro-rata in all raises)
- MOU: 10-year term; automatic renewal unless material breach
- Green bonds: Environmental bond priority in insolvency (Law 20,720 Art 134)

### 6.6 Opco Governance — Aegis-Forge Clusters

Each Aegis-Forge opco (Norte, Centro, Sur) has:

| Governance Element | Specification |
|--------------------|---------------|
| Board | 5 directors: 2 Tardigradia, 1 FBC Trust, 1 Independent, 1 Municipal (rotating) |
| Community Veto | Water/environment/indigenous — requires FBC + Municipal director consent |
| CVO Liaison | Regional CVO rep attends all board meetings (advisory) |
| Budget Approval | Annual budget >$10M requires Tardigradia Chile SpA Board approval |
| Key Metrics | Safety (TRIR), Environmental (zero exceedances), Community (CVO satisfaction), Production (kg REE oxide) |
