## 5. Transition Execution Mechanics

This chapter specifies the legal and operational mechanics for executing the facilitator-to-owner transition once all Chapter 3 triggers are verified.

### 5.1 Transition Structure — Share Purchase & Asset Contribution

The transition executes through a **dual-track structure** ensuring clean legal separation:

```
TRANSITION EXECUTION STRUCTURE

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TRACK A: SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT (SPA)                        │
│  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│  │ Seller: CSM Chile SpA (facilitator nominee holder)         │ │
│  │ Buyer: Tardigradia Holding Company (Delaware LP)           │ │
│  │ Target: 100% shares of Tardigradia Chile SpA               │ │
│  │ Consideration: $F_{upfront} (cash at closing)              │ │
│  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                              │
                              ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TRACK B: ASSET CONTRIBUTION & LICENSE ASSIGNMENT               │
│  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│  │ Contributor: Tardigradia Holding Company                   │ │
│  │ Recipient: Tardigradia Chile SpA (now wholly-owned)        │ │
│  │ Assets Contributed:                                         │ │
│  │   • Capital commitments (Series A + project finance)       │ │
│  │   • Aegis-Forge Chile license (assigned from CSM)          │ │
│  │   • Community agreements (assigned from CSM)               │ │
│  │   • Permits/RCA (transferred by operation of law)          │ │
│  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.2 Share Purchase Agreement — Key Terms

| Provision | Term | Rationale |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Purchase Price** | $F_{upfront} = 0.025 × V_{asset}$ (Chapter 4) | Formulaic, no negotiation |
| **Payment** | 100% cash at closing | Certainty for CSM |
| **Closing Condition** | Trigger Certification (Chapter 3) = PASS | Binary, objective |
| **Representations & Warranties** | Standard + mining-specific | Title, permits, communities, environmental |
| **Indemnification** | Survival 24 months; cap 15% purchase price | Balanced risk allocation |
| **Facilitator Royalty** | 1% gross revenue Years 1-10 (capped) | Separate agreement, survives SPA |
| **CSM Non-Compete** | 3 years, Chile magnetic minerals | Protects Tardigradia |
| **Key Person** | CSM transition lead stays 6 months | Knowledge transfer |
| **Governing Law** | Chile (asset SPA); New York (holdco SPA) | Asset location vs. holdco jurisdiction |

### 5.3 Asset Contribution Agreement — Key Terms

| Provision | Term |
|-----------|------|
| **Capital Contribution** | Series A proceeds + project finance draws per budget |
| **License Assignment** | CSM assigns Aegis-Forge exclusive Chile license to Tardigradia Chile SpA |
| **Community Agreement Assignment** | CSM assigns FBC trust deeds, CDC charters, community agreements |
| **Permit Transfer** | By operation of Chilean law (titular change notification to SEA/DGA) |
| **Employee Transfer** | CSM Chile SpA employees transfer to Tardigradia Chile SpA (Art 186 Labor Code) |
| **IP Assignment** | All facilitator work product (databases, models, permits) assigned |

### 5.4 Earnout Structure — Aligning Incentives Post-Transition

While the facilitator fee is primarily upfront + royalty, an **earnout** aligns CSM with Tardigradia's long-term success:

| Earnout Milestone | Payment | Timing | Condition |
|-------------------|---------|--------|-----------|
| **First Commercial REE Oxide Sale** | $2M | Within 30 days of sale | Aegis-Forge Norte producing 99.5% REE oxide |
| **First Domestic NdFeB Magnet Sale** | $3M | Within 30 days of sale | Magnet Chile SpA selling to third party |
| **First CSM Motor Integration** | $5M | Within 30 days of delivery | Motor Integration Chile SpA delivers to CSMFAB |
| **DFARS-Qualified Magnet Delivery** | $5M | Within 30 days of delivery | Magnet meets MIL-STD-1540; DoD purchase order |
| **Year 3 EBITDA > $100M** | $10M | Year 3 audit | Consolidated audited EBITDA |
| **Year 5 EBITDA > $180M** | $15M | Year 5 audit | Consolidated audited EBITDA |

**Total Earnout Potential: $40M** (in addition to $F_{upfront}$ + royalty)

**Earnout Cap:** Aggregate earnout ≤ 2.0 × $F_{upfront}$

**Earnout Security:** Tardigradia posts letter of credit (LOC) for 50% of max earnout at closing; balance funded from operating cash flow.
