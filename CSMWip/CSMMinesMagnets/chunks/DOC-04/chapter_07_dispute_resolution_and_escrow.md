## 7. Dispute Resolution & Escrow

This chapter establishes the dispute resolution framework and escrow mechanisms protecting all parties — especially community trusts — during and after transition.

### 7.1 Dispute Resolution Philosophy

**Principles:**
1. **Speed over perfection** — Mining operations cannot afford years of litigation
2. **Community parity** — Communities have equal access to justice as corporate parties
3. **Technical expertise** — Mining/environmental disputes decided by experts, not generalist judges
4. **Preserve operations** — Interim measures maintain production during disputes
5. **Chilean jurisdiction** — Asset disputes in Chile; holdco disputes in Delaware/NY

### 7.2 Escalation Ladder — Binding & Enforceable

| Level | Mechanism | Timeline | Binding | Cost Allocation |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **1. Direct Negotiation** | Party representatives (CEO/Community Dir) | 15 business days | No | Each bears own |
| **2. CVO Mediation** | CVO Chair facilitates (community disputes) | 20 business days | If settlement signed | Tardigradia 70% / Community 30% |
| **3. Expert Determination** | 3 experts (1 each party, 1 joint) | 45 business days | **Yes (technical)** | Tardigradia 60% / Other 40% |
| **4. ICC Arbitration** | 3 arbitrators; Spanish; Chile law (assets) / NY law (holdco) | 120 business days | **Yes (all)** | Tardigradia 65% / Other 35% |
| **5. Courts** | Chilean courts (asset disputes); Delaware/ NY (holdco) | Per court calendar | **Yes** | Loser pays (Chile); each bears own (US) |

**Community Legal Aid Fund:** $200k/year from FBC Holding Trust (DOC-03 Ch 7) covers community costs at Levels 2-5.

### 7.3 Dispute Categories & Applicable Forums

| Dispute Type | Primary Forum | Fallback | Key Considerations |
|--------------|---------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Community-impacting** (water, env, indigenous) | CVO Mediation → Expert Determination | ICC Arbitration (Chile seat) | Community veto = injunctive relief standard |
| **Facilitator fee / royalty** | Expert Determination (accounting) | ICC Arbitration | Formulaic; limited factual dispute |
| **Earnout milestones** | Expert Determination (technical) | ICC Arbitration | Independent engineer verification |
| **SHA / Governance** | ICC Arbitration (NY law, NY seat) | NY Courts | Holdco governance; Delaware LP agreement |

### 7.4 Expert Determination — Detailed Procedure

**Applicable to:** Technical disputes (production, environmental metrics, valuation, earnout milestones)

**Panel Composition:**
- Expert 1: Appointed by Claimant (mining engineer / geochemist / accountant)
- Expert 2: Appointed by Respondent (same discipline)
- Expert 3 (Chair): Jointly appointed; if no agreement in 10 days → ICC Court appoints

**Qualifications:**
- 15+ years relevant experience
- No conflicts (independence declaration)
- Spanish fluency (Chile-seated) / English (NY-seated)
- Recognized by: ICMM, SME, AusIMM, or Chilean Colegio de Ingenieros

**Procedure:**
1. Written submissions (30 pages max each) — 15 days
2. Site visit / data room access — 10 days
3. Hearing (virtual or Santiago/NY) — 5 days max
4. Written determination (reasoned) — 20 days
5. **Determination is final and binding** — no appeal on merits

**Cost Cap:** Total expert fees capped at $500k per determination (shared per cost allocation table)

### 7.5 ICC Arbitration — Detailed Procedure

**Rules:** ICC Arbitration Rules 2021
**Language:** Spanish (Chile-seated) / English (NY-seated)
**Seat:** Santiago (asset disputes) / New York (holdco disputes)
**Law:** Chilean law (assets) / New York law (holdco)
**Arbitrators:** 3 (1 each party, 1 Chair jointly/ICC)
**Confidentiality:** ICC Rules Art 22(3) + explicit agreement
**Interim Measures:** Available from arbitral tribunal + Chilean courts (Art 35)

**Community-Specific Protections:**
- Community may request consolidated arbitration (multiple communities vs. Tardigradia)
- Arbitrators must include 1 with Latin American mining/indigenous rights experience
- Hearings in Spanish; translation provided
- Community witnesses may testify remotely from their communities
- Award enforceable in Chile (NY Convention + Chilean Arbitration Law 19,971)

### 7.6 Escrow Mechanisms — Protecting All Parties

| Escrow | Purpose | Amount | Agent | Release Conditions |
|--------|---------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| **Facilitator Fee Escrow** | Secure $F_{upfront}$ at closing | 100% of $F_{upfront}$ | BancoEstado / BCI | Released to CSM at closing (simultaneous with share transfer) |
| **Earnout Security** | Secure earnout payments | 50% of max earnout ($20M) | BancoEstado / BCI | Released to CSM per milestone certification |
| **Environmental Bond** | RCA compliance (Law 19,300) | 5% CAPEX ($36.7M/cluster) | SEA-approved institution | Released per closure plan; community coupons paid |
| **Community Royalty Escrow** | Guarantee 2% gross revenue to FBC | 12 months estimated royalty | BancoEstado (trust dept) | Monthly release to FBC trusts; Tardigradia replenishes |
| **Indemnification Escrow** | SPA indemnity cap (15% purchase price) | 15% of $F_{upfront}$ | BancoEstado / BCI | Released at 24-month survival expiry (less claims) |
| **Water Rights Escrow** | Secure water for communities | Equivalent to 50 L/s × community count | DGA / BancoEstado | Released to community water systems per Water Fund (DOC-03 Ch 5) |

**Escrow Agent Selection:**
- Chilean bank (BancoEstado, BCI, Santander) for Chile-seated escrows
- US bank (JPM, BofA, Wells) for NY-seated escrows
- Dual-agent structure for cross-border escrows

**Escrow Agreement Terms:**
- Governed by Chilean law (Chile escrows) / NY law (US escrows)
- Agent liability: Gross negligence / willful misconduct only
- Investment: Conservative (Chilean Central Bank instruments / US Treasuries)
- Reporting: Monthly statements to all parties; quarterly to CVO

### 7.7 Interim Measures — Preserving Operations

**During any dispute (Levels 1-5):**

| Measure | Standard | Authority |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Status Quo Order** | Maintain current operations; no asset disposal | Arbitral tribunal / Chilean court |
| **Community Protection Order** | Halt activity if CVO veto triggered | CVO Chair (immediate, 72 hrs) → Court confirmation |
| **Royalty Payment Order** | Continue 1% facilitator + 2% community royalty | Arbitral tribunal (summary procedure) |
| **Environmental Compliance Order** | Maintain RCA compliance; no exceedances | SEA / Environmental Court |
| **Key Personnel Retention** | Prevent poaching of VP Ops, Community Dir | Arbitral tribunal |

**Expedited Timeline:** Interim measure hearings within 5 business days of application.
