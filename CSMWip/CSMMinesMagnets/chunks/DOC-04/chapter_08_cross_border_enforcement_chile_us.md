### 8.3 Enforcement Mechanisms — Chile ↔ US

#### 8.3.1 Chilean Judgments in US

| Mechanism | Basis | Timeline | Success Factors |
|-----------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Exequatur (Recognition)** | Chilean Code of Civil Procedure Art 246 + US state law (Uniform Foreign Money-Judgments Recognition Act) | 6-18 months | Final, conclusive, non-appealable Chilean judgment |
| **Comity** | Hilton v. Guyot (US Supreme Court) | Case-specific | Reciprocity; fair procedure; no public policy violation |
| **Full Faith & Credit** | NOT applicable (foreign judgment) | N/A | Only for US state-to-state |

**Key Requirement:** Chilean judgment must be **firme y ejecutoriada** (final and enforceable) — all appeals exhausted or waived.

#### 8.3.2 US Judgments in Chile

| Mechanism | Basis | Timeline | Success Factors |
|-----------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Exequatur** | Chilean Code of Civil Procedure Art 246 | 6-18 months | Final US judgment; proper service; no Chilean public policy violation |
| **Arbitral Awards (NY Convention)** | Law 19,971 (Chile Arbitration Law) + NY Convention | 3-6 months | **Preferred path** — ICC awards enforceable directly |

**Critical:** Use **ICC arbitration** (not court litigation) for cross-border disputes. Arbitral awards under NY Convention have streamlined enforcement in both Chile and US.

### 8.4 Share Pledge & Security Perfection — Cross-Border

**Tardigradia Holdco Level (Delaware LP):**
- LP interests pledged under **UCC Article 9** (New York)
- Control agreement with secured party (lender)
- Perfection by control (not filing) for LP interests

**Tardigradia Chile SpA Level (Chilean SpA):**
- Shares pledged under **Chilean Pledge Law (Ley 19,983)**
- Registration in **Conservador de Bienes Raíces** (Santiago)
- Perfection by registration (not possession)

**Opco Level (Chilean SpAs):**
- Same as Chile SpA — pledge registration in CBR
- **Cross-collateralization:** All opco shares cross-pledged to project finance lenders

**Community Trust Shares (FBC):**
- **NOT pledged** — anti-capture protection (DOC-03 Ch 7.5)
- Tag-along rights only; no forced sale
- Environmental bonds: First claim priority (Law 20,720 Art 134)

### 8.5 Foreign Investment Protection — Treaty Framework

| Treaty / Agreement | Protection | Dispute Forum | Status |
|--------------------|------------|---------------|--------|
| **Chile-US BIT** | Fair & equitable treatment; expropriation; MFN | ICSID / UNCITRAL | **Signed 2023; not yet in force** (Senate ratification pending) |
| **CPTPP** (Chile member) | Investment chapter (Art 9) — FET, expropriation, performance requirements | CPTPP Arbitration (ICSID/UNCITRAL) | **In force for Chile since 2023** |
| **Chile-EU Association Agreement** | Investment liberalization; dispute settlement | EU-Chile Joint Committee | In force 2003; modernized 2023 |
| **NY Convention** | Arbitral award enforcement | Domestic courts | Chile 1975; US 1970 |
| **ICSID Convention** | Investment dispute arbitration | ICSID | Chile 1991; US 1966 |

**CPTPP Investment Chapter (Art 9) — Key for Tardigradia:**
- Applies to "covered investments" (includes mining concessions, SpA shares)
- Fair & Equitable Treatment (FET) standard
- Protection against indirect expropriation
- Performance requirement prohibitions (local content, technology transfer)
- **Transparency:** Amicus curiae submissions allowed (community NGOs can participate)

### 8.7 Insolvency — Cross-Border Protection

| Scenario | Chile (Law 20,720) | US (Chapter 11 / 15) | Coordination |
|----------|-------------------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Tardigradia Holdco (LP) insolvency** | Not applicable | Chapter 11 (Delaware) | Foreign representative seeks recognition in Chile (Law 20,720 Art 170) |
| **Tardigradia Chile SpA insolvency** | Reorganization (Art 28) / Liquidation (Art 120) | Chapter 15 (ancillary) | Chilean court = main proceeding; US = ancillary |
| **Opco insolvency** | Same as Chile SpA | Chapter 15 if needed | Each opco separate; ring-fenced by project finance |
| **Community Trust Priority** | Environmental bonds first (Art 134) | Not applicable | **Chile law governs** — community protected |
| **FBC Trust Assets** | Trust assets segregated (Law 19,802) | Trust assets protected | **Not part of bankruptcy estate** |

**Key Insolvency Protections for Communities:**
1. Environmental bonds = first claim (Art 134, Law 20,720)
2. FBC trust assets = not bankruptcy estate (Law 19,802 Art 15)
3. CDC assets = ring-fenced for community use
4. Green bond coupons = paid to communities directly
