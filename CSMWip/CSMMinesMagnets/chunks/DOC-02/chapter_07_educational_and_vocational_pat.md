## 7. Educational and Vocational Pathways -- Technical Schools, Universities, Skills Gaps

Chile mining workforce pipeline: Technical high schools (liceos tecnico-profesionales) -> Technical training centers (CFT/IP) -> Universities. The system produces 15,000 mining-related graduates/year but has critical gaps: automation/digital skills, environmental technology, gender diversity, and advanced materials processing (which Aegis-Forge requires). This chapter maps the education landscape and Tardigradia training investment opportunity.

### 7.4 Community Education -- Beyond Workforce

- School partnerships: STEM kits for municipal schools (magnetism, geology, renewable energy)
- Adult education: Literacy, digital skills, entrepreneurship for spouses of miners
- Environmental monitoring training: Community water/air sampling (citizen science) -- builds trust, provides data
- Cultural preservation: Fund Atacameno/Diaguita language and heritage programs in schools

Education investment is not CSR -- it is workforce risk mitigation and social license infrastructure. Every trained local hire is one less contractor, one more rooted family, one more community advocate.
