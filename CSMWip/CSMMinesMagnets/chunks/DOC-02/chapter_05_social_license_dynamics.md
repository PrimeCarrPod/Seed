## Social License Dynamics
## 5. Social License Dynamics -- Consulta Indigena, Community Agreements, Conflicts

Social license to operate (SLO) in Chile is not a single permit -- it is a layered, ongoing negotiation between companies, communities, indigenous organizations, municipalities, and the state. This chapter maps the SLO landscape for magnetic mineral districts and identifies Tardigradia entry points.

### 5.1 SLO Framework -- Legal and Informal Layers

| Layer | Instrument | Authority | Enforceability | Tardigradia Relevance |
|-------|------------|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Constitutional | Indigenous rights (Art 12, ILO 169) | State | Supreme Court | Consulta is mandatory |
| Environmental | RCA (Environmental Permit) | SEA | Legally binding | Aegis-Forge zero-water = faster RCA |
| Sectoral | Mining concessions, water rights | Sernageomin, DGA | Administrative | Concession acquisition path |
| Contractual | Community agreements (convenios) | Private + community | Civil court | Primary Tardigradia tool |
| Relational | Trust, communication, presence | Ongoing | Social/political | Daily operations, local hiring |

### 5.2 Community Agreement Models -- Comparative Analysis

| Agreement | Company | Community | Annual Value | Key Terms | Replicability for Tardigradia |
|-----------|---------|-----------|--------------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Codelco-Andacollo | Codelco | Andacollo (Diaguita) | $12M | 40% local hiring, scholarships, health, water fund | High -- municipal partnership |
| Antofagasta Minerals | Los Pelambres | Caimanes/Quilimari | $8M | Water infrastructure, education, productive development | High -- water focus |
| Candelaria-Tierra Amarilla | Lundin | Tierra Amarilla (Diaguita) | $25M | 40% local hiring, $200M fund, environmental monitoring | Medium -- large scale |
| Carmen de Andacollo | Teck | Andacollo | $10M | Scholarships, health center, local procurement | High -- health/education |
| Sierra Gorda | KGHM | Sierra Gorda (Atacameno) | $5M | Employment, training, community center | Medium -- smaller community |

Pattern: Per-capita community investment ranges $1,000-5,000/person/year. Larger communities (Tierra Amarilla 12k) get larger absolute funds; smaller communities (Sierra Gorda 3k) get higher per-capita. Tardigradia should budget $3,000/person/year for direct community investment (education, health, water, productive projects) -- separate from wages/taxes.

### 5.3 Conflict Flashpoints -- Where SLO Breaks Down

| Conflict | Location | Trigger | Outcome | Lesson |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|--------|
| Water theft accusation | Caimanes (Los Pelambres) | Tailings dam aquifer recharge | 10-year lawsuit, Supreme Court ordered mitigation | Water = existential |
| Glacial impact | Los Andes (Los Bronces) | Glacier retreat acceleration | Expansion blocked, Anglo divesting Chilean assets | Glaciers = red line |
| Marine ecosystem | La Higuera (Dominga) | Humboldt penguin reserve | Project rejected (Committee of Ministers) | Biodiversity veto |
| Dust/health | Mejillones | Coal plants + port dust | Health emergency declared, plant closures | Cumulative impact matters |
| Indigenous exclusion | Collahuasi | Aymara not consulted initially | Operational delays, reputation damage | Early Consulta = cheaper |

Tardigradia SLO strategy: Enter at Relational Layer first. Before concessions, before SEIA, before Consulta -- build presence in Vallenar, Tierra Amarilla, Andacollo, Sierra Gorda. Fund community water monitoring. Sponsor technical education. Be known as the zero-water, zero-tailings company before asking for anything.

### 5.4 Municipal Governance -- The Overlooked Partner

Municipalities receive Royalty Minera (Law 21,210): 1% ad valorem on copper, 2% on lithium, distributed by formula (population, poverty, mining intensity). 2023 distributions:

| Municipality | Royalty Received (US$M) | % of Budget | Key Investments |
|--------------|--------------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Calama | 42 | 35% | Hospital, university campus, sports complex |
| Antofagasta | 38 | 18% | Waterfront, cultural center, transit |
| Vallenar | 18 | 42% | Water pipes, rural electrification, school |
| Tierra Amarilla | 12 | 55% | Health post, community center, roads |
| Andacollo | 9 | 48% | Water treatment, tourism infrastructure |
| Sierra Gorda | 6 | 60% | All basic services (municipality runs on royalty) |

Municipalities are fiscal dependents of mining. Mayors are de facto mining regulators (building permits, land use, community voice). Tardigradia must engage mayors as primary partners -- they control the local social license infrastructure.
