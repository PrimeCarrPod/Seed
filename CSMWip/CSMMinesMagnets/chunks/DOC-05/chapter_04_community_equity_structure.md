## Chapter 4: Community Equity Structure

### 4.1 Equity Philosophy — Consent as Capital, Not Charity

The FBC (Fondo de Beneficio Comunitario) trust equity structure is not assembled from CSR best practices; it is **forced** by the logical necessity of converting community consent into **legal capital with binding veto** that cannot be captured, diluted, or bypassed. The structure rests on a single, irreducible constraint: **Consent = Equity + Veto** — the proposition that Chilean communities hosting magnetic mineral operations must hold legal equity with hard veto rights over water, environment, and indigenous boundaries. No additional postulates — no "benefit agreements" without equity, no "community funds" without governance, no "social license" without legal teeth — are required. The constraint is simply: *Community equity is the price of social license; veto is the enforcement mechanism*. Everything else is the shadow this constraint casts.

The apparent complexity of multi-trust architecture — FBC Holding (15% holdco), FBC Norte/Centro/Sur (20% opco each), FBC Manufacturing (15%), FBC Integration (10%), CDC Development Corporations, CVO Oversight — dissolves when viewed through the lens of this constraint. Each trust is a **deterministic node** in the consent topology: Holding trust governs strategic direction; Opco trusts govern cluster operations; Manufacturing/Integration trusts govern downstream value capture; CDCs govern local development; CVO governs operational veto. All are *derived* from the consent topology. No reduction occurs; the complexity is **resolved** because the constraint set is complete.

### 4.2 FBC Trust Architecture — Legal Form & Capitalization

**Legal Vehicle:** Fideicomiso (Trust) under **Law 19,802** (Trust Law) + **Law 20,712** (Public Trusts) — provides asset segregation (Art 15), trustee fiduciary duties, beneficiary enforcement rights.

| Trust | Level | Equity Stake | Capitalization Source | Beneficiaries | Trustee |
|-------|-------|--------------|----------------------|---------------|---------|
| **FBC Holding** | Holdco | 15% Tardigradia Chile SpA (Class B) | Series A: 15% of $75M = $11.25M | Host communes (3+ regions) | Independent fiduciary (banco fiduciario) |
| **FBC Norte** | Opco | 20% Aegis-Forge Norte SpA (Class B) | Project finance equity: 20% of $184M = $36.8M | Antofagasta region communes | Independent fiduciary |
| **FBC Centro** | Opco | 20% Aegis-Forge Centro SpA (Class B) | Project finance equity: 20% of $184M = $36.8M | Atacama region communes | Independent fiduciary |
| **FBC Sur** | Opco | 20% Aegis-Forge Sur SpA (Class B) | Project finance equity: 20% of $184M = $36.8M | Coquimbo region communes | Independent fiduciary |
| **FBC Manufacturing** | Opco | 15% Magnet Chile SpA (Class B) | Project finance equity: 15% of $350M = $52.5M | All host communes (pro-rata) | Independent fiduciary |
| **FBC Integration** | Opco | 10% Motor Integration Chile SpA (Class B) | Series C/strategic: 10% of $200M = $20M | All host communes (pro-rata) | Independent fiduciary |

**Total Community Equity Value (at Series B close): ~$220M across 6 trusts.**

### 4.3 Vesting & Anti-Dilution — Structural Protection

**Vesting Schedule (per trust deed):**

| Milestone | Vesting % | Trigger |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Trust formation + initial capitalization | 20% | DOC-04 Trigger 3 (Community agreements) |
| RCA approved + construction start | 30% | DOC-04 Trigger 2 (RCA) |
| Commercial production (first REE oxide sale) | 30% | DOC-04 Ch 5 earnout milestone |
| Year 3 EBITDA > $100M | 20% | DOC-04 Ch 5 earnout milestone |

**Anti-Dilution Protection:**
- **Over-allotment mechanism:** FBC trusts receive equity via over-allotment (not pro-rata issuance). Total opco equity = 105% with FBC's 20% sitting above Tardigradia's 85%.
- **New capital raises:** Only dilute Class A (Tardigradia). Class B percentage automatically adjusts to maintain 20% (or 15%/10%) of issued+outstanding.
- **Tag-along rights:** Full tag-along on any Tardigradia Holdco sale (100% of FBC shares).
- **Drag-along protection:** FBC trusts **cannot be dragged** — community equity is non-transferable except to successor trust.

**Priority Coupon:** 6% annual coupon on capital account, paid before any Tardigradia distributions. Non-cumulative but accrues.

### 4.4 CDC Development Corporations — Local Development Engine

**Legal Vehicle:** Corporación de Desarrollo Comunitario (CDC) under **Law 20,500** (Associations Law) — non-profit, community-governed, tax-exempt for development activities.

| CDC | Region | Funding | Governance | Mandate |
|-----|--------|---------|------------|---------|
| **CDC Norte** | Antofagasta | 15% of Aegis-Forge Norte FCF (min $1.8M/yr) | Community assembly (elected) | Education, health, water infrastructure, productive development |
| **CDC Centro** | Atacama | 15% of Aegis-Forge Centro FCF (min $1.8M/yr) | Community assembly (elected) | Same + indigenous cultural preservation |
| **CDC Sur** | Coquimbo | 15% of Aegis-Forge Sur FCF (min $1.8M/yr) | Community assembly (elected) | Same + agricultural diversification |

**CDC-Tardigradia Interface:**
- Tardigradia appoints 1/7 CDC board seats (non-veto)
- CDC reports quarterly to Tardigradia Board Community & ESG Committee
- CDC Executive Director briefs Community Directors (Dir 3,4) 2 days before board meetings
- Annual community assembly: CDC presents audited accounts + impact report

### 4.5 CVO (Community Veto Oversight) — Operational Veto

**Legal Vehicle:** Unincorporated association under **Law 19,802** (Trust Law) with binding contractual veto rights embedded in:
- Tardigradia Chile SpA SHA
- Opco SHA (each cluster)
- FBC Trust Deeds
- RCA environmental commitments

**Composition:**
- 1 representative per host commune (elected, 2-year terms)
- 1 indigenous authority representative (CONADI recognized)
- 1 environmental NGO representative (rotating)
- 1 municipal representative (rotating mayors)
- Chair: Elected by members (2-year term)

**Veto Scope (Binding, Non-Appealable at Opco Level):**
| Domain | Veto Trigger | Escalation |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| Water extraction > permitted | Any exceedance | Immediate halt (72 hrs) → Board confirmation |
| Environmental exceedance | Any RCA parameter breach | Immediate halt → SEA notification |
| Indigenous site impact | Any unapproved disturbance | Immediate halt → Consulta Indígena re-opening |
| Community health/safety | CVO Chair determination | Immediate halt → Independent expert review |

**CVO Process:**
1. CVO Chair issues **Protection Order** (immediate, 72-hour duration)
2. Tardigradia Board must confirm/reject within 72 hours
3. If Board rejects → Automatic escalation to **Expert Determination** (DOC-04 Ch 7)
4. Expert Determination binding within 45 days
5. Community Legal Aid Fund: $200k/yr from FBC Holding covers CVO costs

**CVO Data Access:** Real-time SCADA feeds (water, emissions, production), monthly environmental reports, quarterly community impact assessments.
