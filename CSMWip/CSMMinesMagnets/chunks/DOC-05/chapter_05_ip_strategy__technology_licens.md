## Chapter 5: IP Strategy & Technology Licensing

### 5.1 IP Philosophy — Technology as Boundary Condition, Not Commodity

The Tardigradia IP strategy is not assembled from portfolio maximization; it is **forced** by the logical necessity of a technology deployment where **processing physics dictates legal structure**. The strategy rests on a single, irreducible constraint: **Aegis-Forge Thermal Cascade = Zero-Waste Boundary** — the proposition that the 17-stage thermal cascade (800-2,200°C, ZrB₂-SiC) is the *only* processing route achieving zero aqueous discharge, zero tailings, and $4.32/kg OPEX in Chilean Atacama conditions. No additional postulates — no alternative process licensing, no patent thickets for obstruction, no trade secret hoarding — are required. The constraint is simply: *Technology license enables sovereign production; IP protection enables technology transfer; know-how transfer enables community operations*. Everything else is the shadow this constraint casts.

### 5.5 Export Control Compliance — Dual-Use Management

**Classification:**
- **Magnets (NdFeB, SmCo, Fe₁₆N₂):** EAR 1C003 (magnets with >1.5 T remanence) / ITAR XII (defense articles)
- **REE Oxides (Nd, Pr, Dy, Tb):** EAR 1C351 (chemical precursors)
- **Thermal Cascade Technology:** EAR 2E003 (technology for 1C003)

**Compliance Program:**
- **Shared Screening:** Dow Jones / World-Check for all counterparties
- **End-Use Certificates:** Required for all magnet/REE oxide sales
- **Dual-Use License:** BIS license for US-origin tech (Aegis-Forge) re-export from Chile
- **ITAR Registration:** Motor Integration Chile SpA registers as foreign person (DDTC)
- **CPTPP Art 9.16:** Amicus curiae for NGOs on export control transparency
