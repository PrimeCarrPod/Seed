## Chapter 2: Capital Stack & Fundraising Sequence

### 2.1 Capital Stack Overview — $1.3B Total Deployment

The Tardigradia capital stack is not assembled from arbitrary financing rounds; it is **forced** by the logical necessity of a staged deployment that matches capital to de-risking milestones. The stack rests on a single, irreducible constraint: **Capital Follows Triggers** — each tranche deploys only when specific Chapter 3 (DOC-04) transition triggers are verified. No additional postulates — no blind pool commitments, no front-loaded equity, no community capital calls — are required. The constraint is simply: *Trigger verification releases capital; capital enables next trigger; production validates all*. Everything else is the shadow this constraint casts.

### 2.3 Waterfall & Distribution Mechanics

**Holdco LP Waterfall (Delaware LP Agreement):**

| Tier | Distribution | Allocation |
|------|--------------|------------|
| 1 | Return of Capital (ROC) | 100% to LPs pro-rata until capital returned |
| 2 | Preferred Return (8% IRR) | 100% to LPs pro-rata until 8% IRR achieved |
| 3 | GP Catch-up | 20% to GP (ACS/CSM) until GP = 20% of cumulative distributions |
| 4 | Carried Interest | 80% LPs / 20% GP thereafter |

**Opco Waterfall (per SpA):**

| Tier | Distribution | Allocation |
|------|--------------|------------|
| 1 | Debt Service | 100% to project finance lenders |
| 2 | Environmental Bonds | Community coupons (Law 20,720 Art 134) |
| 3 | FBC Priority Coupon | 6% on FBC capital account |
| 4 | ROC | Pro-rata to all shareholders |
| 5 | Residual | Pro-rata (Tardigradia 80%, FBC 20%) |

### 2.4 Series A Term Sheet — Key Provisions

| Provision | Term | Rationale |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Pre-money Valuation** | $425M | DCF base $553M × 0.75 (illiquidity/early stage discount) |
| **Raise Amount** | $75M | Funds Norte cluster + pilot + team + FBC capitalization |
| **Price per Unit** | $425M / 100M units = $4.25 | Standard LP unit pricing |
| **Liquidation Preference** | 1x non-participating | Standard venture; converts to common at IPO |
| **Anti-Dilution** | Broad-based weighted average | Protects Series A vs future down rounds |
| **Board Seats** | 2/7 (Strategic LP + Corfo) | Aligned with governance (Ch 3) |
| **Information Rights** | Monthly ops, quarterly financials, annual audit | Standard LP rights |
| **Pro-Rata Rights** | All future rounds | Standard LP rights |
| **Co-Sale/Tag-Along** | Yes | Protects LPs in secondary |
| **Drag-Along** | Yes (60%+ threshold) | Enables exit |
| **Most Favored Nation** | Yes | Protects Series A vs Series B terms |
