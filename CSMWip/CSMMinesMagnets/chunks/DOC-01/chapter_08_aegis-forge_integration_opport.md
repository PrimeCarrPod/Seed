## 8. Aegis-Forge Integration Opportunities -- Mine-Site Deployment Strategy

The Aegis-Forge Thermal Cascade Inductive Material Separator (17-stage, 800-2,200C, closed-loop argon, zero liquid waste) transforms Chile magnetic mineral landscape from byproduct/waste to strategic feedstock. This chapter maps deployment scenarios by district, ownership model, and integration point.

### 8.1 Deployment Architectures -- Three Models

| Model | Description | CAPEX/Unit | Best Fit Districts | Ownership |
|-------|-------------|------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Mine-Site** | 1-3 units at operating mine, feed from LIMS concentrate | 8.4M | CAP IOA, Candelaria, Chuquicamata | Tardigradia JV / Mine owner |
| **Hub-and-Spoke** | Central 5-10 unit hub, multiple mines truck/rail concentrate | 8.4M x N | Antofagasta cluster (5+ mines) | Tardigradia 100% |

### 8.2 Mine-Site Integration Points -- By District

#### 8.2.1 CAP IOA Operations (Los Colorados, El Algarrobo, Cerro Negro, El Romeral)
- **Feed**: LIMS magnetite concentrate (68% Fe, 4% apatite, 2,000 ppm REE)
- **Integration**: Post-LIMS, pre-apatite flotation -- eliminates flotation circuit
- **Units Required**: 4 mines x 2 units each = 8 units (total 24.7 Mt/yr feed)
- **Products**: 99.2% Fe3O4 (17 Mt/yr) + REE oxides (12,000 t REO/yr)
- **CAPEX**: 8 x 8.4M = 47M (vs. 60M for equivalent flotation upgrade)
- **OPEX Savings**: .32/t vs. 2-18/t (acid) = 80M/yr savings at scale
- **Permitting**: 12-18 months (no EIA for water/tailings) vs. 4-5 years

#### 8.2.2 Candelaria (Lundin Mining) -- IOCG Flagship
- **Feed**: LIMS concentrate (65% Fe, 0.8% S, 220 ppm REE, xenotime-rich)
- **Integration**: Parallel to existing mag circuit; sulfur removal enables premium Fe
- **Units**: 2 units (420 kt/yr mag byproduct -> 120 kt/yr high-purity Fe3O4)
- **Strategic Value**: Only Chilean IOCG with xenotime HREE (Dy 120 ppm, Y 420 ppm)
- **Partnership Model**: Tardigradia builds/operates; Lundin supplies feed, offtakes Fe3O4; REE revenue split 60/40

#### 8.2.3 Codelco Porphyry Cluster (Chuquicamata, Radomiro Tomic, El Teniente)
- **Feed**: Mag-pyrrhotite skarn concentrate (55-60% Fe, 1.5-3.5% S)
- **Integration**: Tailings reprocessing + live skarn feed -- converts waste to product
- **Units**: 6 units (3.2 Mt/yr feed -> 900 kt/yr high-purity Fe3O4)
- **Sulfur Byproduct**: 48 kt/yr elemental sulfur (saleable, 50/t = .2M/yr)
- **Partnership Model**: Codelco venture -- Tardigradia technology license + operations; Codelco provides feed/infrastructure

#### 8.2.4 Sierra Moreno REE Project (Advanced Exploration)
- **Feed**: Xenotime-bearing vein concentrate (pre-concentrated by reverse LIMS)
- **Integration**: Greenfield -- Aegis-Forge IS the processing plant (no conventional circuit)
- **Units**: 3 units (500 kt/yr ore -> 15 kt/yr REE oxides, 85% HREE)
- **Strategic Value**: Highest Dy/Tb/Y in Chile; zero-acid processing; 18-month permit
- **Ownership**: Tardigradia 100% (mining concession acquisition target)

### 8.4 Port-Site: Mejillones Strategic Reserve

Mejillones port offers: 18m draft (Capesize), 25 Mt/yr capacity, 200 MW grid, desalinated water, free zone status. Port-site Aegis-Forge enables:
- **Import feed**: Low-grade magnetite from Brazil/Peru/Argentina -> upgrade to Fe16N2 spec
- **Strategic stockpile**: Chilean government REE reserve (Dy, Tb, Y) for defense supply chain
- **Export gateway**: Fe3O4 -> Fe16N2 (Niron Minnesota) or direct to CSM motor lines
- **Units**: 5 units (phased), 5 Mt/yr capacity
