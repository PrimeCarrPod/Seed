## 6. Current Production & Processing Status — Magnetite, Byproducts, and REE Recovery

Chile produces 8-10 Mt/yr of magnetite concentrate (primarily CAP from IOA, minor from IOCG) and generates 5-8 Mt/yr of magnetic byproduct from porphyry operations (currently low-value). REE recovery from domestic sources is effectively zero. This chapter establishes the baseline for Aegis-Forge integration and Tardigradia venture sizing.

### 6.1 CAP Minería — Primary Magnetite Producer

CAP (Compañía de Acero del Pacífico) operates four IOA mines in the Chilean Iron Belt (Los Colorados, El Algarrobo, Cerro Negro Norte, El Romeral). 2023 production:

| Mine | Ore Mined (Mt) | Head Fe (%) | Concentrate (Mt) | Conc. Fe (%) | Conc. P (%) | Recovery (%) |
|------|----------------|-------------|------------------|--------------|-------------|--------------|
| Los Colorados | 18.5 | 42.1 | 9.2 | 68.2 | 0.045 | 94.2 |
| El Algarrobo | 14.2 | 38.7 | 6.8 | 67.8 | 0.052 | 93.5 |
| Cerro Negro Norte | 6.8 | 45.3 | 3.6 | 68.5 | 0.038 | 95.1 |
| El Romeral | 12.1 | 35.8 | 5.1 | 66.9 | 0.058 | 92.8 |
| **Total** | **51.6** | | **24.7** | | | |

Concentrate distribution: 14.2 Mt to Huachipato blast furnace (steel), 10.5 Mt export pellets (Asia, 68% Fe). Pelletizing at Puerto Las Los (CAP) and Guacolda (third party).

Processing flowsheet (all four mines):
1. **Crushing**: Primary gyratory → secondary cone → tertiary HPGR (Los Colorados)
2. **Grinding**: SAG + ball mill → P80 45 µm (ceramic media to reduce Fe contamination)
3. **LIMS**: 3-stage drum separators (rougher, cleaner, recleaner) — 1,200 Gauss
4. **Apatite flotation**: Fatty acid collector, silica depressor — P removal to <0.06%
5. **Dewatering**: Thickener → pressure filter → 8% moisture
6. **Pelletizing** (export): Bentonite binder → induration furnace (1,250°C) → 68% Fe pellets

Key metrics: Power 18-22 kWh/t concentrate; Water 0.8-1.2 m³/t (seawater at Los Colorados); Reagent cost $4.20/t (flotation); OPEX $18-22/t concentrate.

### 6.2 Codelco — Magnetite Byproduct from Porphyry Operations

Codelco's Chuquicamata, Radomiro Tomic, and El Teniente generate magnetic skarn byproduct. 2023: ~3.2 Mt mag-pyrrhotite concentrate at 55-60% Fe, 1.5-3% S. Sold to cement industry ($28/t) and coal wash plants ($32/t). No REE recovery. Pyrrhotite content prevents steelmaking use.

### 6.4 REE Recovery — Current Status: ZERO

No commercial REE production in Chile. All REE in:
- IOCG/IOA apatite → tailings (est. 15,000 t REO/yr lost at CAP alone)
- Porphyry skarn REE minerals (allanite, xenotime) → tailings
- Carbonatite (Pampa Larga) → unexplored
- Ion-adsorption clays → untested

Global context: Chile imports 100% of separated REE oxides (Nd, Dy, Tb, Y) for any domestic magnet manufacturing (currently none). Carrington Storm Motors Fe₁₆N₂ (Niron) pathway requires zero REE — but SmCo and NdFeB doping require Dy/Tb. Chilean HREE from xenotime is the strategic supply.
