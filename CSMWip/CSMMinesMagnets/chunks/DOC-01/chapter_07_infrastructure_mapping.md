## 7. Infrastructure Mapping — Rail, Ports, Grid, Water Access by District

Magnetic mineral economics are dominated by logistics: transport to port, power for processing, water for grinding/flotation. Chile's north-south geography creates distinct infrastructure corridors. This chapter maps each magnetic district to its logistics reality.

### 7.1 Rail Corridors — FCAB and Regional Lines

Ferrocarril de Antofagasta a Bolivia (FCAB) is the backbone: 900 km network connecting interior mines to Antofagasta/Mejillones ports. Key segments for magnetic districts:

| Segment | Length (km) | Capacity (Mt/yr) | Current Utilization | Magnetic Districts Served |
|---------|-------------|------------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Antofagasta → Baquedano | 180 | 45 | 78% | IOCG (Candelaria via branch), IOA (CAP mines) |
| Baquedano → Calama | 160 | 35 | 85% | Porphyry (Chuquicamata, Radomiro Tomic) |
| Calama → Salar de Atacama | 120 | 20 | 60% | Lithium ops, REE (Sierra Moreno) |
| Baquedano → Los Colorados | 45 | 15 | 90% | CAP IOA (Los Colorados, El Algarrobo) |
| Baquedano → Cerro Negro | 60 | 12 | 75% | CAP IOA (Cerro Negro Norte) |
| **Total FCAB** | **900** | | | |

Central Chile (33-38°S): No dedicated mining rail. Los Bronces/El Teniente use truck → San Antonio port (120 km). Los Pelambres: truck → Los Vilos port (45 km) or truck 200 km to Coquimbo.

### 7.3 Power Grid — SING/SIC Interconnection and Renewable Integration

Chile's grid: SING (Norte Grande, 18-29°S) and SIC (Central, 29-42°S) interconnected since 2017 (500 kV Cardones-Polpaico line). 2023 generation mix: 33% solar, 28% wind, 20% gas, 11% hydro, 8% coal.

Aegis-Forge power demand: 1.8 MW per unit (E172 spec). At CAP Los Colorados (120 MW available), 60+ units feasible. At Sierra Moreno (25 MW), 10 units. Grid capacity is NOT a constraint for mine-site deployment.

### 7.4 Water Access — The Critical Constraint

Atacama Desert (18-29°S) is hyper-arid (<15 mm/yr rainfall). Mining water sources:
- **Seawater desalination**: CAP (Los Colorados), Escondida, Spence, Quebrada Blanca — capex $150-300M, opex $1.5-2.5/m³
- **Aquifer extraction**: Limited by DGA (water authority) — declining allocations
- **Continental water**: Los Bronces (glacial melt), El Teniente (Andes runoff) — climate vulnerable

| District | Water Source | Cost (US$/m³) | Allocation (L/s) | Stress Level | Aegis-Forge Advantage |
|----------|--------------|----------------|------------------|--------------|----------------------|
| CAP IOA | Seawater (desal) | 2.20 | 1,200 | **Critical** | **Zero water use** (dry thermal) |
| Candelaria | Seawater (desal) | 2.10 | 800 | Critical | Zero water use |
| Chuquicamata | Aquifer + recycle | 1.80 | 2,500 | High | Zero water use |
| Los Bronces | Glacial + recycle | 1.50 | 1,800 | High | Zero water use |
| El Teniente | Andes runoff | 1.20 | 3,200 | Moderate | Zero water use |
| Sierra Moreno | Aquifer (limited) | 2.50 | 50 | **Severe** | Zero water use |

Aegis-Forge thermal cascade uses **zero process water** — argon atmosphere, no grinding, no flotation, no tailings. This is the single largest operational advantage in water-stressed Chilean districts. Eliminates desalination capex/opex for magnetic separation circuit.

### 7.5 Logistics Cost Model — Mine to Port to Customer

Base case: 1 Mt/yr high-purity Fe₃O₄ (Aegis-Forge product) from each district to Mejillones port, then to US Gulf (Niron Magnetics, Minneapolis) or EU (CRMA strategic projects).

| District | Rail (US$/t) | Port (US$/t) | Ocean Freight (US$/t) | Total Logistics (US$/t) | % of Product Value ($200/t) |
|----------|---------------|---------------|------------------------|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| CAP IOA (Los Colorados) | 4.50 | 3.20 | 28.00 | 35.70 | 17.9% |
| Candelaria IOCG | 5.20 | 3.20 | 28.00 | 36.40 | 18.2% |
| Chuquicamata Porphyry | 4.80 | 3.20 | 28.00 | 36.00 | 18.0% |
| Sierra Moreno REE | 6.50 | 3.20 | 28.00 | 37.70 | 18.9% |
| Los Bronces (truck+rail) | 12.00 | 4.50 | 32.00 | 48.50 | 24.3% |
| El Teniente (truck+rail) | 14.00 | 4.50 | 32.00 | 50.50 | 25.3% |

Northern districts (SING grid, FCAB rail, Mejillones/Antofagasta ports) have 30% logistics cost advantage over Central Chile districts. **Tardigradia venture should prioritize Norte Grande (Antofagasta/Atacama regions) for initial deployment.**
