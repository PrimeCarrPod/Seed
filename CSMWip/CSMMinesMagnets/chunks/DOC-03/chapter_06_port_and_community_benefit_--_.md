## 6. Port and Community Benefit -- Local Hiring, Supplier Development, Infrastructure Sharing

Chile's magnetic mineral export chain runs through five major port complexes. Each port is a community -- Mejillones, Antofagasta, San Antonio, Coronel, Punta Arenas -- with distinct demographics, environmental burdens, and economic structures. Tardigradia's magnet manufacturing and export operations must integrate into these port communities as value creators, not just throughput users.

- **Punta Arenas**: UMAG (Univ. Magallanes) -- "Antarctic Logistics & Critical Minerals" specialization. Long-term strategic investment.

### 6.4 Infrastructure Sharing -- Dual-Use Assets for Community Benefit

#### 6.4.1 Rail Infrastructure -- FCAB Integration

Ferrocarril de Antofagasta a Bolivia (FCAB) carries 90% of northern Chile mineral exports. Tardigradia negotiates:

- **Dedicated magnet trains**: 2x/week Mejillones->Antofagasta->San Antonio, temperature-controlled wagons (15-25°C), vibration-damped (magnet protection). Community benefit: Scheduled passenger cars attached (Mejillones-Calama-Antofagasta commuter service, 4x/day).
- **Community rail fund**: $0.50/t magnet shipped -> $150k/yr per cluster for station upgrades, level crossings, safety.
- **Maintenance apprenticeships**: 10 FCAB apprentices/yr sponsored by Tardigradia, guaranteed interview.

#### 6.4.2 Port Infrastructure -- Shared Facilities

| Asset | Tardigradia Use | Community Use | Governance |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|------------|
| San Antonio Magnet QC Lab | Export certification | Community testing (water, soil, fish) | Joint board 50/50 |
| Mejillones Inert Gas Storage | Argon for Aegis-Forge | Medical oxygen, welding gas for local SMEs | Municipal utility model |
| Antofagasta Rail Yard | Magnet train staging | Community market space (weekends) | Concession agreement |
| Coronel Cold Storage | Future REE concentrate | Fisheries cold chain (primary) | Priority to fisheries |
| Punta Arenas Warehouse | Exploration staging | Emergency supplies (antarctic/regional) | Civil protection priority |

#### 6.4.3 Digital Infrastructure -- Community Data Access

- **Port Environmental Dashboard**: Real-time air (PM2.5, SO2, NOx), water (pH, turbidity, metals), noise at 20 stations per port. Public web/mobile access. Tardigradia funds 100% capital, 50% ops; port authority 50% ops.
- **Magnet Shipment Tracker**: Blockchain-based (Hyperledger Fabric) -- origin mine, Aegis-Forge batch, QC results, carbon footprint. Community can verify "their" minerals are processed cleanly.
- **Community Broadband**: Tardigradia extends fiber to port communities (Mejillones, Coronel gaps) -- 1 Gbps symmetric, $10/mo residential (subsidized).

### 6.5 Just Transition -- Coal/thermal Communities to Magnet Economy

Mejillones (3 coal plants), Coronel (2 coal + biomass), Puchuncaví (Ventanas, 4 coal) -- 4,200 direct jobs at risk from decarbonization. Tardigradia targets:

| Transition Pathway | Target Workers | Training Duration | Target Role | Salary Match |
|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|-------------|--------------|
| Plant operator -> Aegis-Forge operator | 800 | 6 months | Thermal cascade operator | 105% |
| Maintenance -> Magnet maintenance | 600 | 9 months | Vacuum furnace tech | 110% |
| Environmental tech -> QC/Metrology | 400 | 12 months | Magnetic testing | 115% |
| Admin/logistics -> Supply chain coord | 300 | 3 months | Export logistics | 100% |

Funding: $25M Just Transition Fund (Tardigradia 40%, Corfo 30%, IDB 20%, municipalities 10%). Wage insurance during training (100% prior salary x 6 months).
