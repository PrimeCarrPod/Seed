## 5. Environmental Remediation Funds -- Tailings Reprocessing, Water Restoration, Aegis-Forge Deployment

Chilean mining communities live with the environmental legacy of 150+ years of extraction: 740+ tailings deposits (Sernageomin 2024), acid rock drainage in 40+ watersheds, aquifer depletion in Atacama/Coquimbo, and airborne particulates in port corridors. Tardigradia's mutual benefit framework treats remediation not as compliance but as value creation -- deploying Aegis-Forge to reprocess tailings, restore water, and generate revenue for communities.

### 5.2 Aegis-Forge Tailings Reprocessing -- Technical and Economic Model

#### 5.2.1 Process Adaptation for Tailings Feed

Standard Aegis-Forge (E172) processes REE concentrate (95%+ REO). Tailings feed requires pre-concentration:

```
Tailings (0.5-5% Fe, 0.01-0.1% REE)
    |
    v
Magnetic Separation (LIMS/WHIMS) -> Magnetite Concentrate (65% Fe) + REE-enriched tailings (0.5-2% REE)
    |                              |
    v                              v
Iron Ore Sale              Aegis-Forge Feed Prep
                                 |
                                 v
                      Aegis-Forge 17-Stage Cascade
                                 |
                                 v
                      Separated REE Oxides (99.5%+)
```

CAPEX adder for magnetic pre-concentration: $12M per 10-unit cluster. OPEX adder: $1.80/t tailings processed.

|---------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|

| Solvent extraction | 45 | 820,000 |
| **Aegis-Forge** | **0** | **Baseline** |

Water credits valued at DGA (Dirección General de Aguas) market rate: $0.15/m3 (2024). 10-unit cluster = **$355,000/yr water credits**.

#### 5.3.3 Water Restoration Fund Allocation

Tardigradia commits 5% of gross revenue + water credits to Water Restoration Fund:

- Aquifer recharge: Infiltration basins, injection wells (target 5 Mm3/yr per cluster)
- Rural potable water: 50 communities x $50k = $2.5M/yr (systems, treatment, maintenance)
- Irrigation efficiency: Drip conversion, scheduling tech for 2,000 ha smallholder agriculture
- Wetland restoration: 5 priority wetlands (Ramsar/National), $1M/yr
- Community monitoring: 30 stations, real-time data to municipalities, $500k/yr

Fund governance: 40% communities, 30% municipalities, 20% DGA, 10% Tardigradia. Decisions by consensus.

### 5.4 Legacy Site Remediation -- Aegis-Forge Deployment at Abandoned Mines

Chile has 200+ abandoned/orphan mine sites (Sernageomin). Tardigradia proposes **Aegis-Forge Remediation Units** -- mobile, containerized 3-stage cascades deployed to:

1. Recover residual REE/Fe from tailings (revenue funds remediation)
2. Stabilize tailings (geopolymer encapsulation using Aegis-Forge waste heat)
3. Treat acid drainage (thermal destruction of sulfides, pH neutralization)
4. Revegetate with native species (nursery powered by waste heat)

Pilot: **Relaves El Sauce (Atacama, abandoned 1998)** -- 12 Mt tailings, 3.2% Fe, 0.08% REE. 3-unit mobile cascade, 24-month program, $18M total. Revenue share: 50% community trust, 30% Sernageomin remediation fund, 20% Tardigradia cost recovery.

### 5.5 Environmental Bonding -- Financial Assurance Innovation

Current Chilean environmental bonds (RCA requirement): Typically 1-3% of CAPEX, held in conservative instruments. Tardigradia proposes **Performance-Linked Environmental Bonds**:

- Base bond: 5% of CAPEX (vs. 1-3% standard) = $36.7M for 10-unit cluster
- Upside trigger: If community water quality improves >20% vs. baseline, bond reduces 10%/yr
- Downside trigger: If water quality degrades, bond increases 25% + community compensation
- Investment vehicle: Green bond issuance (Santiago Stock Exchange), 6% coupon, 15-yr
- Community receives coupon payments directly ($2.2M/yr) -- tangible, visible benefit
