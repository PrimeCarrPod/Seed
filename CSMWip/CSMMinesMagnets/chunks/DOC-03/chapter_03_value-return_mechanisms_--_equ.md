## 3. Value-Return Mechanisms -- Equity Participation, Technology Transfer, Local Processing

Beyond traditional community agreements, Tardigradia deploys three structural value-return mechanisms that fundamentally shift the community from stakeholder to co-owner. These mechanisms are designed to be legally enforceable, financially material, and scalable across multiple Aegis-Forge clusters.

### 3.1 Equity Participation -- Community Ownership in REE Processing

#### 3.1.1 Legal Structure -- Chilean SpA with Community Trust

Tardigradia Chile SpA (Sociedad por Acciones) holds the Aegis-Forge operating entities. A parallel **Community Benefit Trust (Fideicomiso de Beneficio Comunitario)** holds equity on behalf of host communities:

| Entity | Structure | Community Equity | Governance |
|--------|-----------|------------------|------------|
| Tardigradia Chile SpA | Parent holding | 15% (Class B shares) | Trust appoints 2 of 7 directors |
| Aegis-Forge Norte SpA | Cluster 1 (Vallenar/Tierra Amarilla) | 20% | Trust appoints 1 of 5 directors |
| Aegis-Forge Centro SpA | Cluster 2 (Andacollo/Los Andes) | 20% | Trust appoints 1 of 5 directors |
| Aegis-Forge Sur SpA | Cluster 3 (future Austral) | 20% | Trust appoints 1 of 5 directors |

Class B shares carry: (1) Dividend preference (first 8% of distributable cash), (2) Board appointment rights, (3) Veto on community-impacting decisions (water, emissions, closure), (4) Tag-along rights on any control transaction.

#### 3.1.2 Equity Valuation and Vesting

Equity granted at formation (pre-revenue) -- no cash contribution required from communities. Vesting schedule:

- Year 1-2: 25% vested (construction/employment phase)
- Year 3-5: 50% vested (ramp-up, first revenue)
- Year 6-10: 100% vested (steady state)

Anti-dilution protection: Community equity participates pro-rata in all future capital raises. If Tardigradia raises at >$500M valuation, community trust receives additional shares to maintain percentage.

#### 3.1.3 Dividend Flow Calculation -- 10-Unit Cluster Example

Assumptions per Aegis-Forge unit (500 kg/cycle, 18,250 kg/yr REE oxide at 99.5%):

- Revenue/unit: $821,250/yr (basket $45/kg)
- OPEX/unit: $4.32/kg × 18,250 = $78,840/yr (E172 spec)
- CAPEX amortization (10 yr): $1.84M/yr
- EBITDA/unit: $821,250 - $78,840 - $184,000 (G&A) = $558,410
- 10-unit cluster EBITDA: $5.58M/yr
- Community trust share (15% parent + 20% opco): ~$1.1M/yr distributable
- Per capita (pop 12,000 across 3 communes): ~$92/person/yr from dividends alone

Combined with $3,000/person/yr direct investment: **Total ~$3,092/person/yr** -- 48% above Candelaria benchmark.

#### 3.2.2 Supplier Development Program

Tardigradia commits to developing 50 Chilean SMEs as qualified Aegis-Forge suppliers:

- **Technical assistance**: ACS engineers embedded at supplier sites (6-month rotations)
- **Advance payments**: 30% on PO, 40% at midpoint, 30% on delivery -- solves SME working capital
- **Certification support**: ISO 9001, AS9100, nuclear-grade QA for ZrB2-SiC components
- **Long-term contracts**: 5-year framework agreements with volume commitments
- **Shared IP**: Joint development agreements -- supplier owns process IP, Tardigradia gets royalty-free license

Target SME locations: Vallenar (10), Tierra Amarilla (8), Andacollo (7), Copiapo (15), Calama (10).

### 3.3 Local Processing -- From Concentrate to Magnet in Chile

#### 3.3.1 Vertical Integration Logic

Current Chile REE flow: Mine -> Concentrate -> Export to China -> Separated Oxides -> Magnet -> Import. Value capture: Chile 15% (mining), China 85% (separation, magnet, assembly).

Tardigradia target flow: Mine -> Concentrate -> **Aegis-Forge Separation (Chile)** -> **Metal/Alloy (Chile)** -> **Magnet Manufacturing (Chile)** -> **Motor Integration (Chile/US)**. Value capture: Chile 60%+, Tardigradia/CSM 25%, Export 15%.

#### 3.3.2 Processing Stages and Chilean Footprint

| Stage | Technology | Location | Capacity | Jobs | Capex |
|-------|------------|----------|----------|------|-------|
| Separation | Aegis-Forge 17-stage thermal cascade | Vallenar/Tierra Amarilla | 500 kg/cycle × 10 units | 120 | $184M |
| Metal Reduction | Molten salt electrolysis (REO -> RE metal) | Copiapo industrial park | 5,000 t/yr NdPr | 80 | $120M |
| Alloy/Strip Casting | Melt spinning + HDDR | Copiapo | 3,000 t/yr NdFeB alloy | 60 | $80M |
| Magnet Sintering | Vacuum sintering + coating | Andacollo | 2,000 t/yr finished magnets | 100 | $150M |
| Motor Assembly | CSMFAB000000000020 integration | Los Andes | 500k motors/yr | 200 | $200M |

Total Chilean processing investment: **$734M** over 10 years. Total direct jobs: **560 high-skill positions**.

#### 3.3.3 Economic Impact -- Input-Output Analysis

Using Chile Central Bank 2023 I-O matrix (68 sectors), $734M processing investment generates:

- Direct GDP impact: $734M × 0.42 (processing multiplier) = $308M/yr at steady state
- Indirect (supply chain): $308M × 1.8 = $554M/yr
- Induced (wage spending): $554M × 0.6 = $332M/yr
- **Total annual GDP impact: ~$1.2B/yr** (0.35% of Chile 2023 GDP)
- Tax revenue (27% corporate + 19% VAT + royalties): ~$280M/yr
- Export revenue (magnets + motors): ~$2.5B/yr by Year 10

This transforms Chile from mineral exporter to magnet exporter -- the strategic objective of the National Lithium Strategy extended to REEs.
