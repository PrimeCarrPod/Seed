## Chilean Royalty Regime Analysis -- Law 21,210
## 1. Chilean Royalty Regime Analysis -- Law 21,210 and Municipal Fiscal Architecture

Chile's mineral royalty regime underwent fundamental restructuring with Law 21,210 (Royalty Minera), enacted September 2023 and effective January 2024. This chapter provides the mathematical and legal foundation for Tardigradia's community value-return calculations, establishing the fiscal baseline against which all additional benefit mechanisms must be measured.

The legislative process (2019-2023) reflected tension between fiscal need (pandemic recovery, social demands) and industry competitiveness. Final text balances ad valorem simplicity with margin-based progressivity -- a hybrid design unique in Latin America.

### 1.2 Dual-Rate Structure -- Mathematical Specification

#### 1.2.1 Copper Producers (>50 kt/yr fine copper)

The royalty comprises two simultaneous components:

**Component A -- Ad Valorem (Gross Sales):**
$$R_{av} = 0.01 \times P_{Cu} \times Q_{Cu}$$

Where $P_{Cu}$ = realized copper price (US$/lb), $Q_{Cu}$ = fine copper production (lbs). This component applies to ALL copper producers regardless of scale.

**Component B -- Operating Margin (Progressive):**
$$R_{om} = \tau(M) \times M$$
Where $M$ = operating margin = $(Revenue - Operating Costs - Depreciation) / Revenue$

Progressive rate schedule $\tau(M)$:

| Operating Margin Range | Marginal Rate $\tau$ | Cumulative Rate at Upper Bound |
|------------------------|---------------------|----------------------------------|
| 0% - 15% | 0% | 0% |
| 15% - 25% | 8% | 0.8% |
| 25% - 35% | 12% | 2.0% |
| 35% - 45% | 18% | 3.8% |
| 45% - 55% | 22% | 6.0% |
| 55% - 65% | 26% | 8.6% |
| >65% | 26% | 8.6%+ |

Effective total royalty rate: $R_{total} = R_{av} + R_{om}$ (as % of revenue).

#### 1.2.2 Lithium Producers

$$R_{Li} = 0.018 \times P_{Li} \times Q_{Li} + \tau_{Li}(M) \times M$$

Lithium margin rates: 6.8% (15-25%), 14% (25-35%), 22% (35-45%), 30% (45-55%), 40% (>55%). Higher rates reflect lithium's strategic criticality and lower processing complexity.

#### 1.2.3 Iron Ore and REE Producers

Iron ore (magnetite/hematite): 1% ad valorem only (no margin component). REE concentrates: 1% ad valorem + 8% margin above 15% threshold. These rates directly affect CAP (iron) and future Aegis-Forge REE separation output.

### 1.3 Distribution Formula -- Municipal and Regional Allocation

Total national collection flows to the **Fondo de Compensación Minera** (Mining Compensation Fund) administered by Subsecretaría de Desarrollo Regional (Subdere):

$$F_{municipal} = 0.65 \times F_{total} \times w_{commune}$$
$$F_{regional} = 0.35 \times F_{total} \times w_{region}$$

Communal weight $w_{commune}$:
$$w_{commune} = 0.50 \times \frac{Pop_{commune}}{\sum Pop} + 0.30 \times \frac{Poverty_{commune}}{\sum Poverty} + 0.20 \times \frac{MiningIntensity_{commune}}{\sum MiningIntensity}$$

Where Mining Intensity = (mining employment + royalty base) / population. This formula creates perverse incentives: municipalities that attract MORE mining get HIGHER weight, reinforcing extraction dependency.

### 1.5 Tardigradia Fiscal Impact Calculation

For a hypothetical Aegis-Forge unit processing 500 kg/cycle REE concentrate (18,250 kg/yr) at 99.5% purity:

- REE oxide revenue (basket avg $45/kg): $821,250/yr
- Ad valorem royalty (1%): $8,213/yr
- Margin royalty (assuming 35% margin, 18% rate): $51,683/yr
- Total royalty per unit: ~$60,000/yr
- 10-unit cluster: $600,000/yr to host commune (65% of $600k x weight)

This is MARGINAL to existing copper royalties -- a new fiscal stream from REE processing that did not exist before Aegis-Forge deployment. Tardigradia can quantify this as "new money for the community" in negotiations.
