## 4. Workforce Development -- Apprenticeships, Certifications, Magnet Manufacturing Skills

Tardigradia's value-return framework requires a workforce that does not exist in Chile today: technicians and engineers skilled in rare-earth separation, metal reduction, alloy design, magnet sintering, and motor integration. This chapter designs the talent pipeline from liceo (technical high school) through PhD, anchored in Chilean institutions with CSM/ACS technical oversight.

Key insight: Mining automation/process control skills TRANSFER directly. The gap is in REE-specific chemistry, metallurgy, and magnet physics. Tardigradia targets the gap; leverages the transferable base.

### 4.2 Apprenticeship Architecture -- Dual System (German Model Adapted)

#### 4.2.1 Program Structure

| Year | School (Weeks) | Plant (Weeks) | Focus | Stipend | Certification |
|------|----------------|---------------|-------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | 26 | 26 | Fundamentals: chemistry, physics, safety, basic machining | $400/mo | Técnico Nivel Medio |
| 2 | 20 | 32 | REE separation ops, Aegis-Forge systems, process monitoring | $500/mo | Técnico Nivel Superior |
| 3 | 12 | 40 | Specialization: electrolysis OR sintering OR motor assembly | $650/mo | Especialista |
| 4 | 8 | 44 | Advanced: process optimization, quality systems, mentoring | $800/mo | Maestro / Supervisor |

Total: 4 years, 66 weeks classroom, 142 weeks plant. 68% workplace learning -- exceeds ChileValora dual-training standard (50% minimum).

#### 4.2.2 Institutional Partners

- **Liceos Técnicos**: Liceo B-13 Vallenar, Liceo C-33 Copiapó, Liceo B-3 Andacollo, Liceo Politécnico Los Andes -- co-design curriculum, provide classroom
- **Ceduc-UCN / INACAP**: Técnico Superior programs, accreditation, ChileValora certification
- **Universidad Católica del Norte (UCN)**: Ingeniería de Ejecución (Professional Engineering) articulation -- apprentices earn 60 credits toward degree
- **Universidad de Santiago (USM)**: Metallurgy/Materials Engineering -- R&D track, process modeling
- **Sernageomin**: Safety certification, blasting/explosives handling for mining-adjacent ops
- **ChileValora**: National competency certification, framework alignment

#### 4.2.3 Recruitment and Selection

Target: 40 apprentices/cluster/year (120 total across 3 clusters by Year 3).

Selection criteria (weighted):
- Academic: 30% (math/physics grades, technical aptitude test)
- Geographic: 25% (priority: host commune residents, then province, then region)
- Socioeconomic: 20% (Household Registry score, targeting bottom 60%)
- Diversity: 15% (gender target 40% women, Indigenous target 25% per local demographics)
- Motivation: 10% (interview, practical assessment)

Commitment: 4-year contract + 3-year post-graduation service obligation (or repay training cost ~$45,000).

#### 4.3.2 Magnetic Metrology Lab -- Chile National Capability

Tardigradia funds and operates (with INN Chile) the **Chilean Magnet Metrology Laboratory (LabMag-Chile)** at UCN Coquimbo:

- Capabilities: BHmax (hysteresis), Hci (pulse field), Br (fluxmeter), temp coefficients (-40 to 200°C), corrosion (PCT, SST)
- Standards: MMPA 0100, IEC 60404, ASTM A977, MIL-STD-1540 (for CSM motors)
- Accreditation: ISO 17025 target Year 3, ILAC MRA recognition Year 5
- Service: Open to all Chilean magnet producers (MP Materials Chile, future competitors) -- builds national capability
- Budget: $5M capital (equipment), $800k/yr operating (shared Tardigradia/INN/Corfo)

### 4.5 Community Integration -- Beyond the Plant Gates

- **STEM in Schools**: Tardigradia engineers teach magnetism modules in Grades 8-12 at 15 host-commune schools (3,000 students/yr). Curriculum aligned with Mineduc standards.
- **Community Workshops**: Monthly "Magnets in Your Life" sessions -- speakers, demos, career talks. Free, open, Spanish + local dialects.
- **Women in Magnetics**: Targeted recruitment, mentorship circles, childcare subsidies, flexible shifts. Goal: 40% women in technical roles by Year 5 (industry avg 12%).
- **Indigenous Knowledge Integration**: Atacameño/Diaguita elders advise on land use, water ceremony, cultural protocols for plant sites. Paid advisory roles ($2,000/mo/elder).
