## 🎯 DOC-01 AUTHORING GUIDANCE — Chilean Magnetic Mineral Landscape

### Scope
Comprehensive geological survey of Chiles magnetic mineral endowment, mapped to Carrington Storm Motors magnet specifications and Tardigradia venture requirements.

### Key Sections (10 Chapters Target)

1. **Geological Framework of Chile** — Andean metallogeny, tectonic settings, metallogenic belts
2. **Iron Oxide Copper Gold (IOCG) Belt** — El Romeral, El Algarrobo, Candelaria, Manto Verde; magnetite grades, Fe/Cu/Au ratios
3. **Iron Oxide Apatite (IOA) / Kiruna-type** — Los Colorados, Algarrobo, Cerro Negro Norte; massive magnetite, P/REE associations
4. **Porphyry Cu-Mo Magnetic Byproducts** — Chuquicamata, Escondida, Los Bronces, El Teniente; pyrrhotite/magnetite in skarn/alteration halos
5. **REE Occurrences in Atacama** — Carrizalillo, Sierra Moreno, rare earth element prospects in IOCG/IOA systems
6. **Current Production & Processing Status** — CAP magnetite concentrate (68% Fe), Codelco magnetite byproduct, REE recovery rates (near zero currently)
7. **Infrastructure Mapping** — FCAB rail, Puerto Antofagasta/Mejillones/San Antonio/Coronel/Punta Arenas, SING/SIC grid, water rights
8. **Aegis-Forge Integration Opportunities** — Thermal cascade separation at mine sites vs. central hub; 17-stage vs. conventional magnetic separation
9. **Carrington Storm Motors Magnet Spec Alignment** — Map Chilean Fe/REE output to NdFeB, SmCo, Fe₁₆N₂, FeCo grade requirements
10. **Strategic Recommendations & Traceability Matrix** — Priority districts, partnership models, regulatory pathway, TBD flags

### Required Data Points (Per Chapter)
- Reserves/Resources (JORC/NI 43-101): Tonnes, Fe%, Cu%, Au g/t, REO ppm
- Mineralogy: Magnetite/hematite/goethite/pyrrhotite percentages, liberation size
- Processing: Current flow sheet, recovery %, concentrate grade, tailings disposition
- Economics: CAPEX/OPEX per tonne, transport to port, power cost, water cost
- Magnetics: Saturation magnetization (emu/g), coercivity, Curie temp where measured
- Cross-refs: Sernageomin bulletin #, Cochilco report year, company annual report page
