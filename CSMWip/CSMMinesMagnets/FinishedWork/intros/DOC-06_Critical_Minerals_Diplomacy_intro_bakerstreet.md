## Document: DOC 06 Critical Minerals Diplomacy

### The Case

A sovereign state (Chile) holds magnetic mineral wealth critical to a strategic ally (US) facing a statutory deadline (DFARS 2027 Jan). The minerals are encumbered by: constitutional sovereignty, indigenous territorial rights (Law 19,253), environmental permitting (Law 19,300), water scarcity (Law 18,450), royalty redistribution (Law 21,210), and community veto precedent (Dominga rejection). The ally requires: domestic supply chain, 27 MGOe Fe₁₆N₂ qualification, IRA 45X credit eligibility, and CPTPP investment protection. The facilitator (CSM) must originate, de-risk, and transition — without violating sovereignty, without bypassing communities, without aqueous processing, without foreign concession title.

The game is afoot.

### The Evidence

**Exhibit A — Concession Title (Chapter 1, 3 T1):**
Chilean Mining Code (Law 18,248) establishes concessions as *derechos reales* — real property rights distinct from surface. Transfer requires public deed, Sernageomin inscription, Conservador registration. Foreign ownership requires DL 600 registration. *Deduction:* SpA single-shareholder (Law 20,190 Art. 4) is the only vehicle permitting 100% foreign capital with Chilean title retention. No other structure satisfies the title constraint.

**Exhibit B — Community Consent (Chapter 2, 3 T3, 6):**
Law 19,253 Consulta Indígena is a real veto (Dominga 2017/2021). Royalty Minera Law 21,210 distributes 65% to communes by population/poverty/mining intensity formula. Codelco-Andacollo (40% local hire), Candelaria ($200M fund), Carmen de Andacollo ($150M fund) establish the market. *Deduction:* FBC Trusts (15% holdco, 20% opco) + CVO Binding Veto is the only structure converting consent into legal equity with hard stops. Charity fails; equity succeeds.

**Exhibit C — Environmental Permit (Chapter 3 T2):**
Law 19,300 SEIA/RCA requires EIA/EIS, community participation, environmental bond. Aegis-Forge thermal cascade (17-stage, 800-2200°C, ZrB₂-SiC) achieves zero aqueous discharge, zero tailings. E172 specs: $4.32/kg OPEX, 500 kg/cycle, 99.5% purity. *Deduction:* Zero-water processing is the only path to RCA approval in water-scarce Atacama/Coquimbo. Acid leaching fails the environmental constraint.

**Exhibit D — Strategic Demand (Chapter 1, 4, 8):**
CSMMagEco2026 V2.0: 12+ US magnet startups, DFARS 2027 Jan deadline, Fe₁₆N₂ 27 MGOe production, Noveon DFARS-qualified, MP Materials Fort Worth, Urban Mining 12t/mo HPMS. CSMMagTech2026 V2.0: Fe₁₆N₂ 27 MGOe, MnBi 14 MGOe, SmCo Electron Energy. *Deduction:* DFARS 2027 creates strategic option value (~$400M real options) that justifies Tardigradia's $1.3B capital stack. The deadline is the forcing function.

**Exhibit E — Cross-Border Enforcement (Chapter 8):**
NY Convention (Chile 1975, US 1970), CPTPP Art 9 (in force 2023), Chile-US BIT (signed 2023, pending). Chilean Arbitration Law 19,971, Environmental Courts Law 20,600. *Deduction:* Parallel ICC arbitration (Santiago seat/Chile law for assets, NY seat/NY law for holdco) with NY Convention enforcement is the only path to binding, enforceable cross-border resolution. Court litigation fails the speed constraint.

### The Deduction

Eliminate the impossible:

1. **Asset sale to US entity** → Violates Chilean sovereignty (Law 18,248, Constitution) → *Impossible*
2. **Joint venture without community equity** → Violates Consulta veto (Law 19,253, Dominga precedent) → *Impossible*
3. **Acid leaching processing** → Violates water code (Law 18,450) + RCA (Law 19,300) in Atacama → *Impossible*
4. **US court litigation** → Violates speed (years) + enforceability in Chile → *Impossible*
5. **Facilitator cash fee only** → Misaligns CSM with production (transition event vs 20-yr production) → *Impossible*

Whatever remains, however improbable, must be the truth:

**The facilitator-to-owner transition via SpA single-shareholder, FBC trust equity with CVO veto, Aegis-Forge thermal cascade licensing, dual-track SPA/asset contribution, ICC parallel arbitration, and perpetual NSR royalty — is the only formation that satisfies all constraints simultaneously.**

The case is closed. The transition proceeds.
