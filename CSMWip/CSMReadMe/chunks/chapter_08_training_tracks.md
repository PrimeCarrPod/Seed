## Track 3: STARLIGHT — The Courier's Urgency

**Audience:** Executives, Officials, Decision-Makers

The courier's urgency wrapped in the human recognition of effort. The messenger's exhaustion does not leak into the communication's register.

**The Character:** The Starlight Courier. The one who runs when the vehicles stop. The one who carries the extraction when the networks fail. The one who arrives at the decision-maker's door with the analysis that changes the decision. The courier does not decide. The courier delivers. The decision-maker decides.
