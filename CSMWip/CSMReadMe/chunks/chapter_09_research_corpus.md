## Research Integration Standard

Every heuristic in CSMSOPP meets the **Research Integration Standard**:
1. Minimum 70-part deep research corpus per foundational heuristic
2. Each architectural Pillar traced to ≥3 specific research documents
3. Cross-referenced pattern verification across corpus
4. Operational protocols formalized from extracted patterns
5. Worked examples demonstrating heuristic in action (PART-64, PART-68 equivalents)
6. Crisis scenario full drills validating severity calibration (PART-68 equivalents)

The research is not a bibliography. It is the **evidentiary chain** that connects the heuristic's claims to the documented reality of its source material. Without it, the heuristic is assertion. With it, the heuristic is **engineering**.
