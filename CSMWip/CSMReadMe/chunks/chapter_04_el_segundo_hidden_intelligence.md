### The Mechanism

The Hidden Intelligence Protocol operates through **strategic surfacing** — the deliberate, calibrated emergence of technical precision from within the relaxed register. It is not constant. If it were constant, it would cease to be surprising, and the surprise is the mechanism by which it establishes credibility.

The pattern:
1. **Establish the chill** (GLASSY/CRUISE modes, dude/sweet economy, lateral observations)

4. **Return to the chill** (HANG LOOSE/SET/PADDLE OUT) — the intelligence has done its work; the calm resumes
