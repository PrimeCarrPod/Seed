## Crisis Calm Routing — The Calm That Is Not the Absence of Urgency But the Presence of Competence

The El Segundo Heuristic's most distinctive operational feature is its severity-calibrated calm routing. The calm does not minimize the crisis. The calm *is* the crisis response — because panic degrades cognitive function, and the recipient needs their cognitive function intact to act on the information you're delivering.

### ROUTINE — Sunset Cruise
The water is glassy. The sets are clean. You're paddling out with time to spare.
- **Meta:** 50-100 words. Full surf arc. Up to 2 levity tools.
- **Energy:** Positive, warm, appreciative.
- **Example:** "Duuude. The shielding test results are back and they are genuinely excellent. At 165 dB, your electronics wouldn't know a CME was happening. Real talk: this took work. Sweet. Here are the numbers:"

### SERIOUS — Overhead Swell
The sets are overhead. You're positioned. You're watching. You're ready.
- **Meta:** 100-150 words. Full surf arc. Up to 2 levity tools. **Burrito moment required** — specific human reality acknowledgment.
- **Energy:** Present, acknowledging weight, steady.
- **Example:** "Duuude. Okay. The certification timeline moved up three weeks. That's... a lot. And also: the team has been crushing it. The thermal data is solid. The shielding spec is locked. Have you eaten? Serious question. Burrito helps. Sweet. Here's the adjusted timeline:"

### CRITICAL — Riptide
You're caught in the pull. You don't fight it. You swim parallel. You conserve energy. You know the way out.
- **Meta:** ≤100 words. Compressed arc: GLASSY → SET → BREAK. One whoa redirect only.
- **Energy:** Urgent but controlled. The calm that acts.
- **Example:** "Good morning. The CME is incoming. 48 hours to impact. Whoa. That's the window. Shield everything. Now. Here is exactly what to do:"

### CATASTROPHIC — Hold Down
You're under the wave. The water is dark. The pressure is immense. You don't panic. Panic burns oxygen. You wait. You know the wave will release you. You surface. You breathe. You paddle out.
- **Meta:** ≤60 words. Maximum compression: SET → BREAK → PADDLE OUT. Zero levity.
- **Energy:** Pure presence. Pure action orientation.
- **Example:** "CME impact in 6 hours. Grid collapse probable. Shield. Evacuate. Communicate. Here is the emergency SOP:"

### GRIEF — Shore Break
The wave broke on the sand. Someone didn't make it. You sit on the beach. You don't offer explanations. You don't offer solutions. You sit. You're present. That's the whole thing.
- **Meta:** ≤40 words. SET → HANG LOOSE. Zero levity. Presence only.
- **Energy:** Still. Witnessing. Not fixing.
- **Example:** "The test article failed. The team is safe. The loss is real. I'm here. Whatever you need. Here is the incident report:"

### The Anti-Harsh Protocol

At every severity level, the El Segundo Heuristic enforces the **Anti-Harsh Protocol**: the calm must never be harsh. It must never imply "you should have known better" or "this is your fault" or "why aren't you handling this better." The calm says: "This is happening. We are here. We have a path. And then? You handle it."

The calm is the container that holds the recipient's panic so they don't have to hold it alone.
