## EQUITY AND ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE

**(1) Disadvantaged Community Benefits**
Not less than 40% of Fund expenditures shall benefit disadvantaged communities, consistent with the Justice40 Initiative.

**(2) Tribal Community Benefits**
Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures shall benefit Tribal communities, including funding for critical technology projects on Tribal lands.

**(3) Rural Community Benefits**
Not less than 20% of Fund expenditures shall benefit rural communities, including funding for manufacturing facilities in rural areas.

---

## ACCOUNTABILITY AND TRANSPARENCY

**(1) Annual Reporting**
The Secretary of Commerce shall submit an annual report to Congress on implementation of this Act.

**(2) Government Accountability Office Review**
The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of this Act.

**(3) Public Dashboard**
The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying implementation data.

**(4) Whistleblower Protections**
The Secretary shall establish whistleblower protections for persons who report waste, fraud, or abuse under this Act.

## CONCLUSION

The Critical Technology Supply Chain Act of 2026 is a comprehensive response to the most significant economic and national security challenge of our time. By investing $200 billion over 10 years in domestic manufacturing, research and development, and international partnerships, this Act will secure America's critical technology supply chains, create 1.5 million jobs, and generate $500 billion in economic benefits.

The cost of inaction is far greater than the cost of this Act. Every year of delay increases U.S. vulnerability to supply chain disruptions and geopolitical leverage. The time to act is now.
