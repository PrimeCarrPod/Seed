## SEC. 1117. APPENDIX L — SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 1117.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

## SEC. 1118. APPENDIX M — RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING LAW

**SEC. 1118.** (a) FEDERAL STATUTES.—**This Act amends the following federal statutes:
(A) CHIPS and Science Act of 2022
(B) Defense Production Act of 1950
(C) Trade Act of 1974
(D) National Defense Authorization Act
(E) Buy American Act

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of state or local governments.

**(c) TRIBAL SOVEREIGNTY.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.
