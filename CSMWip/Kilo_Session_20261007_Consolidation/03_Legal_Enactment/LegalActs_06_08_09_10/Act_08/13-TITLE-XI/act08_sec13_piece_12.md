## SEC. 1125. APPENDIX T — FINAL PROVISIONS

**SEC. 1125.** (a) COMPREHENSIVE REFERENCE LIST.—**The Secretary shall maintain a comprehensive reference list of all documents cited in this Act.

**(b) REGULAR UPDATES.—**The reference list shall be updated annually to reflect new information.

**(c) PUBLIC ACCESSIBILITY.—**The reference list shall be accessible to the public through the Department of Commerce website.

## SEC. 1126. FINAL APPENDIX PROVISIONS

**SEC. 1126.** (a) CUMULATIVE REMEDIES.—**The remedies provided under this title are cumulative.

**(b) NO LIMITATION.—**Nothing in this title shall be construed to limit the authority of any federal agency.

**(c) SEVERABILITY.—**If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall not be affected.
