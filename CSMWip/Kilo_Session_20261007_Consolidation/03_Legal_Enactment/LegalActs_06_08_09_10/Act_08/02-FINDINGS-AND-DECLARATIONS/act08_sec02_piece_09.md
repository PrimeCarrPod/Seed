## SECTION 220. ACCOUNTABILITY AND TRANSPARENCY

**SEC. 220.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary of Commerce shall submit an annual report to Congress on implementation of this Act.

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of this Act.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying implementation data.

**(d) WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish whistleblower protections for persons who report waste, fraud, or abuse under this Act.

---

## SECTION 221. CONSTRUCTION AND INTERPRETATION

**SEC. 221.** (a) RULES OF CONSTRUCTION.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to:
(1) Authorize the taking of private property without just compensation
(2) Supersede any existing federal law
(3) Create a private right of action

**(b) SEVERABILITY.—**If any provision of this Act is held invalid, the remainder of the Act shall not be affected.

---

## SECTION 222. SAVINGS CLAUSE

**SEC. 222.** Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of any federal agency under existing law.
