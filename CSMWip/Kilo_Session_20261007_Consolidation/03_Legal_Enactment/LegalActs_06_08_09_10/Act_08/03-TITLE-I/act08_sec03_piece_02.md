## SEC. 103. PURPOSES OF TITLE I

**SEC. 103.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Increase the U.S. share of global semiconductor manufacturing from 12% to 24% by 2036.

**(2)** Create 300,000 direct jobs in semiconductor manufacturing.

**(3)** Invest $60 billion in domestic semiconductor manufacturing.

**(4)** Maintain U.S. technology leadership through research and development.

**(5)** Ensure national security through secure semiconductor supply chains.

**(6)** Strengthen allied cooperation on semiconductor supply chains.

## SEC. 104. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE I

**SEC. 104.** In this title:

**(1) "Semiconductor"**
A material that has electrical conductivity between that of a conductor and an insulator, used in electronic devices, including but not limited to integrated circuits, microprocessors, memory chips, and sensors.

**(2) "Fabrication Facility" or "Fab"**
A manufacturing facility where semiconductors are produced from raw materials.

**(3) "Advanced Packaging"**
The process of assembling and testing semiconductor chips after fabrication.

**(4) "Node"**
A specific semiconductor manufacturing process technology, measured in nanometers (nm).

**(5) "Compound Semiconductor"**
A semiconductor made from two or more elements, such as gallium nitride (GaN) or silicon carbide (SiC).
