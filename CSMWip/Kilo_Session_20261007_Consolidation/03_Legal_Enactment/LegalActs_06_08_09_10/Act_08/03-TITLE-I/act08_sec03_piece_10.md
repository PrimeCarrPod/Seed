## SEC. 119. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN PERFORMANCE METRICS

**SEC. 119.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish performance metrics for semiconductor supply chain programs, including:

**(1)** U.S. share of global semiconductor manufacturing
**(2)** Number of domestic fabrication facilities
**(3)** Domestic semiconductor production capacity
**(4)** Semiconductor workforce size
**(5)** Research and development output

**(b) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall report annually on semiconductor supply chain performance against established metrics.

**(c) BENCHMARKS.—**The Secretary shall establish benchmarks for each performance metric and report on progress toward benchmarks.

## SEC. 120. SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN EVALUATION

**SEC. 120.** (a) INDEPENDENT EVALUATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary shall contract with an independent evaluator to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of semiconductor supply chain programs every 3 years.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The evaluation shall assess:
(A) Effectiveness of semiconductor supply chain strategies
(B) Efficiency of semiconductor supply chain operations
(C) Equity of semiconductor supply chain distribution
(D) Achievement of performance targets
(E) Recommendations for improvement

**(b) REPORT.—**The independent evaluator shall submit a report to Congress on the evaluation findings.
