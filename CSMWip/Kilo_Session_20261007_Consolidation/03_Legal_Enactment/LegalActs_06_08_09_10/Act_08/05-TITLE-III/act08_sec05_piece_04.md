## SEC. 308. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN ACCOUNTABILITY

**SEC. 308.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—**The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall submit an annual performance report on pharmaceutical supply chain programs.

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of pharmaceutical supply chain programs.

---

## SEC. 309. PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN PREEMPTION

**SEC. 309.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national pharmaceutical standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt pharmaceutical standards that are more protective than the national standards.

---

## SEC. 310. SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 310.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
