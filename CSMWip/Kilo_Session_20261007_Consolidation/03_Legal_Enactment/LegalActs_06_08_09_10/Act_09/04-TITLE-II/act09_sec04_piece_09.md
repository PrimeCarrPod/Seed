## SEC. 218. DROUGHT RESILIENCE ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 218.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with drought resilience requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with drought resilience activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 219. DROUGHT RESILIENCE JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 219.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
