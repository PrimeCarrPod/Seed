## SEC. 211. ENVIRONMENTAL FLOWS PROGRAM

**SEC. 211.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established an Environmental Flows Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Restore instream flows for fish and wildlife
(2) Improve water temperature conditions
(3) Support endangered species recovery
(4) Maintain riparian ecosystems
