# TITLE IV — WATER CONSERVATION

## SEC. 401. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 401.** This title may be cited as the "Water Conservation Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 402. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO WATER CONSERVATION

**SEC. 402.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Water Waste**
The United States wastes an estimated 17% of daily water production through leaks and inefficiencies.

**(2) Agricultural Use**
Agricultural water use accounts for 80% of western water consumption, much of it through inefficient irrigation systems.

**(3) Industrial Use**
Industrial water use has declined but remains significant. Water recycling in industry is less than 20%.

**(4) Residential Use**
Residential water use averages 300 gallons per household per day. Water-efficient fixtures can reduce use by 30%.

**(5) Conservation Potential**
Water conservation is the most cost-effective approach to addressing water scarcity. Every dollar invested in conservation returns $5 in avoided costs.

## SEC. 403. PURPOSES OF TITLE IV

**SEC. 403.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Promote water conservation across all sectors through incentives, standards, and education.

**(2)** Reduce agricultural water use through efficient irrigation and crop selection.

**(3)** Reduce industrial water use through recycling and process improvements.

**(4)** Reduce residential water use through efficient fixtures and landscaping.

**(5)** Develop water conservation standards for appliances and equipment.

---

## SEC. 404. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE IV

**SEC. 404.** In this title:

**(1) "Water Conservation"**
Practices and technologies that reduce water consumption or improve water use efficiency.

**(2) "Water Use Efficiency"**
The ratio of beneficial water use to total water withdrawn.

**(3) "Xeriscaping"**
Landscaping that uses drought-tolerant plants and efficient irrigation to reduce water use.

## SEC. 405. AGRICULTURAL WATER CONSERVATION

**SEC. 405.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established an Agricultural Water Conservation Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Install efficient irrigation systems
(2) Implement precision irrigation technologies
(3) Support crop switching to less water-intensive crops
(4) Develop agricultural water management plans

---

## SEC. 406. INDUSTRIAL WATER CONSERVATION

**SEC. 406.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established an Industrial Water Conservation Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Install water recycling systems
(2) Implement process water efficiency improvements
(3) Develop industrial water management plans

## SEC. 407. RESIDENTIAL WATER CONSERVATION

**SEC. 407.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Residential Water Conservation Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Provide rebates for water-efficient fixtures
(2) Support xeriscaping and efficient landscaping
(3) Develop water conservation education programs
(4) Implement tiered water pricing

---

## SEC. 408. WATER CONSERVATION STANDARDS

**SEC. 408.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—The Secretary shall establish water conservation standards for appliances and equipment.

**(b) COVERAGE.—**Standards shall cover:
(1) Toilets and urinals
(2) Showerheads and faucets
(3) Washing machines
(4) Irrigation equipment
