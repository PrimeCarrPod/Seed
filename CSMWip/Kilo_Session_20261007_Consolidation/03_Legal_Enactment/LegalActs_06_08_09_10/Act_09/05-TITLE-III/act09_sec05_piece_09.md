## SEC. 318. FLOOD MANAGEMENT ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 318.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with flood management requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with flood management activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 319. FLOOD MANAGEMENT JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 319.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
