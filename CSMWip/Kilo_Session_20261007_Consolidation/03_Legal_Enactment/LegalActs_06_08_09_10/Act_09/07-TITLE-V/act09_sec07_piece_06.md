## SEC. 513. CLIMATE ADAPTATION ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 513.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—**Any person who fails to comply with climate adaptation requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with climate adaptation activities shall be subject to criminal penalties.

---

## SEC. 514. CLIMATE ADAPTATION JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 514.** Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review.
