## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — ECONOMIC ANALYSIS

**SEC. 215.** (a) COST OF INACTION.—**The cost of inaction on water infrastructure is estimated at $14 billion annually in lost water, property damage, and health costs.

**(b) RETURN ON INVESTMENT.—**Every dollar invested in water infrastructure returns $4.30 in economic benefits.

**(c) JOB CREATION.—**Water infrastructure investment creates 15 jobs per $1 million invested.

## ADDITIONAL FINDINGS — ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE

**SEC. 216.** (a) DISPARATE IMPACTS.—**Water infrastructure failures disproportionately affect low-income communities and communities of color.

**(b) TRIBAL WATER ACCESS.—**An estimated 30% of Navajo Nation households lack access to clean running water.

**(c) RURAL WATER CHALLENGES.—**Rural communities often lack the fiscal capacity to maintain water infrastructure.
