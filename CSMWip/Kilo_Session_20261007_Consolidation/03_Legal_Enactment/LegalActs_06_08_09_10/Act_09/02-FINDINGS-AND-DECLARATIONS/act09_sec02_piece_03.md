## SECTION 203. PURPOSES

**SEC. 203.** The purposes of this Act are to:

**(1)** Modernize the nation's aging water infrastructure through targeted investments and technical assistance.

**(2)** Build drought resilience through water recycling, desalination, conservation, and watershed management.

**(3)** Reduce flood risk through levee improvements, floodplain restoration, and stormwater management.

**(4)** Promote water conservation across all sectors through incentives, standards, and education.

**(5)** Adapt water systems to climate change through resilient infrastructure and integrated planning.

**(6)** Ensure safe and affordable drinking water for all Americans.

**(7)** Protect aquatic ecosystems and maintain environmental flows.

**(8)** Create economic opportunities through water infrastructure investment.

**(9)** Advance environmental justice through prioritized funding for disadvantaged communities.

**(10)** Strengthen federal coordination of water policy and programs.

## SECTION 204. DEFINITIONS

**SEC. 204.** In this Act:

**(1) "Drinking Water System"**
A system for the collection, treatment, storage, and distribution of potable water to the public.

**(2) "Wastewater System"**
A system for the collection, treatment, and disposal of municipal wastewater.

**(3) "Stormwater System"**
A system for the collection, conveyance, and treatment of stormwater runoff.

**(4) "Water Conservation"**
Practices and technologies that reduce water consumption or improve water use efficiency.

**(5) "Water Recycling"**
The treatment and reuse of wastewater for beneficial purposes.

**(6) "Desalination"**
The removal of salt from seawater or brackish water to produce potable water.

**(7) "Environmental Justice Community"**
Any community identified as disadvantaged under the Justice40 Initiative or equivalent screening tool.

**(8) "Tribal Nation"**
Any federally recognized Indian Tribe, Alaska Native village, or Native Hawaiian community.

**(9) "Climate-Resilient Infrastructure"**
Infrastructure designed to withstand projected climate change impacts over its design life.

**(10) "Integrated Water Resources Planning"**
A planning process that considers water supply, water quality, flood management, and ecosystem needs holistically.
