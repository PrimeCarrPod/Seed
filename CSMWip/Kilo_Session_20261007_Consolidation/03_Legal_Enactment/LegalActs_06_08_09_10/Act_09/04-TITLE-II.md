# TITLE II — DROUGHT RESILIENCE

## SEC. 201. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 201.** This title may be cited as the "Drought Resilience Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 202. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO DROUGHT

**SEC. 202.** The Congress finds the following:

**(1) Western Drought**
The western United States is experiencing its worst drought in 1,200 years. The Colorado River, which supplies water to 40 million people, has seen flows decline 20% since 2000.

**(2) Reservoir Depletion**
Lake Mead and Lake Powell, the nation's largest reservoirs, are at historically low levels. At current rates, both reservoirs could reach dead pool within 10 years.

**(3) Agricultural Impact**
Drought causes $8 billion in agricultural losses annually. Crop failures and livestock losses devastate rural communities.

**(4) Ecosystem Damage**
Drought kills fish, destroys wildlife habitat, and increases wildfire risk. Streamflows fall below levels needed to support aquatic life.

**(5) Groundwater Depletion**
Over-pumping of groundwater is causing land subsidence and aquifer depletion. Some aquifers may take centuries to recover.

**(6) Snowpack Decline**
Western snowpack has declined 20-30% since 1950, reducing natural water storage and altering seasonal runoff patterns.

## SEC. 203. PURPOSES OF TITLE II

**SEC. 203.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Build drought resilience through water recycling, desalination, conservation, and watershed management.

**(2)** Restore and maintain reservoir levels in major western reservoirs.

**(3)** Protect agricultural communities from drought impacts.

**(4)** Restore aquatic ecosystems damaged by drought.

**(5)** Recharge depleted groundwater aquifers.

**(6)** Develop alternative water sources for drought-prone communities.

---

## SEC. 204. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE II

**SEC. 204.** In this title:

**(1) "Drought Resilience"**
The ability of water systems to maintain adequate supply during periods of water scarcity.

**(2) "Water Recycling"**
The treatment and reuse of wastewater for beneficial purposes, including potable reuse.

**(3) "Desalination"**
The removal of salt from seawater or brackish water to produce potable water.

**(4) "Aquifer Storage and Recovery (ASR)"**
The injection of treated water into underground aquifers for later recovery.

**(5) "Environmental Flows"**
The quantity, timing, and quality of water flows required to sustain aquatic ecosystems.

## SEC. 205. WATER RECYCLING PROGRAM

**SEC. 205.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Water Recycling Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $10 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Construct water recycling facilities
(2) Upgrade existing treatment plants for water recycling
(3) Develop potable reuse projects
(4) Support agricultural water recycling

**(d) GOAL.—**Increase national water recycling capacity from 3.5 billion gallons per day to 10 billion gallons per day by 2036.

---

## SEC. 206. DESALINATION PROGRAM

**SEC. 206.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Desalination Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Construct seawater desalination facilities
(2) Develop brackish water desalination projects
(3) Fund desalination research and development
(4) Reduce desalination energy consumption

## SEC. 207. GROUNDWATER RECHARGE PROGRAM

**SEC. 207.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Groundwater Recharge Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Construct managed aquifer recharge facilities
(2) Improve natural recharge through watershed management
(3) Develop aquifer storage and recovery projects
(4) Reduce groundwater overdraft

---

## SEC. 208. DROUGHT CONTINGENCY PLANNING

**SEC. 208.** (a) REQUIREMENT.—**All western states shall develop and maintain drought contingency plans.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**Drought contingency plans shall include:
(1) Drought monitoring and early warning
(2) Water use restrictions
(3) Emergency water supply alternatives
(4) Agricultural drought response
(5) Ecosystem protection measures
