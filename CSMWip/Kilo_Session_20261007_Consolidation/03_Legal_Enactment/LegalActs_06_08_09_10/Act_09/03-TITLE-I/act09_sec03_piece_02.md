## SEC. 103. PURPOSES OF TITLE I

**SEC. 103.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Replace aging water mains and service lines to reduce water loss and improve reliability.

**(2)** Upgrade water treatment plants to meet current and future water quality standards.

**(3)** Expand wastewater treatment capacity to eliminate combined sewer overflows.

**(4)** Modernize stormwater management systems to reduce flooding and improve water quality.

**(5)** Deploy smart water technologies to improve system efficiency and reduce losses.

**(6)** Eliminate lead service lines and reduce other contaminants in drinking water.

**(7)** Create jobs in the water infrastructure construction and maintenance sectors.

---

## SEC. 104. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE I

**SEC. 104.** In this title:

**(1) "Lead Service Line"**
A water service line made of lead, including the portion on public property and the portion on private property.

**(2) "Combined Sewer Overflow (CSO)"**
The discharge of untreated wastewater from a combined sewer system during wet weather.

**(3) "Smart Water System"**
A water system that uses sensors, data analytics, and automation to optimize performance.

**(4) "Green Infrastructure"**
Infrastructure that uses natural processes to manage water, including rain gardens, green roofs, and permeable pavements.
