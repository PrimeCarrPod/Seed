## SEC. 111. RURAL WATER INFRASTRUCTURE PROGRAM

**SEC. 111.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Rural Water Infrastructure Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Extend water service to unserved rural areas
(2) Upgrade small water systems
(3) Provide technical assistance to rural communities
(4) Support regionalization of small systems

---

## SEC. 112. TRIBAL WATER INFRASTRUCTURE PROGRAM

**SEC. 112.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established a Tribal Water Infrastructure Program.

**(b) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5 billion over 10 years.

**(c) PURPOSE.—**The program shall:
(1) Provide water service to unserved Tribal households
(2) Upgrade Tribal water systems
(3) Fulfill federal trust responsibilities
(4) Support Tribal water rights settlements
