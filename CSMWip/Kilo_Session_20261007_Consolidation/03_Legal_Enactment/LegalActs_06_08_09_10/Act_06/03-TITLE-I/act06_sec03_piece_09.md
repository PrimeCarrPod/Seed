## SEC. 311. WILDFIRE PREVENTION WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

**SEC. 311.** (a) WORKFORCE EXPANSION.—

**(1) HIRING TARGET.—**The Secretary of Agriculture and Secretary of Interior shall collectively hire an additional 25,000 wildfire prevention professionals within 5 years.

**(2) POSITIONS.—**Prevention professionals shall include:
(A) Foresters and silviculturists
(B) Prescribed fire practitioners
(C) Fuels management specialists
(D) Defensible space inspectors
(E) Community outreach coordinators
(F) Wildfire prevention educators
(G) GIS and remote sensing analysts
(H) Environmental compliance specialists

**(3) SALARY STANDARDS.—**Prevention professionals shall be compensated at rates competitive with private sector positions, with starting salaries of no less than $50,000 annually.

**(b) TRAINING PROGRAMS.—**

**(1) NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREVENTION ACADEMY.—**The Secretary shall establish a National Wildfire Prevention Academy to provide training for prevention professionals, including:
(A) Prescribed fire certification
(B) Fuels management techniques
(C) Environmental compliance
(D) Community engagement
(E) GIS and remote sensing
(F) Fire-adapted community planning

**(2) COMMUNITY COLLEGE PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with community colleges in fire-prone regions to develop wildfire prevention certificate and degree programs.

**(3) APPRENTICESHIP PROGRIPS.—**The Secretary shall establish registered apprenticeship programs for wildfire prevention careers, combining classroom instruction with field experience.

**(c) TRIBAL WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT.—**

**(1) TRIBAL TRAINING CENTERS.—**The Secretary shall fund the establishment of Tribal wildfire prevention training centers.

**(2) CULTURAL BURN PRACTITIONER CERTIFICATION.—**The Secretary shall establish a certification program for cultural burn practitioners, developed in consultation with Tribal nations.

**(d) VETERAN HIRING.—**

**(1) VETERAN PREFERENCE.—**The Secretary shall give hiring preference to veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces for wildfire prevention positions.

**(2) SKILLS TRANSLATION.—**The Secretary shall develop programs to translate military skills to wildfire prevention careers.

**(e) YOUTH CORPS.—**

**(1) WILDFIRE PREVENTION CORPS.—**The Secretary shall establish a Wildfire Prevention Corps for young adults aged 18-25, providing training and employment in wildfire prevention activities.

**(2) SERVICE YEAR.—**Corps members shall serve a minimum of 1 year, receiving a living allowance, health benefits, and education award upon completion.
