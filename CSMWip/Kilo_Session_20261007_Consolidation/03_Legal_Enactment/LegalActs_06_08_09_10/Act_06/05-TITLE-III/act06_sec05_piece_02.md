## SEC. 503. PURPOSES OF TITLE III

**SEC. 503.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a national vegetation management program that treats 25 million acres annually by year 10.

**(2)** Prioritize vegetation management in Wildland-Urban Interface zones, critical watersheds, and emergency access corridors.

**(3)** Expand the use of prescribed fire and cultural burning as primary vegetation management tools.

**(4)** Create markets for biomass removed during vegetation management to offset treatment costs.

**(5)** Build a world-class vegetation management workforce through training and recruitment programs.

**(6)** Streamline environmental review for vegetation management projects while maintaining environmental protections.

**(7)** Establish accountability metrics and reporting requirements to ensure vegetation management programs achieve measurable results.

**(8)** Coordinate vegetation management across federal, state, Tribal, and private lands.

## SEC. 504. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE III

**SEC. 504.** In this title:

**(1) "Vegetation Management"**
The planned manipulation of vegetation to achieve land management objectives, including wildfire risk reduction, forest health improvement, ecosystem restoration, and wildlife habitat enhancement.

**(2) "Hazardous Fuel Reduction"**
The removal or modification of vegetative material to reduce fire hazard and fire behavior potential.

**(3) "Forest Health"**
The condition of a forest as determined by its age structure, species composition, stocking density, and resistance to disturbance.

**(4) "Ecosystem Restoration"**
The process of assisting the recovery of an ecosystem that has been degraded, damaged, or destroyed.

**(5) "Biomass"**
Organic material derived from vegetation management activities, including trees, shrubs, slash, and other plant material.

**(6) "Shaded Fuel Break"**
A strategically located area of reduced tree canopy density designed to reduce fire intensity and provide safe areas for firefighter operations.
