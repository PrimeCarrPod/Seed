## SEC. 522. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW

**SEC. 522.** (a) STREAMLINED REVIEW.—

**(1) CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish categorical exclusions for routine vegetation management activities to streamline environmental review.

**(2) PROGRAMMATIC REVIEWS.—**The Secretary shall conduct programmatic environmental reviews for vegetation management programs to avoid project-by-project review.

**(b) MITIGATION.—

**(1) HABITAT MITIGATION.—**Vegetation management activities that impact wildlife habitat shall include habitat mitigation measures.

**(2) CULTURAL RESOURCE PROTECTION.—**Vegetation management activities shall include measures to protect cultural resources.

**(3) WATER QUALITY PROTECTION.—**Vegetation management activities shall include measures to protect water quality.

---

## SEC. 523. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT COORDINATION WITH OTHER TITLES

**SEC. 523.** (a) COORDINATION WITH TITLE I.—**Vegetation management activities under this title shall be coordinated with wildfire prevention activities under Title I.

**(b) COORDINATION WITH TITLE II.—**Vegetation management activities under this title shall be coordinated with grid hardening activities under Title II, particularly vegetation management around power lines.

**(c) COORDINATION WITH TITLE IV.—**Vegetation management activities under this title shall be coordinated with building standards under Title IV, particularly defensible space requirements.

---

## SEC. 524. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

**SEC. 524.** (a) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on vegetation management activities, including:
(A) Acres treated by type
(B) Treatment effectiveness
(C) Workforce data
(D) Expenditures
(E) Performance metrics

**(b) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of vegetation management programs.

**(c) PUBLIC DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying vegetation management data.

---

## SEC. 525. VEGETATION MANAGEMENT SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 525.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
