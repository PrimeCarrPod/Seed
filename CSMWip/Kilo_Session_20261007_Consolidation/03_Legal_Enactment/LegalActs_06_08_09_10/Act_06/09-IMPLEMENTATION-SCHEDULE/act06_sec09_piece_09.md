## SEC. 919. IMPLEMENTATION ENFORCEMENT

**SEC. 919.** (a) CIVIL PENALTIES.—

**(1) NON-COMPLIANCE.—**Any person who fails to comply with implementation requirements shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.

**(2) FALSE REPORTING.—**Any person who falsely reports implementation data shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to $100,000 per violation.

**(b) CRIMINAL PENALTIES.—

**(1) FRAUD.—**Any person who commits fraud in connection with implementation funding shall be subject to criminal penalties.

**(2) EMBEZZLEMENT.—**Any person who embezzles implementation funds shall be subject to criminal penalties.

## SEC. 920. IMPLEMENTATION JUDICIAL REVIEW

**SEC. 920.** (a) REVIEW.—**Any person aggrieved by a final action of the Secretary under this title may seek judicial review in the United States Court of Appeals for the circuit in which the person resides.

**(b) STANDARD OF REVIEW.—**The court shall set aside any action found to be arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.

## SEC. 921. IMPLEMENTATION SAVINGS CLAUSE

**SEC. 921.** Nothing in this title shall be construed to:

**(1)** Diminish the authority of state or local governments.

**(2)** Affect the regulation of insurance by state insurance commissioners.

**(3)** Affect the authority of the Occupational Safety and Health Administration over workplace safety.

**(4)** Affect the authority of the Environmental Protection Agency over environmental protection.

**(5)** Diminish the sovereignty of Tribal nations.
