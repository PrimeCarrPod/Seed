# TITLE VI — FUNDING MECHANISMS

## SEC. 801. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 801.** This title may be cited as the "Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Funding Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 802. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO FUNDING

**SEC. 802.** The Congress finds the following with respect to funding:

**(1) Funding Gap**
Current federal wildfire funding is insufficient to address the scale of the crisis. The annual funding gap between current spending and estimated need is $30-40 billion. Without additional funding, the wildfire crisis will continue to escalate.

**(2) Current Funding Sources**
Current wildfire funding comes from discretionary appropriations that are subject to annual budget pressures and continuing resolutions. This creates uncertainty and prevents long-term planning.

**(3) Dedicated Funding Needed**
A dedicated, sustainable funding mechanism is needed to provide reliable, long-term funding for wildfire prevention and grid resilience. Dedicated funding should be insulated from annual appropriations battles.

**(4) Multiple Funding Sources**
A combination of funding sources is needed, including carbon pricing, green bonds, utility assessments, and federal appropriations. No single source is sufficient to address the full need.

**(5) Economic Return**
Every dollar invested in wildfire prevention returns $5.90 in avoided damages. The economic return on investment justifies significant public investment.

**(6) Intergenerational Equity**
Wildfire prevention investments benefit future generations. Funding mechanisms should reflect the intergenerational nature of the benefits.

## SEC. 803. PURPOSES OF TITLE VI

**SEC. 803.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a dedicated, sustainable funding mechanism for wildfire prevention and grid resilience.

**(2)** Create a National Wildfire Prevention Fund to receive and distribute funding.

**(3)** Establish a carbon pricing mechanism to generate dedicated revenue.

**(4)** Authorize the issuance of green bonds for wildfire prevention and grid resilience.

**(5)** Create utility assessment mechanisms to fund grid hardening.

**(6)** Establish accountability and transparency requirements for fund management.

**(7)** Ensure that funding is distributed equitably, with priority for disadvantaged communities.

**(8)** Provide long-term funding certainty to enable multi-year planning and investment.

---

## SEC. 804. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VI

**SEC. 804.** In this title:

**(1) "National Wildfire Prevention Fund"**
The fund established under Sec. 805 to receive and distribute funding for wildfire prevention and grid resilience.

**(2) "Carbon Pricing"**
A fee on fossil fuel combustion based on carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) emissions, as established under Sec. 806.

**(3) "Green Bond"**
A debt instrument issued by the U.S. Treasury specifically designated for climate resilience and environmental infrastructure investment.

**(4) "Utility Assessment"**
A fee assessed on electrical utility customers to fund grid hardening activities.

**(5) "Disadvantaged Community"**
Any community identified as disadvantaged under the Justice40 Initiative, CalEnviroScreen, or equivalent state screening tool.

## SEC. 805. NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREVENTION FUND

**SEC. 805.** (a) ESTABLISHMENT.—There is established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the "National Wildfire Prevention Fund."

**(b) PURPOSES.—**The Fund shall provide financial assistance for:

**(1)** Wildfire prevention activities under Title I
**(2)** Grid hardening activities under Title II
**(3)** Vegetation management activities under Title III
**(4)** Fire-resistant construction activities under Title IV
**(5)** Wildfire response modernization under Title V
**(6)** Implementation activities under Title VII
**(7)** Economic impact mitigation under Title IX
**(8)** Enforcement activities under Title X

**(c) FUNDING SOURCES.—**The Fund shall receive revenues from:

**(1)** Carbon pricing receipts under Sec. 806
**(2)** Green bond proceeds under Sec. 807
**(3)** Utility assessments under Sec. 808
**(4)** Federal appropriations under Sec. 809
**(5)** Interest earnings on Fund balances
**(6)** Repayments of loans made from the Fund

**(d) ALLOCATION.—**Funds in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund shall be allocated as follows:

**(1)** Title I (Wildfire Prevention): 25%
**(2)** Title II (Grid Hardening): 25%
**(3)** Title III (Vegetation Management): 15%
**(4)** Title IV (Building Standards): 10%
**(5)** Title V (Response Modernization): 15%
**(6)** Title VII (Implementation): 5%
**(7)** Title IX (Economic Impact): 3%
**(8)** Title X (Enforcement): 2%

**(e) MANAGEMENT.—**The Fund shall be managed by the Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy and the Secretary of Interior.

**(f) REPORTING.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on Fund receipts, expenditures, and balances.

## SEC. 806. CARBON PRICING MECHANISM

**SEC. 806.** (a) CARBON FEE.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**There is imposed a fee on fossil fuel combustion based on carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) emissions.

**(2) RATE.—**The fee shall be:
(A) $25 per metric ton of CO2e in year 1
(B) Increasing by $5 per metric ton annually
(C) Capped at $100 per metric ton

**(3) COVERAGE.—**The fee shall apply to:
(A) Coal combustion
(B) Petroleum combustion
(C) Natural gas combustion
(D) Other fossil fuel combustion

**(b) REVENUE ALLOCATION.—**Revenue from the carbon fee shall be allocated as follows:

**(1)** 50% to the National Wildfire Prevention Fund
**(2)** 25% to a Climate Resilience Fund for other climate adaptation activities
**(3)** 15% to a Just Transition Fund for workers and communities affected by the transition from fossil fuels
**(4)** 10% to a Research and Development Fund for clean energy technologies

**(c) BORDER ADJUSTMENT.—**

**(1) IMPORTS.—**A carbon border adjustment shall be imposed on imports from countries without comparable carbon pricing.

**(2) EXPORTS.—**A carbon border adjustment rebate shall be provided to U.S. exporters to maintain competitiveness.

**(d) REPORTING.—**The Secretary of the Treasury shall submit an annual report to Congress on carbon fee receipts and allocations.

---

## SEC. 807. GREEN BOND PROGRAM

**SEC. 807.** (a) AUTHORIZATION.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to issue green bonds to finance wildfire prevention and grid resilience activities.

**(2) AMOUNT.—**The Secretary is authorized to issue up to $50,000,000,000 in green bonds over 10 years.

**(3) TERMS.—**Green bonds shall have:
(A) A term of 30 years
(B) An interest rate equal to the 30-year Treasury rate plus 0.5%
(C) Interest exempt from state and local taxation

**(b) USE OF PROCEEDS.—**Proceeds from green bonds shall be deposited in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund.

**(c) REPORTING.—**The Secretary of the Treasury shall submit an annual report to Congress on green bond issuance and use of proceeds.

## SEC. 808. UTILITY ASSESSMENT MECHANISM

**SEC. 808.** (a) GRID HARDENING ASSESSMENT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Each state public utilities commission shall assess a grid hardening fee on electrical utility customers in high fire risk areas.

**(2) RATE.—**The fee shall be:
(A) $2.00 per month for residential customers
(B) $10.00 per month for commercial customers
(C) $50.00 per month for industrial customers

**(3) EXEMPTIONS.—**Low-income customers (below 200% of federal poverty level) shall be exempt from the grid hardening fee.

**(b) REVENUE ALLOCATION.—**Revenue from the grid hardening fee shall be allocated as follows:

**(1)** 70% to the National Wildfire Prevention Fund for grid hardening activities
**(2)** 20% to state wildfire prevention programs
**(3)** 10% to low-income customer assistance programs

**(c) REPORTING.—**State public utilities commissions shall submit annual reports to the Secretary of Energy on grid hardening fee receipts and allocations.

---

## SEC. 809. FEDERAL APPROPRIATIONS

**SEC. 809.** (a) ANNUAL APPROPRIATIONS.—There is authorized to be appropriated to the National Wildfire Prevention Fund $10,000,000,000 annually for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2036.

**(b) EMERGENCY APPROPRIATIONS.—**There is authorized to be appropriated an additional $5,000,000,000 annually for emergency wildfire prevention and response activities.

**(c) AVAILABILITY.—**Funds appropriated under this section shall remain available until expended.

## SEC. 811. EQUITABLE DISTRIBUTION

**SEC. 811.** (a) DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITY PRIORITY.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 40% of Fund expenditures shall benefit disadvantaged communities.

**(2) DEFINITION.—**For purposes of this section, "disadvantaged community" means any community identified as disadvantaged under the Justice40 Initiative, CalEnviroScreen, or equivalent state screening tool.

**(b) RURAL COMMUNITY PRIORITY.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 20% of Fund expenditures shall benefit rural communities.

**(2) DEFINITION.—**For purposes of this section, "rural community" means any community with a population of less than 50,000 that is not part of a metropolitan statistical area.

**(c) TRIBAL COMMUNITY PRIORITY.—

**(1) SET-ASIDE.—**Not less than 10% of Fund expenditures shall benefit Tribal communities.

**(2) CONSULTATION.—**Fund expenditures benefiting Tribal communities shall be developed in consultation with the affected Tribal nations.

## SEC. 824. ADDITIONAL FUNDING SOURCES

**SEC. 824.** (a) INSURANCE ASSESSMENT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of the Treasury shall assess a wildfire resilience fee on property insurance policies in high fire risk areas.

**(2) RATE.—**The fee shall be 0.5% of the annual premium for property insurance policies in high fire risk areas.

**(3) REVENUE.—**Revenue from the insurance assessment shall be deposited in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund.

**(b) REAL ESTATE TRANSFER TAX.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of the Treasury shall assess a wildfire resilience fee on real estate transfers in high fire risk areas.

**(2) RATE.—**The fee shall be 0.1% of the transfer value for real estate in high fire risk areas.

**(3) REVENUE.—**Revenue from the real estate transfer tax shall be deposited in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund.

**(c) CORPORATE ENVIRONMENTAL TAX.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**The Secretary of the Treasury shall assess a wildfire resilience fee on corporations with revenues exceeding $1,000,000,000.

**(2) RATE.—**The fee shall be 0.01% of gross revenues for corporations with revenues exceeding $1,000,000,000.

**(3) REVENUE.—**Revenue from the corporate environmental tax shall be deposited in the National Wildfire Prevention Fund.

---

**SEC. 825.** (a) INVESTMENT POLICY.—

**(1) PRINCIPAL PROTECTION.—**The primary investment objective shall be protection of principal.
