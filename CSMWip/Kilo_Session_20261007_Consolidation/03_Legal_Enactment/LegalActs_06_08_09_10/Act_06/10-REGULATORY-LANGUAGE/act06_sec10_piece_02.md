## SEC. 1003. PURPOSES OF TITLE VIII

**SEC. 1003.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Amend existing federal statutes to implement the provisions of this Act.

**(2)** Establish clear regulatory language for implementation.

**(3)** Coordinate regulatory language with existing federal, state, and local regulations.

**(4)** Provide for enforcement of regulatory requirements.

**(5)** Ensure adaptability of regulatory language to changing conditions.
