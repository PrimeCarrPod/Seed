## SEC. 409. UTILITY ACCOUNTABILITY AND OVERSIGHT

**SEC. 409.** (a) ANNUAL SAFETY REPORTS.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**All electrical utilities in high fire risk areas shall submit annual safety reports to the Secretary of Energy and applicable state public utilities commissions.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**Annual safety reports shall include:
(A) Ignition risk assessment
(B) Grid hardening progress
(C) Vegetation management compliance
(D) PSPS event summary
(E) Equipment condition assessment
(F) Workforce training records
(G) Customer complaint data
(H) Financial expenditure report

**(b) INDEPENDENT SAFETY ASSESSMENTS.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL ASSESSMENTS.—**The Secretary shall conduct biennial independent safety assessments of all electrical utilities in high fire risk areas.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**Assessments shall evaluate:
(A) Compliance with grid hardening standards
(B) Effectiveness of vegetation management
(C) PSPS protocol compliance
(D) Equipment maintenance practices
(E) Workforce competency
(F) Financial adequacy of hardening programs

**(c) ENFORCEMENT.—**

**(1) PENALTIES.—**The Secretary may impose penalties of up to $1,000,000 per day for violations of grid hardening standards.

**(2) COMPLIANCE ORDERS.—**The Secretary may issue compliance orders requiring utilities to take specific actions to achieve compliance with grid hardening standards.

**(3) MANAGEMENT CHANGES.—**In cases of persistent non-compliance, the Secretary may require changes to utility management or governance.

**(d) RATEPAYER ADVOCACY.—**

**(1) CONSUMER ADVOCATE.—**The Secretary shall establish an Office of the Consumer Advocate to represent ratepayer interests in grid hardening proceedings.

**(2) BILL PROTECTIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish protections against rate increases that exceed 3% annually for residential customers, with exceptions for documented grid hardening costs.
