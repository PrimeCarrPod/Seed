## SEC. 417. GRID HARDENING COORDINATION

**SEC. 417.** (a) INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) FEDERAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary of Energy shall coordinate grid hardening activities with:
(A) Department of Agriculture
(B) Department of Interior
(C) Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
(D) Environmental Protection Agency
(E) Department of Homeland Security

**(2) STATE COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall coordinate with state public utilities commissions on grid hardening standards and enforcement.

**(b) UTILITY COORDINATION.—**

**(1) REGIONAL COORDINATION.—**The Secretary shall establish regional grid hardening coordination groups, including utilities, state regulators, and federal agencies.

**(2) BEST PRACTICES.—**The Secretary shall develop and disseminate best practices for grid hardening.

**(c) INTERNATIONAL COORDINATION.—**

**(1) INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS.—**The Secretary shall partner with international organizations on grid hardening research and best practices.

**(2) TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER.—**The Secretary shall facilitate technology transfer of grid hardening technologies to and from other countries.

## SEC. 418. GRID HARDENING REPORTING

**SEC. 418.** (a) ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REPORT.—

**(1) IN GENERAL.—**Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress an annual performance report on grid hardening programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The report shall include:
(A) Miles of line hardened by type
(B) Ignition risk reduction achieved
(C) PSPS event trends
(D) Utility compliance rates
(E) Expenditures by program
(F) Performance metrics and trends

**(b) GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE REVIEW.—**

**(1) BIENNIAL REVIEW.—**The Comptroller General shall conduct a biennial review of grid hardening programs.

**(2) CONTENTS.—**The review shall assess:
(A) Program effectiveness
(B) Efficiency of operations
(C) Adequacy of funding
(D) Compliance with standards
(E) Achievement of targets
