## SEC. 705. FEDERAL WILDFIRE RESPONSE FLEET MODERNIZATION

**SEC. 705.** (a) AIR TANKER MODERNIZATION.—

**(1) REPLACEMENT.—**The Secretary of Agriculture shall develop and implement a plan to replace the federal air tanker fleet with next-generation aircraft within 10 years.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**Next-generation air tankers shall:
(A) Have a minimum capacity of 3,000 gallons
(B) Be capable of night operations with night vision technology
(C) Have a maximum response time of 30 minutes from dispatch
(D) Be equipped with real-time fire mapping sensors
(E) Have a service life of at least 30 years

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000,000 over 10 years for air tanker modernization.

**(b) HELICOPTER MODERNIZATION.—

**(1) REPLACEMENT.—**The Secretary shall develop and implement a plan to replace the federal helicopter fleet with next-generation helicopters within 10 years.

**(2) REQUIREMENTS.—**Next-generation helicopters shall:
(A) Have a minimum capacity of 1,000 gallons
(B) Be capable of night operations with night vision technology
(C) Have a maximum response time of 20 minutes from dispatch
(D) Be equipped with real-time fire mapping sensors

**(3) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $3,000,000,000 over 10 years for helicopter modernization.

**(c) GROUND EQUIPMENT MODERNIZATION.—

**(1) FIRE ENGINES.—**The Secretary shall replace aging fire engines with next-generation engines equipped with:
(A) GPS tracking
(B) Real-time communication
(C) Night vision capabilities
(D) Remote-controlled nozzles

**(2) FUNDING.—**There is authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000,000 over 10 years for ground equipment modernization.
