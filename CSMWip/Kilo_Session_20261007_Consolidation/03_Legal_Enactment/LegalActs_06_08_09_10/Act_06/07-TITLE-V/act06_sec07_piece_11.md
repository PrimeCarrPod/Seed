## SEC. 725. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION FOR CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

**SEC. 725.** (a) HOSPITALS AND MEDICAL FACILITIES.—

**(1) PROTECTION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop wildfire protection plans for hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI.

**(2) EVACUATION PROTOCOLS.—**The Secretary shall develop evacuation protocols for hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI.

**(b) EMERGENCY RESPONSE FACILITIES.—

**(1) PROTECTION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop wildfire protection plans for emergency response facilities in the WUI.

**(2) REDUNDANT SYSTEMS.—**The Secretary shall ensure that emergency response facilities have redundant communication and power systems.

**(c) WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS.—

**(1) PROTECTION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop wildfire protection plans for water and wastewater systems in the WUI.

**(2) BACKUP SYSTEMS.—**The Secretary shall ensure that water and wastewater systems have backup power and supply systems.

## SEC. 726. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION FOR SCHOOLS

**SEC. 726.** (a) SCHOOL PROTECTION PLANS.—

**(1) WILDFIRE PROTECTION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop wildfire protection plans for schools in the WUI.

**(2) EVACUATION DRILLS.—**Schools in the WUI shall conduct wildfire evacuation drills at least twice annually.

**(b) SHELTER-IN-PLACE FACILITIES.—

**(1) DESIGNATION.—**The Secretary shall designate shelter-in-place facilities in schools where evacuation is not possible.

**(2) STANDARDS.—**Shelter-in-place facilities shall meet fire-resistant construction standards and have independent air filtration systems.
