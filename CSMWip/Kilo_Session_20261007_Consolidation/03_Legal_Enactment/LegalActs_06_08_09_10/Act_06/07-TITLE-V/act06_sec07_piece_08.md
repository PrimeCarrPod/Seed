## SEC. 717. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION SAFETY STANDARDS

**SEC. 717.** (a) FIREFIGHTER SAFETY.—

**(1) OSHA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary of Labor, in coordination with the Secretary of Agriculture, shall establish occupational safety standards for wildland firefighters, including:
(A) Heat illness prevention
(B) Smoke exposure limits
(C) Fatigue management
(D) Personal protective equipment standards
(E) Emergency response procedures

**(2) TRAINING REQUIREMENTS.—**All wildland firefighters shall complete mandatory safety training before fire assignments.

**(b) PUBLIC SAFETY.—

**(1) EVACUATION SAFETY.—**The Secretary shall establish safety standards for community evacuations during wildfire events.

**(2) SHELTER-IN-PLACE.—**The Secretary shall establish standards for shelter-in-place facilities in areas where evacuation is not possible.

## SEC. 718. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW

**SEC. 718.** (a) STREAMLINED REVIEW.—

**(1) CATEGORICAL EXCLUSIONS.—**The Secretary shall establish categorical exclusions for routine wildfire response activities.

**(2) PROGRAMMATIC REVIEWS.—**The Secretary shall conduct programmatic environmental reviews for wildfire response programs.

**(b) MITIGATION.—

**(1) HABITAT MITIGATION.—**Wildfire response activities that impact wildlife habitat shall include habitat mitigation measures.

**(2) CULTURAL RESOURCE PROTECTION.—**Wildfire response activities shall include measures to protect cultural resources.

---

## SEC. 719. RESPONSE MODERNIZATION DATA AND ANALYTICS

**SEC. 719.** (a) NATIONAL WILDFIRE RESPONSE DATABASE.—

**(1) ESTABLISHMENT.—**The Secretary shall establish a national database for wildfire response data, including:
(A) Fleet status and availability
(B) Response time data
(C) Resource allocation data
(D) Fire outcome data
(E) Workforce data

**(2) DATA STANDARDS.—**The Secretary shall establish data standards for wildfire response data collection and reporting.

**(b) PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS.—

**(1) FIRE BEHAVIOR MODELING.—**The Secretary shall develop predictive models for fire behavior, incorporating:
(A) Weather data
(B) Fuel data
(C) Topography
(D) Historical fire data

**(2) RESOURCE ALLOCATION.—**The Secretary shall develop tools for optimizing wildfire response resource allocation.

**(c) PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY.—**

**(1) ONLINE DASHBOARD.—**The Secretary shall maintain a public online dashboard displaying wildfire response data.

**(2) ANNUAL REPORT.—**The Secretary shall submit an annual report to Congress on wildfire response modernization.
