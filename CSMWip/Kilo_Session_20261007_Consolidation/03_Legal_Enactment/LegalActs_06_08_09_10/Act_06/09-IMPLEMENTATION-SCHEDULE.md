# TITLE VII — IMPLEMENTATION SCHEDULE

## SEC. 901. SHORT TITLE

**SEC. 901.** This title may be cited as the "Wildfire Prevention and Grid Resilience Implementation Act of 2026."

---

## SEC. 902. FINDINGS SPECIFIC TO IMPLEMENTATION

**SEC. 902.** The Congress finds the following with respect to implementation:

**(1) Urgency**
The wildfire crisis demands immediate action. Every year of delay results in increased fire severity, property damage, and loss of life. Implementation must begin immediately upon enactment.

**(2) Phased Approach**
A phased implementation approach is necessary to allow for capacity building, workforce development, and technology deployment. Phasing must balance urgency with feasibility.

**(3) Coordination Complexity**
Implementation requires coordination across multiple federal agencies, states, Tribal nations, local governments, utilities, and private stakeholders. Clear roles and responsibilities are essential.

**(4) Adaptive Management**
Implementation must incorporate adaptive management principles, allowing for course correction based on experience and changing conditions.

**(5) Accountability**
Clear timelines, milestones, and accountability mechanisms are essential to ensure implementation stays on track.

**(6) Long-Term Commitment**
Wildfire prevention is a long-term effort requiring sustained commitment over 10+ years. Implementation must be designed for long-term success.

## SEC. 903. PURPOSES OF TITLE VII

**SEC. 903.** The purposes of this title are to:

**(1)** Establish a phased implementation schedule for all titles of this Act.

**(2)** Define clear roles and responsibilities for implementation.

**(3)** Establish milestones and deadlines for implementation activities.

**(4)** Create accountability mechanisms to ensure implementation stays on track.

**(5)** Provide for adaptive management and course correction.

**(6)** Ensure coordination across federal, state, Tribal, local, and private stakeholders.

**(7)** Establish reporting requirements to track implementation progress.

---

## SEC. 904. DEFINITIONS FOR TITLE VII

**SEC. 904.** In this title:

**(1) "Implementation Plan"**
The comprehensive plan developed under Sec. 905 to guide implementation of this Act.

**(2) "Milestone"**
A specific, measurable achievement that marks progress toward implementation objectives.

**(3) "Adaptive Management"**
A systematic process for continually improving management policies and practices by learning from the outcomes of operational programs.

**(4) "Stakeholder"**
Any federal agency, state government, Tribal nation, local government, utility, or private entity involved in implementation.

## SEC. 905. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

**SEC. 905.** (a) IN GENERAL.—Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment, the Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy, the Secretary of Interior, and other relevant federal agencies, shall develop a comprehensive implementation plan for this Act.

**(b) CONTENTS.—**The implementation plan shall include:

**(1) Roles and Responsibilities**
Clear definition of roles and responsibilities for each federal agency, state government, Tribal nation, local government, and utility involved in implementation.

**(2) Phased Schedule**
A phased implementation schedule with specific milestones and deadlines for each title of this Act.

**(3) Resource Requirements**
An assessment of the resources (funding, personnel, equipment, technology) required for implementation.

**(4) Coordination Mechanisms**
Mechanisms for coordinating implementation across federal, state, Tribal, local, and private stakeholders.

**(5) Performance Metrics**
Quantifiable performance metrics for measuring implementation progress.

**(6) Risk Assessment**
An assessment of implementation risks and mitigation strategies.

**(7) Adaptive Management Framework**
A framework for adaptive management, including procedures for course correction based on experience.

**(c) UPDATES.—**The implementation plan shall be updated annually to reflect progress, lessons learned, and changing conditions.

**(d) PUBLIC COMMENT.—**The Secretary shall provide for public comment on the implementation plan and annual updates.

---

**(3)** Grid hardening standards published

## SEC. 911. IMPLEMENTATION COORDINATION

**SEC. 911.** (a) FEDERAL INTERAGENCY COORDINATION.—

**(1) NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREVENTION COUNCIL.—**There is established a National Wildfire Prevention Council to coordinate implementation across federal agencies.

**(2) MEMBERSHIP.—**The Council shall include:
(A) Secretary of Agriculture (Chair)
(B) Secretary of Energy
(C) Secretary of Interior
(D) Secretary of Homeland Security
(E) Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency
(F) Director of the Federal Emergency Management Agency
(G) Other relevant federal officials

**(3) FUNCTIONS.—**The Council shall:
(A) Coordinate implementation across federal agencies
(B) Resolve interagency conflicts
(C) Develop joint implementation strategies
(D) Report annually to Congress on implementation progress

**(b) STATE COORDINATION.—

**(1) STATE IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**States shall develop state implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(2) STATE COORDINATORS.—**Each state shall designate a state wildfire prevention coordinator.

**(c) TRIBAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) TRIBAL CONSULTATION.—**The Secretary shall consult with Tribal nations on implementation activities affecting Tribal lands or resources.

**(2) TRIBAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Tribal nations may develop Tribal implementation plans that align with the national implementation plan.

**(d) LOCAL COORDINATION.—

**(1) LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**Local governments in high fire risk areas shall develop local implementation plans.

**(2) LOCAL COORDINATORS.—**Local governments shall designate local wildfire prevention coordinators.

## SEC. 913. IMPLEMENTATION PREEMPTION

**SEC. 913.** (a) FEDERAL STANDARDS.—**The national implementation standards established under this title shall preempt any inconsistent state or local standards, except where state standards are more protective.

**(b) STATE AUTHORITY.—**States may adopt implementation standards that are more protective than the national standards.

## SEC. 922. IMPLEMENTATION FOR CRITICAL FACILITIES

**SEC. 922.** (a) HOSPITALS AND MEDICAL FACILITIES.—

**(1) IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop implementation plans for wildfire protection of hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI.

**(2) EVACUATION PROTOCOLS.—**The Secretary shall develop evacuation protocols for hospitals and medical facilities in the WUI.

**(b) EMERGENCY RESPONSE FACILITIES.—

**(1) IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop implementation plans for wildfire protection of emergency response facilities in the WUI.

**(2) REDUNDANT SYSTEMS.—**The Secretary shall ensure that emergency response facilities have redundant communication and power systems.

**(c) WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS.—

**(1) IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop implementation plans for wildfire protection of water and wastewater systems in the WUI.

**(2) BACKUP SYSTEMS.—**The Secretary shall ensure that water and wastewater systems have backup power and supply systems.

---

## SEC. 923. IMPLEMENTATION FOR SCHOOLS

**SEC. 923.** (a) SCHOOL IMPLEMENTATION PLANS.—

**(1) WILDFIRE PROTECTION PLANS.—**The Secretary shall develop implementation plans for wildfire protection of schools in the WUI.

**(2) EVACUATION DRILLS.—**Schools in the WUI shall conduct wildfire evacuation drills at least twice annually.

**(b) SHELTER-IN-PLACE FACILITIES.—

**(1) DESIGNATION.—**The Secretary shall designate shelter-in-place facilities in schools where evacuation is not possible.

**(2) STANDARDS.—**Shelter-in-place facilities shall meet fire-resistant construction standards and have independent air filtration systems.
