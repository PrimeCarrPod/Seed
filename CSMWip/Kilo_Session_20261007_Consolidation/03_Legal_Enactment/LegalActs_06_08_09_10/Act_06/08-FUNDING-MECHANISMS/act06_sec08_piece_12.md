## SEC. 828. FUND SUNSET AND REVIEW

**SEC. 828.** (a) SUNSET.—**The authorities provided under this title shall expire on September 30, 2036, unless reauthorized by Congress.

**(b) REVIEW.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a comprehensive review of Fund activities, including:

**(1)** Assessment of Fund effectiveness
**(2)** Recommendations for reauthorization
**(3)** Proposed modifications to Fund structure
**(4)** Assessment of remaining wildfire risk
**(5)** Recommendations for future funding levels

---

## SEC. 829. FUND TRANSITION

**SEC. 829.** (a) TRANSITION PLAN.—**Not later than September 30, 2035, the Secretary shall develop a transition plan for the orderly wind-down of Fund activities if the Fund is not reauthorized.

**(b) COMPLETION OF PROJECTS.—**The transition plan shall provide for the completion of all ongoing projects and the fulfillment of all outstanding commitments.

---

## SEC. 830. FUND SEVERABILITY

**SEC. 830.** If any provision of this title or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the title and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
